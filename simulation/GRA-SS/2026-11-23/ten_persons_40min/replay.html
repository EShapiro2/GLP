Ride Auction Replay

Forty minutes of the Second-Score Auction run under sGLP: six drivers and four riders at real Manhattan pickup points. Drivers bid on every call they receive. The rider always awards the leading bid; if the driver's phone answers that he already has a ride, she skips him and the next bid wins. Every act you see was accepted by the app's rules in the recorded run.
