\section{Complete API Summary}
\label{sec:summary}
%==============================================================================
The following table summarizes the complete networking API.  The first three groups are medium-agnostic; the BLE and IP groups are medium-specific; the system predicates are the GLP body seam (Section~\ref{sec:system-predicates}).
\begin{longtable}{L{2.2cm} L{5cm} L{3cm} L{3.5cm}}
\toprule
\rowcolor{headerblue}
\textcolor{white}{\textbf{Category}} &
\textcolor{white}{\textbf{Function / Predicate}} &
\textcolor{white}{\textbf{Direction}} &
\textcolor{white}{\textbf{madGLP Role}} \\
\midrule
\endhead
\multicolumn{4}{l}{\textbf{Identity (agnostic)}} \\
\midrule
Setup & \func{putIdentity()} & Runtime $\to$ Network & Install agent key pair \\
\rowcolor{rowgray}
Query & \func{getIdentity()} & Runtime $\leftarrow$ Network & Retrieve agent public key \\
\midrule
\multicolumn{4}{l}{\textbf{Connection \& Reachability (agnostic)}} \\
\midrule
Connect & \func{onPeerConnected(cb)} & Runtime $\leftarrow$ Network & Peer reachable; pk, transport \\
\rowcolor{rowgray}
Disconnect & \func{onPeerDisconnected(cb)} & Runtime $\leftarrow$ Network & Peer unreachable on a transport; queue messages \\
Reachable & \func{isPeerReachable(pk)} & Runtime $\to$ Network & Check before send \\
\rowcolor{rowgray}
Transports & \func{peerTransports(pk)} & Runtime $\to$ Network & Media now reaching pk (proximity) \\
\midrule
\multicolumn{4}{l}{\textbf{Communication (agnostic)}} \\
\midrule
Send & \func{send(pubKey, payload)} & Runtime $\to$ Network & Implements madGLP Send transaction \\
\rowcolor{rowgray}
Receive & \func{onMessageReceived(cb)} & Runtime $\leftarrow$ Network & Feeds madGLP Receive transaction \\
\midrule
\multicolumn{4}{l}{\textbf{BLE}} \\
\midrule
\rowcolor{rowgray}
Scan & \func{getPeers()} & Runtime $\to$ Network & Find nearby agents (proximity) \\
Notify & \func{onPeerDiscovered(cb)} & Runtime $\leftarrow$ Network & Nearby device matching Grassroots prefix \\
\rowcolor{rowgray}
Policy & \func{setTrustLevel(level)} & Runtime $\to$ Network & Set BLE cold-call trust level \\
\midrule
\multicolumn{4}{l}{\textbf{IP}} \\
\midrule
\rowcolor{rowgray}
Connectivity & \func{onConnectivityStatusChanged(cb)} & Runtime $\leftarrow$ Network & Public IP change; (old?, new?) \\
Own address & \func{getPublicAddress()} & Runtime $\leftarrow$ Network & Current public ip:port, share with peers \\
\rowcolor{rowgray}
Gen link & \func{generatePeerLink()} & Runtime $\to$ Network & Create signed RV-mediated invite URI \\
Use link & \func{consumePeerLink(uri)} & Runtime $\to$ Network & Redeem invite through RV and import inviter \\
\rowcolor{rowgray}
Feed address & \func{putPeerAddress(pk, address)} & Runtime $\to$ Network & Hand the layer a GLP-learned peer address \\
\midrule
\multicolumn{4}{l}{\textbf{System predicates (GLP body seam)}} \\
\midrule
\rowcolor{rowgray}
Address & \code{peer\_address/2} & GLP $\leftarrow$ Network & Observed public address of a connected peer \\
Punch & \code{punch\_udp/1} & GLP $\to$ Network & Initiate UDP hole-punch toward an address \\
\rowcolor{rowgray}
Sign & \code{sign/2} & GLP $\leftrightarrow$ Network & Ed25519 signature under the agent's key \\
Verify & \code{valid\_attestation/4} & GLP $\to$ Network & Guard: a signature validly binds two public keys \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\subsection{Correspondence to IsolateManager}
The following table shows how each \code{IsolateManager} responsibility maps to networking calls:
\begin{longtable}{L{5cm} L{4cm} L{4.5cm}}
\toprule
\rowcolor{headerblue}
\textcolor{white}{\textbf{IsolateManager}} &
\textcolor{white}{\textbf{Networking}} &
\textcolor{white}{\textbf{Notes}} \\
\midrule
\endhead
Route message to target isolate & \func{send(pubKey, payload)} & Resolve agent name to pubkey, serialize, transmit \\
\rowcolor{rowgray}
Deliver to isolate's incoming stream & \func{onMessageReceived(cb)} & Deserialize, dispatch to Receive transaction \\
Register isolate for agent & \func{onPeerConnected(cb)} & BLE/UDX connection replaces isolate spawn \\
\rowcolor{rowgray}
Cold-call to index-0 serializer & \func{send(pubKey, payload)} & Same primitive; a cold-call is an assignment to the index-0 serializer \\
Global link assignment delivery & \func{send(pubKey, payload)} & Same primitive; distinguished by the global name \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
