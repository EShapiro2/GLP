Organisation
Organisation renames Guardians, and extends and amends it (GSC, From Grassroots Contract to Grassroots App, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06231).
Renaming of Guardians: an organisation for a ward and an officer for a guardian.
Vocabulary: x is the founder of z = founder(x,z); z has y as its first officer = first_officer(z,y); y holds the role r in z = role(y,z,r); z has y in the role r = has_role(z,y,r); x is a participant of z = participant(x,z); z has x as a participant = has_participant(z,x); the bylaws of z entitle x to propose motions = proposer(x,z); z has m proposed = proposed(z,m); the bylaws of z entitle x to vote on m = voter(x,z,m); z holds x's vote on m = vote(z,x,m); the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z = decided(z,m); the bylaws of z entitle x to take m = taker(x,z,m); z has m taken = taken(z,m); the bylaws of z entitle x to amend them as m says = amender(x,z,m); the amendment m is decided according to the bylaws of z = amendment_decided(z,m); z has its bylaws amended = amended(z).
Roles: those who decide m for z = act_deciders(z,m); those who admit participants of z = admitters(z), listed by z has y as an officer and y is an officer of z; those who expel participants of z = expellers(z), listed by z has y as an officer and y is an officer of z.
x is entitled to propose m in z: x is an officer of z and the bylaws of z entitle x to propose motions.
x is entitled to vote on m in z: x is a participant of z and the bylaws of z entitle x to vote on m.
x is entitled to take m for z: x is an officer of z, z has m proposed and the bylaws of z entitle x to take m.
x is entitled to amend the bylaws of z as m says: x is an officer of z, z has m proposed and the bylaws of z entitle x to amend them as m says.
After x proposes m in z, which requires z does not have m proposed: z has m proposed.
After x votes on m in z, which requires z does not hold x's vote on m: z holds x's vote on m.
After x takes m for z, which requires z does not have m taken: z has m taken.
After y becomes a participant of z, which requires y is not a participant of z: y is a participant of z and z has y as a participant.
After y ends y's participation in z: no longer y is a participant of z and no longer z has y as a participant.
After g ends y's participation in z: no longer y is a participant of z and no longer z has y as a participant.
After y ends y's participation and y's office in z: no longer y is a participant of z, no longer z has y as a participant, no longer y is an officer of z, no longer z has y as an officer, y holds no role in z and z has y in no role.
After g ends y's participation and y's office in z: no longer y is a participant of z, no longer z has y as a participant, no longer y is an officer of z, no longer z has y as an officer, y holds no role in z and z has y in no role.
After x amends the bylaws of z as m says, which requires z does not have m taken: z has its bylaws amended and z has m taken.
