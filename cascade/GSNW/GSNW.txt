GSNW: Grassroots Social Network with Wards
GSNW amends GSN and GnW.
Alice is a person, Carol a ward.
1. ■ A ward's messages.  Carol may send a message to a friend, and to a group of which she is a member.
2. ■ A ward's group.  Carol may create a group, of which she is the leader and first member, and may close it by leaving it, as may Alice, a guardian of Carol.
Pragmas
A ward's messages: Carol [Ward message] <text>
  Carol [Ward post] <text>
  Carol [Ward post own] <text>
A ward's group: Carol [Ward create] <name>
  Carol [Ward close]
  Alice [Guardian close] <group>
Ward and person: Bob [Ward join] <person>
  Carol: Bob asks you to join his group.
  Alice: Bob asks that your ward Carol join his group.
  Carol [Ward leave] [Ward remove]
  Alice [Ward withdraw] <person> <group>
Ward and ward: Carol [Wards join] <person>
  Dave: Carol asks you to join her group.
  Alice: Carol asks that Dave join her group.
  Bob: Carol asks that your ward Dave join her group.
