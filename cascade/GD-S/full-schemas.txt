GD-S: Separating GD (expanded schemas)
GKK:0 - | An *identity* is a unique key pair.
GKK:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. A party to a contract must have an identity.
GKK:1 ○ Establish: identity(Alice), knows(Alice,Alice) <- ~identity(Alice) guarded by Alice.
GKK:1 ○ | Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
GKK:2 ■ Acquaint: knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice) <- guarded by Alice, Bob.
GKK:3 ■ Introduce: knows(Bob,Carol) <- knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice), knows(Alice,Carol) guarded by Alice, Bob.
GSG:0 - | Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts.
GSG:0 - | Alice and Bob are persons.
GSG:1+GD-S:1 ○ Befriend: friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice) <- knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice), ~friend(Alice,Bob), ~friend(Bob,Alice), ~distrusts(Alice,Bob), ~distrusts(Bob,Alice) guarded by Alice, Bob.
GSG:1 ○ | Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
GSG:2 ■ Unfriend: ~friend(Alice,Bob), ~friend(Bob,Alice) <- friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice) guarded by Alice.
GSG:3 ○ | Loss of Trust.
GSG:3 ○ | Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
GD:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
GD:1 ■ Distrust: distrusts(Alice,Carol) <- knows(Alice,Carol), ~friend(Alice,Carol), ~friend(Carol,Alice), ~distrusts(Alice,Carol) guarded by Alice.
GD:1 ■ Distrust friend: distrusts(Alice,Carol), ~friend(Alice,Carol), ~friend(Carol,Alice) <- ~distrusts(Alice,Carol), friend(Alice,Carol), friend(Carol,Alice) guarded by Alice.
GD:2 ■ Propagate: distrusts(Alice,Carol) <- ~knows(Alice,Carol), friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice), distrusts(Bob,Carol), ~distrusts(Alice,Carol) guarded by Alice.
GD:3 ■ Cease: ~distrusts(Alice,Carol) <- distrusts(Alice,Carol) guarded by Alice.
GD-S:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
GD-S:2 ○ | Taking sides.
GD-S:2 ○ | Alice, whose friend Bob has declared distrust of Carol, will eventually declare distrust of Carol or end her friendship with Bob, unless Bob withdraws the declaration.
