GnW: Guardians 'n' Wards
Of GKK: An *identity* is a unique key pair. A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of GSG: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts. Alice and Bob are persons.
Of GnW: Bob *trusts* Carol, a ward of Alice, when he trusts Alice. The *decision rule*, a parameter of GnW, says who decides for a ward and how. A ward takes an act of a party by those who decide for it. Alice, Bob and Erin are persons, Carol and Dave wards; in clause 4 Alice and Bob are possibly one person.
GKK:1, GnW:3. ■ Establish: Alice may establish an identity, and then Alice is a person. Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
GKK:2, GnW:3. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
GKK:3, GnW:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other, Alice knows Carol, Alice is a person, Bob is a person and Carol is a person.
GSG:1, GnW:3. ■ Befriend: Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other, Alice is a person and Bob is a person. Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
GSG:2, GnW:3. ■ Person unfriend: Alice may end her friendship with Bob, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
GSG:3. ○ Loss of Trust. Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
GnW:1. ■ Enter: Alice may create Carol, and Carol must consent, provided Alice is a person. Alice chooses Carol's key pair.
GnW:2. ■ Guardian decisions. Make guardian: Those who decide for Carol may make Bob a guardian of Carol, with the consent of Bob, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired, Bob is a person, Carol has Alice as a guardian and Bob counts Alice as a friend. Resign: Bob may end his guardianship of Carol. End guardian: Those who decide for Carol, other than Bob, may end Bob's guardianship of Carol. Guardian unfriend: Bob may end his friendship with his ward Carol. Carol may not end a friendship with her guardian.
GnW:3. ■ Ward and person. Ward acquaint: Carol and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Carol is a ward, Carol has Alice as a guardian and Bob is a person. Ward befriend: Carol and Bob may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Bob is a person, Carol and Bob know each other and Alice and Bob are friends. Ward end: Carol may end her friendship with Bob, provided Carol does not have Bob as a guardian. Unfriend: Bob may end his friendship with Carol, provided Bob is not a guardian of Carol. Ward unfriend: Alice may end the friendship of Carol and Bob, provided Carol has Alice as a guardian and Bob is not a guardian of Carol.
GnW:4. ■ Wards. Wards acquaint: Carol and Dave may jointly come to know each other, provided Carol is a ward, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Dave is a ward and Dave has Bob as a guardian. Wards befriend: Carol and Dave may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice and Bob, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Dave is a ward, Dave is not retired, Dave has Bob as a guardian, Carol and Dave know each other, Alice knows Dave and Alice and Bob are friends. Wards of one guardian befriend: Carol and Dave may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Dave is a ward, Dave is not retired, Dave has Alice as a guardian and Carol and Dave know each other.
GnW:5. ■ Retire: Those who decide for Carol may retire Carol. Carol can then take no act.
