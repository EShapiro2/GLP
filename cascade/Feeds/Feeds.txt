Feeds
Feeds extends and restricts Labelled Overlay.
A *feed* is named by its author.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Bob and Carol possibly one person.
1. ■ Post: Alice has a feed and may post to it an item, created or received.
2. ■ Deliver: Alice, who has a Carol-pointer to Bob who knows her, receives from Bob every item of Carol's feed that Bob holds.
3. ■ Labeled point transitive: Labelled Overlay:2, only if Carol knows Alice.
Logic pragmas
Feeds extends and restricts Labelled Overlay.
A *feed* is named by its author.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Item an item, Bob and Carol possibly one person.
1. ■ Post: Alice may post Item to her feed. Alice has a feed, and the item is created or received.
2. ■ Deliver: Alice receives from Bob the item Item of Carol's feed, provided Alice has a Carol-pointer to Bob, Bob knows Alice and Bob holds the item Item of Carol's feed.
3. ■ Labeled point transitive: Labelled Overlay:2, only if Carol knows Alice.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x holds the item t of z's feed = item(x,t,z); x has received the item t of z's feed from y = received(x,t,z,y).
After x posts t to x's feed, which requires x does not hold the item t of x's feed: x holds the item t of x's feed.
After x receives from y the item t of z's feed, which requires x does not hold the item t of z's feed: x holds the item t of z's feed and x has received the item t of z's feed from y.
Interface pragmas
Posts: the items Alice holds.
Feeds: the items Alice holds, by feed, each once.
