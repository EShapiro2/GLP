Warded Groups
Warded Groups extends, restricts and strengthens Groups and Guardians.
After w withdraws y from x's g: no longer y is a member of the group g of x and no longer x has y as a member of the group g.
After y closes the group g of z: no longer z is the leader of g, z has no one as a member of the group g and no one is a member of the group g of z.
