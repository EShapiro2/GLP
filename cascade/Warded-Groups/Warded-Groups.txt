Warded Groups
Warded Groups inherits from Groups and Guardians.
Alice is a person, Wendy a ward.
1. ■ A ward's messages. Ward message: Wendy may send a message to a friend (Groups:1). Ward post: Wendy may send a message to a group (Groups:4).
2. ■ A ward's group. Ward create: Wendy may create a group (Groups:2). Ward close: Wendy may close it (Groups:2). Guardian close: Alice, a guardian of Wendy, may close it.
3. ■ Ward join: Wendy may join a group (Groups:3) only with the consent of Alice, a guardian of Wendy.
Logic pragmas
Warded Groups unites Groups and a strengthening of Guardians, and extends and augments the union.
Alice and Bob are persons, Wendy and Will wards, Grupo a group, Text a message; in clause 1 Wendy is possibly Alice.
1. ■ A ward's messages. Message: Groups:1, only if Alice is a person. Post: Groups:4 Post, only if Bob is a person. Post own: Groups:4 Post own, only if Alice is a person. Ward message: Wendy may send Text to Bob, provided Wendy is a ward, Wendy is not retired and Wendy and Bob are friends. Ward post: Wendy may send Text to the group Grupo of Alice, provided Wendy is a ward, Wendy is not retired and Wendy is a member of the group Grupo of Alice. Ward post own: Wendy may send Text to the group Grupo of Wendy, provided Wendy is a ward, Wendy is not retired and Wendy is a member of the group Grupo of Wendy.
2. ■ A ward's group. Create: Groups:2 Create, only if Alice is a person. Close: Groups:2 Close, only if Alice is a person. Ward create: Wendy may create Grupo, provided Wendy is a ward and Wendy is not retired. Ward close: Wendy may close Grupo by leaving it, provided Wendy is a ward and Wendy is not retired. Guardian close: Alice may close the group Grupo of Wendy, provided Alice is a guardian of Wendy.
3. ■ Join: Groups:3 Join, only if Bob is a person. Ward join: Bob may add Wendy to Grupo, with the consent of Wendy and Alice, provided Bob is the leader of Grupo, Bob is a person, Bob and Wendy are friends, Wendy has Alice as a guardian, Alice is a guardian of Wendy and Alice and Bob are friends. Wards join: Wendy may add Will to Grupo, with the consent of Will, Alice and Bob, provided Wendy is the leader of Grupo, Wendy is a ward, Wendy has Alice as a guardian, Alice is a guardian of Wendy, Wendy and Will are friends, Will is a ward, Will has Bob as a guardian, Bob is a guardian of Will, Alice knows Will and Alice and Bob are friends. Wards of one guardian join: Wendy may add Will to Grupo, with the consent of Will and Alice, provided Wendy is the leader of Grupo, Wendy is a ward, Wendy has Alice as a guardian, Alice is a guardian of Wendy, Wendy and Will are friends, Will is a ward, Will has Alice as a guardian and Alice is a guardian of Will. Ward withdraw: Alice may withdraw Wendy from Bob's Grupo, provided Wendy has Alice as a guardian and Alice is a guardian of Wendy. Ward add: Wendy may add Bob to Grupo, with the consent of Bob and Alice, provided Wendy is the leader of Grupo, Wendy is a ward, Wendy is not retired, Wendy has Alice as a guardian, Alice is a guardian of Wendy, Wendy and Bob are friends and Alice and Bob are friends. Ward leave: Wendy may end her membership in Alice's Grupo, provided Wendy is a ward and Wendy is not retired. Ward remove: Wendy may end Bob's membership in Grupo, provided Wendy is the leader of Grupo, Wendy is a ward and Wendy is not retired. Guardian unfriend: Guardians:2 Guardian unfriend, and then Bob is a member of no group of Wendy, Wendy is a member of no group of Bob, Bob has Wendy as a member of no group and Wendy has Bob as a member of no group. Ward end: Guardians:3 Ward end, and then Wendy is a member of no group of Bob, Bob is a member of no group of Wendy, Wendy has Bob as a member of no group and Bob has Wendy as a member of no group. Unfriend: Guardians:3 Unfriend, and then Bob is a member of no group of Wendy, Wendy is a member of no group of Bob, Bob has Wendy as a member of no group and Wendy has Bob as a member of no group. Ward unfriend: Guardians:3 Ward unfriend, and then Wendy is a member of no group of Bob, Bob is a member of no group of Wendy, Wendy has Bob as a member of no group and Bob has Wendy as a member of no group. Retire: Guardians:5 Retire, and then Wendy is a member of no group of anyone and no one has Wendy as a member of any group.
Logic pragmas.
After w withdraws y from x's g: no longer y is a member of the group g of x and no longer x has y as a member of the group g.
After y closes the group g of z: no longer z is the leader of g, z has no one as a member of the group g and no one is a member of the group g of z.
