Local Overlay
Local Overlay restricts Overlay.
A person has a *location*. d > 0, an integer, and e > 0 are parameters of Local Overlay.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
1. ■ Point friend: Overlay:1, only if either Alice points to fewer than d persons and Bob is closest to Alice among all of Alice's unpointed friends, or Bob is closer to Alice by more than e than some person pointed to by Alice.
2. ■ Point transitive: Overlay:2, only if Carol is closer to Alice by more than e than some person pointed to by Alice.
3. ■ Unpoint: Overlay:3, only if Alice points to more than d persons, with Bob furthest from Alice among them.
