Promoted Gossip
Promoted Gossip inherits from Gossip and Community Bank.
The budget of a post is Orga-coins, and the cost of a forward is a number of them. The gossip protocol also says what part of the budget is the holder's.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a community.
1. ■ Post: Gossip:1, and then the budget of the post comes from the Orga-coins Alice holds.
2. ■ Tell: Gossip:2, and then Bob's part of the budget goes to Bob with the post.
3. ■ Keep: Alice may take her part of the budget of a post she holds, according to the gossip protocol.
Logic pragmas
Promoted Gossip unites Gossip and Community Bank, and extends and augments the union.
The budget of a post is Orga-coins, and the cost of a forward is a number of them. The gossip protocol also says what part of the budget is the holder's.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a community, Kept a number.
1. ■ Post: Gossip:1, and then no longer Alice holds Budget Orga-coins, Alice holds Budget Orga-coins for Post and Alice has a part of Post to take.
2. ■ Tell: Gossip:2, and then no longer Alice holds Part Orga-coins for Post, Bob holds Part Orga-coins for Post and Bob has a part of Post to take.
3. ■ Keep: Alice may take Kept Orga-coins of Post, provided Alice holds the post Post and the gossip protocol leaves Alice Kept of Post.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x holds n z-coins for p = post_coins(x,p,z,*n); x has a part of p to take = part_due(x,p); the gossip protocol leaves x n of p = leaves(x,p,n).
After x takes n z-coins of p: no longer x has a part of p to take, no longer x holds n z-coins for p and x holds n z-coins.
Environment: the gossip protocol leaves x n of p.
