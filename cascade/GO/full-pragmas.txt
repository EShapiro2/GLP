GO: Grassroots Organisation
Of GKK: An *identity* is a unique key pair. A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of GSG: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts. Alice and Bob are persons.
Of GnW: Bob *trusts* Carol, an organisation of Alice, when he trusts Alice. The *decision rule*, a parameter of GnW, says who decides for an organisation and how. An organisation takes an act of a party by those who decide for it. Alice, Bob and Erin are persons, Carol and Dave organisations; in clause 4 Alice and Bob are possibly one person.
Of GO: An *organisation* is a ward with *participants*, which include its *officers*, each with a *role*. The *bylaws*, a parameter of GO, name the roles of an organisation and say who decides each of its acts and how, their own amendment included. Alice and Bob are persons, Gov and Orga organisations, Motion an act of Orga, Role a role. Parameters: x is entitled to propose m in z; x is entitled to vote on m in z; x is entitled to take m for z; the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z.
GKK:1, GnW:3. ■ Establish: Alice may establish an identity, and then Alice is a person. Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
GKK:2, GnW:3. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
GKK:3, GnW:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other, Alice knows Carol, Alice is a person, Bob is a person and Carol is a person.
GSG:1, GnW:3. ■ Befriend: Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other, Alice is a person and Bob is a person. Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
GSG:2, GnW:3. ■ Person unfriend: Alice may end her friendship with Bob, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
GSG:3. ○ Loss of Trust. Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
GnW:1. ■ Enter: Alice may create Carol, and Carol must consent, provided Alice is a person. Alice chooses Carol's key pair.
GnW:2. ■ Guardian decisions.
GnW:2, GO:4. ■ Appoint: Those who decide for Carol may make Bob an officer of Carol, with the consent of Bob, provided Carol is an organisation, Carol is not retired, Bob is a person, Carol has Alice as an officer, Bob counts Alice as a friend and Bob is a participant of Carol, and then Bob holds the role Role in Carol and Carol has Bob in the role Role. Resign: Bob may end his guardianship of Carol, and then Bob holds no role in Carol and Carol has Bob in no role. End officer: Those who decide for Carol, other than Bob, may end Bob's guardianship of Carol, and then Bob holds no role in Carol and Carol has Bob in no role. Officer unfriend: Bob may end his friendship with his organisation Carol, and then Bob holds no role in Carol and Carol has Bob in no role. Carol may not end a friendship with her officer.
GnW:3. ■ Ward and person. Organisation acquaint: Carol and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Carol is an organisation, Carol has Alice as an officer and Bob is a person. Organisation befriend: Carol and Bob may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Carol is an organisation, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as an officer, Bob is a person, Carol and Bob know each other and Alice and Bob are friends. Organisation end: Carol may end her friendship with Bob, provided Carol does not have Bob as an officer. Unfriend: Bob may end his friendship with Carol, provided Bob is not an officer of Carol. Organisation unfriend: Alice may end the friendship of Carol and Bob, provided Carol has Alice as an officer and Bob is not an officer of Carol.
GnW:4. ■ Wards. Organisations acquaint: Carol and Dave may jointly come to know each other, provided Carol is an organisation, Carol has Alice as an officer, Dave is an organisation and Dave has Bob as an officer. Organisations befriend: Carol and Dave may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice and Bob, provided Carol is an organisation, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as an officer, Dave is an organisation, Dave is not retired, Dave has Bob as an officer, Carol and Dave know each other, Alice knows Dave and Alice and Bob are friends. Organisations of one officer befriend: Carol and Dave may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Carol is an organisation, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as an officer, Dave is an organisation, Dave is not retired, Dave has Alice as an officer and Carol and Dave know each other.
GnW:5, GO:4. ■ Retire: Those who decide for Carol may retire Carol, and then no one holds any role in Carol and Carol has no one in any role. Carol can then take no act.
GO:1. ■ Found: Gov may create Orga with Alice as its first officer in the role Role, with the consent of Alice, and Orga must consent, provided Alice is a person.
GO:2. ■ Acts of the organisation. Propose: Bob may propose Motion in Orga, provided Bob is entitled to propose Motion in Orga. Vote: Bob may vote on Motion in Orga, provided Bob is entitled to vote on Motion in Orga, Orga is an organisation and Orga has Motion proposed. Act: Alice may take Motion for Orga, provided Alice is entitled to take Motion for Orga and the votes decide Motion according to the bylaws of Orga.
GO:3. ■ Participation. Join: Bob may become a participant of Orga, with the consent of those who decide for Orga. Leave: Bob may end his participation in Orga, provided Bob is not an officer of Orga. Officer leave: Bob may end his participation and his office in Orga. Expel: Those who decide for Orga may end Bob's participation in Orga, provided Bob is not an officer of Orga. Officer expel: Those who decide for Orga may end Bob's participation and Bob's office in Orga. Every role Bob holds in Orga ends with his participation.
GO:4. ■ Officers. The decision names his role.
GO:5. ■ Amend: Alice may amend the bylaws of Orga as Motion says, provided Alice is entitled to take Motion for Orga, Orga is an organisation, Orga has Motion proposed and the votes decide Motion according to the bylaws of Orga.
GO:6. ○ Accountability. Orga will be accountable at law for its undertakings where it is a juridical person, and every officer who decided an act of Orga will be accountable for it with Orga.
