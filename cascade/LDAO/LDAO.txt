LDAO: Liquid DAO
LDAO extends DAO.
Alice and Bob are persons.
1. ■ Delegate: A member of a DAO, the steward included, may make another member, whom they know, their delegate in it, one at a time; the steward records the delegation.
2. ■ Revoke: A member may revoke their delegation; the steward erases it.
3. ■ Count: After the delegate of a member who has not voted on a proposal has a vote on it counted, the steward counts the member as voting the same way, and the member can no longer vote on it.
Logic pragmas
LDAO extends DAO.
Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Delos a DAO and Prop a proposal, Carol or Dave possibly Alice.
1. ■ Delegate: Carol may make Dave her delegate in Alice's Delos, provided Carol is a member of Alice's Delos, Dave is a member of Alice's Delos, Carol knows Dave and Alice is the steward of Delos. Delegate own: Alice may make Dave her delegate in Alice's Delos, provided Alice is a member of Alice's Delos, Dave is a member of Alice's Delos, Alice knows Dave and Alice is the steward of Delos. Delegate to the steward: Carol may make Alice her delegate in Alice's Delos, provided Carol is a member of Alice's Delos, Alice is a member of Alice's Delos, Carol knows Alice and Alice is the steward of Delos.
2. ■ Revoke: Carol may revoke her delegation to Dave in Alice's Delos. Revoke own: Alice may revoke her delegation to Dave in Alice's Delos.
3. ■ Count for: Alice counts Carol as voting for Prop of Delos, provided Alice holds the delegation of Carol to Dave in Delos, Alice holds Dave's vote for Prop of Delos and Alice has not decided Prop of Delos. Count own for: Alice counts Alice as voting for Prop of Delos, provided Alice holds the delegation of Alice to Dave in Delos, Alice holds Dave's vote for Prop of Delos and Alice has not decided Prop of Delos. Count against: Alice counts Carol as voting against Prop of Delos, provided Alice holds the delegation of Carol to Dave in Delos, Alice holds Dave's vote against Prop of Delos and Alice has not decided Prop of Delos. Count own against: Alice counts Alice as voting against Prop of Delos, provided Alice holds the delegation of Alice to Dave in Delos, Alice holds Dave's vote against Prop of Delos and Alice has not decided Prop of Delos. Carol can no longer vote on Prop.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x has a delegate in d = delegating(x,d); x holds the delegation of y to z in d = delegation(x,y,z,d).
After y makes z y's delegate in x's d, which requires y does not have a delegate in d: y has a delegate in d and x holds the delegation of y to z in d.
After y revokes y's delegation to z in x's d: no longer y has a delegate in d and no longer x holds the delegation of y to z in d.
After x counts y as voting for p of d, which requires y has not voted on p of d: y has voted on p of d and x holds y's vote for p of d.
After x counts y as voting against p of d, which requires y has not voted on p of d: y has voted on p of d and x holds y's vote against p of d.

Delegations: the delegations Alice records.
