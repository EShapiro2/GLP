Networked Organisation
Networked Organisation extends Backed Currency and restricts it.
Vocabulary: z has the stake at the number n = stake(z,n); x has z-coins locked as the customer of y = customer_lock(x,y,z); y has z-coins locked as the provider of x = provider_lock(y,x,z); w holds the dispute of x and y over z-coins = dispute(w,x,y,z).
z-coins are locked between the provider y and the customer x: y has z-coins locked as the provider of x and x has z-coins locked as the customer of y.
x is an arbiter of z: x holds the role Arbiter in z and z has x in the role Arbiter.
the earlier stake of z has ended: z has the stake at no number.
x is a customer of z: x accepts a Dollar-denominated z-coin in payment.
y is a provider of z: z has the stake at the number S, y holds Held z-coins and Held is at least S.
After g sets the stake of z at n: the earlier stake of z has ended and z has the stake at the number n.
After x raises to w x's dispute with the provider y over z-coins, which requires w does not hold the dispute of x and y over z-coins: w holds the dispute of x and y over z-coins.
After y raises to w y's dispute with the customer x over z-coins, which requires w does not hold the dispute of x and y over z-coins: w holds the dispute of x and y over z-coins.
After w decides the release to x and to y of the z-coins locked between them: no longer w holds the dispute of x and y over z-coins and no longer z-coins are locked between the provider y and the customer x.
Networked Organisation:0 - | A *networked organisation* is a backed community currency whose bylaws also name the role *arbiter*. A *provider* is a person whose *stake*, the Orga-coins they hold, is at least *S*, a number of Orga-coins, and a *customer* is a person who accepts Orga-coins. A treasurer is a provider. A *dispute* is over Orga-coins locked between a provider and a customer, on *terms* that set the arbiter's fee.
Networked Organisation:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Orga a networked organisation, S and Held numbers; in clause 2 Alice is the customer and Bob the provider.
Networked Organisation:0 - | Parameters: x has z-coins locked as the customer of y; y has z-coins locked as the provider of x.
Networked Organisation:1 ■ Set stake: ~stake(Orga,_), stake(Orga,S) <- organisation(Orga) guarded by treasurers(Orga).
Networked Organisation:2 ■ | Arbitration.
Networked Organisation:2 ■ Raise: dispute(Carol,Alice,Bob,Orga) <- provider_lock(Bob,Alice,Orga), customer_lock(Alice,Bob,Orga), role(Carol,Orga,Arbiter), has_role(Orga,Carol,Arbiter), ~dispute(Carol,Alice,Bob,Orga) guarded by Alice.
Networked Organisation:2 ■ Provider raise: dispute(Carol,Alice,Bob,Orga) <- provider_lock(Bob,Alice,Orga), customer_lock(Alice,Bob,Orga), role(Carol,Orga,Arbiter), has_role(Orga,Carol,Arbiter), ~dispute(Carol,Alice,Bob,Orga) guarded by Bob.
Networked Organisation:2 ■ Arbitrate: ~dispute(Carol,Alice,Bob,Orga), ~provider_lock(Bob,Alice,Orga), ~customer_lock(Alice,Bob,Orga) <- dispute(Carol,Alice,Bob,Orga), provider_lock(Bob,Alice,Orga), customer_lock(Alice,Bob,Orga) guarded by Carol.
Networked Organisation:2 ■ | The coins are released less the fee the terms set, which is released to Carol.
