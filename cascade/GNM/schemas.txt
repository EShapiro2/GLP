GNM: Network Marketplace
GNM extends GNO and GDO (GRH, Grassroots Ride-Hailing, in preparation) and restricts REC.
Vocabulary: y has l posted in z = posted(y,l,z); x has received l of y in z = received(x,l,y,z); x has ordered the good of l from y = ordered(x,l,y); y has an order of the good of l from x = ordered_from(y,l,x); x has attested the delivery of the good of l by y = delivered(x,l,y).
y is a seller of z: y is a provider of z.
x is a buyer of z: x is a customer of z.
x and y have an order of l: x has ordered the good of l from y and y has an order of the good of l from x.
After y posts l in z, which requires y does not have l posted in z: y has l posted in z.
After l of y goes to x in z, which requires x has not received l of y in z: x has received l of y in z.
After x passes l of y to w in z, which requires w has not received l of y in z: w has received l of y in z.
After y withdraws l from z: no longer y has l posted in z.
After x orders the good of l from y in z, which requires x and y do not have an order of l: x and y have an order of l and z-coins are locked between the provider y and the customer x.
After x attests the delivery of the good of l by y in z: x has attested the delivery of the good of l by y, no longer x and y have an order of l and no longer z-coins are locked between the provider y and the customer x.
After x cancels x's order of l from y in z: no longer x and y have an order of l and no longer z-coins are locked between the provider y and the customer x.
After y cancels y's order of l by x in z: no longer x and y have an order of l and no longer z-coins are locked between the provider y and the customer x.
GNM:0 - | A *seller* is a provider and a *buyer* a customer. A *listing* names a good, its price in Orga-coins and how it is delivered; its terms say what is released to whom on cancellation.
GNM:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Orga a network organisation, Mark a fiat currency, Listing a listing, Skill a skill or a service; in clause 1 Carol is a buyer, and in clause 5 Skill is the listing.
GNM:1 ■ | Listing.
GNM:1 ■ Post: posted(Bob,Listing,Orga) <- stake(Orga,S), coin(Bob,Orga,*Held), ge(Held,S), ~posted(Bob,Listing,Orga) guarded by Bob.
GNM:1 ■ | Bob posts the listing to the buyers he points to.
GNM:1 ■ Send listing: received(Alice,Listing,Bob,Orga) <- posted(Bob,Listing,Orga), points_to(Bob,Alice), accepts(Alice,Orga,Dollar), ~received(Alice,Listing,Bob,Orga).
GNM:1 ■ Pass: received(Carol,Listing,Bob,Orga) <- accepts(Alice,Orga,Dollar), received(Alice,Listing,Bob,Orga), points_to(Alice,Carol), accepts(Carol,Orga,Mark), ~received(Carol,Listing,Bob,Orga) guarded by Alice.
GNM:1 ■ Withdraw listing: ~posted(Bob,Listing,Orga) <- posted(Bob,Listing,Orga) guarded by Bob.
GNM:2 ■ Order: ordered(Alice,Listing,Bob), ordered_from(Bob,Listing,Alice), provider_lock(Bob,Alice,Orga), customer_lock(Alice,Bob,Orga) <- accepts(Alice,Orga,Dollar), posted(Bob,Listing,Orga), knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice), ~ordered(Alice,Listing,Bob), ~ordered_from(Bob,Listing,Alice) guarded by Alice, Bob.
GNM:2 ■ | Alice's payment and the deposit the terms set for Bob, on top of his stake, are then locked.
GNM:3 ◆ Deliver: delivered(Alice,Listing,Bob), ~ordered(Alice,Listing,Bob), ~ordered_from(Bob,Listing,Alice), ~provider_lock(Bob,Alice,Orga), ~customer_lock(Alice,Bob,Orga) <- ordered(Alice,Listing,Bob), ordered_from(Bob,Listing,Alice), provider_lock(Bob,Alice,Orga), customer_lock(Alice,Bob,Orga) guarded by Alice.
GNM:3 ◆ | The payment and the deposit are then released to Bob.
GNM:4 ■ Cancel: ~ordered(Alice,Listing,Bob), ~ordered_from(Bob,Listing,Alice), ~provider_lock(Bob,Alice,Orga), ~customer_lock(Alice,Bob,Orga) <- ordered(Alice,Listing,Bob), ordered_from(Bob,Listing,Alice), provider_lock(Bob,Alice,Orga), customer_lock(Alice,Bob,Orga) guarded by Alice.
GNM:4 ■ Seller cancel: ~ordered(Alice,Listing,Bob), ~ordered_from(Bob,Listing,Alice), ~provider_lock(Bob,Alice,Orga), ~customer_lock(Alice,Bob,Orga) <- ordered(Alice,Listing,Bob), ordered_from(Bob,Listing,Alice), provider_lock(Bob,Alice,Orga), customer_lock(Alice,Bob,Orga) guarded by Bob.
GNM:4 ■ | The payment and the deposit are then released to Alice and to Bob as the terms say.
GNM:5 ◆ Recommend: REC:1 <- delivered(Alice,Skill,Bob).
