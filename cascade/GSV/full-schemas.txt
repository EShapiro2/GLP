GSV: Grassroots Services (expanded schemas)
GKK:0 - | An *identity* is a unique key pair.
GKK:0 - | A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity.
GKK:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
GKK:0 - | Reflexive: x knows y.
GKK:1+GnW:3 ■ Establish: identity(Alice), person(Alice) <- ~identity(Alice) guarded by Alice.
GKK:1 ○ | Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
GKK:2+GnW:3 ■ Acquaint: knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice) <- person(Alice), person(Bob) guarded by Alice, Bob.
GKK:3+GnW:3 ■ Introduce: knows(Bob,Carol) <- knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice), knows(Alice,Carol), person(Alice), person(Bob), person(Carol) guarded by Alice, Bob.
GSG:0 - | Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts.
GSG:0 - | Alice and Bob are persons.
GSG:1+GnW:3 ■ Befriend: friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice) <- knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice), ~friend(Alice,Bob), ~friend(Bob,Alice), person(Alice), person(Bob) guarded by Alice, Bob.
GSG:1 ○ | Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
GSG:2+GnW:3 ■ Person unfriend: ~friend(Alice,Bob), ~friend(Bob,Alice) <- friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice), person(Alice), person(Bob) guarded by Alice.
GSG:3 ○ | Loss of Trust.
GSG:3 ○ | Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
GnW:0 - | Bob *trusts* Carol, a ward of Alice, when he trusts Alice. The *decision rule*, a parameter of GnW, says who decides for a ward and how. A ward takes an act of a party by those who decide for it.
GnW:0 - | Alice, Bob and Erin are persons, Carol and Dave wards; in clause 4 Alice and Bob are possibly one person.
GnW:1 ■ Enter: identity(Carol), ward(Carol), has_guardian(Carol,Alice), creator(Alice,Carol), guardian(Alice,Carol), knows(Alice,Carol), knows(Carol,Alice), friend(Alice,Carol), friend(Carol,Alice) <- person(Alice), ~identity(Carol) guarded by Alice, Carol must Carol.
GnW:1 ■ | Alice chooses Carol's key pair.
GnW:2 ■ | Guardian decisions.
GnW:2 ■ Make guardian: guardian(Bob,Carol), has_guardian(Carol,Bob), knows(Bob,Carol), knows(Carol,Bob), friend(Bob,Carol), friend(Carol,Bob) <- ward(Carol), ~retired(Carol), person(Bob), has_guardian(Carol,Alice), friend(Bob,Alice), ~guardian(Bob,Carol), ~has_guardian(Carol,Bob) guarded by guardians(Carol), Bob.
GnW:2 ■ Resign: ~guardian(Bob,Carol), ~has_guardian(Carol,Bob) <- guardian(Bob,Carol), has_guardian(Carol,Bob) guarded by Bob.
GnW:2 ■ End guardian: ~guardian(Bob,Carol), ~has_guardian(Carol,Bob) <- guardian(Bob,Carol), has_guardian(Carol,Bob) guarded by guardians(Carol,-Bob).
GnW:2 ■ Guardian unfriend: ~friend(Bob,Carol), ~friend(Carol,Bob), ~guardian(Bob,Carol), ~has_guardian(Carol,Bob) <- friend(Bob,Carol), friend(Carol,Bob), guardian(Bob,Carol), has_guardian(Carol,Bob) guarded by Bob.
GnW:2 ■ | Carol may not end a friendship with her guardian.
GnW:3 ■ | Ward and person.
GnW:3 ■ Ward acquaint: knows(Carol,Bob), knows(Bob,Carol) <- ward(Carol), has_guardian(Carol,Alice), person(Bob) guarded by Carol, Bob.
GnW:3 ■ Ward befriend: friend(Carol,Bob), friend(Bob,Carol) <- ward(Carol), ~retired(Carol), has_guardian(Carol,Alice), person(Bob), knows(Carol,Bob), knows(Bob,Carol), friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice), ~friend(Carol,Bob), ~friend(Bob,Carol) guarded by Carol, Bob, Alice.
GnW:3 ■ Ward end: ~friend(Carol,Bob), ~friend(Bob,Carol) <- ~guardian(Bob,Carol), friend(Carol,Bob), friend(Bob,Carol) guarded by Carol.
GnW:3 ■ Unfriend: ~friend(Bob,Carol), ~friend(Carol,Bob) <- ~guardian(Bob,Carol), friend(Bob,Carol), friend(Carol,Bob) guarded by Bob.
GnW:3 ■ Ward unfriend: ~friend(Carol,Bob), ~friend(Bob,Carol) <- has_guardian(Carol,Alice), ~guardian(Bob,Carol), friend(Carol,Bob), friend(Bob,Carol) guarded by Alice.
GnW:4 ■ | Wards.
GnW:4 ■ Wards acquaint: knows(Carol,Dave), knows(Dave,Carol) <- ward(Carol), has_guardian(Carol,Alice), ward(Dave), has_guardian(Dave,Bob) guarded by Carol, Dave possibly Alice=Bob.
GnW:4 ■ Wards befriend: friend(Carol,Dave), friend(Dave,Carol) <- ward(Carol), ~retired(Carol), has_guardian(Carol,Alice), ward(Dave), ~retired(Dave), has_guardian(Dave,Bob), knows(Carol,Dave), knows(Dave,Carol), knows(Alice,Dave), friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice), ~friend(Carol,Dave), ~friend(Dave,Carol) guarded by Carol, Dave, Alice, Bob possibly Alice=Bob.
GnW:4 ■ Wards of one guardian befriend: friend(Carol,Dave), friend(Dave,Carol) <- ward(Carol), ~retired(Carol), has_guardian(Carol,Alice), ward(Dave), ~retired(Dave), has_guardian(Dave,Alice), knows(Carol,Dave), knows(Dave,Carol), ~friend(Carol,Dave), ~friend(Dave,Carol) guarded by Carol, Dave, Alice.
GnW:5 ■ Retire: retired(Carol), ~guardian(_,Carol), ~has_guardian(Carol,_), ~friend(Carol,_), ~friend(_,Carol) <- ~retired(Carol) guarded by guardians(Carol).
GnW:5 ■ | Carol can then take no act.
GSV:0 - | A *service* is a ward operated by a machine at an address every person can reach.
GSV:0 - | Alice is a person, Dave a service.
