Appointments
Of Knowledge: An *identity* is a unique cryptographic key pair. A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of Friendship: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts.
Of Guardians: Bob *trusts* Orga, an organisation of Alice, when he trusts Alice. The *decision rule*, which the creator of an organisation decides, says who decides for an organisation and how. An organisation takes an act of a party by those who decide for the organisation. In clause 4 Alice and Bob are possibly one person.
Of Organisation: An *organisation* is a ward with *participants*, which include its *officers*, each with a *role*. The *bylaws*, which its founder decides, name the roles of an organisation and say who decides each of its acts and how, their own amendment included. Environment: the bylaws of z entitle x to propose motions; the bylaws of z entitle x to vote on m; the bylaws of z entitle x to take m; the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z; the bylaws of z entitle x to amend them as m says; the amendment m is decided according to the bylaws of z.
Of Booking: An organisation has *resources*, each with *slots*. A slot is *free* when it is not booked. A booking is *outstanding* until its slot has passed or it is cancelled. The bylaws name the *bound*, the number of outstanding bookings a participant may have. Environment: z has the slot s of the resource r; x has fewer outstanding bookings in z than the bound.
Of Appointments: A resource is the time of a *provider*, an officer of the organisation.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Orga, Will and Gov organisations, Motion an act of Orga, Role a role, Res a resource, Slot a slot.
