DGC: Denominated Grassroots Currencies
DGC extends GB and GDC, and restricts GB.
A *Dollar-denominated Alice-bond* is an Alice-bond issued as Alice's debt of one Dollar, due at its maturity date.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
1. ■ Issue bond: Alice may issue Alice-bonds denominated by a fiat currency, of a maturity later than the current date.
2. ■ Swap, Redeem, Escrow: GC:2, GC:4, GB:2, GB:3 and GB:4, only if the coins and bonds are of one denomination.
3. ■ Accept: Alice may accept Dollar-denominated Carol-coins in payment up to a limit of her choosing, if she holds a Dollar-denominated Carol-coin or Carol-bond; each such coin paid to Alice counts against the limit, and Alice may accept again.
4. ■ Pay: Alice may pay Bob, whom she knows, coins she holds that Bob accepts.
Logic pragmas
DGC extends GB and GDC, and restricts GB.
A *Dollar-denominated Alice-bond* is an Alice-bond issued as Alice's debt of one Dollar, due at its maturity date.
Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Dollar a fiat currency, Day a maturity date, Today a date, Cond a condition Alice, Bob and Carol agree, Sum a number; in clause 4 Carol is possibly Alice or Bob.
1. ■ Issue bond: Alice may issue Sum Dollar-denominated Alice-bonds due Day, provided the current date is Today and Day is more than Today.
2. ■ Swap: GB:2, only if the coins and bonds of Carol and Dave are of one denomination. Redeem: GB:3, only if the coins and bonds of Bob and Carol are of one denomination. Escrow: GB:4, only if the coins and bonds that Carol holds in escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond are of one denomination.
3. ■ Accept: Alice may accept one more Dollar-denominated Carol-coin in payment, provided Alice holds a Dollar-denominated Carol-coin. Accept by bond: Alice may accept one more Dollar-denominated Carol-coin in payment, provided Alice holds a Carol-bond due someone and Carol has issued a Dollar-denominated bond due someone. Each such coin paid to Alice counts against the limit, and Alice may accept again.
4. ■ Pay: Alice may pay Bob a Carol-coin that Bob accepts as denominated by Dollar, provided Alice knows Bob and Carol has issued coins of the denomination Dollar.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x has issued an f-denominated bond due e = issued_bond_in(x,f,e); the coins and bonds of z and w are of one denomination, for x = one_denomination(x,z,w); the coins and bonds that z holds in escrow from x for y on k are of one denomination = escrow_one_denomination(z,x,y,k); x accepts an f-denominated z-coin in payment = accepts(x,z,f).
x holds an f-denominated z-coin: x holds a z-coin and z has issued coins of the denomination f.
x holds an f-denominated z-bond due e: x holds a z-bond due e and z has issued an f-denominated bond due e.
After x issues n f-denominated x-bonds due e: x holds n x-bonds due e and x has issued an f-denominated bond due e.
After x accepts one more f-denominated z-coin in payment: x accepts an f-denominated z-coin in payment.
After x pays y a z-coin that y accepts as denominated by f: no longer x holds a z-coin, y holds a z-coin and no longer y accepts an f-denominated z-coin in payment.
