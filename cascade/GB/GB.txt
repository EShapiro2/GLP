GB: Grassroots Bonds
GB restricts and extends GC.
A *bond* is a note that is a unit of its issuer's debt, due at its maturity date, which it includes. An *Alice-bond* is a bond issued by Alice. A bond is *mature* when its maturity date is at most the current date.
Alice and Bob are parties.
1. ■ Issue bond: GN:1, for bonds, with maturity later than the current date.
2. ■ Redeem: Alice may redeem a Bob-coin, or a mature Bob-bond, she holds against any coin or bond held by Bob, whom she knows.
Logic pragmas
GB restricts and extends GC.
A *bond* is a note that is a unit of its issuer's debt, due at its maturity date, which it includes. An *Alice-bond* is a bond issued by Alice. A bond is *mature* when its maturity date is at most the current date.
Alice, Bob and Carol are parties, Day and Term maturity dates, Today a date; Carol is possibly Alice or Bob.
1. ■ Issue bond: GN:1, for bonds, only if the current date is Today and Day is more than Today.
2. ■ Redeem: GC:2. Redeem coin against bond: Alice may redeem a Bob-coin against a Carol-bond due Day, provided Alice knows Bob. Redeem bond: Alice may redeem a Bob-bond due Day against a Carol-coin, provided Alice knows Bob, the current date is Today for Bob and Day is at most Today. Redeem bond against bond: Alice may redeem a Bob-bond due Day against a Carol-bond due Term, provided Alice knows Bob, the current date is Today for Bob and Day is at most Today.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x holds a y-bond due e = bond(x,y,e); x holds n y-bonds due e = bond(x,y,e,*n); the current date is e, for x = today(x,e); z holds n w-bonds due e in escrow from x for y on k = escrow_bond(z,w,e,x,y,k,*n).
After x redeems a y-coin against a z-bond due e: no longer x holds a y-coin, y holds a y-coin, no longer y holds a z-bond due e, x holds a z-bond due e and x knows z.
After x redeems a y-bond due e against a z-coin: no longer x holds a y-bond due e, y holds a y-bond due e, no longer y holds a z-coin, x holds a z-coin and x knows z.
After x redeems a y-bond due e against a z-bond due f: no longer x holds a y-bond due e, y holds a y-bond due e, no longer y holds a z-bond due f, x holds a z-bond due f and x knows z.
Kind of note: bond, y-bonds due Day for y-notes.
Interface pragmas
Bonds: the bonds Alice holds.
Issue bond: Alice [Issue bond] <maturity> <amount>
Swap: Alice [Swap coin for bond] <amount given> <issuer wanted> <maturity wanted> <amount wanted> [Swap bonds] <amount given> <issuer wanted> <maturity wanted> <amount wanted>
  Bob: Alice asks to swap coins or bonds with you.
Pay: Alice [Pay bond] <amount>
  Bob: Alice asks to pay bonds to you.
Redeem: Alice [Redeem coin against bond] <issuer wanted> <maturity wanted> [Redeem bond] <issuer wanted> [Redeem bond against bond] <issuer wanted> <maturity wanted>
Escrow: Alice [Deposit bond] <amount> <condition>
  Carol: Alice asks to deposit bonds with you.
  Carol [Release bond] <amount>
  Bob: Carol asks to release bonds to you.
  Carol [Return bond] <amount>
  Alice: Carol asks to return bonds to you.
