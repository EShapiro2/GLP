GB: Grassroots Bonds
GB extends GC.
Vocabulary: x holds a y-bond due e = bond(x,y,e); x holds n y-bonds due e = bond(x,y,e,*n); the current date is e, for x = today(x,e); z holds n w-coins in escrow from x for y on k = escrow_coin(z,w,x,y,k,*n); z holds n w-bonds due e in escrow from x for y on k = escrow_bond(z,w,e,x,y,k,*n).
After x issues n x-bonds due e: x holds n x-bonds due e.
After x and y swap n z-coins for m w-bonds due e: no longer x holds n z-coins, y holds n z-coins, no longer y holds m w-bonds due e, x holds m w-bonds due e, y knows z and x knows w.
After x and y swap n z-bonds due e for m w-bonds due f: no longer x holds n z-bonds due e, y holds n z-bonds due e, no longer y holds m w-bonds due f, x holds m w-bonds due f, y knows z and x knows w.
After x redeems a y-coin against a z-bond due e: no longer x holds a y-coin, y holds a y-coin, no longer y holds a z-bond due e, x holds a z-bond due e and x knows z.
After x redeems a y-bond due e against a z-coin: no longer x holds a y-bond due e, y holds a y-bond due e, no longer y holds a z-coin, x holds a z-coin and x knows z.
After x redeems a y-bond due e against a z-bond due f: no longer x holds a y-bond due e, y holds a y-bond due e, no longer y holds a z-bond due f, x holds a z-bond due f and x knows z.
After x deposits n w-coins with z for y on k: no longer x holds n w-coins and z holds n w-coins in escrow from x for y on k.
After x deposits n w-bonds due e with z for y on k: no longer x holds n w-bonds due e and z holds n w-bonds due e in escrow from x for y on k.
After z and y release n w-coins that x deposited on k: no longer z holds n w-coins in escrow from x for y on k, y holds n w-coins and y knows w.
After z and y release n w-bonds due e that x deposited on k: no longer z holds n w-bonds due e in escrow from x for y on k, y holds n w-bonds due e and y knows w.
After z and x return n w-coins deposited for y on k: no longer z holds n w-coins in escrow from x for y on k, x holds n w-coins and x knows w.
After z and x return n w-bonds due e deposited for y on k: no longer z holds n w-bonds due e in escrow from x for y on k, x holds n w-bonds due e and x knows w.
GB:0 - | A *bond* is a unit of debt, due at its maturity date, that includes its issuer's public key and its maturity date and is signed by their private key. An *Alice-bond* is a bond issued by Alice. A bond is *mature* when its maturity date is at most the current date.
GB:0 - | Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are parties, Day and Term maturity dates, Today a date, Cond a condition Alice, Bob and Carol agree, Sum and Total numbers; in clauses 2 and 3 Carol and Dave are possibly Alice, Bob or one person, and in clause 4 Dave is possibly Alice, Bob or Carol.
GB:1 ■ Issue bond: bond(Alice,Alice,Day,*Sum) <- today(Alice,Today), gt(Day,Today) guarded by Alice.
