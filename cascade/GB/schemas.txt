GB: Grassroots Bonds
GB restricts and extends GC.
Vocabulary: x holds a y-bond due e = bond(x,y,e); x holds n y-bonds due e = bond(x,y,e,*n); the current date is e, for x = today(x,e); z holds n w-bonds due e in escrow from x for y on k = escrow_bond(z,w,e,x,y,k,*n).
After x redeems a y-coin against a z-bond due e: no longer x holds a y-coin, y holds a y-coin, no longer y holds a z-bond due e, x holds a z-bond due e and x knows z.
After x redeems a y-bond due e against a z-coin: no longer x holds a y-bond due e, y holds a y-bond due e, no longer y holds a z-coin, x holds a z-coin and x knows z.
After x redeems a y-bond due e against a z-bond due f: no longer x holds a y-bond due e, y holds a y-bond due e, no longer y holds a z-bond due f, x holds a z-bond due f and x knows z.
GB:0 - | A *bond* is a note that is a unit of its issuer's debt, due at its maturity date, which it includes. An *Alice-bond* is a bond issued by Alice. A bond is *mature* when its maturity date is at most the current date.
GB:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are parties, Day and Term maturity dates, Today a date; Carol is possibly Alice or Bob.
GB:0 - | Kind of note: bond, y-bonds due Day for y-notes.
