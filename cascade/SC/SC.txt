SC: Shareholding Corporation
SC instantiates GCorp with shareholding corporation bylaws and restricts and extends it.
*Share* is a denomination, and a *share* of Orga is an Orga-coin denominated in Share. A *shareholding corporation* is a corporation whose bylaws name two roles, *shareholder*, held by every person who holds a share of Orga, and *director*, held by its first officer; the shareholders decide the issue of shares and the appointment and the removal of directors, by a majority of the shares they hold, and the directors jointly decide the other acts of Orga.
Orga is a shareholding corporation.
1. ■ Issue shares: Those who decide the issue of shares may issue a number of shares of Orga to a person known to Orga; the first shares are issued to its first officer.
2. ■ Dividend. Set dividend: The directors may set a dividend, a number of Dollars a share. Claim: A shareholder may then claim it from Orga, once, in Dollar-denominated coins for the shares they hold.
3. ■ Redeem: GC:4 and GB:3, only if the coin is of a denomination other than Share.

Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Orga a shareholding corporation, Dollar a denomination, Div a dividend, a number of Dollars a share, Sum, Held and Total numbers; Carol is possibly Alice, Bob or Orga.
1. ■ Issue shares: Those who decide the issue of shares of Orga may issue Sum shares of Orga to Bob, provided Orga is an organisation and Orga knows Bob. The first shares are issued to its first officer.
2. ■ Dividend. Set dividend: The directors of Orga may set Div as a dividend of Orga, provided Orga is an organisation. Claim: Bob may claim Total Dollar-denominated Carol-coins from Orga as the dividend Div, provided Bob holds a share of Orga, Bob holds exactly Held shares of Orga, Orga has Div as a dividend, Total is Held times Div, Carol has issued coins of the denomination Dollar and Bob accepts a Dollar-denominated Carol-coin in payment.
3. ■ Redeem: GB:3 Redeem, only if the Bob-coin that Alice redeems is of a denomination other than Share. Redeem coin against bond: GB:3 Redeem coin against bond, only if the Bob-coin that Alice redeems is of a denomination other than Share.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: z has issued shares = issued_shares(z); x holds a share of z = share(x,z); x holds n shares of z = share(x,z,*n); x holds exactly n shares of z = share(x,z,#n); z has v as a dividend = dividend(z,v); z has paid y the dividend v in the denomination f = paid_dividend(z,y,v,f); the y-coin that x redeems is of a denomination other than Share = other_denomination(x,y,Share).
Roles: the directors of z = directors(z); those who decide the issue of shares of z = share_deciders(z).
After g issues n shares of z to y: y holds n shares of z and z has issued shares.
After g sets v as a dividend of z, which requires z does not have v as a dividend: z has v as a dividend.
After y claims n f-denominated w-coins from z as the dividend v, which requires z has not paid y the dividend v in the denomination f: no longer z holds n w-coins, y holds n w-coins and z has paid y the dividend v in the denomination f.
