SC: Shareholding Corporation
SC instantiates GCorp with shareholding corporation bylaws, and restricts and extends it.
Vocabulary: z has issued shares = issued_shares(z); x holds a share of z = share(x,z); x holds n shares of z = share(x,z,*n); x holds exactly n shares of z = share(x,z,#n); z has v as a dividend = dividend(z,v); z has paid y the dividend v in the denomination f = paid_dividend(z,y,v,f); the y-coin that x redeems is of a denomination other than Share = other_denomination(x,y,Share).
Roles: the directors of z = directors(z); those who decide the issue of shares of z = share_deciders(z).
After g issues n shares of z to y: y holds n shares of z and z has issued shares.
After g sets v as a dividend of z, which requires z does not have v as a dividend: z has v as a dividend.
After y claims n f-denominated w-coins from z as the dividend v, which requires z has not paid y the dividend v in the denomination f: no longer z holds n w-coins, y holds n w-coins and z has paid y the dividend v in the denomination f.
SC:0 - | *Share* is a denomination, and a *share* of Orga is an Orga-coin denominated in Share. A *shareholding corporation* is a corporation whose bylaws name two roles, *shareholder*, held by every person who holds a share of Orga, and *director*, held by its first officer; the shareholders decide the issue of shares and the appointment and the removal of directors, by a majority of the shares they hold, and the directors jointly decide the other acts of Orga.
SC:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Orga a shareholding corporation, Dollar a denomination, Div a dividend, a number of Dollars a share, Sum, Held and Total numbers; Carol is possibly Alice, Bob or Orga.
SC:1 ■ Issue shares: share(Bob,Orga,*Sum), issued_shares(Orga) <- organisation(Orga), knows(Orga,Bob) guarded by share_deciders(Orga).
SC:1 ■ | The first shares are issued to its first officer.
SC:2 ■ | Dividend.
SC:2 ■ Set dividend: dividend(Orga,Div) <- organisation(Orga), ~dividend(Orga,Div) guarded by directors(Orga).
SC:2 ■ Claim: ~coin(Orga,Carol,*Total), coin(Bob,Carol,*Total), paid_dividend(Orga,Bob,Div,Dollar) <- share(Bob,Orga), share(Bob,Orga,#Held), dividend(Orga,Div), product(Total,Held,Div), issued_in(Carol,Dollar), accepts(Bob,Carol,Dollar), ~paid_dividend(Orga,Bob,Div,Dollar), coin(Orga,Carol,*Total) guarded by Bob possibly Bob=Carol, Carol=Orga.
SC:3 ■ Redeem: GB:3 Redeem <- other_denomination(Alice,Bob,Share).
SC:3 ■ Redeem coin against bond: GB:3 Redeem coin against bond <- other_denomination(Alice,Bob,Share).
