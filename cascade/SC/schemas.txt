SC: Shareholding Corporation
SC instantiates GCorp with shareholding corporation bylaws; role(x,z,Director) for proposer(x,z,m); role(x,z,Director) for taker(x,z,m); role(x,z,Director) for voter(x,z,m); vote(z,>=Votes,m), sum(More,Votes,1), has_role(z,<More,Director) for decided(z,m), and restricts and extends it.
Vocabulary: z has issued shares = issued_shares(z); x holds a share of z = share(x,z); x holds n shares of z = share(x,z,*n); x holds exactly n shares of z = share(x,z,#n); z has v as a dividend = dividend(z,v); z has paid y the dividend v in the denomination f = paid_dividend(z,y,v,f); the y-coin that x redeems is of a denomination other than Share = other_denomination(x,y,Share); z holds v's vote to set u as a dividend = vote_dividend(z,v,u); z holds v's vote to issue n shares of z to y = vote_issue(z,v,y,n); the votes to issue n shares of z to y carry a majority of the shares of z = share_majority(z,y,n).
Roles: the directors of z = directors(z).
x is a director of z: x holds the role Director in z.
After v votes to issue n shares of z to y, which requires z does not hold v's vote to issue n shares of z to y: z holds v's vote to issue n shares of z to y.
After x issues n shares of z to y: y holds n shares of z, z has issued shares and z holds no one's vote to issue n shares of z to y.
After w votes to set u as a dividend of z, which requires z does not hold w's vote to set u as a dividend: z holds w's vote to set u as a dividend.
After x sets v as a dividend of z, which requires z does not have v as a dividend: z has v as a dividend and z holds no one's vote to set v as a dividend.
After y claims n f-denominated w-coins from z as the dividend v, which requires z has not paid y the dividend v in the denomination f: no longer z holds n w-coins, y holds n w-coins and z has paid y the dividend v in the denomination f.
SC:0 - | *Share* is a denomination, and a *share* of Orga is an Orga-coin denominated in Share. A *shareholding corporation* is a corporation whose bylaws name two roles, *shareholder*, held by every person who holds a share of Orga, and *director*, held by its first officer; the shareholders decide the issue of shares and the appointment and the removal of directors, by a majority of the shares they hold, and the directors jointly decide the other acts of Orga.
SC:0 - | Alice, Bob, Carol and Erin are persons, Orga a shareholding corporation, Dollar a denomination, Div a dividend, a number of Dollars a share, Sum, Held, Total, Votes and More numbers; Carol is possibly Alice, Bob or Orga.
SC:0 - | Environment: the y-coin that x redeems is of a denomination other than Share; the votes to issue n shares of z to y carry a majority of the shares of z.
