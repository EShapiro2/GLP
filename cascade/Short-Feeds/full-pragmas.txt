Of Knowledge: An *identity* is a unique cryptographic key pair. A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of Friendship: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts.
Of Labelled Overlay: A pointer may be labeled by a person, and a pointer labeled by Dave is a *Dave-pointer*. Friends Alice and Bob have a Bob-pointer from Alice to Bob and an Alice-pointer from Bob to Alice. Erin is possibly any of the others. In clause 2 Carol is possibly Dave.
Of Feeds: A *feed* is named by its author. Bob and Carol are possibly one person.
Of Short Feeds: Carol and Dave are possibly one person.
Alice, Bob, Carol, Dave and Erin are persons, Item an item.
Knowledge:1. ○ Establish: Alice may establish an identity. Alice will make herself known by her public key, sign her acts with her private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
Knowledge:2. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
Knowledge:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice is a person, Bob is a person, Carol is a person, Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
Friendship:1, Labelled Overlay:4. ■ Befriend: Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice is a person, Bob is a person and Alice and Bob know each other, and then Alice has a Bob-pointer to Bob and Bob has an Alice-pointer to Alice. Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
Friendship:2, Labelled Overlay:4. ■ Unfriend: Alice may end her friendship with Bob, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person, and then no longer Alice has a Bob-pointer to Bob and no longer Bob has an Alice-pointer to Alice.
Friendship:3. ○ Loss of Trust. Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
Overlay:1. ■ Point friend: Alice may point to Bob, provided Alice and Bob are friends.
Overlay:2. ■ Point transitive: Alice may point to Carol, provided Alice knows Carol, Alice points to Bob and Bob points to Carol.
Overlay:3. ■ Unpoint: Alice may cease pointing to Bob.
Labelled Overlay:1. ■ Labeled point friend: Alice may add a Dave-pointer to Bob, provided Alice and Bob are friends and Bob has a Dave-pointer to Erin.
Labelled Overlay:2, Feeds:3. ■ Labeled point transitive: Alice may add a Dave-pointer to Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice has a Dave-pointer to Bob and Bob has a Dave-pointer to Carol. Labeled point transitive friend: Alice may add a Dave-pointer to Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice and Bob are friends and Bob has a Dave-pointer to Carol.
Labelled Overlay:3. ■ Labeled unpoint: Alice may remove her Dave-pointer to Carol. Labeled unpoint self: Alice may remove her Dave-pointer to Dave, provided Alice does not count Dave as a friend.
Labelled Overlay:4. ■ Friend pointers.
Feeds:1. ■ Post: Alice may post Item to her feed. Alice has a feed, and the item is created or received.
Feeds:2. ■ Deliver: Alice receives from Bob the item Item of Carol's feed, provided Alice has a Carol-pointer to Bob, Bob knows Alice and Bob holds the item Item of Carol's feed.
Short Feeds:1. ■ Shorten: Alice replaces a Dave-pointer to Bob by a Dave-pointer to Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other and Bob has a Dave-pointer to Carol.
