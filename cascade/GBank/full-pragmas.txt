GBank: Grassroots Bank
Of GKK: An *identity* is a unique key pair. A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of GSG: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts. Alice and Bob are persons.
Of GnW: Bob *trusts* Carol, an organisation of Alice, when he trusts Alice. The *decision rule*, a parameter of GnW, says who decides for an organisation and how. An organisation takes an act of a party by those who decide for it. Alice, Bob and Erin are persons, Carol and Dave organisations; in clause 4 Alice and Bob are possibly one person.
Of GO: An *organisation* is a ward with *participants*, which include its *officers*, each with a *role*. The *bylaws*, a parameter of GO, name the roles of an organisation and say who decides each of its acts and how, their own amendment included. Alice and Bob are persons, Gov and Orga organisations, Motion an act of Orga, Role a role. Parameters: x is entitled to propose m in z; x is entitled to vote on m in z; x is entitled to take m for z; the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z.
Of GC: A *coin* is a unit of debt that includes its issuer's public key and is signed by their private key. An *Alice-coin* is a coin issued by Alice. Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are parties, Sum and Total numbers, Carol and Dave possibly Alice, Bob or one person.
Of GB: A *bond* is a unit of debt, due at its maturity date, that includes its issuer's public key and its maturity date and is signed by their private key. An *Alice-bond* is a bond issued by Alice. A bond is *mature* when its maturity date is at most the current date. Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are parties, Day and Term maturity dates, Today a date, Cond a condition Alice, Bob and Carol agree, Sum and Total numbers; in clauses 2 and 3 Carol and Dave are possibly Alice, Bob or one person, and in clause 4 Dave is possibly Alice, Bob or Carol.
Of GDC: *Dollar* names one denomination, and a *Dollar-denominated Alice-coin* is an Alice-coin issued as Alice's debt of one Dollar. Alice and Bob are parties, Dollar a fiat currency, Sum a number.
Of DGC: A *Dollar-denominated Alice-bond* is an Alice-bond issued as Alice's debt of one Dollar, due at its maturity date. Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are parties, Dollar a fiat currency, Day and Due maturity dates, Today a date, Cond a condition Alice, Bob and Carol agree, Sum and Limit numbers; in clause 4 Carol is possibly Alice or Bob. Environment: the coins and bonds of z and w are of one denomination, for x.
Of GBank: A *bank* is an organisation whose business is issuing coins and bonds against the coins and bonds it holds.
