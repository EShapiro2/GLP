GSN: Grassroots Social Network
GSN extends and strengthens GSG.
A *message*, a parameter of GSN, is what a person sends.
Alice and Bob are persons.
1. ■ Message: Alice may send a message to her friend Bob.
2. ■ Group. Create: Alice may create a group, of which she is the leader and first member. Close: Alice may close the group by leaving it.
3. ■ Joining and Leaving. Join: Alice, the leader of a group, may add her friend Bob to it, with the consent of Bob. Remove, Leave: Either Alice or Bob may then end Bob's membership in the group, which also ends when Alice and Bob cease to be friends.
4. ■ Group messaging. Post: A member of a group may send a message to the group. Deliver: Its leader delivers it to every current member.
Logic pragmas
GSN extends and strengthens GSG.
A *message*, a parameter of GSN, is what a person sends.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Grupo a group, Text a message, Bob and Carol possibly Alice or one person.
1. ■ Message: Alice may send Text to Bob, provided Alice and Bob are friends.
2. ■ Group. Create: Alice may create Grupo. Alice is then its leader and first member. Close: Alice may close Grupo by leaving it.
3. ■ Joining and Leaving. Join: Alice may add Bob to Grupo, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo and Alice and Bob are friends. Remove: Alice may end Bob's membership in Grupo, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo. Leave: Bob may end his membership in Alice's Grupo. Unfriend: GSG:2, and then Alice is a member of no group of Bob, Bob is a member of no group of Alice, Alice has Bob as a member of no group and Bob has Alice as a member of no group.
4. ■ Group messaging. Post: Bob may send Text to the group Grupo of Alice, provided Bob is a member of the group Grupo of Alice. Post own: Alice may send Text to the group Grupo of Alice, provided Alice is a member of the group Grupo of Alice. Deliver: Alice delivers Text of Bob in Grupo to Carol, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo, Alice has the post Text of Bob in Grupo and Alice has Carol as a member of the group Grupo. Deliver leader: Alice delivers Text of Bob in Grupo to the leader Alice, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo and Alice has the post Text of Bob in Grupo.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: y has the message t from x = message(y,x,t); x is the leader of g = leader(x,g); x is a member of the group g of y = member(x,g,y); x has y as a member of the group g = listed(x,y,g); y has the post t of x in g = posted(y,t,x,g); z has the message t of x in g delivered by y = group_message(z,x,t,g,y); y has delivered the message t of x in g to z = group_delivered(y,x,t,g,z).
x is a member of g: x is a member of the group g of someone.
After x sends t to y: y has the message t from x.
After x creates g, which requires x is not the leader of g: x is the leader of g, x is a member of the group g of x and x has x as a member of the group g.
After x closes g by leaving it: no longer x is the leader of g, x has no one as a member of the group g and no one is a member of the group g of x.
After x adds y to g, which requires y is not a member of g: y is a member of the group g of x and x has y as a member of the group g.
After x ends y's membership in g: no longer y is a member of the group g of x and no longer x has y as a member of the group g.
After y ends y's membership in x's g: no longer y is a member of the group g of x and no longer x has y as a member of the group g.
After x sends t to the group g of y, which requires y does not have the post t of x in g: y has the post t of x in g.
After y delivers t of x in g to z, which requires y has not delivered the message t of x in g to z: y has delivered the message t of x in g to z and z has the message t of x in g delivered by y.
After y delivers t of x in g to the leader y, which requires y has not delivered the message t of x in g to y: y has delivered the message t of x in g to y and y has the message t of x in g delivered by y.
