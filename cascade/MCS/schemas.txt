MCS: Mutual Credit Society
MCS instantiates GBank with mutual credit society bylaws; participant(x,z) for proposer(x,z,m); participant(x,z) for taker(x,z,m); participant(x,z) for voter(x,z,m); vote(z,>=Votes,m), product(Double,Votes,2), has_participant(z,<Double) for decided(z,m), and restricts and extends it.
Vocabulary: every member of z is then in the red by at most 1000 coins, for x = credit_limit(x,z).
y is a member of z: y is a participant of z.
After y redeems with z an x-coin against a z-coin: no longer y holds an x-coin, z holds an x-coin, no longer z holds a z-coin and y holds a z-coin.
MCS:0 - | A *mutual credit society* is a bank whose bylaws name one role, *member*, held by every participant; the members jointly decide its acts. A member is *in the red* by their coins in circulation less the other persons' coins they hold.
