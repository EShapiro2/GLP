Nonprofit
Nonprofit restricts Corporation.
A *nonprofit* is a corporation that pays none of its coins, notes or bonds to its officers.
Alice and Bob are parties, Orga a nonprofit.
1. ■ Nondistribution. Pay for Alice: Notes:3 Pay for Alice, only if Bob is not an officer of Alice. Pay issuer for Alice: Notes:3 Pay issuer for Alice, only if Bob is not an officer of Alice. Pay bond for Alice: Notes:3 Pay bond for Alice, only if Bob is not an officer of Alice.
