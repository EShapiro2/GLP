AGORA: Grassroots Barter
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.  AGORA extends GB.  An instrument is a coin, a bond or an item.
1. ■ Making.  Alice may make an Alice-item, one of a kind, which they then hold.
2. ■ Market.  Alice may open a market under a name of their own not used before; Alice is its keeper and first member.
3. ■ Joining.  Alice, the keeper of a market, may admit Bob, whom they know, by an act of both; Bob thereby agrees to come to know whoever posts to the market.
4. ■ Posting.  Bob, a member of a market, may post to its wall an item they hold at the time, or something they want, each at most once, and so may Alice, its keeper; the keeper sees every post on their own wall and delivers it to every other member, the poster included, who thereby knows its poster.
5. ■ Withdrawal.  Bob may withdraw a post of theirs; Alice, the keeper, removes it from their own wall and from the wall of every member it was delivered to, and Bob may not post it to that market again.
6. ■ Trading.  GB's swap and escrow apply to items as to coins and bonds; a post is not withdrawn when its item is traded.
Pragmas
Items: the items Alice holds.
Markets: the markets Alice is a member of.
Offers: the wall offers Alice sees.
Wants: the wall wants Alice sees.
Making: Alice [Make] <item>
Market: Alice [Open] <market>
Joining: Alice [Admit] <person>
  Bob: Alice invites you to their market.
Posting: Bob [Offer] <maker> <item> [Want] <description>
  Alice [Offer own] <maker> <item> [Want own] <description>
Withdrawal: Bob [Withdraw offer] <maker> <item> [Withdraw want] <description>
  Alice [Withdraw own offer] <maker> <item> [Withdraw own want] <description>
Trading: Alice [Barter] <maker given> <item given> <maturity given> <amount given> <maker wanted> <item wanted> <maturity wanted> <amount wanted>
  Bob: Alice asks to trade with you.
  Alice [Deposit item] <maker> <item>
  Carol: Alice asks to deposit an item with you.
  Carol [Release item] <maker> <item>
  Bob: Carol asks to release an item to you.
  Carol [Return item] <maker> <item>
  Alice: Carol asks to return an item to you.
