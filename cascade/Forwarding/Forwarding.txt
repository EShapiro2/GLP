Forwarding
Forwarding inherits from Friendship.
The *author* of an item is its first signer.
Alice and Bob are persons.
1. ■ Sign: Alice may create an item by signing it.
2. ■ Forward: Alice may forward an item she holds to her friend Bob, adding her signature to the signatures the item carries.
Logic pragmas
Forwarding extends Friendship.
The *author* of an item is its first signer.
Alice and Bob are persons, Item an item.
1. ■ Sign: Alice may create Item by signing it.
2. ■ Forward: Alice may forward Item to Bob, adding her signature to the signatures Item carries, provided Alice holds the item Item and Alice and Bob are friends.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x holds the item t = item(x,t).
After x creates t by signing it, which requires x does not hold the item t: x holds the item t.
After x forwards t to y, adding x's signature to the signatures t carries: y holds the item t signed by x.
Interface pragmas
Items: the items Alice holds, each with its signatures.
Sign: Alice [Sign] <text>
Forward: Alice [Forward]
