Mutual Credit
Of Knowledge: An *identity* is a unique cryptographic key pair. A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of Friendship: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts.
Of Guardians: Bob *trusts* Orga, an organisation of Alice, when he trusts Alice. The *decision rule*, a parameter of Guardians, says who decides for an organisation and how. An organisation takes an act of a party by those who decide for the organisation. In clause 4 Alice and Bob are possibly one person. Default: any officer of z for those who decide for z.
Of Organisation: An *organisation* is a ward with *participants*, which include its *officers*, each with a *role*. The *bylaws*, a parameter of Organisation, name the roles of an organisation and say who decides each of its acts and how, their own amendment included. Parameters: x is entitled to propose m in z; x is entitled to vote on m in z; x is entitled to take m for z; the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z; x is entitled to amend the bylaws of z as m says; the amendment m is decided according to the bylaws of z. Default: those who decide for z for those who decide the officers of z. Default: those who decide for z for those who admit participants of z. Default: those who decide for z for those who expel participants of z. Default: x is an officer of z for x is entitled to propose m in z. Default: x is an officer of z for x is entitled to vote on m in z. Default: x is an officer of z for x is entitled to take m for z. Default: z holds the votes of at least Votes persons on m, Double is Votes times 2 and z has fewer than Double persons as an officer for the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z. Default: x is entitled to take m for z for x is entitled to amend the bylaws of z as m says. Default: z is an organisation, z has m proposed and the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z for the amendment m is decided according to the bylaws of z.
Of Notes: A *note* includes its issuer's public key, is signed by their private key, and gives its holder a right against its issuer. An *Alice-note* is a note issued by Alice. In clause 2 Carol and Dave are possibly Alice, Bob or one person, in clause 3 Carol is possibly Alice or Bob, and in clause 4 Dave is possibly Alice, Bob or Carol.
Of Coins: A *coin* is a note that is a unit of its issuer's debt. An *Alice-coin* is a coin issued by Alice. Carol is possibly Alice or Bob. Kind of note: coin, y-coins for y-notes; its acts keep their names.
Of Bonds: A *bond* is a note that is a unit of its issuer's debt, due at its maturity date, which it includes. An *Alice-bond* is a bond issued by Alice. A bond is *mature* when its maturity date is at most the current date. Kind of note: bond, y-bonds due Day for y-notes.
Of Denominated Coins: *Dollar* names one denomination, and a *Dollar-denominated Alice-coin* is an Alice-coin issued as Alice's debt of one Dollar. Kind of note: coin, Dollar-denominated y-coins for y-coins.
Of Denominated Currencies: A *Dollar-denominated Alice-bond* is an Alice-bond issued as Alice's debt of one Dollar, due at its maturity date. In clause 4 Carol is possibly Alice or Bob. Kind of note: bond, Dollar-denominated y-bonds due Day for y-bonds due Day.
Of Bank: A *bank* is an organisation whose business is issuing coins and bonds against the coins and bonds it holds.
Of Mutual Credit: A *mutual credit society* is a bank whose bylaws name one role, *member*, held by every participant; the members jointly decide its acts. A member is *in the red* by their coins in circulation less the other persons' coins they hold. Environment: every member of z is then in the red by at most 1000 coins, for x.
