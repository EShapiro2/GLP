Bonds
Bonds inherits from Coins.
A *bond* is a note that is a unit of its issuer's debt, due at its maturity date, which it includes. An *Alice-bond* is a bond issued by Alice. A bond is *mature* when its maturity date is at most the current date.
Alice and Bob are parties.
1. ■ Redeem: Alice may redeem a Bob-coin, or a mature Bob-bond, she holds against any coin or bond held by Bob, whom she knows.
Logic pragmas
Bonds extends Coins.
A *bond* is a note that is a unit of its issuer's debt, due at its maturity date, which it includes. An *Alice-bond* is a bond issued by Alice. A bond is *mature* when its maturity date is at most the current date.
Alice, Bob and Carol are parties, Day and Term maturity dates, Today a date; Carol is possibly Alice or Bob.
1. ■ Redeem: Coins:2. Redeem coin against bond: Alice may redeem a Bob-coin against a Carol-bond due Day, provided Alice knows Bob. Redeem bond: Alice may redeem a Bob-bond due Day against a Carol-coin, provided Alice knows Bob, the current date is Today for Bob and Day is at most Today. Redeem bond against bond: Alice may redeem a Bob-bond due Day against a Carol-bond due Term, provided Alice knows Bob, the current date is Today for Bob and Day is at most Today.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x holds a y-bond due e = note(x,y,bond(e),*1); x holds n y-bonds due e = note(x,y,bond(e),*n); the current date is e, for x = today(x,e); z holds n w-bonds due e in escrow from x for y on k = escrow_note(z,w,bond(e),x,y,k,*n).
After x redeems a y-coin against a z-bond due e: no longer x holds a y-coin, y holds a y-coin, no longer y holds a z-bond due e, x holds a z-bond due e and x knows z.
After x redeems a y-bond due e against a z-coin: no longer x holds a y-bond due e, y holds a y-bond due e, no longer y holds a z-coin, x holds a z-coin and x knows z.
After x redeems a y-bond due e against a z-bond due f: no longer x holds a y-bond due e, y holds a y-bond due e, no longer y holds a z-bond due f, x holds a z-bond due f and x knows z.
Interface pragmas
Swap: Alice [Swap] <amount given> <issuer wanted> <type wanted> <amount wanted>
  Bob: Alice asks to swap coins or bonds with you.
Pay: Alice [Pay] <amount>
  Bob: Alice asks to pay you coins or bonds.
Redeem: Alice [Redeem coin against bond] <issuer wanted> <maturity wanted> [Redeem bond] <issuer wanted> [Redeem bond against bond] <issuer wanted> <maturity wanted>
Escrow: Alice [Deposit] <amount> <condition>
  Carol: Alice asks to deposit coins or bonds with you.
  Carol [Release] <amount>
  Bob: Carol asks to release coins or bonds to you.
  Carol [Return] <amount>
  Alice: Carol asks to return coins or bonds to you.
