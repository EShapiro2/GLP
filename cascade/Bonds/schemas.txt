Bonds
Bonds extends Coins.
Vocabulary: x holds a y-bond due e = note(x,y,bond(e)); x holds n y-bonds due e = note(x,y,bond(e),*n); the current date is e, for x = today(x,e); z holds n w-bonds due e in escrow from x for y on k = escrow_note(z,w,bond(e),x,y,k,*n).
After x redeems a y-coin against a z-bond due e: no longer x holds a y-coin, y holds a y-coin, no longer y holds a z-bond due e, x holds a z-bond due e and x knows z.
After x redeems a y-bond due e against a z-coin: no longer x holds a y-bond due e, y holds a y-bond due e, no longer y holds a z-coin, x holds a z-coin and x knows z.
After x redeems a y-bond due e against a z-bond due f: no longer x holds a y-bond due e, y holds a y-bond due e, no longer y holds a z-bond due f, x holds a z-bond due f and x knows z.
Bonds:0 - | A *bond* is a note that is a unit of its issuer's debt, due at its maturity date, which it includes. An *Alice-bond* is a bond issued by Alice. A bond is *mature* when its maturity date is at most the current date.
Bonds:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are parties, Day and Term maturity dates, Today a date; Carol is possibly Alice or Bob.
Bonds:1 ■ Redeem: Coins:2 <-.
Bonds:1 ■ Redeem coin against bond: ~note(Alice,Bob,coin), note(Bob,Bob,coin), ~note(Bob,Carol,bond(Day)), note(Alice,Carol,bond(Day)), knows(Alice,Carol) <- knows(Alice,Bob), note(Alice,Bob,coin), note(Bob,Carol,bond(Day)) guarded by Alice possibly Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
Bonds:1 ■ Redeem bond: ~note(Alice,Bob,bond(Day)), note(Bob,Bob,bond(Day)), ~note(Bob,Carol,coin), note(Alice,Carol,coin), knows(Alice,Carol) <- knows(Alice,Bob), today(Bob,Today), le(Day,Today), note(Alice,Bob,bond(Day)), note(Bob,Carol,coin) guarded by Alice possibly Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
Bonds:1 ■ Redeem bond against bond: ~note(Alice,Bob,bond(Day)), note(Bob,Bob,bond(Day)), ~note(Bob,Carol,bond(Term)), note(Alice,Carol,bond(Term)), knows(Alice,Carol) <- knows(Alice,Bob), today(Bob,Today), le(Day,Today), note(Alice,Bob,bond(Day)), note(Bob,Carol,bond(Term)) guarded by Alice possibly Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
