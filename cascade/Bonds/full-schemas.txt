Bonds (expanded schemas)
Knowledge:0 - | An *identity* is a unique cryptographic key pair.
Knowledge:0 - | A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity.
Bonds:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Dave a party, Cond a condition Alice, Bob and Carol agree, Sum and Total numbers, Type and Other types, Day and Term maturity dates, Today a date.
Knowledge:0 - | Reflexive: x knows y.
Knowledge:1 ○ Establish: identity(Alice), person(Alice) <- ~identity(Alice) guarded by Alice.
Knowledge:1 ○ | Alice will make herself known by her public key, sign her acts with her private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
Knowledge:2 ■ Acquaint: knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice) <- person(Alice), person(Bob) guarded by Alice, Bob.
Knowledge:3 ■ Introduce: knows(Bob,Carol) <- person(Alice), person(Bob), person(Carol), knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice), knows(Alice,Carol) guarded by Alice, Bob.
Notes:0 - | A *note* includes its issuer's public key and a unique note identifier, is signed by their private key, and gives its holder a right against its issuer. An *Alice-note* is a note issued by Alice.
Notes:0 - | In clause 2 Carol and Dave are possibly Alice, Bob or one person, in clause 3 Carol is possibly Alice or Bob, and in clause 4 Dave is possibly Alice, Bob or Carol.
Notes:1 ■ Issue: note(Alice,Alice,Type,*Sum) <- guarded by Alice.
Notes:2 ■ Swap: ~note(Alice,Carol,Type,*Sum), note(Bob,Carol,Type,*Sum), ~note(Bob,Dave,Other,*Total), note(Alice,Dave,Other,*Total), knows(Bob,Carol), knows(Alice,Dave) <- knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice), note(Alice,Carol,Type,*Sum), note(Bob,Dave,Other,*Total) guarded by Alice, Bob possibly Alice=Carol, Alice=Dave, Bob=Carol, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
Notes:3 ■ Pay: ~note(Alice,Carol,Type,*Sum), note(Bob,Carol,Type,*Sum), knows(Bob,Carol) <- knows(Alice,Bob), note(Alice,Carol,Type,*Sum) guarded by Alice, Bob possibly Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
Notes:3+Coins:1 ■ Pay issuer: ~note(Alice,Bob,Type,*Sum), note(Bob,Bob,Type,*Sum) <- knows(Alice,Bob), note(Alice,Bob,Type,*Sum) guarded by Alice.
Notes:4 ○ | Escrow.
Notes:4 ○ Deposit: ~note(Alice,Dave,Type,*Sum), escrow_note(Carol,Dave,Type,Alice,Bob,Cond,*Sum) <- knows(Alice,Carol), knows(Carol,Alice), knows(Alice,Bob), note(Alice,Dave,Type,*Sum) guarded by Alice, Carol possibly Alice=Dave, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
Notes:4 ○ Release: ~escrow_note(Carol,Dave,Type,Alice,Bob,Cond,*Sum), note(Bob,Dave,Type,*Sum), knows(Bob,Dave) <- knows(Carol,Bob), knows(Bob,Carol), escrow_note(Carol,Dave,Type,Alice,Bob,Cond,*Sum) guarded by Carol, Bob possibly Alice=Dave, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
Notes:4 ○ Return: ~escrow_note(Carol,Dave,Type,Alice,Bob,Cond,*Sum), note(Alice,Dave,Type,*Sum), knows(Alice,Dave) <- knows(Carol,Alice), knows(Alice,Carol), escrow_note(Carol,Dave,Type,Alice,Bob,Cond,*Sum) guarded by Carol, Alice possibly Alice=Dave, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
Notes:4 ○ | Carol will release them only if Cond is met, and return them only if it fails.
Coins:0 - | A *coin* is a note that is a unit of its issuer's debt. An *Alice-coin* is a coin issued by Alice.
Coins:0 - | Carol is possibly Alice or Bob.
Coins:2+Bonds:1 ■ Redeem: ~note(Alice,Bob,coin,*1), note(Bob,Bob,coin,*1), ~note(Bob,Carol,coin,*1), note(Alice,Carol,coin,*1), knows(Alice,Carol) <- knows(Alice,Bob), note(Alice,Bob,coin,*1), note(Bob,Carol,coin,*1) guarded by Alice possibly Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
Coins:3 ○ | Pricing.
Coins:3 ○ | Alice will price her offerings in Alice-coins and accept them in payment at those prices.
Bonds:0 - | A *bond* is a note that is a unit of its issuer's debt, due at its maturity date, which it includes. An *Alice-bond* is a bond issued by Alice. A bond is *mature* when its maturity date is at most the current date.
Bonds:1 ■ Redeem coin against bond: ~note(Alice,Bob,coin,*1), note(Bob,Bob,coin,*1), ~note(Bob,Carol,bond(Day),*1), note(Alice,Carol,bond(Day),*1), knows(Alice,Carol) <- knows(Alice,Bob), note(Alice,Bob,coin,*1), note(Bob,Carol,bond(Day),*1) guarded by Alice possibly Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
Bonds:1 ■ Redeem bond: ~note(Alice,Bob,bond(Day),*1), note(Bob,Bob,bond(Day),*1), ~note(Bob,Carol,coin,*1), note(Alice,Carol,coin,*1), knows(Alice,Carol) <- knows(Alice,Bob), today(Bob,Today), le(Day,Today), note(Alice,Bob,bond(Day),*1), note(Bob,Carol,coin,*1) guarded by Alice possibly Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
Bonds:1 ■ Redeem bond against bond: ~note(Alice,Bob,bond(Day),*1), note(Bob,Bob,bond(Day),*1), ~note(Bob,Carol,bond(Term),*1), note(Alice,Carol,bond(Term),*1), knows(Alice,Carol) <- knows(Alice,Bob), today(Bob,Today), le(Day,Today), note(Alice,Bob,bond(Day),*1), note(Bob,Carol,bond(Term),*1) guarded by Alice possibly Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
