GDC: Grassroots Denominated Coins
Of GKK: An *identity* is a unique key pair. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. A party to a contract must have an identity. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of GC: A *coin* is a unit of debt that includes its issuer's public key and is signed by their private key. An *Alice-coin* is a coin issued by Alice. Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Sum and Total numbers, Carol and Dave possibly Alice, Bob or one person.
Of GDC: *Dollar* names one denomination, and a *Dollar-denominated Alice-coin* is an Alice-coin issued as Alice's debt of one Dollar. Alice and Bob are persons, Dollar a fiat currency, Sum a number.
GKK:1. ○ Establish: Alice may establish an identity. Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
GKK:2. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
GKK:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
GC:1, GDC:1. ■ Issue: Alice may issue Sum Alice-coins, provided Alice has issued coins of the denomination Dollar.
GC:2. ■ Swap: Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Carol-coins for Total Dave-coins, provided Alice and Bob know each other.
GC:3. ■ Pay: Alice may pay Bob Sum Bob-coins, provided Alice knows Bob.
GC:4. ■ Redeem: Alice may redeem a Bob-coin against a Carol-coin, provided Alice knows Bob.
GC:5. ○ Pricing. Alice will price her offerings in Alice-coins and accept them in payment at those prices.
GDC:1. ■ First issue: Alice may issue Sum Dollar-denominated Alice-coins, provided Alice has issued coins of no denomination.
GDC:2. ○ Denomination. A Dollar-denominated Alice-coin is due now; Alice will redeem it on presentation, by a Dollar-denominated coin the bearer accepts or by one Dollar.
GDC:3. ○ Pricing. Alice will accept Dollar-denominated Alice-coins in payment for her offerings at one Dollar each.
