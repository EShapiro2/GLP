GDC: Grassroots Denominated Coins (flat text)
GKK:0 - | An *identity* is a unique key pair.
GKK:0 - | A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity.
GKK:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
GKK:0 - | Reflexive: x knows y.
GKK:1 ○ Establish: Alice may Alice has an identity and Alice is a person, provided not Alice has an identity.
GKK:1 ○ | Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
GKK:2 ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly Alice knows Bob and Bob knows Alice.
GKK:3 ■ Introduce: Alice and Bob may jointly Bob knows Carol, provided Alice knows Bob, Bob knows Alice and Alice knows Carol.
GN:0 - | A *note* includes its issuer's public key, is signed by their private key, and gives its holder a right against its issuer. An *Alice-note* is a note issued by Alice.
GN:0 - | Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are parties, Cond a condition Alice, Bob and Carol agree, Sum and Total numbers; in clause 2 Carol and Dave are possibly Alice, Bob or one person, in clause 3 Carol is possibly Alice or Bob, and in clause 4 Dave is possibly Alice, Bob or Carol.
GN:1+GDC:1 ■ Issue: Alice may Alice holds Sum Alice-coins, provided Alice has issued coins of the denomination Dollar.
GN:2 ■ Swap: Alice and Bob may jointly no longer Alice holds Sum Carol-coins, Bob holds Sum Carol-coins, no longer Bob holds Total Dave-coins, Alice holds Total Dave-coins, Bob knows Carol and Alice knows Dave, provided Alice knows Bob and Bob knows Alice. Possibly one: Alice=Carol, Alice=Dave, Bob=Carol, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
GN:3 ■ Pay: Alice and Bob may jointly no longer Alice holds Sum Carol-coins, Bob holds Sum Carol-coins and Bob knows Carol, provided Alice knows Bob. Possibly one: Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
GN:3+GC:1 ■ Pay issuer: Alice may no longer Alice holds Sum Bob-coins and Bob holds Sum Bob-coins, provided Alice knows Bob.
GN:4 ■ | Escrow.
GN:4 ■ Deposit: Alice and Carol may jointly no longer Alice holds Sum Dave-coins and Carol holds Sum Dave-coins in escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond, provided Alice knows Carol, Carol knows Alice and Alice knows Bob. Possibly one: Alice=Dave, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
GN:4 ■ Release: Carol and Bob may jointly no longer Carol holds Sum Dave-coins in escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond, Bob holds Sum Dave-coins and Bob knows Dave, provided Carol knows Bob and Bob knows Carol. Possibly one: Alice=Dave, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
GN:4 ■ Return: Carol and Alice may jointly no longer Carol holds Sum Dave-coins in escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond, Alice holds Sum Dave-coins and Alice knows Dave, provided Carol knows Alice and Alice knows Carol. Possibly one: Alice=Dave, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
GN:4 ■ | Carol will release them only if Cond is met, and return them only if it fails.
GC:0 - | A *coin* is a note that is a unit of its issuer's debt. An *Alice-coin* is a coin issued by Alice.
GC:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are parties, Sum a number, Carol possibly Alice or Bob.
GC:0 - | Kind of note: coin, y-coins for y-notes; its acts keep their names.
GC:2 ■ Redeem: Alice may no longer Alice holds a Bob-coin, Bob holds a Bob-coin, no longer Bob holds a Carol-coin, Alice holds a Carol-coin and Alice knows Carol, provided Alice knows Bob. Possibly one: Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
GC:3 ○ | Pricing.
GC:3 ○ | Alice will price her offerings in Alice-coins and accept them in payment at those prices.
GDC:0 - | *Dollar* names one denomination, and a *Dollar-denominated Alice-coin* is an Alice-coin issued as Alice's debt of one Dollar.
GDC:0 - | Alice and Bob are parties, Dollar a fiat currency, Sum a number.
GDC:1 ■ First issue: Alice may Alice holds Sum Alice-coins and Alice has issued coins of the denomination Dollar, provided not Alice has issued coins of the denomination anyone.
GDC:2 ○ | Denomination.
GDC:2 ○ | A Dollar-denominated Alice-coin is due now; Alice will redeem it on presentation, by a Dollar-denominated coin the bearer accepts or by one Dollar.
GDC:3 ○ | Pricing.
GDC:3 ○ | Alice will accept Dollar-denominated Alice-coins in payment for her offerings at one Dollar each.
