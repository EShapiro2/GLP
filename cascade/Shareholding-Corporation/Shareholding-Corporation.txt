Shareholding Corporation
Shareholding Corporation unites Corporation and an augmentation of a lift of Shares, and extends and augments the union.
A *shareholding corporation* is a corporation whose bylaws name two roles, *shareholder*, held by every person who holds a share of Orga, and *director*, held by its first officer.
Orga is a shareholding corporation.
1. ■ Dividend. Set dividend: The directors may set a dividend, a number of Dollars a share. Claim: A shareholder may then claim it from Orga, once, in Dollar-denominated coins for the shares they hold.
Logic pragmas
Shareholding Corporation unites Corporation and an augmentation of a lift of Shares, and extends and augments the union.
A *shareholding corporation* is a corporation whose bylaws name two roles, *shareholder*, held by every person who holds a share of Orga, and *director*, held by its first officer.
Alice, Bob, Carol and Erin are persons, Orga a shareholding corporation, Dollar a fiat currency, Div a dividend, a number of Dollars a share, Held, Total, Votes and Double numbers; Carol is possibly Alice, Bob or Orga.
1. ■ Dividend. Set dividend: Alice may set Div as a dividend of Orga, provided Alice is a director of Orga, Orga holds the votes of at least Votes persons to set Div as a dividend, Double is Votes times 2 and Orga has fewer than Double persons in the role Director. Vote on dividend: Erin may vote to set Div as a dividend of Orga, provided Erin is a director of Orga. Claim: Bob may claim Total Dollar-denominated Carol-coins from Orga as the dividend Div, provided Bob holds a share of Orga, Bob holds exactly Held shares of Orga, Orga has Div as a dividend, Total is Held times Div, Carol has issued coins of the denomination Dollar and Bob accepts a Dollar-denominated Carol-coin in payment. Claim own coins: Bob may claim Total Dollar-denominated Bob-coins from Orga as the dividend Div, provided Bob holds a share of Orga, Bob holds exactly Held shares of Orga, Orga has Div as a dividend, Total is Held times Div, Bob has issued coins of the denomination Dollar and Bob accepts a Dollar-denominated Bob-coin in payment. Claim coins of the corporation: Bob may claim Total Dollar-denominated Orga-coins from Orga as the dividend Div, provided Bob holds a share of Orga, Bob holds exactly Held shares of Orga, Orga has Div as a dividend, Total is Held times Div, Orga has issued coins of the denomination Dollar and Bob accepts a Dollar-denominated Orga-coin in payment.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: z has v as a dividend = dividend(z,v); z has paid y the dividend v in the denomination f = paid_dividend(z,y,v,f); z holds v's vote to set u as a dividend = vote_dividend(z,v,u); x holds an f-denominated share of z = note(x,z,share,f); x holds n f-denominated shares of z = note(x,z,share,f,*n); x holds exactly n f-denominated shares of z = note(x,z,share,f,#n).
Vocabulary: z has issued shares = issued_shares(z); the shareholders of z decided the issue of shares by a majority of the shares they hold = share_majority(z); the shareholders of z decided the officers by a majority of the shares they hold = officer_majority(z).
Roles: the directors of z = directors(z); the first officer of z = first_officers(z), listed by z has y as its first officer.
x is a director of z: x holds the role Director in z.
After w votes to set u as a dividend of z, which requires z does not hold w's vote to set u as a dividend: z holds w's vote to set u as a dividend.
After x sets v as a dividend of z, which requires z does not have v as a dividend: z has v as a dividend and z holds no one's vote to set v as a dividend.
After y claims n f-denominated w-coins from z as the dividend v, which requires z has not paid y the dividend v in the denomination f: no longer z holds n f-denominated w-coins, y holds n f-denominated w-coins and z has paid y the dividend v in the denomination f.
Environment: z has issued shares; the shareholders of z decided the issue of shares by a majority of the shares they hold; the shareholders of z decided the officers by a majority of the shares they hold.
