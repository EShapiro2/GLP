GKK:0 - | An *identity* is a unique key pair.
GKK:0 - | A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity.
GKK:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
GKK:0 - | Reflexive: x knows y.
GKK:1 ○ Establish: Alice may Alice has an identity and Alice is a person, provided not Alice has an identity.
GKK:1 ○ | Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
GKK:2 ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly Alice knows Bob and Bob knows Alice.
GKK:3 ■ Introduce: Alice and Bob may jointly Bob knows Carol, provided Alice knows Bob, Bob knows Alice and Alice knows Carol.
GN:0 - | A *note* includes its issuer's public key, is signed by their private key, and gives its holder a right against its issuer. An *Alice-note* is a note issued by Alice.
GN:0 - | Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are parties, Cond a condition Alice, Bob and Carol agree, Sum and Total numbers; in clause 2 Carol and Dave are possibly Alice, Bob or one person, in clause 3 Carol is possibly Alice or Bob, and in clause 4 Dave is possibly Alice, Bob or Carol.
GN:1 ■ Issue: Alice may Alice holds Sum Alice-coins.
GN:1+GB:1 ■ Issue bond: Alice may Alice holds Sum Alice-bonds due Day, provided the current date is Today for Alice and Day is more than Today.
GN:2 ■ Swap: Alice and Bob may jointly no longer Alice holds Sum Carol-coins, Bob holds Sum Carol-coins, no longer Bob holds Total Dave-coins, Alice holds Total Dave-coins, Bob knows Carol and Alice knows Dave, provided Alice knows Bob and Bob knows Alice. Possibly one: Alice=Carol, Alice=Dave, Bob=Carol, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
GN:2 ■ Swap coin for bond: Alice and Bob may jointly no longer Alice holds Sum Carol-coins, Bob holds Sum Carol-coins, no longer Bob holds Total Dave-bonds due Day, Alice holds Total Dave-bonds due Day, Bob knows Carol and Alice knows Dave, provided Alice knows Bob and Bob knows Alice. Possibly one: Alice=Carol, Alice=Dave, Bob=Carol, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
GN:2 ■ Swap bonds: Alice and Bob may jointly no longer Alice holds Sum Carol-bonds due Day, Bob holds Sum Carol-bonds due Day, no longer Bob holds Total Dave-bonds due Term, Alice holds Total Dave-bonds due Term, Bob knows Carol and Alice knows Dave, provided Alice knows Bob and Bob knows Alice. Possibly one: Alice=Carol, Alice=Dave, Bob=Carol, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
GN:3 ■ Pay: Alice and Bob may jointly no longer Alice holds Sum Carol-coins, Bob holds Sum Carol-coins and Bob knows Carol, provided Alice knows Bob. Possibly one: Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
GN:3+GC:1 ■ Pay issuer: Alice may no longer Alice holds Sum Bob-coins and Bob holds Sum Bob-coins, provided Alice knows Bob.
GN:3 ■ Pay bond: Alice and Bob may jointly no longer Alice holds Sum Carol-bonds due Day, Bob holds Sum Carol-bonds due Day and Bob knows Carol, provided Alice knows Bob. Possibly one: Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
GN:4 ■ | Escrow.
GN:4 ■ Deposit: Alice and Carol may jointly no longer Alice holds Sum Dave-coins and Carol holds Sum Dave-coins in escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond, provided Alice knows Carol, Carol knows Alice and Alice knows Bob. Possibly one: Alice=Dave, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
GN:4 ■ Deposit bond: Alice and Carol may jointly no longer Alice holds Sum Dave-bonds due Day and Carol holds Sum Dave-bonds due Day in escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond, provided Alice knows Carol, Carol knows Alice and Alice knows Bob. Possibly one: Alice=Dave, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
GN:4 ■ Release: Carol and Bob may jointly no longer Carol holds Sum Dave-coins in escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond, Bob holds Sum Dave-coins and Bob knows Dave, provided Carol knows Bob and Bob knows Carol. Possibly one: Alice=Dave, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
GN:4 ■ Release bond: Carol and Bob may jointly no longer Carol holds Sum Dave-bonds due Day in escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond, Bob holds Sum Dave-bonds due Day and Bob knows Dave, provided Carol knows Bob and Bob knows Carol. Possibly one: Alice=Dave, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
GN:4 ■ Return: Carol and Alice may jointly no longer Carol holds Sum Dave-coins in escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond, Alice holds Sum Dave-coins and Alice knows Dave, provided Carol knows Alice and Alice knows Carol. Possibly one: Alice=Dave, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
GN:4 ■ Return bond: Carol and Alice may jointly no longer Carol holds Sum Dave-bonds due Day in escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond, Alice holds Sum Dave-bonds due Day and Alice knows Dave, provided Carol knows Alice and Alice knows Carol. Possibly one: Alice=Dave, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
GN:4 ■ | Carol will release them only if Cond is met, and return them only if it fails.
GC:0 - | A *coin* is a note that is a unit of its issuer's debt. An *Alice-coin* is a coin issued by Alice.
GC:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are parties, Sum a number, Carol possibly Alice or Bob.
GC:0 - | Kind of note: coin, y-coins for y-notes; its acts keep their names.
GC:2+GB:2 ■ Redeem: Alice may no longer Alice holds a Bob-coin, Bob holds a Bob-coin, no longer Bob holds a Carol-coin, Alice holds a Carol-coin and Alice knows Carol, provided Alice knows Bob. Possibly one: Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
GC:3 ○ | Pricing.
GC:3 ○ | Alice will price her offerings in Alice-coins and accept them in payment at those prices.
GB:0 - | A *bond* is a note that is a unit of its issuer's debt, due at its maturity date, which it includes. An *Alice-bond* is a bond issued by Alice. A bond is *mature* when its maturity date is at most the current date.
GB:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are parties, Day and Term maturity dates, Today a date; Carol is possibly Alice or Bob.
GB:0 - | Kind of note: bond, y-bonds due Day for y-notes.
GB:2 ■ Redeem coin against bond: Alice may no longer Alice holds a Bob-coin, Bob holds a Bob-coin, no longer Bob holds a Carol-bond due Day, Alice holds a Carol-bond due Day and Alice knows Carol, provided Alice knows Bob. Possibly one: Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
GB:2 ■ Redeem bond: Alice may no longer Alice holds a Bob-bond due Day, Bob holds a Bob-bond due Day, no longer Bob holds a Carol-coin, Alice holds a Carol-coin and Alice knows Carol, provided Alice knows Bob, the current date is Today for Bob and Day is at most Today. Possibly one: Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
GB:2 ■ Redeem bond against bond: Alice may no longer Alice holds a Bob-bond due Day, Bob holds a Bob-bond due Day, no longer Bob holds a Carol-bond due Term, Alice holds a Carol-bond due Term and Alice knows Carol, provided Alice knows Bob, the current date is Today for Bob and Day is at most Today. Possibly one: Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
