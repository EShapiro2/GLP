AGORA: Grassroots Barter (expanded schemas)
GKK:0 - | An *identity* is a unique key pair.
GKK:0 - | A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity.
GKK:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
GKK:0 - | Reflexive: x knows y.
GKK:1 ○ Establish: identity(Alice) <- ~identity(Alice) guarded by Alice.
GKK:1 ○ | Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
GKK:2 ■ Acquaint: knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice) <- guarded by Alice, Bob.
GKK:3 ■ Introduce: knows(Bob,Carol) <- knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice), knows(Alice,Carol) guarded by Alice, Bob.
GC:0 - | A *coin* is a unit of debt that includes its issuer's public key and is signed by their private key. An *Alice-coin* is a coin issued by Alice.
GC:0 - | Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are parties, Sum and Total numbers, Carol and Dave possibly Alice, Bob or one person.
GC:1 ■ Issue: coin(Alice,Alice,*Sum) <- guarded by Alice.
GC:2+GB:2 ■ Swap: ~coin(Alice,Carol,*Sum), coin(Bob,Carol,*Sum), ~coin(Bob,Dave,*Total), coin(Alice,Dave,*Total), knows(Bob,Carol), knows(Alice,Dave) <- knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice), coin(Alice,Carol,*Sum), coin(Bob,Dave,*Total) guarded by Alice, Bob possibly Alice=Carol, Alice=Dave, Bob=Carol, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
GC:3 ■ Pay: ~coin(Alice,Bob,*Sum), coin(Bob,Bob,*Sum) <- knows(Alice,Bob), coin(Alice,Bob,*Sum) guarded by Alice.
GC:4+GB:3 ■ Redeem: ~coin(Alice,Bob), coin(Bob,Bob), ~coin(Bob,Carol), coin(Alice,Carol), knows(Alice,Carol) <- knows(Alice,Bob), coin(Alice,Bob), coin(Bob,Carol) guarded by Alice possibly Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
GC:5 ○ | Pricing.
GC:5 ○ | Alice will price her offerings in Alice-coins and accept them in payment at those prices.
GB:0 - | A *bond* is a unit of debt, due at its maturity date, that includes its issuer's public key and its maturity date and is signed by their private key. An *Alice-bond* is a bond issued by Alice. A bond is *mature* when its maturity date is at most the current date.
GB:0 - | Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are parties, Day and Term maturity dates, Today a date, Cond a condition Alice, Bob and Carol agree, Sum and Total numbers; in clauses 2 and 3 Carol and Dave are possibly Alice, Bob or one person, and in clause 4 Dave is possibly Alice, Bob or Carol.
GB:1 ■ Issue bond: bond(Alice,Alice,Day,*Sum) <- today(Alice,Today), gt(Day,Today) guarded by Alice.
GB:2 ■ Swap coin for bond: ~coin(Alice,Carol,*Sum), coin(Bob,Carol,*Sum), ~bond(Bob,Dave,Day,*Total), bond(Alice,Dave,Day,*Total), knows(Bob,Carol), knows(Alice,Dave) <- knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice), coin(Alice,Carol,*Sum), bond(Bob,Dave,Day,*Total) guarded by Alice, Bob possibly Alice=Carol, Alice=Dave, Bob=Carol, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
GB:2 ■ Swap bonds: ~bond(Alice,Carol,Day,*Sum), bond(Bob,Carol,Day,*Sum), ~bond(Bob,Dave,Term,*Total), bond(Alice,Dave,Term,*Total), knows(Bob,Carol), knows(Alice,Dave) <- knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice), bond(Alice,Carol,Day,*Sum), bond(Bob,Dave,Term,*Total) guarded by Alice, Bob possibly Alice=Carol, Alice=Dave, Bob=Carol, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
GB:3 ■ Redeem coin against bond: ~coin(Alice,Bob), coin(Bob,Bob), ~bond(Bob,Carol,Day), bond(Alice,Carol,Day), knows(Alice,Carol) <- knows(Alice,Bob), coin(Alice,Bob), bond(Bob,Carol,Day) guarded by Alice possibly Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
GB:3 ■ Redeem bond: ~bond(Alice,Bob,Day), bond(Bob,Bob,Day), ~coin(Bob,Carol), coin(Alice,Carol), knows(Alice,Carol) <- knows(Alice,Bob), today(Bob,Today), le(Day,Today), bond(Alice,Bob,Day), coin(Bob,Carol) guarded by Alice possibly Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
GB:3 ■ Redeem bond against bond: ~bond(Alice,Bob,Day), bond(Bob,Bob,Day), ~bond(Bob,Carol,Term), bond(Alice,Carol,Term), knows(Alice,Carol) <- knows(Alice,Bob), today(Bob,Today), le(Day,Today), bond(Alice,Bob,Day), bond(Bob,Carol,Term) guarded by Alice possibly Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
GB:4 ■ | Escrow.
GB:4 ■ Deposit: ~coin(Alice,Dave,*Sum), escrow_coin(Carol,Dave,Alice,Bob,Cond,*Sum) <- knows(Alice,Carol), knows(Carol,Alice), knows(Alice,Bob), coin(Alice,Dave,*Sum) guarded by Alice, Carol possibly Alice=Dave, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
GB:4 ■ Deposit bond: ~bond(Alice,Dave,Day,*Sum), escrow_bond(Carol,Dave,Day,Alice,Bob,Cond,*Sum) <- knows(Alice,Carol), knows(Carol,Alice), knows(Alice,Bob), bond(Alice,Dave,Day,*Sum) guarded by Alice, Carol possibly Alice=Dave, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
GB:4 ■ Release: ~escrow_coin(Carol,Dave,Alice,Bob,Cond,*Sum), coin(Bob,Dave,*Sum), knows(Bob,Dave) <- knows(Carol,Bob), knows(Bob,Carol), escrow_coin(Carol,Dave,Alice,Bob,Cond,*Sum) guarded by Carol, Bob possibly Alice=Dave, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
GB:4 ■ Release bond: ~escrow_bond(Carol,Dave,Day,Alice,Bob,Cond,*Sum), bond(Bob,Dave,Day,*Sum), knows(Bob,Dave) <- knows(Carol,Bob), knows(Bob,Carol), escrow_bond(Carol,Dave,Day,Alice,Bob,Cond,*Sum) guarded by Carol, Bob possibly Alice=Dave, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
GB:4 ■ Return: ~escrow_coin(Carol,Dave,Alice,Bob,Cond,*Sum), coin(Alice,Dave,*Sum), knows(Alice,Dave) <- knows(Carol,Alice), knows(Alice,Carol), escrow_coin(Carol,Dave,Alice,Bob,Cond,*Sum) guarded by Carol, Alice possibly Alice=Dave, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
GB:4 ■ Return bond: ~escrow_bond(Carol,Dave,Day,Alice,Bob,Cond,*Sum), bond(Alice,Dave,Day,*Sum), knows(Alice,Dave) <- knows(Carol,Alice), knows(Alice,Carol), escrow_bond(Carol,Dave,Day,Alice,Bob,Cond,*Sum) guarded by Carol, Alice possibly Alice=Dave, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
AGORA:0 - | An *item* is a one-of-a-kind thing named by its maker.
AGORA:0 - | Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Item and Thing items, Wish something wanted, Mart a market, Day a maturity date, Cond a condition Alice, Bob and Carol agree, Sum a number; in clauses 4 and 5 Carol is possibly Alice, and in clause 6 Dave is possibly Alice or Bob.
AGORA:1 ■ Make: made(Alice,Item), item(Alice,Item) <- ~made(Alice,Item) guarded by Alice.
AGORA:2 ■ Open: keeper(Alice,Mart), member(Alice,Mart,Alice), has_member(Alice,Alice,Mart) <- ~keeper(Alice,Mart) guarded by Alice.
AGORA:3 ■ Join: member(Bob,Mart,Alice), has_member(Alice,Bob,Mart) <- keeper(Alice,Mart), knows(Alice,Bob), ~member(Bob,Mart,_) guarded by Alice, Bob.
AGORA:4 ■ | Posting.
AGORA:4 ■ Offer: posted(Carol,Item,Mart), post(Alice,Carol,Item,Mart) <- member(Carol,Mart,Alice), keeper(Alice,Mart), item(Carol,Item), ~posted(Carol,Item,Mart) guarded by Carol possibly Alice=Carol.
AGORA:4 ■ Offer own: posted(Alice,Item,Mart), post(Alice,Alice,Item,Mart) <- member(Alice,Mart,Alice), keeper(Alice,Mart), item(Alice,Item), ~posted(Alice,Item,Mart) guarded by Alice.
AGORA:4 ■ Want: posted(Carol,Wish,Mart), post(Alice,Carol,Wish,Mart) <- member(Carol,Mart,Alice), keeper(Alice,Mart), ~posted(Carol,Wish,Mart) guarded by Carol possibly Alice=Carol.
AGORA:4 ■ Want own: posted(Alice,Wish,Mart), post(Alice,Alice,Wish,Mart) <- member(Alice,Mart,Alice), keeper(Alice,Mart), ~posted(Alice,Wish,Mart) guarded by Alice.
AGORA:4 ■ Deliver: delivered_post(Alice,Carol,Item,Mart,Bob), post(Bob,Carol,Item,Mart), knows(Bob,Carol) <- keeper(Alice,Mart), post(Alice,Carol,Item,Mart), has_member(Alice,Bob,Mart), ~delivered_post(Alice,Carol,Item,Mart,Bob) possibly Alice=Carol.
AGORA:4 ■ Deliver to poster: delivered_post(Alice,Carol,Item,Mart,Carol), post(Carol,Carol,Item,Mart) <- keeper(Alice,Mart), post(Alice,Carol,Item,Mart), ~keeper(Carol,Mart), ~delivered_post(Alice,Carol,Item,Mart,Carol) possibly Alice=Carol.
AGORA:5 ■ Withdraw: ~post(Alice,Carol,Item,Mart), ~post(_,Carol,Item,Mart), ~delivered_post(Alice,Carol,Item,Mart,_) <- post(Alice,Carol,Item,Mart) guarded by Carol possibly Alice=Carol.
AGORA:5 ■ Withdraw own: ~post(Alice,Alice,Item,Mart), ~post(_,Alice,Item,Mart), ~delivered_post(Alice,Alice,Item,Mart,_) <- post(Alice,Alice,Item,Mart) guarded by Alice.
AGORA:5 ■ | Carol may not post Item to Mart again.
AGORA:6 ■ Swap item for coin: ~item(Alice,Item), item(Bob,Item), ~coin(Bob,Dave,*Sum), coin(Alice,Dave,*Sum) <- knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice), item(Alice,Item), coin(Bob,Dave,*Sum) guarded by Alice, Bob possibly Alice=Dave, Bob=Dave.
AGORA:6 ■ Swap item for bond: ~item(Alice,Item), item(Bob,Item), ~bond(Bob,Dave,Day,*Sum), bond(Alice,Dave,Day,*Sum) <- knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice), item(Alice,Item), bond(Bob,Dave,Day,*Sum) guarded by Alice, Bob possibly Alice=Dave, Bob=Dave.
AGORA:6 ■ Swap items: ~item(Alice,Item), item(Bob,Item), ~item(Bob,Thing), item(Alice,Thing) <- knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice), item(Alice,Item), item(Bob,Thing) guarded by Alice, Bob.
AGORA:6 ■ Deposit item: ~item(Alice,Item), escrow_item(Carol,Item,Alice,Bob,Cond) <- knows(Alice,Carol), knows(Carol,Alice), knows(Alice,Bob), item(Alice,Item) guarded by Alice, Carol.
AGORA:6 ■ Release item: ~escrow_item(Carol,Item,Alice,Bob,Cond), item(Bob,Item) <- knows(Carol,Bob), knows(Bob,Carol), escrow_item(Carol,Item,Alice,Bob,Cond) guarded by Carol, Bob.
AGORA:6 ■ Return item: ~escrow_item(Carol,Item,Alice,Bob,Cond), item(Alice,Item) <- knows(Carol,Alice), knows(Alice,Carol), escrow_item(Carol,Item,Alice,Bob,Cond) guarded by Carol, Alice.
