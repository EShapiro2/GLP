Friendship
Friendship extends Knowledge.
Vocabulary: x counts y as a friend = friend(x,y).
x and y are friends: x counts y as a friend and y counts x as a friend.
After x and y become friends, which requires x and y are not friends: x and y are friends.
x and y are no longer friends: no longer x and y are friends.
After x ends x's friendship with y: x and y are no longer friends.
Friendship:0 - | Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts.
Friendship:0 - | Alice and Bob are persons.
Friendship:1 ○ Befriend: friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice) <- person(Alice), person(Bob), knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice), ~friend(Alice,Bob), ~friend(Bob,Alice) guarded by Alice, Bob.
Friendship:1 ○ | Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
Friendship:2 ■ Unfriend: ~friend(Alice,Bob), ~friend(Bob,Alice) <- person(Alice), person(Bob), friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice) guarded by Alice.
Friendship:3 ○ | Loss of Trust.
Friendship:3 ○ | Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
