GLDAO: Grassroots Liquid DAO
GLDAO extends GDAO.
Vocabulary: y has a delegate in z = delegating(y,z); z holds the delegation of y to x = delegation(z,y,x).
After y makes x y's delegate in z, which requires y does not have a delegate in z: y has a delegate in z and z holds the delegation of y to x.
After y revokes y's delegation to x in z: no longer y has a delegate in z and no longer z holds the delegation of y to x.
GLDAO:0 - | A member's *delegate* is another member whose vote counts for theirs.
GLDAO:0 - | Bob and Carol are persons.
GLDAO:1 ■ | Delegating.
GLDAO:1 ■ Delegate: delegating(Bob,Orga), delegation(Orga,Bob,Carol) <- participant(Bob,Orga), participant(Carol,Orga), knows(Bob,Carol), ~delegating(Bob,Orga) guarded by Bob.
GLDAO:1 ■ Revoke: ~delegating(Bob,Orga), ~delegation(Orga,Bob,Carol) <- delegating(Bob,Orga), delegation(Orga,Bob,Carol) guarded by Bob.
GLDAO:2 ■ | Count.
GLDAO:2 ■ | A member who has not voted on a proposal counts as voting as their delegate did, down a chain of delegates, and can no longer vote on it.
