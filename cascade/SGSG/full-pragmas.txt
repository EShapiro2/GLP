SGSG: Secure Grassroots Social Graph
Of GKK: An *identity* is a unique key pair. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. A party to a contract must have an identity.
Of GSG: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts. Alice and Bob are persons.
Of SGSG: The *identity record* of Alice is her *identity custodians*, persons she names, and her *threshold*, a share of them greater than half. The *records* of Alice are her friends and, for each friend, the friends of that friend. Alice suffers a *state loss* when her machine loses some or all of its state and she keeps her private key, and an *identity loss* when she loses her private key. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Alicia a new identity of Alice.
GKK:1. ○ Establish: Alice may establish an identity. Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
GKK:2. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
GKK:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
GSG:1. ○ Befriend: Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other. Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
GSG:2. ■ Unfriend: Alice may end her friendship with Bob.
GSG:3. ○ Loss of Trust. Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
SGSG:1. ■ Name custodian: Alice may name Carol her identity custodian, provided Alice knows Carol and Alice does not have a fixed identity record. Identity record: Alice may fix her identity record. It then changes only by the replacement of a custodian's lost identity (SGSG:5).
SGSG:2. ■ Friendship updates. Tell: Alice tells Carol of the friendship of Alice and Bob, provided Alice and Carol are friends and Alice and Bob are friends. Tell end: Alice tells Carol of the end of the friendship of Alice and Bob, provided Alice and Carol are friends and Alice and Bob are not friends. Send record: Alice sends Bob the identity custodian Carol, provided Alice and Bob are friends, Alice has a fixed identity record and Alice has Carol as an identity custodian.
SGSG:3. ■ Checkpoint: Alice may send Bob a checkpoint of her records, provided Alice and Bob are friends.
SGSG:4. ■ State recovery. Restore: Bob restores the records of Alice, provided Alice has suffered a state loss and Bob counts Alice as a friend.
SGSG:5. ◆ Identity replacement. Replace: Alicia may ask Carol to vouch that Alicia is Alice, provided Alicia knows Carol and Carol is an identity custodian of Alice. Alicia asks only after an identity loss of Alice. Vouch: Carol may vouch that Alicia is Alice, provided Carol has been asked to vouch that Alicia is Alice and Carol is an identity custodian of Alice. Accept: Bob accepts Alicia in place of Alice, provided Bob counts Alice as a friend and at least the threshold of the identity custodians Bob records for Alice have vouched that Alicia is Alice. Accept custodian: Bob accepts Alicia as an identity custodian in place of Alice, provided Bob has accepted Alicia in place of Alice and Bob has Alice as an identity custodian. Bob tells Alicia the friends of Alice he records, and tells his friends of the change.
