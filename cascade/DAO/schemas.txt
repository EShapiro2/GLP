DAO extends Knowledge.
Vocabulary: x is the steward of d = steward(x,d); x is a member of y's d = member(x,d,y); x has y as a member of d = listed(x,y,d); x has proposed p to y's d = proposed(x,p,d,y); x holds p of d = proposal(x,p,d); x has delivered p of d to y = delivered(x,p,d,y); x has voted on p of d = voted(x,p,d); x holds y's vote for p of d = vote_for(x,y,p,d); x holds y's vote against p of d = vote_against(x,y,p,d); x has delivered y's vote on p of d to z = delivered_vote(x,y,p,d,z); x has decided p of d = decided(x,p,d); x holds p of d as passed = passed(x,p,d); x holds p of d as rejected = rejected(x,p,d); x has delivered the decision on p of d to y = delivered_decision(x,p,d,y).
Vocabulary: x holds d as deciding by consensus = consensus(x,d); p asks x's d to decide by consensus = asks_consensus(x,p,d); p asks x's d to decide by a majority = asks_majority(x,p,d).
After x founds d under a new name of x's, which requires x is not the steward of d: x is the steward of d, x is a member of x's d and x has x as a member of d.
After x admits y to d, which requires y is not a member of x's d: y is a member of x's d and x has y as a member of d.
After y makes the proposal p to x's d, which requires x does not hold p of d: y has proposed p to x's d and x holds p of d.
After x delivers p of d to y, which requires x has not delivered p of d to y: x has delivered p of d to y and y holds p of d.
After y votes for p of x's d, which requires y has not voted on p of d: y has voted on p of d and x holds y's vote for p of d.
After y votes against p of x's d, which requires y has not voted on p of d: y has voted on p of d and x holds y's vote against p of d.
After x delivers y's vote for p of d to z, which requires x has not delivered y's vote on p of d to z: x has delivered y's vote on p of d to z and z holds y's vote for p of d.
After x delivers y's vote against p of d to z, which requires x has not delivered y's vote on p of d to z: x has delivered y's vote on p of d to z and z holds y's vote against p of d.
After x passes p of d, which requires x has not decided p of d: x has decided p of d and x holds p of d as passed.
After x rejects p of d, which requires x has not decided p of d: x has decided p of d and x holds p of d as rejected.
After x delivers to y the decision that p of d is passed, which requires x has not delivered the decision on p of d to y: x has delivered the decision on p of d to y and y holds p of d as passed.
After x delivers to y the decision that p of d is rejected, which requires x has not delivered the decision on p of d to y: x has delivered the decision on p of d to y and y holds p of d as rejected.
After x makes d decide by consensus, which requires x does not hold d as deciding by consensus: x holds d as deciding by consensus.
After x makes d decide by a majority: no longer x holds d as deciding by consensus.
