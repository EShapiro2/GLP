DAO
Of Knowledge: An *identity* is a unique cryptographic key pair. A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of DAO: Carol is possibly Alice or Bob. Environment: p asks x's d to decide by consensus; p asks x's d to decide by a majority.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Delos a DAO, Prop a proposal, Votes and Double numbers.
Knowledge:1. ○ Establish: Alice may establish an identity. Alice will make herself known by her public key, sign her acts with her private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
Knowledge:2. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
Knowledge:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
DAO:1. ■ Found: Alice may found Delos under a new name of hers.
DAO:2. ■ Join: Alice may admit Bob to Delos, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice is the steward of Delos and Alice knows Bob.
DAO:3. ■ Proposing. Propose: Carol may make the proposal Prop to Alice's Delos, provided Carol is a member of Alice's Delos and Alice is the steward of Delos. Propose own: Alice may make the proposal Prop to Alice's Delos, provided Alice is a member of Alice's Delos and Alice is the steward of Delos. Deliver proposal: Alice delivers Prop of Delos to Bob, provided Alice is the steward of Delos, Alice holds Prop of Delos and Alice has Bob as a member of Delos.
DAO:4. ■ Voting. Vote for: Carol may vote for Prop of Alice's Delos, provided Carol is a member of Alice's Delos, Carol holds Prop of Delos, Alice is the steward of Delos and Alice has not decided Prop of Delos. Vote own for: Alice may vote for Prop of Alice's Delos, provided Alice is a member of Alice's Delos, Alice holds Prop of Delos, Alice is the steward of Delos and Alice has not decided Prop of Delos. Vote against: Carol may vote against Prop of Alice's Delos, provided Carol is a member of Alice's Delos, Carol holds Prop of Delos, Alice is the steward of Delos and Alice has not decided Prop of Delos. Vote own against: Alice may vote against Prop of Alice's Delos, provided Alice is a member of Alice's Delos, Alice holds Prop of Delos, Alice is the steward of Delos and Alice has not decided Prop of Delos. Deliver vote for: Alice delivers Carol's vote for Prop of Delos to Bob, provided Alice is the steward of Delos, Alice holds Carol's vote for Prop of Delos and Alice has Bob as a member of Delos. Deliver vote against: Alice delivers Carol's vote against Prop of Delos to Bob, provided Alice is the steward of Delos, Alice holds Carol's vote against Prop of Delos and Alice has Bob as a member of Delos.
DAO:5. ■ Deciding. Pass: Alice may pass Prop of Delos, provided Alice is the steward of Delos, Alice holds Prop of Delos, Alice does not hold Delos as deciding by consensus, Alice holds the votes of at least Votes persons for Prop of Delos, Double is Votes times 2 and Alice has fewer than Double persons as a member of Delos. Reject: Alice may reject Prop of Delos, provided Alice is the steward of Delos, Alice holds Prop of Delos, Alice does not hold Delos as deciding by consensus, Alice holds the votes of at least Votes persons against Prop of Delos, Double is Votes times 2 and Alice has fewer than Double persons as a member of Delos. Deliver pass: Alice delivers to Bob the decision that Prop of Delos is passed, provided Alice is the steward of Delos, Alice holds Prop of Delos as passed and Alice has Bob as a member of Delos. Deliver reject: Alice delivers to Bob the decision that Prop of Delos is rejected, provided Alice is the steward of Delos, Alice holds Prop of Delos as rejected and Alice has Bob as a member of Delos.
DAO:6. ■ Majority. The steward may pass a proposal only if more than half of the DAO's members at that time have voted for it, and reject it only if more than half have voted against it; while the DAO decides by consensus, clause 7 applies instead.
DAO:7. ■ Consensus. Pass by consensus: Alice may pass Prop of Delos, provided Alice is the steward of Delos, Alice holds Prop of Delos, Alice holds Delos as deciding by consensus, Alice holds the votes of at least Votes persons for Prop of Delos and Alice has at most Votes persons as a member of Delos. Reject by consensus: Alice may reject Prop of Delos, provided Alice is the steward of Delos, Alice holds Prop of Delos, Alice holds Delos as deciding by consensus and Alice holds the votes of more than 0 persons against Prop of Delos.
DAO:8. ■ Amending. Decide by consensus: Alice may make Delos decide by consensus, provided Alice is the steward of Delos, Alice holds Prop of Delos as passed and Prop asks Alice's Delos to decide by consensus. Decide by a majority: Alice may make Delos decide by a majority, provided Alice is the steward of Delos, Alice holds Prop of Delos as passed and Prop asks Alice's Delos to decide by a majority.
