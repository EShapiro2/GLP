Community Network
Community Network inherits from Backed Community Bank (SGC, Sovereign Grassroots Currencies: A CBDC Architecture for Credit and Monetary Policy, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27727).
A *community network* is created by a backed community, and uses its community coins. Its bylaws name the role *arbiter*. A *provider* is a member of the community whose *stake*, the community coins they hold, is at least *S*, a number of community coins, and a *customer* is a member of the community who accepts community coins. A *dispute* is over community coins locked between a provider and a customer, on *terms* that set the arbiter's fee.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Orga a community network.
1. ■ Set stake: Those who decide for Orga may set S.
2. ■ Arbitration. Raise: Either Alice or Bob, the parties to a dispute, may raise it to an arbiter, Carol. Arbitrate: Carol may then decide the release of the locked coins to Alice and to Bob, less the fee the terms set, which is released to her.
3. ■ Members. Found: Organisation:1, only if Alice is a member of the community. Join: Organisation:3, only if Bob is a member of the community.
4. ○ Loss of membership. Those who decide for Orga will end Bob's participation in Orga if he ceases to be a member of the community.

Community Network extends Backed Community Bank, and augments it (SGC, Sovereign Grassroots Currencies: A CBDC Architecture for Credit and Monetary Policy, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27727).
A *community network* is created by a backed community, and uses its community coins. Its bylaws name the role *arbiter*. A *provider* is a member of the community whose *stake*, the community coins they hold, is at least *S*, a number of community coins, and a *customer* is a member of the community who accepts community coins. A *dispute* is over community coins locked between a provider and a customer, on *terms* that set the arbiter's fee.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Orga a community network, Gov an organisation, Deal an agreement, S, Held, Sum, Total, Pot, Back, Rest, Part and Fee numbers; in clause 2 Alice is the customer and Bob the provider.
1. ■ Set stake: Those who decide for Orga may set the stake of Orga at S, provided Orga is an organisation.
2. ■ Arbitration. Raise: Alice may raise to Carol her dispute with the provider Bob over Deal in Orga, provided Sum and Total Gov-coins are locked in Orga on Deal between the provider Bob and the customer Alice and Carol is an arbiter of Orga. Provider raise: Bob may raise to Carol his dispute with the customer Alice over Deal in Orga, provided Sum and Total Gov-coins are locked in Orga on Deal between the provider Bob and the customer Alice and Carol is an arbiter of Orga. Arbitrate: Carol may decide the release of Back Gov-coins to Alice and Rest Gov-coins to Bob of the Sum and Total locked between them on Deal in Orga less her fee Fee, provided the terms of Deal set the arbiter's fee at Fee, Pot is Sum plus Total, Part is Back plus Rest and Pot is Part plus Fee. Arbitrate for the customer: Carol may decide the release of Back Gov-coins to the customer Alice of the Sum and Total locked with the provider Bob on Deal in Orga less her fee Fee, provided the terms of Deal set the arbiter's fee at Fee, Pot is Sum plus Total and Pot is Back plus Fee. Arbitrate for the provider: Carol may decide the release of Rest Gov-coins to the provider Bob of the Sum and Total locked with the customer Alice on Deal in Orga less her fee Fee, provided the terms of Deal set the arbiter's fee at Fee, Pot is Sum plus Total and Pot is Rest plus Fee. Arbitrate without fee: Carol may decide the release without fee of Back Gov-coins to Alice and Rest Gov-coins to Bob of the Sum and Total locked between them on Deal in Orga, provided the terms of Deal set the arbiter's fee at Fee, Fee is less than 1, Pot is Sum plus Total and Pot is Back plus Rest. Arbitrate for the customer without fee: Carol may decide the release without fee to the customer Alice of the Sum and Total Gov-coins locked with the provider Bob on Deal in Orga, provided the terms of Deal set the arbiter's fee at Fee and Fee is less than 1. Arbitrate for the provider without fee: Carol may decide the release without fee to the provider Bob of the Sum and Total Gov-coins locked with the customer Alice on Deal in Orga, provided the terms of Deal set the arbiter's fee at Fee and Fee is less than 1. Arbitrate for the fee: Carol may decide the release as her fee of the Sum and Total Gov-coins locked between the provider Bob and the customer Alice on Deal in Orga, provided the terms of Deal set the arbiter's fee at Pot and Pot is Sum plus Total. The coins are released less the fee the terms set, which is released to Carol.
3. ■ Members. Found: Organisation:1 Found, only if Alice is a participant of Gov, and then Orga has Gov as its community. Join: Organisation:3 Join, only if Orga has Gov as its community and Bob is a participant of Gov.
4. ○ Loss of membership. Those who decide for Orga will end Bob's participation in Orga if he ceases to be a member of the community.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: z has the stake at the number n = stake(z,n); z has c as its community = community(z,c); x has n f-denominated c-coins locked in z on k as the customer of y = customer_lock(x,y,z,k,c,f,n); y has n f-denominated c-coins locked in z on k as the provider of x = provider_lock(y,x,z,k,c,f,n); w holds the dispute of x and y over k in z = dispute(w,x,y,z,k); the terms of k set the arbiter's fee at n, for w = arbiter_fee(w,k,n).
n and m c-coins are locked in z on k between the provider y and the customer x: x has n Dollar-denominated c-coins locked in z on k as the customer of y and y has m Dollar-denominated c-coins locked in z on k as the provider of x.
x is an arbiter of z: x holds the role Arbiter in z and z has x in the role Arbiter.
the earlier stake of z has ended: z has the stake at no number.
x is a customer of z: x knows z, z has Gov as its community, x is a participant of Gov and x accepts a Dollar-denominated Gov-coin in payment.
y is a provider of z: y knows z, z has Gov as its community, y is a participant of Gov, z has the stake at the number S, y holds Held Gov-coins and Held is at least S.
After g sets the stake of z at n: the earlier stake of z has ended and z has the stake at the number n.
After x raises to w x's dispute with the provider y over k in z, which requires w does not hold the dispute of x and y over k in z: w holds the dispute of x and y over k in z.
After y raises to w y's dispute with the customer x over k in z, which requires w does not hold the dispute of x and y over k in z: w holds the dispute of x and y over k in z.
After w decides the release of p c-coins to x and q c-coins to y of the n and m locked between them on k in z less w's fee f: no longer w holds the dispute of x and y over k in z, no longer n and m c-coins are locked in z on k between the provider y and the customer x, x holds p c-coins, y holds q c-coins and w holds f c-coins.
After w decides the release of p c-coins to the customer x of the n and m locked with the provider y on k in z less w's fee f: no longer w holds the dispute of x and y over k in z, no longer n and m c-coins are locked in z on k between the provider y and the customer x, x holds p c-coins and w holds f c-coins.
After w decides the release of q c-coins to the provider y of the n and m locked with the customer x on k in z less w's fee f: no longer w holds the dispute of x and y over k in z, no longer n and m c-coins are locked in z on k between the provider y and the customer x, y holds q c-coins and w holds f c-coins.
After w decides the release without fee of p c-coins to x and q c-coins to y of the n and m locked between them on k in z: no longer w holds the dispute of x and y over k in z, no longer n and m c-coins are locked in z on k between the provider y and the customer x, x holds p c-coins and y holds q c-coins.
After w decides the release without fee to the customer x of the n and m c-coins locked with the provider y on k in z: no longer w holds the dispute of x and y over k in z, no longer n and m c-coins are locked in z on k between the provider y and the customer x, x holds n c-coins and x holds m c-coins.
After w decides the release without fee to the provider y of the n and m c-coins locked with the customer x on k in z: no longer w holds the dispute of x and y over k in z, no longer n and m c-coins are locked in z on k between the provider y and the customer x, y holds n c-coins and y holds m c-coins.
After w decides the release as w's fee of the n and m c-coins locked between the provider y and the customer x on k in z: no longer w holds the dispute of x and y over k in z, no longer n and m c-coins are locked in z on k between the provider y and the customer x, w holds n c-coins and w holds m c-coins.
Environment: the terms of k set the arbiter's fee at n, for w.
Parameters: x has n f-denominated c-coins locked in z on k as the customer of y; y has n f-denominated c-coins locked in z on k as the provider of x.
