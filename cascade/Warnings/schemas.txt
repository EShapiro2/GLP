Warnings
Warnings extends Organisation, and weakens it.
Vocabulary: z holds x's warning to y = warning(z,x,y); z holds x's warning to y of k = warning_of(z,x,y,k); z holds y's answer to x's warning of k = answered(z,y,x,k); z holds y's answer to x's warning of k as t = answer_text(z,y,x,k,t).
After x warns y in z of k, which requires z does not hold x's warning to y and z does not hold y's answer to x's warning of k: z holds x's warning to y and z holds x's warning to y of k.
After y answers x's warning in z of k with t, which requires z holds x's warning to y of k and z does not hold y's answer to x's warning of k: z holds y's answer to x's warning of k and z holds y's answer to x's warning of k as t.
After x withdraws x's warning to y in z of k: no longer z holds x's warning to y and no longer z holds x's warning to y of k.
After g annuls x's warning to y in z of k: no longer z holds x's warning to y and no longer z holds x's warning to y of k.
Warnings:0 - | A *warning* is a record in an organisation, made by one of its officers, that a participant broke its bylaws or an undertaking; it names the incident. An officer holds at most one warning to a participant at a time, and the participant may answer it once; a warning withdrawn or annulled may be given again of the same incident only if it was not answered.
Warnings:0 - | Alice and Bob are persons, Orga an organisation, Case an incident, Text an answer.
Warnings:1 ■ Warn: warning(Orga,Alice,Bob), warning_of(Orga,Alice,Bob,Case) <- officer(Alice,Orga), has_participant(Orga,Bob), ~warning(Orga,Alice,Bob), ~answered(Orga,Bob,Alice,Case) guarded by Alice.
Warnings:2 ■ Answer: answered(Orga,Bob,Alice,Case), answer_text(Orga,Bob,Alice,Case,Text) <- warning_of(Orga,Alice,Bob,Case), ~answered(Orga,Bob,Alice,Case) guarded by Bob.

Warnings:3 ■ Withdraw: ~warning(Orga,Alice,Bob), ~warning_of(Orga,Alice,Bob,Case) <- warning(Orga,Alice,Bob), warning_of(Orga,Alice,Bob,Case) guarded by Alice.
Warnings:3 ■ Annul: ~warning(Orga,Alice,Bob), ~warning_of(Orga,Alice,Bob,Case) <- warning(Orga,Alice,Bob), warning_of(Orga,Alice,Bob,Case) guarded by deciders(Orga).
