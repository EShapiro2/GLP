ORH: Ride-Hailing Organisation
ORH extends GNO and restricts and strengthens GRA-SS (GRH, Grassroots Ride-Hailing, in preparation).
Vocabulary: the fare and the deposit of x's call c are in z-coins = in_coins(x,z,c); x holds the fare of x's call c = holds_fare(x,c); y holds the deposit of x's call c = holds_deposit(y,x,c).
x holds the fare of x's call c in z-coins: x holds the fare of x's call c and x holds a z-coin.
y holds the deposit of x's call c on top of y's stake in z: y holds the deposit of x's call c and y is a provider of z.
ORH:0 - | A *driver* is a provider and a *rider* a customer.
ORH:0 - | Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a network organisation.
