ORH: Ride-Hailing Organisation
Of GKK: An *identity* is a unique key pair. A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of GSG: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts. Alice and Bob are persons.
Of GnW: Bob *trusts* Carol, an organisation of Alice, when he trusts Alice. The *decision rule*, a parameter of GnW, says who decides for an organisation and how. An organisation takes an act of a party by those who decide for it. Alice, Bob and Erin are persons, Carol and Dave organisations; in clause 4 Alice and Bob are possibly one person.
Of GO: An *organisation* is a ward with *participants*, which include its *officers*, each with a *role*. The *bylaws*, a parameter of GO, name the roles of an organisation and say who decides each of its acts and how, their own amendment included. Alice and Bob are persons, Gov and Orga organisations, Motion an act of Orga, Role a role. Parameters: x is entitled to propose m in z; x is entitled to vote on m in z; x is entitled to take m for z; the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z.
Of GC: A *coin* is a unit of debt that includes its issuer's public key and is signed by their private key. An *Alice-coin* is a coin issued by Alice. Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are parties, Sum and Total numbers, Carol and Dave possibly Alice, Bob or one person.
Of GB: A *bond* is a unit of debt, due at its maturity date, that includes its issuer's public key and its maturity date and is signed by their private key. An *Alice-bond* is a bond issued by Alice. A bond is *mature* when its maturity date is at most the current date. Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are parties, Day and Term maturity dates, Today a date, Cond a condition Alice, Bob and Carol agree, Sum and Total numbers; in clauses 2 and 3 Carol and Dave are possibly Alice, Bob or one person, and in clause 4 Dave is possibly Alice, Bob or Carol.
Of GDC: *Dollar* names one denomination, and a *Dollar-denominated Alice-coin* is an Alice-coin issued as Alice's debt of one Dollar. Alice and Bob are parties, Dollar a fiat currency, Sum a number.
Of DGC: A *Dollar-denominated Alice-bond* is an Alice-bond issued as Alice's debt of one Dollar, due at its maturity date. Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are parties, Dollar a fiat currency, Day and Due maturity dates, Today a date, Cond a condition Alice, Bob and Carol agree, Sum and Limit numbers; in clause 4 Carol is possibly Alice or Bob. Environment: the coins and bonds of z and w are of one denomination, for x.
Of GBank: A *bank* is an organisation whose business is issuing coins and bonds against the coins and bonds it holds.
Of GCC: A *community currency* is a bank whose coins are Dollar-denominated and whose bylaws name one role, *treasurer*; the treasurers jointly decide its acts. Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a community currency, Sum, Votes and More numbers.
Of GBCC: *f*, a fraction 0 ≤ f < 1, is a parameter of GBCC. Orga's *account* is a bank account in Dollars, held in Orga's name by its treasurers. A treasurer, Alice, is *backed* when the Alice-coins Orga holds are at most the Dollars Alice paid into the account and the coins Alice holds of the other treasurers, leaving out the fraction f of them of whom she holds the most. Alice is a person, Orga a community currency. Environment: every treasurer of z stays backed, for x; x stays backed in z.
Of GNO: A *network organisation* is a backed community currency whose bylaws also name the role *arbiter*. A *provider* is a person whose *stake*, the Orga-coins they hold, is at least *S*, a number of Orga-coins, and a *customer* is a person who accepts Orga-coins. A treasurer is a provider. A *dispute* is over Orga-coins locked between a provider and a customer, on *terms* that set the arbiter's fee. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Orga a network organisation, S and Held numbers; in clause 2 Alice is the customer and Bob the provider. Parameters: x has z-coins locked as the customer of y; y has z-coins locked as the provider of x.
Of GDO: Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
Of GDO-LB: A person has a *location*. d > 0, an integer, and e > 0 are parameters of GDO-LB. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. Environment: the bound is n, for x; either x points to fewer than d persons and y is closest to x among all of x's unpointed friends, or y is closer to x by more than e than some person pointed to by x; y is closer to x by more than e than some person pointed to by x; y is furthest from x among the persons x points to.
Of GRA: A *pickup delay* is measured from the award. The *terms* of a call, a parameter of GRA, state a maximum pickup delay, select a bidder, set the fare and the deposit, and say how these are released. Alice is a rider, Bob a driver and Carol a person, Trip a call. A person can be either a rider or a driver but not both. Environment: x is a rider; x is a driver; the bidding period of x's call c has ended; x is at the pickup point with y; x is at the destination with y; no pickup of x's ride is recorded within the maximum pickup delay. Parameters: the terms of x's call c select y; the terms of x's call c select no bidder.
Of GRA-SS: *Second-score terms* state a maximum pickup delay T ≥ 0, a willingness to pay W(τ) = A − λτ with A, λ ≥ 0, and a deposit D ≥ 0. The award is at time t₀. A bid (p, τ) is the fare p ≥ 0 the bidder asks and the pickup delay τ ≥ 0 the bidder promises. Its *score* is W(τ) − p, and it is *admissible* iff τ ≤ T and its score is at least 0. Alice is a rider, Bob a driver and Carol a person. Environment: y is the bidder on x's call c whose admissible bid has the highest score, ties broken as the terms say; no bid on x's call c is admissible; the terms of x's call c set the deposit D; the terms of x's call c set the fare P = W(τ) − s₂, where τ is the pickup delay of y's bid and s₂ is the highest score among the other admissible bids, or 0 if there is none; the fare and the deposit are released to x if x was at the pickup point from t₀ to t₀ + T, and otherwise the fare to x and the deposit to y; the refund min{P, λδ} is released to x, and the rest of the fare and the deposit to y, with δ = max{0, t_pickup − t₀ − τ}.
Of ORH: A *driver* is a provider and a *rider* a customer. Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a network organisation. Environment: the fare and the deposit of x's call c are in z-coins; x holds the fare of x's call c; y holds the deposit of x's call c.
