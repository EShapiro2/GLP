ORH: Ride-Hailing Organisation
ORH extends GNO and restricts and strengthens GRA-SS (GRH, Grassroots Ride-Hailing, in preparation).
A *driver* is a provider and a *rider* a customer.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a network organisation.
1. ■ Award: GRA-SS:2, only if the fare and the deposit are in Orga-coins, Alice holds the fare, and Bob holds the deposit on top of his stake, and the fare and the deposit are then locked between the provider Bob and the customer Alice (GNO:2).
Logic pragmas
ORH extends GNO and restricts and strengthens GRA-SS (GRH, Grassroots Ride-Hailing, in preparation).
A *driver* is a provider and a *rider* a customer.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a network organisation.
1. ■ Award: GRA-SS:2 Award, only if the fare and the deposit of Alice's call Trip are in Orga-coins, Alice holds the fare of Alice's call Trip in Orga-coins and Bob holds the deposit of Alice's call Trip on top of Bob's stake in Orga, and then Bob has Orga-coins locked as the provider of Alice and Alice has Orga-coins locked as the customer of Bob. Arrive: GRA:5, and then no longer Bob has Orga-coins locked as the provider of Alice and no longer Alice has Orga-coins locked as the customer of Bob. Cancel: GRA:6, and then no longer Bob has Orga-coins locked as the provider of Alice and no longer Alice has Orga-coins locked as the customer of Bob.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: the fare and the deposit of x's call c are in z-coins = in_coins(x,z,c); x holds the fare of x's call c = holds_fare(x,c); y holds the deposit of x's call c = holds_deposit(y,x,c).
x holds the fare of x's call c in z-coins: x holds the fare of x's call c and x holds a z-coin.
y holds the deposit of x's call c on top of y's stake in z: y holds the deposit of x's call c and y is a provider of z.
Environment: the fare and the deposit of x's call c are in z-coins; x holds the fare of x's call c; y holds the deposit of x's call c.
