GAS: Grassroots App Store
GAS composes GPD with GSV (GNA, GLP Networking API and Services, in preparation), restricts the composition, and extends it.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Dave a ward.  A store is a service holding a list of programs.
1. ■ Store.  Carol may make her service Dave a store.
2. ○ Listing.  Carol may list in Dave a program developed by Bob, after adding her signature to its signatures, as long as she trusts Bob as a developer; and may delist it.
3. ■ Store Trust.  Alice may trust Dave, Carol being known to her, and may withdraw her trust.
4. ■ Installation from a Store.  Alice may install a program listed in a store she trusts.
Interface pragmas
Look: 🏪, os
Stores: the stores Alice trusts.
Listings: the programs listed in Carol's store.
Store: Carol [Open store]
  Dave: Carol asks you to be a store.
Listing: Carol [List] [Delist]
  Dave: Carol asks you to list or delist a program.
Store Trust: Alice [Trust store] [Distrust store]
Installation from a Store: Alice [Install from store] <program>
Implementation pragmas
Store: the store's page shows its key, its owner and its listings, each program with its contract, the checker's verdict and the artefacts of its cascade; Dave answers anyone who asks for a listed program.
