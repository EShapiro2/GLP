GAS: Grassroots App Store
Of GKK: An *identity* is a unique key pair. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. A party to a contract must have an identity.
Of GSG: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts. Alice and Bob are persons.
Of GFP: The *author* of an item is its first signer. Alice and Bob are persons, Item an item.
Of GPD: A *program* is an item: compiled code, signed by its compiler, that gives the grassroots contract it is compiled from, its clauses and its speech-act schemas; its *developer* is its author. Alice *trusts* Carol *as a developer* when she has declared it and has not withdrawn the declaration. Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Carol and Dave possibly Alice, Bob or one person, Prog a program.
Of GnW: Bob *trusts* Carol, a ward of Alice, when he trusts Alice. The *decision rule*, a parameter of GnW, says who decides for a ward and how. Alice, Bob and Erin are persons, Carol and Dave wards; in clause 4 Alice and Bob are possibly one person.
Of GSV: A *service* is a ward operated by a machine at an address every person can reach. Alice is a person, Dave a service.
Of GAS: A *store* is a service holding a list of programs. Alice *trusts* a store when she has declared it and has not withdrawn the declaration. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Dave a service, Prog a program.
GKK:1, GnW:3. ■ Establish: Alice may establish an identity, and then Alice is a person. Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
GKK:2, GnW:3. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
GKK:3, GnW:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other, Alice knows Carol, Alice is a person, Bob is a person and Carol is a person.
GSG:1, GnW:3. ■ Befriend: Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other, Alice is a person and Bob is a person. Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
GSG:2, GnW:3. ■ Person unfriend: Alice may end her friendship with Bob, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
GSG:3. ○ Loss of Trust. Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
GFP:1. ■ Sign: Alice may create Item by signing it.
GFP:2. ■ Forward: Alice may forward Item to Bob, adding her signature to the signatures Item carries, provided Alice holds the item Item and Alice and Bob are friends.
GPD:1. ◆ Publish: Carol may publish Prog by signing it. Carol attests that its schemas say what its clauses say, that it is the result of compiling the schemas, and that it is expected to behave correctly according to the contract.
GPD:2. ■ Developer Trust. Trust: Alice may declare trust in Carol as a developer, provided Alice knows Carol. Untrust: Alice may withdraw her declaration of trust in Carol as a developer.
GPD:3. ■ Offer: Carol may offer Prog of Dave to Alice, provided Carol holds Prog of Dave and Carol and Alice are friends. Carol offers it by forwarding it to Alice.
GPD:4. ■ Install own: Alice may install her own Prog, provided Alice holds Prog of Alice. Install: Alice may install Prog of Carol, provided Alice holds Prog of Carol and Alice trusts Carol as a developer.
GPD:5. ■ Invite: Alice may invite Bob to join in Prog of Carol, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob are friends, Alice has Prog of Carol installed and Bob trusts Carol as a developer. Bob then has the program installed, with Alice's signature added, and each is a participant of the program with the other.
GPD:6. ■ Leave: Alice may leave Prog of Carol.
GnW:1. ■ Enter: Alice may create Carol, and Carol must consent, provided Alice is a person. Alice chooses Carol's key pair.
GnW:2. ■ Guardian decisions. Make guardian: The guardians of Carol may make Bob a guardian of Carol, with the consent of Bob, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired and Bob is a person. Resign: Bob may end his guardianship of Carol. End guardian: The guardians of Carol may end Bob's guardianship of Carol. Guardian unfriend: Bob may end his friendship with his ward Carol. Carol may not end a friendship with her guardian.
GnW:3. ■ Ward and person. Ward acquaint: Carol and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Carol is a ward, Carol has Alice as a guardian and Bob is a person. Ward befriend: Carol and Bob may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Bob is a person, Carol and Bob know each other and Alice and Bob are friends. Ward end: Carol may end her friendship with Bob, provided Bob is not a guardian of Carol. Unfriend: Bob may end his friendship with Carol, provided Bob is not a guardian of Carol. Ward unfriend: Alice may end the friendship of Carol and Bob, provided Carol has Alice as a guardian and Bob is not a guardian of Carol.
GnW:4. ■ Wards. Wards acquaint: Carol and Dave may jointly come to know each other, provided Carol is a ward, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Dave is a ward and Dave has Bob as a guardian. Wards befriend: Carol and Dave may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice and Bob, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Dave is a ward, Dave is not retired, Dave has Bob as a guardian, Carol and Dave know each other, Alice knows Dave and Alice and Bob are friends. Wards of one guardian befriend: Carol and Dave may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Dave is a ward, Dave is not retired, Dave has Alice as a guardian and Carol and Dave know each other.
GnW:5. ■ Retire: The guardians of Carol may retire Carol. Carol can then take no act.
GAS:1. ■ Open store: Carol may make Dave a store, provided Carol is a guardian of Dave.
GAS:2. ○ Listing. List: Carol may list in Dave Prog of Bob, provided Carol is a guardian of Dave, Dave is a store, Carol trusts Bob as a developer and Carol holds Prog of Bob. Carol lists it after adding her signature to its signatures. Delist: Carol may delist from Dave Prog of Bob, provided Carol is a guardian of Dave. Carol will delist it if she ceases to trust Bob as a developer.
GAS:3. ■ Store trust. Trust store: Alice may declare trust in Dave, provided Dave is a store, Carol is a guardian of Dave, Alice knows Carol and Alice knows Dave. Untrust store: Alice may withdraw her declaration of trust in Dave.
GAS:4. ■ Install from store: Alice may install from a store Prog of Bob, provided Dave lists Prog of Bob and Alice trusts the store Dave.
