Joint Accounts
Of Knowledge: An *identity* is a unique cryptographic key pair. A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of Notes: A *note* includes its issuer's public key, is signed by their private key, and gives its holder a right against its issuer. An *Alice-note* is a note issued by Alice. In clause 2 Carol and Dave are possibly Alice, Bob or one person, in clause 3 Carol is possibly Alice or Bob, and in clause 4 Dave is possibly Alice, Bob or Carol.
Of Coins: A *coin* is a note that is a unit of its issuer's debt. An *Alice-coin* is a coin issued by Alice. Carol is possibly Alice or Bob. Kind of note: coin, y-coins for y-notes; its acts keep their names.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Dave a party, Cond a condition Alice, Bob and Carol agree, Sum and Total numbers, Acct a joint account.
Knowledge:1. ○ Establish: Alice may establish an identity. Alice will make herself known by her public key, sign her acts with her private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
Knowledge:2. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
Knowledge:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
Notes:1. ■ Issue: Alice may issue Sum Alice-coins.
Notes:2. ■ Swap: Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Carol-coins for Total Dave-coins, provided Alice and Bob know each other.
Notes:3. ■ Pay: Alice may pay Bob Sum Carol-coins, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice knows Bob.
Notes:3, Coins:1. ■ Pay issuer: Alice may pay the issuer Bob Sum Bob-coins, provided Alice knows Bob.
Notes:4. ■ Escrow. Deposit coin: Alice may deposit Sum Dave-coins with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other and Alice knows Bob. Release: Carol and Bob may jointly release Sum Dave-coins that Alice deposited on Cond, provided Carol and Bob know each other. Return: Carol and Alice may jointly return Sum Dave-coins deposited for Bob on Cond, provided Carol and Alice know each other. Carol will release them only if Cond is met, and return them only if it fails.
Coins:2. ■ Redeem: Alice may redeem a Bob-coin against a Carol-coin, provided Alice knows Bob.
Coins:3. ○ Pricing. Alice will price her offerings in Alice-coins and accept them in payment at those prices.
Joint Accounts:1. ■ Open account: Alice and Bob may jointly open the joint account Acct under a new name of Alice's, provided Alice and Bob know each other.
Joint Accounts:2. ■ Deposit: Alice may move Sum Carol-coins into the joint account Acct of Alice and Bob, provided Alice and Bob hold the joint account Acct. Partner deposit: Bob may move Sum Carol-coins into the joint account Acct of Alice and Bob, provided Alice and Bob hold the joint account Acct.
Joint Accounts:3. ■ Withdraw: Alice and Bob may jointly move Sum Carol-coins from the joint account Acct to Alice. Partner withdraw: Alice and Bob may jointly move Sum Carol-coins from the joint account Acct to Bob.
