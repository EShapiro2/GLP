Joint Accounts (flat text)
Knowledge:0 - | An *identity* is a unique cryptographic key pair.
Knowledge:0 - | A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity.
Joint Accounts:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Dave a party, Cond a condition Alice, Bob and Carol agree, Sum and Total numbers, Type and Other types, Acct a joint account.
Knowledge:0 - | Reflexive: x knows y.
Knowledge:1 ○ Establish: Alice may Alice has an identity and Alice is a person, provided not Alice has an identity.
Knowledge:1 ○ | Alice will make herself known by her public key, sign her acts with her private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
Knowledge:2 ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly Alice knows Bob and Bob knows Alice, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
Knowledge:3 ■ Introduce: Alice and Bob may jointly Bob knows Carol, provided Alice is a person, Bob is a person, Carol is a person, Alice knows Bob, Bob knows Alice and Alice knows Carol.
Notes:0 - | A *note* includes its issuer's public key, is signed by their private key, and gives its holder a right against its issuer. An *Alice-note* is a note issued by Alice.
Notes:0 - | In clause 2 Carol and Dave are possibly Alice, Bob or one person, in clause 3 Carol is possibly Alice or Bob, and in clause 4 Dave is possibly Alice, Bob or Carol.
Notes:1 ■ Issue: Alice may Alice holds Sum Alice-notes of type Type.
Notes:2 ■ Swap: Alice and Bob may jointly no longer Alice holds Sum Carol-notes of type Type, Bob holds Sum Carol-notes of type Type, no longer Bob holds Total Dave-notes of type Other, Alice holds Total Dave-notes of type Other, Bob knows Carol and Alice knows Dave, provided Alice knows Bob and Bob knows Alice. Possibly one: Alice=Carol, Alice=Dave, Bob=Carol, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
Notes:3 ■ Pay: Alice and Bob may jointly no longer Alice holds Sum Carol-notes of type Type, Bob holds Sum Carol-notes of type Type and Bob knows Carol, provided Alice knows Bob. Possibly one: Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
Notes:3+Coins:1 ■ Pay issuer: Alice may no longer Alice holds Sum Bob-notes of type Type and Bob holds Sum Bob-notes of type Type, provided Alice knows Bob.
Notes:4 ○ | Escrow.
Notes:4 ○ Deposit: Alice and Carol may jointly no longer Alice holds Sum Dave-notes of type Type and Carol holds Sum Dave-notes of type Type in escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond, provided Alice knows Carol, Carol knows Alice and Alice knows Bob. Possibly one: Alice=Dave, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
Notes:4 ○ Release: Carol and Bob may jointly no longer Carol holds Sum Dave-notes of type Type in escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond, Bob holds Sum Dave-notes of type Type and Bob knows Dave, provided Carol knows Bob and Bob knows Carol. Possibly one: Alice=Dave, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
Notes:4 ○ Return: Carol and Alice may jointly no longer Carol holds Sum Dave-notes of type Type in escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond, Alice holds Sum Dave-notes of type Type and Alice knows Dave, provided Carol knows Alice and Alice knows Carol. Possibly one: Alice=Dave, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
Notes:4 ○ | Carol will release them only if Cond is met, and return them only if it fails.
Coins:0 - | A *coin* is a note that is a unit of its issuer's debt. An *Alice-coin* is a coin issued by Alice.
Coins:0 - | Carol is possibly Alice or Bob.
Coins:2 ■ Redeem: Alice may no longer Alice holds a Bob-coin, Bob holds a Bob-coin, no longer Bob holds a Carol-coin, Alice holds a Carol-coin and Alice knows Carol, provided Alice knows Bob. Possibly one: Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
Coins:3 ○ | Pricing.
Coins:3 ○ | Alice will price her offerings in Alice-coins and accept them in payment at those prices.

Joint Accounts:2 ■ Partner deposit: Bob may no longer Bob holds Sum Carol-coins, Alice keeps Sum Carol-coins in Acct with Bob and Alice knows Carol, provided Alice holds the joint account Acct with Bob and Bob holds the joint account Acct with Alice. Possibly one: Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
