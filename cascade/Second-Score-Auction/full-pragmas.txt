Second-Score Auction
Of Knowledge: An *identity* is a unique cryptographic key pair. A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of Friendship: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts.
Of Auction: A *pickup delay* is measured from the award. The *terms* of a call state a maximum pickup delay T, a willingness to pay A − Lτ and a deposit D. A bid's *score* is A − Lτ − p, for its fare p and its pickup delay τ; it is *admissible* iff τ ≤ T and its score is at least 0. A person is at a place, two numbers X and Y; the simulation moves them. A, L, T, D, Fare, Delay, Cost, W, Score, Best, Second, Price, X, Y, PX. PY. QX. QY, Late, Due, Refund. A person can be either a rider or a driver but not both. Environment: x is a rider; x is a driver.
Of Second-Score Auction: *Second-score terms* state a maximum pickup delay T ≥ 0, a willingness to pay W(τ) = A − λτ with A, λ ≥ 0, and a deposit D ≥ 0. The award is at time t₀. A bid (p, τ) is the fare p ≥ 0 the bidder asks and the pickup delay τ ≥ 0 the bidder promises. Its *score* is W(τ) − p, and it is *admissible* iff τ ≤ T and its score is at least 0.
Alice is a rider, Bob and Carol drivers, Trip a call, End, Today, Start and Pickup dates, Rest a numbers.
Knowledge:1. ○ Establish: Alice may establish an identity. Alice will make herself known by her public key, sign her acts with her private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
Knowledge:2. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
Knowledge:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
Friendship:1. ○ Befriend: Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other. Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
Friendship:2. ■ Unfriend: Alice may end her friendship with Bob.
Friendship:3. ○ Loss of Trust. Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
Overlay:1. ■ Point friend: Alice may point to Bob, provided Alice and Bob are friends.
Overlay:2, Second-Score Auction:1. ■ Point transitive: Alice may point to Carol, provided Alice knows Carol, Alice points to Bob, Bob points to Carol and Carol is a driver.
Overlay:3. ■ Unpoint: Alice may cease pointing to Bob.
Auction:1. ■ Call. Move: Alice may move to X and Y. Call: Alice may call for bids on a ride as Trip until End with the terms A by L by T by D from PX and PY to QX and QY, provided Alice is a rider, Alice does not have a ride, the current date is Today, End is more than Today, A is at least 0, L is at least 0, T is at least 0 and D is at least 0. Send call: Alice's call Trip until End from PX and PY goes to Bob, provided Alice has the call Trip open, Alice knows the end End of Alice's call Trip, Alice knows the pickup PX and PY of Alice's call Trip and Alice points to Bob. Forward call: Bob forwards Alice's call Trip until End from PX and PY to Carol, provided Bob has received Alice's call Trip from Alice, Bob knows the end End of Alice's call Trip, Bob knows the pickup PX and PY of Alice's call Trip and Bob points to Carol.
Auction:2. ■ Bid: Bob may bid Fare with delay Delay on Alice's call Trip, provided Bob is a driver, Bob has received Alice's call Trip, Bob knows the end End of Alice's call Trip, the current date is Today, End is more than Today, Fare is at least 0 and Delay is at least 0.
Auction:3. ■ Award. Rank ahead even: Alice may rank Bob's bid of Fare with delay Delay on her call Trip ahead of Carol with Score over Best with Price, provided Alice is not re-ranking Alice's call Trip, Alice knows the end End of Alice's call Trip, the current date is Today, End is at most Today, Alice's call Trip has the terms A by L by T by D, Cost is L times Delay, A is W plus Cost, Delay is at most T, W is Score plus Fare, Score is at least 0, Score is more than Best and the second score of Alice's call Trip is Best.
