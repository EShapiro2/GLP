Shares
Shares restricts GO and Notes.
A *share* of Orga is an Orga-note that gives its holder a vote in Orga and a part of its dividends.
Orga is an organisation.
1. ■ Issue shares: Notes:1, for shares, with the consent of those who decide for Orga.
Logic pragmas
Shares restricts GO and Notes.
A *share* of Orga is an Orga-note that gives its holder a vote in Orga and a part of its dividends.
Alice is a party, Orga an organisation.
1. ■ Issue shares: Notes:1, for shares, only if Alice is an organisation, with the consent of those who decide the issue of shares of Alice.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x holds a share of z = share(x,z); x holds n shares of z = share(x,z,*n); x holds exactly n shares of z = share(x,z,#n); z holds n shares of w in escrow from x for y on k = escrow_share(z,w,x,y,k,*n).
Roles: those who decide the issue of shares of z = share_deciders(z).
Default: those who decide for z for those who decide the issue of shares of z.
Kind of note: share, shares of y for y-notes.
