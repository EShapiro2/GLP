Of GKK: An *identity* is a unique key pair. A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of GSG: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts. Alice and Bob are persons.
Of GnW: Bob *trusts* Carol, an organisation of Alice, when he trusts Alice. The *decision rule*, a parameter of GnW, says who decides for an organisation and how. An organisation takes an act of a party by those who decide for it. Alice and Bob are persons, Carol and Dave organisations; in clause 4 Alice and Bob are possibly one person. Default: any officer of z for those who decide for z.
Of GO: An *organisation* is a ward with *participants*, which include its *officers*, each with a *role*. The *bylaws*, a parameter of GO, name the roles of an organisation and say who decides each of its acts and how, their own amendment included. Alice and Bob are persons, Gov and Orga organisations, Motion an act of Orga, Role a role, Votes and Double numbers. Parameters: x is entitled to propose m in z; x is entitled to vote on m in z; x is entitled to take m for z; the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z. Default: those who decide for z for those who decide the officers of z. Default: x is an officer of z for x is entitled to propose m in z. Default: x is an officer of z for x is entitled to vote on m in z. Default: x is an officer of z for x is entitled to take m for z. Default: z holds the votes of at least Votes persons on m, Double is Votes times 2 and z has fewer than Double persons as an officer for the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z.
Of Notes: A *note* includes its issuer's public key, is signed by their private key, and gives its holder a right against its issuer. An *Alice-note* is a note issued by Alice. Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are parties, Cond a condition Alice, Bob and Carol agree, Sum and Total numbers; in clause 2 Carol and Dave are possibly Alice, Bob or one person, in clause 3 Carol is possibly Alice or Bob, and in clause 4 Dave is possibly Alice, Bob or Carol.
Of Shares: A *share* of Orga is an Orga-note that gives its holder a vote in Orga and a part of its dividends. Alice is a party, Orga an organisation. Default: those who decide for z for those who decide the issue of shares of z. Kind of note: share, shares of y for y-notes.
