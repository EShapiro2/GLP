CnC: Consumers' Cooperative
CnC instantiates GCoop and TC with bylaws that delegate no act and restricts and extends them.
A *consumers' cooperative* is a cooperative that is a trading company. Its *fee* is a number of Dollars the members set, and the *cost* of a good is the coins Orga paid for it.
Bob and Erin are persons, Orga a consumers' cooperative.
1. ■ Fee. Set fee: The members may set the fee. Pay fee: Bob may pay Orga the fee. Join: GO:3, only if Bob has paid Orga the fee.
2. ■ Sell: TC:3, only if Erin is a member and pays the cost of the good.
Logic pragmas
CnC instantiates GCoop and TC with bylaws that delegate no act; x is a member of z for x is entitled to propose m in z; x is a member of z for x is entitled to take m for z; x is a member of z for x is entitled to vote on m in z; z holds the votes of at least Votes persons on m, Double is Votes times 2 and z has fewer than Double persons as a participant for the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z, and restricts and extends them.
A *consumers' cooperative* is a cooperative that is a trading company. Its *fee* is a number of Dollars the members set, and the *cost* of a good is the coins Orga paid for it.
Bob, Dave and Erin are persons, Orga a consumers' cooperative, Ware a good, Dollar a fiat currency, Fee, Sum, Votes and Double numbers.
1. ■ Fee. Set fee: Those who decide for Orga may set the fee of Orga at Fee, provided Orga is an organisation. Pay fee: Bob may pay Orga the fee in Fee Dave-coins, provided Orga has the fee at the number Fee, Orga accepts a Dollar-denominated Dave-coin in payment and Bob knows Orga. Join: GO:3 Join, only if Bob has paid Orga the fee.
2. ■ Sell: TC:3, only if Erin is a member of Orga and Orga has paid exactly Sum coins for Ware.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: y has paid z the fee = paid_fee(z,y); z has the fee at the number n = fee(z,n).
the earlier fee of z has ended: z has the fee at no number.
After g sets the fee of z at n: the earlier fee of z has ended and z has the fee at the number n.
After y pays z the fee in n w-coins, which requires y has not paid z the fee: no longer y holds n w-coins, z holds n w-coins and y has paid z the fee.
