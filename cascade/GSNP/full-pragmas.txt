GSNP: Grassroots Social Network with Provenance
Of GKK: An *identity* is a unique key pair. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. A party to a contract must have an identity. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of GSG: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts. Alice and Bob are persons.
Of GSN: A *message*, a parameter of GSN, is what a person sends. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Grupo a group, Text a message, Bob and Carol possibly Alice or one person.
Of GnW: Bob *trusts* Carol, an AI agent of Alice, when he trusts Alice. The *decision rule*, a parameter of GnW, says who decides for an AI agent and how. Alice, Bob and Erin are persons, Carol and Dave AI agents; in clause 4 Alice and Bob are possibly one person.
Of GFP: The *author* of an item is its first signer. Alice and Bob are persons, Item an item.
Of GAI: The *developer* of an AI agent is the signer of its code. Alice and Bob are persons, Alf an AI agent, Proof and Seal attestations, Item an item; in clause 2 Carol and Dave are AI agents.
Of GSNP: Alf is an AI agent, Alice and Bob are persons, Grupo a group, Text a message, Item an item, Proof an attestation.
GKK:1, GnW:3. ■ Establish: Alice may establish an identity, and then Alice is a person. Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
GKK:2, GnW:3. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
GKK:3, GnW:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other, Alice knows Carol, Alice is a person, Bob is a person and Carol is a person.
GSG:1, GnW:3. ■ Befriend: Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other, Alice is a person and Bob is a person. Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
GSG:2, GSN:3, GnW:3. ■ Person unfriend: Alice may end Bob's membership in _, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person, and then no longer Alice counts Bob as a friend, no longer Bob counts Alice as a friend, Alice is a member of no group of Bob and Bob has Alice as a member of no group.
GSG:3. ○ Loss of Trust. Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
GSN:1, GSNP:1. ■ Message: Alice may send Text to Bob, provided Alice and Bob are friends, Alice is a person, Alice holds the item Text and Alice is the author of Text.
GSN:2. ■ Group.
GSN:2, GSNP:1. ■ Create: Alice may create Grupo, provided Alice is a person. Alice is then its leader and first member. Close: Alice may close Grupo by leaving it, provided Alice is a person.
GSN:3. ■ Joining and Leaving.
GSN:3, GSNP:1. ■ Join: Alice may add Bob to Grupo, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo, Alice and Bob are friends, Alice is a person and Bob is a person. Remove: Alice may end Bob's membership in Grupo, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo and Alice is a person. Leave: Bob may end his membership in Alice's Grupo, provided Bob is a person.
GSN:4. ■ Group messaging.
GSN:4, GSNP:1. ■ Post: Bob may send Text to the group Grupo of Alice, provided Bob is a member of the group Grupo of Alice, Bob is a person, Bob holds the item Text and Bob is the author of Text. Deliver: Alice delivers Text of Bob in Grupo to Carol, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo, Alice has the post Text of Bob in Grupo, Alice has Carol as a member of the group Grupo and Alice is not the author of Text. Deliver leader: Alice delivers Text of Bob in Grupo to Alice, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo, Alice has the post Text of Bob in Grupo and Alice is not the author of Text.
GnW:1, GAI:2. ■ Enter: Alice may create Carol, and Carol must consent, provided Alice is a person, Alice holds the item Proof and Alice is the author of Proof, and then Carol holds the attestation Proof and Alice holds a record of Carol's attestation Proof. Alice chooses Carol's key pair.
GnW:2. ■ Guardian decisions.
GnW:2, GAI:2. ■ Make guardian: The guardians of Carol may make Bob a guardian of Carol, with the consent of Bob, provided Carol is an AI agent, Carol is not retired, Bob is a person, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Bob counts Alice as a friend and Carol holds the attestation Proof, and then Bob holds a record of Carol's attestation Proof.
GnW:2. ■ Resign: Bob may end his guardianship of Carol. End guardian: The guardians of Carol, other than Bob, may end Bob's guardianship of Carol. Guardian unfriend: Bob may end his friendship with his AI agent Carol. Carol may not end a friendship with her guardian.
GnW:3. ■ Ward and person.
GnW:3, GAI:2. ■ AI agent acquaint: Carol and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Carol is an AI agent, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Bob is a person and Carol holds the attestation Proof, and then Bob holds a record of Carol's attestation Proof. AI agent befriend: Carol and Bob may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Carol is an AI agent, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Bob is a person, Carol and Bob know each other, Alice and Bob are friends and Carol holds the attestation Proof. AI agent end: Carol may end her friendship with Bob, provided Bob is not a guardian of Carol and Carol holds the attestation Proof.
GnW:3. ■ Unfriend: Bob may end his friendship with Carol, provided Bob is not a guardian of Carol. AI agent unfriend: Alice may end the friendship of Carol and Bob, provided Carol has Alice as a guardian and Bob is not a guardian of Carol.
GnW:4. ■ Wards.
GnW:4, GAI:2. ■ AI agents acquaint: Carol and Dave may jointly come to know each other, provided Carol is an AI agent, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Dave is an AI agent, Dave has Bob as a guardian, Carol holds the attestation Proof and Dave holds the attestation Seal, and then Carol holds a record of Dave's attestation Seal and Dave holds a record of Carol's attestation Proof. AI agents befriend: Carol and Dave may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice and Bob, provided Carol is an AI agent, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Dave is an AI agent, Dave is not retired, Dave has Bob as a guardian, Carol and Dave know each other, Alice knows Dave, Alice and Bob are friends, Carol holds the attestation Proof and Dave holds the attestation Seal. AI agents of one guardian befriend: Carol and Dave may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Carol is an AI agent, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Dave is an AI agent, Dave is not retired, Dave has Alice as a guardian, Carol and Dave know each other, Carol holds the attestation Proof and Dave holds the attestation Seal.
GnW:5. ■ Retire: The guardians of Carol may retire Carol. Carol can then take no act.
