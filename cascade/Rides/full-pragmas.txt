Rides
Of Knowledge: An *identity* is a unique cryptographic key pair. A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of Friendship: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts.
Of Guardians: Bob *trusts* Orga, an organisation of Alice, when he trusts Alice. The *decision rule*, which the creator of an organisation decides, says who decides for an organisation and how. An organisation takes an act of a party by those who decide for the organisation. In clause 4 Alice and Bob are possibly one person.
Of Organisation: An *organisation* is a ward with *participants*, which include its *officers*, each with a *role*. The *bylaws*, which its founder decides, name the roles of an organisation and say who decides each of its acts and how, their own amendment included. Environment: the bylaws of z entitle x to propose motions; the bylaws of z entitle x to vote on m; the bylaws of z entitle x to take m; the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z; the bylaws of z entitle x to amend them as m says; the amendment m is decided according to the bylaws of z.
Of Notes: A *note* includes its issuer's public key and a unique note identifier, is signed by their private key, and gives its holder a right against its issuer. An *Alice-note* is a note issued by Alice. In clause 2 Carol and Dave are possibly Alice, Bob or one person, in clause 3 Carol is possibly Alice or Bob, and in clause 4 Dave is possibly Alice, Bob or Carol.
Of Coins: A *coin* is a note that is a unit of its issuer's debt. An *Alice-coin* is a coin issued by Alice. Carol is possibly Alice or Bob.
Of Bonds: A *bond* is a note that is a unit of its issuer's debt, due at its maturity date, which it includes. An *Alice-bond* is a bond issued by Alice. A bond is *mature* when its maturity date is at most the current date.
Of Denominated Coins: *Dollar* names one denomination, and a *Dollar-denominated Alice-coin* is an Alice-coin issued as Alice's debt of one Dollar. Kind of note: note, Dollar-denominated y-notes of type t for y-notes of type t.
Of Denominated Currencies: A *Dollar-denominated Alice-bond* is an Alice-bond issued as Alice's debt of one Dollar, due at its maturity date. In clause 4 Carol is possibly Alice or Bob.
Of Bank: A *bank* is an organisation whose business is issuing coins and bonds against the coins and bonds it holds.
Of Community Bank: A *community* is a bank whose coins are Dollar-denominated. The coins it issues are *community coins*. Its bylaws name two roles, *member*, held by every participant, and *treasurer*.
Of Backed Community Bank: *f* is a fraction, 0 ≤ f < 1. Orga's *account* is a bank account in Dollars, held in Orga's name by its treasurers. A treasurer, Alice, is *backed* when the Alice-coins Orga holds are at most the Dollars Alice paid into the account and the coins Alice holds of the other treasurers, leaving out the fraction f of them of whom she holds the most. Environment: every treasurer of z stays backed, for x; x stays backed in z.
Of Community Network: A *community network* is created by a backed community, and uses its community coins. Its bylaws name the role *arbiter*. A *provider* is a member of the community whose *stake*, the community coins they hold, is at least *S*, a number of community coins, and a *customer* is a member of the community who accepts community coins. A *dispute* is over community coins locked between a provider and a customer, on *terms* that set the arbiter's fee. In clause 2 Alice is the customer and Bob the provider. Environment: the terms of k set the arbiter's fee at n, for w. Parameters: x has n f-denominated c-coins locked in z on k as the customer of y; y has n f-denominated c-coins locked in z on k as the provider of x.
Of Local Overlay: A person has a *location*. d > 0, an integer, and e > 0 are numbers. Environment: the bound is n, for x; either x points to fewer than d persons and y is closest to x among all of x's unpointed friends, or y is closer to x by more than e than some person pointed to by x; y is closer to x by more than e than some person pointed to by x; y is furthest from x among the persons x points to.
Of Auction: A *pickup delay* is measured from the award. The *terms* of a call state a maximum pickup delay, select a bidder, set the fare and the deposit, and say how these are released. A person can be either a rider or a driver but not both. Environment: x is a rider; x is a driver; the bidding period of x's call c has ended; x is at the pickup point with y; x is at the destination with y; no pickup of x's ride is recorded within the maximum pickup delay; the terms of x's call c select y; the terms of x's call c select no bidder.
Of Second-Score Auction: *Second-score terms* state a maximum pickup delay T ≥ 0, a willingness to pay W(τ) = A − λτ with A, λ ≥ 0, and a deposit D ≥ 0. The award is at time t₀. A bid (p, τ) is the fare p ≥ 0 the bidder asks and the pickup delay τ ≥ 0 the bidder promises. Its *score* is W(τ) − p, and it is *admissible* iff τ ≤ T and its score is at least 0. Environment: y is the bidder on x's call c whose admissible bid has the highest score, ties broken as the terms say; no bid on x's call c is admissible; the terms of x's call c set the deposit D; the terms of x's call c set the fare P = W(τ) − s₂, where τ is the pickup delay of y's bid and s₂ is the highest score among the other admissible bids, or 0 if there is none; the fare and the deposit are released to x if x was at the pickup point from t₀ to t₀ + T, and otherwise the fare to x and the deposit to y; the refund min{P, λδ} is released to x, and the rest of the fare and the deposit to y, with δ = max{0, t_pickup − t₀ − τ}.
Of Rides: A *driver* is a provider and a *rider* a customer. Environment: the fare and the deposit of x's call c are n and m z-coins; the terms of c release p to x and q to y on arrival; the terms of c release p to x and q to y on cancellation.
