Bank:0 - | A *bank* is an organisation whose business is issuing coins and bonds against the coins and bonds it holds.
Central Bank:0 - | A *central bank* is a bank created by an organisation, Gov (Organisation:1), as the *sovereign* of a denomination, Dollar: its Dollar-denominated coins and bonds are the sovereign coins and bonds of the Dollar. Its bylaws name two roles, *governor*, held by its first officer, and *committee member*; the governors jointly decide the acts of the bank, but the officers of Gov decide the appointment and the removal of its officers, and the committee members, the monetary policy committee, set by a majority the *policy rate* for each term, the return over that term at which the bank lends its coins against bonds. A participant who holds no role is a *counterparty*.
