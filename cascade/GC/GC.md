# The Grassroots Currencies Social Contract

A *party* is a person, natural or legal (with the gender-neutral pronoun 'they'), with *Alice*, *Bob* and *Carol* as variables naming distinct parties to the contract.  A *grassroots coin* is a unit of debt that names its issuer, with *Alice-coin* referring to a grassroots coin issued by Alice.

1. *Minting.*  Alice may decide to issue Alice-coins.
2. *Swap.*  Alice and Bob may jointly decide to swap grassroots coins they hold.
3. *Payment.*  Alice may decide to pay Bob-coins they hold to Bob.
4. *Redemption.*  Alice may decide to redeem a Bob-coin they hold against any grassroots coin held by Bob.
5. *Pricing.*  Alice prices their offerings in Alice-coins and accepts them in payment at those prices.
