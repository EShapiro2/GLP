GC: Grassroots Coins
GC extends GKK.
Vocabulary: x holds a y-coin = coin(x,y); x holds n y-coins = coin(x,y,*n); x holds exactly n y-coins = coin(x,y,#n).
After x issues n x-coins: x holds n x-coins.
After x and y swap n z-coins for m w-coins: no longer x holds n z-coins, y holds n z-coins, no longer y holds m w-coins, x holds m w-coins, y knows z and x knows w.
After x pays y n y-coins: no longer x holds n y-coins and y holds n y-coins.
After x redeems a y-coin against a z-coin: no longer x holds a y-coin, y holds a y-coin, no longer y holds a z-coin, x holds a z-coin and x knows z.
GC:0 - | A *coin* is a unit of debt that includes its issuer's public key and is signed by their private key. An *Alice-coin* is a coin issued by Alice.
GC:0 - | Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are parties, Sum and Total numbers, Carol and Dave possibly Alice, Bob or one person.
GC:1 ■ Issue: coin(Alice,Alice,*Sum) <- guarded by Alice.
GC:2 ■ Swap: ~coin(Alice,Carol,*Sum), coin(Bob,Carol,*Sum), ~coin(Bob,Dave,*Total), coin(Alice,Dave,*Total), knows(Bob,Carol), knows(Alice,Dave) <- knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice), coin(Alice,Carol,*Sum), coin(Bob,Dave,*Total) guarded by Alice, Bob possibly Alice=Carol, Alice=Dave, Bob=Carol, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
GC:3 ■ Pay: ~coin(Alice,Bob,*Sum), coin(Bob,Bob,*Sum) <- knows(Alice,Bob), coin(Alice,Bob,*Sum) guarded by Alice.
GC:4 ■ Redeem: ~coin(Alice,Bob), coin(Bob,Bob), ~coin(Bob,Carol), coin(Alice,Carol), knows(Alice,Carol) <- knows(Alice,Bob), coin(Alice,Bob), coin(Bob,Carol) guarded by Alice possibly Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
GC:5 ○ | Pricing.
GC:5 ○ | Alice will price her offerings in Alice-coins and accept them in payment at those prices.
