GFF-FR-BC: Fault-Resilient GFF with Back-Channels
Of GKK: An *identity* is a unique key pair. A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of GSG: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts. Alice and Bob are persons.
Of GDO: Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
Of GDO-L: A pointer may be labeled by a person, and a pointer labeled by Dave is a *Dave-pointer*. Friends Alice and Bob have a Bob-pointer from Alice to Bob and an Alice-pointer from Bob to Alice. Alice, Bob, Carol, Dave and Erin are persons, Erin is possibly any of the others; in clause 2 Carol is possibly Dave.
Of GFF: A *feed* is named by its author. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Item an item, Bob and Carol possibly one person.
Of GFF-FR: d > 1, an integer, is a parameter of GFF-FR. Alice, Bob, Carol, Dave and Erin are persons, Dave and Erin are possibly any of the others.
Of GFF-FR-BC: Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Item an item, Bob and Dave possibly one person.
GKK:1. ○ Establish: Alice may establish an identity. Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
GKK:2. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
GKK:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
GSG:1, GDO-L:4. ■ Befriend: Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other, and then Alice has a Bob-pointer to Bob and Bob has an Alice-pointer to Alice. Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
GSG:2, GDO-L:4. ■ Unfriend: Alice may end her friendship with Bob, and then no longer Alice has a Bob-pointer to Bob and no longer Bob has an Alice-pointer to Alice.
GSG:3. ○ Loss of Trust. Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
GDO:1. ■ Point friend: Alice may point to Bob, provided Alice and Bob are friends.
GDO:2. ■ Point transitive: Alice may point to Carol, provided Alice knows Carol, Alice points to Bob and Bob points to Carol.
GDO:3. ■ Unpoint: Alice may cease pointing to Bob.
GDO-L:1. ■ Labeled point friend: Alice may add a Dave-pointer to Bob, provided Alice and Bob are friends and Bob has a Dave-pointer to Erin.
GDO-L:2, GFF:3. ■ Labeled point transitive: Alice may add a Dave-pointer to Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice has a Dave-pointer to Bob and Bob has a Dave-pointer to Carol. Labeled point transitive friend: Alice may add a Dave-pointer to Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice and Bob are friends and Bob has a Dave-pointer to Carol.
GDO-L:3. ■ Labeled unpoint: Alice may remove her Dave-pointer to Carol. Labeled unpoint self: Alice may remove her Dave-pointer to Dave, provided Alice does not count Dave as a friend.
GDO-L:4. ■ Friend pointers.
GFF:1. ■ Post: Alice may post Item to her feed. Alice has a feed, and the item is created or received.
GFF:2. ■ Deliver: Alice receives from Bob the item Item of Carol's feed, provided Alice has a Carol-pointer to Bob, Bob knows Alice and Bob holds the item Item of Carol's feed.
GFF-FR:1. ■ Redundant point: Alice adds a Carol-pointer to Bob, provided Alice has a Carol-pointer to Dave, Alice has a Carol-pointer to fewer than D persons, Alice and Bob are friends and Bob has a Carol-pointer to Erin.
GFF-FR-BC:1. ■ Back forward: Alice forwards the item Item of Dave's feed to Carol, provided Alice has a Dave-pointer to Bob, Alice has a Dave-pointer to Carol, Alice holds the item Item of Dave's feed and Alice has received the item Item of Dave's feed from Bob.
