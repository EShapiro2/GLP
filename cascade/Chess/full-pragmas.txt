Chess
Of Knowledge: An *identity* is a unique cryptographic key pair. A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of Friendship: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts.
Of Chess: The *rules of chess* are the Laws of Chess of FIDE, the International Chess Federation. A *position* is the board with the side to move, the rights to castle and to capture en passant, the positions since the last capture or pawn move and the number of moves made, so that it shows a draw by repetition or by the fifty-move or seventy-five-move rule. The *time limit* of a game is the number of days a player has for each move. Functions: b is the first position; v is the legality of m on b; c is the position after m on b; s is the side to move of b; v is the mate of b; v is the draw of b; v is the automatic draw of b; n is the moves made on b.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Game a game, Move a move, Board and Next positions, Side, Limit and Made numbers, Day, Today and Due dates.
Knowledge:1. ○ Establish: Alice may establish an identity. Alice will make herself known by her public key, sign her acts with her private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
Knowledge:2. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
Knowledge:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice is a person, Bob is a person, Carol is a person, Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
Friendship:1. ○ Befriend: Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice is a person, Bob is a person and Alice and Bob know each other. Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
Friendship:2. ■ Unfriend: Alice may end her friendship with Bob, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
Friendship:3. ○ Loss of Trust. Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
Chess:1. ■ Start. Start: Alice and Bob may jointly start Game at Board on Day with Limit days a move, provided Alice and Bob are friends, Board is the first position and the current date is Day. Start as black: Bob and Alice may jointly start Game at Board on Day with Limit days a move, provided Alice and Bob are friends, Board is the first position and the current date is Day for Alice.
Chess:2. ■ Move. Move: Alice may move Game against Bob from Board of Day to Next of Today, provided Alice has the colour Side in Game, Side is the side to move of Board, 1 is the legality of Move on Board, Next is the position after Move on Board, 0 is the automatic draw of Next and the current date is Today. Drawing move: Alice may claim a draw in Game against Bob at Board of Day, provided Alice has the colour Side in Game, Side is the side to move of Board, 1 is the legality of Move on Board, Next is the position after Move on Board and 1 is the automatic draw of Next.
Chess:3. ■ Win: Alice may claim Game against Bob at Board of Day, provided Bob has the colour Side in Game, Side is the side to move of Board and 1 is the mate of Board.
Chess:4. ■ Time: Alice may claim Game against Bob on time at Board of Day, provided Bob has the colour Side in Game, Side is the side to move of Board, Bob has the time limit Limit in Game, Due is Day plus Limit, the current date is Today and Today is more than Due.
Chess:5. ■ Resign: Alice may resign Game against Bob at Board of Day.
Chess:6. ■ Draw. Draw: Alice and Bob may jointly draw Game at Board of Day, provided Made is the moves made on Board and Made is more than 1. Claim draw: Alice may claim a draw in Game against Bob at Board of Day, provided Alice has the colour Side in Game, Side is the side to move of Board and 1 is the draw of Board.
Chess:7. ○ Fair play. Alice will choose each of her moves herself, with no help from a chess engine.
