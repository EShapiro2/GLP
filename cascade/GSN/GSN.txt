GSN: Grassroots Social Network
GSN amends GSG.
Alice and Bob are persons.
1. ■ Messaging.  Alice may send a message to her friend Bob.
2. ■ Group.  Alice may create a group, of which she is the leader and first member; Alice may close the group by leaving it.
3. ■ Joining and Leaving.  Alice, the leader of a group, may add her friend Bob to it, with Bob's consent; either Alice or Bob may then end Bob's membership in the group, which also ends when Alice and Bob cease to be friends.
4. ■ Group messaging.  A member of a group may send a message to the group, and its leader delivers it to every current member.
Pragmas
Groups: the groups Alice is a member of.
Messages: the messages Alice received.
Posts: the posts Alice received in her groups.
Messaging: Alice [Message] <text>
Group: Alice [Create] <name> [Close]
Joining and Leaving: Alice [Join]
  Bob: Alice asks you to join her group.
  Alice [Remove]
  Bob [Leave]
Group messaging: Bob [Post] <text>
  Alice [Post own] <text>
