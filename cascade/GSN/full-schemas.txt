GSN: Grassroots Social Network (expanded schemas)
GKK:0 - | An *identity* is a unique key pair.
GKK:0 - | A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity.
GKK:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
GKK:0 - | Reflexive: x knows y.
GKK:1 ○ Establish: identity(Alice) <- ~identity(Alice) guarded by Alice.
GKK:1 ○ | Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
GKK:2 ■ Acquaint: knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice) <- guarded by Alice, Bob.
GKK:3 ■ Introduce: knows(Bob,Carol) <- knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice), knows(Alice,Carol) guarded by Alice, Bob.
GSG:0 - | Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts.
GSG:0 - | Alice and Bob are persons.
GSG:1 ○ Befriend: friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice) <- knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice), ~friend(Alice,Bob), ~friend(Bob,Alice) guarded by Alice, Bob.
GSG:1 ○ | Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
GSG:2+GSN:3 ■ Unfriend: ~friend(Alice,Bob), ~friend(Bob,Alice), ~member(Alice,_,Bob), ~member(Bob,_,Alice), ~listed(Alice,Bob,_), ~listed(Bob,Alice,_) <- friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice) guarded by Alice.
GSG:3 ○ | Loss of Trust.
GSG:3 ○ | Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
GSN:0 - | A *message*, a parameter of GSN, is what a person sends.
GSN:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Grupo a group, Text a message, Bob and Carol possibly Alice or one person.
GSN:1 ■ Message: message(Bob,Alice,Text) <- friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice) guarded by Alice possibly Alice=Bob.
GSN:2 ■ | Group.
GSN:2 ■ Create: leader(Alice,Grupo), member(Alice,Grupo,Alice), listed(Alice,Alice,Grupo) <- ~leader(Alice,Grupo) guarded by Alice.
GSN:2 ■ | Alice is then its leader and first member.
GSN:2 ■ Close: ~leader(Alice,Grupo), ~listed(Alice,_,Grupo), ~member(_,Grupo,Alice) <- leader(Alice,Grupo) guarded by Alice.
GSN:3 ■ | Joining and Leaving.
GSN:3 ■ Join: member(Bob,Grupo,Alice), listed(Alice,Bob,Grupo) <- leader(Alice,Grupo), friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice), ~member(Bob,Grupo,_) guarded by Alice, Bob possibly Alice=Bob.
GSN:3 ■ Remove: ~member(Bob,Grupo,Alice), ~listed(Alice,Bob,Grupo) <- leader(Alice,Grupo), member(Bob,Grupo,Alice), listed(Alice,Bob,Grupo) guarded by Alice possibly Alice=Bob.
GSN:3 ■ Leave: ~member(Bob,Grupo,Alice), ~listed(Alice,Bob,Grupo) <- member(Bob,Grupo,Alice), listed(Alice,Bob,Grupo) guarded by Bob possibly Alice=Bob.
GSN:4 ■ | Group messaging.
GSN:4 ■ Post: posted(Alice,Text,Bob,Grupo) <- member(Bob,Grupo,Alice), ~posted(Alice,Text,Bob,Grupo) guarded by Bob possibly Alice=Bob.
GSN:4 ■ Post own: posted(Alice,Text,Alice,Grupo) <- member(Alice,Grupo,Alice), ~posted(Alice,Text,Alice,Grupo) guarded by Alice.
GSN:4 ■ Deliver: group_delivered(Alice,Bob,Text,Grupo,Carol), group_message(Carol,Bob,Text,Grupo,Alice) <- leader(Alice,Grupo), posted(Alice,Text,Bob,Grupo), listed(Alice,Carol,Grupo), ~group_delivered(Alice,Bob,Text,Grupo,Carol) possibly Alice=Bob, Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
GSN:4 ■ Deliver leader: group_delivered(Alice,Bob,Text,Grupo,Alice), group_message(Alice,Bob,Text,Grupo,Alice) <- leader(Alice,Grupo), posted(Alice,Text,Bob,Grupo), ~group_delivered(Alice,Bob,Text,Grupo,Alice) possibly Alice=Bob.
