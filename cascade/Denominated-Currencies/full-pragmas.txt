Denominated Currencies
Of Knowledge: An *identity* is a unique cryptographic key pair. A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of Notes: A *note* includes its issuer's public key, is signed by their private key, and gives its holder a right against its issuer. An *Alice-note* is a note issued by Alice. In clause 2 Carol and Dave are possibly Alice, Bob or one person, in clause 3 Carol is possibly Alice or Bob, and in clause 4 Dave is possibly Alice, Bob or Carol.
Of Coins: A *coin* is a note that is a unit of its issuer's debt. An *Alice-coin* is a coin issued by Alice. Carol is possibly Alice or Bob. Kind of note: coin, y-coins for y-notes; its acts keep their names.
Of Bonds: A *bond* is a note that is a unit of its issuer's debt, due at its maturity date, which it includes. An *Alice-bond* is a bond issued by Alice. A bond is *mature* when its maturity date is at most the current date. Kind of note: bond, y-bonds due Day for y-notes.
Of Denominated Coins: *Dollar* names one denomination, and a *Dollar-denominated Alice-coin* is an Alice-coin issued as Alice's debt of one Dollar. Kind of note: coin, Dollar-denominated y-coins for y-coins.
Of Denominated Currencies: A *Dollar-denominated Alice-bond* is an Alice-bond issued as Alice's debt of one Dollar, due at its maturity date. In clause 4 Carol is possibly Alice or Bob. Kind of note: bond, Dollar-denominated y-bonds due Day for y-bonds due Day.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Dave a party, Cond a condition Alice, Bob and Carol agree, Sum, Total and Limit numbers, Day, Term and Due maturity dates, Today a date, Dollar a fiat currency.
Knowledge:1. ○ Establish: Alice may establish an identity. Alice will make herself known by her public key, sign her acts with her private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
Knowledge:2. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
Knowledge:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
Notes:1, Denominated Coins:1. ■ Issue: Alice may issue Sum Dollar-denominated Alice-coins, provided Alice has issued coins of the denomination Dollar.
Notes:1, Bonds:1. ■ Issue bond: Alice may issue Sum Dollar-denominated Alice-bonds due Day, provided the current date is Today and Day is more than Today.
Notes:2, Denominated Currencies:2. ○ Swap: Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Dollar-denominated Carol-coins for Total Dollar-denominated Dave-coins, provided Alice and Bob know each other. Swap coin for bond: Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Dollar-denominated Carol-coins for Total Dollar-denominated Dave-bonds due Day, provided Alice and Bob know each other. Swap bonds: Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Dollar-denominated Carol-bonds due Day for Total Dollar-denominated Dave-bonds due Term, provided Alice and Bob know each other.
Notes:3. ■ Pay: Alice may pay Bob Sum Dollar-denominated Carol-coins, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice knows Bob.
Notes:3, Coins:1. ■ Pay issuer: Alice may pay the issuer Bob Sum Dollar-denominated Bob-coins, provided Alice knows Bob.
Notes:3. ■ Pay bond: Alice may pay Bob Sum Dollar-denominated Carol-bonds due Day, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice knows Bob.
Notes:4. ○ Escrow.
Notes:4, Denominated Currencies:2. ○ Deposit: Alice may deposit Sum more Dollar-denominated Dave-coins with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice knows Bob and Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in no denomination. Deposit bond: Alice may deposit Sum more Dollar-denominated Dave-bonds due Day with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice knows Bob and Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in no denomination.
Notes:4. ○ Release: Carol and Bob may jointly release Sum Dollar-denominated Dave-coins that Alice deposited on Cond, provided Carol and Bob know each other. Release bond: Carol and Bob may jointly release Sum Dollar-denominated Dave-bonds due Day that Alice deposited on Cond, provided Carol and Bob know each other. Return: Carol and Alice may jointly return Sum Dollar-denominated Dave-coins deposited for Bob on Cond, provided Carol and Alice know each other. Return bond: Carol and Alice may jointly return Sum Dollar-denominated Dave-bonds due Day deposited for Bob on Cond, provided Carol and Alice know each other. Carol will release them only if Cond is met, and return them only if it fails.
Coins:2, Bonds:2, Denominated Currencies:2. ○ Redeem: Alice may redeem a Dollar-denominated Bob-coin against a Dollar-denominated Carol-coin, provided Alice knows Bob.
Coins:3. ○ Pricing. Alice will price her offerings in Alice-coins and accept them in payment at those prices.
Bonds:2, Denominated Currencies:2. ○ Redeem coin against bond: Alice may redeem a Dollar-denominated Bob-coin against a Dollar-denominated Carol-bond due Day, provided Alice knows Bob. Redeem bond: Alice may redeem a Dollar-denominated Bob-bond due Day against a Dollar-denominated Carol-coin, provided Alice knows Bob, the current date is Today for Bob and Day is at most Today. Redeem bond against bond: Alice may redeem a Dollar-denominated Bob-bond due Day against a Dollar-denominated Carol-bond due Term, provided Alice knows Bob, the current date is Today for Bob and Day is at most Today.
Denominated Coins:1. ■ First issue: Alice may issue Sum Dollar-denominated Alice-coins for the first time, provided Alice has issued coins of no denomination.
Denominated Coins:2. ○ Denomination. A Dollar-denominated Alice-coin is due now; Alice will redeem it on presentation, by a Dollar-denominated coin the bearer accepts or by one Dollar.
Denominated Coins:3. ○ Pricing. Alice will accept Dollar-denominated Alice-coins in payment for her offerings at one Dollar each.
Denominated Currencies:1. ■ Issue denominated bond: Alice may issue Sum Dollar-denominated Alice-bonds due Day on record, provided the current date is Today and Day is more than Today.
Denominated Currencies:2. ○ Deposit own coins: Alice may deposit Sum more Dollar-denominated Alice-coins with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice knows Bob and Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in no denomination. Deposit with the issuer: Alice may deposit Sum more Dollar-denominated Carol-coins with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice knows Bob and Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in no denomination. Deposit more: Alice may deposit Sum more Dollar-denominated Dave-coins with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice knows Bob and Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in the denomination Dollar. Deposit own bonds: Alice may deposit Sum more Dollar-denominated Alice-bonds due Day with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice knows Bob and Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in no denomination. Deposit bond with the issuer: Alice may deposit Sum more Dollar-denominated Carol-bonds due Day with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice knows Bob and Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in no denomination. Deposit bond more: Alice may deposit Sum more Dollar-denominated Dave-bonds due Day with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice knows Bob and Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in the denomination Dollar.
Denominated Currencies:3. ■ Accept: Alice may accept Limit more Dollar-denominated Carol-coins in payment, provided Alice holds a Dollar-denominated Carol-coin. Accept by bond: Alice may accept Limit more Dollar-denominated Carol-coins in payment, provided Alice holds a Dollar-denominated Carol-bond due Day, Carol has issued a Dollar-denominated bond due Due, Day is at most Due and Due is at most Day. Each such coin paid to Alice counts against the limit, and Alice may accept again.
Denominated Currencies:4. ■ Pay accepted coins: Alice may pay Bob a Carol-coin that Bob accepts as denominated by Dollar, provided Alice knows Bob and Carol has issued coins of the denomination Dollar. Pay own accepted coins: Alice may pay Bob an Alice-coin that Bob accepts as denominated by Dollar, provided Alice knows Bob and Alice has issued coins of the denomination Dollar. Pay accepted coins to the issuer: Alice may pay Bob a Bob-coin that Bob accepts as denominated by Dollar, provided Alice knows Bob and Bob has issued coins of the denomination Dollar.
