GD: Grassroots Distrust
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.  GD extends GSG.
1. ■ Distrust.  Alice, who knows Carol and does not distrust Carol, may distrust Carol; if they are friends, this ends their friendship.
2. ■ Propagated distrust.  Alice, who is not a friend of Carol and does not distrust Carol, and whose friend Bob distrusts Carol, may distrust Carol.
3. ■ Ceasing to distrust.  Alice, who distrusts Carol, may cease to distrust Carol.
Pragmas
Distrusted: the persons Alice distrusts.
Distrust: Alice [Distrust]
Propagated distrust: Alice [Propagate] <person>
Ceasing to distrust: Alice [Cease]
