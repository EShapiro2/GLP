Disputes
Disputes extends Organisation.
Vocabulary: z has d between x and y raised to w = raised(z,d,x,y,w); z has w's decision on d between x and y = decision(z,w,d,x,y); w has d between x and y to arbitrate in z = case(w,z,d,x,y).
x is an arbiter of z: x holds the role Arbiter in z.
After x raises d with y in z to w, which requires z does not have d between x and y raised to w: z has d between x and y raised to w and w has d between x and y to arbitrate in z.
After w decides d between x and y in z: no longer z has d between x and y raised to w, no longer w has d between x and y to arbitrate in z and z has w's decision on d between x and y.
Disputes:0 - | The bylaws of an organisation name the role *arbiter*. A *dispute* is a disagreement between two participants of an organisation about an act of the organisation or an undertaking.
Disputes:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Orga an organisation, Issue a dispute.
Disputes:1 ■ Raise: raised(Orga,Issue,Alice,Bob,Carol), case(Carol,Orga,Issue,Alice,Bob) <- has_participant(Orga,Alice), has_participant(Orga,Bob), has_role(Orga,Carol,Arbiter), ~raised(Orga,Issue,Alice,Bob,Carol) guarded by Alice.
Disputes:2 ■ Decide: ~raised(Orga,Issue,Alice,Bob,Carol), ~case(Carol,Orga,Issue,Alice,Bob), decision(Orga,Carol,Issue,Alice,Bob) <- raised(Orga,Issue,Alice,Bob,Carol), case(Carol,Orga,Issue,Alice,Bob) guarded by Carol.
Disputes:3 ○ | Abide.
Disputes:3 ○ | Alice and Bob will abide by the decision of Carol.
