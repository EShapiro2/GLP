Encounters
Encounters extends Knowledge (GSC, From Grassroots Contract to Grassroots App, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06231).
A person is *nearby* Alice when her phone reaches theirs by Bluetooth or the local network, without the Internet; a *place* is where Alice is.
Alice and Bob are persons.
1. ■ Open place: Alice may declare the place she is in open. Close place: Alice may close it.
2. ■ Encounter: Alice and Bob, nearby each other, who have both declared the place open, come to know each other.
Logic pragmas
Encounters extends Knowledge (GSC, From Grassroots Contract to Grassroots App, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06231).
A person is *nearby* Alice when her phone reaches theirs by Bluetooth or the local network, without the Internet; a *place* is where Alice is.
Alice and Bob are persons.
1. ■ Open place: Alice may declare her place open. Close place: Alice may close her place.
2. ■ Encounter: Alice and Bob come to know each other, and Alice and Bob must consent, provided Alice does not know Bob, Bob does not know Alice, Alice and Bob are nearby each other, Alice has declared the place open and Bob has declared the place open.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x is nearby y = nearby(x,y); x has declared the place open = open_place(x).
x and y are nearby each other: x is nearby y and y is nearby x.
After x declares x's place open, which requires x has not declared the place open: x has declared the place open.
After x closes x's place: no longer x has declared the place open.
Environment: x is nearby y.

Implementation pragmas
Open place: while the place is open Alice's app sets the trust of Bluetooth and of the local network open, and closed otherwise (GNA).
Nearby: an environment predicate, nearby(Alice, Bob), the persons the networking layer reports nearby, which Alice's app keeps current.
Encounter: Alice stands on [Open place] until [Close place]; the two phones exchange keys (GNA, open trust).
