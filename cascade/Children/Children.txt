Children
Children renames Warded Groups, and extends and weakens it.
Alice and Bob are persons, Wendy and Will children.
1. ◆ Enter: Guardians:1, only if Alice is a legal parent of the minor who operates Wendy.
2. ■ A child's acquaintances. Child acquaint: Guardians:3, only if Alice knows Bob, and with the consent of Alice. Children acquaint: Guardians:4, only if Alice knows Will and Bob knows Wendy, and with the consent of Alice and Bob, who know each other, Alice and Bob possibly one person.
3. ■ Child create: Warded Groups:2, only with the consent of Alice, a parent of Wendy. Only a child, Will, may join the group.
4. ○ Of age: The creator of Wendy may declare Wendy of age, which ends every guardianship of Wendy, and Wendy is then a person. The creator will do so if the minor who operates Wendy comes of age, and not before.
5. ○ A minor who is not a ward. Alice, aware that someone is a minor though not a ward, will neither befriend the minor nor let her child befriend the minor, and will report it to the minor's legal parents if she knows who they are.
Logic pragmas
Children renames Warded Groups, and extends and weakens it.
Alice and Bob are persons, Wendy and Will children; in clause 2 Alice and Bob are possibly one person.
1. ◆ Enter: Guardians:1. Alice is a legal parent of the minor who operates Wendy.
2. ■ A child's acquaintances. Child acquaint: Guardians:3 Ward acquaint, only if Alice knows Bob and Alice is a parent of Wendy, with the consent of Alice. Children acquaint: Guardians:4 Wards acquaint, only if Alice knows Will, Bob knows Wendy, Alice and Bob know each other, Alice is a parent of Wendy and Bob is a parent of Will, with the consent of Alice and Bob.
3. ■ Child create: Warded Groups:2 Ward create, only if Wendy has Alice as a parent, with the consent of Alice. Join: Groups:3 Join, only if Alice is a person. Child add: Warded Groups:3 Ward add, Will in place of Bob, only if Will is a child. Only a child, Will, may join the group.
4. ○ Of age: Alice may declare Wendy of age, provided Alice is the creator of Wendy and Wendy has Bob as a parent. Wendy is then a person. The creator will do so if the minor who operates Wendy comes of age, and not before.
5. ○ A minor who is not a ward. Alice, aware that someone is a minor though not a ward, will neither befriend the minor nor let her child befriend the minor, and will report it to the minor's legal parents if she knows who they are.
Logic pragmas.
Renaming of Warded Groups: a child for a ward and a parent for a guardian.
Vocabulary: z is of age = of_age(z).
Roles: the parents of z = parents(z), listed by z has y as a parent and y is a parent of z.
every guardianship of z has ended: no one is a parent of z and z has no one as a parent.
After x declares z of age, which requires z is not of age: z is of age, no longer z is a child and every guardianship of z has ended and z is a person.
