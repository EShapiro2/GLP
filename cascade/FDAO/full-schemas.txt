FDAO: Funded DAO (expanded schemas)
GKK:0 - | An *identity* is a unique key pair.
GKK:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. A party to a contract must have an identity.
GKK:0 - | Reflexive: x knows y.
GKK:1 ○ Establish: identity(Alice) <- ~identity(Alice) guarded by Alice.
GKK:1 ○ | Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
GKK:2 ■ Acquaint: knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice) <- guarded by Alice, Bob.
GKK:3 ■ Introduce: knows(Bob,Carol) <- knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice), knows(Alice,Carol) guarded by Alice, Bob.
DAO:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Delos a DAO and Prop a proposal, Votes and Double numbers, Carol possibly Alice or Bob.
DAO:1 ■ Found: steward(Alice,Delos), member(Alice,Delos,Alice), listed(Alice,Alice,Delos) <- ~steward(Alice,Delos) guarded by Alice.
DAO:2 ■ Join: member(Bob,Delos,Alice), listed(Alice,Bob,Delos) <- steward(Alice,Delos), knows(Alice,Bob), ~member(Bob,Delos,Alice) guarded by Alice, Bob.
DAO:3 ■ | Proposing.
DAO:3 ■ Propose: proposed(Carol,Prop,Delos,Alice), proposal(Alice,Prop,Delos) <- member(Carol,Delos,Alice), steward(Alice,Delos), ~proposal(Alice,Prop,Delos) guarded by Carol possibly Alice=Carol.
DAO:3 ■ Deliver proposal: delivered(Alice,Prop,Delos,Bob), proposal(Bob,Prop,Delos) <- steward(Alice,Delos), proposal(Alice,Prop,Delos), listed(Alice,Bob,Delos), ~delivered(Alice,Prop,Delos,Bob).
DAO:4 ■ | Voting.
DAO:4 ■ Vote for: voted(Carol,Prop,Delos), vote_for(Alice,Carol,Prop,Delos) <- member(Carol,Delos,Alice), proposal(Carol,Prop,Delos), steward(Alice,Delos), ~decided(Alice,Prop,Delos), ~voted(Carol,Prop,Delos) guarded by Carol possibly Alice=Carol.
DAO:4 ■ Vote against: voted(Carol,Prop,Delos), vote_against(Alice,Carol,Prop,Delos) <- member(Carol,Delos,Alice), proposal(Carol,Prop,Delos), steward(Alice,Delos), ~decided(Alice,Prop,Delos), ~voted(Carol,Prop,Delos) guarded by Carol possibly Alice=Carol.
DAO:4 ■ Deliver vote for: delivered_vote(Alice,Carol,Prop,Delos,Bob), vote_for(Bob,Carol,Prop,Delos) <- steward(Alice,Delos), vote_for(Alice,Carol,Prop,Delos), listed(Alice,Bob,Delos), ~delivered_vote(Alice,Carol,Prop,Delos,Bob) possibly Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
DAO:4 ■ Deliver vote against: delivered_vote(Alice,Carol,Prop,Delos,Bob), vote_against(Bob,Carol,Prop,Delos) <- steward(Alice,Delos), vote_against(Alice,Carol,Prop,Delos), listed(Alice,Bob,Delos), ~delivered_vote(Alice,Carol,Prop,Delos,Bob) possibly Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
DAO:5 ■ | Deciding.
DAO:5 ■ Pass: decided(Alice,Prop,Delos), passed(Alice,Prop,Delos) <- steward(Alice,Delos), proposal(Alice,Prop,Delos), vote_for(Alice,>=Votes,Prop,Delos), product(Double,Votes,2), listed(Alice,<Double,Delos), ~decided(Alice,Prop,Delos) guarded by Alice.
DAO:5 ■ Reject: decided(Alice,Prop,Delos), rejected(Alice,Prop,Delos) <- steward(Alice,Delos), proposal(Alice,Prop,Delos), vote_against(Alice,>=Votes,Prop,Delos), product(Double,Votes,2), listed(Alice,<Double,Delos), ~decided(Alice,Prop,Delos) guarded by Alice.
DAO:5 ■ Deliver pass: delivered_decision(Alice,Prop,Delos,Bob), passed(Bob,Prop,Delos) <- steward(Alice,Delos), passed(Alice,Prop,Delos), listed(Alice,Bob,Delos), ~delivered_decision(Alice,Prop,Delos,Bob).
DAO:5 ■ Deliver reject: delivered_decision(Alice,Prop,Delos,Bob), rejected(Bob,Prop,Delos) <- steward(Alice,Delos), rejected(Alice,Prop,Delos), listed(Alice,Bob,Delos), ~delivered_decision(Alice,Prop,Delos,Bob).
DAO:6 ■ | Majority.
DAO:6 ■ | The steward may pass a proposal only if more than half of the DAO's members at that time have voted for it, and reject it only if more than half have voted against it.
GC:0 - | A *coin* is a unit of debt that includes its issuer's public key and is signed by their private key. An *Alice-coin* is a coin issued by Alice.
GC:0 - | Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Sum and Total numbers, Carol and Dave possibly Alice, Bob or one person.
GC:1 ■ Issue: coin(Alice,Alice,*Sum) <- guarded by Alice.
GC:2 ■ Swap: ~coin(Alice,Carol,*Sum), coin(Bob,Carol,*Sum), ~coin(Bob,Dave,*Total), coin(Alice,Dave,*Total), knows(Bob,Carol), knows(Alice,Dave) <- knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice), coin(Alice,Carol,*Sum), coin(Bob,Dave,*Total) guarded by Alice, Bob possibly Alice=Carol, Alice=Dave, Bob=Carol, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
GC:3 ■ Pay: ~coin(Alice,Bob,*Sum), coin(Bob,Bob,*Sum) <- knows(Alice,Bob), coin(Alice,Bob,*Sum) guarded by Alice.
GC:4 ■ Redeem: ~coin(Alice,Bob), coin(Bob,Bob), ~coin(Bob,Carol), coin(Alice,Carol), knows(Alice,Carol) <- knows(Alice,Bob), coin(Alice,Bob), coin(Bob,Carol) guarded by Alice possibly Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
GC:5 ○ | Pricing.
GC:5 ○ | Alice will price her offerings in Alice-coins and accept them in payment at those prices.
FDAO:0 - | Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Delos a DAO and Prop a proposal, Sum a number, Carol possibly Alice or Bob, Dave possibly Alice, Bob or Carol.
FDAO:1 ■ Fund: ~coin(Carol,Dave,*Sum), dao_coin(Alice,Dave,Delos,*Sum) <- member(Carol,Delos,Alice), steward(Alice,Delos), coin(Carol,Dave,*Sum) guarded by Carol possibly Alice=Carol.
FDAO:2 ■ Grant: ~dao_coin(Alice,Dave,Delos,*Sum), coin(Carol,Dave,*Sum), granted(Alice,Prop,Delos) <- steward(Alice,Delos), passed(Alice,Prop,Delos), proposed(Carol,Prop,Delos,Alice), ~granted(Alice,Prop,Delos), dao_coin(Alice,Dave,Delos,*Sum) guarded by Alice possibly Alice=Carol.
FDAO:3 ○ | Amount.
FDAO:3 ○ | The steward will grant a passed proposal the amount it asks for, as far as the DAO's coins allow.
