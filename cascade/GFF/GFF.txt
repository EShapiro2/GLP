GFF: Grassroots Feeds and Followers
Alice, Bob and Carol are agents.
1. ■ Feed.  Alice has a feed and may post to it an item, created or received.
2. ■ Following.  Friends follow each other.  Alice, who knows Bob but is not their friend, may follow Bob through their friend Carol that follows Bob.
3. ■ Unfollow.  Alice, who follows Bob, may cease following Bob.
4. ■ Delivery.  Bob sends every item they post to every friend; Carol, who follows Bob, forwards every item on Bob's feed to every friend who follows Bob through Carol.
5. ■ Updating.  Every so often, Carol updates their friend Alice whom Carol follows.
Pragmas
Posts: Alice's feed.
Feeds: the agents Alice follows; under an agent followed, the friend Alice follows them through, the friends who follow them, and the items of their feed, each once, the signatures of each of its paths under it.
Feed: Alice [Post] <item>
Following: Alice [Follow] <person>
Unfollow: Alice [Unfollow]
