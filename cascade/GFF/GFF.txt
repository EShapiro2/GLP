GFF: Grassroots Feeds and Followers
Alice, Bob and Carol are agents.
1. ■ Feed.  Alice has a feed and may post to it an item, created or received.
2. ■ Following.  Friends follow each other.  Alice, who knows Bob, may decide to follow Bob, as well as end their following.  Alice, who decided to follow Bob and whose friend Carol follows Bob, follows Bob through Carol.
3. ■ Follower discovery.  Alice, who decided to follow Bob, asks their friend Carol whether Carol follows Bob, and Carol answers with a path to Bob, or that they do not.
4. ■ Delivery.  Bob sends every item they post to every friend; Carol, who follows Bob, sends every item on Bob's feed, after adding their signature, to every friend who follows Bob through Carol.
Pragmas
Posts: the items Alice posted.
Feeds: the agents Alice follows.
Feed: Alice [Post] <item>
Following: Alice [Follow]
  Alice [Unfollow]
