GPD: Grassroots Programs Dissemination
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ○ Befriend.  Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other.
5. ■ Unfriend.  Alice may end her friendship with Bob.
6. ■ Sign.  Alice may create an item by signing it.
7. ■ Forward.  Alice may forward an item of Carol to Bob, provided Alice holds an item of Carol and Alice and Bob are friends.
8. ◆ Publish.  Carol may publish a program by signing it.
9. ■ Trust.  Alice may declare trust in Carol as a developer, provided Alice knows Carol.
10. ■ Untrust.  Alice may withdraw her declaration of trust in Carol as a developer.
11. ■ Offer.  Carol may offer a program of Dave to Alice, provided Carol holds a program of Dave and Carol and Alice are friends.
12. ■ Install own.  Alice may install her own program, provided Alice holds a program of Alice.
13. ■ Install.  Alice may install a program of Carol, provided Alice holds a program of Carol and Alice trusts Carol as a developer.
14. ■ Invite.  Alice may invite Bob to join in a program of Carol, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob are friends, Alice has a program of Carol installed and Bob trusts Carol as a developer.
15. ■ Leave.  Alice may leave a program of Carol.
