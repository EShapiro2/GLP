GPD: Grassroots Programs Dissemination (flat text)
GKK:0 - | An *identity* is a unique key pair, chosen by the person who has it.
GKK:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. A party to a contract must have an identity.
GKK:1 ○ Establish: Alice may Alice has an identity and Alice knows Alice, provided not Alice has an identity.
GKK:1 ○ | Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
GKK:2 ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly Alice knows Bob and Bob knows Alice.
GKK:3 ■ Introduce: Alice and Bob may jointly Bob knows Carol, provided Alice knows Bob, Bob knows Alice and Alice knows Carol.
GSG:0 - | Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts.
GSG:0 - | Alice and Bob are persons.
GSG:1 ○ Befriend: Alice and Bob may jointly Alice counts Bob as a friend and Bob counts Alice as a friend, provided Alice knows Bob, Bob knows Alice, not Alice counts Bob as a friend and not Bob counts Alice as a friend.
GSG:1 ○ | Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
GSG:2 ■ Unfriend: Alice may no longer Alice counts Bob as a friend and no longer Bob counts Alice as a friend.
GSG:3 ○ | Loss of Trust.
GSG:3 ○ | Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
GFP:0 - | The *author* of an item is its first signer.
GFP:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Carol possibly Alice or Bob.
GFP:1 ■ Sign: Alice may Alice holds an item of Alice.
GFP:2 ■ Forward: Alice may Bob holds an item of Carol, provided Alice holds an item of Carol, Alice counts Bob as a friend and Bob counts Alice as a friend. Possibly one: Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
GFP:2 ■ | Alice adds her signature to the signatures the item carries.
GPD:0 - | A *program* is an item: compiled code, signed by its compiler, that gives the grassroots contract it is compiled from, its clauses and its speech-act schemas; its *developer* is its author. Alice *trusts* Carol *as a developer* when she has declared it and has not withdrawn the declaration.
GPD:0 - | Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Carol and Dave possibly Alice, Bob or one person.
GPD:1 ◆ Publish: Carol may Carol holds a program of Carol.
GPD:1 ◆ | Carol attests that its schemas say what its clauses say, that it is the result of compiling the schemas, and that it is expected to behave correctly according to the contract.
GPD:2 ■ | Developer Trust.
GPD:2 ■ Trust: Alice may Alice trusts Carol as a developer, provided Alice knows Carol and not Alice trusts Carol as a developer. Possibly one: Alice=Carol.
GPD:2 ■ Untrust: Alice may no longer Alice trusts Carol as a developer. Possibly one: Alice=Carol.
GPD:3 ■ Offer: Carol may Alice holds a program of Dave, provided Carol holds a program of Dave, Carol counts Alice as a friend and Alice counts Carol as a friend. Possibly one: Alice=Carol, Alice=Dave, Carol=Dave.
GPD:3 ■ | Carol offers it by forwarding it to Alice.
GPD:4 ■ Install own: Alice may Alice has a program of Alice installed, provided Alice holds a program of Alice and not Alice has a program of Alice installed.
GPD:4 ■ Install: Alice may Alice has a program of Carol installed, provided Alice holds a program of Carol, Alice trusts Carol as a developer and not Alice has a program of Carol installed. Possibly one: Alice=Carol.
GPD:5 ■ Invite: Alice and Bob may jointly Bob holds a program of Carol, Bob has a program of Carol installed, Alice is a participant of a program of Carol with Bob and Bob is a participant of a program of Carol with Alice, provided Alice counts Bob as a friend, Bob counts Alice as a friend, Alice has a program of Carol installed and Bob trusts Carol as a developer. Possibly one: Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
GPD:5 ■ | Bob then has the program installed, with Alice's signature added, and each is a participant of the program with the other.
GPD:6 ■ Leave: Alice may no longer Alice has a program of Carol installed, no longer Alice is a participant of a program of Carol with anyone and no longer anyone is a participant of a program of Carol with Alice. Possibly one: Alice=Carol.
