GPD: Grassroots Programs Dissemination
Of GKK: An *identity* is a unique key pair, chosen by the person who has it. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. A party to a contract must have an identity.
Of GSG: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts. Alice and Bob are persons.
Of GFP: The *author* of an item is its first signer. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Carol possibly Alice or Bob.
Of GPD: A *program* is an item: compiled code, signed by its compiler, that gives the grassroots contract it is compiled from, its clauses and its speech-act schemas; its *developer* is its author. Alice *trusts* Carol *as a developer* when she has declared it and has not withdrawn the declaration. Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Carol and Dave possibly Alice, Bob or one person.
GKK:1. ○ Establish: Alice may establish an identity. Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
GKK:2. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
GKK:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
GSG:1. ○ Befriend: Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other. Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
GSG:2. ■ Unfriend: Alice may end her friendship with Bob.
GSG:3. ○ Loss of Trust. Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
GFP:1. ■ Sign: Alice may create an item by signing it.
GFP:2. ■ Forward: Alice may forward an item of Carol to Bob, provided Alice holds an item of Carol and Alice and Bob are friends. Alice adds her signature to the signatures the item carries.
GPD:1. ◆ Publish: Carol may publish a program by signing it. Carol attests that its schemas say what its clauses say, that it is the result of compiling the schemas, and that it is expected to behave correctly according to the contract.
GPD:2. ■ Developer Trust. Trust: Alice may declare trust in Carol as a developer, provided Alice knows Carol. Untrust: Alice may withdraw her declaration of trust in Carol as a developer.
GPD:3. ■ Offer: Carol may offer a program of Dave to Alice, provided Carol holds a program of Dave and Carol and Alice are friends. Carol offers it by forwarding it to Alice.
GPD:4. ■ Install own: Alice may install her own program, provided Alice holds a program of Alice. Install: Alice may install a program of Carol, provided Alice holds a program of Carol and Alice trusts Carol as a developer.
GPD:5. ■ Invite: Alice may invite Bob to join in a program of Carol, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob are friends, Alice has a program of Carol installed and Bob trusts Carol as a developer. Bob then has the program installed, with Alice's signature added, and each is a participant of the program with the other.
GPD:6. ■ Leave: Alice may leave a program of Carol.
