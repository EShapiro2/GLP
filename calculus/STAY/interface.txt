STAY: Grassroots Stays
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ■ Issue.  Alice may issue Sum Alice-coins.
5. ■ Swap.  Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Carol-coins for Total Dave-coins, provided Alice and Bob know each other.
6. ■ Pay.  Alice may pay Bob Sum Bob-coins, provided Alice knows Bob.
7. ■ Redeem.  Alice may redeem a Bob-coin against a Carol-coin, provided Alice knows Bob.
8. ◆ List place.  Alice may list Place under a new name of hers, provided Alice can host guests at Place.
9. ■ Book.  Bob may book a stay at Alice's Place on Night for Sum Alice-coins, with the consent of Alice, provided Bob knows Alice, Alice lists Place, the current date is Today and Night is more than Today.
10. ■ Cancel.  Alice may cancel Bob's stay at Place on Night for Sum Alice-coins, provided the current date is Today and Night is more than Today.
11. ■ Review.  Bob may review his stay at Alice's Place on Night, provided Bob has a stay at Alice's Place on Night for Sum Alice-coins, the current date is Today and Night is at most Today.
