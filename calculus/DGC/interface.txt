DGC: Denominated Grassroots Currencies
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ■ Issue.  Alice may issue Sum Alice-coins, provided Alice has issued coins of the denomination Dollar.
5. ■ Swap.  Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Carol-coins for Total Dave-coins, provided Alice and Bob know each other and the coins and bonds of Carol and Dave are of one denomination.
6. ■ Pay.  Alice may pay Bob Sum Bob-coins, provided Alice knows Bob.
7. ■ Redeem.  Alice may redeem a Bob-coin against a Carol-coin, provided Alice knows Bob and the coins and bonds of Bob and Carol are of one denomination.
8. ■ Issue bond.  Alice may issue Sum Alice-bonds due Day, provided the current date is Today and Day is more than Today.
9. ■ Swap coin for bond.  Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Carol-coins for Total Dave-bonds due Day, provided Alice and Bob know each other and the coins and bonds of Carol and Dave are of one denomination.
10. ■ Swap bonds.  Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Carol-bonds due Day for Total Dave-bonds due Term, provided Alice and Bob know each other and the coins and bonds of Carol and Dave are of one denomination.
11. ■ Redeem coin against bond.  Alice may redeem a Bob-coin against a Carol-bond due Day, provided Alice knows Bob and the coins and bonds of Bob and Carol are of one denomination.
12. ■ Redeem bond.  Alice may redeem a Bob-bond due Day against a Carol-coin, provided Alice knows Bob, the current date is Today for Bob, Day is at most Today and the coins and bonds of Bob and Carol are of one denomination.
13. ■ Redeem bond against bond.  Alice may redeem a Bob-bond due Day against a Carol-bond due Term, provided Alice knows Bob, the current date is Today for Bob, Day is at most Today and the coins and bonds of Bob and Carol are of one denomination.
14. ■ Deposit.  Alice may deposit Sum more Dollar-denominated Dave-coins with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice knows Bob, Dave has issued coins of the denomination Dollar and Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in no denomination.
15. ■ Deposit bond.  Alice may deposit Sum more Dollar-denominated Dave-bonds due Day with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice knows Bob, Dave has issued a Dollar-denominated bond due Day and Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in no denomination.
16. ■ Release.  Carol and Bob may jointly release Sum Dave-coins that Alice deposited on Cond, provided Carol and Bob know each other.
17. ■ Release bond.  Carol and Bob may jointly release Sum Dave-bonds due Day that Alice deposited on Cond, provided Carol and Bob know each other.
18. ■ Return.  Carol and Alice may jointly return Sum Dave-coins deposited for Bob on Cond, provided Carol and Alice know each other.
19. ■ Return bond.  Carol and Alice may jointly return Sum Dave-bonds due Day deposited for Bob on Cond, provided Carol and Alice know each other.
20. ■ First issue.  Alice may issue Sum Dollar-denominated Alice-coins, provided Alice has issued coins of no denomination.
21. ■ Issue denominated bond.  Alice may issue Sum Dollar-denominated Alice-bonds due Day, provided the current date is Today and Day is more than Today.
22. ■ Deposit own coins.  Alice may deposit Sum more Dollar-denominated Alice-coins with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice knows Bob, Alice has issued coins of the denomination Dollar and Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in no denomination.
23. ■ Deposit with the issuer.  Alice may deposit Sum more Dollar-denominated Carol-coins with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice knows Bob, Carol has issued coins of the denomination Dollar and Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in no denomination.
24. ■ Deposit more.  Alice may deposit Sum more Dollar-denominated Dave-coins with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice knows Bob, Dave has issued coins of the denomination Dollar and Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in the denomination Dollar.
25. ■ Deposit own bonds.  Alice may deposit Sum more Dollar-denominated Alice-bonds due Day with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice knows Bob, Alice has issued a Dollar-denominated bond due Day and Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in no denomination.
26. ■ Deposit bond with the issuer.  Alice may deposit Sum more Dollar-denominated Carol-bonds due Day with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice knows Bob, Carol has issued a Dollar-denominated bond due Day and Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in no denomination.
27. ■ Deposit bond more.  Alice may deposit Sum more Dollar-denominated Dave-bonds due Day with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice knows Bob, Dave has issued a Dollar-denominated bond due Day and Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in the denomination Dollar.
28. ■ Accept.  Alice may accept Limit more Dollar-denominated Carol-coins in payment, provided Alice holds a Dollar-denominated Carol-coin.
29. ■ Accept by bond.  Alice may accept Limit more Dollar-denominated Carol-coins in payment, provided Alice holds a Carol-bond due Day, Carol has issued a Dollar-denominated bond due Due, Day is at most Due and Due is at most Day.
30. ■ Pay accepted coins.  Alice may pay Bob a Carol-coin that Bob accepts as denominated by Dollar, provided Alice knows Bob and Carol has issued coins of the denomination Dollar.
31. ■ Pay own accepted coins.  Alice may pay Bob an Alice-coin that Bob accepts as denominated by Dollar, provided Alice knows Bob and Alice has issued coins of the denomination Dollar.
32. ■ Pay accepted coins to the issuer.  Alice may pay Bob a Bob-coin that Bob accepts as denominated by Dollar, provided Alice knows Bob and Bob has issued coins of the denomination Dollar.
