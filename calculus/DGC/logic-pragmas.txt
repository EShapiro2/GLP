DGC: Denominated Grassroots Currencies
DGC extends GB and GDC, and restricts and strengthens GB.
A *Dollar-denominated Alice-bond* is an Alice-bond issued as Alice's debt of one Dollar, due at its maturity date.
Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Dollar a fiat currency, Day and Due maturity dates, Today a date, Cond a condition Alice, Bob and Carol agree, Sum and Limit numbers; in clause 4 Carol is possibly Alice or Bob.
1. ■ Issue denominated bond: Alice may issue Sum Dollar-denominated Alice-bonds due Day, provided the current date is Today and Day is more than Today.
2. ■ Swap: GB:2, only if the coins and bonds of Carol and Dave are of one denomination. Redeem: GB:3, only if the coins and bonds of Bob and Carol are of one denomination. Deposit: GB:4 Deposit, only if Dave has issued coins of the denomination Dollar and Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in no denomination, and then Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in the denomination Dollar. Deposit more: Alice may deposit Sum more Dollar-denominated Dave-coins with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice knows Bob, Dave has issued coins of the denomination Dollar and Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in the denomination Dollar. Deposit bond: GB:4 Deposit bond, only if Dave has issued a Dollar-denominated bond due Day and Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in no denomination, and then Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in the denomination Dollar. Deposit bond more: Alice may deposit Sum more Dollar-denominated Dave-bonds due Day with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice knows Bob, Dave has issued a Dollar-denominated bond due Day and Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in the denomination Dollar.
3. ■ Accept: Alice may accept Limit more Dollar-denominated Carol-coins in payment, provided Alice holds a Dollar-denominated Carol-coin. Accept by bond: Alice may accept Limit more Dollar-denominated Carol-coins in payment, provided Alice holds a Carol-bond due Day and Carol has issued a Dollar-denominated bond due Due. Each such coin paid to Alice counts against the limit, and Alice may accept again.
4. ■ Pay accepted coins: Alice may pay Bob a Carol-coin that Bob accepts as denominated by Dollar, provided Alice knows Bob and Carol has issued coins of the denomination Dollar.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x has issued an f-denominated bond due e = issued_bond_in(x,f,e); the coins and bonds of z and w are of one denomination, for x = one_denomination(x,z,w); z holds the escrow from x for y on k in the denomination f = escrow_denomination(z,x,y,k,f); x accepts an f-denominated z-coin in payment = accepts(x,z,f); x accepts n f-denominated z-coins in payment = accepts(x,z,f,*n).
x holds an f-denominated z-coin: x holds a z-coin and z has issued coins of the denomination f.
x holds an f-denominated z-bond due e: x holds a z-bond due e and z has issued an f-denominated bond due e.
After x issues n f-denominated x-bonds due e: x holds n x-bonds due e and x has issued an f-denominated bond due e.
After x accepts n more f-denominated z-coins in payment: x accepts n f-denominated z-coins in payment.
After x pays y a z-coin that y accepts as denominated by f: no longer x holds a z-coin, y holds a z-coin and no longer y accepts an f-denominated z-coin in payment.
After x deposits n more f-denominated w-coins with z for y on k: no longer x holds n w-coins, z holds n w-coins in escrow from x for y on k and z holds the escrow from x for y on k in the denomination f.
After x deposits n more f-denominated w-bonds due e with z for y on k: no longer x holds n w-bonds due e, z holds n w-bonds due e in escrow from x for y on k and z holds the escrow from x for y on k in the denomination f.
Environment: the coins and bonds of z and w are of one denomination, for x.
