The names of the 66 contracts (Udi, 2026-11-16, to Calculus #8), on main since 24318f2c (2026-11-17).
The first and the fifth column are the names, rows and atoms of before the renaming; their headings keep their words,
since the readers of this table find it by them.

A name is one plain word, or two in their English order.  The title line of a contract is its name.

The file name is the name with a hyphen for each space and no apostrophe: NAME.txt, NAME.glp, cascade/NAME/.
The row of cascade.sh and the atom of contract_named are the file name in lower case, a hyphen as an underscore.

How a reader knows a name: a name is one of the names of this table and nothing else.  Match the known names longest
first, so that "Community Bank restricts Bank and extends Coins." names Community Bank, Bank and Coins, and
"Trading Company and Bank" two contracts.
A first line is: NAME, then its relations (extends, restricts, relaxes, strengthens, instantiates ... with ..., composes
... with ...), the contracts after a relation joined by commas and "and"; a reference in parentheses may follow a name.
A citation is: NAME, a colon, the clause's number, then the act's name where one act is cited: "Community Bank:1",
"Notes:3", "Organisation:4 Appoint".  The act's name is the name of an act of the clause cited, as the cited contract's
full schemas print it; a reader matches the names of that clause's acts longest first, as "Deposit bond with the issuer"
before "Deposit bond".  A reader with no schemas at hand takes the words after the number up to the first comma or full
stop: "Organisation:3 Officer leave, only if ...".

An apostrophe in a name is the ASCII one, ', in the table and in every contract.

A test may add made-up contracts: rows of the same form after the table's, in a table of its own that it builds from
this one.

The fifth column is the row and atom before the renaming: the name of the first column in lower case, every
character that is not a letter or a digit an underscore.
