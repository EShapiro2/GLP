The names of the 66 contracts, for the publication after that of 2026-11-16 (Udi, 2026-11-16, to Calculus #8).
Not yet made: on main the contracts have the names of the first column.

A name is one plain word, or two in their English order.  The title line of a contract is its name.

The file name is the name with a hyphen for each space and no apostrophe: NAME.txt, NAME.glp, cascade/NAME/.
The row of cascade.sh and the atom of contract_named are the file name in lower case, a hyphen as an underscore.

How a reader knows a name: a name is one of the names of this table and nothing else.  Match the known names longest
first, so that "Community Bank restricts Bank and extends Coins." names Community Bank, Bank and Coins, and
"Trading Company and Bank" two contracts.
A first line is: NAME, then its relations (extends, restricts, relaxes, strengthens, instantiates ... with ..., composes
... with ...), the contracts after a relation joined by commas and "and"; a reference in parentheses may follow a name.
A citation is: NAME, a colon, the clause's number, then the act's name where one act is cited: "Community Bank:1",
"Notes:3", "Organisation:4 Appoint".
