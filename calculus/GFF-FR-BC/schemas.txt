GFF-FR-BC: Fault-Resilient GFF with Back-Channels
GFF-FR-BC extends GFF-FR.
Vocabulary: x has received an item of w's feed first from y = received_first(x,w,y); z has received an item of w's feed back from x = received_back(z,w,x).
After x forwards an item of w's feed to z: z holds an item of w's feed and z has received an item of w's feed back from x.
GFF-FR-BC:0 - | Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Bob and Dave possibly one person.
GFF-FR-BC:1 ■ Back forward: feed_item(Carol,Dave), received_back(Carol,Dave,Alice) <- pointer(Alice,Bob,Dave), pointer(Alice,Carol,Dave), received_first(Alice,Dave,Bob) possibly Bob=Dave.
