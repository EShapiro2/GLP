GFF-FR-BC: Fault-Resilient GFF with Back-Channels
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ■ Befriend.  Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other, and then Alice has a Bob-pointer to Bob and Bob has an Alice-pointer to Alice.
5. ■ Unfriend.  Alice may end her friendship with Bob, and then no longer Alice has a Bob-pointer to Bob and no longer Bob has an Alice-pointer to Alice.
6. ■ Point friend.  Alice may point to Bob, provided Alice and Bob are friends.
7. ■ Point transitive.  Alice may point to Carol, provided Alice knows Carol, Alice points to Bob and Bob points to Carol.
8. ■ Unpoint.  Alice may cease pointing to Bob.
9. ■ Labeled point friend.  Alice may add a Dave-pointer to Bob, provided Alice and Bob are friends and Bob has a Dave-pointer to someone.
10. ■ Labeled point transitive.  Alice may add a Dave-pointer to Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice has a Dave-pointer to Bob and Bob has a Dave-pointer to Carol.
11. ■ Labeled point transitive friend.  Alice may add a Dave-pointer to Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice and Bob are friends and Bob has a Dave-pointer to Carol.
12. ■ Labeled unpoint.  Alice may remove her Dave-pointer to Bob.
13. ■ Post.  Alice may post an item to her feed.
14. ■ Deliver.  Alice receives from Bob an item of Carol's feed, provided Alice has a Carol-pointer to Bob, Bob knows Alice and Bob holds an item of Carol's feed.
15. ■ Redundant point.  Alice adds a Carol-pointer to Bob, provided Alice has a Carol-pointer to someone, Alice has a Carol-pointer to fewer than D persons, Alice and Bob are friends and Bob has a Carol-pointer to someone.
16. ■ Back forward.  Alice forwards an item of Dave's feed to Carol, provided Alice has a Dave-pointer to Bob, Alice has a Dave-pointer to Carol and Alice has received an item of Dave's feed first from Bob.
