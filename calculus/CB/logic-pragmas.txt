CB: Community Bank
CB instantiates GBank with community bank bylaws; x is a director of z for x is entitled to propose m in z; x is a director of z for x is entitled to take m for z; x is a director of z for x is entitled to vote on m in z; z holds the votes of at least Votes persons on m, More is Votes plus 1 and z has fewer than More persons in the role Director for the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z, and restricts and extends it.
A *community bank* is a bank whose bylaws name two roles, *member*, held by every participant, and *director*, held by its first officer and by members; the directors jointly decide the acts of the bank, but the members jointly appoint and remove directors. A member's *credit line* is a number of coins.
Bob is a person, Orga a community bank, Sum, Line, Held, Total, Votes and More numbers.
1. ■ Leave: GO:3 Leave, only if Bob holds exactly 0 Orga-coins. Leave owed nothing: GO:3 Leave, only if Orga holds exactly 0 Bob-coins. Officer leave: GO:3 Officer leave, only if Bob holds exactly 0 Orga-coins. Officer leave owed nothing: GO:3 Officer leave, only if Orga holds exactly 0 Bob-coins.
2. ■ Set credit line: The directors of Orga may set Bob's credit line in Orga at Line, provided Orga is an organisation and Orga has Bob as a participant.
3. ■ Credit: Bob may swap Sum Bob-coins for Sum Orga-coins, provided Orga holds exactly Held Bob-coins, Orga has Bob's credit line at the number Line, Total is Held plus Sum and Total is at most Line.
4. ○ Community currency. A member will accept Orga-coins in payment for their offerings at the prices they post.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: z has y's credit line at the number n = credit_line(z,y,n).
Roles: the directors of z = directors(z), listed by z has y in the role Director and y holds the role Director in z.
x is a director of z: x holds the role Director in z.
y's earlier credit line in z has ended: z has y's credit line at no number.
After g sets y's credit line in z at n: y's earlier credit line in z has ended and z has y's credit line at the number n.
After y swaps n y-coins for n z-coins: no longer y holds n y-coins, z holds n y-coins, no longer z holds n z-coins and y holds n z-coins.
