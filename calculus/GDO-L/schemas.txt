GDO-L: Grassroots Directed Overlay with Labels
GDO-L extends and strengthens GDO.
Vocabulary: x has a w-pointer to y = pointer(x,y,w).
After x adds a w-pointer to y, which requires x does not have a w-pointer to y: x has a w-pointer to y.
After x removes x's w-pointer to y: no longer x has a w-pointer to y.
GDO-L:0 - | A pointer may be labeled by a person, and a pointer labeled by Dave is a *Dave-pointer*. Friends Alice and Bob have a Bob-pointer from Alice to Bob and an Alice-pointer from Bob to Alice.
GDO-L:0 - | Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Carol and Dave possibly one person.
GDO-L:1 ■ Labeled point friend: pointer(Alice,Bob,Dave) <- friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice), pointer(Bob,_,Dave), ~pointer(Alice,Bob,Dave) guarded by Alice.
GDO-L:2 ■ Labeled point transitive: pointer(Alice,Carol,Dave) <- knows(Alice,Carol), pointer(Alice,Bob,Dave), pointer(Bob,Carol,Dave), ~pointer(Alice,Carol,Dave) guarded by Alice possibly Carol=Dave.
GDO-L:2 ■ Labeled point transitive friend: pointer(Alice,Carol,Dave) <- knows(Alice,Carol), friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice), pointer(Bob,Carol,Dave), ~pointer(Alice,Carol,Dave) guarded by Alice possibly Carol=Dave.
GDO-L:3 ■ Labeled unpoint: ~pointer(Alice,Bob,Dave) <- pointer(Alice,Bob,Dave) guarded by Alice.
GDO-L:4 ■ | Friend pointers.
GDO-L:4 ■ Befriend: GSG:1 + pointer(Alice,Bob,Bob), pointer(Bob,Alice,Alice) <-.
GDO-L:4 ■ Unfriend: GSG:2 + ~pointer(Alice,Bob,Bob), ~pointer(Bob,Alice,Alice) <-.
