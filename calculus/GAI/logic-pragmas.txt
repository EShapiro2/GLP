GAI: Accountable AI Agents
GAI instantiates GnW with the guardians of z for those who decide for z, an AI agent for a ward and a principal for a creator, composes it with GFP, and restricts, strengthens and extends the composition (GSC, From Grassroots Contract to Grassroots App, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06231).
The *developer* of an AI agent is the signer of its code.
Alice, Bob and Erin are persons, Alf an AI agent, Proof and Seal attestations; in clause 2 Carol and Dave are AI agents and Erin is possibly Alf or Bob.
1. ○ AI developer and guardian accountability. The developer of Alf will be accountable for Alf's defects. Every guardian of Alf will be accountable for operating it recklessly.
2. ■ AI acts. Enter: GnW:1, and then Alf holds the attestation Proof and Alice holds a record of Alf's attestation Proof. The principal of Alf attests at its creation, signed over Alf's public key, that Alf runs code signed by its developer. Make guardian: GnW:2 Make guardian, only if Alf holds the attestation Proof, and then Bob holds a record of Alf's attestation Proof. Ward acquaint: GnW:3 Ward acquaint, only if Alf holds the attestation Proof, and then Bob holds a record of Alf's attestation Proof. Ward befriend: GnW:3 Ward befriend, only if Alf holds the attestation Proof. Ward end: GnW:3 Ward end, only if Alf holds the attestation Proof. Wards acquaint: GnW:4 Wards acquaint, only if Carol holds the attestation Proof and Dave holds the attestation Seal, and then Carol holds a record of Dave's attestation Seal and Dave holds a record of Carol's attestation Proof. Wards befriend: GnW:4 Wards befriend, only if Carol holds the attestation Proof and Dave holds the attestation Seal. Wards of one guardian befriend: GnW:4 Wards of one guardian befriend, only if Carol holds the attestation Proof and Dave holds the attestation Seal. Sign: GFP:1, only if Alice is a person. Forward: GFP:2, only if Alice is a person. Agent sign: Alf may create an item by signing it, provided Alf is an AI agent and Alf holds the attestation Proof. Agent forward: Alf may forward an item of Erin to Bob, provided Alf is an AI agent, Alf holds the attestation Proof, Alf holds an item of Erin and Alf and Bob are friends. Every act of Alf requires this attestation and names its developer and its principal.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: z holds the attestation t = attestation(z,t); y holds a record of z's attestation t = attestation_record(y,z,t).
Roles: the guardians of z = guardians(z), listed by z has y as a guardian, y is a guardian of z and y holds a record of z's attestation Proof.
