GAI: Accountable AI Agents
1. ■ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity, and then Alice is a person.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other, Alice knows Carol, Alice is a person, Bob is a person and Carol is a person.
4. ■ Befriend.  Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other, Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
5. ■ Person unfriend.  Alice may end her friendship with Bob, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
6. ■ Enter.  Alice may create Carol, and Carol must consent, provided Alice is a person, and then Carol holds the attestation Proof and Alice holds a record of Carol's attestation Proof.
7. ■ Make guardian.  The guardians of Carol may make Bob a guardian of Carol, with the consent of Bob, provided Carol is an AI agent, Carol is not retired, Bob is a person and Carol holds the attestation Proof, and then Bob holds a record of Carol's attestation Proof.
8. ■ Resign.  Bob may end his guardianship of Carol.
9. ■ End guardian.  The guardians of Carol may end Bob's guardianship of Carol.
10. ■ Guardian unfriend.  Bob may end his friendship with his AI agent Carol.
11. ■ Ward acquaint.  Carol and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Carol is an AI agent, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Bob is a person and Carol holds the attestation Proof, and then Bob holds a record of Carol's attestation Proof.
12. ■ Ward befriend.  Carol and Bob may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Carol is an AI agent, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Bob is a person, Carol and Bob know each other, Alice and Bob are friends and Carol holds the attestation Proof.
13. ■ Ward end.  Carol may end her friendship with Bob, provided Bob is not a guardian of Carol and Carol holds the attestation Proof.
14. ■ Unfriend.  Bob may end his friendship with Carol, provided Bob is not a guardian of Carol.
15. ■ Ward unfriend.  Alice may end the friendship of Carol and Bob, provided Carol has Alice as a guardian and Bob is not a guardian of Carol.
16. ■ Wards acquaint.  Carol and Dave may jointly come to know each other, provided Carol is an AI agent, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Dave is an AI agent, Dave has Bob as a guardian, Carol holds the attestation Proof and Dave holds the attestation Seal, and then Carol holds a record of Dave's attestation Seal and Dave holds a record of Carol's attestation Proof.
17. ■ Wards befriend.  Carol and Dave may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice and Bob, provided Carol is an AI agent, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Dave is an AI agent, Dave is not retired, Dave has Bob as a guardian, Carol and Dave know each other, Alice knows Dave, Alice and Bob are friends, Carol holds the attestation Proof and Dave holds the attestation Seal.
18. ■ Wards of one guardian befriend.  Carol and Dave may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Carol is an AI agent, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Dave is an AI agent, Dave is not retired, Dave has Alice as a guardian, Carol and Dave know each other, Carol holds the attestation Proof and Dave holds the attestation Seal.
19. ■ Retire.  The guardians of Carol may retire Carol.
20. ■ Sign.  Alice may create Item by signing it, provided Alice is a person.
21. ■ Forward.  Alice may forward Item to Bob, adding her signature to the signatures Item carries, provided Alice holds the item Item, Alice and Bob are friends and Alice is a person.
22. ■ Agent sign.  Alf may create Item by signing it, provided Alf is an AI agent and Alf holds the attestation Proof.
23. ■ Agent forward.  Alf may forward Item to Bob, adding its signature to the signatures Item carries, provided Alf is an AI agent, Alf holds the attestation Proof, Alf holds the item Item and Alf and Bob are friends.
