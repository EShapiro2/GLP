GAI: Accountable AI Agents
GAI instantiates GnW with the guardians deciding jointly, an AI agent for a ward and a principal for a creator, composes it with GFP, and restricts, strengthens and extends the composition (GSC, From Grassroots Contract to Grassroots App, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06231).
The *developer* of an AI agent is the signer of its code.
Alice is a person and Alf an AI agent.
1. ○ AI developer and guardian accountability. The developer of Alf will be accountable for Alf's defects. Every guardian of Alf will be accountable for operating it recklessly.
2. ■ AI acts. Enter: The principal of Alf attests at its creation (GnW:1), signed over Alf's public key, that Alf runs code signed by its developer. Every act of Alf requires this attestation and names its developer and its principal.
