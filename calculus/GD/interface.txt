GD: Grassroots Distrust
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ○ Befriend.  Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other.
5. ■ Unfriend.  Alice may end her friendship with Bob.
6. ■ Distrust.  Alice may declare distrust of Carol, provided Alice knows Carol and Alice and Carol are not friends.
7. ■ Distrust friend.  Alice may declare distrust of her friend Carol, provided Alice knows Carol.
8. ■ Propagate.  Alice may declare distrust of Carol, provided Alice does not know Carol, Alice and Bob are friends and Bob has declared distrust of Carol.
9. ■ Cease.  Alice may withdraw her declaration of distrust of Carol.
