GRA: Grassroots Ride Auction
GRA extends GDO-LB.
A *pickup delay* is measured from the award. The *terms* of a call, a parameter of GRA, state a maximum pickup delay, select a bidder, set the fare and the deposit, and say how these are released.
Alice is a rider, Bob a driver and Carol a person, Trip a call. A person can be either a rider or a driver but not both.
1. ■ Call. Call: Alice may call for bids on a ride as Trip, provided Alice is a rider and Alice does not have a ride. Alice calls for bids from the persons Alice points to, stating the pickup point, the destination, the bidding period and the terms. Send call: Alice's call Trip goes to Bob, provided Alice has the call Trip open and Alice points to Bob. Forward call: Bob forwards Alice's call Trip to Carol, provided Bob has received Alice's call Trip from Alice and Bob points to Carol.
2. ■ Bid: Bob may bid a fare and a pickup delay on Alice's call Trip, provided Bob is a driver, Bob has received Alice's call Trip and the bidding period of Alice's call Trip has not ended. Alice may not open a bid before then.
3. ■ Award. Award: Alice may award the ride of her call Trip to Bob, provided the bidding period of Alice's call Trip has ended, Bob has a bid on Alice's call Trip and the terms of Alice's call Trip select Bob. The ride is awarded at the fare the terms set, and Alice's fare and the deposit the terms set for Bob are then locked. Close: Alice may close her call Trip, provided the terms of Alice's call Trip select no bidder.
4. ■ Pickup: The pickup of Alice and Bob is recorded, provided Alice and Bob have a ride and Alice and Bob are both at the pickup point.
5. ■ Arrive: The fare and the deposit are released to Alice and to Bob as the terms say, provided Alice and Bob are both at the destination.
6. ■ Cancel: Alice may cancel her ride with Bob, provided no pickup of Alice's ride is recorded within the maximum pickup delay. The fare and the deposit are then released to Alice and to Bob as the terms say.
7. ■ Withdraw: Alice may withdraw her call Trip, provided the bidding period of Alice's call Trip has not ended.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x is a rider = rider(x); x is a driver = driver(x); x has the call c open = open_call(x,c); x has a ride = ride(x); x rides with y = rides_with(x,y); x has a release of the ride with y = released(x,y); y has received x's call c = call(y,x,c); y has received x's call c from x = direct_call(y,x,c); x has sent x's call c to y = sent_call(x,y,c); y has forwarded x's call c to z = forwarded(y,x,z,c); y has a bid on x's call c = bid(y,x,c); the bidding period of x's call c has ended = period_ended(x,c); the terms of x's call c select y = selects(x,y,c); the terms of x's call c select no bidder = selects_none(x,c); x's fare is locked = fare_locked(x); x's deposit is locked = deposit_locked(x); x is at the pickup point with y = at_pickup(x,y); x is at the destination with y = at_destination(x,y); x has a pickup recorded with y = pickup(x,y); no pickup of x's ride is recorded within the maximum pickup delay = no_pickup(x).
x and y have a ride: x has a ride, y has a ride, x rides with y and y rides with x.
x and y are both at the pickup point: x is at the pickup point with y and y is at the pickup point with x.
x and y are both at the destination: x is at the destination with y and y is at the destination with x.
After x calls for bids on a ride as c, which requires x has no call open: x has the call c open.
After x's call c goes to y, which requires y has not received x's call c: x has sent x's call c to y, y has received x's call c and y has received x's call c from x.
After y forwards x's call c to z, which requires y has not forwarded x's call c to z: y has forwarded x's call c to z and z has received x's call c.
After y bids a fare and a pickup delay on x's call c: y has a bid on x's call c.
After x awards the ride of x's call c to y, which requires y does not have a ride: no longer x has the call c open, x and y have a ride, x's fare is locked and y's deposit is locked.
After x closes x's call c: no longer x has the call c open.
After the pickup of x and y is recorded, which requires x does not have a pickup recorded with y and y does not have a pickup recorded with x: x has a pickup recorded with y and y has a pickup recorded with x.
After the fare and the deposit are released to x and to y as the terms say: no longer x's fare is locked, no longer y's deposit is locked, no longer x and y have a ride, x has a pickup recorded with no one, y has a pickup recorded with no one, x has a release of the ride with y and y has a release of the ride with x.
After x cancels x's ride with y: no longer x's fare is locked, no longer y's deposit is locked, no longer x and y have a ride, x has a pickup recorded with no one, y has a pickup recorded with no one, x has a release of the ride with y and y has a release of the ride with x.
After x withdraws x's call c: no longer x has the call c open.
Environment: x is a rider; x is a driver; the bidding period of x's call c has ended; x is at the pickup point with y; x is at the destination with y; no pickup of x's ride is recorded within the maximum pickup delay.
Parameters: the terms of x's call c select y; the terms of x's call c select no bidder.
