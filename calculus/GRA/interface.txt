GRA: Grassroots Ride Auction
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ○ Befriend.  Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other.
5. ■ Unfriend.  Alice may end her friendship with Bob.
6. ■ Point friend.  Alice may point to Bob, provided Alice and Bob are friends and either Alice points to fewer than N persons and Bob is closest to Alice among all of Alice's unpointed friends, or Bob is closer to Alice by more than E than some person pointed to by Alice.
7. ■ Point transitive.  Alice may point to Carol, provided Alice knows Carol, Alice points to Bob, Bob points to Carol and Carol is closer to Alice by more than E than some person pointed to by Alice.
8. ■ Unpoint.  Alice may cease pointing to Bob, provided Alice points to more than N persons and Bob is furthest from Alice among the persons Alice points to.
9. ■ Call.  Alice may call for bids on a ride as Trip, provided Alice does not have a ride.
10. ■ Send call.  Alice's call Trip goes to Bob, provided Alice has the call Trip open and Alice points to Bob.
11. ■ Forward call.  Bob forwards Alice's call Trip to Carol, provided Bob has received Alice's call Trip and Bob points to Carol.
12. ■ Bid.  Bob may bid a fare and a pickup delay on Alice's call Trip, provided Bob has received Alice's call Trip and the bidding period of Alice's call Trip has not ended.
13. ■ Award.  Alice may award the ride of her call Trip to Bob, provided the bidding period of Alice's call Trip has ended, Bob has a bid on Alice's call Trip and the terms of Alice's call Trip select Bob.
14. ■ Close.  Alice may close her call Trip, provided the terms of Alice's call Trip select no bidder.
15. ■ Pickup.  The pickup of Alice and Bob is recorded, provided Alice and Bob are both at the pickup point.
16. ■ Arrive.  The fare and the deposit are released to Alice and to Bob as the terms say, provided Alice and Bob are both at the destination.
17. ■ Cancel.  The fare and the deposit are released to Alice and to Bob as the terms say, with the consent of Alice, provided no pickup of Alice's ride is recorded within the maximum pickup delay.
18. ■ Withdraw.  Alice may close her call Trip, provided the bidding period of Alice's call Trip has not ended.
