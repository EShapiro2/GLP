MCS: Mutual Credit Society
MCS instantiates GBank with mutual credit society bylaws, and restricts and extends it.
A *mutual credit society* is a bank whose bylaws name one role, *member*, held by every participant; the members jointly decide its acts. A member is *in the red* by their coins in circulation less the other persons' coins they hold.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a mutual credit society.
1. ■ Leave: GO:3 Leave, only if the Orga-coins Bob holds and the Bob-coins Orga holds have been redeemed against each other. Officer leave: GO:3 Officer leave, only if the Orga-coins Bob holds and the Bob-coins Orga holds have been redeemed against each other. Expel: GO:3 Expel, only if the Orga-coins Bob holds and the Bob-coins Orga holds have been redeemed against each other. Officer expel: GO:3 Officer expel, only if the Orga-coins Bob holds and the Bob-coins Orga holds have been redeemed against each other.
2. ■ Redeem: Bob may redeem with Orga an Alice-coin against an Orga-coin, provided Bob is a member of Orga and Alice is a member of Orga.
3. ■ Credit limit. Swap: DGC:2, only if every member of Orga is then in the red by at most 1000 coins. Pay: GC:3, only if every member of Orga is then in the red by at most 1000 coins. Pay: DGC:4, only if every member of Orga is then in the red by at most 1000 coins.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: the z-coins y holds and the y-coins z holds have been redeemed against each other = redeemed(z,y); every member of z is then in the red by at most 1000 coins, for x = credit_limit(x,z).
y is a member of z: y is a participant of z.
After y redeems with z an x-coin against a z-coin: no longer y holds an x-coin, z holds an x-coin, no longer z holds a z-coin and y holds a z-coin.
