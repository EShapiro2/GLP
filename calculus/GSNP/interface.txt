7. ■ Create.  Alice may create Grupo.
8. ■ Close.  Alice may close Grupo by leaving it.
9. ■ Join.  Alice may add Bob to Grupo, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo and Alice and Bob are friends.
10. ■ Remove.  Alice may end Bob's membership in Grupo, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo.
11. ■ Leave.  Bob may end his membership in Alice's Grupo.
12. ■ Post.  Bob may forward Text to the group Grupo of Alice, adding his signature to the signatures Text carries, provided Bob is a member of the group Grupo of Alice, Alice does not have the post Text of Bob in Grupo and Bob holds the item Text.

17. ■ End guardian.  The guardians of Carol may end Bob's guardianship of Carol.
18. ■ Guardian unfriend.  Bob may end his friendship with his AI agent Carol, and then Bob is a member of no group of Carol, Carol is a member of no group of Bob, Bob has Carol as a member of no group and Carol has Bob as a member of no group.
19. ■ Ward acquaint.  Carol and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Carol is an AI agent, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Bob is a person and Carol holds the attestation Proof, and then Bob holds a record of Carol's attestation Proof.
