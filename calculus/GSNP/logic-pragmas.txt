GSNP: Grassroots Social Network with Provenance
GSNP instantiates GSN with y holds the item t signed by x for y has the message t from x; z holds the item t signed by y for z has the message t of x in g delivered by y, composes it with GAI (GSC, From Grassroots Contract to Grassroots App, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06231), and extends the composition.
Alf is an AI agent, Alice and Bob are persons, Grupo a group, Text a message, Item an item.
1. ■ Message: GSN:1, only if Alice holds the item Text. Post: GSN:4 Post, only if Bob holds the item Text. Agent message: Alf may send Text to Bob, provided Alf holds the item Text and Alf and Bob are friends. Guardian unfriend: GnW:2 Guardian unfriend, and then Bob is a member of no group of Alf, Alf is a member of no group of Bob, Bob has Alf as a member of no group and Alf has Bob as a member of no group. Ward end: GnW:3 Ward end, and then Alf is a member of no group of Bob, Bob is a member of no group of Alf, Alf has Bob as a member of no group and Bob has Alf as a member of no group. Unfriend: GnW:3 Unfriend, and then Bob is a member of no group of Alf, Alf is a member of no group of Bob, Bob has Alf as a member of no group and Alf has Bob as a member of no group. Ward unfriend: GnW:3 Ward unfriend, and then Alf is a member of no group of Bob, Bob is a member of no group of Alf, Alf has Bob as a member of no group and Bob has Alf as a member of no group.
2. ■ Post forward: Bob may forward Item to the group Grupo of Alice, adding his signature to the signatures Item carries, provided Bob is a member of the group Grupo of Alice, Bob is not the leader of Grupo and Bob holds the item Item.
Logic pragmas.
After x sends t to y: y holds the item t signed by x.
After x forwards t to the group g of y, adding x's signature to the signatures t carries: y has the post t of x in g.
