GSNP: Grassroots Social Network with Provenance
Of GKK: An *identity* is a unique key pair. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. A party to a contract must have an identity.
Of GSG: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts. Alice and Bob are persons.
Of GSN: A *message*, a parameter of GSN, is what a person sends. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Grupo a group, Text a message, Bob and Carol possibly Alice or one person.
Of GnW: Bob *trusts* Carol, an AI agent of Alice, when he trusts Alice. The *decision rule*, a parameter of GnW, says who decides for an AI agent and how. Alice, Bob and Erin are persons, Carol and Dave AI agents; in clause 4 Alice and Bob are possibly one person.
Of GFP: The *author* of an item is its first signer. Alice and Bob are persons, Item an item.
Of GAI: The *developer* of an AI agent is the signer of its code. Alice and Bob are persons, Alf an AI agent, Proof and Seal attestations, Item an item; in clause 2 Carol and Dave are AI agents.
Of GSNP: Alf is an AI agent, Alice and Bob are persons, Grupo a group, Text a message, Item an item.
GKK:1, GnW:3. ■ Establish: Alice may establish an identity, and then Alice is a person. Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
GKK:2, GnW:3. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
GKK:3, GnW:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other, Alice knows Carol, Alice is a person, Bob is a person and Carol is a person.
GSG:1, GnW:3. ■ Befriend: Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other, Alice is a person and Bob is a person. Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
GSG:2, GSN:3, GnW:3. ■ Unfriend: Alice may end Bob's membership in _, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person, and then no longer Alice counts Bob as a friend, no longer Bob counts Alice as a friend, Alice is a member of no group of Bob and Bob has Alice as a member of no group.
GSG:3. ○ Loss of Trust. Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
GSN:1, GSNP:1. ■ Message: Alice may send Text to Bob, provided Alice and Bob are friends and Alice holds the item Text.
GSN:2. ■ Group. Create: Alice may create Grupo. Alice is then its leader and first member. Close: Alice may close Grupo by leaving it.
GSN:3. ■ Joining and Leaving. Join: Alice may add Bob to Grupo, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo and Alice and Bob are friends. Remove: Alice may end Bob's membership in Grupo, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo. Leave: Bob may end his membership in Alice's Grupo.
GSN:4. ■ Group messaging.
GSN:4, GSNP:1. ■ Post: Bob may forward Text to the group Grupo of Alice, adding his signature to the signatures Text carries, provided Bob is a member of the group Grupo of Alice, Alice does not have the post Text of Bob in Grupo and Bob holds the item Text.
GSN:4. ■ Deliver: Alice delivers Text of Bob in Grupo to Carol, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo, Alice has the post Text of Bob in Grupo, Alice has Bob as a member of the group Grupo and Alice has Carol as a member of the group Grupo.
GnW:1, GAI:2. ■ Enter: Alice may create Carol, and Carol must consent, provided Alice is a person, and then Carol holds the attestation Proof and Alice holds a record of Carol's attestation Proof. Alice chooses Carol's key pair.
GnW:2. ■ Guardian decisions.
GnW:2, GAI:2. ■ Make guardian: The guardians of Carol may make Bob a guardian of Carol, with the consent of Bob, provided Carol is an AI agent, Carol is not retired, Bob is a person and Carol holds the attestation Proof, and then Bob holds a record of Carol's attestation Proof.
GnW:2. ■ Resign: Bob may end his guardianship of Carol. End guardian: The guardians of Carol may end Bob's guardianship of Carol.
GnW:2, GSNP:1. ■ Guardian unfriend: Bob may end his friendship with his AI agent Carol, and then Bob is a member of no group of Carol, Carol is a member of no group of Bob, Bob has Carol as a member of no group and Carol has Bob as a member of no group. Carol may not end a friendship with her guardian.
GnW:3. ■ Ward and person.
GnW:3, GAI:2. ■ Ward acquaint: Carol and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Carol is an AI agent, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Bob is a person and Carol holds the attestation Proof, and then Bob holds a record of Carol's attestation Proof. Ward befriend: Carol and Bob may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Carol is an AI agent, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Bob is a person, Carol and Bob know each other, Alice and Bob are friends and Carol holds the attestation Proof.
GnW:3, GAI:2, GSNP:1. ■ Ward end: Carol may end Bob's membership in _, provided Bob is not a guardian of Carol and Carol holds the attestation Proof, and then no longer Carol counts Bob as a friend, no longer Bob counts Carol as a friend, Carol is a member of no group of Bob and Bob has Carol as a member of no group.
GnW:3, GSNP:1. ■ Unfriend: Bob may end Carol's membership in _, provided Bob is not a guardian of Carol, and then no longer Bob counts Carol as a friend, no longer Carol counts Bob as a friend, Bob is a member of no group of Carol and Carol has Bob as a member of no group. Ward unfriend: Alice may end the friendship of Carol and Bob, provided Carol has Alice as a guardian and Bob is not a guardian of Carol, and then Carol is a member of no group of Bob, Bob is a member of no group of Carol, Carol has Bob as a member of no group and Bob has Carol as a member of no group.
GnW:4. ■ Wards.
GnW:4, GAI:2. ■ Wards acquaint: Carol and Dave may jointly come to know each other, provided Carol is an AI agent, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Dave is an AI agent, Dave has Bob as a guardian, Carol holds the attestation Proof and Dave holds the attestation Seal, and then Carol holds a record of Dave's attestation Seal and Dave holds a record of Carol's attestation Proof. Wards of one guardian befriend: Carol and Dave may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Carol is an AI agent, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Dave is an AI agent, Dave is not retired, Dave has Alice as a guardian, Carol and Dave know each other, Carol holds the attestation Proof and Dave holds the attestation Seal.
GnW:5. ■ Retire: The guardians of Carol may retire Carol. Carol can then take no act.
