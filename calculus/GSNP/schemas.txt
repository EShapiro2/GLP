GSNP: Grassroots Social Network with Provenance
GSNP instantiates GSN with item(y,t@x) for message(y,x,t); item(z,t@y) for group_message(z,x,t,g,y), composes it with GAI (GSC, From Grassroots Contract to Grassroots App, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06231), and extends the composition.
After x sends t to y: y holds the item t signed by x.
After x forwards t to the group g of y, adding x's signature to the signatures t carries: y has the post t of x in g.
GSNP:0 - | Alf is an AI agent, Alice and Bob are persons, Grupo a group, Text a message, Item an item.
GSNP:1 ■ Message: GSN:1 <- item(Alice,Text).
GSNP:1 ■ Post: GSN:4 Post <- item(Bob,Text).
GSNP:1 ■ Agent message: item(Bob,Text@Alf) <- item(Alf,Text), friend(Alf,Bob), friend(Bob,Alf) guarded by Alf.
