FDAO: Funded DAO
FDAO extends DAO and GC.
1. ■ Fund: A member of a DAO may give it coins they hold; its steward keeps them apart as the DAO's.
2. ■ Grant: The steward may grant the proposer of a passed proposal, once per proposal, any amount of the DAO's coins of one issuer.
3. ○ Amount. The steward will grant a passed proposal the amount it asks for, as far as the DAO's coins allow.
