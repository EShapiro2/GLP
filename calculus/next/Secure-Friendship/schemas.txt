Secure Friendship
Secure Friendship extends Friendship.
Vocabulary: x has z as an identity custodian = custodian(x,z); z is an identity custodian of x = custodian_of(z,x); x has a fixed identity record = fixed(x); x has the threshold n = threshold(x,n); z records the friendship of x and y = records_friend(z,x,y); y records z as an identity custodian of x = records_custodian(y,x,z); y keeps a checkpoint of x = checkpoint(y,x); x has suffered a state loss = state_loss(x); x has recovered records from y = recovered(x,y); z has been asked to vouch that w is x = asked(z,w,x); z has vouched that w is x = vouched(z,w,x); at least the threshold of the identity custodians y records for x have vouched that w is x = threshold_vouched(y,x,w); y has accepted w in place of x on the vouch of c = accepted(y,w,x,c).
After x names z x's identity custodian, which requires x does not have z as an identity custodian: x has z as an identity custodian and z is an identity custodian of x.
After x fixes x's identity record with the threshold n, which requires x does not have a fixed identity record: x has a fixed identity record and x has the threshold n.
After x tells z of the friendship of x and y, which requires z does not record the friendship of x and y: z records the friendship of x and y.
After x tells z of the end of the friendship of x and y: no longer z records the friendship of x and y.
After x sends y the identity custodian z, which requires y does not record z as an identity custodian of x: y records z as an identity custodian of x.
After x sends y a checkpoint of x's friends and x's identity record: y keeps a checkpoint of x.
After y restores the records of x, which requires x does not count y as a friend: x counts y as a friend and x has recovered records from y.
After w asks z to vouch that w is x, which requires z has not been asked to vouch that w is x: z has been asked to vouch that w is x.
After z vouches that w is x, which requires z has not vouched that w is x: z has vouched that w is x.
After y accepts w in place of x on the vouch of c, which requires y has not accepted w in place of x on the vouch of anyone: no longer y counts x as a friend, y counts w as a friend, w counts y as a friend and y has accepted w in place of x on the vouch of c.
After y accepts w as an identity custodian in place of x: no longer y has x as an identity custodian, no longer x is an identity custodian of y, y has w as an identity custodian and w is an identity custodian of y.
Secure Friendship:0 - | The *identity record* of Alice is her *identity custodians*, persons she names, and her *threshold*, a share of them greater than half. The *records* of Alice are her friends and, for each friend, the friends of that friend. Alice suffers a *state loss* when her machine loses some or all of its state and she keeps her private key, and an *identity loss* when she loses her private key.
Secure Friendship:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Alicia a new identity of Alice, Share and Double numbers.
Secure Friendship:0 - | Environment: x has suffered a state loss; at least the threshold of the identity custodians y records for x have vouched that w is x.
Secure Friendship:1 ■ Name custodian: custodian(Alice,Carol), custodian_of(Carol,Alice) <- knows(Alice,Carol), ~fixed(Alice), ~custodian(Alice,Carol) guarded by Alice.
Secure Friendship:1 ■ Identity record: fixed(Alice), threshold(Alice,Share) <- custodian(Alice,>=Share), product(Double,Share,2), custodian(Alice,<Double), ~fixed(Alice) guarded by Alice.
Secure Friendship:1 ■ | It then changes only by the replacement of a custodian's lost identity (Secure Friendship:5).
Secure Friendship:2 ■ | Friendship updates.
Secure Friendship:2 ■ Tell: records_friend(Carol,Alice,Bob) <- friend(Alice,Carol), friend(Carol,Alice), friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice), ~records_friend(Carol,Alice,Bob).
Secure Friendship:2 ■ Tell end: ~records_friend(Carol,Alice,Bob) <- friend(Alice,Carol), friend(Carol,Alice), ~friend(Alice,Bob), ~friend(Bob,Alice), records_friend(Carol,Alice,Bob).
Secure Friendship:2 ■ Send record: records_custodian(Bob,Alice,Carol) <- friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice), fixed(Alice), custodian(Alice,Carol), ~records_custodian(Bob,Alice,Carol).
Secure Friendship:3 ■ Checkpoint: checkpoint(Bob,Alice) <- friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice).
Secure Friendship:3 ■ | Bob keeps it.
