GSNW: Grassroots Social Network with Wards
GSNW extends, restricts and strengthens GSN and GnW.
Alice is a person, Carol a ward.
1. ■ A ward's messages. Ward message: Carol may send a message to a friend (GSN:1). Ward post: Carol may send a message to a group (GSN:4).
2. ■ A ward's group. Ward create: Carol may create a group (GSN:2). Ward close: Carol may close it (GSN:2). Guardian close: Alice, a guardian of Carol, may close it.
3. ■ Ward join: Carol may join a group (GSN:3) only with the consent of Alice, a guardian of Carol.
