Encounters
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity once.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ■ Open place.  Alice may declare the place she is in open.
5. ■ Close place.  Alice may close it.
6. ■ Encounter.  Alice and Bob, nearby each other, who have both declared the place open, come to know each other, and Alice must consent, and Bob must consent.
