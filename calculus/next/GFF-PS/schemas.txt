GFF-PS: Path-Shortening GFF
GFF-PS extends GFF.
After x replaces a w-pointer to y by a w-pointer to z, which requires x does not have a w-pointer to z: no longer x has a w-pointer to y and x has a w-pointer to z.
GFF-PS:0 - | Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Carol and Dave possibly one person.
GFF-PS:1 ■ Shorten: ~pointer(Alice,Bob,Dave), pointer(Alice,Carol,Dave) <- knows(Alice,Carol), knows(Carol,Alice), pointer(Bob,Carol,Dave), ~pointer(Alice,Carol,Dave), pointer(Alice,Bob,Dave) possibly Carol=Dave.
