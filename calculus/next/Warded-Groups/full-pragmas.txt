Warded Groups
Of Knowledge: An *identity* is a unique cryptographic key pair. A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of Friendship: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts. Alice and Bob are persons.
Of Groups: A *message*, a parameter of Groups, is what a person sends. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Grupo a group, Text a message, Bob and Carol possibly Alice or one person.
Of Guardians: Bob *trusts* Carol, a ward of Alice, when he trusts Alice. The *decision rule*, a parameter of Guardians, says who decides for a ward and how. A ward takes an act of a party by those who decide for the ward. Alice and Bob are persons, Carol and Dave wards; in clause 4 Alice and Bob are possibly one person. Default: any guardian of z for those who decide for z.
Of Warded Groups: Alice and Bob are persons, Carol and Dave wards, Grupo a group, Text a message; in clause 1 Carol is possibly Alice.
