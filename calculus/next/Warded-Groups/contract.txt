Warded Groups
Warded Groups extends, restricts and strengthens Groups and Guardians.
Alice is a person, Carol a ward.
1. ■ A ward's messages. Ward message: Carol may send a message to a friend (Groups:1). Ward post: Carol may send a message to a group (Groups:4).
2. ■ A ward's group. Ward create: Carol may create a group (Groups:2). Ward close: Carol may close it (Groups:2). Guardian close: Alice, a guardian of Carol, may close it.
3. ■ Ward join: Carol may join a group (Groups:3) only with the consent of Alice, a guardian of Carol.
