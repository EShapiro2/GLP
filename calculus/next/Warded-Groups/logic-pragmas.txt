Warded Groups
Warded Groups extends, restricts and strengthens Groups and Guardians.
Alice and Bob are persons, Carol and Dave wards, Grupo a group, Text a message; in clause 1 Carol is possibly Alice.
1. ■ A ward's messages. Message: Groups:1, only if Alice is a person. Post: Groups:4 Post, only if Bob is a person. Post own: Groups:4 Post own, only if Alice is a person. Ward message: Carol may send Text to Bob, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired and Carol and Bob are friends. Ward post: Carol may send Text to the group Grupo of Alice, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired and Carol is a member of the group Grupo of Alice. Ward post own: Carol may send Text to the group Grupo of Carol, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired and Carol is a member of the group Grupo of Carol.
2. ■ A ward's group. Create: Groups:2 Create, only if Alice is a person. Close: Groups:2 Close, only if Alice is a person. Ward create: Carol may create Grupo, provided Carol is a ward and Carol is not retired. Ward close: Carol may close Grupo by leaving it, provided Carol is a ward and Carol is not retired. Guardian close: Alice may close the group Grupo of Carol, provided Alice is a guardian of Carol.
3. ■ Join: Groups:3 Join, only if Bob is a person. Ward join: Bob may add Carol to Grupo, with the consent of Carol and Alice, provided Bob is the leader of Grupo, Bob is a person, Bob and Carol are friends, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Alice is a guardian of Carol and Alice and Bob are friends. Wards join: Carol may add Dave to Grupo, with the consent of Dave, Alice and Bob, provided Carol is the leader of Grupo, Carol is a ward, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Alice is a guardian of Carol, Carol and Dave are friends, Dave is a ward, Dave has Bob as a guardian, Bob is a guardian of Dave, Alice knows Dave and Alice and Bob are friends. Wards of one guardian join: Carol may add Dave to Grupo, with the consent of Dave and Alice, provided Carol is the leader of Grupo, Carol is a ward, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Alice is a guardian of Carol, Carol and Dave are friends, Dave is a ward, Dave has Alice as a guardian and Alice is a guardian of Dave. Ward withdraw: Alice may withdraw Carol from Bob's Grupo, provided Carol has Alice as a guardian and Alice is a guardian of Carol. Ward add: Carol may add Bob to Grupo, with the consent of Bob and Alice, provided Carol is the leader of Grupo, Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Alice is a guardian of Carol, Carol and Bob are friends and Alice and Bob are friends. Ward leave: Carol may end her membership in Alice's Grupo, provided Carol is a ward and Carol is not retired. Ward remove: Carol may end Bob's membership in Grupo, provided Carol is the leader of Grupo, Carol is a ward and Carol is not retired. Guardian unfriend: Guardians:2 Guardian unfriend, and then Bob is a member of no group of Carol, Carol is a member of no group of Bob, Bob has Carol as a member of no group and Carol has Bob as a member of no group. Ward end: Guardians:3 Ward end, and then Carol is a member of no group of Bob, Bob is a member of no group of Carol, Carol has Bob as a member of no group and Bob has Carol as a member of no group. Unfriend: Guardians:3 Unfriend, and then Bob is a member of no group of Carol, Carol is a member of no group of Bob, Bob has Carol as a member of no group and Carol has Bob as a member of no group. Ward unfriend: Guardians:3 Ward unfriend, and then Carol is a member of no group of Bob, Bob is a member of no group of Carol, Carol has Bob as a member of no group and Bob has Carol as a member of no group. Retire: Guardians:5 Retire, and then Carol is a member of no group of anyone and no one has Carol as a member of any group.
Logic pragmas.
After w withdraws y from x's g: no longer y is a member of the group g of x and no longer x has y as a member of the group g.
After y closes the group g of z: no longer z is the leader of g, z has no one as a member of the group g and no one is a member of the group g of z.
