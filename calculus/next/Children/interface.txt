Children
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity once.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ■ Befriend.  Alice and Bob, who trust each other and are not friends, may jointly become friends.
5. ■ Person unfriend.  Alice may end her friendship with Bob.
6. ■ Message.  Alice may send a message to her friend Bob.
7. ■ Create.  Alice may create a group, of which she is the leader and first member.
8. ■ Close.  Alice may close the group by leaving it.
9. ■ Join.  Alice, the leader of a group, may add her friend Bob to it, with the consent of Bob.
10. ■ Remove.  Either Alice or Bob may then end Bob's membership in the group, which also ends when Alice and Bob cease to be friends.
11. ■ Leave.  Either Alice or Bob may then end Bob's membership in the group, which also ends when Alice and Bob cease to be friends.
12. ■ Post.  A member of a group may send a message to the group.
13. ■ Post own.  A member of a group may send a message to the group.
14. ■ Deliver.  Its leader delivers it to every current member.
15. ■ Deliver leader.  Its leader delivers it to every current member.
16. ◆ Enter.  Alice may create a child, Carol, and establish Carol's identity by choosing Carol's key pair, only if Alice is a legal parent of the minor who operates Carol, and Carol must consent.
17. ■ Make parent.  Those who decide for Carol may make Bob, a friend of a parent of Carol, a parent and a friend of Carol, with the consent of Bob.
18. ■ Resign.  Bob may end his guardianship of Carol.
19. ■ End parent.  Those who decide for Carol, other than Bob, may end Bob's guardianship.
20. ■ Parent unfriend.  Bob may end his friendship with Carol, which ends his guardianship.
21. ■ Child acquaint.  Carol, a child of Alice, and Bob may jointly come to know each other, only if Alice knows Bob, and with the consent of Alice.
22. ■ Child befriend.  Carol may enter a relation with Bob with the consent of Bob and Alice, provided Carol and Bob know each other and Alice and Bob are friends.
23. ■ Child end.  Carol, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Carol.
24. ■ Unfriend.  Carol, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Carol.
25. ■ Child unfriend.  Carol, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Carol.
26. ■ Children acquaint.  Carol, a child of Alice, and Dave, a child of Bob, may jointly come to know each other, only if Alice knows Dave and Bob knows Carol, and with the consent of Alice and Bob, who know each other, Alice and Bob possibly one person.
27. ■ Children befriend.  Carol may enter a relation with Dave with the consent of Dave, Alice and Bob, provided Carol and Dave know each other, Alice knows Dave and is a friend of Bob, Alice and Bob possibly one person.
28. ■ Children of one parent befriend.  Carol may enter a relation with Dave with the consent of Dave, Alice and Bob, provided Carol and Dave know each other, Alice knows Dave and is a friend of Bob, Alice and Bob possibly one person.
29. ■ Retire.  Those who decide for Carol may retire Carol; every guardianship and relation of Carol then ends and Carol can take no act.
30. ■ Child message.  Carol may send a message to a friend (Groups:1).
31. ■ Child post.  Carol may send a message to a group (Groups:4).
32. ■ Child post own.  Carol may send a message to a group (Groups:4).
33. ■ Child create.  Carol may create a group (Groups:2), only with the consent of Alice, a parent of Carol.
34. ■ Child close.  Carol may close it (Groups:2).
35. ■ Parent close.  Alice, a parent of Carol, may close it.
36. ■ Child join.  Carol may join a group (Groups:3) only with the consent of Alice, a parent of Carol.
37. ■ Children join.  Carol may join a group (Groups:3) only with the consent of Alice, a parent of Carol.
38. ■ Children of one parent join.  Carol may join a group (Groups:3) only with the consent of Alice, a parent of Carol.
39. ■ Child withdraw.  Carol may join a group (Groups:3) only with the consent of Alice, a parent of Carol.
40. ■ Child leave.  Carol may join a group (Groups:3) only with the consent of Alice, a parent of Carol.
41. ■ Child remove.  Carol may join a group (Groups:3) only with the consent of Alice, a parent of Carol.
42. ○ Of age.  The creator of Carol may declare Carol of age, which ends every guardianship of Carol, and Carol is then a person.
