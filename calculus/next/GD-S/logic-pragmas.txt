GD-S: Separating GD
GD-S restricts GD.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
1. ○ Befriend: GSG:1, only if Alice has not declared distrust of Bob and Bob has not declared distrust of Alice.
2. ○ Taking sides. Alice, whose friend Bob has declared distrust of Carol, will eventually declare distrust of Carol or end her friendship with Bob, unless Bob withdraws the declaration.
