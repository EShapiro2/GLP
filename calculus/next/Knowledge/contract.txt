Knowledge
An *identity* is a unique cryptographic key pair.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity.
1. ○ Establish: Alice may establish an identity once. Alice will make herself known by her public key, sign her acts with her private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
2. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
