AppStore
AppStore composes Programs with Services (GNA, GLP Networking API and Services, in preparation) and extends the composition.
A *store* is a service holding a list of programs. Alice *trusts* a store when she has declared it and has not withdrawn the declaration.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Dave a service.
1. ■ Open store: Carol may make her service Dave a store.
2. ○ Listing. List: Carol may list in Dave a program developed by Bob, after adding her signature to its signatures, provided she trusts Bob as a developer. Delist: Carol may delist it. Carol will delist it if she ceases to trust Bob as a developer.
3. ■ Store trust. Trust store: Alice may declare trust in Dave, Carol being known to her. Untrust store: Alice may withdraw the declaration.
4. ■ Install from store: Alice may install a program listed in a store she trusts.
