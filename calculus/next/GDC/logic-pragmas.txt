GDC: Grassroots Denominated Coins
GDC extends and restricts GC.
*Dollar* names one denomination, and a *Dollar-denominated Alice-coin* is an Alice-coin issued as Alice's debt of one Dollar.
Alice and Bob are parties, Dollar a fiat currency, Sum a number.
1. ■ First issue: Alice may issue Sum Dollar-denominated Alice-coins, provided Alice has issued coins of no denomination. Issue: GN:1 Issue, only if Alice has issued coins of the denomination Dollar.
2. ○ Denomination. A Dollar-denominated Alice-coin is due now; Alice will redeem it on presentation, by a Dollar-denominated coin the bearer accepts or by one Dollar.
3. ○ Pricing. Alice will accept Dollar-denominated Alice-coins in payment for her offerings at one Dollar each.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x has issued coins of the denomination f = issued_in(x,f).
After x issues n f-denominated x-coins: x holds n x-coins and x has issued coins of the denomination f.
