SPLLC: Single-Person Limited-Liability Company
SPLLC instantiates GCorp with single-person limited-liability company bylaws; officer(x,z) for proposer(x,z,m); officer(x,z) for taker(x,z,m); officer(x,z) for voter(x,z,m).
SPLLC:0 - | A *single-person limited-liability company* is a corporation whose bylaws name one role, the *sole officer*, who decides every act of the company, and admit no participant but the sole officer.
SPLLC:0 - | Orga is a single-person limited-liability company.
