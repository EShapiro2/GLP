Guardians
Of Knowledge: An *identity* is a unique key pair. A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of Friendship: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts. Alice and Bob are persons.
Of Guardians: Bob *trusts* Carol, a ward of Alice, when he trusts Alice. The *decision rule*, a parameter of Guardians, says who decides for a ward and how. A ward takes an act of a party by those who decide for it. Alice and Bob are persons, Carol and Dave wards; in clause 4 Alice and Bob are possibly one person. Default: any guardian of z for those who decide for z.
Knowledge:1. ○ Establish: Alice may establish an identity. Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
Knowledge:2, Guardians:3. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
Knowledge:3, Guardians:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other, Alice knows Carol, Alice is a person, Bob is a person and Carol is a person.
Friendship:1, Guardians:3. ■ Befriend: Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other, Alice is a person and Bob is a person. Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
Friendship:2, Guardians:3. ■ Person unfriend: Alice may end her friendship with Bob, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
Friendship:3. ○ Loss of Trust. Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
Guardians:1. ■ Enter: Alice may create Carol, and Carol must consent, provided Alice is a person. Alice chooses Carol's key pair.
Guardians:2. ■ Guardian decisions. Make guardian: Any guardian of Carol may make Bob a guardian of Carol, with the consent of Bob, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired, Bob is a person, Carol has Alice as a guardian and Bob counts Alice as a friend. Resign: Bob may end his guardianship of Carol. End guardian: Any guardian of Carol, other than Bob, may end Bob's guardianship of Carol. Guardian unfriend: Bob may end his friendship with his ward Carol. Carol may not end a friendship with her guardian.
Guardians:3. ■ Ward and person. Ward acquaint: Carol and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Carol is a ward, Carol has Alice as a guardian and Bob is a person. Ward befriend: Carol and Bob may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Bob is a person, Carol and Bob know each other and Alice and Bob are friends. Ward end: Carol may end her friendship with Bob, provided Carol does not have Bob as a guardian. Unfriend: Bob may end his friendship with Carol, provided Bob is not a guardian of Carol. Ward unfriend: Alice may end the friendship of Carol and Bob, provided Carol has Alice as a guardian and Bob is not a guardian of Carol.
Guardians:4. ■ Wards. Wards acquaint: Carol and Dave may jointly come to know each other, provided Carol is a ward, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Dave is a ward and Dave has Bob as a guardian. Wards befriend: Carol and Dave may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice and Bob, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Dave is a ward, Dave is not retired, Dave has Bob as a guardian, Carol and Dave know each other, Alice knows Dave and Alice and Bob are friends. Wards of one guardian befriend: Carol and Dave may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Dave is a ward, Dave is not retired, Dave has Alice as a guardian and Carol and Dave know each other.
Guardians:5. ■ Retire: Any guardian of Carol may retire Carol. Carol can then take no act.
