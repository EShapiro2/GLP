Directory
Directory extends Rendezvous.
Vocabulary: d is a directory = directory(d); d lists x by e under m on its page = lists_shown(d,x,e,m); d lists x by e under m off its page = lists_unshown(d,x,e,m); y holds an invitation link of x from d = holds_link(y,x,d).
After x makes d a directory, which requires d is not a directory: d is a directory.
After x lists with d by e under m on its page, which requires d does not list x by e under m on its page: d lists x by e under m on its page.
After x lists with d by e under m off its page, which requires d does not list x by e under m off its page: d lists x by e under m off its page.
After x delists from d by e under m on its page: no longer d lists x by e under m on its page.
After x delists from d by e under m off its page: no longer d lists x by e under m off its page.
After y looks x up on d's page: y holds an invitation link of x from d.
After y looks x up with d by address: y holds an invitation link of x from d.
Directory:0 - | A *directory* is a service with a page every person can read.
Directory:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Dora a service, Mail an email address, Name a name.
Directory:1 ■ Open directory: directory(Dora) <- guardian(Carol,Dora), ~directory(Dora) guarded by Carol.
Directory:2 ■ | Listing.
Directory:2 ■ List me: lists_shown(Dora,Alice,Mail,Name) <- directory(Dora), knows(Alice,Dora), ~lists_shown(Dora,Alice,Mail,Name) guarded by Alice, Dora.
Directory:2 ■ List me unshown: lists_unshown(Dora,Alice,Mail,Name) <- directory(Dora), knows(Alice,Dora), ~lists_unshown(Dora,Alice,Mail,Name) guarded by Alice, Dora.
Directory:2 ■ | Dora consents having verified Mail as an email address of Alice.
Directory:2 ■ | Alice lists with a name if she chooses, shown on Dora's page or not, and her app then keeps Dora supplied with invitation links of hers.
Directory:2 ■ Delist me: ~lists_shown(Dora,Alice,Mail,Name) <- lists_shown(Dora,Alice,Mail,Name) guarded by Alice.
Directory:2 ■ Delist me unshown: ~lists_unshown(Dora,Alice,Mail,Name) <- lists_unshown(Dora,Alice,Mail,Name) guarded by Alice.
Directory:3 ■ Lookup: holds_link(Bob,Alice,Dora) <- knows(Bob,Dora), lists_shown(Dora,Alice,Mail,Name) guarded by Bob.
Directory:3 ■ Lookup by address: holds_link(Bob,Alice,Dora) <- knows(Bob,Dora), lists_unshown(Dora,Alice,Mail,Name) guarded by Bob.
Directory:3 ■ | Bob looks Alice up on Dora's page if she is shown there, and by her email address and name if not.
