GRA: Grassroots Ride Auction (expanded schemas)
GKK:0 - | An *identity* is a unique key pair.
GKK:0 - | A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity.
GKK:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
GKK:0 - | Reflexive: x knows y.
GKK:1 ○ Establish: identity(Alice), person(Alice) <- ~identity(Alice) guarded by Alice.
GKK:1 ○ | Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
GKK:2 ■ Acquaint: knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice) <- guarded by Alice, Bob.
GKK:3 ■ Introduce: knows(Bob,Carol) <- knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice), knows(Alice,Carol) guarded by Alice, Bob.
GSG:0 - | Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts.
GSG:0 - | Alice and Bob are persons.
GSG:1 ○ Befriend: friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice) <- knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice), ~friend(Alice,Bob), ~friend(Bob,Alice) guarded by Alice, Bob.
GSG:1 ○ | Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
GSG:2 ■ Unfriend: ~friend(Alice,Bob), ~friend(Bob,Alice) <- friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice) guarded by Alice.
GSG:3 ○ | Loss of Trust.
GSG:3 ○ | Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
GDO:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
GDO:1+GDO-LB:1 ■ Point friend: points_to(Alice,Bob) <- friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice), ~points_to(Alice,Bob), may_point_friend(Alice,Bob) guarded by Alice.
GDO:2+GDO-LB:2 ■ Point transitive: points_to(Alice,Carol) <- knows(Alice,Carol), points_to(Alice,Bob), points_to(Bob,Carol), ~points_to(Alice,Carol), closer(Alice,Carol) guarded by Alice.
GDO:3+GDO-LB:3 ■ Unpoint: ~points_to(Alice,Bob) <- points_to(Alice,Bob), bound(Alice,D), points_to(Alice,>D), furthest(Alice,Bob) guarded by Alice.
GDO-LB:0 - | A person has a *location*. d > 0, an integer, and e > 0 are parameters of GDO-LB.
GDO-LB:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
GDO-LB:0 - | Environment: the bound is n, for x; either x points to fewer than d persons and y is closest to x among all of x's unpointed friends, or y is closer to x by more than e than some person pointed to by x; y is closer to x by more than e than some person pointed to by x; y is furthest from x among the persons x points to.
GRA:0 - | A *pickup delay* is measured from the award. The *terms* of a call, a parameter of GRA, state a maximum pickup delay, select a bidder, set the fare and the deposit, and say how these are released.
GRA:0 - | Alice is a rider and Bob a driver, Trip a call. A person can be either a rider or a driver but not both.
GRA:0 - | Environment: x is a rider; x is a driver; the bidding period of x's call c has ended; x is at the pickup point with y; x is at the destination with y; no pickup of x's ride is recorded within the maximum pickup delay.
GRA:0 - | Parameters: the terms of x's call c select y; the terms of x's call c select no bidder.
GRA:1 ■ | Call.
GRA:1 ■ Call: open_call(Alice,Trip) <- rider(Alice), ~ride(Alice), ~open_call(Alice,_) guarded by Alice.
GRA:1 ■ | Alice calls for bids from the persons Alice points to, stating the pickup point, the destination, the bidding period and the terms.
GRA:1 ■ Send call: sent_call(Alice,Bob,Trip), call(Bob,Alice,Trip), direct_call(Bob,Alice,Trip) <- open_call(Alice,Trip), points_to(Alice,Bob), ~call(Bob,Alice,Trip).
GRA:1 ■ Forward call: forwarded(Bob,Alice,Carol,Trip), call(Carol,Alice,Trip) <- direct_call(Bob,Alice,Trip), points_to(Bob,Carol), ~forwarded(Bob,Alice,Carol,Trip).
GRA:2 ■ Bid: bid(Bob,Alice,Trip) <- driver(Bob), call(Bob,Alice,Trip), ~period_ended(Alice,Trip) guarded by Bob.
GRA:2 ■ | Alice may not open a bid before then.
GRA:3 ■ | Award.
GRA:3 ■ Award: ~open_call(Alice,Trip), ride(Alice), ride(Bob), rides_with(Alice,Bob), rides_with(Bob,Alice), fare_locked(Alice), deposit_locked(Bob) <- period_ended(Alice,Trip), bid(Bob,Alice,Trip), selects(Alice,Bob,Trip), ~ride(Bob), open_call(Alice,Trip) guarded by Alice.
GRA:3 ■ | The ride is awarded at the fare the terms set, and Alice's fare and the deposit the terms set for Bob are then locked.
GRA:3 ■ Close: ~open_call(Alice,Trip) <- selects_none(Alice,Trip), open_call(Alice,Trip) guarded by Alice.
GRA:4 ■ Pickup: pickup(Alice,Bob), pickup(Bob,Alice) <- ride(Alice), ride(Bob), rides_with(Alice,Bob), rides_with(Bob,Alice), at_pickup(Alice,Bob), at_pickup(Bob,Alice), ~pickup(Alice,Bob), ~pickup(Bob,Alice).
GRA:5 ■ Arrive: ~fare_locked(Alice), ~deposit_locked(Bob), ~ride(Alice), ~ride(Bob), ~rides_with(Alice,Bob), ~rides_with(Bob,Alice), ~pickup(Alice,_), ~pickup(Bob,_), released(Alice,Bob), released(Bob,Alice) <- at_destination(Alice,Bob), at_destination(Bob,Alice), fare_locked(Alice), deposit_locked(Bob), ride(Alice), ride(Bob), rides_with(Alice,Bob), rides_with(Bob,Alice).
GRA:6 ■ Cancel: ~fare_locked(Alice), ~deposit_locked(Bob), ~ride(Alice), ~ride(Bob), ~rides_with(Alice,Bob), ~rides_with(Bob,Alice), ~pickup(Alice,_), ~pickup(Bob,_), released(Alice,Bob), released(Bob,Alice) <- no_pickup(Alice), fare_locked(Alice), deposit_locked(Bob), ride(Alice), ride(Bob), rides_with(Alice,Bob), rides_with(Bob,Alice) guarded by Alice.
GRA:6 ■ | The fare and the deposit are then released to Alice and to Bob as the terms say.
GRA:7 ■ Withdraw: ~open_call(Alice,Trip) <- ~period_ended(Alice,Trip), open_call(Alice,Trip) guarded by Alice.
