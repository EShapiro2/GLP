MCS: Mutual Credit Society
MCS instantiates GBank with mutual credit society bylaws; x is a member of z for x is entitled to propose m in z; x is a member of z for x is entitled to take m for z; x is a member of z for x is entitled to vote on m in z; z holds the votes of at least Votes persons on m, Double is Votes times 2 and z has fewer than Double persons as a participant for the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z, and restricts and extends it.
A *mutual credit society* is a bank whose bylaws name one role, *member*, held by every participant; the members jointly decide its acts. A member is *in the red* by their coins in circulation less the other persons' coins they hold.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a mutual credit society.
1. ■ Leave: GO:3 Leave, only if Bob holds exactly 0 Orga-coins. Leave owed nothing: GO:3 Leave, only if Orga holds exactly 0 Bob-coins. Officer leave: GO:3 Officer leave, only if Bob holds exactly 0 Orga-coins. Officer leave owed nothing: GO:3 Officer leave, only if Orga holds exactly 0 Bob-coins.
2. ■ Redeem member's coin: Bob may redeem with Orga an Alice-coin against an Orga-coin, provided Bob is a member of Orga and Alice is a member of Orga.
3. ■ Credit limit. Swap: DGC:2, only if every member of Orga is then in the red by at most 1000 coins. Pay: Notes:3, only if every member of Orga is then in the red by at most 1000 coins. Pay issuer: Coins:1, only if every member of Orga is then in the red by at most 1000 coins. Pay: DGC:4, only if every member of Orga is then in the red by at most 1000 coins.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: every member of z is then in the red by at most 1000 coins, for x = credit_limit(x,z).
y is a member of z: y is a participant of z.
After y redeems with z an x-coin against a z-coin: no longer y holds an x-coin, z holds an x-coin, no longer z holds a z-coin and y holds a z-coin.
Environment: every member of z is then in the red by at most 1000 coins, for x.
