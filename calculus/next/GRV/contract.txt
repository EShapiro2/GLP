GRV: Grassroots Rendezvous
GRV extends GSV.
An *invitation link* of Alice's is a term she signs, naming her, a rendezvous server, a serial and an expiry.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Dave a service.
1. ■ Open server: Carol may make her service Dave a rendezvous server.
2. ■ Invite by link: Alice may register with Dave an invitation link of hers that names Dave, with a new serial.
3. ■ Use link: Bob may present to Dave an invitation link of Alice's registered with Dave that has not expired; Dave then gives Alice Bob's address and Bob Alice's, and the link is spent.
4. ■ Reconnection. Present: Alice and Bob, who are friends, may each present to Dave the other's signature over their friendship. Match: Dave then gives each the address of the other.
