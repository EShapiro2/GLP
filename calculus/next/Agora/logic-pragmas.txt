Agora
Agora extends Bonds.
An *item* is a one-of-a-kind thing named by its maker.
Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Item and Thing items, Wish something wanted, Mart a market, Day a maturity date, Cond a condition Alice, Bob and Carol agree, Sum a number; in clauses 4 and 5 Carol is possibly Alice, and in clause 6 Dave is possibly Alice or Bob.
1. ■ Make: Alice may make the item Item.
2. ■ Open: Alice may open Mart under a new name of hers.
3. ■ Join: Alice may admit Bob to Mart, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice is the keeper of Mart and Alice knows Bob.
4. ■ Posting. Offer: Carol may post Item to the wall of Alice's Mart, provided Carol is a member of the market Mart of Alice, Alice is the keeper of Mart and Carol holds the item Item. Offer own: Alice may post Item to the wall of Alice's Mart, provided Alice is a member of the market Mart of Alice, Alice is the keeper of Mart and Alice holds the item Item. Want: Carol may post Wish to the wall of Alice's Mart, provided Carol is a member of the market Mart of Alice and Alice is the keeper of Mart. Want own: Alice may post Wish to the wall of Alice's Mart, provided Alice is a member of the market Mart of Alice and Alice is the keeper of Mart. Deliver: Alice delivers Carol's post of Item in Mart to Bob, provided Alice is the keeper of Mart, Alice sees Carol's post of Item on the wall of Mart and Alice has Bob as a member of Mart. Deliver to poster: Alice delivers Carol's post of Item in Mart back to Carol, provided Alice is the keeper of Mart, Alice sees Carol's post of Item on the wall of Mart and Carol is not the keeper of Mart.
5. ■ Withdraw: Carol may withdraw her post of Item in Alice's Mart. Withdraw own: Alice may withdraw her post of Item in Alice's Mart. Carol may not post Item to Mart again.
6. ■ Swap item for coin: Alice and Bob may jointly swap the item Item for Sum Dave-coins, provided Alice and Bob know each other. Swap item for bond: Alice and Bob may jointly swap the item Item for Sum Dave-bonds due Day, provided Alice and Bob know each other. Swap items: Alice and Bob may jointly swap the item Item for the item Thing, provided Alice and Bob know each other. Deposit item: Alice may deposit the item Item with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other and Alice knows Bob. Release item: Carol and Bob may jointly release the item Item that Alice deposited on Cond, provided Carol and Bob know each other. Return item: Carol and Alice may jointly return the item Item deposited for Bob on Cond, provided Carol and Alice know each other.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x has made i = made(x,i); x holds the item i = item(x,i); x is the keeper of m = keeper(x,m); x is a member of the market m of y = member(x,m,y); x has y as a member of m = has_member(x,y,m); y has posted i to m = posted(y,i,m); x sees y's post of i on the wall of m = post(x,y,i,m); x has delivered y's post of i in m to z = delivered_post(x,y,i,m,z); z holds the item i in escrow from x for y on k = escrow_item(z,i,x,y,k).
x is a member of m: x is a member of the market m of someone.
After x makes the item i, which requires x has not made i: x has made i and x holds the item i.
After x opens m under a new name of x's, which requires x is not the keeper of m: x is the keeper of m, x is a member of the market m of x and x has x as a member of m.
After x admits y to m, which requires y is not a member of m: y is a member of the market m of x and x has y as a member of m.
After y posts i to the wall of x's m, which requires y has not posted i to m: y has posted i to m and x sees y's post of i on the wall of m.
After x delivers y's post of i in m to z, which requires x has not delivered y's post of i in m to z: x has delivered y's post of i in m to z, z sees y's post of i on the wall of m and z knows y.
After x delivers y's post of i in m back to y, which requires x has not delivered y's post of i in m to y: x has delivered y's post of i in m to y and y sees y's post of i on the wall of m.
After y withdraws y's post of i in x's m: no longer x sees y's post of i on the wall of m, no one sees y's post of i on the wall of m and x has delivered y's post of i in m to no one.
After x and y swap the item i for n w-coins: no longer x holds the item i, y holds the item i, no longer y holds n w-coins and x holds n w-coins.
After x and y swap the item i for n w-bonds due e: no longer x holds the item i, y holds the item i, no longer y holds n w-bonds due e and x holds n w-bonds due e.
After x and y swap the item i for the item j: no longer x holds the item i, y holds the item i, no longer y holds the item j and x holds the item j.
After x deposits the item i with z for y on k: no longer x holds the item i and z holds the item i in escrow from x for y on k.
After z and y release the item i that x deposited on k: no longer z holds the item i in escrow from x for y on k and y holds the item i.
After z and x return the item i deposited for y on k: no longer z holds the item i in escrow from x for y on k and x holds the item i.
