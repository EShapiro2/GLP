Agora
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity once.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ■ Issue.  Alice may issue Alice-notes, for bonds, with maturity later than the current date.
5. ■ Issue bond.  Alice may issue Alice-notes, for bonds, with maturity later than the current date.
6. ■ Swap.  Alice and Bob, who know each other, may jointly swap notes they hold.
7. ■ Swap coin for bond.  Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Carol-coins for Total Dave-bonds due Day, provided Alice and Bob know each other.
8. ■ Swap bonds.  Alice and Bob, who know each other, may jointly swap notes they hold.
9. ■ Pay.  Alice may pay notes she holds to Bob, whom she knows, with his consent.
10. ■ Pay issuer.  Alice may pay notes she holds to Bob, whom she knows, with his consent.
11. ■ Pay bond.  Alice may pay notes she holds to Bob, whom she knows, with his consent.
12. ■ Deposit.  Alice may deposit notes with Carol for Bob, on a condition the three agree, with the consent of Carol.
13. ■ Deposit bond.  Alice may deposit notes with Carol for Bob, on a condition the three agree, with the consent of Carol.
14. ■ Release.  Carol and Bob may jointly release them to Bob.
15. ■ Release bond.  Carol and Bob may jointly release them to Bob.
16. ■ Return.  Carol and Alice may jointly return them to Alice.
17. ■ Return bond.  Carol and Alice may jointly return them to Alice.
18. ■ Redeem.  Alice may redeem a Bob-coin she holds against any coin held by Bob, whom she knows.
19. ■ Redeem coin against bond.  Alice may redeem a Bob-coin, or a mature Bob-bond, she holds against any coin or bond held by Bob, whom she knows.
20. ■ Redeem bond.  Alice may redeem a Bob-coin, or a mature Bob-bond, she holds against any coin or bond held by Bob, whom she knows.
21. ■ Redeem bond against bond.  Alice may redeem a Bob-coin, or a mature Bob-bond, she holds against any coin or bond held by Bob, whom she knows.
22. ■ Make.  Alice may make an item, which she then holds.
23. ■ Open.  Alice may open a market under a new name of hers; Alice is then its keeper and first member.
24. ■ Join.  Alice, the keeper of a market, may admit Bob, whom she knows, with the consent of Bob.
25. ■ Offer.  A member of a market may post to its wall an item they hold, or something they want, each once; its keeper sees the post on their own wall.
26. ■ Offer own.  A member of a market may post to its wall an item they hold, or something they want, each once; its keeper sees the post on their own wall.
27. ■ Want.  A member of a market may post to its wall an item they hold, or something they want, each once; its keeper sees the post on their own wall.
28. ■ Want own.  A member of a market may post to its wall an item they hold, or something they want, each once; its keeper sees the post on their own wall.
29. ■ Deliver.  The keeper delivers it to every member but the keeper, the poster included; each member then knows its poster.
30. ■ Deliver to poster.  The keeper delivers it to every member but the keeper, the poster included; each member then knows its poster.
31. ■ Withdraw.  A member may withdraw a post of theirs; the keeper removes it from their own wall and from the wall of every member it was delivered to, and the member may not post it to that market again.
32. ■ Withdraw own.  A member may withdraw a post of theirs; the keeper removes it from their own wall and from the wall of every member it was delivered to, and the member may not post it to that market again.
33. ■ Swap item for coin.  Alice and Bob may jointly swap the item Item for Sum Dave-coins, provided Alice and Bob know each other.
34. ■ Swap item for bond.  Alice and Bob may jointly swap the item Item for Sum Dave-bonds due Day, provided Alice and Bob know each other.
35. ■ Swap items.  Notes:2 and Notes:4 apply to items as to notes.
36. ■ Deposit item.  Notes:2 and Notes:4 apply to items as to notes.
37. ■ Release item.  Notes:2 and Notes:4 apply to items as to notes.
38. ■ Return item.  Notes:2 and Notes:4 apply to items as to notes.
