GSN: Grassroots Social Network
Of GKK: An *identity* is a unique key pair. A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of GSG: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts. Alice and Bob are persons.
Of GSN: A *message*, a parameter of GSN, is what a person sends. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Grupo a group, Text a message, Bob and Carol possibly Alice or one person.
GKK:1. ○ Establish: Alice may establish an identity. Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
GKK:2. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
GKK:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
GSG:1. ○ Befriend: Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other. Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
GSG:2, GSN:3. ■ Unfriend: Alice may end her friendship with Bob, and then Alice is a member of no group of Bob, Bob is a member of no group of Alice, Alice has Bob as a member of no group and Bob has Alice as a member of no group.
GSG:3. ○ Loss of Trust. Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
GSN:1. ■ Message: Alice may send Text to Bob, provided Alice and Bob are friends.
GSN:2. ■ Group. Create: Alice may create Grupo. Alice is then its leader and first member. Close: Alice may close Grupo by leaving it.
GSN:3. ■ Joining and Leaving. Join: Alice may add Bob to Grupo, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo and Alice and Bob are friends. Remove: Alice may end Bob's membership in Grupo, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo. Leave: Bob may end his membership in Alice's Grupo.
GSN:4. ■ Group messaging. Post: Bob may send Text to the group Grupo of Alice, provided Bob is a member of the group Grupo of Alice. Post own: Alice may send Text to the group Grupo of Alice, provided Alice is a member of the group Grupo of Alice. Deliver: Alice delivers Text of Bob in Grupo to Carol, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo, Alice has the post Text of Bob in Grupo and Alice has Carol as a member of the group Grupo. Deliver leader: Alice delivers Text of Bob in Grupo to Alice, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo and Alice has the post Text of Bob in Grupo.
