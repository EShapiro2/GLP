Organisation
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity once.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ■ Befriend.  Alice and Bob, who trust each other and are not friends, may jointly become friends.
5. ■ Person unfriend.  Alice may end her friendship with Bob.
6. ■ Enter.  Alice may create an organisation, Carol, and establish Carol's identity by choosing Carol's key pair, and Carol must consent.
7. ■ Appoint.  Those who decide for Carol may make Bob, a friend of an officer of Carol, an officer and a friend of Carol, with the consent of Bob, only if Bob is a participant; the decision names his role.
8. ■ Resign.  Bob may end his guardianship of Carol.
9. ■ End officer.  Those who decide for Carol, other than Bob, may end Bob's guardianship.
10. ■ Officer unfriend.  Bob may end his friendship with Carol, which ends his guardianship.
11. ■ Organisation acquaint.  Carol, an organisation of Alice, and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
12. ■ Organisation befriend.  Carol may enter a relation with Bob with the consent of Bob and Alice, provided Carol and Bob know each other and Alice and Bob are friends.
13. ■ Organisation end.  Carol, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Carol.
14. ■ Unfriend.  Carol, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Carol.
15. ■ Organisation unfriend.  Carol, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Carol.
16. ■ Organisations acquaint.  Carol, an organisation of Alice, and Dave, an organisation of Bob, may jointly come to know each other.
17. ■ Organisations befriend.  Carol may enter a relation with Dave with the consent of Dave, Alice and Bob, provided Carol and Dave know each other, Alice knows Dave and is a friend of Bob, Alice and Bob possibly one person.
18. ■ Organisations of one officer befriend.  Carol may enter a relation with Dave with the consent of Dave, Alice and Bob, provided Carol and Dave know each other, Alice knows Dave and is a friend of Bob, Alice and Bob possibly one person.
19. ■ Retire.  Those who decide for Carol may retire Carol; every guardianship and relation of Carol then ends and Carol can take no act.
20. ■ Found.  An organisation may create Orga (Guardians:1) with the consent of Alice; Alice is then its first officer, and Orga must consent.
21. ■ Propose.  An officer, or a person the bylaws name, may propose an act of Orga.
22. ■ Vote.  Those who decide the act may vote on it.
23. ■ Act.  An officer the bylaws name for the act may take it for Orga after the votes decide it according to the bylaws.
24. ■ Join.  Bob may become a participant of Orga with the consent of those who decide for Orga.
25. ■ Leave.  Either Bob or those who decide for Orga may end Bob's participation, and with it every role he holds.
26. ■ Officer leave.  Either Bob or those who decide for Orga may end Bob's participation, and with it every role he holds.
27. ■ Expel.  Either Bob or those who decide for Orga may end Bob's participation, and with it every role he holds.
28. ■ Officer expel.  Either Bob or those who decide for Orga may end Bob's participation, and with it every role he holds.
29. ■ Amend.  Those who decide it may amend the bylaws.
