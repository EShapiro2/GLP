GO: Grassroots Organisation
GO instantiates GnW with bylaws, an organisation for a ward and an officer for a guardian, and restricts, strengthens and extends it (GSC, From Grassroots Contract to Grassroots App, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06231).
