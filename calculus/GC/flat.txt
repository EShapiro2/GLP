GKK:0 - | An *identity* is a unique key pair.
GKK:0 - | A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity.
GKK:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
GKK:0 - | Reflexive: x knows y.
GKK:1 ○ Establish: Alice may Alice has an identity, provided not Alice has an identity.

GKK:2 ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly Alice knows Bob and Bob knows Alice.

GC:0 - | A *coin* is a unit of debt that includes its issuer's public key and is signed by their private key. An *Alice-coin* is a coin issued by Alice.
GC:0 - | Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are parties, Sum and Total numbers, Carol and Dave possibly Alice, Bob or one person.
GC:1 ■ Issue: Alice may Alice holds Sum Alice-coins.
GC:2 ■ Swap: Alice and Bob may jointly no longer Alice holds Sum Carol-coins, Bob holds Sum Carol-coins, no longer Bob holds Total Dave-coins, Alice holds Total Dave-coins, Bob knows Carol and Alice knows Dave, provided Alice knows Bob and Bob knows Alice. Possibly one: Alice=Carol, Alice=Dave, Bob=Carol, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
GC:3 ■ Pay: Alice may no longer Alice holds Sum Bob-coins and Bob holds Sum Bob-coins, provided Alice knows Bob.
GC:4 ■ Redeem: Alice may no longer Alice holds a Bob-coin, Bob holds a Bob-coin, no longer Bob holds a Carol-coin, Alice holds a Carol-coin and Alice knows Carol, provided Alice knows Bob. Possibly one: Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
GC:5 ○ | Pricing.
GC:5 ○ | Alice will price her offerings in Alice-coins and accept them in payment at those prices.
