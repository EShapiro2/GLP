GC: Grassroots Coins
GC extends GKK.
A *coin* is a unit of debt that includes its issuer's public key and is signed by their private key. An *Alice-coin* is a coin issued by Alice.
Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Sum and Total numbers, Carol and Dave possibly Alice, Bob or one person.
1. ■ Issue: Alice may issue Sum Alice-coins.
2. ■ Swap: Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Carol-coins for Total Dave-coins, provided Alice and Bob know each other.
3. ■ Pay: Alice may pay Bob Sum Bob-coins, provided Alice knows Bob.
4. ■ Redeem: Alice may redeem a Bob-coin against a Carol-coin, provided Alice knows Bob.
5. ○ Pricing. Alice will price her offerings in Alice-coins and accept them in payment at those prices.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x holds a y-coin = coin(x,y); x holds n y-coins = coin(x,y,*n); x holds exactly n y-coins = coin(x,y,#n).
After x issues n x-coins: x holds n x-coins.
After x and y swap n z-coins for m w-coins: no longer x holds n z-coins, y holds n z-coins, no longer y holds m w-coins, x holds m w-coins, y knows z and x knows w.
After x pays y n y-coins: no longer x holds n y-coins and y holds n y-coins.
After x redeems a y-coin against a z-coin: no longer x holds a y-coin, y holds a y-coin, no longer y holds a z-coin, x holds a z-coin and x knows z.
