GC: Grassroots Coins
GC extends GKK.
A *coin* is a unit of debt that includes its issuer's public key and is signed by their private key. An *Alice-coin* is a coin issued by Alice.
Alice and Bob are persons.
1. ■ Issue: Alice may issue Alice-coins.
2. ■ Swap: Alice and Bob, who know each other, may jointly swap coins they hold.
3. ■ Pay: Alice may pay Bob-coins she holds to Bob, whom she knows.
4. ■ Redeem: Alice may redeem a Bob-coin she holds against any coin held by Bob, whom she knows.
5. ○ Pricing. Alice will price her offerings in Alice-coins and accept them in payment at those prices.
