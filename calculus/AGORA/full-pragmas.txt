AGORA: Grassroots Barter
Of GKK: An *identity* is a unique key pair. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. A party to a contract must have an identity.
Of GC: A *coin* is a unit of debt that includes its issuer's public key and is signed by their private key. An *Alice-coin* is a coin issued by Alice. Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Sum and Total numbers, Carol and Dave possibly Alice, Bob or one person.
Of GB: A *bond* is a unit of debt, due at its maturity date, that includes its issuer's public key and its maturity date and is signed by their private key. An *Alice-bond* is a bond issued by Alice. A bond is *mature* when its maturity date is at most the current date. Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Day and Term maturity dates, Today a date, Cond a condition Alice, Bob and Carol agree, Sum and Total numbers; in clauses 2 and 3 Carol and Dave are possibly Alice, Bob or one person, and in clause 4 Dave is possibly Alice, Bob or Carol.
Of AGORA: An *item* is a one-of-a-kind thing named by its maker. Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Item and Thing items, Wish something wanted, Mart a market, Day a maturity date, Cond a condition Alice, Bob and Carol agree, Sum a number; in clauses 4 and 5 Carol is possibly Alice, and in clause 6 Dave is possibly Alice or Bob.
GKK:1. ○ Establish: Alice may establish an identity. Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
GKK:2. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
GKK:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
GC:1. ■ Issue: Alice may issue Sum Alice-coins.
GC:2, GB:2. ■ Swap: Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Carol-coins for Total Dave-coins, provided Alice and Bob know each other.
GC:3. ■ Pay: Alice may pay Bob Sum Bob-coins, provided Alice knows Bob.
GC:4, GB:3. ■ Redeem: Alice may redeem a Bob-coin against a Carol-coin, provided Alice knows Bob.
GC:5. ○ Pricing. Alice will price her offerings in Alice-coins and accept them in payment at those prices.
GB:1. ■ Issue bond: Alice may issue Sum Alice-bonds due Day, provided the current date is Today and Day is more than Today.
GB:2. ■ Swap coin for bond: Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Carol-coins for Total Dave-bonds due Day, provided Alice and Bob know each other. Swap bonds: Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Carol-bonds due Day for Total Dave-bonds due Term, provided Alice and Bob know each other.
GB:3. ■ Redeem coin against bond: Alice may redeem a Bob-coin against a Carol-bond due Day, provided Alice knows Bob. Redeem bond: Alice may redeem a Bob-bond due Day against a Carol-coin, provided Alice knows Bob, the current date is Today and Day is at most Today. Redeem bond against bond: Alice may redeem a Bob-bond due Day against a Carol-bond due Term, provided Alice knows Bob, the current date is Today and Day is at most Today.
GB:4. ■ Escrow. Deposit: Alice may deposit Sum Dave-coins with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other and Alice knows Bob. Deposit bond: Alice may deposit Sum Dave-bonds due Day with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other and Alice knows Bob. Release: Carol and Bob may jointly release Sum Dave-coins that Alice deposited on Cond, provided Carol and Bob know each other and Carol finds Cond met. Release bond: Carol and Bob may jointly release Sum Dave-bonds due Day that Alice deposited on Cond, provided Carol and Bob know each other and Carol finds Cond met. Return: Carol and Alice may jointly return Sum Dave-coins deposited for Bob on Cond, provided Carol and Alice know each other and Carol finds Cond failed. Return bond: Carol and Alice may jointly return Sum Dave-bonds due Day deposited for Bob on Cond, provided Carol and Alice know each other and Carol finds Cond failed.
AGORA:1. ■ Make: Alice may make the item Item.
AGORA:2. ■ Open: Alice may open Mart under a new name of hers.
AGORA:3. ■ Join: Alice may admit Bob to Mart, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice is the keeper of Mart and Alice knows Bob.
AGORA:4. ■ Posting. Offer: Carol may post Item to the wall of Alice's Mart, provided Carol is a member of the market Mart of Alice, Alice is the keeper of Mart and Carol holds the item Item. Want: Carol may post Wish to the wall of Alice's Mart, provided Carol is a member of the market Mart of Alice and Alice is the keeper of Mart. Deliver: Alice delivers Carol's post of Item in Mart to Bob, provided Alice is the keeper of Mart, Alice sees Carol's post of Item on the wall of Mart and Alice has Bob as a member of Mart. Deliver to poster: Alice delivers Carol's post of Item in Mart back to Carol, provided Alice is the keeper of Mart, Alice sees Carol's post of Item on the wall of Mart and Carol is not the keeper of Mart.
AGORA:5. ■ Withdraw: Carol may withdraw her post of Item in Alice's Mart. Carol may not post Item to Mart again.
AGORA:6. ■ Swap item for coin: Alice and Bob may jointly swap the item Item for Sum Dave-coins, provided Alice and Bob know each other. Swap item for bond: Alice and Bob may jointly swap the item Item for Sum Dave-bonds due Day, provided Alice and Bob know each other. Swap items: Alice and Bob may jointly swap the item Item for the item Thing, provided Alice and Bob know each other. Deposit item: Alice may deposit the item Item with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other and Alice knows Bob. Release item: Carol and Bob may jointly release the item Item that Alice deposited on Cond, provided Carol and Bob know each other and Carol finds Cond met. Return item: Carol and Alice may jointly return the item Item deposited for Bob on Cond, provided Carol and Alice know each other and Carol finds Cond failed.
