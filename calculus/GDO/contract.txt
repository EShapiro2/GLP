GDO: Grassroots Directed Overlay
GDO extends GSG.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
1. ■ Point friend: Alice, who does not point to her friend Bob, may point to Bob.
2. ■ Point transitive: Alice, who knows Carol and points to Bob who points to Carol, may point to Carol.
3. ■ Unpoint: Alice, who points to Bob, may cease pointing to Bob.
