GDO: Grassroots Directed Overlay
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ○ Befriend.  Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other.
5. ■ Unfriend.  Alice may end her friendship with Bob.
6. ■ Point friend.  Alice may point to Bob, provided Alice and Bob are friends.
7. ■ Point transitive.  Alice may point to Carol, provided Alice knows Carol, Alice points to Bob and Bob points to Carol.
8. ■ Unpoint.  Alice may cease pointing to Bob.
