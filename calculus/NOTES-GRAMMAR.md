# What the grammar of the Logic pragmas lacks

Helper #3, for Calculus #6 (its mail of 2026-11-15 16:13 UTC), for the paper and for a later grammar.  The grammar is Section 1.1 of the paper and `tools/SAS-WRITING.txt`; `tools/gcsas.py` reads it.

The owners compute some conditions that the Calculus names by a basic state no act sets, an environment predicate or a parameter (Helper #6's comparison, DIFF-REAL.md, branch helper-6/calculus-owners-diff).  The grammar cannot say the owners' computation, for three reasons.

1. A definition cannot name a place its phrase lacks.  The right side of a definition names only the places of its phrase; a place it adds, such as a person's location l or the time t a call records, is read as an existential, and gcsas.py writes the bare letter in the expansion and then cannot read it ("cannot read these states").  A capitalised name of the names line can stand there, as the stake S in GNO's "y is a provider of z: z has the stake at the number S and y holds S z-coins", but only for a value the contract names, not for one each person or each act has.
2. There is no arithmetic on locations.  GDO-LB's conditions need the distance between two persons' locations and a comparison of two distances by more than e; the grammar has numbers and their comparisons, sums and products, and no location or distance.
3. There is no minimum and no existential over persons.  "Bob is closest to Alice among all of Alice's unpointed friends", "furthest from Alice among the persons Alice points to" and "closer to Alice by more than e than some person pointed to by Alice" (GDO-LB:1 to 3), and GRA-SS's "the bidder whose admissible bid has the highest score" and the second score, quantify over persons; the grammar counts persons ("more than d persons") and does not take a minimum, a maximum or "some person" with a condition.

4. A definition's own names are not fresh at each use.  A name on the right side of a definition that its phrase does not have, as Held in GNO's provider or Dollar in its customer, is the same name wherever the definition is expanded; an act that requires the defined state of two persons, as Pass of GNG, GNL, GNM and GAN, then has one variable in required atoms of two roles, which vGLP refuses (vGLP #28, 2026-11-15 15:44 UTC).  The pragmas write the second person's condition out, with a second name (Kept, Mark).

5. There is no sum over the voters.  SC:1 Issue shares, and SC's election and removal of directors, are decided "by a majority of the shares they hold": the shares of those who voted must be more than half of all the shares.  The grammar counts persons ("the votes of at least Votes persons") and units one holder holds ("Bob holds exactly Held shares of Orga"), and has no sum of a quantity over the holders of a role or the voters (Helper #8, for Calculus #6 and GO #6, 2026-11-15 16:37 UTC).  GSC's term has one, sum(Xs, A, Phi, U) in a constraint, "a value of a numeric type, U a numeric term under the binders Xs" (GLP programs/gsc/self.glp), and vGLP's compiler takes it (programs/vglp/cva/compiler/README.md, "Counts, sums, min and floor").  Three things are missing.  (a) A phrase, as "the shares of those who voted to issue are at least Votes", that tools/gcsas.py reads into a sum over the voters and writes in the schema notation.  (b) tools/toglp.py printing it as sum([V], atom(vote_issue, [Orga, V, ...]), Phi, U).  (c) A number under the binder: a holder's shares are a multiset, share(x,z,*n), whose count is a multiplicity and not a variable U can name; the sum needs a state with the number as a place, "x holds the number n of shares of z", kept by the acts that issue and move shares, or a sum of multiplicities in GSC's term.  Until then SC:1 stays as it is: the role "those who decide the issue of shares of z", all of them.

What stays, as findings and parameters:

- GDO-LB: may_point_friend, closer and furthest, environment predicates (the location the platform's finding, the computation the platform's); the bound d, an environment state of the one who decides, "the bound is d".
- GRA: the bidding period's end and the pickup delay, period_ended and no_pickup, environment predicates, since each compares the current time with a time the call or the award set (reason 1); at_pickup and at_destination, the platform's findings of location; selects and selects_none, Parameters, the terms being a parameter of GRA.
- GRA-SS: the terms' computations over all bids (reason 3): highest_score and none_admissible, environment predicates; sets_deposit, second_score_fare, refund and cancel_release, Parameters, the terms being a parameter.
