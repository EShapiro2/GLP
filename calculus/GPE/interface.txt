GPE: Grassroots Physical Encounters
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ■ Open place.  Alice may declare her place open.
5. ■ Close place.  Alice may close her place.
6. ■ Encounter.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Alice and Bob are nearby each other, Alice has declared the place open and Bob has declared the place open.
