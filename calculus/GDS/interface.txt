GDS: Grassroots Directory Service
1. ■ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity, and then Alice is a person.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other, Alice knows Carol, Alice is a person, Bob is a person and Carol is a person.
4. ■ Befriend.  Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other, Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
5. ■ Person unfriend.  Alice may end her friendship with Bob, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
6. ■ Enter.  Alice may create Carol, and Carol must consent, provided Alice is a person.
7. ■ Make guardian.  The guardians of Carol may make Bob a guardian of Carol, with the consent of Bob, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired and Bob is a person.
8. ■ Resign.  Bob may end his guardianship of Carol.
9. ■ End guardian.  The guardians of Carol may end Bob's guardianship of Carol.
10. ■ Guardian unfriend.  Bob may end his friendship with his ward Carol.
11. ■ Ward acquaint.  Carol and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Carol is a ward, Carol has Alice as a guardian and Bob is a person.
12. ■ Ward befriend.  Carol and Bob may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Bob is a person, Carol and Bob know each other and Alice and Bob are friends.
13. ■ Ward end.  Carol may end her friendship with Bob, provided Bob is not a guardian of Carol.
14. ■ Unfriend.  Bob may end his friendship with Carol, provided Bob is not a guardian of Carol.
15. ■ Ward unfriend.  Alice may end the friendship of Carol and Bob, provided Carol has Alice as a guardian and Bob is not a guardian of Carol.
16. ■ Wards acquaint.  Carol and Dave may jointly come to know each other, provided Carol is a ward, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Dave is a ward and Dave has Bob as a guardian.
17. ■ Wards befriend.  Carol and Dave may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice and Bob, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Dave is a ward, Dave is not retired, Dave has Bob as a guardian, Carol and Dave know each other, Alice knows Dave and Alice and Bob are friends.
18. ■ Wards of one guardian befriend.  Carol and Dave may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Dave is a ward, Dave is not retired, Dave has Alice as a guardian and Carol and Dave know each other.
19. ■ Retire.  The guardians of Carol may retire Carol.
20. ■ Open server.  Carol may make Dave a rendezvous server, provided Carol is a guardian of Dave.
21. ■ Invite by link.  Alice may register Link with Dave, provided Alice has signed Link as an invitation link that names Dave and Dave is a rendezvous server.
22. ■ Use link.  Bob may present Link of Alice to Dave, provided Bob knows Dave and Dave does not hold Link expired.
23. ■ Present.  Alice may present to Dave the signature of Bob over the friendship of Alice and Bob, provided Alice and Bob are friends, Alice knows Dave and Dave is a rendezvous server.
24. ■ Match.  Dave matches Alice and Bob.
25. ■ Open directory.  Carol may make Dora a directory, provided Carol is a guardian of Dora.
26. ■ List.  Alice may list with Dora, provided Dora is a directory and Dora has verified an email address of Alice.
27. ■ Delist.  Alice may delist from Dora.
28. ■ Lookup.  Bob may look Alice up with Dora, provided Bob knows Dora and Dora lists Alice.
