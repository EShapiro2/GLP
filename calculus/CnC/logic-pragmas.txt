CnC: Consumers' Cooperative
CnC instantiates GCoop and TC with bylaws that delegate no act, and restricts them.
A *consumers' cooperative* is a cooperative that is a trading company. Its *fee* is a number of Dollars the members set, and the *cost* of a good is the coins Orga paid for it.
Bob and Erin are persons, Orga a consumers' cooperative, Ware a good, Sum a number.
1. ■ Join: GO:3 Join, only if Bob has paid Orga the fee.
2. ■ Sell: TC:3, only if Erin is a member of Orga and Orga has paid exactly Sum coins for Ware.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: y has paid z the fee = paid_fee(z,y).
