Knowledge
An *identity* is a unique key pair, chosen by the person who has it.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. A party to a contract must have an identity.
1. ○ Establish: Alice may establish an identity. Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
2. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x has an identity = identity(x); x knows y = knows(x,y).
x and y know each other: x knows y and y knows x.
After x establishes an identity, which requires x does not have an identity: x has an identity and x knows x.
After x and y come to know each other: x and y know each other.
After x introduces z to y: y knows z.
