Knowledge
Vocabulary: x has an identity = identity(x); x knows y = knows(x,y).
x and y know each other: x knows y and y knows x.
After x establishes an identity, which requires x does not have an identity: x has an identity and x knows x.
After x and y come to know each other: x and y know each other.
After x introduces z to y: y knows z.
Knowledge:0 - | An *identity* is a unique key pair, chosen by the person who has it.
Knowledge:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. A party to a contract must have an identity.
Knowledge:1 ○ Establish: identity(Alice), knows(Alice,Alice) <- ~identity(Alice) guarded by Alice.
Knowledge:1 ○ | Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
Knowledge:2 ■ Acquaint: knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice) <- guarded by Alice, Bob.
Knowledge:3 ■ Introduce: knows(Bob,Carol) <- knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice), knows(Alice,Carol) guarded by Alice, Bob.
