P: Pronouns and possessives (flat text)
P:0 - | Alice, Bob, Erin and Frank are persons, Carol a person, Orga an organisation, Club a club.
P:1 ■ Ward end: Carol may Carol has ended Carol's friendship with Bob, provided not Carol has Bob as a guardian.
P:2 ■ Greet guardian: Bob may Bob has greeted Alice, provided Bob has Alice as a guardian.
P:2 ■ Greet known: Erin may Erin has greeted Bob, provided Erin knows Bob.
P:2 ■ Greet self: Frank may Frank has greeted Erin, provided Frank knows Frank.
P:3 ■ Greet friend: Alice may Alice has greeted Frank, provided Frank is a friend of Alice.
P:3 ■ Greet stranger: Alice may Alice has greeted Bob, provided not Bob is a friend of Alice.
P:3 ■ Greet of hers: Erin may Erin has greeted Frank, provided Frank is a friend of Erin.
P:4 ■ Greet member: Frank may Frank has greeted Orga, provided Orga has Frank as a member.
P:4 ■ Greet outsider: Orga may Orga has greeted Bob, provided not Orga has Bob as a member.
P:4 ■ Greet their member: Orga may Orga has greeted Frank, provided Orga has Frank as a member.
P:4 ■ Greet in her club: Alice may Alice has greeted Frank, provided Frank is a member of Alice's Club.
