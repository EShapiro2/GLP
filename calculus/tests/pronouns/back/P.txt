P: Pronouns and possessives
Alice, Bob, Erin and Frank are persons, Carol a person, Orga an organisation, Club a club.
1. ■ Ward end: Carol may end her friendship with Bob, provided Carol does not have Bob as a guardian.
2. ■ Greet guardian: Bob may greet Alice, provided Bob has Alice as a guardian. Greet known: Erin may greet Bob, provided Erin knows Bob. Greet self: Frank may greet Erin, provided Frank knows Frank.
3. ■ Greet friend: Alice may greet Frank, provided Frank is a friend of Alice. Greet stranger: Alice may greet Bob, provided Bob is not a friend of Alice. Greet of hers: Erin may greet Frank, provided Frank is a friend of Erin.
4. ■ Greet member: Frank may greet Orga, provided Orga has Frank as a member. Greet outsider: Orga may greet Bob, provided Orga does not have Bob as a member. Greet their member: Orga may greet Frank, provided Orga has Frank as a member. Greet in her club: Alice may greet Frank, provided Frank is a member of Alice's Club.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x has y as a guardian = has_guardian(x,y); x is a friend of y = friend(x,y); x knows y = knows(x,y); x has greeted y = greeted(x,y); x has y as a member = member(x,y); x has ended x's friendship with y = ended(x,y); x is a member of y's c = club_member(x,c,y).
After x ends x's friendship with y: x has ended x's friendship with y.
After x greets y: x has greeted y.
