CLX: Club of Officers
Of Knowledge: An *identity* is a unique key pair. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. A party to a contract must have an identity. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of CLB: Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a club.
Of CLX: Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a club.
Knowledge:1. ○ Establish: Alice may establish an identity. Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
Knowledge:2. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
Knowledge:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
CLB:1. ■ Admit: The officers of Orga may make Bob a member of Orga, with the consent of Bob. Expel: The officers of Orga, other than Bob, may end Bob's membership in Orga.
