Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: z holds y's ideal value at the number n = ideal(z,y,n); z has the status quo at the number n = status(z,n); z has passed n = passed(z,n).
z counts y as gaining at least w from n over s: z holds y's ideal value at the number i, f is the difference from i to s, d is the difference from i to n, g is d plus w and f is at least g.
After z passes n: z has passed n.
