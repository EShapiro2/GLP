MRC: Club
MRC extends GKK.
Vocabulary: y is a member of z = member(y,z); z has y as a member = has_member(z,y).
Roles: those who decide for z = deciders(z), listed by z has y as a member and y is a member of z.
After y joins z, which requires y is not a member of z: y is a member of z and z has y as a member.
MRC:0 - | Bob is a person, Orga a club.
MRC:1 ■ Join: member(Bob,Orga), has_member(Orga,Bob) <- ~member(Bob,Orga) guarded by Bob, deciders(Orga) must deciders(Orga).
