P: Greetings
P extends Knowledge.
Alice and Bob are persons.
1. ■ Greet: Alice may greet Bob, with the consent of those who decide for Bob, provided Alice knows Bob.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x has greeted y = greeted(x,y).
Roles: those who decide for z = deciders(z); the friends of z = friends(z).
After x greets y: x has greeted y.
Default: the friends of z for those who decide for z.
