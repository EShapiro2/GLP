P: Parameters
Vocabulary: x points to y = points(x,y); x counts y as far = far(x,y); y is beyond d of x = beyond(x,y).
After x points to y, which requires x does not point to y: x points to y.
After x marks y as far, which requires x does not count y as far: x counts y as far.
P:0 - | d > 1, an integer, is a parameter of P.
P:0 - | Alice and Bob are persons.
P:0 - | Environment: y is beyond d of x.
P:1 ■ Point: points(Alice,Bob) <- points(Alice,<D), ~points(Alice,Bob) guarded by Alice.
P:1 ■ Mark far: far(Alice,Bob) <- beyond(Alice,Bob), ~far(Alice,Bob) guarded by Alice.
