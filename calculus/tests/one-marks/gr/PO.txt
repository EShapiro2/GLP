PO: Possibly One
Alice, Bob, Carol, Dave and Erin are persons, Erin is possibly any of the others; in clause 2 Carol is possibly Dave; in clause 3 Carol is possibly Alice or Bob.
1. ■ Tag: Alice may tag Bob for Dave, provided Bob has a tag on Erin for Dave.
2. ■ Pass: Alice may tag Carol for Dave, provided Alice has a tag on Bob for Dave.
3. ■ Note: Alice may tag Bob for Carol.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x has a tag on y for w = tag(x,y,w).
After x tags y for w, which requires x does not have a tag on y for w: x has a tag on y for w.
