K: Base
Alice and Bob are persons.
1. ■ Meet: Alice and Bob may jointly meet.
2. ■ Part: Alice may part from Bob.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x has met y = met(x,y).
After x and y meet, which requires x has not met y: x has met y and y has met x.
After x parts from y: no longer x has met y and no longer y has met x.
