FK: Function kinds
Orga is a person, Board and Next positions, Move a move, Side a number.
1. ■ Play: Orga may play from Board to Next, provided Orga is at Board, Side is the side to move of Board, Orga has the side Side and Next is the position after Move on Board.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: z is at b = at(z,b); z has the side s = side_of(z,s); s is the side to move of b = side(s,b); c is the position after m on b = after(c,m,b).
Functions: s is the side to move of b; c is the position after m on b.
After z plays from b to c: no longer z is at b and z is at c.
