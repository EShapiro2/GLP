OR: Organisation run by its directors
Of O: Alice is a person, Orga an organisation, Motion a motion. Parameters: x is entitled to vote on m in z; the votes decide m in z. Default: x is a member of z for x is entitled to vote on m in z. Default: z holds the votes of at least Votes persons on m, Double is Votes times 2 and z has fewer than Double persons as a member for the votes decide m in z.
Of OR: Alice is a person, Orga an organisation, Motion a motion. Default: x holds the role Director in z for x is entitled to vote on m in z.
O:1. ■ Vote: Alice may vote on Motion in Orga, provided Alice holds the role Director in Orga.
O:1, OR:1. ■ Act: Alice may take Motion for Orga, provided Alice is a member of Orga, Orga holds the votes of at least Votes persons on Motion, Double is Votes times 2, Orga has fewer than Double persons as a member and Alice holds the role Director in Orga.
