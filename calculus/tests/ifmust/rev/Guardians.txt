Guardians
Guardians extends and restricts Friendship.
A *relation* is a friendship or the membership of a group. Bob *trusts* Carol, a ward of Alice, when he trusts Alice. The *decision rule*, a parameter of Guardians, says who decides for a ward and how. A ward takes an act of a party by those who decide for it.
Alice, Bob and Erin are persons, Carol and Dave wards.
1. ■ Enter: Alice may create a ward, Carol, and establish Carol's identity by choosing Carol's key pair. Alice is then the creator, a guardian and a friend of Carol.
2. ■ Guardian decisions. Make guardian: Those who decide for Carol may make Bob, a friend of a guardian of Carol, a guardian and a friend of Carol, with the consent of Bob. Resign: Bob may end his guardianship of Carol. End guardian: Those who decide for Carol, other than Bob, may end Bob's guardianship. Guardian unfriend: Bob may end his friendship with Carol, which ends his guardianship. Carol may not end a friendship with her guardian.
3. ■ Ward and person. Ward acquaint: Carol, a ward of Alice, and Bob may jointly come to know each other. Ward befriend: Carol may enter a relation with Bob with the consent of Bob and Alice, provided Carol and Bob know each other and Alice and Bob are friends. Ward end, Unfriend, Ward unfriend: Carol, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Carol.
4. ■ Wards. Wards acquaint: Carol, a ward of Alice, and Dave, a ward of Bob, may jointly come to know each other. Wards befriend: Carol may enter a relation with Dave with the consent of Dave, Alice and Bob, provided Carol and Dave know each other, Alice knows Dave and is a friend of Bob, Alice and Bob possibly one person.
5. ■ Retire: Those who decide for Carol may retire Carol; every guardianship and relation of Carol then ends and Carol can take no act.
