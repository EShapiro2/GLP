Bank
Bank extends Knowledge.
A *bank* keeps accounts. The *bylaws*, a parameter of Bank, say who may open an account at the bank.
Alice and Bob are persons, Acct an account.
1. ■ Open: Alice and Bob may jointly open the account Acct, provided Alice and Bob know each other. Bank accounts stay open until both close them.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x holds the account n with y = account(x,n,y).
After x and y open the account n: x holds the account n with y and y holds the account n with x.
