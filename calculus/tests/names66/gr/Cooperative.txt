Cooperative
Cooperative extends Knowledge.
A *cooperative* is owned by its members.
Alice and Bob are persons, Coop a cooperative.
1. ■ Join: Alice may join the cooperative Coop, with the consent of Bob, provided Bob is a member of Coop. Cooperative members vote.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x is a member of c = member(x,c).
After x joins the cooperative c: x is a member of c.
