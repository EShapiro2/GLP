A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity.
A *guardian* of a ward decides for it. The *decision rule*, a parameter of Guardians, says who decides for a ward and how.

Knowledge:2. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
Knowledge:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
Guardians:1. ■ Appoint: Alice may make Bob a guardian, provided Alice knows Bob. Guardians are persons.
