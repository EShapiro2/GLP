BYX: Bylaws Extended
Of MO: Bob is a person, Orga an organisation, Motion an act of Orga. Parameters: x is entitled to vote on m in z; the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z.
Of CUR: Alice and Bob are persons, Sum and Total numbers.
Of OC: Erin and Frank are persons, Alice and Bob organisations, Orga an organisation, Sum and Total numbers.
Of BY: Votes and Double are numbers.
Of BYX: Alice is an organisation.
MO:1. ■ Vote: Bob may vote on Motion in Orga, provided Bob is a member of Orga.
CUR:1. ■ Pay: Alice may pay Bob Sum Alice-coins, provided Alice knows Bob.
CUR:1, OC:1. ■ Pay for an organisation: Alice may pay Bob Sum Alice-coins, taken for Alice by Erin, provided Alice knows Bob, Erin is a member of Alice, Alice holds the votes of at least VotesAlice persons on whether Alice pays Bob Sum Alice-coins, DoubleAlice is VotesAlice times 2 and Alice has fewer than DoubleAlice persons as a participant, and then Alice holds no one's vote on whether Alice pays Bob Sum Alice-coins.
OC:1. ■ Vote of Alice for Pay: Voter may vote on whether Alice pays Bob Sum Alice-coins in Alice, provided Voter is a member of Alice.
CUR:2. ■ Swap: Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Alice-coins for Total Bob-coins.
CUR:2, OC:3. ■ Swap of two organisations: Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Alice-coins for Total Bob-coins, taken for Alice by Erin and for Bob by Frank, provided Erin is a member of Alice, Frank is a member of Bob, Alice holds the votes of at least VotesAlice persons on whether Alice and Bob swap Sum Alice-coins for Total Bob-coins, DoubleAlice is VotesAlice times 2, Alice has fewer than DoubleAlice persons as a participant, Bob holds the votes of at least VotesBob persons on whether Alice and Bob swap Sum Alice-coins for Total Bob-coins, DoubleBob is VotesBob times 2 and Bob has fewer than DoubleBob persons as a participant, and then Alice holds no one's vote on whether Alice and Bob swap Sum Alice-coins for Total Bob-coins and Bob holds no one's vote on whether Alice and Bob swap Sum Alice-coins for Total Bob-coins.
OC:3. ■ Vote of Alice for Swap: Voter may vote on whether Alice and Bob swap Sum Alice-coins for Total Bob-coins in Alice, provided Voter is a member of Alice. Vote of Bob for Swap: Voter may vote on whether Alice and Bob swap Sum Alice-coins for Total Bob-coins in Bob, provided Voter is a member of Bob.
CUR:2, OC:3. ■ Swap for Alice: Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Alice-coins for Total Bob-coins, taken for Alice by Erin, provided Erin is a member of Alice, Alice holds the votes of at least VotesAlice persons on whether Alice and Bob swap Sum Alice-coins for Total Bob-coins, DoubleAlice is VotesAlice times 2 and Alice has fewer than DoubleAlice persons as a participant, and then Alice holds no one's vote on whether Alice and Bob swap Sum Alice-coins for Total Bob-coins.
OC:2. ■ Grant: Alice may grant Bob a badge of Orga, provided Alice is a member of Orga, Orga holds the votes of at least VotesOrga persons on whether Alice grants Bob a badge of Orga, DoubleOrga is VotesOrga times 2 and Orga has fewer than DoubleOrga persons as a participant, and then Orga holds no one's vote on whether Alice grants Bob a badge of Orga. Vote of Orga for Grant: Voter may vote on whether Alice grants Bob a badge of Orga in Orga, provided Voter is a member of Orga.
