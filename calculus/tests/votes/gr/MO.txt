MO: A Minimal Organisation
Bob is a person, Orga an organisation, Motion an act of Orga.
1. ■ Vote: Bob may vote on Motion in Orga, provided Bob is entitled to vote on Motion in Orga.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x is a member of z = member(x,z); z has x as a participant = has_participant(z,x); z holds x's vote on m = vote(z,x,m); x is entitled to vote on m in z = voter(x,z,m); the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z = decided(z,m).
After x votes on m in z, which requires z does not hold x's vote on m: z holds x's vote on m.
Parameters: x is entitled to vote on m in z; the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z.
