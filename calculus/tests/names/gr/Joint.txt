Joint: Joint Accounts
Joint extends GKK.
Alice and Bob are persons, Acct a joint account.
1. ■ Open: Alice and Bob may jointly open the joint account Acct, provided Alice and Bob know each other.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x holds the joint account n with y = joint(x,n,y).
x and y hold the joint account n: x holds the joint account n with y and y holds the joint account n with x.
After x and y open the joint account n: x and y hold the joint account n.
