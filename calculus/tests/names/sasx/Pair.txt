Pair: Joint Accounts of Friends (expanded schemas)
GKK:0 - | An *identity* is a unique key pair.
GKK:0 - | A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity.
GKK:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
GKK:0 - | Reflexive: x knows y.
GKK:1 ○ Establish: identity(Alice), person(Alice) <- ~identity(Alice) guarded by Alice.
GKK:1 ○ | Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
GKK:2 ■ Acquaint: knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice) <- guarded by Alice, Bob.
GKK:3 ■ Introduce: knows(Bob,Carol) <- knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice), knows(Alice,Carol) guarded by Alice, Bob.
Joint:0 - | Alice and Bob are persons, Acct a joint account.
Joint:1+Pair:1 ■ Open: joint(Alice,Acct,Bob), joint(Bob,Acct,Alice) <- knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice), knows(Alice,Bob) guarded by Alice, Bob.
