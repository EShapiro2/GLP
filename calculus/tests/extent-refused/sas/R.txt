Vocabulary: x has y as a guardian = has_guardian(x,y); y is a guardian of x = guardian(y,x); x is a member of the group g of y = member(x,g,y); x has y as a member of the group g = listed(x,y,g); x is retired = retired(x).
After x becomes a guardian of z: x is a guardian of z and z has x as a guardian.
After x adds y to g: x has y as a member of the group g.
After x retires z: z is retired, no one is a guardian of z, z has no one as a guardian, z is a member of no group of anyone and no one has z as a member of any group.
