D: Dial
Orga is a person, Old, New and Gap numbers.
1. ■ Move: Orga may move the dial to New, provided Orga has the dial at the number Old, Gap is the difference from Old to New and Gap is at most 5.

the earlier dial of z has ended: z has the dial at no number.
After z moves the dial to m: the earlier dial of z has ended and z has the dial at the number m.
