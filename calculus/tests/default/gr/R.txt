R: Ruled
Alice is a person, Carol a ward.
1. ■ Retire: Those who decide for Carol may retire Carol.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: z has y as a guardian = has_guardian(z,y); z is retired = retired(z).
Roles: those who decide for z = deciders(z), listed by z has y as a guardian; any guardian of z = any_guardian(z), any one listed by z has y as a guardian.
After g retires z, which requires z is not retired: z is retired.
Default: any guardian of z for those who decide for z.
