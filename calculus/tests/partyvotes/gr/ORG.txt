ORG: An Organisation
Bob is a person, Orga an organisation, Motion an act of Orga.
1. ■ Vote: Bob may vote on Motion in Orga, provided Bob is entitled to vote on Motion in Orga.
2. ■ Retire: Those who decide for Orga may retire Orga.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x is a member of z = member(x,z); z has x as a participant = has_participant(z,x); z holds x's vote on m = vote(z,x,m); x is entitled to vote on m in z = voter(x,z,m); the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z = decided(z,m); x is entitled to take m for z = taker(x,z,m); z has y as a guardian = has_guardian(z,y); y is a guardian of z = guardian(y,z); z is retired = retired(z).
Roles: those who decide for z = deciders(z), listed by z has y as a guardian and y is a guardian of z.
After x votes on m in z, which requires z does not hold x's vote on m: z holds x's vote on m.
After g retires z, which requires z is not retired: z is retired.
Parameters: x is entitled to vote on m in z; the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z; x is entitled to take m for z.
