BYL: Bylaws
Of ORG: Bob is a person, Orga an organisation, Motion an act of Orga. Parameters: x is entitled to vote on m in z; the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z; x is entitled to take m for z.
Of CUR: A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity. Alice and Bob are parties, Sum and Total numbers.
Of BYL: Votes and Double are numbers.
ORG:1. ■ Vote: Bob may vote on Motion in Orga, provided Bob is a member of Orga.
ORG:2. ■ Retire: Those who decide for Orga may retire Orga.
CUR:1. ■ Pay: Alice may pay Bob Sum Alice-coins, provided Alice knows Bob. Pay for Alice: Alice may pay Bob Sum Alice-coins, taken for Alice by AliceOfficer, provided Alice knows Bob, AliceOfficer knows Alice, AliceOfficer knows Bob, AliceOfficer is a member of Alice, Alice holds the votes of at least VotesAlice persons on whether Alice pays Bob Sum Alice-coins, DoubleAlice is VotesAlice times 2 and Alice has fewer than DoubleAlice persons as a participant, and then Alice holds no one's vote on whether Alice pays Bob Sum Alice-coins. Vote of Alice for Pay: Voter may vote on whether Alice pays Bob Sum Alice-coins in Alice, provided Voter is a member of Alice and Alice knows Bob.
CUR:2. ■ Swap: Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Alice-coins for Total Bob-coins. Swap for Alice: Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Alice-coins for Total Bob-coins, taken for Alice by AliceOfficer, provided AliceOfficer knows Alice, AliceOfficer knows Bob, AliceOfficer is a member of Alice, Alice holds the votes of at least VotesAlice persons on whether Alice and Bob swap Sum Alice-coins for Total Bob-coins, DoubleAlice is VotesAlice times 2 and Alice has fewer than DoubleAlice persons as a participant, and then Alice holds no one's vote on whether Alice and Bob swap Sum Alice-coins for Total Bob-coins. Vote of Alice for Swap: Voter may vote on whether Alice and Bob swap Sum Alice-coins for Total Bob-coins in Alice, provided Voter is a member of Alice and Alice knows Bob. Swap for Bob: Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Alice-coins for Total Bob-coins, taken for Bob by BobOfficer, provided BobOfficer knows Alice, BobOfficer knows Bob, BobOfficer is a member of Bob, Bob holds the votes of at least VotesBob persons on whether Alice and Bob swap Sum Alice-coins for Total Bob-coins, DoubleBob is VotesBob times 2 and Bob has fewer than DoubleBob persons as a participant, and then Bob holds no one's vote on whether Alice and Bob swap Sum Alice-coins for Total Bob-coins. Vote of Bob for Swap: Voter may vote on whether Alice and Bob swap Sum Alice-coins for Total Bob-coins in Bob, provided Voter is a member of Bob and Bob knows Alice. Swap for Alice and Bob: Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Alice-coins for Total Bob-coins, taken for Alice by AliceOfficer and for Bob by BobOfficer, provided AliceOfficer knows Alice, AliceOfficer knows Bob, BobOfficer knows Alice, BobOfficer knows Bob, AliceOfficer is a member of Alice, Alice holds the votes of at least VotesAlice persons on whether Alice and Bob swap Sum Alice-coins for Total Bob-coins, DoubleAlice is VotesAlice times 2, Alice has fewer than DoubleAlice persons as a participant, BobOfficer is a member of Bob, Bob holds the votes of at least VotesBob persons on whether Alice and Bob swap Sum Alice-coins for Total Bob-coins, DoubleBob is VotesBob times 2 and Bob has fewer than DoubleBob persons as a participant, and then Alice holds no one's vote on whether Alice and Bob swap Sum Alice-coins for Total Bob-coins and Bob holds no one's vote on whether Alice and Bob swap Sum Alice-coins for Total Bob-coins.
