B:2 ■ Set: Orga may no longer Orga has Bob's line at the number anyone and Orga has Bob's line at the number Line.
B:3 ■ Credit: Bob may no longer Bob holds Sum Bob-coins and Orga holds Sum Bob-coins, provided Orga holds exactly Held Bob-coins, Orga has Bob's line at the number Line, Total is Held plus Sum and Total is at most Line.
