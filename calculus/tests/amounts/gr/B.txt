B: Bank
Bob is a person, Orga a bank, Sum, Line, Held and Total numbers.
1. ■ Issue: Bob may issue Sum Bob-coins.
2. ■ Set: Orga may set Bob's line at Line.
3. ■ Credit: Bob may give Orga Sum Bob-coins, provided Orga holds exactly Held Bob-coins, Orga has Bob's line at the number Line, Total is Held plus Sum and Total is at most Line. Double: Bob may give Orga 2 Bob-coins, provided Line is more than 2.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x holds a y-coin = coin(x,y); x holds n y-coins = coin(x,y,*n); x holds exactly n y-coins = coin(x,y,#n); z has y's line at the number n = line(z,y,n).
y's earlier line in z has ended: z has y's line at no number.
After x issues n x-coins: x holds n x-coins.
After z sets y's line at n: y's earlier line in z has ended and z has y's line at the number n.
After y gives z n y-coins: no longer y holds n y-coins and z holds n y-coins.
