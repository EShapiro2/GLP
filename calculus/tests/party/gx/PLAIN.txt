PLAIN: A Currency Without Wards
Of CUR: A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity. Alice and Bob are parties, Sum and Total numbers.
Of PLAIN: Alice and Bob are parties, Sum a number.
CUR:1, PLAIN:1. ■ Pay known: Alice may pay Bob Sum Alice-coins, provided Alice knows Bob and Bob knows Alice.
CUR:2. ■ Swap: Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Alice-coins for Total Bob-coins.
