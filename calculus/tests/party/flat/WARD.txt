WARD: Wards (flat text)
WARD:0 - | A ward takes an act of a party by those who decide for it.
WARD:0 - | Alice is a person, Carol a ward.
WARD:1 ■ Enter: Alice may Carol is a ward, Carol has Alice as a guardian and Alice is a guardian of Carol, provided Alice is a person.
WARD:2 ■ Retire: Those who decide for Carol may Carol is retired, provided not Carol is retired.
