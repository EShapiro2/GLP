GAS: Grassroots App Store
1. ■ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity, and then Alice is a person.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other, Alice knows Carol, Alice is a person, Bob is a person and Carol is a person.
4. ■ Befriend.  Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other, Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
5. ■ Person unfriend.  Alice may end her friendship with Bob, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
6. ■ Sign.  Alice may create Item by signing it.
7. ■ Forward.  Alice may forward Item to Bob, adding her signature to the signatures Item carries, provided Alice holds the item Item and Alice and Bob are friends.
8. ◆ Publish.  Carol may publish Prog by signing it.
9. ■ Trust.  Alice may declare trust in Carol as a developer, provided Alice knows Carol.
10. ■ Untrust.  Alice may withdraw her declaration of trust in Carol as a developer.
11. ■ Offer.  Carol may offer Prog of Dave to Alice, provided Carol holds Prog of Dave and Carol and Alice are friends.
12. ■ Install own.  Alice may install her own Prog, provided Alice holds Prog of Alice.
13. ■ Install.  Alice may install Prog of Carol, provided Alice holds Prog of Carol and Alice trusts Carol as a developer.
14. ■ Invite.  Alice may invite Bob to join in Prog of Carol, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob are friends, Alice has Prog of Carol installed and Bob trusts Carol as a developer.
15. ■ Leave.  Alice may leave Prog of Carol.
16. ■ Enter.  Alice may create Carol, and Carol must consent, provided Alice is a person.
17. ■ Make guardian.  The guardians of Carol may make Bob a guardian of Carol, with the consent of Bob, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired, Bob is a person, Carol has Alice as a guardian and Bob counts Alice as a friend.
18. ■ Resign.  Bob may end his guardianship of Carol.
19. ■ End guardian.  The guardians of Carol, other than Bob, may end Bob's guardianship of Carol.
20. ■ Guardian unfriend.  Bob may end his friendship with his ward Carol.
21. ■ Ward acquaint.  Carol and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Carol is a ward, Carol has Alice as a guardian and Bob is a person.
22. ■ Ward befriend.  Carol and Bob may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Bob is a person, Carol and Bob know each other and Alice and Bob are friends.
23. ■ Ward end.  Carol may end her friendship with Bob, provided Bob is not a guardian of Carol.
24. ■ Unfriend.  Bob may end his friendship with Carol, provided Bob is not a guardian of Carol.
25. ■ Ward unfriend.  Alice may end the friendship of Carol and Bob, provided Carol has Alice as a guardian and Bob is not a guardian of Carol.
26. ■ Wards acquaint.  Carol and Dave may jointly come to know each other, provided Carol is a ward, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Dave is a ward and Dave has Bob as a guardian.
27. ■ Wards befriend.  Carol and Dave may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice and Bob, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Dave is a ward, Dave is not retired, Dave has Bob as a guardian, Carol and Dave know each other, Alice knows Dave and Alice and Bob are friends.
28. ■ Wards of one guardian befriend.  Carol and Dave may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Dave is a ward, Dave is not retired, Dave has Alice as a guardian and Carol and Dave know each other.
29. ■ Retire.  The guardians of Carol may retire Carol.
30. ■ Open store.  Carol may make Dave a store, provided Carol is a guardian of Dave.
31. ○ List.  Carol may list in Dave Prog of Bob, provided Carol is a guardian of Dave, Dave is a store, Carol trusts Bob as a developer and Carol holds Prog of Bob.
32. ○ Delist.  Carol may delist from Dave Prog of Bob, provided Carol is a guardian of Dave.
33. ■ Trust store.  Alice may declare trust in Dave, provided Dave is a store, Carol is a guardian of Dave, Alice knows Carol and Alice knows Dave.
34. ■ Untrust store.  Alice may withdraw her declaration of trust in Dave.
35. ■ Install from store.  Alice may install from a store Prog of Bob, provided Dave lists Prog of Bob and Alice trusts the store Dave.
