GNG: Network Gig Work
1. ■ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity, and then Alice is a person.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other, Alice knows Carol, Alice is a person, Bob is a person and Carol is a person.
4. ■ Befriend.  Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other, Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
5. ■ Person unfriend.  Alice may end her friendship with Bob, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
6. ■ Enter.  Alice may create Carol, and Carol must consent, provided Alice is a person.
7. ■ Appoint.  Those who decide for Carol may make Bob an officer of Carol, with the consent of Bob, provided Carol is an organisation, Carol is not retired, Bob is a person, Carol has Alice as an officer, Bob counts Alice as a friend, Bob is a participant of Carol and every treasurer of Carol stays backed for Carol, and then Bob holds the role Role in Carol and Carol has Bob in the role Role.
8. ■ Resign.  Bob may end his guardianship of Carol, provided every treasurer of Carol stays backed, and then Bob holds no role in Carol and Carol has Bob in no role.
9. ■ End officer.  Those who decide for Carol, other than Bob, may end Bob's guardianship of Carol, provided every treasurer of Carol stays backed for Carol, and then Bob holds no role in Carol and Carol has Bob in no role.
10. ■ Officer unfriend.  Bob may end his friendship with his organisation Carol, provided every treasurer of Carol stays backed, and then Bob holds no role in Carol and Carol has Bob in no role.
11. ■ Organisation acquaint.  Carol and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Carol is an organisation, Carol has Alice as an officer and Bob is a person.
12. ■ Organisation befriend.  Carol and Bob may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Carol is an organisation, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as an officer, Bob is a person, Carol and Bob know each other and Alice and Bob are friends.
13. ■ Organisation end.  Carol may end her friendship with Bob, provided Bob is not an officer of Carol.
14. ■ Unfriend.  Bob may end his friendship with Carol, provided Bob is not an officer of Carol.
15. ■ Organisation unfriend.  Alice may end the friendship of Carol and Bob, provided Carol has Alice as an officer and Bob is not an officer of Carol.
16. ■ Organisations acquaint.  Carol and Dave may jointly come to know each other, provided Carol is an organisation, Carol has Alice as an officer, Dave is an organisation and Dave has Bob as an officer.
17. ■ Organisations befriend.  Carol and Dave may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice and Bob, provided Carol is an organisation, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as an officer, Dave is an organisation, Dave is not retired, Dave has Bob as an officer, Carol and Dave know each other, Alice knows Dave and Alice and Bob are friends.
18. ■ Organisations of one officer befriend.  Carol and Dave may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Carol is an organisation, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as an officer, Dave is an organisation, Dave is not retired, Dave has Alice as an officer and Carol and Dave know each other.
19. ■ Retire.  Those who decide for Carol may retire Carol, and then no one holds any role in Carol and Carol has no one in any role.
20. ■ Found.  Gov may create Orga with Alice as its first officer in the role Role, with the consent of Alice, and Orga must consent, provided Alice is a person.
21. ■ Propose.  Bob may propose Motion in Orga, provided Bob is a treasurer of Orga.
22. ■ Vote.  Bob may vote on Motion in Orga, provided Bob is a treasurer of Orga, Orga is an organisation and Orga has Motion proposed.
23. ■ Act.  Alice may take Motion for Orga, provided Alice is a treasurer of Orga, Orga holds at least Votes persons's vote on Motion, More is Votes plus 1 and Orga has fewer than More persons in the role Treasurer.
24. ■ Join.  Bob may become a participant of Orga, with the consent of those who decide for Orga.
25. ■ Leave.  Bob may end his participation in Orga, provided Bob is not an officer of Orga and every treasurer of Orga stays backed.
26. ■ Officer leave.  Bob may end his participation and his office in Orga, provided every treasurer of Orga stays backed.
27. ■ Expel.  Those who decide for Orga may end Bob's participation in Orga, provided Bob is not an officer of Orga and every treasurer of Orga stays backed for Orga.
28. ■ Officer expel.  Those who decide for Orga may end Bob's participation and Bob's office in Orga, provided every treasurer of Orga stays backed for Orga.
29. ■ Amend.  Alice may amend the bylaws of Orga as Motion says, provided Alice is a treasurer of Orga, Orga is an organisation, Orga has Motion proposed, Orga holds at least Votes persons's vote on Motion, More is Votes plus 1 and Orga has fewer than More persons in the role Treasurer.
30. ■ Issue.  Alice may issue Sum Alice-coins, provided Alice has issued coins of the denomination Dollar.
31. ■ Swap.  Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Carol-coins for Total Dave-coins, provided Alice and Bob know each other, the coins and bonds of Carol and Dave are of one denomination and every treasurer of Orga stays backed.
32. ■ Pay.  Alice may pay Bob Sum Bob-coins, provided Alice knows Bob and every treasurer of Orga stays backed.
33. ■ Redeem.  Alice may redeem a Bob-coin against a Carol-coin, provided Alice knows Bob, the coins and bonds of Bob and Carol are of one denomination and every treasurer of Orga stays backed.
34. ■ Issue bond.  Alice may issue Sum Alice-bonds due Day, provided the current date is Today and Day is more than Today.
35. ■ Swap coin for bond.  Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Carol-coins for Total Dave-bonds due Day, provided Alice and Bob know each other, the coins and bonds of Carol and Dave are of one denomination and every treasurer of Orga stays backed.
36. ■ Swap bonds.  Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Carol-bonds due Day for Total Dave-bonds due Term, provided Alice and Bob know each other, the coins and bonds of Carol and Dave are of one denomination and every treasurer of Orga stays backed.
37. ■ Redeem coin against bond.  Alice may redeem a Bob-coin against a Carol-bond due Day, provided Alice knows Bob, the coins and bonds of Bob and Carol are of one denomination and every treasurer of Orga stays backed.
38. ■ Redeem bond.  Alice may redeem a Bob-bond due Day against a Carol-coin, provided Alice knows Bob, the current date is Today for Bob, Day is at most Today, the coins and bonds of Bob and Carol are of one denomination and every treasurer of Orga stays backed.
39. ■ Redeem bond against bond.  Alice may redeem a Bob-bond due Day against a Carol-bond due Term, provided Alice knows Bob, the current date is Today for Bob, Day is at most Today, the coins and bonds of Bob and Carol are of one denomination and every treasurer of Orga stays backed.
40. ■ Deposit.  Alice may deposit Sum more Dollar-denominated Dave-coins with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice knows Bob, Dave has issued coins of the denomination Dollar, Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in no denomination and every treasurer of Orga stays backed.
41. ■ Deposit bond.  Alice may deposit Sum more Dollar-denominated Dave-bonds due Day with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice knows Bob, Dave has issued a Dollar-denominated bond due Day, Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in no denomination and every treasurer of Orga stays backed.
42. ■ Release.  Carol and Bob may jointly release Sum Dave-coins that Alice deposited on Cond, provided Carol and Bob know each other and every treasurer of Orga stays backed.
43. ■ Release bond.  Carol and Bob may jointly release Sum Dave-bonds due Day that Alice deposited on Cond, provided Carol and Bob know each other and every treasurer of Orga stays backed.
44. ■ Return.  Carol and Alice may jointly return Sum Dave-coins deposited for Bob on Cond, provided Carol and Alice know each other and every treasurer of Orga stays backed.
45. ■ Return bond.  Carol and Alice may jointly return Sum Dave-bonds due Day deposited for Bob on Cond, provided Carol and Alice know each other and every treasurer of Orga stays backed.
46. ■ First issue.  Alice may issue Sum Dollar-denominated Alice-coins, provided Alice has issued coins of no denomination.
47. ■ Issue denominated bond.  Alice may issue Sum Dollar-denominated Alice-bonds due Day, provided the current date is Today and Day is more than Today.
48. ■ Deposit more.  Alice may deposit Sum more Dollar-denominated Dave-coins with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice knows Bob, Dave has issued coins of the denomination Dollar and Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in the denomination Dollar.
49. ■ Deposit bond more.  Alice may deposit Sum more Dollar-denominated Dave-bonds due Day with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice knows Bob, Dave has issued a Dollar-denominated bond due Day and Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in the denomination Dollar.
50. ■ Accept.  Alice may accept Limit more Dollar-denominated Carol-coins in payment, provided Alice holds a Dollar-denominated Carol-coin.
51. ■ Accept by bond.  Alice may accept Limit more Dollar-denominated Carol-coins in payment, provided Alice holds a Carol-bond due Day and Carol has issued a Dollar-denominated bond due Due.
52. ■ Pay accepted coins.  Alice may pay Bob a Carol-coin that Bob accepts as denominated by Dollar, provided Alice knows Bob, Carol has issued coins of the denomination Dollar and every treasurer of Orga stays backed.
53. ■ Draw.  Alice may swap Sum Alice-coins for Sum Orga-coins, provided Alice is a treasurer of Orga and Alice stays backed in Orga.
54. ◆ Record.  The treasurers of Orga may record a Dollar as paid into the account of Orga by Alice, with the consent of Alice, provided Orga is an organisation and Alice is a treasurer of Orga.
55. ■ Set stake.  The treasurers of Orga may set the stake of Orga at S, provided Orga is an organisation.
56. ■ Raise.  Alice may raise to Carol her dispute with the provider Bob over Orga-coins, provided Orga-coins are locked between the provider Bob and the customer Alice and Carol is an arbiter of Orga.
57. ■ Provider raise.  Bob may raise to Carol his dispute with the customer Alice over Orga-coins, provided Orga-coins are locked between the provider Bob and the customer Alice and Carol is an arbiter of Orga.
58. ■ Arbitrate.  Carol may decide the release to Alice and to Bob of the Orga-coins locked between them.
59. ■ Point friend.  Alice may point to Bob, provided Alice and Bob are friends.
60. ■ Point transitive.  Alice may point to Carol, provided Alice knows Carol, Alice points to Bob and Bob points to Carol.
61. ■ Unpoint.  Alice may cease pointing to Bob.
62. ◆ Recommend.  Alice may recommend Bob for Skill, provided Alice knows Bob and Alice has accepted Skill of Bob.
63. ■ Show.  Bob may show Carol Alice's recommendation for Skill, provided Bob holds Alice's recommendation for Skill and Bob knows Carol.
64. ■ Withdraw.  Alice may withdraw her recommendation of Bob for Skill.
65. ■ Post.  Alice may post Job in Orga, provided Alice is a client of Orga.
66. ■ Send job.  Job of Alice goes to Bob in Orga, provided Alice has Job posted in Orga, Alice points to Bob and Bob is a worker of Orga.
67. ■ Pass.  Bob may pass Job of Alice to Carol in Orga, provided Bob is a worker of Orga, Bob has received Job of Alice in Orga, Bob points to Carol, Orga has the stake at the number S, Carol holds Kept Orga-coins and Kept is at least S.
68. ■ Withdraw job.  Alice may withdraw Job from Orga.
69. ■ Engage.  Alice may engage Bob for Job in Orga, with the consent of Bob, provided Bob is a worker of Orga, Alice has Job posted in Orga and Alice and Bob know each other.
70. ◆ Deliver.  Bob may attest the delivery of Job to Alice, provided Alice and Bob have an engagement for Job.
71. ■ Accept work.  Alice may accept Job of Bob in Orga, provided Bob has attested the delivery of Job to Alice.
72. ■ Reject.  Alice may reject Job of Bob raising to Carol the dispute over Orga-coins, provided Bob has attested the delivery of Job to Alice, Orga-coins are locked between the provider Bob and the customer Alice and Carol is an arbiter of Orga.
73. ■ Cancel.  Alice may cancel her engagement of Bob for Job in Orga, provided Bob has not attested the delivery of Job to Alice.
74. ■ Worker cancel.  Bob may cancel his engagement by Alice for Job in Orga, provided Bob has not attested the delivery of Job to Alice.
75. ■ Lapse.  Alice may cancel her engagement of Bob for Job in Orga, provided no delivery of Job is attested by the deadline.
