Writing a contract in the grammar, for tools/gcsas.py

rev/NAME.txt is the contract as revised with Udi.  gr/NAME.txt says the same in the grammar of Section 1.1 of the paper, so that tools/gcsas.py compiles it to schemas (sas/), recovers it from them (back/), expands it on the text (flat/) and on the schemas (sasx/), and checks the three.  Models: gr/GKK.txt, GSG.txt, Coins.txt, GD.txt, GD-S.txt, GnW.txt, GSV.txt, GO.txt.  Read them, their rev/ originals, and Sections 1 and 2 of sections/01-contracts-schemas.tex first.

THE FILE, line by line, in this order
1. The title, as in rev/.
2. The first line, as in rev/, with its reference in parentheses if it has one.  An instantiation is written "T instantiates P with ITEMS, and ..."; the items are separated by ", " and " and ", or all by "; " if one of them has a comma or an "and".  An item is: a word for a word, with their articles ("an organisation for a ward"); a state or a role for a state or a role of P, with places ("the guardians of z for those who decide for z"); or prose ("bylaws"), which does nothing.  A state put for a state may have one more name, as GO's "y holds the role Role in x for y is a guardian of x": each act of P with the state then has the name Role; where the state must not hold, or ends for someone unnamed, the name is anyone ("Bob holds no role in Orga").  Write the items that put a state before those that put a word.  "no role" and "some role" are written for a place after "the role".
3. "Vocabulary: PHRASE = pred(x,y); PHRASE = pred(x)." the basic states this contract adds, each a phrase with places and its predicate.  The first place of the predicate is the holder, an agent (a person, a ward, an organisation).  A fact two agents hold is two basic states or a defined state of two.  A state whose holder a person would not name is written "PHRASE, for x = pred(x,e)", as "e is later than the current date, for x = later(x,e)": a sentence writes "Day is later than the current date", and its holder is the first of those who take the act; in a citation, the first of those who take the act cited.
4. "Roles: PHRASE = pred(z)." phrases for those who act, as "those who decide for z".
5. Definitions, one a line.  A state: "x and y are friends: x counts y as a friend and y counts x as a friend."  An act: "After x ends x's friendship with y: no longer x and y are friends." --- the act phrase after "After", with its subject places first ("x", "x and y", "x, y and z") and its verb in the third person if the subject is one place; then the states that hold after it, and "no longer" before each state that ends.
6. Prose lines of rev/ that define a term for the reader (they have *term*), kept as they are; and the names line of rev/, with every name the clauses use.  A contract whose own clauses and definitions use no name has no names line, in rev/ or here: GA, "GA composes GPE with GAS and GDS." and nothing else; the names of the acts it has from other contracts are those contracts'.
7. The clauses: "N. MARK SENTENCE SENTENCE ...", N and MARK as in rev/.

SENTENCES
An act:      "Name: Alice may PHRASE[, with the consent of PERSONS][, provided CONDITIONS][, on the votes of NAME for it]."
             "Name: Alice and Bob may jointly PHRASE[...]."      several who act together
             "Name: PHRASE[, provided CONDITIONS]."               an act nobody decides: the full act phrase, present tense
A citation:  "Name: C:n[ Act name][, only if CONDITIONS][, with the consent of PERSONS]."   it restricts the acts of clause C:n (the one named, if named) and renames a single act to Name
             "Name: C:n[ Act name], for KINDs[, only if CONDITIONS][, with the consent of PERSONS]."   a restriction for a special case: of the acts cited, only the forms of the kinds of note named (KINDS OF NOTE)
             "Name: C:n[ Act name][, only if ...][, with the consent of ...], decided by ROLE in place of ROLE2[, and then ...]."   the role that decides the act cited put for the one it had, those other than whom kept: GO:4's Appoint and End officer, decided by those who decide the officers of Orga, a role of GO with the default those who decide for Orga
             "Name: C:n Act name, taken for NAME by NAME[ and for NAME by NAME][, only if ...][, with the consent of ...][, and then ...][, on the votes of NAME[ and of NAME] for it]."   see TAKEN FOR A PARTY, ON VOTES
Anything else is kept word for word and has no schema: an undertaking ("Alice will ..."), a "must", a "may not", the title of a clause ("Proposing.").  A kept sentence must not begin "Word: ".
A place of a state may hold "someone" (a condition: the state holds of some person) or "no one" (a condition: of no person; an effect: the state that then holds, which ends it for every person of whom it held): "Bob has a Dave-pointer to someone", "no one is a member of g".  Give such an effect a phrase a person would write, defined once: "z has no guardians: no one is a guardian of z and z has no one as a guardian."  Never "no longer anyone ...".  In a schema it is the wildcard _.
A place of a condition may count: "fewer than D persons", "more than D persons", "at most 3 persons", "at least 2 persons", the number a parameter written with a capital or digits: "Alice points to more than D persons".  In a schema it is the count, >D.  A counted place before "'s" is written "the votes of at least Votes persons", the noun after "'s" in the plural: "Orga holds the votes of at least Votes persons on Motion", read and printed for "at least Votes persons's vote".  A name of the kind issuer, "Iris an issuer", is the issuer of the coins an act moves and no party to it: its schema holds it as a value, and the denomination is said of the receiver, "Bob accepts Sum Dollar-denominated Iris-coins in payment".
CONDITIONS and the right side of a definition are lists of states, with ", " and " and "; "not" before a condition that must not hold.  PERSONS are names and role phrases.

WHAT THE PROGRAM CAN READ
- A place is one lowercase letter, b to z (never a); it may carry 's or a hyphenated suffix (x's, x-pointer).  A name is one capitalised word of letters (Alice, Orga, Prop).  Every thing a sentence speaks of is a name of the names line: "Alice and Bob are persons, Delos a DAO, Prop a proposal."  A contract with no sentence of its own has no names line.
- After "may" the verb is in its base form and the subject's own "x's" is written her, his, its (Alice her, Bob his, Carol her, Dave his, Erin her, any other name its); "of x's" at the end of its noun phrase is written of hers, of his, of its ("under a new name of hers").
- The owners' words: "x holds a y-coin", and so "a Bob-coin", "an Alice-bond due Day", "Orga-coins", "a Dollar-denominated Carol-coin"; "a" or "an" before a place is written as the name put for it needs.
- No phrase has "may", ", provided", ", with the consent of", or ". " followed by a capital.
- A phrase of a contract this one is written from is used as that contract writes it; after an instantiation by a word, with the new word.
- Every place of an act phrase is its subject or a place of one of its effects: else the contract cannot be recovered from the schema ("no act phrase has these effects").
- Two act phrases may have the same effects (GnW: Resign and End guardian).

HOW TO SAY IT (decisions taken for Udi's later approval; follow them)
1. Keep the words of rev/ wherever the grammar allows.  Change nothing of substance.  Invent no rule.
2. One sentence an act.  "Either Alice or Bob may", "Vote for, Vote against:", "Remove, Leave:" are two sentences with two names.  An act with an effect "if ..." is two acts (GD:1).
3. "Alice, who knows Bob, may ..." becomes "..., provided Alice knows Bob".
4. A condition never repeats a state the act ends: the schema already requires it.  Where the text says "new", "once", "not yet", add the "not ..." condition.
5. An act moves an amount of units, a number that is a name of the names line ("Sum and Total numbers"): "Alice may pay Bob Sum Bob-coins".  The vocabulary has the state of one unit and of a number of them: "x holds a y-coin = coin(x,y); x holds n y-coins = coin(x,y,*n)", the last argument *n saying n copies; the definition of the act is of the amount ("After x pays y n y-coins: no longer x holds n y-coins and y holds n y-coins").  An act a clause writes of one unit stays of one unit (Coins:4, DGC:3, DGC:4).  The state of an agent is a multiset.
6. What the grammar cannot break down --- "every member", "more than half", "at most d", "before the night", a computed amount --- is a basic state named by its phrase, with the names it speaks of as places and its holder first ("more than half of the members of d have voted for p = majority_for(d,p)").  Keep such phrases few and close to the text.
7. A state of mind (trust, belief) is no condition: it goes in an undertaking sentence (GSG:1).  A declaration on record is a state (GD).
8. An act the machine takes with nobody deciding ("the steward delivers it to every member") is an act with no persons.
9. A parameter of rev/ (the decision rule of GnW, the message of GSN, the terms of GRA) is a basic state or a role that a contract written from this one replaces in its first line; or prose, if no clause's schema depends on it.
   A parameter may have a default, a line of the Logic pragmas, "Default: STATE for PARAMETER.", written as a first line writes a binding: "Default: x is an officer of z for x is entitled to vote on m in z."  A contract written from it whose chain of first lines binds the parameter overrides the default; one that does not takes it, and the contract itself is printed at its defaults.  A default may name another parameter, which a first line may bind.  A role may have a default too, GnW's decision rule, "Default: any guardian of z for those who decide for z.", given by a first line that puts a role for it or instantiates its contract with the rule in words ("GO instantiates GnW with bylaws").  A role "= any_guardian(z), any one listed by STATES" is one whom the states list, deciding alone; a role "listed by" is every one of them.  A parameter with no default stays open, and the term parametric in it (GRA's two, GNO's two).  A count counts the places after the holder: GO's decision counts "z has fewer than Double persons as an officer"; a contract that names other voters binds the decision with its own count, "z has fewer than Double persons in the role Director", since a count of holders ("fewer than Double persons are entitled to vote on m in z") is no term GSC's check takes.
10. A citation "X:n, where ..." is written "only if" with basic states for what follows "where".  A citation of several clauses is several citations.  A citation is written in the names of its own contract: a name that the act cited does not have stands for the one name of its kind of that act which the citation does not write (GO:4 writes Orga, an organisation, for GnW:2's Carol, a ward, which GO's first line makes an organisation); with no such name it is one more name of the act.  The kinds are those of the names lines.
11. A sentence of rev/ that only says what cannot happen, or repeats a definition, is kept as a kept sentence.

POSSIBLY ONE PERSON
The names of a sentence are different persons, but for those the names line says are possibly one: "Carol possibly Alice or Bob", "Carol and Dave possibly Alice, Bob or one person", "in clause 4 Dave is possibly Alice, Bob or Carol", "Frank is possibly any of the others or Orga".  The program writes with each schema the pairs of its names that are possibly one ("possibly Alice=Carol").  Where a condition cannot hold of one person, as "Alice and Bob are friends", write the case of one person as a sentence of its own (GnW:4, Wards of one guardian befriend).

RUN AND CHECK
python3 tools/gcsas.py NAME [NAME ...]     prints, for each contract and those it is written from: commute, return, recover.
Wanted: "commute yes; return yes; recover same".  "recover equivalent" is acceptable where two wordings have the same schemas; say which in the notes.  Then read sas/NAME.txt against rev/NAME.txt: each schema must say what its sentence says.
Do not edit tools/gcsas.py, or a contract of gr/ that is not on your list: note what you need of either and go on.
Notes go in gr/NOTES-<your group>.txt, one line each: "NAME:n D ..." a decision you took; "NAME:n Q ..." a question for Udi; "TOOL ..." what the program could not read; "PARENT NAME ..." what you need changed in a contract not yours.

NUMBERS
Every contract has the states of numbers "n is at most m", "n is at least m", "n is more than m", "n is less than m", "n is m plus k", "n is m times k" and "n is the difference from m to k", the larger of m and k less the smaller (le, ge, gt, lt, sum, product, difference; toglp.py writes a difference as its two linear cases joined by or, which the checker decides); a place of a number holds a name of the kind number or a numeral.  A state of units has a third form, "x holds exactly n y-coins = coin(x,y,#n)": n of them and no more, a condition only.  A number that an act sets is a place after its noun, "z has y's credit line at the number n", so that "at no number" ends the one set before; give that a phrase, defined once: "y's earlier credit line in z has ended: z has y's credit line at no number." (CB:2, CB:3, SCB:3, SC:2, CnC:2).

STRENGTHENING
A citation may add effects to the act it cites: "Name: C:n[ Act name][, only if CONDITIONS][, with the consent of PERSONS], and then EFFECTS."  The effects are basic states of this contract or of another it is written from, never of the contract cited: "Befriend: GSG:1, and then Alice has a Bob-pointer to Bob and Bob has an Alice-pointer to Alice."  "no longer STATE" ends a state, which the act then requires; a state of no one ends it for all without requiring it: "Alice is a member of no group of Bob"; of two places of no one named the second is written "any": "no one holds any role in Orga".  Do not also write "only if" a state the strengthening ends.  The first line says "strengthens".

TAKEN FOR A PARTY, ON VOTES
For the paper's Section 1.1:
(a) A citation "Swap for Orga: DGC:2 Swap, taken for Alice by Erin, only if Erin is an officer of Alice." adds to the contract one more act, the act cited taken for Alice by Erin, with the conditions, consents and effects the citation adds; the act cited stays as it was, for Alice to take herself.  In the schema of the new act Erin guards in Alice's place, and Alice remains a party to it, whose atoms the act requires and changes ("and for Bob by Frank" takes the act for both parties of a joint act).
(b) An act or a citation ending ", on the votes of Orga for it" is decided by Orga's votes on that very act: the program derives from GO's states of a motion m the states of this motion, "Orga holds Voter's vote on whether Orga pays Bob Sum Orga-coins", "Voter is entitled to vote on whether ... in Orga" and "the votes decide whether ... according to the bylaws of Orga", adds the vote act "Vote of Orga for Pay: Voter may vote on whether Orga pays Bob Sum Orga-coins in Orga, provided Voter is entitled to vote on whether ...", requires of the act that the votes decide it, and has it clear the votes ("and then Orga holds no one's vote on whether ...").  The entitlement and the decision are parameters, as GO's are, and a contract whose first line binds GO's "x is entitled to vote on m in z" and "the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z" (GDAO, GCC, WC) binds them with the same states, m read as "whether" and the act, so that the decision becomes the count its bylaws set: "Orga holds the votes of at least Votes persons on whether Orga pays Bob Sum Orga-coins, Double is Votes times 2 and Orga has fewer than Double persons as a participant".
The rules: a citation taken for a party, or on votes, names one act; NAME is a name of the act; the contract is written from GO and does not itself bind GO's states of a motion; the one who votes is named Voter, so an act on votes does not name Voter.  The motion is the act phrase with its names: two citations of one act on one organisation's votes, or two acts of one phrase (DGC's Deposit and Deposit more), share its votes and its vote act.  In the schemas the citation is written "DGC:2 Swap, taken for Alice by Erin, on the votes of Alice + ... <- ..." and an act "... guarded by Alice on the votes of Orga."; the derived predicates are GO's with the act's name and the organisation's, vote_pay_alice, voter_pay_alice, decided_pay_alice.

PARTIES AND WARDS (derived, not written)
For the paper's Section 1.1:
(c) A name a contract declares a party is a person or a ward: in a contract with wards, every act whose guards include names its own contract declares parties keeps its person's form and has, derived and not written, for each nonempty set S of those guards, a ward's form "<Act> for Alice" or "<Act> for Alice and Bob", in which each ward of S takes the act by one who decides for it.  Written from GO, that one is the officer AliceOfficer, as the citation "Swap for Alice: Coins:2 Swap, taken for Alice by AliceOfficer, only if AliceOfficer is entitled to take it for Alice, on the votes of Alice for it." would have it (GO:2's Act, the entitlement and the decision bound as the bylaws bind GO's); written from GnW alone, it is those who decide for Alice.
The rules: a contract has wards when its lexicon has GnW's role "those who decide for z"; the forms are derived once, in the first contract with wards that has the act (GBank, GCorp, GCoop), and inherited from it; an act that names a ward as such (GnW's, GO's own) and an act of persons are not derived.  A citation of an act by its name also restricts its ward's forms, and renames them with it ("Pay for Alice" for "Pay accepted coins for Alice"); a vote act is cited by no citation of the clause it comes from.  In sasx/ a ward's form is written "... guarded by AliceOfficer, Bob taken for Alice by AliceOfficer."; toglp.py writes a party, AliceOfficer and Voter as persons.  The numbers a decision on the votes of Alice counts with are named for Alice, VotesAlice and MoreAlice, so that an act on the votes of two organisations counts the votes of each against its own number.

PRONOUNS AND POSSESSIVES
A sentence may say what follows its act phrase as a person says it (Udi, 2026-11-16: "Ward end: Carol may end her friendship with Bob, provided he is not her guardian" is crystal clear).  gcsas.py reads a sentence first as written; where that reading fails, it reads it again with its pronouns and possessive states put in the plain form, in what follows the act phrase or the cited act (", provided", ", only if", ", with the consent of", ", and then"); in the act phrase "her" is the subject's own, as above.
- A pronoun stands for the one person the sentence has named before it whom it can name: she, her, hers, herself for Alice, Alicia, Carol, Erin; he, him, his, himself for Bob, Dave, Frank; it, its, itself, they, them, their, theirs, themselves for any other agent name (an organisation, an AI agent, a service, an issuer).  A pronoun that could name two such persons, or names none, is refused, naming them; it is never guessed.  "her" before a word that is not a preposition or a conjunction ("her guardian", "her Delos", "her call Trip") is the possessive, else the object; "of hers", "of his" are "of NAME".
- A possessive state by the vocabulary's own phrases.  Where the vocabulary has "x has y as a N", "Y is X's N" ("he is her guardian") is "X has Y as a N", and "Y is not X's N" is "X does not have Y as a N".  Where it has no such phrase and has "x is a N of y", "Y is X's N" is "Y is a N of X", negated likewise.  A phrase the vocabulary has as written stays as written.
- The flat text, the expanded sentences and the recovered contract print the plain form; a contract written so recovers "equivalent".
For the paper, Section 1.1: "A sentence may use pronouns and possessives for the persons it has already named, as 'provided he is not her guardian': a pronoun stands for the one person named before it whom it can name, and a possessive 'Y is X's N' is the vocabulary's 'X has Y as a N', or else its 'Y is a N of X'; where a pronoun could name two persons, the sentence is refused."

RELAXATION (Udi, 2026-11-16, to Calculus #7)
A relaxing citation adds an act and keeps the act it cites:
  "Name: C:n[ Act name], without the consent of PERSONS, without CONDITIONS, if CONDITIONS."
At least one of the two "without" parts; each part may be left out, in this order.  The act Name is the act of C:n (the
one named; the clause must have one act, or the citation names it) without the consents of PERSONS, without the
conditions named, and with the conditions after "if".  A condition dropped must be a condition of the act, not an atom
its head ends; a person dropped must be one who decides it or must consent.  "if X is Y", of two names of the act, says
that one agent is both: the act is written with Y for X ("Pay issuer: Notes:3, without the consent of Bob, if Carol is
Bob": the issuer of the notes paid is the payee).  The names are those of the citing contract, as in every citation
(rule 10).  The first line says "relaxes".  In the schemas the citation is the line "C:n Act relaxed; one X=Y; without
the consent of P; without LITERALS <- IF-LITERALS", and the act added follows the act cited, labelled C:n+N:k.

A CONTRACT HAS NO REDUNDANT ACT (Udi, 2026-11-16)
An act is redundant if another act of the contract logically implies it.  The will of one who is asked is a condition of the act; one who must consent is not asked, and their will is no condition.  So GKK:2 Acquaint, which asks Alice and Bob, does not make GPE:2 Encounter redundant, which asks no one (Udi, 2026-11-16, to Calculus #7).
An act X is redundant when another act Y of the same full contract has the same effects under a one-to-one renaming of
names of one kind, and Y's conditions and persons are among X's, Y allowing each binding of one agent X allows
(tools/redundant.py).  A redundant act is removed from the expanded schemas, the flat text and the term; of two acts
equal both ways the one of the citing contract stays, else the first.  gcsas.py prints "NAME removed C:n Act, made
redundant by C:m Act2"; a later citation of the act removed is refused, naming the act that took its place.  So a
relaxation with no "if" has the relaxed act in place of the act cited, and one with an "if" has both.  The removal is
behind a switch (gcsas.py --remove; a case of tests/ with a file 'remove'), off for the 62 until the Calculus says.

KINDS OF NOTE (Udi, 2026-11-16: a coin, a bond and a share are special cases of a note, not instances)
Notes writes its acts of notes, "x holds n y-notes = note(x,y,*n)".  A contract with a special case declares it in its
Logic pragmas, after its definitions:
  "Kind of note: WORD, NOUN for y-notes[; its acts keep their names]."
as Coins "Kind of note: coin, y-coins for y-notes; its acts keep their names.", Bonds "Kind of note: bond, y-bonds due Day
for y-notes.", Shares "Kind of note: share, shares of y for y-notes.".  Its vocabulary has the state of each note state of
Notes with the noun put for "y-notes" (Bonds: "x holds n y-bonds due e" and "z holds n w-bonds due e in escrow from x for y
on k"), and a name the noun adds (Day) is of a kind of its names line with as many names as an act has notes (Day and
Term maturity dates).  gcsas.py then writes each act of notes as one act for each kind of each of its notes
(NOTES-GRAMMAR.md, "Kinds of note"): never instantiate Notes with a word for "note", and never write the acts of a kind
by hand.  Cite Notes's clause for every kind ("Swap: Notes:2, only if ..."), or a form by its name ("Issue: Notes:1 Issue",
the coins; "Deposit bond: Notes:4 Deposit bond").
A restriction for a special case (Udi, 2026-11-16, to Calculus #8: "Restriction: an implication in C has additional
conditions or guards in C', possibly for a special case of C that C' defines"): "Issue bond: Notes:1, for bonds, only if
the current date is Today and Day is more than Today." restricts Notes:1's form for bonds, and renames it when it is the
one act cited; its forms for the other kinds stay as they are, and so does the act of notes, for a kind added later.
The kinds are named in the plural, "for bonds", "for coins and bonds", each a kind of note of the contract.  A
contract that writes an act for a special case cites it so; it does not write the act again beside it.

A ROLE PUT BY CASES (Calculus #8, 2026-11-16: the bylaws say who decides each act, and SC's definition says it for the
issue of shares)
A first line may put a role for a role by cases, each a role while states, the item written with "; " between items:
  "...; the first officer of z while z has not issued shares, or the shareholders of z while the votes to issue shares
  of z carry a majority of the shares of z for those who decide the issue of shares of z, ..."
Each act the role decides, or must consent to, is then one act for each case: the case's role in the role's place and
its states among the conditions, its places those of the role.  The act of the first case keeps the act's name; the
others are named by it and the case's role, "Issue shares by shareholders" (a ward's form keeps its suffix last).  A
role a contract may want put by cases is declared a parameter of its own, with a default ("Default: those who decide
for z for those who decide the issue of shares of z."), so that acts of another role (GO:3's Join) keep theirs.
In sas/ the item is "first_officers(z) while ~share(_,z) or shareholders(z) while share_majority(z) for share_deciders(z)".
A ward's form of an act that a role of the ward itself decides (Shares:1, with the consent of those who decide the issue of
shares of Alice) is taken for the ward by that role, with no vote: "Issue shares for Alice: ... guarded by
share_deciders(Alice) taken for Alice by share_deciders(Alice)"; so SC's binding of GO's states of a motion, the
directors' votes, is for the other acts of Orga (Calculus #8, 2026-11-16).
