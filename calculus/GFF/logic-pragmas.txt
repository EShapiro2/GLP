GFF: Grassroots Feeds and Followers
GFF extends and restricts GDO-L.
A *feed* is named by its author.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Bob and Carol possibly one person.
1. ■ Post: Alice may post an item to her feed. Alice has a feed, and the item is created or received.
2. ■ Deliver: Alice receives from Bob an item of Carol's feed, provided Alice has a Carol-pointer to Bob, Bob knows Alice and Bob holds an item of Carol's feed.
3. ■ Labeled point transitive: GDO-L:2, only if Carol knows Alice.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x holds an item of z's feed = feed_item(x,z); x has received an item of z's feed from y = received(x,z,y).
After x posts an item to x's feed: x holds an item of x's feed.
After x receives from y an item of z's feed: x holds an item of z's feed and x has received an item of z's feed from y.
