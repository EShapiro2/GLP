SPLLC: Single-Person Limited-Liability Company
SPLLC instantiates GCorp with single-person limited-liability company bylaws; x is an officer of z for x is entitled to propose m in z; x is an officer of z for x is entitled to take m for z; x is an officer of z for x is entitled to vote on m in z; z holds the votes of at least Votes persons on m, More is Votes plus 1 and z has fewer than More persons as an officer for the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z.
A *single-person limited-liability company* is a corporation whose bylaws name one role, the *sole officer*, who decides every act of the company, and admit no participant but the sole officer.
Orga is a single-person limited-liability company, Votes and More numbers.
