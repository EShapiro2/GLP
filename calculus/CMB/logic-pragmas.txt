CMB: Commercial Bank
CMB instantiates GBank with commercial bank bylaws, and restricts and extends it.
A *commercial bank* is a bank that may issue Dollar-denominated coins and bonds, whose bylaws name two roles, *director*, held by its first officer, and *loan officer*; the directors jointly decide the acts of the bank, but a loan officer decides a loan within their *lending limit*, a number of Dollars. A participant who holds no role is a *customer*.
Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Dave possibly Bob, Orga a commercial bank, Dollar a fiat currency, Day a maturity date, Sum, Total and Limit numbers.
1. ■ Leave: GO:3 Leave, only if Orga is an organisation, with the consent of the directors of Orga. Officer leave: GO:3 Officer leave, only if Orga is an organisation, with the consent of the directors of Orga. Expel: GO:3 Expel, only if Orga is an organisation, with the consent of Bob and the directors of Orga. Officer expel: GO:3 Officer expel, only if Orga is an organisation, with the consent of Bob and the directors of Orga.
2. ■ Deposit: Bob may swap Sum Dave-coins for Sum Orga-coins, provided Bob is a customer of Orga and Dave has issued coins of the denomination Dollar.
3. ■ Loan: Bob and those who decide the loan for Orga may jointly swap Sum Orga-coins for Total Bob-bonds due Day, provided Orga is an organisation and Bob is a customer of Orga.
4. ■ Set lending limit: The directors of Orga may set the lending limit of Carol in Orga at Limit, with the consent of Carol, provided Orga is an organisation, Carol is a person and Carol is a loan officer of Orga.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: y is a customer of z = customer(y,z); x is a loan officer of z = loan_officer(x,z); z has the lending limit of x at the number n = lending_limit(z,x,n).
Roles: the directors of z = directors(z); those who decide the loan for z = loan_deciders(z).
After y swaps n w-coins for n z-coins: no longer y holds n w-coins, z holds n w-coins, no longer z holds n z-coins and y holds n z-coins.
After y and g swap n z-coins for m y-bonds due e: no longer z holds n z-coins, y holds n z-coins, no longer y holds m y-bonds due e and z holds m y-bonds due e.
the earlier lending limit of x in z has ended: z has the lending limit of x at no number.
After g sets the lending limit of x in z at n: the earlier lending limit of x in z has ended and z has the lending limit of x at the number n.
