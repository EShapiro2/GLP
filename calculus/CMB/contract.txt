CMB: Commercial Bank
CMB instantiates GBank with commercial bank bylaws and restricts and extends it.
A *commercial bank* is a bank that may issue Dollar-denominated coins and bonds, whose bylaws name two roles, *director*, held by its first officer, and *loan officer*; the directors jointly decide the acts of the bank, but a loan officer decides a loan within their *lending limit*, a number of Dollars. A participant who holds no role is a *customer*.
Bob and Carol are persons, Orga a commercial bank.
1. ■ Leave, Expel: GO:3, only with the consent of both Bob and the directors.
2. ■ Deposit: Bob, a customer, may swap Dollar-denominated coins he holds for Orga-coins.
3. ■ Loan: Bob, a customer, and those who decide the loan for Orga may jointly swap Orga-coins for Bob-bonds.
4. ■ Set lending limit: The directors may set the lending limit of Carol, a loan officer, with her consent.
