What changed since a687ab8:
1. Parties (Udi, 2026-11-15).  GKK: 'A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity.'  GC, GDC, GB, DGC: Alice, Bob
   and Carol are parties.  GnW: 'A ward takes an act of a party by those who decide for it.'  So an organisation, a
   ward, holds, pays and issues coins and bonds.  In GO's family each act of a party has, beside the person's form, the
   organisation's: '<Act> for <Role>', taken for it by an officer on its votes (GO:2), with a vote act; they are derived
   by the tools, about 90 schemas a contract.
2. Roles a contract calls possibly one person are marked one(Name, Other) where GSC's Definition Binding lets one agent
   bear both; where it does not, the contract has an act of one role beside the act: Post own, Ward post own, Propose
   own, Vote own for and against, Delegate own, Delegate to the steward, Revoke own, Count own for and against, Deposit
   own coins and bonds, Deposit with the issuer, Pay own accepted coins, Pay accepted coins to the issuer, Claim own
   coins, Claim coins of the corporation, Bank deposit own coins, Offer own, Want own, Withdraw own.
3. A ward's acts in a group: Ward leave, Ward remove, Ward add (GSNW); in CSSN Child add is restricted so that no
   adult joins a child's group (CSSN:3).
4. DGC's Accept by bond: the bond held and the bond issued in the denomination are of one maturity.
5. A ward's Retire clears her listings at the leaders of her groups (GSNW, CSSN).
6. The tools, after a review: recovery keeps a stated condition beside a wildcard deletion; a role that must consent;
   a negated comparison or count; the readable text keeps every inherited restriction; adopt.py, toglp.py, cascade.py
   and publish.py stop on a failed step; one who must consent is not shown as asked; cascade.py passes the term to the
   reader by a file; tests/ has 30 cases.
