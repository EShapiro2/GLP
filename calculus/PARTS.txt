4. CDC: Constitutional Democratic Community, owner GO, 'CDC restricts and strengthens GO.'; GCoop written from it.  GNO: Grassroots Networked Organisation, 'a networked organisation' in GNO, GAN, GNG, GNL, GNM, ORH.

7. Defaults of parameters; pronouns and possessives; relations.txt for a contract's own default and for 'with D and F'.
8. Names: Agora, Joint, Stay, Lib, Rec; Notes, Coins, Bonds, Shares, each with a title of one word.  adopt.py and owners.py find an owner's file under its old name by GLP_NAME until it is renamed.
