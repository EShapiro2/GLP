SGSG: Secure Grassroots Social Graph
SGSG extends GSG.
The *identity record* of Alice is her *identity custodians*, persons she names, and her *threshold*, a share of them greater than half. The *records* of Alice are her friends and, for each friend, the friends of that friend. Alice suffers a *state loss* when her machine loses some or all of its state and she keeps her private key, and an *identity loss* when she loses her private key.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Alicia a new identity of Alice.
1. ■ Name custodian: Alice may name Carol her identity custodian, provided Alice knows Carol and Alice does not have a fixed identity record. Identity record: Alice may fix her identity record. It then changes only by the replacement of a custodian's lost identity (SGSG:5).
2. ■ Friendship updates. Tell: Alice tells Carol of the friendship of Alice and Bob, provided Alice and Carol are friends and Alice and Bob are friends. Tell end: Alice tells Carol of the end of the friendship of Alice and Bob, provided Alice and Carol are friends and Alice and Bob are not friends. Send record: Alice sends Bob the identity custodian Carol, provided Alice and Bob are friends, Alice has a fixed identity record and Alice has Carol as an identity custodian.
3. ■ Checkpoint: Alice may send Bob a checkpoint of her records, provided Alice and Bob are friends.
4. ■ State recovery. Restore: Bob restores the records of Alice, provided Alice has suffered a state loss and Bob counts Alice as a friend.
5. ◆ Identity replacement. Replace: Alicia may ask Carol to vouch that Alicia is Alice, provided Alicia knows Carol and Carol is an identity custodian of Alice. Alicia asks only after an identity loss of Alice. Vouch: Carol may vouch that Alicia is Alice, provided Carol has been asked to vouch that Alicia is Alice and Carol is an identity custodian of Alice. Accept: Bob accepts Alicia in place of Alice, provided Bob counts Alice as a friend and at least the threshold of the identity custodians Bob records for Alice have vouched that Alicia is Alice. Accept custodian: Bob accepts Alicia as an identity custodian in place of Alice, provided Bob has accepted Alicia in place of Alice and Bob has Alice as an identity custodian. Bob tells Alicia the friends of Alice he records, and tells his friends of the change.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x has z as an identity custodian = custodian(x,z); z is an identity custodian of x = custodian_of(z,x); x has a fixed identity record = fixed(x); z records the friendship of x and y = records_friend(z,x,y); y records z as an identity custodian of x = records_custodian(y,x,z); y keeps a checkpoint of x = checkpoint(y,x); x has suffered a state loss = state_loss(x); x has recovered records from y = recovered(x,y); z has been asked to vouch that w is x = asked(z,w,x); z has vouched that w is x = vouched(z,w,x); at least the threshold of the identity custodians y records for x have vouched that w is x = threshold_vouched(y,x,w); y has accepted w in place of x = accepted(y,w,x).
After x names z x's identity custodian, which requires x does not have z as an identity custodian: x has z as an identity custodian and z is an identity custodian of x.
After x fixes x's identity record, which requires x does not have a fixed identity record: x has a fixed identity record.
After x tells z of the friendship of x and y, which requires z does not record the friendship of x and y: z records the friendship of x and y.
After x tells z of the end of the friendship of x and y: no longer z records the friendship of x and y.
After x sends y the identity custodian z, which requires y does not record z as an identity custodian of x: y records z as an identity custodian of x.
After x sends y a checkpoint of x's records: y keeps a checkpoint of x.
After y restores the records of x, which requires x has not recovered records from y: x counts y as a friend and x has recovered records from y.
After w asks z to vouch that w is x, which requires z has not been asked to vouch that w is x: z has been asked to vouch that w is x.
After z vouches that w is x, which requires z has not vouched that w is x: z has vouched that w is x.
After y accepts w in place of x, which requires y has not accepted w in place of x: no longer y counts x as a friend, y counts w as a friend, w counts y as a friend and y has accepted w in place of x.
After y accepts w as an identity custodian in place of x: no longer y has x as an identity custodian, no longer x is an identity custodian of y, y has w as an identity custodian and w is an identity custodian of y.
