SGSG: Secure Grassroots Social Graph (expanded schemas)
GKK:0 - | An *identity* is a unique key pair, chosen by the person who has it.
GKK:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. A party to a contract must have an identity.
GKK:1 ○ Establish: identity(Alice), knows(Alice,Alice) <- ~identity(Alice) guarded by Alice.
GKK:1 ○ | Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
GKK:2 ■ Acquaint: knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice) <- guarded by Alice, Bob.
GKK:3 ■ Introduce: knows(Bob,Carol) <- knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice), knows(Alice,Carol) guarded by Alice, Bob.
GSG:0 - | Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts.
GSG:0 - | Alice and Bob are persons.
GSG:1 ○ Befriend: friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice) <- knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice), ~friend(Alice,Bob), ~friend(Bob,Alice) guarded by Alice, Bob.
GSG:1 ○ | Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
GSG:2 ■ Unfriend: ~friend(Alice,Bob), ~friend(Bob,Alice) <- friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice) guarded by Alice.
GSG:3 ○ | Loss of Trust.
GSG:3 ○ | Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
SGSG:0 - | The *identity record* of Alice is her *identity custodians*, persons she names, and her *threshold*, a share of them greater than half. The *records* of Alice are her friends and, for each friend, the friends of that friend. Alice suffers a *state loss* when her machine loses some or all of its state and she keeps her private key, and an *identity loss* when she loses her private key.
SGSG:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Alicia a new identity of Alice.
SGSG:1 ■ Name custodian: custodian(Alice,Carol), custodian_of(Carol,Alice) <- knows(Alice,Carol), ~fixed(Alice), ~custodian(Alice,Carol) guarded by Alice.
SGSG:1 ■ Identity record: fixed(Alice) <- ~fixed(Alice) guarded by Alice.
SGSG:1 ■ | It then changes only by the replacement of a custodian's lost identity (SGSG:5).
SGSG:2 ■ | Friendship updates.
SGSG:2 ■ Tell: records_friend(Carol,Alice,Bob) <- friend(Alice,Carol), friend(Carol,Alice), friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice), ~records_friend(Carol,Alice,Bob).
SGSG:2 ■ Tell end: ~records_friend(Carol,Alice,Bob) <- friend(Alice,Carol), friend(Carol,Alice), ~friend(Alice,Bob), ~friend(Bob,Alice), records_friend(Carol,Alice,Bob).
SGSG:2 ■ Send record: records_custodian(Bob,Alice,Carol) <- friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice), fixed(Alice), custodian(Alice,Carol), ~records_custodian(Bob,Alice,Carol).
SGSG:3 ■ Checkpoint: checkpoint(Bob,Alice) <- friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice) guarded by Alice.
SGSG:4 ■ | State recovery.
SGSG:4 ■ Restore: friend(Alice,Bob), recovered(Alice,Bob) <- state_loss(Alice), friend(Bob,Alice), ~recovered(Alice,Bob).
SGSG:5 ◆ | Identity replacement.
SGSG:5 ◆ Replace: asked(Carol,Alicia,Alice) <- knows(Alicia,Carol), custodian_of(Carol,Alice), ~asked(Carol,Alicia,Alice) guarded by Alicia.
SGSG:5 ◆ | Alicia asks only after an identity loss of Alice.
SGSG:5 ◆ Vouch: vouched(Carol,Alicia,Alice) <- asked(Carol,Alicia,Alice), custodian_of(Carol,Alice), ~vouched(Carol,Alicia,Alice) guarded by Carol.
SGSG:5 ◆ Accept: ~friend(Bob,Alice), friend(Bob,Alicia), friend(Alicia,Bob), accepted(Bob,Alicia,Alice) <- friend(Bob,Alice), threshold_vouched(Bob,Alice,Alicia), ~accepted(Bob,Alicia,Alice), friend(Bob,Alice).
SGSG:5 ◆ Accept custodian: ~custodian(Bob,Alice), ~custodian_of(Alice,Bob), custodian(Bob,Alicia), custodian_of(Alicia,Bob) <- accepted(Bob,Alicia,Alice), custodian(Bob,Alice), custodian(Bob,Alice), custodian_of(Alice,Bob).
SGSG:5 ◆ | Bob tells Alicia the friends of Alice he records, and tells his friends of the change.
