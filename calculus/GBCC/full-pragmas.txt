GBCC: Grassroots Backed Community Currency
Of GKK: An *identity* is a unique key pair, chosen by the person who has it. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. A party to a contract must have an identity.
Of GSG: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts. Alice and Bob are persons.
Of GnW: Bob *trusts* Carol, an organisation of Alice, when he trusts Alice. The *decision rule*, a parameter of GnW, says who decides for an organisation and how. Alice, Bob and Erin are persons, Carol and Dave organisations; in clause 4 Alice and Bob are possibly one person.
Of GO: An *organisation* is a ward with *participants*, which include its *officers*, each with a *role*. The *bylaws*, a parameter of GO, name the roles of an organisation and say who decides each of its acts and how, their own amendment included. Alice and Bob are persons, Gov and Orga organisations, Motion an act of Orga, Role a role.
Of GC: A *coin* is a unit of debt that includes its issuer's public key and is signed by their private key. An *Alice-coin* is a coin issued by Alice. Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Sum and Total numbers, Carol and Dave possibly Alice, Bob or one person.
Of GB: A *bond* is a unit of debt, due at its maturity date, that includes its issuer's public key and its maturity date and is signed by their private key. An *Alice-bond* is a bond issued by Alice. A bond is *mature* when its maturity date is at most its holder's date. Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Day and Term maturity dates, Today a date, Cond a condition Alice, Bob and Carol agree, Sum and Total numbers; in clauses 2 and 3 Carol and Dave are possibly Alice, Bob or one person, and in clause 4 Dave is possibly Alice, Bob or Carol.
Of GDC: *Dollar* names one denomination, and a *Dollar-denominated Alice-coin* is an Alice-coin issued as Alice's debt of one Dollar. Alice and Bob are persons, Dollar a fiat currency, Sum a number.
Of DGC: A *Dollar-denominated Alice-bond* is an Alice-bond issued as Alice's debt of one Dollar, due at its maturity date. Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Dollar a fiat currency, Day a maturity date, Today a date, Cond a condition Alice, Bob and Carol agree, Sum a number; in clause 4 Carol is possibly Alice or Bob.
Of GBank: A *bank* is an organisation whose business is issuing coins and bonds against the coins and bonds it holds.
Of GCC: A *community currency* is a bank whose coins are Dollar-denominated and whose bylaws name one role, *treasurer*; the treasurers jointly decide its acts. Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a community currency, Sum a number.
Of GBCC: *f*, a fraction 0 ≤ f < 1, is a parameter of GBCC. Orga's *account* is a bank account in Dollars, held in Orga's name by its treasurers. A treasurer, Alice, is *backed* when the Alice-coins Orga holds are at most the Dollars Alice paid into the account and the coins Alice holds of the other treasurers, leaving out the fraction f of them of whom she holds the most. Alice is a person, Orga a community currency.
GKK:1, GnW:3. ■ Establish: Alice may establish an identity, and then Alice is a person. Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
GKK:2, GnW:3. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
GKK:3, GnW:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other, Alice knows Carol, Alice is a person, Bob is a person and Carol is a person.
GSG:1, GnW:3. ■ Befriend: Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other, Alice is a person and Bob is a person. Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
GSG:2, GnW:3. ■ Person unfriend: Alice may end her friendship with Bob, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
GSG:3. ○ Loss of Trust. Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
GnW:1. ■ Enter: Alice may create Carol, and Carol must consent, provided Alice is a person. Alice chooses Carol's key pair.
GnW:2. ■ Guardian decisions.
GnW:2, GO:4, GBCC:1. ■ Appoint: Those who decide for Carol may make Bob an officer of Carol, with the consent of Bob, provided Carol is an organisation, Carol is not retired, Bob is a person, Bob is a participant of Carol and every treasurer of Carol stays backed for Carol, and then Bob holds the role Role in Carol and Carol has Bob in the role Role. Resign: Bob may end his guardianship of Carol, provided every treasurer of Carol stays backed, and then Bob holds no role in Carol and Carol has Bob in no role. End guardian: Those who decide for Carol may end Bob's guardianship of Carol, provided every treasurer of Carol stays backed for Carol, and then Bob holds no role in Carol and Carol has Bob in no role. Guardian unfriend: Bob may end his friendship with his organisation Carol, provided every treasurer of Carol stays backed, and then Bob holds no role in Carol and Carol has Bob in no role. Carol may not end a friendship with her officer.
GnW:3. ■ Ward and person. Ward acquaint: Carol and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Carol is an organisation, Carol has Alice as an officer and Bob is a person. Ward befriend: Carol and Bob may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Carol is an organisation, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as an officer, Bob is a person, Carol and Bob know each other and Alice and Bob are friends. Ward end: Carol may end her friendship with Bob, provided Bob is not an officer of Carol. Unfriend: Bob may end his friendship with Carol, provided Bob is not an officer of Carol. Ward unfriend: Alice may end the friendship of Carol and Bob, provided Carol has Alice as an officer and Bob is not an officer of Carol.
GnW:4. ■ Wards. Wards acquaint: Carol and Dave may jointly come to know each other, provided Carol is an organisation, Carol has Alice as an officer, Dave is an organisation and Dave has Bob as an officer. Wards befriend: Carol and Dave may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice and Bob, provided Carol is an organisation, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as an officer, Dave is an organisation, Dave is not retired, Dave has Bob as an officer, Carol and Dave know each other, Alice knows Dave and Alice and Bob are friends. Wards of one guardian befriend: Carol and Dave may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Carol is an organisation, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as an officer, Dave is an organisation, Dave is not retired, Dave has Alice as an officer and Carol and Dave know each other.
GnW:5, GO:4. ■ Retire: Those who decide for Carol may retire Carol, and then no one holds any role in Carol and Carol has no one in any role. Carol can then take no act.
GO:1. ■ Found: Gov may create Orga with Alice as its first officer in the role Role, with the consent of Alice, and Orga must consent, provided Alice is a person.
GO:2. ■ Acts of the organisation. Propose: Bob may propose Motion in Orga, provided Bob is entitled to propose Motion in Orga. Vote: Those who decide Motion for Orga may vote on Motion in Orga, provided Orga is an organisation and Orga has Motion proposed. Act: Alice may take Motion for Orga, provided Alice is entitled to take Motion for Orga and the votes decide Motion according to the bylaws of Orga.
GO:3. ■ Participation. Join: Bob may become a participant of Orga, with the consent of those who decide for Orga.
GO:3, GBCC:1. ■ Leave: Bob may end his participation in Orga, provided Bob is not an officer of Orga and every treasurer of Orga stays backed. Officer leave: Bob may end his participation and his office in Orga, provided every treasurer of Orga stays backed. Expel: Those who decide for Orga may end Bob's participation in Orga, provided Bob is not an officer of Orga and every treasurer of Orga stays backed for Orga. Officer expel: Those who decide for Orga may end Bob's participation and Bob's office in Orga, provided every treasurer of Orga stays backed for Orga. Every role Bob holds in Orga ends with his participation.
GO:4. ■ Officers. The decision names his role.
GO:5. ■ Amend: Those who decide the amendment of the bylaws of Orga may amend the bylaws of Orga, provided Orga is an organisation.
GO:6. ○ Accountability. Orga will be accountable at law for its undertakings where it is a juridical person, and every officer who decided an act of Orga will be accountable for it with Orga.
GC:1, GDC:1. ■ Issue: Alice may issue Sum Alice-coins, provided Alice has issued coins of the denomination Dollar.
GC:2, GB:2, DGC:2, GBCC:1. ■ Swap: Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Carol-coins for Total Dave-coins, provided Alice and Bob know each other, the coins and bonds of Carol and Dave are of one denomination and every treasurer of Orga stays backed.
GC:3, GBCC:1. ■ Pay: Alice may pay Bob Sum Bob-coins, provided Alice knows Bob and every treasurer of Orga stays backed.
GC:4, GB:3, DGC:2, GBCC:1. ■ Redeem: Alice may redeem a Bob-coin against a Carol-coin, provided Alice knows Bob, the coins and bonds of Bob and Carol are of one denomination and every treasurer of Orga stays backed.
GC:5. ○ Pricing. Alice will price her offerings in Alice-coins and accept them in payment at those prices.
GB:1. ■ Issue bond: Alice may issue Sum Alice-bonds due Day, provided Alice's date is Today and Day is more than Today.
GB:2, DGC:2, GBCC:1. ■ Swap coin for bond: Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Carol-coins for Total Dave-bonds due Day, provided Alice and Bob know each other, the coins and bonds of Carol and Dave are of one denomination and every treasurer of Orga stays backed. Swap bonds: Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Carol-bonds due Day for Total Dave-bonds due Term, provided Alice and Bob know each other, the coins and bonds of Carol and Dave are of one denomination and every treasurer of Orga stays backed.
GB:3, DGC:2, GBCC:1. ■ Redeem coin against bond: Alice may redeem a Bob-coin against a Carol-bond due Day, provided Alice knows Bob, the coins and bonds of Bob and Carol are of one denomination and every treasurer of Orga stays backed. Redeem bond: Alice may redeem a Bob-bond due Day against a Carol-coin, provided Alice knows Bob, Alice's date is Today, Day is at most Today, the coins and bonds of Bob and Carol are of one denomination and every treasurer of Orga stays backed. Redeem bond against bond: Alice may redeem a Bob-bond due Day against a Carol-bond due Term, provided Alice knows Bob, Alice's date is Today, Day is at most Today, the coins and bonds of Bob and Carol are of one denomination and every treasurer of Orga stays backed.
GB:4. ■ Escrow.
GB:4, DGC:2, GBCC:1. ■ Deposit: Alice may deposit Sum Dave-coins with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice knows Bob, the coins and bonds that Carol holds in escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond are of one denomination and every treasurer of Orga stays backed. Deposit bond: Alice may deposit Sum Dave-bonds due Day with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice knows Bob, the coins and bonds that Carol holds in escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond are of one denomination and every treasurer of Orga stays backed. Release: Carol and Bob may jointly release Sum Dave-coins that Alice deposited on Cond, provided Carol and Bob know each other, Carol finds Cond met, the coins and bonds that Carol holds in escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond are of one denomination and every treasurer of Orga stays backed. Release bond: Carol and Bob may jointly release Sum Dave-bonds due Day that Alice deposited on Cond, provided Carol and Bob know each other, Carol finds Cond met, the coins and bonds that Carol holds in escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond are of one denomination and every treasurer of Orga stays backed. Return: Carol and Alice may jointly return Sum Dave-coins deposited for Bob on Cond, provided Carol and Alice know each other, Carol finds Cond failed, the coins and bonds that Carol holds in escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond are of one denomination and every treasurer of Orga stays backed. Return bond: Carol and Alice may jointly return Sum Dave-bonds due Day deposited for Bob on Cond, provided Carol and Alice know each other, Carol finds Cond failed, the coins and bonds that Carol holds in escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond are of one denomination and every treasurer of Orga stays backed.
GB:5. ○ Date. Alice keeps her date at the current date.
GDC:1. ■ First issue: Alice may issue Sum Dollar-denominated Alice-coins, provided Alice has issued coins of no denomination.
GDC:2. ○ Denomination. A Dollar-denominated Alice-coin is due now; Alice will redeem it on presentation, by a Dollar-denominated coin the bearer accepts or by one Dollar.
GDC:3. ○ Pricing. Alice will accept Dollar-denominated Alice-coins in payment for her offerings at one Dollar each.
DGC:1. ■ Issue bond: Alice may issue Sum Dollar-denominated Alice-bonds due Day, provided Alice's date is Today and Day is more than Today.
DGC:3. ■ Accept: Alice may accept one more Dollar-denominated Carol-coin in payment, provided Alice holds a Dollar-denominated Carol-coin. Accept by bond: Alice may accept one more Dollar-denominated Carol-coin in payment, provided Alice holds a Carol-bond due someone and Carol has issued a Dollar-denominated bond due someone. Each such coin paid to Alice counts against the limit, and Alice may accept again.
DGC:4, GBCC:1. ■ Pay: Alice may pay Bob a Carol-coin that Bob accepts as denominated by Dollar, provided Alice knows Bob, Carol has issued coins of the denomination Dollar and every treasurer of Orga stays backed.
GCC:1, GBCC:2. ■ Draw: Alice may swap Sum Alice-coins for Sum Orga-coins, provided Alice is a treasurer of Orga and Alice stays backed in Orga.
GCC:2. ○ Fiat redemption. Any treasurer will redeem an Orga-coin Bob holds against one Dollar.
GBCC:1. ■ Treasurers.
GBCC:3. ◆ Record: The treasurers of Orga may record a Dollar as paid into the account of Orga by Alice, with the consent of Alice, provided Orga is an organisation and Alice is a treasurer of Orga. Alice, a treasurer, may pay Dollars into the account, and the treasurers may then jointly record them as paid in by Alice.
GBCC:4. ○ Fiat redemption. GCC:2, from the account while it holds Dollars, and from a treasurer if it holds none.
