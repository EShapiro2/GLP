GB: Grassroots Bonds (flat text)
GKK:0 - | An *identity* is a unique key pair, chosen by the person who has it.
GKK:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. A party to a contract must have an identity.
GKK:1 ○ Establish: Alice may Alice has an identity and Alice knows Alice, provided not Alice has an identity.
GKK:1 ○ | Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
GKK:2 ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly Alice knows Bob and Bob knows Alice.
GKK:3 ■ Introduce: Alice and Bob may jointly Bob knows Carol, provided Alice knows Bob, Bob knows Alice and Alice knows Carol.
GC:0 - | A *coin* is a unit of debt that includes its issuer's public key and is signed by their private key. An *Alice-coin* is a coin issued by Alice.
GC:0 - | Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Sum and Total numbers, Carol and Dave possibly Alice, Bob or one person.
GC:1 ■ Issue: Alice may Alice holds Sum Alice-coins.
GC:2+GB:2 ■ Swap: Alice and Bob may jointly no longer Alice holds Sum Carol-coins, Bob holds Sum Carol-coins, no longer Bob holds Total Dave-coins and Alice holds Total Dave-coins, provided Alice knows Bob and Bob knows Alice. Possibly one: Alice=Carol, Alice=Dave, Bob=Carol, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
GC:3 ■ Pay: Alice may no longer Alice holds Sum Bob-coins and Bob holds Sum Bob-coins, provided Alice knows Bob.
GC:4+GB:3 ■ Redeem: Alice may no longer Alice holds a Bob-coin, Bob holds a Bob-coin, no longer Bob holds a Carol-coin and Alice holds a Carol-coin, provided Alice knows Bob. Possibly one: Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
GC:5 ○ | Pricing.
GC:5 ○ | Alice will price her offerings in Alice-coins and accept them in payment at those prices.
GB:0 - | A *bond* is a unit of debt, due at its maturity date, that includes its issuer's public key and its maturity date and is signed by their private key. An *Alice-bond* is a bond issued by Alice. A bond is *mature* when its maturity date is at most its holder's date.
GB:0 - | Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Day and Term maturity dates, Today a date, Cond a condition Alice, Bob and Carol agree, Sum and Total numbers; in clauses 2 and 3 Carol and Dave are possibly Alice, Bob or one person, and in clause 4 Dave is possibly Alice, Bob or Carol.
GB:1 ■ Issue bond: Alice may Alice holds Sum Alice-bonds due Day, provided Alice's date is Today and Day is more than Today.
GB:2 ■ Swap coin for bond: Alice and Bob may jointly no longer Alice holds Sum Carol-coins, Bob holds Sum Carol-coins, no longer Bob holds Total Dave-bonds due Day and Alice holds Total Dave-bonds due Day, provided Alice knows Bob and Bob knows Alice. Possibly one: Alice=Carol, Alice=Dave, Bob=Carol, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
GB:2 ■ Swap bonds: Alice and Bob may jointly no longer Alice holds Sum Carol-bonds due Day, Bob holds Sum Carol-bonds due Day, no longer Bob holds Total Dave-bonds due Term and Alice holds Total Dave-bonds due Term, provided Alice knows Bob and Bob knows Alice. Possibly one: Alice=Carol, Alice=Dave, Bob=Carol, Bob=Dave, Carol=Dave.
GB:3 ■ Redeem coin against bond: Alice may no longer Alice holds a Bob-coin, Bob holds a Bob-coin, no longer Bob holds a Carol-bond due Day and Alice holds a Carol-bond due Day, provided Alice knows Bob. Possibly one: Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
GB:3 ■ Redeem bond: Alice may no longer Alice holds a Bob-bond due Day, Bob holds a Bob-bond due Day, no longer Bob holds a Carol-coin and Alice holds a Carol-coin, provided Alice knows Bob, Alice's date is Today and Day is at most Today. Possibly one: Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
