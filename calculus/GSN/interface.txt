GSN: Grassroots Social Network
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ○ Befriend.  Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other.
5. ■ Unfriend.  Alice may end Bob's membership in _, and then no longer Alice counts Bob as a friend, no longer Bob counts Alice as a friend, Alice is a member of no group of Bob and Bob has Alice as a member of no group.
6. ■ Message.  Alice may send Text to Bob, provided Alice and Bob are friends.
7. ■ Create.  Alice may create Grupo.
8. ■ Close.  Alice may close Grupo by leaving it.
9. ■ Join.  Alice may add Bob to Grupo, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo and Alice and Bob are friends.
10. ■ Remove.  Alice may end Bob's membership in Grupo, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo.
11. ■ Leave.  Bob may end his membership in Alice's Grupo.
12. ■ Post.  Bob may send Text to the group Grupo of Alice, provided Bob is a member of the group Grupo of Alice.
13. ■ Deliver.  Alice delivers Text of Bob in Grupo to Carol, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo, Alice has the post Text of Bob in Grupo and Alice has Carol as a member of the group Grupo.
14. ■ Deliver leader.  Alice delivers Text of Bob in Grupo to Alice, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo and Alice has the post Text of Bob in Grupo.
