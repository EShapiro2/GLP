GSN: Grassroots Social Network (flat text)
GKK:0 - | An *identity* is a unique key pair, chosen by the person who has it.
GKK:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. A party to a contract must have an identity.
GKK:1 ○ Establish: Alice may Alice has an identity and Alice knows Alice, provided not Alice has an identity.
GKK:1 ○ | Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
GKK:2 ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly Alice knows Bob and Bob knows Alice.
GKK:3 ■ Introduce: Alice and Bob may jointly Bob knows Carol, provided Alice knows Bob, Bob knows Alice and Alice knows Carol.
GSG:0 - | Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts.
GSG:0 - | Alice and Bob are persons.
GSG:1 ○ Befriend: Alice and Bob may jointly Alice counts Bob as a friend and Bob counts Alice as a friend, provided Alice knows Bob, Bob knows Alice, not Alice counts Bob as a friend and not Bob counts Alice as a friend.
GSG:1 ○ | Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
GSG:2+GSN:3 ■ Unfriend: Alice may no longer Alice counts Bob as a friend, no longer Bob counts Alice as a friend, no longer Alice is a member of the group anyone of Bob, no longer Bob is a member of the group anyone of Alice, no longer Alice has Bob as a member of the group anyone and no longer Bob has Alice as a member of the group anyone.
GSG:3 ○ | Loss of Trust.
GSG:3 ○ | Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
GSN:0 - | A *message*, a parameter of GSN, is what a person sends.
GSN:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Grupo a group, Bob and Carol possibly Alice or one person.
GSN:1 ■ Message: Alice may Bob has a message from Alice, provided Alice counts Bob as a friend and Bob counts Alice as a friend. Possibly one: Alice=Bob.
GSN:2 ■ | Group.
GSN:2 ■ Create: Alice may Alice is the leader of Grupo, Alice is a member of the group Grupo of Alice and Alice has Alice as a member of the group Grupo, provided not Alice is the leader of Grupo.
GSN:2 ■ | Alice is then its leader and first member.
GSN:2 ■ Close: Alice may no longer Alice is the leader of Grupo, no longer Alice has anyone as a member of the group Grupo and no longer anyone is a member of the group Grupo of anyone.
GSN:3 ■ | Joining and Leaving.
GSN:3 ■ Join: Alice and Bob may jointly Bob is a member of the group Grupo of Alice and Alice has Bob as a member of the group Grupo, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo, Alice counts Bob as a friend, Bob counts Alice as a friend and not Bob is a member of the group Grupo of anyone. Possibly one: Alice=Bob.
GSN:3 ■ Remove: Alice may no longer Bob is a member of the group Grupo of Alice and no longer Alice has Bob as a member of the group Grupo, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo. Possibly one: Alice=Bob.
GSN:3 ■ Leave: Bob may no longer Bob is a member of the group Grupo of Alice and no longer Alice has Bob as a member of the group Grupo. Possibly one: Alice=Bob.
GSN:4 ■ | Group messaging.
GSN:4 ■ Post: Bob may Bob has posted a message to Grupo, provided Bob is a member of the group Grupo of someone.
GSN:4 ■ Deliver: Carol has a message of Bob in Grupo and Alice has delivered a message of Bob in Grupo to Carol, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo, Bob has posted a message to Grupo, Alice has Bob as a member of the group Grupo and Alice has Carol as a member of the group Grupo. Possibly one: Alice=Bob, Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
