GSN: Grassroots Social Network
GSN extends and strengthens GSG.
Vocabulary: y has a message from x = message(y,x); x is the leader of g = leader(x,g); x is a member of the group g of y = member(x,g,y); x has y as a member of the group g = listed(x,y,g); x has posted a message to g = posted(x,g); z has a message of x in g = group_message(z,x,g); y has delivered a message of x in g to z = group_delivered(y,x,g,z).
x is a member of g: x is a member of the group g of someone.
After x sends a message to y: y has a message from x.
After x creates g, which requires x is not the leader of g: x is the leader of g, x is a member of the group g of x and x has x as a member of the group g.
After x closes g by leaving it: no longer x is the leader of g, x has no one as a member of the group g and no one is a member of g.
After x adds y to g, which requires y is not a member of g: y is a member of the group g of x and x has y as a member of the group g.
After x ends y's membership in g: no longer y is a member of the group g of x and no longer x has y as a member of the group g.
After y ends y's membership in x's g: no longer y is a member of the group g of x and no longer x has y as a member of the group g.
After x sends a message to the group g: x has posted a message to g.
After y delivers a message of x in g to z: z has a message of x in g and y has delivered a message of x in g to z.
GSN:0 - | A *message*, a parameter of GSN, is what a person sends.
GSN:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Grupo a group, Bob and Carol possibly Alice or one person.
GSN:1 ■ Message: message(Bob,Alice) <- friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice) guarded by Alice possibly Alice=Bob.
GSN:2 ■ | Group.
GSN:2 ■ Create: leader(Alice,Grupo), member(Alice,Grupo,Alice), listed(Alice,Alice,Grupo) <- ~leader(Alice,Grupo) guarded by Alice.
GSN:2 ■ | Alice is then its leader and first member.
GSN:2 ■ Close: ~leader(Alice,Grupo), ~listed(Alice,_,Grupo), ~member(_,Grupo,_) <- leader(Alice,Grupo) guarded by Alice.
GSN:3 ■ | Joining and Leaving.
GSN:3 ■ Join: member(Bob,Grupo,Alice), listed(Alice,Bob,Grupo) <- leader(Alice,Grupo), friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice), ~member(Bob,Grupo,_) guarded by Alice, Bob possibly Alice=Bob.
GSN:3 ■ Remove: ~member(Bob,Grupo,Alice), ~listed(Alice,Bob,Grupo) <- leader(Alice,Grupo), member(Bob,Grupo,Alice), listed(Alice,Bob,Grupo) guarded by Alice possibly Alice=Bob.
GSN:3 ■ Leave: ~member(Bob,Grupo,Alice), ~listed(Alice,Bob,Grupo) <- member(Bob,Grupo,Alice), listed(Alice,Bob,Grupo) guarded by Bob possibly Alice=Bob.
GSN:3 ■ Unfriend: GSG:2 + ~member(Alice,_,Bob), ~member(Bob,_,Alice), ~listed(Alice,Bob,_), ~listed(Bob,Alice,_) <-.
GSN:4 ■ | Group messaging.
GSN:4 ■ Post: posted(Bob,Grupo) <- member(Bob,Grupo,_) guarded by Bob.
GSN:4 ■ Deliver: group_message(Carol,Bob,Grupo), group_delivered(Alice,Bob,Grupo,Carol) <- leader(Alice,Grupo), posted(Bob,Grupo), listed(Alice,Bob,Grupo), listed(Alice,Carol,Grupo) possibly Alice=Bob, Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
