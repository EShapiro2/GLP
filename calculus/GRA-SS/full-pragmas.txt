GRA-SS: Grassroots Ride Auction, Second-Score Restriction
Of GKK: An *identity* is a unique key pair. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. A party to a contract must have an identity.
Of GSG: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts. Alice and Bob are persons.
Of GDO: Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
Of GDO-LB: A person has a *location*. N > 0, an integer, and E > 0 are parameters of GDO-LB. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
Of GRA: A *pickup delay* is measured from the award. The *terms* of a call, a parameter of GRA, state a maximum pickup delay, select a bidder, set the fare and the deposit, and say how these are released. Alice is a rider, Bob a driver and Carol a person, Trip a call. A person can be either a rider or a driver but not both.
Of GRA-SS: *Second-score terms* state a maximum pickup delay T ≥ 0, a willingness to pay W(τ) = A − λτ with A, λ ≥ 0, and a deposit D ≥ 0. The award is at time t₀. A bid (p, τ) is the fare p ≥ 0 the bidder asks and the pickup delay τ ≥ 0 the bidder promises. Its *score* is W(τ) − p, and it is *admissible* iff τ ≤ T and its score is at least 0. Alice is a rider, Bob a driver and Carol a person.
GKK:1. ○ Establish: Alice may establish an identity. Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
GKK:2. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
GKK:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
GSG:1. ○ Befriend: Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other. Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
GSG:2. ■ Unfriend: Alice may end her friendship with Bob.
GSG:3. ○ Loss of Trust. Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
GDO:1, GDO-LB:1. ■ Point friend: Alice may point to Bob, provided Alice and Bob are friends and either Alice points to fewer than N persons and Bob is closest to Alice among all of Alice's unpointed friends, or Bob is closer to Alice by more than E than some person pointed to by Alice.
GDO:2, GDO-LB:2, GRA-SS:1. ■ Point transitive: Alice may point to Carol, provided Alice knows Carol, Alice points to Bob, Bob points to Carol, Carol is closer to Alice by more than E than some person pointed to by Alice and Carol is a driver.
GDO:3, GDO-LB:3. ■ Unpoint: Alice may cease pointing to Bob, provided Alice points to more than N persons and Bob is furthest from Alice among the persons Alice points to.
GRA:1. ■ Call. Call: Alice may call for bids on a ride as Trip, provided Alice does not have a ride. Alice calls for bids from the persons Alice points to, stating the pickup point, the destination, the bidding period and the terms. Send call: Alice's call Trip goes to Bob, provided Alice has the call Trip open and Alice points to Bob. Forward call: Bob forwards Alice's call Trip to Carol, provided Bob has received Alice's call Trip and Bob points to Carol.
GRA:2. ■ Bid: Bob may bid a fare and a pickup delay on Alice's call Trip, provided Bob has received Alice's call Trip and the bidding period of Alice's call Trip has not ended. Alice may not open a bid before then.
GRA:3. ■ Award.
GRA:3, GRA-SS:2. ■ Award: Alice may award the ride of her call Trip to Bob, provided the bidding period of Alice's call Trip has ended, Bob has a bid on Alice's call Trip, the terms of Alice's call Trip select Bob, Bob is the bidder on Alice's call Trip whose admissible bid has the highest score, ties broken as the terms say, the terms of Alice's call Trip set the deposit D and the terms of Alice's call Trip set the fare P = W(τ) − s₂, where τ is the pickup delay of Bob's bid and s₂ is the highest score among the other admissible bids, or 0 if there is none. The ride is awarded at the fare the terms set, and Alice's fare and the deposit the terms set for Bob are then locked. Close: Alice may close her call Trip, provided the terms of Alice's call Trip select no bidder and no bid on Alice's call Trip is admissible.
GRA:4. ■ Pickup: The pickup of Alice and Bob is recorded, provided Alice and Bob are both at the pickup point.
GRA:5, GRA-SS:3. ■ Arrive: The fare and the deposit are released to Alice and to Bob as the terms say, provided Alice and Bob are both at the destination and the refund min{P, λδ} is released to Alice, and the rest of the fare and the deposit to Bob, with δ = max{0, t_pickup − t₀ − τ}.
GRA:6, GRA-SS:4. ■ Cancel: The fare and the deposit are released to Alice and to Bob as the terms say, with the consent of Alice, provided no pickup of Alice's ride is recorded within the maximum pickup delay and the fare and the deposit are released to Alice if Alice was at the pickup point from t₀ to t₀ + T, and otherwise the fare to Alice and the deposit to Bob. The fare and the deposit are then released to Alice and to Bob as the terms say.
GRA:7. ■ Withdraw: Alice may close her call Trip, provided the bidding period of Alice's call Trip has not ended.
