GRA-SS: Grassroots Ride Auction, Second-Score Restriction
GRA-SS instantiates GRA with second-score terms, and restricts it.
*Second-score terms* state a maximum pickup delay T ≥ 0, a willingness to pay W(τ) = A − λτ with A, λ ≥ 0, and a deposit D ≥ 0. The award is at time t₀. A bid (p, τ) is the fare p ≥ 0 the bidder asks and the pickup delay τ ≥ 0 the bidder promises. Its *score* is W(τ) − p, and it is *admissible* iff τ ≤ T and its score is at least 0.
Alice is a rider, Bob a driver and Carol a person.
1. ■ Point transitive: GDO-LB:2, only if Carol is a driver.
2. ■ Award. Award: GRA:3 Award, only if Bob is the bidder on Alice's call Trip whose admissible bid has the highest score, ties broken as the terms say, the terms of Alice's call Trip set the deposit D and the terms of Alice's call Trip set the fare P = W(τ) − s₂, where τ is the pickup delay of Bob's bid and s₂ is the highest score among the other admissible bids, or 0 if there is none. Close: GRA:3 Close, only if no bid on Alice's call Trip is admissible.
3. ■ Arrive: GRA:5, only if the refund min{P, λδ} is released to Alice, and the rest of the fare and the deposit to Bob, with δ = max{0, t_pickup − t₀ − τ}.
4. ■ Cancel: GRA:6, only if the fare and the deposit are released to Alice if Alice was at the pickup point from t₀ to t₀ + T, and otherwise the fare to Alice and the deposit to Bob.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x is a driver = driver(x); y is the bidder on x's call c whose admissible bid has the highest score, ties broken as the terms say = highest_score(x,y,c); no bid on x's call c is admissible = none_admissible(x,c); the terms of x's call c set the deposit D = sets_deposit(x,c); the terms of x's call c set the fare P = W(τ) − s₂, where τ is the pickup delay of y's bid and s₂ is the highest score among the other admissible bids, or 0 if there is none = second_score_fare(x,y,c); the refund min{P, λδ} is released to x, and the rest of the fare and the deposit to y, with δ = max{0, t_pickup − t₀ − τ} = refund(x,y); the fare and the deposit are released to x if x was at the pickup point from t₀ to t₀ + T, and otherwise the fare to x and the deposit to y = cancel_release(x,y).
