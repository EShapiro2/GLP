GRA-SS: Grassroots Ride Auction, Second-Score Restriction
GRA-SS instantiates GRA with second-score terms, and restricts it.
Vocabulary: y is the bidder on x's call c whose admissible bid has the highest score, ties broken as the terms say = highest_score(x,y,c); no bid on x's call c is admissible = none_admissible(x,c); the terms of x's call c set the deposit D = sets_deposit(x,c); the terms of x's call c set the fare P = W(τ) − s₂, where τ is the pickup delay of y's bid and s₂ is the highest score among the other admissible bids, or 0 if there is none = second_score_fare(x,y,c); the refund min{P, λδ} is released to x, and the rest of the fare and the deposit to y, with δ = max{0, t_pickup − t₀ − τ} = refund(x,y); the fare and the deposit are released to x if x was at the pickup point from t₀ to t₀ + T, and otherwise the fare to x and the deposit to y = cancel_release(x,y).
GRA-SS:0 - | *Second-score terms* state a maximum pickup delay T ≥ 0, a willingness to pay W(τ) = A − λτ with A, λ ≥ 0, and a deposit D ≥ 0. The award is at time t₀. A bid (p, τ) is the fare p ≥ 0 the bidder asks and the pickup delay τ ≥ 0 the bidder promises. Its *score* is W(τ) − p, and it is *admissible* iff τ ≤ T and its score is at least 0.
GRA-SS:0 - | Alice is a rider, Bob a driver and Carol a person.
GRA-SS:0 - | Environment: y is the bidder on x's call c whose admissible bid has the highest score, ties broken as the terms say; no bid on x's call c is admissible.
GRA-SS:0 - | Parameters: the terms of x's call c set the deposit D; the terms of x's call c set the fare P = W(τ) − s₂, where τ is the pickup delay of y's bid and s₂ is the highest score among the other admissible bids, or 0 if there is none; the fare and the deposit are released to x if x was at the pickup point from t₀ to t₀ + T, and otherwise the fare to x and the deposit to y; the refund min{P, λδ} is released to x, and the rest of the fare and the deposit to y, with δ = max{0, t_pickup − t₀ − τ}.
GRA-SS:1 ■ Point transitive: GDO-LB:2 <- driver(Carol).
GRA-SS:2 ■ | Award.
GRA-SS:2 ■ Award: GRA:3 Award <- highest_score(Alice,Bob,Trip), sets_deposit(Alice,Trip), second_score_fare(Alice,Bob,Trip).
GRA-SS:2 ■ Close: GRA:3 Close <- none_admissible(Alice,Trip).
GRA-SS:3 ■ Arrive: GRA:5 <- refund(Alice,Bob).
GRA-SS:4 ■ Cancel: GRA:6 <- cancel_release(Alice,Bob).
