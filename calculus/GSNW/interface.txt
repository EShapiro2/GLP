GSNW: Grassroots Social Network with Wards
1. ■ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity, and then Alice is a person.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other, Alice knows Carol, Alice is a person, Bob is a person and Carol is a person.
4. ■ Befriend.  Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other, Alice is a person and Bob is a person.

6. ■ Message.  Alice may send Text to Bob, provided Alice and Bob are friends and Alice is a person.
7. ■ Create.  Alice may create Grupo, provided Alice is a person.
8. ■ Close.  Alice may close Grupo by leaving it, provided Alice is a person.
9. ■ Join.  Alice may add Bob to Grupo, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo, Alice and Bob are friends and Bob is a person.
10. ■ Remove.  Alice may end Bob's membership in Grupo, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo.
11. ■ Leave.  Bob may end his membership in Alice's Grupo.
12. ■ Post.  Bob may send Text to the group Grupo of Alice, provided Bob is a member of the group Grupo of Alice and Bob is a person.
13. ■ Post own.  Alice may send Text to the group Grupo of Alice, provided Alice is a member of the group Grupo of Alice and Alice is a person.
14. ■ Deliver.  Alice delivers Text of Bob in Grupo to Carol, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo, Alice has the post Text of Bob in Grupo and Alice has Carol as a member of the group Grupo.
15. ■ Deliver leader.  Alice delivers Text of Bob in Grupo to Alice, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo and Alice has the post Text of Bob in Grupo.
16. ■ Enter.  Alice may create Carol, and Carol must consent, provided Alice is a person.
17. ■ Make guardian.  Those who decide for Carol may make Bob a guardian of Carol, with the consent of Bob, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired, Bob is a person, Carol has Alice as a guardian and Bob counts Alice as a friend.
18. ■ Resign.  Bob may end his guardianship of Carol.
19. ■ End guardian.  Those who decide for Carol, other than Bob, may end Bob's guardianship of Carol.
20. ■ Guardian unfriend.  Bob may end his friendship with his ward Carol, and then Bob is a member of no group of Carol, Carol is a member of no group of Bob, Bob has Carol as a member of no group and Carol has Bob as a member of no group.
21. ■ Ward acquaint.  Carol and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Carol is a ward, Carol has Alice as a guardian and Bob is a person.
22. ■ Ward befriend.  Carol and Bob may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Bob is a person, Carol and Bob know each other and Alice and Bob are friends.
23. ■ Ward end.  Carol may end Bob's membership in _, provided Bob is not a guardian of Carol, and then no longer Carol counts Bob as a friend, no longer Bob counts Carol as a friend, Carol is a member of no group of Bob and Bob has Carol as a member of no group.

27. ■ Wards befriend. 28. ■ Wards of one guardian befriend.  Carol and Dave may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Dave is a ward, Dave is not retired, Dave has Alice as a guardian and Carol and Dave know each other.
29. ■ Retire.  Those who decide for Carol may retire Carol, and then Carol is a member of no group of anyone and no one has Carol as a member of any group.
30. ■ Ward message.  Carol may send Text to Bob, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired and Carol and Bob are friends.
31. ■ Ward post.  Carol may send Text to the group Grupo of Alice, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired and Carol is a member of the group Grupo of Alice.
32. ■ Ward post own.  Carol may send Text to the group Grupo of Carol, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired and Carol is a member of the group Grupo of Carol.
33. ■ Ward create.  Carol may create Grupo, provided Carol is a ward and Carol is not retired.
34. ■ Ward close.  Carol may close Grupo by leaving it, provided Carol is a ward and Carol is not retired.
35. ■ Guardian close.  Alice may close the group Grupo of Carol, provided Alice is a guardian of Carol.
36. ■ Ward join.  Bob may add Carol to Grupo, with the consent of Carol and Alice, provided Bob is the leader of Grupo, Bob is a person, Bob and Carol are friends, Carol has Alice as a guardian, Alice is a guardian of Carol and Alice and Bob are friends.
37. ■ Wards join. 39. ■ Ward withdraw.  Alice may withdraw Carol from Bob's Grupo, provided Carol has Alice as a guardian and Alice is a guardian of Carol.
