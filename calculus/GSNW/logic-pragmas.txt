GSNW: Grassroots Social Network with Wards
GSNW extends, restricts and strengthens GSN and GnW.
Alice and Bob are persons, Carol a ward, Grupo a group, Text a message.
1. ■ A ward's messages. Message: GSN:1, only if Alice is a person. Post: GSN:4 Post, only if Bob is a person. Ward message: Carol may send Text to Bob, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired and Carol and Bob are friends. Ward post: Carol may send Text to the group Grupo of Alice, provided Carol is a ward, Carol is not retired and Carol is a member of the group Grupo of Alice.
2. ■ A ward's group. Create: GSN:2 Create, only if Alice is a person. Close: GSN:2 Close, only if Alice is a person. Ward create: Carol may create Grupo, provided Carol is a ward and Carol is not retired. Ward close: Carol may close Grupo by leaving it, provided Carol is a ward and Carol is not retired. Guardian close: Alice may close the group Grupo of Carol, provided Alice is a guardian of Carol.
3. ■ Join: GSN:3 Join, only if Bob is a person. Ward join: Bob may add Carol to Grupo, with the consent of Carol and Alice, provided Bob is the leader of Grupo, Bob and Carol are friends and Carol has Alice as a guardian. Guardian unfriend: GnW:2 Guardian unfriend, and then Bob is a member of no group of Carol, Carol is a member of no group of Bob, Bob has Carol as a member of no group and Carol has Bob as a member of no group. Ward end: GnW:3 Ward end, and then Carol is a member of no group of Bob, Bob is a member of no group of Carol, Carol has Bob as a member of no group and Bob has Carol as a member of no group. Unfriend: GnW:3 Unfriend, and then Bob is a member of no group of Carol, Carol is a member of no group of Bob, Bob has Carol as a member of no group and Carol has Bob as a member of no group. Ward unfriend: GnW:3 Ward unfriend, and then Carol is a member of no group of Bob, Bob is a member of no group of Carol, Carol has Bob as a member of no group and Bob has Carol as a member of no group. Retire: GnW:5 Retire, and then Carol is a member of no group of anyone and no one has Carol as a member of any group.
Logic pragmas.
After y closes the group g of z: no longer z is the leader of g, z has no one as a member of the group g and no one is a member of g.
