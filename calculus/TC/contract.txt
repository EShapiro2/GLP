TC: Trading Company
TC extends GCorp.
A *trading company* is a corporation whose bylaws name three roles, *buyer*, *keeper* and *seller*, each held by its first officer. A *good* is a thing it buys and sells.
Alice, Bob, Carol, Dave and Erin are persons, Orga a trading company.
1. ■ Buy: Alice, a buyer, and Dave, who know each other, may jointly decide that Orga pays Dave coins for a good Dave delivers to Orga.
2. ◆ Receive: Bob, a keeper, may record the receipt of a good Orga bought, and Orga then holds it.
3. ■ Sell: Carol, a seller, and Erin, who know each other, may jointly decide that Erin pays Orga coins for a good Orga holds, which is then sold to Erin and Orga holds it no more.
4. ○ Keeping. Bob, a keeper, will keep every good Orga holds, and release a good sold to Erin to Erin.
