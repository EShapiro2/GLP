TC: Trading Company
TC extends GCorp.
A *trading company* is a corporation whose bylaws name three roles, *buyer*, *keeper* and *seller*, each held by its first officer. A *good* is a thing it buys and sells.
Alice, Bob, Carol, Dave, Erin and Frank are persons, Orga a trading company, Ware a good, Sum a number; Frank is possibly any of the others or Orga.
1. ■ Buy: Alice and Dave may jointly decide that Orga pays Dave Sum Frank-coins for Ware, provided Alice is a buyer of Orga and Alice and Dave know each other. Ware is a good Dave delivers to Orga.
2. ◆ Receive: Bob may record the receipt of Ware by Orga, provided Bob is a keeper of Orga and Orga has paid a coin for Ware. Orga then holds it.
3. ■ Sell: Carol and Erin may jointly decide that Erin pays Orga Sum Frank-coins for Ware, provided Carol is a seller of Orga and Carol and Erin know each other. Ware is then sold to Erin and Orga holds it no more.
4. ○ Keeping. Bob, a keeper, will keep every good Orga holds, and release a good sold to Erin to Erin.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: z has paid a coin for v = paid_for(z,v); z has paid n coins for v = paid_for(z,v,*n); z has paid exactly n coins for v = paid_for(z,v,#n); z has received v = received(z,v); z holds the good v = holds_good(z,v); z has sold v to y = sold(z,v,y).
x is a buyer of z: x holds the role Buyer in z.
x is a keeper of z: x holds the role Keeper in z.
x is a seller of z: x holds the role Seller in z.
After x and y decide that z pays y n w-coins for v: no longer z holds n w-coins, y holds n w-coins and z has paid n coins for v.
After x records the receipt of v by z, which requires z has not received v: z has received v and z holds the good v.
After x and y decide that y pays z n w-coins for v: no longer y holds n w-coins, z holds n w-coins, no longer z holds the good v and z has sold v to y.
