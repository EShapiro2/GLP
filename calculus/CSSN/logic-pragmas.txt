CSSN: Child-Safe Social Network
CSSN instantiates GSNW with the parents of z for those who decide for z, a child for a ward and a parent for a guardian, and restricts and extends it.
Alice and Bob are persons, Carol and Dave children; in clause 2 Alice and Bob are possibly one person.
1. ◆ Enter: GnW:1. Alice is a legal parent of the minor who operates Carol.
2. ■ A child's acquaintances. Child acquaint: GnW:3 Ward acquaint, only if Alice knows Bob and Alice is a parent of Carol, with the consent of Alice. Children acquaint: GnW:4 Wards acquaint, only if Alice knows Dave, Bob knows Carol, Alice and Bob know each other, Alice is a parent of Carol and Bob is a parent of Dave, with the consent of Alice and Bob.
3. ■ Child create: GSNW:2 Ward create, only if Carol has Alice as a parent, with the consent of Alice. Join: GSN:3 Join, only if Alice is a person. Child add: GSNW:3 Ward add, only if Bob is a child. Only a child, Dave, may join the group.
4. ○ Of age: Alice may declare Carol of age, provided Alice is the creator of Carol, Alice is a parent of Carol and Carol has Alice as a parent. Carol is then a person. The creator will do so if the minor who operates Carol comes of age, and not before.
5. ○ A minor who is not a ward. Alice, aware that someone is a minor though not a ward, will neither befriend the minor nor let her child befriend the minor, and will report it to the minor's legal parents if she knows who they are.

every guardianship of z has ended: no one is a parent of z and z has no one as a parent.
After x declares z of age, which requires z is not of age: z is of age, no longer z is a child and every guardianship of z has ended and z is a person.
