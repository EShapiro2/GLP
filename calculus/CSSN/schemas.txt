CSSN: Child-Safe Social Network
CSSN instantiates GSNW with parents(z) for deciders(z), a child for a ward and a parent for a guardian, and restricts and extends it.
Vocabulary: x is a legal parent of the minor who operates z = legal_parent(x,z); z is of age = of_age(z).
Roles: the parents of z = parents(z), listed by z has y as a parent and y is a parent of z.
every guardianship of z has ended: no one is a parent of z and z has no one as a parent.
After x declares z of age, which requires z is not of age: z is of age, no longer z is a child and every guardianship of z has ended and z is a person.

CSSN:1 ◆ Enter: GnW:1 <- legal_parent(Alice,Carol).

CSSN:3 ■ Child create: GSNW:2 Ward create <- has_parent(Carol,Alice) guarded by Alice.
