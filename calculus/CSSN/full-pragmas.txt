CSSN: Child-Safe Social Network
Of GKK: An *identity* is a unique key pair. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. A party to a contract must have an identity. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of GSG: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts. Alice and Bob are persons.
Of GSN: A *message*, a parameter of GSN, is what a person sends. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Grupo a group, Text a message, Bob and Carol possibly Alice or one person.
Of GnW: Bob *trusts* Carol, a child of Alice, when he trusts Alice. The *decision rule*, a parameter of GnW, says who decides for a child and how. Alice, Bob and Erin are persons, Carol and Dave children; in clause 4 Alice and Bob are possibly one person.
Of GSNW: Alice and Bob are persons, Carol and Dave children, Grupo a group, Text a message; in clause 1 Carol is possibly Alice.
Of CSSN: Alice and Bob are persons, Carol and Dave children; in clause 2 Alice and Bob are possibly one person.
