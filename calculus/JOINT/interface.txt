JOINT: Joint Accounts
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ■ Issue.  Alice may issue Sum Alice-coins.
5. ■ Swap.  Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Carol-coins for Total Dave-coins, provided Alice and Bob know each other.
6. ■ Pay.  Alice may pay Bob Sum Bob-coins, provided Alice knows Bob.
7. ■ Redeem.  Alice may redeem a Bob-coin against a Carol-coin, provided Alice knows Bob.
8. ■ Open account.  Alice and Bob may jointly open the joint account Acct under a new name of Alice's, provided Alice and Bob know each other.
9. ■ Deposit.  Alice may move Sum Carol-coins into the joint account Acct of Alice and Bob, provided Alice and Bob hold the joint account Acct.
10. ■ Partner deposit.  Bob may move Sum Carol-coins into the joint account Acct of Alice and Bob, provided Alice and Bob hold the joint account Acct.
11. ■ Withdraw.  Alice and Bob may jointly move Sum Carol-coins from the joint account Acct to Alice.
12. ■ Partner withdraw.  Alice and Bob may jointly move Sum Carol-coins from the joint account Acct to Bob.
