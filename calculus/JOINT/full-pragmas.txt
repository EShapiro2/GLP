JOINT: Joint Accounts
Of GKK: An *identity* is a unique key pair. A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity. Alice, Bob and Carol are persons. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of GC: A *coin* is a unit of debt that includes its issuer's public key and is signed by their private key. An *Alice-coin* is a coin issued by Alice. Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are parties, Sum and Total numbers, Carol and Dave possibly Alice, Bob or one person.
Of JOINT: Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Carol possibly Alice or Bob, Acct a joint account, Sum a number.
GKK:1. ○ Establish: Alice may establish an identity. Alice will make herself known by its public key, sign her acts with its private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
GKK:2. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
GKK:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
GC:1. ■ Issue: Alice may issue Sum Alice-coins.
GC:2. ■ Swap: Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Carol-coins for Total Dave-coins, provided Alice and Bob know each other.
GC:3. ■ Pay: Alice may pay Bob Sum Bob-coins, provided Alice knows Bob.
GC:4. ■ Redeem: Alice may redeem a Bob-coin against a Carol-coin, provided Alice knows Bob.
GC:5. ○ Pricing. Alice will price her offerings in Alice-coins and accept them in payment at those prices.
JOINT:1. ■ Open account: Alice and Bob may jointly open the joint account Acct under a new name of Alice's, provided Alice and Bob know each other.
JOINT:2. ■ Deposit: Alice may move Sum Carol-coins into the joint account Acct of Alice and Bob, provided Alice and Bob hold the joint account Acct. Partner deposit: Bob may move Sum Carol-coins into the joint account Acct of Alice and Bob, provided Alice and Bob hold the joint account Acct.
JOINT:3. ■ Withdraw: Alice and Bob may jointly move Sum Carol-coins from the joint account Acct to Alice. Partner withdraw: Alice and Bob may jointly move Sum Carol-coins from the joint account Acct to Bob.
