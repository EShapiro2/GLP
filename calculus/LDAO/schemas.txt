LDAO: Liquid DAO
LDAO extends DAO.
Vocabulary: x has a delegate in d = delegating(x,d); x holds the delegation of y to z in d = delegation(x,y,z,d).
After y makes z y's delegate in x's d, which requires y does not have a delegate in d: y has a delegate in d and x holds the delegation of y to z in d.
After y revokes y's delegation to z in x's d: no longer y has a delegate in d and no longer x holds the delegation of y to z in d.
After x counts y as voting for p of d, which requires y has not voted on p of d: y has voted on p of d and x holds y's vote for p of d.
After x counts y as voting against p of d, which requires y has not voted on p of d: y has voted on p of d and x holds y's vote against p of d.
LDAO:0 - | Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Delos a DAO and Prop a proposal, Carol or Dave possibly Alice.
LDAO:1 ■ Delegate: delegating(Carol,Delos), delegation(Alice,Carol,Dave,Delos) <- member(Carol,Delos,Alice), member(Dave,Delos,Alice), knows(Carol,Dave), steward(Alice,Delos), ~delegating(Carol,Delos) guarded by Carol possibly Alice=Carol, Alice=Dave.
LDAO:2 ■ Revoke: ~delegating(Carol,Delos), ~delegation(Alice,Carol,Dave,Delos) <- delegating(Carol,Delos), delegation(Alice,Carol,Dave,Delos) guarded by Carol possibly Alice=Carol, Alice=Dave.
LDAO:3 ■ Count for: voted(Carol,Prop,Delos), vote_for(Alice,Carol,Prop,Delos) <- delegation(Alice,Carol,Dave,Delos), vote_for(Alice,Dave,Prop,Delos), ~voted(Carol,Prop,Delos) possibly Alice=Carol, Alice=Dave.
LDAO:3 ■ Count against: voted(Carol,Prop,Delos), vote_against(Alice,Carol,Prop,Delos) <- delegation(Alice,Carol,Dave,Delos), vote_against(Alice,Dave,Prop,Delos), ~voted(Carol,Prop,Delos) possibly Alice=Carol, Alice=Dave.
LDAO:3 ■ | Carol can no longer vote on Prop.
