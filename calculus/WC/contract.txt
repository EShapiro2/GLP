WC: Workers' Cooperative
WC instantiates GCoop with bylaws that delegate no act and extends it.
A *workers' cooperative* is a cooperative whose members work for it.
Bob is a person, Orga a workers' cooperative.
1. ■ Pay: Orga may pay Bob, a member, Dollar-denominated coins for his work.
2. ■ Distribute: Orga may distribute Dollar-denominated coins it holds to the members equally.
