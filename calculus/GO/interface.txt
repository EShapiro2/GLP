GO: Grassroots Organisation
1. ■ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity, and then Alice is a person.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other, Alice knows Carol, Alice is a person, Bob is a person and Carol is a person.
4. ■ Befriend.  Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other, Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
5. ■ Person unfriend.  Alice may end her friendship with Bob, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
6. ■ Enter.  Alice may create Carol, and Carol must consent, provided Alice is a person.
7. ■ Appoint.  Those who decide for Carol may make Bob an officer of Carol, with the consent of Bob, provided Carol is an organisation, Carol is not retired, Bob is a person and Bob is a participant of Carol, and then Bob holds the role Role in Carol and Carol has Bob in the role Role.
8. ■ Resign.  Bob may end his guardianship of Carol, and then Bob holds no role in Carol and Carol has Bob in no role.
9. ■ End officer.  Those who decide for Carol may end Bob's guardianship of Carol, and then Bob holds no role in Carol and Carol has Bob in no role.
10. ■ Officer unfriend.  Bob may end his friendship with his organisation Carol, and then Bob holds no role in Carol and Carol has Bob in no role.
11. ■ Ward acquaint.  Carol and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Carol is an organisation, Carol has Alice as an officer and Bob is a person.
12. ■ Ward befriend.  Carol and Bob may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Carol is an organisation, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as an officer, Bob is a person, Carol and Bob know each other and Alice and Bob are friends.
13. ■ Ward end.  Carol may end her friendship with Bob, provided Bob is not an officer of Carol.
14. ■ Unfriend.  Bob may end his friendship with Carol, provided Bob is not an officer of Carol.
15. ■ Ward unfriend.  Alice may end the friendship of Carol and Bob, provided Carol has Alice as an officer and Bob is not an officer of Carol.
16. ■ Wards acquaint.  Carol and Dave may jointly come to know each other, provided Carol is an organisation, Carol has Alice as an officer, Dave is an organisation and Dave has Bob as an officer.
17. ■ Wards befriend.  Carol and Dave may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice and Bob, provided Carol is an organisation, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as an officer, Dave is an organisation, Dave is not retired, Dave has Bob as an officer, Carol and Dave know each other, Alice knows Dave and Alice and Bob are friends.
18. ■ Wards of one guardian befriend.  Carol and Dave may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Carol is an organisation, Carol is not retired, Carol has Alice as an officer, Dave is an organisation, Dave is not retired, Dave has Alice as an officer and Carol and Dave know each other.
19. ■ Retire.  Those who decide for Carol may retire Carol, and then no one holds any role in Carol and Carol has no one in any role.
20. ■ Found.  Gov may create Orga with Alice as its first officer in the role Role, with the consent of Alice, and Orga must consent, provided Alice is a person.
21. ■ Propose.  Bob may propose Motion in Orga, provided Bob is entitled to propose Motion in Orga.
22. ■ Vote.  Those who decide Motion for Orga may vote on Motion in Orga, provided Orga is an organisation and Orga has Motion proposed.
23. ■ Act.  Alice may take Motion for Orga, provided Alice is entitled to take Motion for Orga and the votes decide Motion according to the bylaws of Orga.
24. ■ Join.  Bob may become a participant of Orga, with the consent of those who decide for Orga.
25. ■ Leave.  Bob may end his participation in Orga, provided Bob is not an officer of Orga.
26. ■ Officer leave.  Bob may end his participation and his office in Orga.
27. ■ Expel.  Those who decide for Orga may end Bob's participation in Orga, provided Bob is not an officer of Orga.
28. ■ Officer expel.  Those who decide for Orga may end Bob's participation and Bob's office in Orga.
29. ■ Amend.  Those who decide the amendment of the bylaws of Orga may amend the bylaws of Orga, provided Orga is an organisation.
