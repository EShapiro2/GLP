GAN: Grassroots Ad Network
GAN extends GNO and GDO (GRH, Grassroots Ride-Hailing, in preparation).
An *advertiser* is a provider and a *member* a customer. A member's *profile* is held by the member and by no one else. An *ad* names the profile it is for, the Orga-coins its advertiser pays for a view and for a click, the most one member may earn from it, and its *budget*, the most it pays in all.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Orga a network organisation, Ad an ad; in clause 1 Carol is a member.
1. ■ Posting. Post: Bob may post Ad in Orga, provided Bob is an advertiser of Orga. Bob posts the ad to the members he points to, and its budget is then locked on top of his stake. Send ad: Ad of Bob goes to Alice in Orga, provided Bob has Ad posted in Orga, Bob points to Alice and Alice is a member of Orga. Pass: Alice may pass Ad of Bob to Carol in Orga, provided Alice is a member of Orga, Alice has received Ad of Bob in Orga, Alice points to Carol and Carol is a member of Orga.
2. ■ Show: Alice is shown Ad of Bob, provided Alice is a member of Orga, Alice has received Ad of Bob in Orga and Ad is for the profile of Alice.
3. ◆ Viewing and clicking. View: Alice may attest a view of Ad of Bob, provided Alice has been shown Ad of Bob. Click: Alice may attest a click of Ad of Bob, provided Alice has been shown Ad of Bob. The ad's budget then pays Alice its coins for the view or the click, up to the most one member may earn from it.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: y has d posted in z = posted(y,d,z); y has the budget of d locked in z = budget_locked(y,d,z); x has received d of y in z = received(x,d,y,z); d is for the profile of x = for_profile(x,d); x has been shown d of y = ad_shown(x,d,y); x has attested a view of d of y = viewed(x,d,y); x has attested a click of d of y = clicked(x,d,y).
y is an advertiser of z: y is a provider of z.
x is a member of z: x is a customer of z.
After y posts d in z, which requires y does not have d posted in z: y has d posted in z and y has the budget of d locked in z.
After d of y goes to x in z, which requires x has not received d of y in z: x has received d of y in z.
After x passes d of y to w in z, which requires w has not received d of y in z: w has received d of y in z.
After x is shown d of y, which requires x has not been shown d of y: x has been shown d of y.
After x attests a view of d of y: x has attested a view of d of y.
After x attests a click of d of y: x has attested a click of d of y.
