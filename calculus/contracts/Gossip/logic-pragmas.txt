Gossip
Gossip extends Friendship.
*Posts* have a *budget*, a parameter of Gossip: what a post has when made, and the *cost* of a forward. Gossip has a *gossip protocol*, a parameter: to which of their friends a holder may forward a post, and with what part of the rest of the budget. A person *holds* any post they made or forwarded to them.
Alice and Bob are persons, Post a post, Budget, Rest and Part numbers.
1. ■ Post: Alice may make Post with the budget Budget, provided Budget is a budget of a new post.
2. ■ Tell: Alice may forward Post to Bob with the budget Part, provided Alice holds the post Post, Alice has the budget Budget for Post, Budget covers the cost of a forward leaving Rest, Alice and Bob are friends and the gossip protocol forwards Post to Bob with Part of Rest.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x holds the post p = post(x,p); y holds the post p from x = post_from(y,p,x); x has made the post p = made(x,p); x has the budget n for p = budget(x,p,n); n is a budget of a new post, for x = new_budget(x,n); n covers the cost of a forward leaving m, for x = covers(x,n,m); the gossip protocol forwards p to y with m of n, for x = protocol(x,p,y,m,n).
After x makes p with the budget n, which requires x has not made the post p: x has made the post p, x holds the post p and x has the budget n for p.
After x forwards p to y with the budget n: y holds the post p from x, y holds the post p and y has the budget n for p.
Environment: n is a budget of a new post, for x; n covers the cost of a forward leaving m, for x; the gossip protocol forwards p to y with m of n, for x.
