Labelled Overlay
Labelled Overlay extends and strengthens Overlay.
Vocabulary: x has a w-pointer to y = pointer(x,y,w).
After x adds a w-pointer to y, which requires x does not have a w-pointer to y: x has a w-pointer to y.
After x removes x's w-pointer to y: no longer x has a w-pointer to y.
Labelled Overlay:0 - | A pointer may be labeled by a person, and a pointer labeled by Dave is a *Dave-pointer*. Friends Alice and Bob have a Bob-pointer from Alice to Bob and an Alice-pointer from Bob to Alice.
Labelled Overlay:0 - | Alice, Bob, Carol, Dave and Erin are persons, Erin is possibly any of the others; in clause 2 Carol is possibly Dave.
Labelled Overlay:1 ■ Labeled point friend: pointer(Alice,Bob,Dave) <- friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice), pointer(Bob,Erin,Dave), ~pointer(Alice,Bob,Dave) guarded by Alice possibly Alice=Erin, Bob=Erin, Dave=Erin.
Labelled Overlay:2 ■ Labeled point transitive: pointer(Alice,Carol,Dave) <- knows(Alice,Carol), pointer(Alice,Bob,Dave), pointer(Bob,Carol,Dave), ~pointer(Alice,Carol,Dave) guarded by Alice possibly Carol=Dave.
Labelled Overlay:2 ■ Labeled point transitive friend: pointer(Alice,Carol,Dave) <- knows(Alice,Carol), friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice), pointer(Bob,Carol,Dave), ~pointer(Alice,Carol,Dave) guarded by Alice possibly Carol=Dave.
Labelled Overlay:3 ■ Labeled unpoint: ~pointer(Alice,Carol,Dave) <- pointer(Alice,Carol,Dave) guarded by Alice.
Labelled Overlay:3 ■ Labeled unpoint self: ~pointer(Alice,Dave,Dave) <- ~friend(Alice,Dave), pointer(Alice,Dave,Dave) guarded by Alice.
Labelled Overlay:4 ■ | Friend pointers.
Labelled Overlay:4 ■ Befriend: Friendship:1 + pointer(Alice,Bob,Bob), pointer(Bob,Alice,Alice) <-.
Labelled Overlay:4 ■ Unfriend: Friendship:2 + ~pointer(Alice,Bob,Bob), ~pointer(Bob,Alice,Alice) <-.
