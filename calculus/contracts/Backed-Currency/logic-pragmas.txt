Backed Currency
Backed Currency extends Community Currency and restricts it.
*f*, a fraction 0 ≤ f < 1, is a parameter of Backed Currency. Orga's *account* is a bank account in Dollars, held in Orga's name by its treasurers. A treasurer, Alice, is *backed* when the Alice-coins Orga holds are at most the Dollars Alice paid into the account and the coins Alice holds of the other treasurers, leaving out the fraction f of them of whom she holds the most.
Alice is a person, Orga a community currency.
1. ■ Treasurers. Appoint: Organisation:4 Appoint, only if every treasurer of Orga stays backed. Resign: Organisation:4 Resign, only if every treasurer of Orga stays backed. End officer: Organisation:4 End officer, only if every treasurer of Orga stays backed. Officer unfriend: Organisation:4 Officer unfriend, only if every treasurer of Orga stays backed. Leave: Organisation:3 Leave, only if every treasurer of Orga stays backed. Officer leave: Organisation:3 Officer leave, only if every treasurer of Orga stays backed. Expel: Organisation:3 Expel, only if every treasurer of Orga stays backed. Officer expel: Organisation:3 Officer expel, only if every treasurer of Orga stays backed. Swap: Notes:2, only if every treasurer of Orga stays backed. Pay: Notes:3, only if every treasurer of Orga stays backed. Escrow: Notes:4, only if every treasurer of Orga stays backed. Pay issuer: Coins:1, only if every treasurer of Orga stays backed. Redeem: Bonds:2, only if every treasurer of Orga stays backed. Deposit own coins: Denominated Currencies:2 Deposit own coins, only if every treasurer of Orga stays backed. Deposit with the issuer: Denominated Currencies:2 Deposit with the issuer, only if every treasurer of Orga stays backed. Deposit own bonds: Denominated Currencies:2 Deposit own bonds, only if every treasurer of Orga stays backed. Deposit bond with the issuer: Denominated Currencies:2 Deposit bond with the issuer, only if every treasurer of Orga stays backed. Pay: Denominated Currencies:4, only if every treasurer of Orga stays backed.
2. ■ Draw: Community Currency:1, only if Alice stays backed in Orga.
3. ◆ Record: The treasurers of Orga may record a Dollar as paid into the account of Orga by Alice, with the consent of Alice, provided Orga is an organisation and Alice is a treasurer of Orga. Alice, a treasurer, may pay Dollars into the account, and the treasurers may then jointly record them as paid in by Alice.
4. ○ Fiat redemption. Community Currency:2, from the account while it holds Dollars, and from a treasurer if it holds none.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: every treasurer of z stays backed, for x = all_stay_backed(x,z); x stays backed in z = stays_backed(z,x); z has a Dollar recorded as paid in by x = paid_in(z,x).
Roles: the treasurers of z = treasurers(z), listed by z has y in the role Treasurer and y holds the role Treasurer in z.
After g records a Dollar as paid into the account of z by x: z has a Dollar recorded as paid in by x.
Environment: every treasurer of z stays backed, for x; x stays backed in z.
