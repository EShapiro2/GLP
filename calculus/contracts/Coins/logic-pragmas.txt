Coins
Coins renames Notes, and extends it.
A *coin* is a note that is a unit of its issuer's debt. An *Alice-coin* is a coin issued by Alice.
Alice, Bob and Carol are parties, Sum a number, Carol possibly Alice or Bob.
1. ■ Pay issuer: Notes:3, without the consent of Bob, if Carol is Bob.
2. ■ Redeem: Alice may redeem a Bob-coin against a Carol-coin, provided Alice knows Bob.
3. ○ Pricing. Alice will price her offerings in Alice-coins and accept them in payment at those prices.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x holds a y-coin = coin(x,y); x holds n y-coins = coin(x,y,*n); x holds exactly n y-coins = coin(x,y,#n); z holds n w-coins in escrow from x for y on k = escrow_coin(z,w,x,y,k,*n).
After x pays the issuer y n y-coins: no longer x holds n y-coins and y holds n y-coins.
After x redeems a y-coin against a z-coin: no longer x holds a y-coin, y holds a y-coin, no longer y holds a z-coin, x holds a z-coin and x knows z.
Kind of note: coin, y-coins for y-notes; its acts keep their names.
