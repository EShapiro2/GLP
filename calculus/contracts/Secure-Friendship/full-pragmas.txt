Secure Friendship
Of Knowledge: An *identity* is a unique cryptographic key pair. A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of Friendship: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts.
Of Secure Friendship: The *identity record* of Alice is her *identity custodians*, persons she names, and her *threshold*, a share of them greater than half. The *records* of Alice are her friends and, for each friend, the friends of that friend. Alice suffers a *state loss* when her machine loses some or all of its state and she keeps her private key, and an *identity loss* when she loses her private key. Environment: x has suffered a state loss.
Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Alicia a new identity of Alice, Share and Double numbers.
Knowledge:1. ○ Establish: Alice may establish an identity. Alice will make herself known by her public key, sign her acts with her private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
Knowledge:2. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
Knowledge:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
Friendship:1. ○ Befriend: Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other. Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
Friendship:2. ■ Unfriend: Alice may end her friendship with Bob.
Friendship:3. ○ Loss of Trust. Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
Secure Friendship:1. ■ Name custodian: Alice may name Carol her identity custodian, provided Alice knows Carol and Alice does not have a fixed identity record. Identity record: Alice may fix her identity record with the threshold Share, provided Alice has at least Share persons as an identity custodian, Double is Share times 2 and Alice has fewer than Double persons as an identity custodian. It then changes only by the replacement of a custodian's lost identity (Secure Friendship:5).
Secure Friendship:2. ■ Friendship updates. Tell: Alice tells Carol of the friendship of Alice and Bob, provided Alice and Carol are friends and Alice and Bob are friends. Tell end: Alice tells Carol of the end of the friendship of Alice and Bob, provided Alice and Carol are friends and Alice and Bob are not friends. Send record: Alice sends Bob the identity custodian Carol, provided Alice and Bob are friends, Alice has a fixed identity record and Alice has Carol as an identity custodian. Send threshold: Alice sends Bob the threshold Share, provided Alice and Bob are friends and Alice has the threshold Share.
Secure Friendship:3. ■ Checkpoint: Alice sends Bob a checkpoint of Alice's friends and Alice's identity record, provided Alice and Bob are friends. Bob keeps it.
Secure Friendship:4. ■ State recovery. Restore: Bob restores the records of Alice, provided Alice has suffered a state loss, Bob counts Alice as a friend and Bob keeps a checkpoint of Alice. Restore friend: Bob restores the friendship of Alice with Carol, provided Alice has suffered a state loss, Bob counts Alice as a friend, Bob keeps a checkpoint of Alice and Bob records the friendship of Alice and Carol.
Secure Friendship:5. ◆ Identity replacement. Replace: Alicia may ask Carol to vouch that Alicia is Alice, provided Alicia knows Carol and Carol is an identity custodian of Alice. Alicia asks only after an identity loss of Alice. Vouch: Carol may vouch that Alicia is Alice, provided Carol has been asked to vouch that Alicia is Alice and Carol is an identity custodian of Alice. Present: Alicia may present to Bob the statement of Carol that Alicia is Alice, provided Alicia knows Bob, Alicia has received the vouch of Carol for Alice, Bob counts Alice as a friend and Bob records Carol as an identity custodian of Alice. Accept: Bob accepts Alicia in place of Alice, provided Alicia knows Bob, Bob records the threshold Share of Alice and Bob holds the statements of at least Share persons that Alicia is Alice. Accept custodian: Bob accepts Alicia as an identity custodian in place of Alice, provided Bob has accepted Alicia in place of Alice. Replace custodian: Bob drops Alice from the identity custodians of Dave, provided Bob has accepted Alicia in place of Alice. Tell friends: Bob passes Alicia the friendship of Alice and Dave, provided Bob has accepted Alicia in place of Alice and Bob records the friendship of Alice and Dave. Bob tells Alicia the friends of Alice he records, and tells his friends of the change.
