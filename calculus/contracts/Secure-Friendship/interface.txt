Secure Friendship
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity once.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ○ Befriend.  Alice and Bob, who trust each other and are not friends, may jointly become friends.
5. ■ Unfriend.  Alice may end her friendship with Bob.
6. ■ Name custodian.  Alice may fix her identity record, once.
7. ■ Identity record.  Alice may fix her identity record, once.
8. ■ Tell.  Alice tells each of her friends when she becomes a friend of Bob (Friendship:1) and when she ends a friendship (Friendship:2), and the friend records it.
9. ■ Tell end.  Alice tells each of her friends when she becomes a friend of Bob (Friendship:1) and when she ends a friendship (Friendship:2), and the friend records it.
10. ■ Send record.  Alice sends Bob, when they become friends, her friends and her identity record, and Bob keeps them.
11. ■ Send threshold.  Alice sends Bob the threshold Share, provided Alice and Bob are friends and Alice has the threshold Share.
12. ■ Checkpoint.  Alice sends each of her friends a checkpoint of her friends and her identity record, and the friend keeps it.
13. ■ Restore.  After a state loss of Alice, each friend of Alice sends her the friendship updates and the checkpoints they keep, and Alice records them again.
14. ■ Restore friend.  After a state loss of Alice, each friend of Alice sends her the friendship updates and the checkpoints they keep, and Alice records them again.
15. ◆ Replace.  After an identity loss, Alice may establish a new identity, Alicia (Knowledge:1), and ask her identity custodians to vouch for it.
16. ◆ Vouch.  Carol, an identity custodian of Alice, may vouch, once, that Alicia is Alice.
17. ◆ Present.  Alicia may present to Bob the statement of Carol that Alicia is Alice, provided Alicia knows Bob, Alicia has received the vouch of Carol for Alice, Bob counts Alice as a friend and Bob records Carol as an identity custodian of Alice.
18. ◆ Accept.  Bob, a friend of Alice, accepts Alicia in place of Alice once at least the threshold of the identity custodians he records for Alice have vouched; Alicia is then his friend in place of Alice, and his identity custodian if Alice was.
19. ◆ Accept custodian.  Bob, a friend of Alice, accepts Alicia in place of Alice once at least the threshold of the identity custodians he records for Alice have vouched; Alicia is then his friend in place of Alice, and his identity custodian if Alice was.
20. ◆ Replace custodian.  After an identity loss, Alice may establish a new identity, Alicia (Knowledge:1), and ask her identity custodians to vouch for it.
21. ◆ Tell friends.  Bob passes Alicia the friendship of Alice and Dave, provided Bob has accepted Alicia in place of Alice and Bob records the friendship of Alice and Dave.
