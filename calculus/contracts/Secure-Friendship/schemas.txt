Secure Friendship
Secure Friendship extends Friendship.
Vocabulary: x has z as an identity custodian = custodian(x,z); z is an identity custodian of x = custodian_of(z,x); x has a fixed identity record = fixed(x); x has the threshold n = threshold(x,n); z records the friendship of x and y = records_friend(z,x,y); y records z as an identity custodian of x = records_custodian(y,x,z); y keeps a checkpoint of x = checkpoint(y,x); x has suffered a state loss = state_loss(x); x has recovered records from y = recovered(x,y); x has recovered the friendship with z from y = recovered_friend(x,z,y); z has been asked to vouch that w is x = asked(z,w,x); z has vouched that w is x = vouched(z,w,x); y records the threshold n of x = records_threshold(y,x,n); w has received the vouch of z for x = received_vouch(w,z,x); y holds c's statement that w is x = holds_statement(y,c,w,x); y has accepted w in place of x = accepted(y,w,x); w has learned from y of the friendship of x and d = learned_friend(w,y,x,d); z records from y the replacement of x by w = records_replacement(z,y,x,w); x has recovered from y the friendship with d in place of z = recovered_replaced(x,y,d,z).
After x names z x's identity custodian, which requires x does not have z as an identity custodian: x has z as an identity custodian and z is an identity custodian of x.
After x fixes x's identity record with the threshold n, which requires x does not have a fixed identity record: x has a fixed identity record and x has the threshold n.
After x tells z of the friendship of x and y, which requires z does not record the friendship of x and y: z records the friendship of x and y.
After x tells z of the end of the friendship of x and y: no longer z records the friendship of x and y.
After x sends y the identity custodian z, which requires y does not record z as an identity custodian of x: y records z as an identity custodian of x.
After x sends y the threshold n, which requires y does not record the threshold n of x: y records the threshold n of x.
After x sends y a checkpoint of x's friends and x's identity record, which requires y does not keep a checkpoint of x: y keeps a checkpoint of x.
After x reports to y the friendship of x and z, which requires y does not record the friendship of x and z: y records the friendship of x and z.
After x resends y the identity custodian z, which requires y does not record z as an identity custodian of x: y records z as an identity custodian of x.
After x resends y the threshold n, which requires y does not record the threshold n of x: y records the threshold n of x.
After y restores the records of x, which requires x does not count y as a friend: x counts y as a friend and x has recovered records from y.
After y restores the friendship of x with z, which requires x does not count z as a friend: x counts z as a friend and x has recovered the friendship with z from y.
After y restores the friendship of x with d in place of z, which requires x does not count d as a friend: x counts d as a friend and x has recovered from y the friendship with d in place of z.
After w asks z to vouch that w is x, which requires z has not been asked to vouch that w is x: z has been asked to vouch that w is x.
After z vouches that w is x, which requires z has not vouched that w is x: z has vouched that w is x and w has received the vouch of z for x.
After w presents to y the statement of c that w is x, which requires y does not hold c's statement that w is x: y holds c's statement that w is x.
After y accepts w in place of x, which requires y has not accepted w in place of x: no longer y counts x as a friend, y counts w as a friend, w counts y as a friend and y has accepted w in place of x.
After y accepts w as an identity custodian in place of x: no longer y has x as an identity custodian, y has w as an identity custodian and w is an identity custodian of y.
After y drops x from the identity custodians of d: no longer y records x as an identity custodian of d.
After y tells z of the replacement of x by w, which requires z does not record from y the replacement of x by w: z records from y the replacement of x by w.
After y passes w the friendship of x and d, which requires w has not learned from y of the friendship of x and d: w has learned from y of the friendship of x and d.
Secure Friendship:0 - | The *identity record* of Alice is her *identity custodians*, persons she names, and her *threshold*, a share of them greater than half. The *records* of Alice are her friends and, for each friend, the friends of that friend. Alice suffers a *state loss* when her machine loses some or all of its state and she keeps her private key, and an *identity loss* when she loses her private key.
Secure Friendship:0 - | Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Alicia a new identity of Alice, Share and Double numbers.
