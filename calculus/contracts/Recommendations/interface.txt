Recommendations
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity once.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ◆ Recommend.  Alice may recommend Bob, whom she knows, for something, a skill or a service, once for each thing; Bob keeps the recommendation.
5. ■ Show.  Bob may show a recommendation he holds to Carol, whom he knows, once; Carol then sees who recommended Bob and for what.
6. ■ Withdraw.  Alice may withdraw a recommendation; it is then gone from Bob and from everyone Bob showed it to, and Alice may not make it again.
