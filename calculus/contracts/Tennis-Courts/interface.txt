Tennis Courts
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity once.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ○ Befriend.  Alice and Bob, who trust each other and are not friends, may jointly become friends.
5. ■ Person unfriend.  Alice may end her friendship with Bob.
6. ■ Found.  Alice may create an organisation, Orga, and establish Orga's identity by choosing Orga's key pair, and Orga must consent.
7. ■ Appoint.  Those who decide for Orga may make Bob an officer of Orga, with the consent of Bob, provided Orga is an organisation, Orga is not retired, Bob is a person, Orga has Alice as an officer, Bob counts Alice as a friend and Bob is a participant of Orga, and then Bob holds the role Role in Orga and Orga has Bob in the role Role.
8. ■ Resign.  Bob may end his guardianship of Orga.
9. ■ End officer.  Those who decide for Orga, other than Bob, may end Bob's guardianship.
10. ■ Officer unfriend.  Bob may end his friendship with Orga, which ends his guardianship.
11. ■ Organisation acquaint.  Orga, an organisation of Alice, and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
12. ■ Organisation befriend.  Orga may enter a relation with Bob with the consent of Bob and Alice, provided Orga and Bob know each other and Alice and Bob are friends.
13. ■ Organisation end.  Orga, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Orga.
14. ■ Unfriend.  Orga, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Orga.
15. ■ Organisation unfriend.  Orga, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Orga.
16. ■ Organisations acquaint.  Orga, an organisation of Alice, and Will, an organisation of Bob, may jointly come to know each other.
17. ■ Organisations befriend.  Orga may enter a relation with Will with the consent of Will, Alice and Bob, provided Orga and Will know each other, Alice knows Will and is a friend of Bob, Alice and Bob possibly one person.
18. ■ Organisations of one officer befriend.  Orga may enter a relation with Will with the consent of Will, Alice and Bob, provided Orga and Will know each other, Alice knows Will and is a friend of Bob, Alice and Bob possibly one person.
19. ■ Retire.  Those who decide for Orga may retire Orga; every guardianship and relation of Orga then ends and Orga can take no act.
20. ■ Propose.  An officer, or a person the bylaws name, may propose an act of Orga.
21. ■ Vote.  Those who decide the act may vote on it.
22. ■ Act.  An officer the bylaws name for the act may take it for Orga after the votes decide it according to the bylaws.
23. ■ Join.  Bob may become a participant of Orga with the consent of those who decide for Orga.
24. ■ Leave.  Either Bob or those who decide for Orga may end Bob's participation, and with it every role he holds.
25. ■ Officer leave.  Either Bob or those who decide for Orga may end Bob's participation, and with it every role he holds.
26. ■ Expel.  Either Bob or those who decide for Orga may end Bob's participation, and with it every role he holds.
27. ■ Officer expel.  Either Bob or those who decide for Orga may end Bob's participation, and with it every role he holds.
28. ■ Amend.  Those who decide it may amend the bylaws.
29. ■ Book.  Alice, a participant of Orga with fewer outstanding bookings than the bound, may book any free slot of a court of Orga, by Alice and Carol jointly, each a participant of the Club with fewer outstanding bookings than the bound.
30. ■ Cancel.  Alice may cancel her booking.
31. ■ Post.  Alice may make a post.
32. ■ Tell.  Alice who holds a post, the budget of which covers the cost, may forward it to her friend Bob with a part of the rest of the budget, both according to the gossip protocol.
33. ■ Return.  Bob, who holds two matching posts, one from Alice, forwards to Alice the second post.
34. ■ Meet.  Alice and Carol, both holding matching posts one made by Alice and one made by Carol, may jointly come to know each other, only by Anonymous Rendezvous:1 with the one-time keys of the two posts, at the rendezvous server named by the post whose public key has the smaller hash.
35. ■ Enter.  Alice may create an organisation, Orga, and establish Orga's identity by choosing Orga's key pair, and Wendy must consent.
36. ■ Make guardian.  Those who decide for Wendy may make Bob a guardian of Wendy, with the consent of Bob, provided Wendy is a ward, Wendy is not retired, Bob is a person, Wendy has Alice as a guardian and Bob counts Alice as a friend.
37. ■ Resign 2.  Bob may end his guardianship of Orga.
38. ■ End guardian.  Those who decide for Wendy, other than Bob, may end Bob's guardianship of Wendy.
39. ■ Guardian unfriend.  Bob may end his friendship with his ward Wendy.
40. ■ Ward acquaint.  Wendy and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Wendy is a ward, Wendy has Alice as a guardian and Bob is a person.
41. ■ Ward befriend.  Wendy and Bob may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Wendy is a ward, Wendy is not retired, Wendy has Alice as a guardian, Bob is a person, Wendy and Bob know each other and Alice and Bob are friends.
42. ■ Ward end.  Wendy may end her friendship with Bob, provided Wendy does not have Bob as a guardian.
43. ■ Unfriend 2.  Orga, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Orga.
44. ■ Ward unfriend.  Orga, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Orga.
45. ■ Wards acquaint.  Wendy and Will may jointly come to know each other, provided Wendy is a ward, Wendy has Alice as a guardian, Will is a ward and Will has Bob as a guardian.
46. ■ Wards befriend.  Wendy and Will may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice and Bob, provided Wendy is a ward, Wendy is not retired, Wendy has Alice as a guardian, Will is a ward, Will is not retired, Will has Bob as a guardian, Wendy and Will know each other, Alice knows Will and Alice and Bob are friends.
47. ■ Wards of one guardian befriend.  Wendy may enter a relation with Will with the consent of Will, Alice and Bob, provided Wendy and Will know each other, Alice knows Will and is a friend of Bob, Alice and Bob possibly one person.
48. ■ Retire 2.  Those who decide for Orga may retire Orga; every guardianship and relation of Orga then ends and Orga can take no act.
49. ■ Open server.  Carol may make her service Dave a rendezvous server.
50. ■ Sign link.  Alice may register with Dave an invitation link of hers that names Dave, with a new serial.
51. ■ Invite by link.  Alice may register with Dave an invitation link of hers that names Dave, with a new serial.
52. ■ Use link.  Bob may present to Dave an invitation link of Alice's registered with Dave that has not expired; Dave then gives Alice Bob's address and Bob Alice's, and the link is spent.
53. ■ Present.  Alice and Bob, who are friends, may each present to Dave the other's signature over their friendship.
54. ■ Match.  Dave then gives each the address of the other.
55. ■ Present 2.  Alice and Bob, each holding a one-time key and knowing the public key of the other's, may each present to Dave the two public keys, signed with their own.
56. ■ Match 2.  Dave then gives each the address of the other.
57. ■ Organisation ward acquaint.  Club, an organisation, and Wendy, a ward, may jointly come to know each other.
