1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity once.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ■ Befriend.  Alice and Bob, who trust each other and are not friends, may jointly become friends.
5. ■ Person unfriend.  Alice may end her friendship with Bob.
6. ■ Sign.  Alice may create an item by signing it.
7. ■ Forward.  Alice may forward an item she holds to her friend Bob, adding her signature to the signatures the item carries.
8. ◆ Publish.  Carol may publish a program by signing it, attesting that its schemas say what its clauses say, that it is the result of compiling the schemas, and that it is expected to behave correctly according to the contract.
9. ■ Trust.  Alice may declare trust in Carol, whom she knows, as a developer.
10. ■ Untrust.  Alice may withdraw the declaration.
11. ■ Offer.  Carol may offer a program she holds to her friend Alice by forwarding it to her.
12. ■ Install own.  Alice may install a program she published, as well as a program offered to her whose developer she trusts.
13. ■ Install.  Alice may install a program she published, as well as a program offered to her whose developer she trusts.
14. ■ Invite.  Alice may invite her friend Bob to join her in a program she installed, with the consent of Bob, who trusts its developer; Bob then has the program installed, with Alice's signature added, and each is a participant of the program with the other.
15. ■ Leave.  Alice may leave a program she installed; it is then no longer installed, and her participation in it with every participant ends.
16. ■ Enter.  Alice may create a ward, Carol, and establish Carol's identity by choosing Carol's key pair, and Carol must consent.
17. ■ Make guardian.  Those who decide for Carol may make Bob, a friend of a guardian of Carol, a guardian and a friend of Carol, with the consent of Bob.
18. ■ Resign.  Bob may end his guardianship of Carol.
19. ■ End guardian.  Those who decide for Carol, other than Bob, may end Bob's guardianship.
20. ■ Guardian unfriend.  Bob may end his friendship with Carol, which ends his guardianship.
21. ■ Ward acquaint.  Carol, a ward of Alice, and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
22. ■ Ward befriend.  Carol may enter a relation with Bob with the consent of Bob and Alice, provided Carol and Bob know each other and Alice and Bob are friends.
23. ■ Ward end.  Carol, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Carol.
24. ■ Unfriend.  Carol, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Carol.
25. ■ Ward unfriend.  Carol, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Carol.
26. ■ Wards acquaint.  Carol, a ward of Alice, and Dave, a ward of Bob, may jointly come to know each other.
27. ■ Wards befriend.  Carol may enter a relation with Dave with the consent of Dave, Alice and Bob, provided Carol and Dave know each other, Alice knows Dave and is a friend of Bob, Alice and Bob possibly one person.
28. ■ Wards of one guardian befriend.  Carol may enter a relation with Dave with the consent of Dave, Alice and Bob, provided Carol and Dave know each other, Alice knows Dave and is a friend of Bob, Alice and Bob possibly one person.
29. ■ Retire.  Those who decide for Carol may retire Carol; every guardianship and relation of Carol then ends and Carol can take no act.
30. ■ Open store.  Carol may make her service Dave a store.
31. ○ List.  Carol may list in Dave a program developed by Bob, after adding her signature to its signatures, provided she trusts Bob as a developer.
32. ○ Delist.  Carol may delist it.
33. ■ Trust store.  Alice may declare trust in Dave, Carol being known to her.
34. ■ Untrust store.  Alice may withdraw the declaration.
35. ■ Install from store.  Alice may install a program listed in a store she trusts.
