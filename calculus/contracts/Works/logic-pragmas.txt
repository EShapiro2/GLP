Works
Works extends Community Bank.
A *work* names a task, its *price* in community coins, its *contractor*, who will do it, and its *supervisor*, an officer of the community. The bylaws name the role *arbiter*.
Bob, Carol, Dave and Erin are persons, Orga a community, Job a work, Sum, Back and Rest numbers, Why a reason.
1. ■ Approve: Those who decide for Orga may approve Job of Orga at Sum for Bob under Carol locking Sum Orga-coins, with the consent of Bob, provided Carol is an officer of Orga.
2. ◆ Complete: Bob may attest the completion of Job of Orga, provided Orga has Job approved at Sum for Bob under Carol.
3. ◆ Accept: Carol may accept Job of Bob in Orga releasing to Bob the Sum Orga-coins locked, provided Orga has Job approved at Sum for Bob under Carol.
4. ■ Reject: Carol may reject Job of Bob in Orga for Why, provided Orga has Job approved at Sum for Bob under Carol and Bob has attested the completion of Job of Orga.
5. ■ Cancel: Those who decide for Orga may cancel Job of Orga under Carol releasing Rest Orga-coins to Bob and Back Orga-coins to Orga of the Sum locked, with the consent of Bob, provided Sum is Rest plus Back. Cancel for the contractor: Those who decide for Orga may cancel Job of Orga under Carol releasing to Bob the Sum Orga-coins locked, with the consent of Bob. Cancel for the community: Those who decide for Orga may cancel Job of Orga for Bob under Carol releasing to Orga the Sum Orga-coins locked, with the consent of Bob.
6. ■ Supervise: Those who decide for Orga may put Carol for Erin as the supervisor of Job of Orga at Sum for Bob, provided Carol is an officer of Orga.
7. ■ Arbitration. Raise: Bob may raise Job of Orga to Dave, provided Orga has Job approved at Sum for Bob under Carol and Dave is an arbiter of Orga. Raise for the community: Those who decide for Orga may raise Job of Bob in Orga to Dave, provided Orga has Job approved at Sum for Bob under Carol and Dave is an arbiter of Orga. Arbitrate: Dave may decide the release of Rest Orga-coins to Bob and Back Orga-coins to Orga of the Sum locked on Job under Carol, provided Orga has Job raised to Dave and Sum is Rest plus Back. Arbitrate for the contractor: Dave may decide the release to Bob of the Sum Orga-coins locked on Job of Orga under Carol, provided Orga has Job raised to Dave. Arbitrate for the community: Dave may decide the release to Orga of the Sum Orga-coins locked on Job for Bob under Carol, provided Orga has Job raised to Dave.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: z has w approved at n for y under x = work(z,w,n,y,x); z has w accepted = accepted(z,w); z has w decided = decided_work(z,w); z holds n z-coins locked on w = work_locked(z,w,*n); y has attested the completion of w of z = completed(y,w,z); x has rejected w of y in z for r = rejected(x,w,y,z,r); z has w raised to d = raised_work(z,w,d).
d is an arbiter of z: d holds the role Arbiter in z and z has d in the role Arbiter.
After g approves w of z at n for y under x locking n z-coins, which requires z does not have w decided: z has w decided, z has w approved at n for y under x, no longer z holds n z-coins and z holds n z-coins locked on w.
After y attests the completion of w of z, which requires y has not attested the completion of w of z: y has attested the completion of w of z.
After x accepts w of y in z releasing to y the n z-coins locked: no longer z has w approved at n for y under x, z has w accepted, z has w raised to no one, no longer z holds n z-coins locked on w and y holds n z-coins.
After x rejects w of y in z for r: x has rejected w of y in z for r.
After g cancels w of z under x releasing q z-coins to y and p z-coins to z of the n locked: no longer z has w approved at n for y under x, z has w raised to no one, no longer z holds n z-coins locked on w, y holds q z-coins and z holds p z-coins.
After g cancels w of z under x releasing to y the n z-coins locked: no longer z has w approved at n for y under x, z has w raised to no one, no longer z holds n z-coins locked on w and y holds n z-coins.
After g cancels w of z for y under x releasing to z the n z-coins locked: no longer z has w approved at n for y under x, z has w raised to no one, no longer z holds n z-coins locked on w and z holds n z-coins.
After g puts x for v as the supervisor of w of z at n for y: no longer z has w approved at n for y under v and z has w approved at n for y under x.
After y raises w of z to d, which requires z has w raised to no one: z has w raised to d.
After g raises w of y in z to d, which requires z has w raised to no one: z has w raised to d.
After d decides the release of q z-coins to y and p z-coins to z of the n locked on w under x: no longer z has w approved at n for y under x, no longer z has w raised to d, no longer z holds n z-coins locked on w, y holds q z-coins and z holds p z-coins.
After d decides the release to y of the n z-coins locked on w of z under x: no longer z has w approved at n for y under x, no longer z has w raised to d, no longer z holds n z-coins locked on w and y holds n z-coins.
After d decides the release to z of the n z-coins locked on w for y under x: no longer z has w approved at n for y under x, no longer z has w raised to d, no longer z holds n z-coins locked on w and z holds n z-coins.
