Denominated Currencies
Denominated Currencies extends Bonds and Denominated Coins, and restricts and strengthens Bonds.
Vocabulary: x has issued an f-denominated bond due e = issued_bond_in(x,f,e); x holds an f-denominated y-bond due e = bond(x,y,e,f); x holds n f-denominated y-bonds due e = bond(x,y,e,f,*n); z holds n f-denominated w-bonds due e in escrow from x for y on k = escrow_bond(z,w,e,f,x,y,k,*n); z holds the escrow from x for y on k in the denomination f = escrow_denomination(z,x,y,k,f); x accepts an f-denominated z-coin in payment = accepts(x,z,f); x accepts n f-denominated z-coins in payment = accepts(x,z,f,*n).
After x issues n f-denominated x-bonds due e on record: x holds n f-denominated x-bonds due e and x has issued an f-denominated bond due e.
After x accepts n more f-denominated z-coins in payment: x accepts n f-denominated z-coins in payment.
After x pays y a z-coin that y accepts as denominated by f: no longer x holds an f-denominated z-coin, y holds an f-denominated z-coin and no longer y accepts an f-denominated z-coin in payment.
After x deposits n more f-denominated w-coins with z for y on k: no longer x holds n f-denominated w-coins, z holds n f-denominated w-coins in escrow from x for y on k and z holds the escrow from x for y on k in the denomination f.
After x deposits n more f-denominated w-bonds due e with z for y on k: no longer x holds n f-denominated w-bonds due e, z holds n f-denominated w-bonds due e in escrow from x for y on k and z holds the escrow from x for y on k in the denomination f.
