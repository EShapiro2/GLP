Denominated Currencies
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity once.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ■ Issue.  Alice may issue Alice-notes, for bonds, with maturity later than the current date.
5. ■ Issue bond.  Alice may issue Alice-notes, for bonds, with maturity later than the current date.
6. ○ Swap.  Alice and Bob, who know each other, may jointly swap notes they hold, only if the coins and bonds are of one denomination.
7. ○ Swap coin for bond.  Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Dollar-denominated Carol-coins for Total Dollar-denominated Dave-bonds due Day, provided Alice and Bob know each other.
8. ○ Swap bonds.  Alice and Bob, who know each other, may jointly swap notes they hold, only if the coins and bonds are of one denomination.
9. ■ Pay.  Alice may pay notes she holds to Bob, whom she knows, with his consent.
10. ■ Pay issuer.  Alice may pay notes she holds to Bob, whom she knows, with his consent.
11. ■ Pay bond.  Alice may pay notes she holds to Bob, whom she knows, with his consent.
12. ○ Deposit.  Alice may deposit notes with Carol for Bob, on a condition the three agree, with the consent of Carol, only if the coins and bonds are of one denomination.
13. ○ Deposit bond.  Alice may deposit notes with Carol for Bob, on a condition the three agree, with the consent of Carol, only if the coins and bonds are of one denomination.
14. ○ Release.  Carol and Bob may jointly release them to Bob, only if the coins and bonds are of one denomination.
15. ○ Release bond.  Carol and Bob may jointly release them to Bob, only if the coins and bonds are of one denomination.
16. ○ Return.  Carol and Alice may jointly return them to Alice, only if the coins and bonds are of one denomination.
17. ○ Return bond.  Carol and Alice may jointly return them to Alice, only if the coins and bonds are of one denomination.
18. ○ Redeem.  Alice may redeem a Bob-coin she holds against any coin held by Bob, whom she knows, only if the coins and bonds are of one denomination.
19. ○ Redeem coin against bond.  Alice may redeem a Bob-coin, or a mature Bob-bond, she holds against any coin or bond held by Bob, whom she knows, only if the coins and bonds are of one denomination.
20. ○ Redeem bond.  Alice may redeem a Bob-coin, or a mature Bob-bond, she holds against any coin or bond held by Bob, whom she knows, only if the coins and bonds are of one denomination.
21. ○ Redeem bond against bond.  Alice may redeem a Bob-coin, or a mature Bob-bond, she holds against any coin or bond held by Bob, whom she knows, only if the coins and bonds are of one denomination.
22. ■ First issue.  Alice may issue Alice-coins denominated by a fiat currency.
23. ■ Issue denominated bond.  Alice may issue Alice-bonds denominated by a fiat currency, of a maturity later than the current date.
24. ○ Deposit own coins.  Alice may deposit notes with Carol for Bob, on a condition the three agree, with the consent of Carol, only if the coins and bonds are of one denomination.
25. ○ Deposit with the issuer.  Alice may deposit notes with Carol for Bob, on a condition the three agree, with the consent of Carol, only if the coins and bonds are of one denomination.
26. ○ Deposit more.  Alice may deposit notes with Carol for Bob, on a condition the three agree, with the consent of Carol, only if the coins and bonds are of one denomination.
27. ○ Deposit own bonds.  Alice may deposit notes with Carol for Bob, on a condition the three agree, with the consent of Carol, only if the coins and bonds are of one denomination.
28. ○ Deposit bond with the issuer.  Alice may deposit notes with Carol for Bob, on a condition the three agree, with the consent of Carol, only if the coins and bonds are of one denomination.
29. ○ Deposit bond more.  Alice may deposit notes with Carol for Bob, on a condition the three agree, with the consent of Carol, only if the coins and bonds are of one denomination.
30. ■ Accept.  Alice may accept Dollar-denominated Carol-coins in payment up to a limit of her choosing, if she holds a Dollar-denominated Carol-coin or Carol-bond; each such coin paid to Alice counts against the limit, and Alice may accept again.
31. ■ Accept by bond.  Alice may accept Dollar-denominated Carol-coins in payment up to a limit of her choosing, if she holds a Dollar-denominated Carol-coin or Carol-bond; each such coin paid to Alice counts against the limit, and Alice may accept again.
32. ■ Pay accepted coins.  Alice may pay Bob, whom she knows, coins she holds that Bob accepts.
33. ■ Pay own accepted coins.  Alice may pay Bob, whom she knows, coins she holds that Bob accepts.
34. ■ Pay accepted coins to the issuer.  Alice may pay Bob, whom she knows, coins she holds that Bob accepts.
