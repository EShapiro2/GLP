Denominated Currencies
Denominated Currencies extends Bonds and Denominated Coins, and restricts and strengthens Bonds.
A *Dollar-denominated Alice-bond* is an Alice-bond issued as Alice's debt of one Dollar, due at its maturity date.
Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are parties, Dollar a fiat currency, Day and Due maturity dates, Today a date, Cond a condition Alice, Bob and Carol agree, Sum and Limit numbers; in clause 4 Carol is possibly Alice or Bob.
1. ■ Issue denominated bond: Alice may issue Sum Dollar-denominated Alice-bonds due Day on record, provided the current date is Today and Day is more than Today.
2. ○ Swap: Notes:2. Redeem: Bonds:2. Deposit: Notes:4 Deposit, only if Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in no denomination, and then Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in the denomination Dollar. Deposit own coins: Alice may deposit Sum more Dollar-denominated Alice-coins with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice knows Bob and Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in no denomination. Deposit with the issuer: Alice may deposit Sum more Dollar-denominated Carol-coins with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice knows Bob and Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in no denomination. Deposit more: Alice may deposit Sum more Dollar-denominated Dave-coins with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice knows Bob and Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in the denomination Dollar. Deposit bond: Notes:4 Deposit bond, only if Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in no denomination, and then Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in the denomination Dollar. Deposit own bonds: Alice may deposit Sum more Dollar-denominated Alice-bonds due Day with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice knows Bob and Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in no denomination. Deposit bond with the issuer: Alice may deposit Sum more Dollar-denominated Carol-bonds due Day with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice knows Bob and Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in no denomination. Deposit bond more: Alice may deposit Sum more Dollar-denominated Dave-bonds due Day with Carol for Bob on Cond, with the consent of Carol, provided Alice and Carol know each other, Alice knows Bob and Carol holds the escrow from Alice for Bob on Cond in the denomination Dollar.
3. ■ Accept: Alice may accept Limit more Dollar-denominated Carol-coins in payment, provided Alice holds a Dollar-denominated Carol-coin. Accept by bond: Alice may accept Limit more Dollar-denominated Carol-coins in payment, provided Alice holds a Carol-bond due Day, Carol has issued a Dollar-denominated bond due Due, Day is at most Due and Due is at most Day. Each such coin paid to Alice counts against the limit, and Alice may accept again.
4. ■ Pay accepted coins: Alice may pay Bob a Carol-coin that Bob accepts as denominated by Dollar, provided Alice knows Bob and Carol has issued coins of the denomination Dollar. Pay own accepted coins: Alice may pay Bob an Alice-coin that Bob accepts as denominated by Dollar, provided Alice knows Bob and Alice has issued coins of the denomination Dollar. Pay accepted coins to the issuer: Alice may pay Bob a Bob-coin that Bob accepts as denominated by Dollar, provided Alice knows Bob and Bob has issued coins of the denomination Dollar.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x has issued an f-denominated bond due e = issued_bond_in(x,f,e); x holds an f-denominated y-bond due e = bond(x,y,e,f); x holds n f-denominated y-bonds due e = bond(x,y,e,f,*n); z holds n f-denominated w-bonds due e in escrow from x for y on k = escrow_bond(z,w,e,f,x,y,k,*n); z holds the escrow from x for y on k in the denomination f = escrow_denomination(z,x,y,k,f); x accepts an f-denominated z-coin in payment = accepts(x,z,f); x accepts n f-denominated z-coins in payment = accepts(x,z,f,*n).
Kind of note: bond, Dollar-denominated y-bonds due Day for y-bonds due Day.
After x issues n f-denominated x-bonds due e on record: x holds n f-denominated x-bonds due e and x has issued an f-denominated bond due e.
After x accepts n more f-denominated z-coins in payment: x accepts n f-denominated z-coins in payment.
After x pays y a z-coin that y accepts as denominated by f: no longer x holds an f-denominated z-coin, y holds an f-denominated z-coin and no longer y accepts an f-denominated z-coin in payment.
After x deposits n more f-denominated w-coins with z for y on k: no longer x holds n f-denominated w-coins, z holds n f-denominated w-coins in escrow from x for y on k and z holds the escrow from x for y on k in the denomination f.
After x deposits n more f-denominated w-bonds due e with z for y on k: no longer x holds n f-denominated w-bonds due e, z holds n f-denominated w-bonds due e in escrow from x for y on k and z holds the escrow from x for y on k in the denomination f.
