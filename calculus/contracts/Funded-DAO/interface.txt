Funded DAO
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity once.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ■ Found.  Alice may found a DAO under a new name of hers; Alice is then its steward and first member.
5. ■ Join.  Alice, the steward of a DAO, may admit Bob, whom she knows and who is not yet a member, with the consent of Bob.
6. ■ Propose.  A member of a DAO may make a proposal to it, each proposal once; its steward records it.
7. ■ Propose own.  A member of a DAO may make a proposal to it, each proposal once; its steward records it.
8. ■ Deliver proposal.  The steward delivers it to every member but the steward, the proposer included.
9. ■ Vote for.  A member who holds a proposal of their DAO may vote for or against it, once and before the steward decides it; the steward records the vote.
10. ■ Vote own for.  A member who holds a proposal of their DAO may vote for or against it, once and before the steward decides it; the steward records the vote.
11. ■ Vote against.  A member who holds a proposal of their DAO may vote for or against it, once and before the steward decides it; the steward records the vote.
12. ■ Vote own against.  A member who holds a proposal of their DAO may vote for or against it, once and before the steward decides it; the steward records the vote.
13. ■ Deliver vote for.  A member who holds a proposal of their DAO may vote for or against it, once and before the steward decides it; the steward records the vote.
14. ■ Deliver vote against.  A member who holds a proposal of their DAO may vote for or against it, once and before the steward decides it; the steward records the vote.
15. ■ Pass.  The steward may decide a proposal, passed or rejected, once.
16. ■ Reject.  The steward may decide a proposal, passed or rejected, once.
17. ■ Deliver pass.  The steward may decide a proposal, passed or rejected, once.
18. ■ Deliver reject.  The steward may decide a proposal, passed or rejected, once.
19. ■ Issue.  Alice may issue Alice-notes.
20. ■ Swap.  Alice and Bob, who know each other, may jointly swap notes they hold.
21. ■ Pay.  Alice may pay notes she holds to Bob, whom she knows, with his consent.
22. ■ Pay issuer.  Alice may pay notes she holds to Bob, whom she knows, with his consent.
23. ■ Deposit.  Alice may deposit notes with Carol for Bob, on a condition the three agree, with the consent of Carol.
24. ■ Release.  Carol and Bob may jointly release them to Bob.
25. ■ Return.  Carol and Alice may jointly return them to Alice.
26. ■ Redeem.  Alice may redeem a Bob-coin she holds against any coin held by Bob, whom she knows.
27. ■ Fund.  A member of a DAO may give it coins they hold; its steward keeps them apart as the DAO's.
28. ■ Grant.  The steward may grant the proposer of a passed proposal, once per proposal, any amount of the DAO's coins of one issuer.
