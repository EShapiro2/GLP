Of Knowledge: An *identity* is a unique cryptographic key pair. A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of Friendship: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts.
Of Encounters: A person is *nearby* Alice when her phone reaches theirs by Bluetooth or the local network, without the Internet; a *place* is where Alice is. Environment: x is nearby y.
Of Groups: A *message* is what a person sends. Bob and Carol are possibly Alice or one person.
Of Labelled Overlay: A pointer may be labeled by a person, and a pointer labeled by Dave is a *Dave-pointer*. Friends Alice and Bob have a Bob-pointer from Alice to Bob and an Alice-pointer from Bob to Alice. Erin is possibly any of the others. In clause 2 Carol is possibly Dave.
Of Feeds: A *feed* is named by its author. Bob and Carol are possibly one person.
Of Guardians: Bob *trusts* Wendy, a child of Alice, when he trusts Alice. The *decision rule*, which the creator of a child decides, says who decides for a child and how. A child takes an act of a party by those who decide for the child. In clause 4 Alice and Bob are possibly one person. Bob *trusts* Orga, an organisation of Alice, when he trusts Alice. The *decision rule*, which the creator of an organisation decides, says who decides for an organisation and how. An organisation takes an act of a party by those who decide for the organisation. Bob *trusts* Wendy, a ward of Alice, when he trusts Alice. The *decision rule*, which the creator of a ward decides, says who decides for a ward and how. A ward takes an act of a party by those who decide for the ward.
Of Warded Groups: In clause 1 Wendy is possibly Alice.
Of Children: In clause 2 Alice and Bob are possibly one person.
Of Broadcast: A *broadcast* is a message with a unique identifier, which its maker holds. Δ > 0 is a period of time, and a broadcast is *live* for Δ after it is made. Environment: m is live, for x.
Of Organisation: An *organisation* is a ward with *participants*, which include its *officers*, each with a *role*. The *bylaws*, which its founder decides, name the roles of an organisation and say who decides each of its acts and how, their own amendment included. Environment: the bylaws of z entitle x to propose motions; the bylaws of z entitle x to vote on m; the bylaws of z entitle x to take m; the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z; the bylaws of z entitle x to amend them as m says; the amendment m is decided according to the bylaws of z.
Of Emergencies: A *personal emergency* is a broadcast that includes the location of its maker, a description and the help its maker asks; it ceases to be live when its maker resolves it. A *village emergency* is a broadcast that includes instructions. A *resolution* of a personal emergency and an *answer* to it are broadcasts. Environment: x has no live personal emergency.
Of Notices: A *notice* is a broadcast that includes its text and the area it is for.
Of Notes: A *note* includes its issuer's public key and a unique note identifier, is signed by their private key, and gives its holder a right against its issuer. An *Alice-note* is a note issued by Alice. In clause 2 Carol and Dave are possibly Alice, Bob or one person, in clause 3 Carol is possibly Alice or Bob, and in clause 4 Dave is possibly Alice, Bob or Carol.
Of Coins: A *coin* is a note that is a unit of its issuer's debt. An *Alice-coin* is a coin issued by Alice. Carol is possibly Alice or Bob.
Of Bonds: A *bond* is a note that is a unit of its issuer's debt, due at its maturity date, which it includes. An *Alice-bond* is a bond issued by Alice. A bond is *mature* when its maturity date is at most the current date.
Of Denominated Coins: *Dollar* names one denomination, and a *Dollar-denominated Alice-coin* is an Alice-coin issued as Alice's debt of one Dollar. Kind of note: note, Dollar-denominated y-notes of type t for y-notes of type t.
Of Denominated Currencies: A *Dollar-denominated Alice-bond* is an Alice-bond issued as Alice's debt of one Dollar, due at its maturity date. In clause 4 Carol is possibly Alice or Bob.
Of Bank: A *bank* is an organisation whose business is issuing coins and bonds against the coins and bonds it holds.
Of Community Bank: A *community* is a bank whose coins are Dollar-denominated. The coins it issues are *community coins*. Its bylaws name two roles, *member*, held by every participant, and *treasurer*.
Of Constitution: A *constitutional democratic community* is an organisation whose bylaws name one role, *member*, held by every participant, and are *egalitarian*: the rule by which the members decide an act names no member and no other role. Environment: m keeps the bylaws of z egalitarian.
Of Democratic Community: A *democratic community* is a community that is a constitutional democratic community.
Of Backed Community Bank: *f* is a fraction, 0 ≤ f < 1. Orga's *account* is a bank account in Dollars, held in Orga's name by its treasurers. A treasurer, Alice, is *backed* when the Alice-coins Orga holds are at most the Dollars Alice paid into the account and the coins Alice holds of the other treasurers, leaving out the fraction f of them of whom she holds the most. Environment: every treasurer of z stays backed, for x; x stays backed in z.
Of Disputes: The bylaws of an organisation name the role *arbiter*. A *dispute* is a disagreement between two participants of an organisation about an act of the organisation or an undertaking. A *ruling* is the text of an arbiter's decision on a dispute. A dispute is raised once, to one arbiter, and has one ruling, which both parties receive.
Of Warnings: A *warning* is a record in an organisation, made by one of its officers, that a participant broke its bylaws or an undertaking; it names the incident and the date it is given. An officer holds at most one warning to a participant at a time, and the participant may answer it once; a warning withdrawn or annulled may be given again of the same incident only if it was not answered. A warning stays when the officer who gave it leaves office, until it is withdrawn or annulled. A warning counts toward expelling the participant only from seven days after it is given, so that he has the time to answer it.
Of Works: A *work* names a task, its *price* in community coins, its *contractor*, who will do it, and its *supervisor*, an officer of the community. The bylaws name the role *arbiter*.
Of Mutual Credit: A *mutual credit society* is a bank whose bylaws name one role, *member*, held by every participant. A member is *in the red* by their coins in circulation less the other persons' coins they hold. Environment: every member of z is then in the red by at most 1000 coins, for x.
Of Community Network: A *community network* is created by a backed community, and uses its community coins. Its bylaws name the role *arbiter*. A *provider* is a member of the community whose *stake*, the community coins they hold, is at least *S*, a number of community coins, and a *customer* is a member of the community who accepts community coins. A *dispute* is over community coins locked between a provider and a customer, on *terms* that set the arbiter's fee. In clause 2 Alice is the customer and Bob the provider. Environment: the terms of k set the arbiter's fee at n, for w. Parameters: x has n f-denominated c-coins locked in z on k as the customer of y; y has n f-denominated c-coins locked in z on k as the provider of x.
Of Community Market: A *seller* is a provider and a *buyer* a customer. A *listing* names a good, its price in community coins and how it is delivered; its terms say what is released to whom on cancellation. In clause 1 Carol is a buyer, and in clause 5 Skill is the listing. Environment: the terms of l set the payment at n and the deposit at m, for x; the terms of l release p to the customer and q to the provider on cancellation, for x.
Of Gigs: A *worker* is a provider and a *client* a customer. A *job* names the work, its price in community coins and its deadline; its terms say what is released to whom on cancellation. In clause 1 Carol is a worker, in clause 4 an arbiter, and in clause 6 Skill is the job. Environment: no delivery of j is attested by the deadline, for x; the terms of j set the payment at n and the deposit at m, for x; the terms of j release p to the customer and q to the provider on cancellation, for x.
Of Local Overlay: A person has a *location*. d > 0, an integer, and e > 0 are numbers. Environment: the bound is n, for x; either x points to fewer than d persons and y is closest to x among all of x's unpointed friends, or y is closer to x by more than e than some person pointed to by x; y is closer to x by more than e than some person pointed to by x; y is furthest from x among the persons x points to.
Of Auction: A *pickup delay* is measured from the award. The *terms* of a call state a maximum pickup delay, select a bidder, set the fare and the deposit, and say how these are released. A person can be either a rider or a driver but not both. Environment: x is a rider; x is a driver; the bidding period of x's call c has ended; x is at the pickup point with y; x is at the destination with y; no pickup of x's ride is recorded within the maximum pickup delay; the terms of x's call c select y; the terms of x's call c select no bidder.
Of Second-Score Auction: *Second-score terms* state a maximum pickup delay T ≥ 0, a willingness to pay W(τ) = A − λτ with A, λ ≥ 0, and a deposit D ≥ 0. The award is at time t₀. A bid (p, τ) is the fare p ≥ 0 the bidder asks and the pickup delay τ ≥ 0 the bidder promises. Its *score* is W(τ) − p, and it is *admissible* iff τ ≤ T and its score is at least 0. Environment: y is the bidder on x's call c whose admissible bid has the highest score, ties broken as the terms say; no bid on x's call c is admissible; the terms of x's call c set the deposit D; the terms of x's call c set the fare P = W(τ) − s₂, where τ is the pickup delay of y's bid and s₂ is the highest score among the other admissible bids, or 0 if there is none; the fare and the deposit are released to x if x was at the pickup point from t₀ to t₀ + T, and otherwise the fare to x and the deposit to y; the refund min{P, λδ} is released to x, and the rest of the fare and the deposit to y, with δ = max{0, t_pickup − t₀ − τ}.
Of Rides: A *driver* is a provider and a *rider* a customer. Environment: the fare and the deposit of x's call c are n and m z-coins; the terms of c release p to x and q to y on arrival; the terms of c release p to x and q to y on cancellation.
Of Lodging: A *host* is a provider and a *traveller* a customer. A *listing* names a place, the nights offered and the price a night in community coins; its terms say what is released to whom on cancellation. In clause 1 Carol is a traveller, in clause 5 an arbiter and Deal is the listing, and in clause 6 Skill is the listing. Environment: x is at the place of l on the first night; x leaves the place of l after the last night; it is before the first night of l, for x; no check-in at l is recorded on the first night while x is at the place; the terms of l set the payment at n and the deposit at m, for x; the terms of l release p to the customer and q to the provider on cancellation, for x.
Of Ads: An *advertiser* is a member of the community who posts ads. A member's *profile* is held by the member and by no one else. An *ad* names the profile it is for, the community coins its advertiser pays for a view and for a click, the most one member may earn from it, and its *budget*, the most it pays in all. Environment: d is for the profile of x.
Of Gossip: *Posts* have a *budget*: what a post has when made, and the *cost* of a forward. Gossip has a *gossip protocol*: to which of their friends a holder may forward a post, and with what part of the rest of the budget. A person *holds* any post they made or forwarded to them. Environment: n is a budget of a new post, for x; n covers the cost of a forward leaving m, for x; the gossip protocol forwards p to y with m of n, for x.
Of Matching: Posts have a *test*, whether two *match*. Environment: p and q match, for x.
Of Services: A *service* is a ward operated by a machine at an address every person can reach.
Of Rendezvous: An *invitation link* of Alice's is a term she signs, naming her, a rendezvous server, a serial and an expiry.
Of Anonymous Rendezvous: A *one-time key* is a key pair its maker uses for one meeting. Environment: x holds the one-time key k; x knows the public key of k.
Of Anonymous Matching: Each post carries the public key of a one-time key of its maker, and names a rendezvous server. Environment: p carries the public key of k, for x; d is named by the one of p and q whose public key has the smaller hash, for x.
Of Free Matching: The budget of a post is an integer, the cost of a forward is one, and the gossip protocol is forwarding to any friend with the budget less one.
Of Jobs: A post is a *vacancy*, stating the work and whom it seeks, or a *candidacy*, stating who its maker is and the work they seek. A vacancy and a candidacy match when each states what the other seeks.
Of Help: A post is a *service*, stating work its maker does, or a *need*, stating work its maker seeks done. A service and a need match when the service is the work the need seeks.
Of Lending: A post is a *loan* or a *gift*, stating a thing its maker lends or gives away, or a *wish*, stating a thing its maker seeks to borrow or receive. A loan or a gift matches a wish when it is of the thing the wish seeks.
Of Lost and Found: A post is a *loss*, stating a thing its maker lost, or a *find*, stating a thing its maker found. A loss and a find match when they are of the same thing.
Of Carpool: A post is a *commute*, stating a route, its times, and whether its maker drives, rides or either. Two commutes match when their routes and times agree and one maker drives and the other rides.
Of Home Exchange: A post is a *home*, stating a dwelling, when it is free, and where and when its maker seeks one. Two homes match when each is where and when the other's maker seeks.
Of Partners: A post is a *plan*, stating an activity, its place and its time, and whom its maker seeks for it. Two plans match when they are of the same activity, place and time and each maker is whom the other seeks.
Of Dating: A post is a *profile*, stating who its maker is and whom they seek. Two profiles match when each states whom the other seeks.
Of Care: A *circle* is a group for the care of a person, its *patient*. A *visit* is a time at which the patient needs a person with them. Bob and Carol are possibly Alice.
Of Booking: An organisation has *resources*, each with *slots*. A slot is *free* when it is not booked. A booking is *outstanding* until its slot has passed or it is cancelled. The bylaws name the *bound*, the number of outstanding bookings a participant may have. Environment: z has the slot s of the resource r; x has fewer outstanding bookings in z than the bound. An organisation has *courts*, each with *slots*. A slot is *free* when it is not booked. A booking is *outstanding* until its slot has passed or it is cancelled. The bylaws name the *bound*, the number of outstanding bookings a participant may have. Environment: z has the slot s of the court r; x has fewer outstanding bookings in z than the bound. An organisation has *rooms*, each with *slots*. A slot is *free* when it is not booked. A booking is *outstanding* until its slot has passed or it is cancelled. The bylaws name the *bound*, the number of outstanding bookings a participant may have. Environment: z has the slot s of the room r; x has fewer outstanding bookings in z than the bound.
Of Meeting Rooms: A resource is a *room*.
Of Appointments: A resource is the time of a *provider*, an officer of the organisation.
Of Tennis Courts: The activity is tennis, the place is a *club*, an organisation that has a fixed number of courts, and the time is a slot. A booking is of the one who books and of a *partner*, and counts among the outstanding bookings of each.
Of Rehearsal Rooms: The activity is music, the place is a *studio*, an organisation that has rehearsal rooms, and the time is a slot.
Of Tools: A resource is a *tool*, a thing that the organisation owns and its participants use. A tool is *withdrawn* while it cannot be used.
