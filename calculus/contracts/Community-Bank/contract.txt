Community Bank
Community Bank instantiates Bank with community bank bylaws and restricts and extends it.
A *community bank* is a bank whose bylaws name two roles, *member*, held by every participant, and *director*, held by its first officer and by members. A member's *credit line* is a number of coins.
Bob is a person, Orga a community bank.
1. ■ Leave: Organisation:3, only if the Orga-coins Bob holds and the Bob-coins Orga holds have been redeemed against each other.
2. ■ Set credit line: The directors may set Bob's credit line.
3. ■ Credit: Bob may swap Bob-coins he holds for Orga-coins, provided the Bob-coins Orga holds do not exceed his credit line.
4. ○ Community currency. A member will accept Orga-coins in payment for their offerings at the prices they post.
