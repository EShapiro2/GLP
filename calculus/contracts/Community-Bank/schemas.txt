Community Bank
Community Bank instantiates Bank with community bank bylaws, and restricts and extends it.
Vocabulary: z has y's credit line at the number n = credit_line(z,y,n).
Roles: the directors of z = directors(z), listed by z has y in the role Director and y holds the role Director in z.
x is a director of z: x holds the role Director in z.
y's earlier credit line in z has ended: z has y's credit line at no number.
After g sets y's credit line in z at n: y's earlier credit line in z has ended and z has y's credit line at the number n.
After y swaps n y-coins for n z-coins: no longer y holds n y-coins, z holds n y-coins, no longer z holds n z-coins and y holds n z-coins.
