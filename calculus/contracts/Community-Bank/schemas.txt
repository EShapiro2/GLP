Community Bank
Community Bank extends Bank.
x is a member of z: x is a participant of z.
x is a treasurer of z: x holds the role Treasurer in z.
After x swaps n x-coins for n z-coins: no longer x holds n x-coins, z holds n x-coins, no longer z holds n z-coins and x holds n z-coins.
Community Bank:0 - | A *community* is a bank whose coins are Dollar-denominated. The coins it issues are *community coins*. Its bylaws name two roles, *member*, held by every participant, and *treasurer*.
Community Bank:0 - | Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a community, Sum a number.
Community Bank:1 ■ Draw: ~note(Alice,Alice,coin,*Sum), note(Orga,Alice,coin,*Sum), ~note(Orga,Orga,coin,*Sum), note(Alice,Orga,coin,*Sum) <- role(Alice,Orga,Treasurer), note(Alice,Alice,coin,*Sum), note(Orga,Orga,coin,*Sum) guarded by Alice.
Community Bank:2 ○ | Fiat redemption.
Community Bank:2 ○ | Any treasurer will redeem an Orga-coin Bob holds against one Dollar.
