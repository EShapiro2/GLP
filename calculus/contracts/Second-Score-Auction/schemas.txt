Second-Score Auction
Second-Score Auction weakens Auction.
Vocabulary: y is the bidder on x's call c whose admissible bid has the highest score, ties broken as the terms say = highest_score(x,y,c); no bid on x's call c is admissible = none_admissible(x,c); the terms of x's call c set the deposit D = sets_deposit(x,c); the terms of x's call c set the fare P = W(τ) − s₂, where τ is the pickup delay of y's bid and s₂ is the highest score among the other admissible bids, or 0 if there is none = second_score_fare(x,y,c); the refund min{P, λδ} is released to x, and the rest of the fare and the deposit to y, with δ = max{0, t_pickup − t₀ − τ} = refund(x,y); the fare and the deposit are released to x if x was at the pickup point from t₀ to t₀ + T, and otherwise the fare to x and the deposit to y = cancel_release(x,y).
Second-Score Auction:0 - | *Second-score terms* state a maximum pickup delay T ≥ 0, a willingness to pay W(τ) = A − λτ with A, λ ≥ 0, and a deposit D ≥ 0. The award is at time t₀. A bid (p, τ) is the fare p ≥ 0 the bidder asks and the pickup delay τ ≥ 0 the bidder promises. Its *score* is W(τ) − p, and it is *admissible* iff τ ≤ T and its score is at least 0.
