Resilient Feeds
Resilient Feeds extends Feeds.
d > 1, an integer, is a parameter of Resilient Feeds.
Alice, Bob, Carol, Dave and Erin are persons, Dave and Erin are possibly any of the others.
1. ■ Redundant point: Alice adds a Carol-pointer to Bob, provided Alice has a Carol-pointer to Dave, Alice has a Carol-pointer to fewer than d persons, Alice and Bob are friends and Bob has a Carol-pointer to Erin.
