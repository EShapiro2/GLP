Resilient Feeds
Resilient Feeds extends Feeds.
Vocabulary: y has been pointed to by x with a w-pointer = pointed(y,x,w).
After x adds a redundant w-pointer to y, which requires x does not have a w-pointer to y and y has not been pointed to by x with a w-pointer: x has a w-pointer to y and y has been pointed to by x with a w-pointer.
