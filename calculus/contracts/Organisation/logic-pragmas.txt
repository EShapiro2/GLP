Organisation
Organisation instantiates Guardians with bylaws, an organisation for a ward and an officer for a guardian, and restricts, strengthens and extends it (GSC, From Grassroots Contract to Grassroots App, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06231).
An *organisation* is a ward with *participants*, which include its *officers*, each with a *role*. The *bylaws*, which its founder decides, name the roles of an organisation and say who decides each of its acts and how, their own amendment included.
Alice and Bob are persons, Gov and Orga organisations, Motion an act of Orga, Role a role, Votes and Double numbers.
1. ■ Found: Guardians:1, with the consent of Gov, and then Orga has Alice as its first officer, Alice holds the role Role in Orga, Orga has Alice in the role Role, Alice is a participant of Orga, Orga has Alice as a participant and Gov is the founder of Orga.
2. ■ Acts of the organisation. Propose: Bob may propose Motion in Orga, provided Bob is entitled to propose Motion in Orga. Vote: Bob may vote on Motion in Orga, provided Bob is entitled to vote on Motion in Orga, Orga is an organisation and Orga has Motion proposed. Act: Alice may take Motion for Orga, provided Alice is entitled to take Motion for Orga and the votes decide Motion according to the bylaws of Orga.
3. ■ Participation. Join: Bob may become a participant of Orga, with the consent of those who admit participants of Orga. Leave: Bob may end his participation in Orga, provided Bob is not an officer of Orga. Officer leave: Bob may end his participation and his office in Orga. Expel: Those who expel participants of Orga may end Bob's participation in Orga, provided Bob is not an officer of Orga. Officer expel: Those who expel participants of Orga may end Bob's participation and Bob's office in Orga. Every role Bob holds in Orga ends with his participation.
4. ■ Officers. Appoint: Guardians:2 Make guardian, only if Bob is a participant of Orga, and then Bob holds the role Role in Orga and Orga has Bob in the role Role. The decision names his role. Resign: Guardians:2 Resign, and then Bob holds no role in Orga and Orga has Bob in no role. End officer: Guardians:2 End guardian, and then Bob holds no role in Orga and Orga has Bob in no role. Officer unfriend: Guardians:2 Guardian unfriend, and then Bob holds no role in Orga and Orga has Bob in no role. Retire: Guardians:5, and then no one holds any role in Orga and Orga has no one in any role.
5. ■ Amend: Alice may amend the bylaws of Orga as Motion says, provided Alice is entitled to amend the bylaws of Orga as Motion says and the amendment Motion is decided according to the bylaws of Orga.
6. ○ Accountability. Orga will be accountable at law for its undertakings where it is a juridical person, and every officer who decided an act of Orga will be accountable for it with Orga.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x is the founder of z = founder(x,z); z has y as its first officer = first_officer(z,y); y holds the role r in z = role(y,z,r); z has y in the role r = has_role(z,y,r); x is a participant of z = participant(x,z); z has x as a participant = has_participant(z,x); x is entitled to propose m in z = proposer(x,z,m); z has m proposed = proposed(z,m); x is entitled to vote on m in z = voter(x,z,m); z holds x's vote on m = vote(z,x,m); the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z = decided(z,m); x is entitled to take m for z = taker(x,z,m); z has m taken = taken(z,m); x is entitled to amend the bylaws of z as m says = amender(x,z,m); the amendment m is decided according to the bylaws of z = amendment_decided(z,m); z has its bylaws amended = amended(z).
Roles: those who decide m for z = act_deciders(z,m); those who admit participants of z = admitters(z); those who expel participants of z = expellers(z).
After x proposes m in z, which requires z does not have m proposed: z has m proposed.
After x votes on m in z, which requires z does not hold x's vote on m: z holds x's vote on m.
After x takes m for z, which requires z does not have m taken: z has m taken.
After y becomes a participant of z, which requires y is not a participant of z: y is a participant of z and z has y as a participant.
After y ends y's participation in z: no longer y is a participant of z and no longer z has y as a participant.
After g ends y's participation in z: no longer y is a participant of z and no longer z has y as a participant.
After y ends y's participation and y's office in z: no longer y is a participant of z, no longer z has y as a participant, no longer y is an officer of z, no longer z has y as an officer, y holds no role in z and z has y in no role.
After g ends y's participation and y's office in z: no longer y is a participant of z, no longer z has y as a participant, no longer y is an officer of z, no longer z has y as an officer, y holds no role in z and z has y in no role.
After x amends the bylaws of z as m says, which requires z does not have m taken: z has its bylaws amended and z has m taken.
Environment: x is entitled to propose m in z; x is entitled to vote on m in z; x is entitled to take m for z; the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z; x is entitled to amend the bylaws of z as m says; the amendment m is decided according to the bylaws of z.
