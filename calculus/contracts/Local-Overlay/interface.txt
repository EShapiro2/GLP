1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity once.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ○ Befriend.  Alice and Bob, who trust each other and are not friends, may jointly become friends.
5. ■ Unfriend.  Alice may end her friendship with Bob.
6. ■ Point friend.  Alice, who does not point to her friend Bob, may point to Bob, only if either Alice points to fewer than d persons and Bob is closest to Alice among all of Alice's unpointed friends, or Bob is closer to Alice by more than e than some person pointed to by Alice.
7. ■ Point transitive.  Alice, who knows Carol and points to Bob who points to Carol, may point to Carol, only if Carol is closer to Alice by more than e than some person pointed to by Alice.
8. ■ Unpoint.  Alice, who points to Bob, may cease pointing to Bob, only if Alice points to more than d persons, with Bob furthest from Alice among them.
