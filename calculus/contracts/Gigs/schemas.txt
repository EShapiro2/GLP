Gigs
Gigs unites Community Network, Overlay and Recommendations, and extends and weakens the union (GRH, Grassroots Ride-Hailing, in preparation).
Vocabulary: x has j posted in z = posted(x,j,z); y has received j of x in z = received(y,j,x,z); x has engaged y for j = engaged(x,y,j); y is engaged by x for j = engaged_by(y,x,j); y has attested the delivery of j to x = delivered(y,j,x); x has accepted j of y = accepted(x,j,y); x has rejected j of y = rejected(x,j,y); no delivery of j is attested by the deadline, for x = lapsed(x,j); the terms of j set the payment at n and the deposit at m, for x = terms(x,j,n,m); the terms of j release p to the customer and q to the provider on cancellation, for x = cancel_terms(x,j,p,q).
y is a worker of z: y is a provider of z.
x is a client of z: x is a customer of z.
x and y have an engagement for j: x has engaged y for j and y is engaged by x for j.
After x posts j in z, which requires x does not have j posted in z: x has j posted in z.
After j of x goes to y in z, which requires y has not received j of x in z: y has received j of x in z.
After y passes j of x to w in z, which requires w has not received j of x in z: w has received j of x in z.
After x withdraws j from z: no longer x has j posted in z.
After x engages y for j in z locking n and m c-coins, which requires x and y do not have an engagement for j: x and y have an engagement for j, no longer x holds n c-coins, no longer y holds m c-coins and n and m c-coins are locked in z on j between the provider y and the customer x.
After y attests the delivery of j to x, which requires y has not attested the delivery of j to x: y has attested the delivery of j to x.
After x accepts j of y in z releasing to y the n and m c-coins locked: x has accepted j of y, no longer x and y have an engagement for j, no longer n and m c-coins are locked in z on j between the provider y and the customer x, y holds n c-coins and y holds m c-coins.
After x rejects j of y raising to w the dispute in z, which requires x has not rejected j of y and w does not hold the dispute of x and y over j in z: x has rejected j of y and w holds the dispute of x and y over j in z.
After x cancels x's engagement of y for j in z releasing p and q of the n and m c-coins locked: no longer x and y have an engagement for j, no longer n and m c-coins are locked in z on j between the provider y and the customer x, x holds p c-coins and y holds q c-coins.
After x cancels x's engagement of y for j in z taking back the n and m c-coins locked: no longer x and y have an engagement for j, no longer n and m c-coins are locked in z on j between the provider y and the customer x, x holds n c-coins and x holds m c-coins.
After y cancels y's engagement by x for j in z releasing p and q of the n and m c-coins locked: no longer x and y have an engagement for j, no longer n and m c-coins are locked in z on j between the provider y and the customer x, x holds p c-coins and y holds q c-coins.
After x cancels x's engagement of y for j in z releasing to y the n and m c-coins locked: no longer x and y have an engagement for j, no longer n and m c-coins are locked in z on j between the provider y and the customer x, y holds n c-coins and y holds m c-coins.
After y cancels y's engagement by x for j in z releasing to x the n and m c-coins locked: no longer x and y have an engagement for j, no longer n and m c-coins are locked in z on j between the provider y and the customer x, x holds n c-coins and x holds m c-coins.
After y cancels y's engagement by x for j in z taking back the n and m c-coins locked: no longer x and y have an engagement for j, no longer n and m c-coins are locked in z on j between the provider y and the customer x, y holds n c-coins and y holds m c-coins.
Gigs:0 - | A *worker* is a provider and a *client* a customer. A *job* names the work, its price in community coins and its deadline; its terms say what is released to whom on cancellation.
Gigs:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Orga a community network, Gov an organisation, Kept, Sum, Total, Top, Pot, Back and Rest numbers, Job a job, Skill a skill or a service; in clause 1 Carol is a worker, in clause 4 an arbiter, and in clause 6 Skill is the job.
