Gigs
Gigs extends Networked Organisation and Overlay (GRH, Grassroots Ride-Hailing, in preparation) and restricts Recommendations.
Vocabulary: x has j posted in z = posted(x,j,z); y has received j of x in z = received(y,j,x,z); x has engaged y for j = engaged(x,y,j); y is engaged by x for j = engaged_by(y,x,j); y has attested the delivery of j to x = delivered(y,j,x); x has accepted j of y = accepted(x,j,y); x has rejected j of y = rejected(x,j,y); no delivery of j is attested by the deadline, for x = lapsed(x,j).
y is a worker of z: y is a provider of z.
x is a client of z: x is a customer of z.
x and y have an engagement for j: x has engaged y for j and y is engaged by x for j.
After x posts j in z, which requires x does not have j posted in z: x has j posted in z.
After j of x goes to y in z, which requires y has not received j of x in z: y has received j of x in z.
After y passes j of x to w in z, which requires w has not received j of x in z: w has received j of x in z.
After x withdraws j from z: no longer x has j posted in z.
After x engages y for j in z, which requires x and y do not have an engagement for j: x and y have an engagement for j and z-coins are locked between the provider y and the customer x.
After y attests the delivery of j to x, which requires y has not attested the delivery of j to x: y has attested the delivery of j to x.
After x accepts j of y in z: x has accepted j of y, no longer x and y have an engagement for j and no longer z-coins are locked between the provider y and the customer x.
After x rejects j of y raising to w the dispute over z-coins, which requires x has not rejected j of y and w does not hold the dispute of x and y over z-coins: x has rejected j of y and w holds the dispute of x and y over z-coins.
After x cancels x's engagement of y for j in z: no longer x and y have an engagement for j and no longer z-coins are locked between the provider y and the customer x.
After y cancels y's engagement by x for j in z: no longer x and y have an engagement for j and no longer z-coins are locked between the provider y and the customer x.
Gigs:0 - | A *worker* is a provider and a *client* a customer. A *job* names the work, its price in Orga-coins and its deadline; its terms say what is released to whom on cancellation.
Gigs:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Orga a networked organisation, Kept a number, Job a job, Skill a skill or a service; in clause 1 Carol is a worker, in clause 4 an arbiter, and in clause 6 Skill is the job.
Gigs:0 - | Environment: no delivery of j is attested by the deadline, for x.
Gigs:1 ■ | Job.
Gigs:1 ■ Post: posted(Alice,Job,Orga) <- accepts(Alice,Orga,Dollar), ~posted(Alice,Job,Orga) guarded by Alice.
Gigs:1 ■ | Alice posts the job to the workers she points to.
Gigs:1 ■ Send job: received(Bob,Job,Alice,Orga) <- posted(Alice,Job,Orga), points_to(Alice,Bob), stake(Orga,S), coin(Bob,Orga,*Held), ge(Held,S), ~received(Bob,Job,Alice,Orga).
Gigs:1 ■ Pass: received(Carol,Job,Alice,Orga) <- stake(Orga,S), coin(Bob,Orga,*Held), ge(Held,S), received(Bob,Job,Alice,Orga), points_to(Bob,Carol), stake(Orga,S), coin(Carol,Orga,*Kept), ge(Kept,S), ~received(Carol,Job,Alice,Orga) guarded by Bob.
Gigs:1 ■ Withdraw job: ~posted(Alice,Job,Orga) <- posted(Alice,Job,Orga) guarded by Alice.
Gigs:2 ■ Engage: engaged(Alice,Bob,Job), engaged_by(Bob,Alice,Job), provider_lock(Bob,Alice,Orga), customer_lock(Alice,Bob,Orga) <- stake(Orga,S), coin(Bob,Orga,*Held), ge(Held,S), posted(Alice,Job,Orga), knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice), ~engaged(Alice,Bob,Job), ~engaged_by(Bob,Alice,Job) guarded by Alice, Bob.
Gigs:2 ■ | Alice's payment and the deposit the terms set for Bob, on top of his stake, are then locked.
Gigs:3 ◆ Deliver: delivered(Bob,Job,Alice) <- engaged(Alice,Bob,Job), engaged_by(Bob,Alice,Job), ~delivered(Bob,Job,Alice) guarded by Bob.
Gigs:4 ■ | Acceptance.
Gigs:4 ■ Accept work: accepted(Alice,Job,Bob), ~engaged(Alice,Bob,Job), ~engaged_by(Bob,Alice,Job), ~provider_lock(Bob,Alice,Orga), ~customer_lock(Alice,Bob,Orga) <- delivered(Bob,Job,Alice), engaged(Alice,Bob,Job), engaged_by(Bob,Alice,Job), provider_lock(Bob,Alice,Orga), customer_lock(Alice,Bob,Orga) guarded by Alice.
Gigs:4 ■ | The payment and the deposit are then released to Bob.
Gigs:4 ■ Reject: rejected(Alice,Job,Bob), dispute(Carol,Alice,Bob,Orga) <- delivered(Bob,Job,Alice), provider_lock(Bob,Alice,Orga), customer_lock(Alice,Bob,Orga), role(Carol,Orga,Arbiter), has_role(Orga,Carol,Arbiter), ~rejected(Alice,Job,Bob), ~dispute(Carol,Alice,Bob,Orga) guarded by Alice.
Gigs:4 ■ | Alice raises the dispute by Networked Organisation:2.
Gigs:5 ■ | Cancelling.
Gigs:5 ■ Cancel: ~engaged(Alice,Bob,Job), ~engaged_by(Bob,Alice,Job), ~provider_lock(Bob,Alice,Orga), ~customer_lock(Alice,Bob,Orga) <- ~delivered(Bob,Job,Alice), engaged(Alice,Bob,Job), engaged_by(Bob,Alice,Job), provider_lock(Bob,Alice,Orga), customer_lock(Alice,Bob,Orga) guarded by Alice.
Gigs:5 ■ Worker cancel: ~engaged(Alice,Bob,Job), ~engaged_by(Bob,Alice,Job), ~provider_lock(Bob,Alice,Orga), ~customer_lock(Alice,Bob,Orga) <- ~delivered(Bob,Job,Alice), engaged(Alice,Bob,Job), engaged_by(Bob,Alice,Job), provider_lock(Bob,Alice,Orga), customer_lock(Alice,Bob,Orga) guarded by Bob.
Gigs:5 ■ | The payment and the deposit are then released to Alice and to Bob as the terms say.
Gigs:5 ■ Lapse: ~engaged(Alice,Bob,Job), ~engaged_by(Bob,Alice,Job), ~provider_lock(Bob,Alice,Orga), ~customer_lock(Alice,Bob,Orga) <- lapsed(Alice,Job), engaged(Alice,Bob,Job), engaged_by(Bob,Alice,Job), provider_lock(Bob,Alice,Orga), customer_lock(Alice,Bob,Orga) guarded by Alice.
Gigs:5 ■ | The payment and the deposit are then both released to Alice.
