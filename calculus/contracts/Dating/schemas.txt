Dating
Dating instantiates Matching with a test of the match of profiles, and restricts it.
x has no profile: x has made the post no one.
Dating:0 - | A post is a *profile*, stating who its maker is and whom they seek. Two profiles match when each states whom the other seeks.
Dating:0 - | Alice is a person.
Dating:1 ■ Post: Matching:1 <- ~made(Alice,_).
