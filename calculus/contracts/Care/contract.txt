Care
Care inherits from Groups.
A *circle* is a group for the care of a person, its *patient*. A *visit* is a time at which the patient needs a person with them.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
1. ◆ Open: Alice may create a circle (Groups:2) for her own care, or for the care of Carol, attesting that Carol asked her to.
2. ■ Ask: A member of a circle may post a visit to it.
3. ○ Take: Bob, a member of the circle, may take a posted visit. Bob will make the visit, or release it in time for another member to take it.
4. ■ Release: Bob, the leader or the patient may release a visit Bob took; it is then posted again.
5. ◆ Report. Made: Bob may attest to the circle that he made a visit he took. Missed: The leader or the patient may attest to the circle that a visit taken was missed.
6. ■ End: The patient may close a circle for their care.
