Care
Care extends Groups.
A *circle* is a group for the care of a person, its *patient*. A *visit* is a time at which the patient needs a person with them.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Grupo a group, Visit a visit, Bob and Carol possibly Alice.
1. ◆ Open: Alice may open Grupo for the care of Carol, provided Alice knows Carol.
2. ■ Ask: Bob may post Visit in Alice's Grupo, provided Bob is a member of the group Grupo of Alice and Alice has Grupo as a circle.
3. ○ Take: Bob may take Visit in Alice's Grupo, provided Bob is a member of the group Grupo of Alice and Alice has the visit Visit posted in Grupo. Bob will make the visit, or release it in time for another member to take it.
4. ■ Release: Bob may release Visit in Alice's Grupo, provided Alice has the visit Visit taken by Bob in Grupo. Release for: Alice may release Visit of Bob in Grupo, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo and Alice has the visit Visit taken by Bob in Grupo. Release by the patient: Carol may release Visit of Bob in the circle Grupo of Alice, provided Alice has Grupo as a circle for Carol and Alice has the visit Visit taken by Bob in Grupo.
5. ◆ Report. Made: Bob may attest Visit in Alice's Grupo made, provided Alice has the visit Visit taken by Bob in Grupo. Missed: Alice may attest Visit of Bob in Grupo missed, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo and Alice has the visit Visit taken by Bob in Grupo. Missed by the patient: Carol may attest Visit of Bob in the circle Grupo of Alice missed, provided Alice has Grupo as a circle for Carol and Alice has the visit Visit taken by Bob in Grupo. Contest: Alice may contest Visit of Bob in Grupo, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo and Alice has the visit Visit made by Bob in Grupo.
6. ■ End: Carol may end the circle Grupo of Alice, provided Alice has Grupo as a circle for Carol.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x has g as a circle = circle(x,g); x has g as a circle for z = circle_for(x,g,z); x has the visit v posted in g = visit_posted(x,v,g); x has the visit v taken by y in g = visit_taken(x,v,y,g); x has the visit v made by y in g = visit_made(x,v,y,g); x has the visit v missed by y in g = visit_missed(x,v,y,g).
After x opens g for the care of z, which requires x is not the leader of g: x is the leader of g, x is a member of the group g of x, x has x as a member of the group g, x has g as a circle and x has g as a circle for z.
After y posts v in x's g, which requires x does not have the visit v posted in g: x has the visit v posted in g.
After y takes v in x's g: no longer x has the visit v posted in g and x has the visit v taken by y in g.
After y releases v in x's g: no longer x has the visit v taken by y in g and x has the visit v posted in g.
After x releases v of y in g: no longer x has the visit v taken by y in g and x has the visit v posted in g.
After y attests v in x's g made: no longer x has the visit v taken by y in g and x has the visit v made by y in g.
After x attests v of y in g missed: no longer x has the visit v taken by y in g and x has the visit v missed by y in g.
After x contests v of y in g, which requires x does not have the visit v missed by y in g: x has the visit v missed by y in g.
After z releases v of y in the circle g of x: no longer x has the visit v taken by y in g and x has the visit v posted in g.
After z attests v of y in the circle g of x missed: no longer x has the visit v taken by y in g and x has the visit v missed by y in g.
After z ends the circle g of x: no longer x is the leader of g, x has no one as a member of the group g, no one is a member of the group g of x, no longer x has g as a circle and no longer x has g as a circle for z.
