Disputes
Disputes extends Organisation.
Vocabulary: z has d between x and y raised to w = raised(z,d,x,y,w); z has w's decision on d between x and y with the ruling r = decision(z,w,d,x,y,r); w has d between x and y to arbitrate in z = case(w,z,d,x,y); z has a case of d = dispute(z,d); x has the ruling r on d in z = ruling(x,z,d,r).
x is an arbiter of z: x holds the role Arbiter in z.
After x raises d with y in z to w, which requires z does not have a case of d: z has a case of d, z has d between x and y raised to w and w has d between x and y to arbitrate in z.
After w decides d between x and y in z with the ruling r: no longer z has d between x and y raised to w, no longer w has d between x and y to arbitrate in z, z has w's decision on d between x and y with the ruling r, x has the ruling r on d in z and y has the ruling r on d in z.
Disputes:0 - | The bylaws of an organisation name the role *arbiter*. A *dispute* is a disagreement between two participants of an organisation about an act of the organisation or an undertaking. A *ruling* is the text of an arbiter's decision on a dispute. A dispute is raised once, to one arbiter, and has one ruling, which both parties receive.
Disputes:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Orga an organisation, Issue a dispute, Ruling a ruling.
Disputes:1 ■ Raise: dispute(Orga,Issue), raised(Orga,Issue,Alice,Bob,Carol), case(Carol,Orga,Issue,Alice,Bob) <- has_participant(Orga,Alice), has_participant(Orga,Bob), has_role(Orga,Carol,Arbiter), ~dispute(Orga,Issue) guarded by Alice.
Disputes:2 ■ Decide: ~raised(Orga,Issue,Alice,Bob,Carol), ~case(Carol,Orga,Issue,Alice,Bob), decision(Orga,Carol,Issue,Alice,Bob,Ruling), ruling(Alice,Orga,Issue,Ruling), ruling(Bob,Orga,Issue,Ruling) <- raised(Orga,Issue,Alice,Bob,Carol), case(Carol,Orga,Issue,Alice,Bob) guarded by Carol.
Disputes:3 ○ | Abide.
Disputes:3 ○ | Alice and Bob will abide by the decision of Carol.
