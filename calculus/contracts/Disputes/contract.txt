Disputes
Disputes inherits from Organisation.
The bylaws of an organisation name the role *arbiter*. A *dispute* is a disagreement between two participants of an organisation about an act of the organisation or an undertaking. A *ruling* is the text of an arbiter's decision on a dispute. A dispute is raised once, to one arbiter, and has one ruling, which both parties receive.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Orga an organisation, Issue a dispute, Ruling a ruling.
1. ■ Raise: Alice may raise Issue with Bob in Orga to Carol, provided Orga has Alice as a participant, Orga has Bob as a participant and Orga has Carol in the role Arbiter.
2. ■ Decide: Carol may decide Issue between Alice and Bob in Orga with the ruling Ruling.
3. ○ Abide. Alice and Bob will abide by the decision of Carol.
