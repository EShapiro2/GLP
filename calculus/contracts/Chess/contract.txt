Chess
Chess inherits from Friendship.
The *rules of chess* are the Laws of Chess of FIDE, the International Chess Federation. A *position* is the board with the side to move, the rights to castle and to capture en passant, the positions since the last capture or pawn move and the number of moves made, so that it shows a draw by repetition or by the fifty-move or seventy-five-move rule. The *time limit* of a game is the number of days a player has for each move.
Alice and Bob are persons.
1. ■ Start. Start: Alice and Bob may jointly start a game of chess with a time limit, provided they are friends and neither has started a game of that name; Alice then plays white and Bob black, from the first position. Start as black: Alice and Bob may jointly start a game of chess with a time limit, provided they are friends and neither has started a game of that name; Alice then plays black and Bob white, from the first position.
2. ■ Move. Move: Alice may move in a game against Bob, provided it is her turn, the move is legal by the rules of chess and the position after it does not end the game drawn; the game is then at the position after the move, from the date of the move. Drawing move: Alice may move in a game against Bob, provided it is her turn, the move is legal by the rules of chess and the position after it ends the game drawn by the rules of chess, by stalemate, a dead position, the position the fifth time or the seventy-five-move rule; the game is then drawn.
3. ■ Win: Alice may claim a game against Bob, provided Bob is to move and is checkmated; Alice has then won the game.
4. ■ Time: Alice may claim a game against Bob on time, provided Bob is to move and the time limit has passed since the last move; Alice has then won the game.
5. ■ Resign: Alice may resign a game against Bob; Bob has then won the game.
6. ■ Draw. Draw: Alice and Bob may jointly agree a draw in a game, provided each has made a move in it. Claim draw: Alice may claim a draw in a game against Bob, provided it is her turn and the position may be claimed drawn by the rules of chess, by the position the third time or the fifty-move rule; the game is then drawn.
7. ○ Fair play. Alice will choose each of her moves herself, with no help from a chess engine.
