Auction
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity once.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ○ Befriend.  Alice and Bob, who trust each other and are not friends, may jointly become friends.
5. ■ Unfriend.  Alice may end her friendship with Bob.
6. ■ Point friend.  Alice, who does not point to her friend Bob, may point to Bob, only if either Alice points to fewer than d persons and Bob is closest to Alice among all of Alice's unpointed friends, or Bob is closer to Alice by more than e than some person pointed to by Alice.
7. ■ Point transitive.  Alice, who knows Carol and points to Bob who points to Carol, may point to Carol, only if Carol is closer to Alice by more than e than some person pointed to by Alice.
8. ■ Unpoint.  Alice, who points to Bob, may cease pointing to Bob, only if Alice points to more than d persons, with Bob furthest from Alice among them.
9. ■ Call.  Alice, who has no open call and no ride, may call for bids on a ride from the persons Alice points to, stating the pickup point, the destination, the bidding period and the terms.
10. ■ Send call.  Alice, who has no open call and no ride, may call for bids on a ride from the persons Alice points to, stating the pickup point, the destination, the bidding period and the terms.
11. ■ Forward call.  Each of them forwards the call to the persons they point to.
12. ■ Bid.  Bob, who received the call, may bid a fare and a pickup delay before the bidding period ends; Alice may not open a bid before then.
13. ■ Award.  After the bidding period ends, Alice may award the ride to the bidder the terms select among the bidders who have no ride, at the fare the terms set; Alice's fare and the deposit the terms set for Bob are then locked.
14. ■ Close.  If the terms select no bidder, Alice may close the call.
15. ■ Pickup.  The pickup is recorded when Alice and Bob are both at the pickup point.
16. ■ Arrive.  When Alice and Bob are both at the destination, either of them may claim the release of the fare and the deposit, which are then released to Alice and to Bob as the terms say.
17. ■ Driver arrive.  When Alice and Bob are both at the destination, either of them may claim the release of the fare and the deposit, which are then released to Alice and to Bob as the terms say.
18. ■ Cancel.  If no pickup is recorded within the maximum pickup delay, Alice may cancel; the fare and the deposit are then released to Alice and to Bob as the terms say.
19. ■ Withdraw.  Alice may withdraw her open call before the bidding period ends.
