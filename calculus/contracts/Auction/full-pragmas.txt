Auction
Of Knowledge: An *identity* is a unique cryptographic key pair. A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of Friendship: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts.
Of Local Overlay: A person has a *location*. d > 0, an integer, and e > 0 are parameters of Local Overlay. Environment: the bound is n, for x; either x points to fewer than d persons and y is closest to x among all of x's unpointed friends, or y is closer to x by more than e than some person pointed to by x; y is closer to x by more than e than some person pointed to by x; y is furthest from x among the persons x points to.
Of Auction: A *pickup delay* is measured from the award. The *terms* of a call state a maximum pickup delay, select a bidder, set the fare and the deposit, and say how these are released. A person can be either a rider or a driver but not both. Environment: x is a rider; x is a driver; the bidding period of x's call c has ended; x is at the pickup point with y; x is at the destination with y; no pickup of x's ride is recorded within the maximum pickup delay; the terms of x's call c select y; the terms of x's call c select no bidder.
Alice is a rider, Bob a driver, Carol a person, Trip a call.
Knowledge:1. ○ Establish: Alice may establish an identity. Alice will make herself known by her public key, sign her acts with her private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
Knowledge:2. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
Knowledge:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
Friendship:1. ○ Befriend: Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other. Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
Friendship:2. ■ Unfriend: Alice may end her friendship with Bob.
Friendship:3. ○ Loss of Trust. Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
Overlay:1, Local Overlay:1. ■ Point friend: Alice may point to Bob, provided Alice and Bob are friends and either Alice points to fewer than D persons and Bob is closest to Alice among all of Alice's unpointed friends, or Bob is closer to Alice by more than E than some person pointed to by Alice.
Overlay:2, Local Overlay:2. ■ Point transitive: Alice may point to Carol, provided Alice knows Carol, Alice points to Bob, Bob points to Carol and Carol is closer to Alice by more than E than some person pointed to by Alice.
Overlay:3, Local Overlay:3. ■ Unpoint: Alice may cease pointing to Bob, provided the bound is D, Alice points to more than D persons and Bob is furthest from Alice among the persons Alice points to.
Auction:1. ■ Call. Call: Alice may call for bids on a ride as Trip, provided Alice is a rider and Alice does not have a ride. Alice calls for bids from the persons Alice points to, stating the pickup point, the destination, the bidding period and the terms. Send call: Alice's call Trip goes to Bob, provided Alice has the call Trip open and Alice points to Bob. Forward call: Bob forwards Alice's call Trip to Carol, provided Bob has received Alice's call Trip from Alice and Bob points to Carol.
Auction:2. ■ Bid: Bob may bid a fare and a pickup delay on Alice's call Trip, provided Bob is a driver, Bob has received Alice's call Trip and the bidding period of Alice's call Trip has not ended. Alice may not open a bid before then.
Auction:3. ■ Award. Award: Alice may award the ride of her call Trip to Bob, provided the bidding period of Alice's call Trip has ended, Bob has a bid on Alice's call Trip and the terms of Alice's call Trip select Bob. The ride is awarded at the fare the terms set, and Alice's fare and the deposit the terms set for Bob are then locked. Close: Alice may close her call Trip, provided the terms of Alice's call Trip select no bidder.
Auction:4. ■ Pickup: The pickup of Alice and Bob is recorded, provided Alice and Bob have a ride and Alice and Bob are both at the pickup point.
Auction:5. ■ Arrive: The fare and the deposit are released to Alice and to Bob as the terms say, provided Alice and Bob are both at the destination.
Auction:6. ■ Cancel: The fare and the deposit are released to Alice and to Bob as the terms say, with the consent of Alice, provided no pickup of Alice's ride is recorded within the maximum pickup delay. The fare and the deposit are then released to Alice and to Bob as the terms say.
Auction:7. ■ Withdraw: Alice may close her call Trip, provided the bidding period of Alice's call Trip has not ended.
