Agents
Agents unites a renaming of Guardians and Forwarding, and extends and amends the union (GSC, From Grassroots Contract to Grassroots App, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06231).
Renaming of Guardians: an AI agent for a ward and a principal for a creator.
Vocabulary: z holds the attestation t = attestation(z,t); y holds a record of z's attestation t = attestation_record(y,z,t).
Roles: the guardians of z = guardians(z), listed by z has y as a guardian, y is a guardian of z and y holds a record of z's attestation Proof.
Agents:0 - | The *developer* of an AI agent is the signer of its code.
Agents:0 - | Alice and Bob are persons, Alf an AI agent, Proof and Seal attestations, Item an item; in clause 2 Will is an AI agent.
Agents:1 ○ | AI developer and guardian accountability.
Agents:1 ○ | The developer of Alf will be accountable for Alf's defects.
Agents:1 ○ | Every guardian of Alf will be accountable for operating it recklessly.
