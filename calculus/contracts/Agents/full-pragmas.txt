Agents
Of Knowledge: An *identity* is a unique cryptographic key pair. A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of Friendship: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts.
Of Guardians: Bob *trusts* Alf, an AI agent of Alice, when he trusts Alice. The *decision rule*, a parameter of Guardians, says who decides for an AI agent and how. An AI agent takes an act of a party by those who decide for the AI agent. In clause 4 Alice and Bob are possibly one person. Default: any guardian of z for those who decide for z.
Of Forwarding: The *author* of an item is its first signer.
Of Agents: The *developer* of an AI agent is the signer of its code. In clause 2 Will is an AI agent.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Alf and Will AI agents, Item an item, Proof and Seal attestations.
Knowledge:1. ○ Establish: Alice may establish an identity. Alice will make herself known by her public key, sign her acts with her private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
Knowledge:2, Guardians:3. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
Knowledge:3, Guardians:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other, Alice knows Carol, Alice is a person, Bob is a person and Carol is a person.
Friendship:1, Guardians:3. ■ Befriend: Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other, Alice is a person and Bob is a person. Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
Friendship:2, Guardians:3. ■ Person unfriend: Alice may end her friendship with Bob, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
Friendship:3. ○ Loss of Trust. Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
Guardians:1, Agents:2. ■ Enter: Alice may create Alf, and Alf must consent, provided Alice is a person, Alice holds the item Proof and Alice is the author of Proof, and then Alf holds the attestation Proof and Alice holds a record of Alf's attestation Proof. Alice chooses Alf's key pair.
Guardians:2. ■ Guardian decisions.
Guardians:2, Agents:2. ■ Make guardian: The guardians of Alf may make Bob a guardian of Alf, with the consent of Bob, provided Alf is an AI agent, Alf is not retired, Bob is a person, Alf has Alice as a guardian, Bob counts Alice as a friend and Alf holds the attestation Proof, and then Bob holds a record of Alf's attestation Proof.
Guardians:2. ■ Resign: Bob may end his guardianship of Alf. End guardian: The guardians of Alf, other than Bob, may end Bob's guardianship of Alf. Guardian unfriend: Bob may end his friendship with his AI agent Alf. Alf may not end a friendship with her guardian.
Guardians:3. ■ Ward and person.
Guardians:3, Agents:2. ■ AI agent acquaint: Alf and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Alf is an AI agent, Alf has Alice as a guardian, Bob is a person and Alf holds the attestation Proof, and then Bob holds a record of Alf's attestation Proof. AI agent befriend: Alf and Bob may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Alf is an AI agent, Alf is not retired, Alf has Alice as a guardian, Bob is a person, Alf and Bob know each other, Alice and Bob are friends and Alf holds the attestation Proof. AI agent end: Alf may end its friendship with Bob, provided Alf does not have Bob as a guardian and Alf holds the attestation Proof.
Guardians:3. ■ Unfriend: Bob may end his friendship with Alf, provided Bob is not a guardian of Alf. AI agent unfriend: Alice may end the friendship of Alf and Bob, provided Alf has Alice as a guardian and Bob is not a guardian of Alf.
Guardians:4. ■ Wards.
Guardians:4, Agents:2. ■ AI agents acquaint: Alf and Will may jointly come to know each other, provided Alf is an AI agent, Alf has Alice as a guardian, Will is an AI agent, Will has Bob as a guardian, Alf holds the attestation Proof and Will holds the attestation Seal, and then Alf holds a record of Will's attestation Seal and Will holds a record of Alf's attestation Proof. AI agents befriend: Alf and Will may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice and Bob, provided Alf is an AI agent, Alf is not retired, Alf has Alice as a guardian, Will is an AI agent, Will is not retired, Will has Bob as a guardian, Alf and Will know each other, Alice knows Will, Alice and Bob are friends, Alf holds the attestation Proof and Will holds the attestation Seal. AI agents of one guardian befriend: Alf and Will may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Alf is an AI agent, Alf is not retired, Alf has Alice as a guardian, Will is an AI agent, Will is not retired, Will has Alice as a guardian, Alf and Will know each other, Alf holds the attestation Proof and Will holds the attestation Seal.
Guardians:5. ■ Retire: The guardians of Alf may retire Alf. Alf can then take no act.
Forwarding:1, Agents:2. ■ Sign: Alice may create Item by signing it, provided Alice is a person.
Forwarding:2, Agents:2. ■ Forward: Alice may forward Item to Bob, adding her signature to the signatures Item carries, provided Alice holds the item Item, Alice and Bob are friends and Alice is a person.
Agents:1. ○ AI developer and guardian accountability. The developer of Alf will be accountable for Alf's defects. Every guardian of Alf will be accountable for operating it recklessly.
Agents:2. ■ AI acts. The principal of Alf attests at its creation, signed over Alf's public key, that Alf runs code signed by its developer. Agent sign: Alf may create Item by signing it, provided Alf is an AI agent, Alf holds the attestation Proof and Alf has Alice as a guardian. Agent forward: Alf may forward Item to Bob, adding its signature to the signatures Item carries, provided Alf is an AI agent, Alf holds the attestation Proof, Alf has Alice as a guardian, Alf holds the item Item and Alf and Bob are friends. Every act of Alf requires this attestation and names its developer and its principal.
