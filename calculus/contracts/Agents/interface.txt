Agents
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity once.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ■ Befriend.  Alice and Bob, who trust each other and are not friends, may jointly become friends.
5. ■ Person unfriend.  Alice may end her friendship with Bob.
6. ■ Enter.  Alice may create an AI agent, Alf, and establish Alf's identity by choosing Alf's key pair, and Alf must consent.
7. ■ Make guardian.  Those who decide for Alf may make Bob, a friend of a guardian of Alf, a guardian and a friend of Alf, with the consent of Bob.
8. ■ Resign.  Bob may end his guardianship of Alf.
9. ■ End guardian.  Those who decide for Alf, other than Bob, may end Bob's guardianship.
10. ■ Guardian unfriend.  Bob may end his friendship with Alf, which ends his guardianship.
11. ■ AI agent acquaint.  Alf, an AI agent of Alice, and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
12. ■ AI agent befriend.  Alf may enter a relation with Bob with the consent of Bob and Alice, provided Alf and Bob know each other and Alice and Bob are friends.
13. ■ AI agent end.  Alf, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Alf.
14. ■ Unfriend.  Alf, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Alf.
15. ■ AI agent unfriend.  Alf, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Alf.
16. ■ AI agents acquaint.  Alf, an AI agent of Alice, and Will, an AI agent of Bob, may jointly come to know each other.
17. ■ AI agents befriend.  Alf may enter a relation with Will with the consent of Will, Alice and Bob, provided Alf and Will know each other, Alice knows Will and is a friend of Bob, Alice and Bob possibly one person.
18. ■ AI agents of one guardian befriend.  Alf may enter a relation with Will with the consent of Will, Alice and Bob, provided Alf and Will know each other, Alice knows Will and is a friend of Bob, Alice and Bob possibly one person.
19. ■ Retire.  Those who decide for Alf may retire Alf; every guardianship and relation of Alf then ends and Alf can take no act.
20. ■ Sign.  Alice may create an item by signing it.
21. ■ Forward.  Alice may forward an item she holds to her friend Bob, adding her signature to the signatures the item carries.
22. ■ Agent sign.  The principal of Alf attests at its creation (Guardians:1), signed over Alf's public key, that Alf runs code signed by its developer.
23. ■ Agent forward.  The principal of Alf attests at its creation (Guardians:1), signed over Alf's public key, that Alf runs code signed by its developer.
