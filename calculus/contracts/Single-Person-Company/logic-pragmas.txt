Single-Person Company
Single-Person Company instantiates Corporation with single-person limited-liability company bylaws; x is an officer of z for x is entitled to propose m in z; x is an officer of z for x is entitled to take m for z; x is an officer of z for x is entitled to vote on m in z, and restricts it.
A *single-person limited-liability company* is a corporation whose bylaws name one role, the *sole officer*, who decides every act of the company, and admit no participant but the sole officer.
Bob is a person, Orga a single-person limited-liability company.
