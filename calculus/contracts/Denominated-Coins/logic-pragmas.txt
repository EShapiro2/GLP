Denominated Coins
Denominated Coins lifts Coins, and extends and weakens it.
*Dollar* names one denomination, and a *Dollar-denominated Alice-coin* is an Alice-coin issued as Alice's debt of one Dollar.
Alice and Bob are parties, Dollar a fiat currency, Sum a number.
1. ■ First issue: Alice may issue Sum Dollar-denominated Alice-coins for the first time, provided Alice has issued coins of no denomination. Issue: Notes:1 Issue, only if Alice has issued coins of the denomination Dollar.
2. ○ Denomination. A Dollar-denominated Alice-coin is due now; Alice will redeem it on presentation, by a Dollar-denominated coin the bearer accepts or by one Dollar.
3. ○ Pricing. Alice will accept Dollar-denominated Alice-coins in payment for her offerings at one Dollar each.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x has issued coins of the denomination f = issued_in(x,f); x holds n f-denominated y-notes of type t = note(x,y,t,f,*n); z holds n f-denominated w-notes of type t in escrow from x for y on k = escrow_note(z,w,t,f,x,y,k,*n); x holds an f-denominated y-coin = note(x,y,coin,f,*1); x holds n f-denominated y-coins = note(x,y,coin,f,*n); x holds exactly n f-denominated y-coins = note(x,y,coin,f,#n); z holds n f-denominated w-coins in escrow from x for y on k = escrow_note(z,w,coin,f,x,y,k,*n).
Kind of note: note, Dollar-denominated y-notes of type t for y-notes of type t.
After x issues n f-denominated x-coins for the first time: x holds n f-denominated x-coins and x has issued coins of the denomination f.
