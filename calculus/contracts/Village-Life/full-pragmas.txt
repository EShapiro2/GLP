Village Life
Of Knowledge: An *identity* is a unique cryptographic key pair. A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of Friendship: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts.
Of Gossip: *Posts* have a *budget*: what a post has when made, and the *cost* of a forward. Gossip has a *gossip protocol*: to which of their friends a holder may forward a post, and with what part of the rest of the budget. A person *holds* any post they made or forwarded to them. Environment: n is a budget of a new post, for x; n covers the cost of a forward leaving m, for x; the gossip protocol forwards p to y with m of n, for x.
Of Matching: Posts have a *test*, whether two *match*. Environment: p and q match, for x.
Of Guardians: Bob *trusts* Wendy, a ward of Alice, when he trusts Alice. The *decision rule*, which the creator of a ward decides, says who decides for a ward and how. A ward takes an act of a party by those who decide for the ward. In clause 4 Alice and Bob are possibly one person.
Of Services: A *service* is a ward operated by a machine at an address every person can reach.
Of Rendezvous: An *invitation link* of Alice's is a term she signs, naming her, a rendezvous server, a serial and an expiry.
Of Anonymous Rendezvous: A *one-time key* is a key pair its maker uses for one meeting. Environment: x holds the one-time key k; x knows the public key of k.
Of Anonymous Matching: Each post carries the public key of a one-time key of its maker, and names a rendezvous server. Environment: p carries the public key of k, for x; d is named by the one of p and q whose public key has the smaller hash, for x.
Of Free Matching: The budget of a post is an integer, the cost of a forward is one, and the gossip protocol is forwarding to any friend with the budget less one.
Of Jobs: A post is a *vacancy*, stating the work and whom it seeks, or a *candidacy*, stating who its maker is and the work they seek. A vacancy and a candidacy match when each states what the other seeks.
Of Help: A post is a *service*, stating work its maker does, or a *need*, stating work its maker seeks done. A service and a need match when the service is the work the need seeks.
Of Lending: A post is a *loan* or a *gift*, stating a thing its maker lends or gives away, or a *wish*, stating a thing its maker seeks to borrow or receive. A loan or a gift matches a wish when it is of the thing the wish seeks.
Of Lost and Found: A post is a *loss*, stating a thing its maker lost, or a *find*, stating a thing its maker found. A loss and a find match when they are of the same thing.
Of Carpool: A post is a *commute*, stating a route, its times, and whether its maker drives, rides or either. Two commutes match when their routes and times agree and one maker drives and the other rides.
Of Home Exchange: A post is a *home*, stating a dwelling, when it is free, and where and when its maker seeks one. Two homes match when each is where and when the other's maker seeks.
Of Partners: A post is a *plan*, stating an activity, its place and its time, and whom its maker seeks for it. Two plans match when they are of the same activity, place and time and each maker is whom the other seeks.
Of Dating: A post is a *profile*, stating who its maker is and whom they seek. Two profiles match when each states whom the other seeks.
Of Groups: A *message* is what a person sends. Bob and Carol are possibly Alice or one person.
Of Care: A *circle* is a group for the care of a person, its *patient*. A *visit* is a time at which the patient needs a person with them. Bob and Carol are possibly Alice.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Post and Reply posts, Budget, Rest and Part numbers, Other and Key one-time keys, Wendy and Will wards, Dave a service, Link an invitation link, Tome a thing, Day a day, Today a date, Grupo a group, Text a message, Visit a visit.
Knowledge:1. ○ Establish: Alice may establish an identity. Alice will make herself known by her public key, sign her acts with her private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
Knowledge:2. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
Knowledge:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice is a person, Bob is a person, Carol is a person, Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
Friendship:1. ○ Befriend: Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice is a person, Bob is a person and Alice and Bob know each other. Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
Friendship:2, Guardians:3, Groups:3. ■ Person unfriend: Alice may end her friendship with Bob, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person, and then Alice is a member of no group of Bob, Bob is a member of no group of Alice, Alice has Bob as a member of no group and Bob has Alice as a member of no group.
Friendship:3. ○ Loss of Trust. Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
Gossip:1. ■ Post: Alice may make Post with the budget Budget, provided Budget is a budget of a new post.
Gossip:2. ■ Tell: Alice may forward Post to Bob with the budget Part, provided Alice holds the post Post, Alice has the budget Budget for Post, Budget covers the cost of a forward leaving Rest, Alice and Bob are friends and the gossip protocol forwards Post to Bob with Part of Rest.
Matching:1. ■ Return: Bob forwards Other to Alice, provided Bob holds the post Post from Alice, Bob holds the post Other and Post and Other match.
Matching:2, Anonymous Matching:1. ■ Meet: Alice and Carol may jointly come to know each other, provided Alice has made the post Post, Carol has made the post Other, Alice holds the post Other, Carol holds the post Post, Post and Other match, Alice has the address of Carol and Carol has the address of Alice.
Guardians:1. ■ Enter: Alice may create Wendy, and Wendy must consent, provided Alice is a person. Alice chooses Wendy's key pair.
Guardians:2. ■ Guardian decisions. Make guardian: Those who decide for Wendy may make Bob a guardian of Wendy, with the consent of Bob, provided Wendy is a ward, Wendy is not retired, Bob is a person, Wendy has Alice as a guardian and Bob counts Alice as a friend. Resign: Bob may end his guardianship of Wendy. End guardian: Those who decide for Wendy, other than Bob, may end Bob's guardianship of Wendy. Guardian unfriend: Bob may end his friendship with his ward Wendy. Wendy may not end a friendship with her guardian.
Guardians:3. ■ Ward and person. Ward acquaint: Wendy and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Wendy is a ward, Wendy has Alice as a guardian and Bob is a person. Ward befriend: Wendy and Bob may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Wendy is a ward, Wendy is not retired, Wendy has Alice as a guardian, Bob is a person, Wendy and Bob know each other and Alice and Bob are friends. Ward end: Wendy may end her friendship with Bob, provided Wendy does not have Bob as a guardian. Unfriend: Bob may end his friendship with Wendy, provided Bob is not a guardian of Wendy. Ward unfriend: Alice may end the friendship of Wendy and Bob, provided Wendy has Alice as a guardian and Bob is not a guardian of Wendy.
Guardians:4. ■ Wards. Wards acquaint: Wendy and Will may jointly come to know each other, provided Wendy is a ward, Wendy has Alice as a guardian, Will is a ward and Will has Bob as a guardian. Wards befriend: Wendy and Will may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice and Bob, provided Wendy is a ward, Wendy is not retired, Wendy has Alice as a guardian, Will is a ward, Will is not retired, Will has Bob as a guardian, Wendy and Will know each other, Alice knows Will and Alice and Bob are friends. Wards of one guardian befriend: Wendy and Will may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Wendy is a ward, Wendy is not retired, Wendy has Alice as a guardian, Will is a ward, Will is not retired, Will has Alice as a guardian and Wendy and Will know each other.
Guardians:5. ■ Retire: Those who decide for Wendy may retire Wendy. Wendy can then take no act.
Rendezvous:1. ■ Open server: Carol may make Dave a rendezvous server, provided Carol is a guardian of Dave.
Rendezvous:2. ■ Invite by link. Sign link: Alice may sign Link as an invitation link that names Dave, provided Alice knows Dave. Invite by link: Alice may register Link with Dave, provided Alice has signed Link as an invitation link that names Dave, Alice knows Dave, Dave is a rendezvous server and Dave does not hold Link spent for Alice. Link has a new serial.
Rendezvous:3. ■ Use link: Bob may present Link of Alice to Dave, provided Bob knows Dave and Dave does not hold Link expired. The link is then spent.
Rendezvous:4. ■ Reconnection. Present: Alice may present to Dave the signature of Bob over the friendship of Alice and Bob, provided Alice and Bob are friends, Alice knows Dave and Dave is a rendezvous server. Match: Dave matches Alice and Bob.
Anonymous Rendezvous:1. ■ Meet.
Anonymous Rendezvous:1, Anonymous Matching:1. ■ Present: Alice may present to Dave the keys Key and Other signed with Key, provided Alice holds the one-time key Key, Alice knows the public key of Other, Alice knows Dave, Dave is a rendezvous server, Alice has made the post Post, Alice holds the post Reply, Post carries the public key of Key, Reply carries the public key of Other and Dave is named by the one of Post and Reply whose public key has the smaller hash.
Anonymous Rendezvous:1. ■ Match: Dave matches Alice and Bob on Key and Other.
Anonymous Matching:1. ■ Meet.
Library:1. ◆ Shelve: Alice may put Tome on her shelf under a new name of hers. Tome is a thing of Alice's own.
Library:2. ■ Lend: Alice may lend Bob Tome from her shelf until Day, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other, the current date is Today and Day is more than Today.
Library:3. ■ Return: Bob may return Tome borrowed from Alice until Day.
Library:4. ○ On time. Bob will return a borrowed thing by the agreed day.
Groups:1. ■ Message: Alice may send Text to Bob, provided Alice and Bob are friends.
Groups:2. ■ Group. Create: Alice may create Grupo. Alice is then its leader and first member. Close: Alice may close Grupo by leaving it.
Groups:3. ■ Joining and Leaving. Join: Alice may add Bob to Grupo, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo and Alice and Bob are friends. Remove: Alice may end Bob's membership in Grupo, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo. Leave: Bob may end his membership in Alice's Grupo.
Groups:4. ■ Group messaging. Post: Bob may send Text to the group Grupo of Alice, provided Bob is a member of the group Grupo of Alice. Post own: Alice may send Text to the group Grupo of Alice, provided Alice is a member of the group Grupo of Alice. Deliver: Alice delivers Text of Bob in Grupo to Carol, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo, Alice has the post Text of Bob in Grupo and Alice has Carol as a member of the group Grupo. Deliver leader: Alice delivers Text of Bob in Grupo to the leader Alice, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo and Alice has the post Text of Bob in Grupo.
Care:1. ◆ Open: Alice may open Grupo for the care of Carol, provided Alice knows Carol.
Care:2. ■ Ask: Bob may post Visit in Alice's Grupo, provided Bob is a member of the group Grupo of Alice and Alice has Grupo as a circle.
Care:3. ○ Take: Bob may take Visit in Alice's Grupo, provided Bob is a member of the group Grupo of Alice. Bob will make the visit, or release it in time for another member to take it.
Care:4. ■ Release: Bob may release Visit in Alice's Grupo. Release for: Alice may release Visit of Bob in Grupo, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo. Release by the patient: Carol may release Visit of Bob in the circle Grupo of Alice, provided Alice has Grupo as a circle for Carol.
Care:5. ◆ Report. Made: Bob may attest Visit in Alice's Grupo made. Missed: Alice may attest Visit of Bob in Grupo missed, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo. Missed by the patient: Carol may attest Visit of Bob in the circle Grupo of Alice missed, provided Alice has Grupo as a circle for Carol. Contest: Alice may contest Visit of Bob in Grupo, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo and Alice has the visit Visit made by Bob in Grupo.
Care:6. ■ End: Carol may end the circle Grupo of Alice.
