Village Life
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity once.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ○ Befriend.  Alice and Bob, who trust each other and are not friends, may jointly become friends.
5. ■ Person unfriend.  Alice may end her friendship with Bob.
6. ■ Post.  Alice may make a post.
7. ■ Tell.  Alice who holds a post, the budget of which covers the cost, may forward it to her friend Bob with a part of the rest of the budget, both according to the gossip protocol.
8. ■ Return.  Bob, who holds two matching posts, one from Alice, forwards to Alice the second post.
9. ■ Meet.  Alice and Carol, both holding matching posts one made by Alice and one made by Carol, may jointly come to know each other, only by Anonymous Rendezvous:1 with the one-time keys of the two posts, at the rendezvous server named by the post whose public key has the smaller hash.
10. ■ Enter.  Alice may create a ward, Wendy, and establish Wendy's identity by choosing Wendy's key pair, and Wendy must consent.
11. ■ Make guardian.  Those who decide for Wendy may make Bob, a friend of a guardian of Wendy, a guardian and a friend of Wendy, with the consent of Bob.
12. ■ Resign.  Bob may end his guardianship of Wendy.
13. ■ End guardian.  Those who decide for Wendy, other than Bob, may end Bob's guardianship.
14. ■ Guardian unfriend.  Bob may end his friendship with Wendy, which ends his guardianship.
15. ■ Ward acquaint.  Wendy, a ward of Alice, and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
16. ■ Ward befriend.  Wendy may enter a relation with Bob with the consent of Bob and Alice, provided Wendy and Bob know each other and Alice and Bob are friends.
17. ■ Ward end.  Wendy, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Wendy.
18. ■ Unfriend.  Wendy, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Wendy.
19. ■ Ward unfriend.  Wendy, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Wendy.
20. ■ Wards acquaint.  Wendy, a ward of Alice, and Will, a ward of Bob, may jointly come to know each other.
21. ■ Wards befriend.  Wendy may enter a relation with Will with the consent of Will, Alice and Bob, provided Wendy and Will know each other, Alice knows Will and is a friend of Bob, Alice and Bob possibly one person.
22. ■ Wards of one guardian befriend.  Wendy may enter a relation with Will with the consent of Will, Alice and Bob, provided Wendy and Will know each other, Alice knows Will and is a friend of Bob, Alice and Bob possibly one person.
23. ■ Retire.  Those who decide for Wendy may retire Wendy; every guardianship and relation of Wendy then ends and Wendy can take no act.
24. ■ Open server.  Carol may make her service Dave a rendezvous server.
25. ■ Sign link.  Alice may register with Dave an invitation link of hers that names Dave, with a new serial.
26. ■ Invite by link.  Alice may register with Dave an invitation link of hers that names Dave, with a new serial.
27. ■ Use link.  Bob may present to Dave an invitation link of Alice's registered with Dave that has not expired; Dave then gives Alice Bob's address and Bob Alice's, and the link is spent.
28. ■ Present.  Alice and Bob, who are friends, may each present to Dave the other's signature over their friendship.
29. ■ Match.  Dave then gives each the address of the other.
30. ■ Present 2.  Alice and Bob, each holding a one-time key and knowing the public key of the other's, may each present to Dave the two public keys, signed with their own.
31. ■ Match 2.  Dave then gives each the address of the other.
32. ◆ Shelve.  Alice may put a thing of her own on her shelf, under a new name of hers.
33. ■ Lend.  Alice may lend Bob a thing from her shelf until a day later than the current date, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other; it then leaves the shelf, and Bob holds it.
34. ■ Return 2.  Bob may return a borrowed thing; it then goes back on Alice's shelf.
35. ■ Message.  Alice may send a message to her friend Bob.
36. ■ Create.  Alice may create a group, of which she is the leader and first member.
37. ■ Close.  Alice may close the group by leaving it.
38. ■ Join.  Alice, the leader of a group, may add her friend Bob to it, with the consent of Bob.
39. ■ Remove.  Either Alice or Bob may then end Bob's membership in the group, which also ends when Alice and Bob cease to be friends.
40. ■ Leave.  Either Alice or Bob may then end Bob's membership in the group, which also ends when Alice and Bob cease to be friends.
41. ■ Post 2.  A member of a group may send a message to the group.
42. ■ Post own.  A member of a group may send a message to the group.
43. ■ Deliver.  Its leader delivers it to every current member.
44. ■ Deliver leader.  Its leader delivers it to every current member.
45. ◆ Open.  Alice may create a circle (Groups:2) for her own care, or for the care of Carol, attesting that Carol asked her to.
46. ■ Ask.  A member of a circle may post a visit to it.
47. ○ Take.  Bob, a member of the circle, may take a posted visit.
48. ■ Release.  Bob, the leader or the patient may release a visit Bob took; it is then posted again.
49. ■ Release for.  Bob, the leader or the patient may release a visit Bob took; it is then posted again.
50. ■ Release by the patient.  Bob, the leader or the patient may release a visit Bob took; it is then posted again.
51. ◆ Made.  Bob may attest to the circle that he made a visit he took.
52. ◆ Missed.  The leader or the patient may attest to the circle that a visit taken was missed.
53. ◆ Missed by the patient.  The leader or the patient may attest to the circle that a visit taken was missed.
54. ◆ Contest.  Alice may contest Visit of Bob in Grupo, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo and Alice has the visit Visit made by Bob in Grupo.
55. ■ End.  The patient may close a circle for their care.
