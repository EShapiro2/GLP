Provenance Groups
Provenance Groups unites Groups and a strengthening of AI Agents, and extends and augments the union (GSC, From Grassroots Contract to Grassroots App, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06231).
Alf is an AI agent, Alice and Bob are persons, Grupo a group, Text a message, Item an item, Proof an attestation.
1. ■ Message: Groups:1, only if Alice is a person, Alice holds the item Text and Alice is the author of Text, and then Bob holds the item Text. Post: Groups:4 Post, only if Bob is a person, Bob holds the item Text and Bob is the author of Text. Post own: Groups:4 Post own, only if Alice is a person, Alice holds the item Text and Alice is the author of Text. Agent message: Alf may send Text to Bob, provided Alf is an AI agent, Alf holds the attestation Proof, Alf has Alice as a guardian, Alf holds the item Text and Alf and Bob are friends. Create: Groups:2 Create, only if Alice is a person. Close: Groups:2 Close, only if Alice is a person. Join: Groups:3 Join, only if Alice is a person and Bob is a person. Remove: Groups:3 Remove, only if Alice is a person. Leave: Groups:3 Leave, only if Bob is a person. Deliver: Groups:4 Deliver, only if not Alice is the author of Text, and then Carol holds the item Text signed by Alice. Deliver leader: Groups:4 Deliver leader, only if not Alice is the author of Text, and then Alice holds the item Text signed by Alice. Deliver own: Alice delivers Text as its author in Grupo to Carol, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo, Alice has the post Text of Alice in Grupo, Alice has Carol as a member of the group Grupo and Alice is the author of Text.
2. ■ Post forward: Bob may forward Item to the group Grupo of Alice, adding his signature to the signatures Item carries, provided Bob is a person, Bob is a member of the group Grupo of Alice, Bob is not the leader of Grupo and Bob holds the item Item.
Logic pragmas.
After x sends t to y: y has the message t from x and y holds the item t.
After y delivers t as its author in g to z, which requires y has not delivered the message t of y in g to z: y has delivered the message t of y in g to z, z has the message t of y in g delivered by y and z holds the item t.
After x forwards t to the group g of y, adding x's signature to the signatures t carries: y has the post t signed by x of x in g.
