Provenance Groups
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity once.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ■ Befriend.  Alice and Bob, who trust each other and are not friends, may jointly become friends.
5. ■ Person unfriend.  Alice may end her friendship with Bob.
6. ■ Message.  Alice may send a message to her friend Bob.
7. ■ Create.  Alice may create a group, of which she is the leader and first member.
8. ■ Close.  Alice may close the group by leaving it.
9. ■ Join.  Alice, the leader of a group, may add her friend Bob to it, with the consent of Bob.
10. ■ Remove.  Either Alice or Bob may then end Bob's membership in the group, which also ends when Alice and Bob cease to be friends.
11. ■ Leave.  Either Alice or Bob may then end Bob's membership in the group, which also ends when Alice and Bob cease to be friends.
12. ■ Post.  A member of a group may send a message to the group.
13. ■ Post own.  A member of a group may send a message to the group.
14. ■ Deliver.  Its leader delivers it to every current member.
15. ■ Deliver leader.  Its leader delivers it to every current member.
16. ■ Enter.  Alice may create an AI agent, Carol, and establish Carol's identity by choosing Carol's key pair, and Carol must consent.
17. ■ Make guardian.  Those who decide for Carol may make Bob, a friend of a guardian of Carol, a guardian and a friend of Carol, with the consent of Bob.
18. ■ Resign.  Bob may end his guardianship of Carol.
19. ■ End guardian.  Those who decide for Carol, other than Bob, may end Bob's guardianship.
20. ■ Guardian unfriend.  Bob may end his friendship with Carol, which ends his guardianship.
21. ■ AI agent acquaint.  Carol, an AI agent of Alice, and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
22. ■ AI agent befriend.  Carol may enter a relation with Bob with the consent of Bob and Alice, provided Carol and Bob know each other and Alice and Bob are friends.
23. ■ AI agent end.  Carol, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Carol.
24. ■ Unfriend.  Carol, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Carol.
25. ■ AI agent unfriend.  Carol, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Carol.
26. ■ AI agents acquaint.  Carol, an AI agent of Alice, and Dave, an AI agent of Bob, may jointly come to know each other.
27. ■ AI agents befriend.  Carol may enter a relation with Dave with the consent of Dave, Alice and Bob, provided Carol and Dave know each other, Alice knows Dave and is a friend of Bob, Alice and Bob possibly one person.
28. ■ AI agents of one guardian befriend.  Carol may enter a relation with Dave with the consent of Dave, Alice and Bob, provided Carol and Dave know each other, Alice knows Dave and is a friend of Bob, Alice and Bob possibly one person.
29. ■ Retire.  Those who decide for Carol may retire Carol; every guardianship and relation of Carol then ends and Carol can take no act.
30. ■ Sign.  Alice may create an item by signing it.
31. ■ Forward.  Alice may forward an item she holds to her friend Bob, adding her signature to the signatures the item carries.
32. ■ Agent sign.  The principal of Alf attests at its creation (Guardians:1), signed over Alf's public key, that Alf runs code signed by its developer.
33. ■ Agent forward.  The principal of Alf attests at its creation (Guardians:1), signed over Alf's public key, that Alf runs code signed by its developer.
34. ■ Agent message.  An AI agent may send a message to a friend (Groups:1).
35. ■ Deliver own.  An AI agent may send a message to a friend (Groups:1).
36. ■ Post forward.  A member of a group, not its leader, may forward an item they hold to the group, adding their signature.
