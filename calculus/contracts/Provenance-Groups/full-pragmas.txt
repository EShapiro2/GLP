Provenance Groups
Of Knowledge: An *identity* is a unique cryptographic key pair. A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of Friendship: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts.
Of Groups: A *message* is what a person sends. Bob and Carol are possibly Alice or one person.
Of Guardians: Bob *trusts* Alf, an AI agent of Alice, when he trusts Alice. The *decision rule*, which the principal of an AI agent decides, says who decides for an AI agent and how. An AI agent takes an act of a party by those who decide for the AI agent. In clause 4 Alice and Bob are possibly one person.
Of Forwarding: The *author* of an item is its first signer.
Of AI Agents: The *developer* of an AI agent is the signer of its code. In clause 2 Will is an AI agent.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Grupo a group, Text a message, Alf and Will AI agents, Item an item, Proof and Seal attestations.
Knowledge:1. ○ Establish: Alice may establish an identity. Alice will make herself known by her public key, sign her acts with her private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
Knowledge:2. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
Knowledge:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice is a person, Bob is a person, Carol is a person, Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
Friendship:1. ○ Befriend: Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice is a person, Bob is a person and Alice and Bob know each other. Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
Friendship:2, Groups:3, Guardians:3. ■ Person unfriend: Alice may end Bob's membership in _, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person, and then no longer Alice counts Bob as a friend, no longer Bob counts Alice as a friend, Alice is a member of no group of Bob and Bob has Alice as a member of no group.
Friendship:3. ○ Loss of Trust. Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
Groups:1, Provenance Groups:1. ■ Message: Alice may send Text to Bob, provided Alice and Bob are friends, Alice is a person, Alice holds the item Text and Alice is the author of Text, and then Bob holds the item Text.
Groups:2. ■ Group.
Groups:2, Provenance Groups:1. ■ Create: Alice may create Grupo, provided Alice is a person. Alice is then its leader and first member. Close: Alice may close Grupo by leaving it, provided Alice is a person.
Groups:3. ■ Joining and Leaving.
Groups:3, Provenance Groups:1. ■ Join: Alice may add Bob to Grupo, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo, Alice and Bob are friends, Alice is a person and Bob is a person. Remove: Alice may end Bob's membership in Grupo, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo and Alice is a person. Leave: Bob may end his membership in Alice's Grupo, provided Bob is a person.
Groups:4. ■ Group messaging.
Groups:4, Provenance Groups:1. ■ Post: Bob may send Text to the group Grupo of Alice, provided Bob is a member of the group Grupo of Alice, Bob is a person, Bob holds the item Text and Bob is the author of Text. Post own: Alice may send Text to the group Grupo of Alice, provided Alice is a member of the group Grupo of Alice, Alice is a person, Alice holds the item Text and Alice is the author of Text. Deliver: Alice delivers Text of Bob in Grupo to Carol, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo, Alice has the post Text of Bob in Grupo, Alice has Carol as a member of the group Grupo and Alice is not the author of Text, and then Carol holds the item Text signed by Alice. Deliver leader: Alice delivers Text of Bob in Grupo to Alice, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo, Alice has the post Text of Bob in Grupo and Alice is not the author of Text, and then Alice holds the item Text signed by Alice.
Guardians:1, AI Agents:2. ■ Enter: Alice may create Alf, and Alf must consent, provided Alice is a person, Alice holds the item Proof and Alice is the author of Proof, and then Alf holds the attestation Proof and Alice holds a record of Alf's attestation Proof. Alice chooses Alf's key pair.
Guardians:2. ■ Guardian decisions.
Guardians:2, AI Agents:2. ■ Make guardian: The guardians of Alf may make Bob a guardian of Alf, with the consent of Bob, provided Alf is an AI agent, Alf is not retired, Bob is a person, Alf has Alice as a guardian, Bob counts Alice as a friend and Alf holds the attestation Proof, and then Bob holds a record of Alf's attestation Proof.
Guardians:2. ■ Resign: Bob may end his guardianship of Alf. End guardian: The guardians of Alf, other than Bob, may end Bob's guardianship of Alf. Guardian unfriend: Bob may end his friendship with his AI agent Alf. Alf may not end a friendship with her guardian.
Guardians:3. ■ Ward and person.
Guardians:3, AI Agents:2. ■ AI agent acquaint: Alf and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Alf is an AI agent, Alf has Alice as a guardian, Bob is a person and Alf holds the attestation Proof, and then Bob holds a record of Alf's attestation Proof. AI agent befriend: Alf and Bob may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Alf is an AI agent, Alf is not retired, Alf has Alice as a guardian, Bob is a person, Alf and Bob know each other, Alice and Bob are friends and Alf holds the attestation Proof. AI agent end: Alf may end its friendship with Bob, provided Alf does not have Bob as a guardian and Alf holds the attestation Proof.
Guardians:3. ■ Unfriend: Bob may end his friendship with Alf, provided Bob is not a guardian of Alf. AI agent unfriend: Alice may end the friendship of Alf and Bob, provided Alf has Alice as a guardian and Bob is not a guardian of Alf.
Guardians:4. ■ Wards.
Guardians:4, AI Agents:2. ■ AI agents acquaint: Alf and Will may jointly come to know each other, provided Alf is an AI agent, Alf has Alice as a guardian, Will is an AI agent, Will has Bob as a guardian, Alf holds the attestation Proof and Will holds the attestation Seal, and then Alf holds a record of Will's attestation Seal and Will holds a record of Alf's attestation Proof. AI agents befriend: Alf and Will may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice and Bob, provided Alf is an AI agent, Alf is not retired, Alf has Alice as a guardian, Will is an AI agent, Will is not retired, Will has Bob as a guardian, Alf and Will know each other, Alice knows Will, Alice and Bob are friends, Alf holds the attestation Proof and Will holds the attestation Seal. AI agents of one guardian befriend: Alf and Will may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Alf is an AI agent, Alf is not retired, Alf has Alice as a guardian, Will is an AI agent, Will is not retired, Will has Alice as a guardian, Alf and Will know each other, Alf holds the attestation Proof and Will holds the attestation Seal.
Guardians:5. ■ Retire: The guardians of Alf may retire Alf. Alf can then take no act.
Forwarding:1, AI Agents:2. ■ Sign: Alice may create Item by signing it, provided Alice is a person.
Forwarding:2, AI Agents:2. ■ Forward: Alice may forward Item to Bob, adding her signature to the signatures Item carries, provided Alice holds the item Item, Alice and Bob are friends and Alice is a person.
AI Agents:1. ○ AI developer and guardian accountability. The developer of Alf will be accountable for Alf's defects. Every guardian of Alf will be accountable for operating it recklessly.
AI Agents:2. ■ AI acts. The principal of Alf attests at its creation, signed over Alf's public key, that Alf runs code signed by its developer. Agent sign: Alf may create Item by signing it, provided Alf is an AI agent, Alf holds the attestation Proof and Alf has Alice as a guardian. Agent forward: Alf may forward Item to Bob, adding its signature to the signatures Item carries, provided Alf is an AI agent, Alf holds the attestation Proof, Alf has Alice as a guardian, Alf holds the item Item and Alf and Bob are friends. Every act of Alf requires this attestation and names its developer and its principal.

Deliver own: Alice delivers Text as its author in Grupo to Carol, provided Alice is the leader of Grupo, Alice has the post Text of Alice in Grupo, Alice has Carol as a member of the group Grupo and Alice is the author of Text.
