Interface pragmas
Make: Alice [Make] <item>
Open: Alice [Open] <mart>
Join: Alice [Join]
  Bob: Alice asks for your consent to join.
Posting: Carol [Offer]
  Alice [Offer own]
  Carol [Want] <wish>
  Alice [Want own] <wish>
Withdraw: Carol [Withdraw] <alice> <item> <mart>
  Alice [Withdraw own]
Swap, Escrow: Alice [Swap item for coin] <dave>
  Bob: Alice asks for your consent to swap item for coin.
  Alice [Swap item for bond] <dave>
  Bob: Alice asks for your consent to swap item for bond.
  Alice [Swap items] <thing>
  Bob: Alice asks for your consent to swap items.
  Alice [Deposit item] <cond>
  Carol: Alice asks for your consent to deposit item.
  Carol [Release item]
  Bob: Carol asks for your consent to release item.
  Carol [Return item]
  Alice: Carol asks for your consent to return item.
