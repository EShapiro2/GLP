Warded Groups
Warded Groups unites Groups and a strengthening of Guardians, and extends and amends the union.
Alice is a person, Wendy a ward.
1. ■ A ward's messages. Ward message: Wendy may send a message to a friend (Groups:1). Ward post: Wendy may send a message to a group (Groups:4).
2. ■ A ward's group. Ward create: Wendy may create a group (Groups:2). Ward close: Wendy may close it (Groups:2). Guardian close: Alice, a guardian of Wendy, may close it.
3. ■ Ward join: Wendy may join a group (Groups:3) only with the consent of Alice, a guardian of Wendy.
