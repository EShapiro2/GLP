Warded Groups
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity once.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ○ Befriend.  Alice and Bob, who trust each other and are not friends, may jointly become friends.
5. ■ Person unfriend.  Alice may end her friendship with Bob.
6. ■ Message.  Alice may send a message to her friend Bob.
7. ■ Create.  Alice may create a group, of which she is the leader and first member.
8. ■ Close.  Alice may close the group by leaving it.
9. ■ Join.  Alice, the leader of a group, may add her friend Bob to it, with the consent of Bob.
10. ■ Remove.  Either Alice or Bob may then end Bob's membership in the group, which also ends when Alice and Bob cease to be friends.
11. ■ Leave.  Either Alice or Bob may then end Bob's membership in the group, which also ends when Alice and Bob cease to be friends.
12. ■ Post.  A member of a group may send a message to the group.
13. ■ Post own.  A member of a group may send a message to the group.
14. ■ Deliver.  Its leader delivers it to every current member.
15. ■ Deliver leader.  Its leader delivers it to every current member.
16. ■ Enter.  Alice may create a ward, Wendy, and establish Wendy's identity by choosing Wendy's key pair, and Wendy must consent.
17. ■ Make guardian.  Those who decide for Wendy may make Bob, a friend of a guardian of Wendy, a guardian and a friend of Wendy, with the consent of Bob.
18. ■ Resign.  Bob may end his guardianship of Wendy.
19. ■ End guardian.  Those who decide for Wendy, other than Bob, may end Bob's guardianship.
20. ■ Guardian unfriend.  Bob may end his friendship with Wendy, which ends his guardianship.
21. ■ Ward acquaint.  Wendy, a ward of Alice, and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
22. ■ Ward befriend.  Wendy may enter a relation with Bob with the consent of Bob and Alice, provided Wendy and Bob know each other and Alice and Bob are friends.
23. ■ Ward end.  Wendy, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Wendy.
24. ■ Unfriend.  Wendy, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Wendy.
25. ■ Ward unfriend.  Wendy, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Wendy.
26. ■ Wards acquaint.  Wendy, a ward of Alice, and Will, a ward of Bob, may jointly come to know each other.
27. ■ Wards befriend.  Wendy may enter a relation with Will with the consent of Will, Alice and Bob, provided Wendy and Will know each other, Alice knows Will and is a friend of Bob, Alice and Bob possibly one person.
28. ■ Wards of one guardian befriend.  Wendy may enter a relation with Will with the consent of Will, Alice and Bob, provided Wendy and Will know each other, Alice knows Will and is a friend of Bob, Alice and Bob possibly one person.
29. ■ Retire.  Those who decide for Wendy may retire Wendy; every guardianship and relation of Wendy then ends and Wendy can take no act.
30. ■ Ward message.  Wendy may send a message to a friend (Groups:1).
31. ■ Ward post.  Wendy may send a message to a group (Groups:4).
32. ■ Ward post own.  Wendy may send a message to a group (Groups:4).
33. ■ Ward create.  Wendy may create a group (Groups:2).
34. ■ Ward close.  Wendy may close it (Groups:2).
35. ■ Guardian close.  Alice, a guardian of Wendy, may close it.
36. ■ Ward join.  Wendy may join a group (Groups:3) only with the consent of Alice, a guardian of Wendy.
37. ■ Wards join.  Wendy may join a group (Groups:3) only with the consent of Alice, a guardian of Wendy.
38. ■ Wards of one guardian join.  Wendy may join a group (Groups:3) only with the consent of Alice, a guardian of Wendy.
39. ■ Ward withdraw.  Wendy may join a group (Groups:3) only with the consent of Alice, a guardian of Wendy.
40. ■ Ward add.  Wendy may join a group (Groups:3) only with the consent of Alice, a guardian of Wendy.
41. ■ Ward leave.  Wendy may join a group (Groups:3) only with the consent of Alice, a guardian of Wendy.
42. ■ Ward remove.  Wendy may join a group (Groups:3) only with the consent of Alice, a guardian of Wendy.
