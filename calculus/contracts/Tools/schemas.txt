Tools
Tools extends Booking, and weakens it.
Vocabulary: z has r held by x = held(z,r,x); z has r handed to x for s by y = handed(z,r,x,s,y); z has r taken back from x by y = taken_back(z,r,x,y); z has r withdrawn by y = withdrawn_by(z,r,y); z has r withdrawn = withdrawn(z,r).
After y and x attest the handover of r of z for s, which requires z has r held by no one: z has r held by x and z has r handed to x for s by y.
After y attests the return of r of z by x: no longer z has r held by x and z has r taken back from x by y.
After y withdraws r of z, which requires z does not have r withdrawn: z has r withdrawn and z has r withdrawn by y.
After y restores r of z: no longer z has r withdrawn and z has r withdrawn by no one.
Tools:0 - | A resource is a *tool*, a thing that the organisation owns and its participants use. A tool is *withdrawn* while it cannot be used.
Tools:0 - | Alice and Carol are persons, Orga an organisation, Res a tool, Slot a slot.
Tools:1 ◆ Hand over: held(Orga,Res,Alice), handed(Orga,Res,Alice,Slot,Carol) <- has_officer(Orga,Carol), booking(Alice,Orga,Res,Slot), ~withdrawn(Orga,Res), ~held(Orga,Res,_) guarded by Carol, Alice.
Tools:2 ○ | Return.
Tools:2 ○ | Alice will return the tool to an officer of Orga by the end of her slot, as she took it.
Tools:3 ◆ Take back: ~held(Orga,Res,Alice), taken_back(Orga,Res,Alice,Carol) <- has_officer(Orga,Carol), held(Orga,Res,Alice) guarded by Carol.
Tools:4 ■ Withdraw: withdrawn(Orga,Res), withdrawn_by(Orga,Res,Carol) <- has_officer(Orga,Carol), ~withdrawn(Orga,Res) guarded by Carol.
Tools:4 ■ Restore: ~withdrawn(Orga,Res), ~withdrawn_by(Orga,Res,_) <- has_officer(Orga,Carol), withdrawn(Orga,Res) guarded by Carol.
Tools:5 ■ Book: Booking:1 <- ~withdrawn(Orga,Res).
