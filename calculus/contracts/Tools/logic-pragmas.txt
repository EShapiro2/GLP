Tools
Tools extends Booking, and weakens it.
A resource is a *tool*, a thing that the organisation owns and its participants use. A tool is *withdrawn* while it cannot be used.
Alice and Carol are persons, Orga an organisation, Res a tool, Slot a slot.
1. ◆ Hand over: Carol and Alice may jointly attest the handover of Res of Orga for Slot, provided Orga has Carol as an officer, Alice has a booking of Slot of Res in Orga and Orga does not have Res withdrawn.
2. ○ Return. Alice will return the tool to an officer of Orga by the end of her slot, as she took it.
3. ◆ Take back: Carol may attest the return of Res of Orga by Alice, provided Orga has Carol as an officer and Orga has Res held by Alice.
4. ■ Withdraw: Carol may withdraw Res of Orga, provided Orga has Carol as an officer. Restore: Carol may restore Res of Orga, provided Orga has Carol as an officer and Orga has Res withdrawn.
5. ■ Book: Booking:1, only if Orga does not have Res withdrawn.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: z has r held by x = held(z,r,x); z has r handed to x for s by y = handed(z,r,x,s,y); z has r taken back from x by y = taken_back(z,r,x,y); z has r withdrawn by y = withdrawn_by(z,r,y); z has r withdrawn = withdrawn(z,r).
After y and x attest the handover of r of z for s, which requires z has r held by no one: z has r held by x and z has r handed to x for s by y.
After y attests the return of r of z by x: no longer z has r held by x and z has r taken back from x by y.
After y withdraws r of z, which requires z does not have r withdrawn: z has r withdrawn and z has r withdrawn by y.
After y restores r of z: no longer z has r withdrawn and z has r withdrawn by no one.
