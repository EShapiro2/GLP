Feeds
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity once.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ■ Befriend.  Alice and Bob, who trust each other and are not friends, may jointly become friends.
5. ■ Unfriend.  Alice may end her friendship with Bob.
6. ■ Point friend.  Alice, who does not point to her friend Bob, may point to Bob.
7. ■ Point transitive.  Alice, who knows Carol and points to Bob who points to Carol, may point to Carol.
8. ■ Unpoint.  Alice, who points to Bob, may cease pointing to Bob.
9. ■ Labeled point friend.  Alice, who has no Dave-pointer to her friend Bob, may add one, if Bob has a Dave-pointer.
10. ■ Labeled point transitive.  Alice, who knows Carol and either has a Dave-pointer to Bob or is Bob's friend, may add a Dave-pointer to Carol, if Bob has a Dave-pointer to Carol, only if Carol knows Alice.
11. ■ Labeled point transitive friend.  Alice, who knows Carol and either has a Dave-pointer to Bob or is Bob's friend, may add a Dave-pointer to Carol, if Bob has a Dave-pointer to Carol, only if Carol knows Alice.
12. ■ Labeled unpoint.  Alice, who has a Dave-pointer to Carol, may remove it, unless Carol is Dave and her friend.
13. ■ Labeled unpoint self.  Alice, who has a Dave-pointer to Carol, may remove it, unless Carol is Dave and her friend.
14. ■ Post.  Alice has a feed and may post to it an item, created or received.
15. ■ Deliver.  Alice, who has a Carol-pointer to Bob who knows her, receives from Bob every item of Carol's feed that Bob holds.
