Consumers' Cooperative
Consumers' Cooperative unites Cooperative and an amendment of Trading Company, and extends and amends the union.
Vocabulary: y has paid z the fee = paid_fee(z,y); z has the fee at the number n = fee(z,n).
the earlier fee of z has ended: z has the fee at no number.
After g sets the fee of z at n: the earlier fee of z has ended and z has the fee at the number n.
After y pays z the fee in n w-coins, which requires y has not paid z the fee: no longer y holds n w-coins, z holds n w-coins and y has paid z the fee.
Consumers' Cooperative:0 - | A *consumers' cooperative* is a cooperative that is a trading company. Its *fee* is a number of Dollars the members set, and the *cost* of a good is the coins Orga paid for it.
Consumers' Cooperative:0 - | Bob, Dave and Erin are persons, Orga a consumers' cooperative, Ware a good, Dollar a fiat currency, Fee, Sum, Votes and Double numbers.
Consumers' Cooperative:1 ■ | Fee.
Consumers' Cooperative:1 ■ Set fee: ~fee(Orga,_), fee(Orga,Fee) <- organisation(Orga) guarded by deciders(Orga).
Consumers' Cooperative:1 ■ Pay fee: ~note(Bob,Dave,coin,*Fee), note(Orga,Dave,coin,*Fee), paid_fee(Orga,Bob) <- fee(Orga,Fee), accepts(Orga,Dave,Dollar), knows(Bob,Orga), ~paid_fee(Orga,Bob), note(Bob,Dave,coin,*Fee) guarded by Bob.
Consumers' Cooperative:1 ■ Join: Organisation:3 Join <- paid_fee(Orga,Bob).
Consumers' Cooperative:2 ■ Sell: Trading Company:3 <- participant(Erin,Orga), paid_for(Orga,Ware,#Sum).
