AI Agents
AI Agents unites a renaming of Guardians and Forwarding, and extends and augments the union (GSC, From Grassroots Contract to Grassroots App, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06231).
The *developer* of an AI agent is the signer of its code.
Alice and Bob are persons, Alf an AI agent, Proof and Seal attestations, Item an item; in clause 2 Will is an AI agent.
1. ○ AI developer and guardian accountability. The developer of Alf will be accountable for Alf's defects. Every guardian of Alf will be accountable for operating it recklessly.
2. ■ AI acts. Enter: Guardians:1, only if Alice holds the item Proof and Alice is the author of Proof, and then Alf holds the attestation Proof and Alice holds a record of Alf's attestation Proof. The principal of Alf attests at its creation, signed over Alf's public key, that Alf runs code signed by its developer. Make guardian: Guardians:2 Make guardian, only if Alf holds the attestation Proof, and then Bob holds a record of Alf's attestation Proof. AI agent acquaint: Guardians:3 Ward acquaint, only if Alf holds the attestation Proof, and then Bob holds a record of Alf's attestation Proof. AI agent befriend: Guardians:3 Ward befriend, only if Alf holds the attestation Proof. AI agent end: Guardians:3 Ward end, only if Alf holds the attestation Proof. AI agents acquaint: Guardians:4 Wards acquaint, only if Alf holds the attestation Proof and Will holds the attestation Seal, and then Alf holds a record of Will's attestation Seal and Will holds a record of Alf's attestation Proof. AI agents befriend: Guardians:4 Wards befriend, only if Alf holds the attestation Proof and Will holds the attestation Seal. AI agents of one guardian befriend: Guardians:4 Wards of one guardian befriend, only if Alf holds the attestation Proof and Will holds the attestation Seal. Sign: Forwarding:1, only if Alice is a person. Forward: Forwarding:2, only if Alice is a person. Agent sign: Alf may create Item by signing it, provided Alf is an AI agent, Alf holds the attestation Proof and Alf has Alice as a guardian. Agent forward: Alf may forward Item to Bob, adding its signature to the signatures Item carries, provided Alf is an AI agent, Alf holds the attestation Proof, Alf has Alice as a guardian, Alf holds the item Item and Alf and Bob are friends. Every act of Alf requires this attestation and names its developer and its principal.
Logic pragmas.
Renaming of Guardians: an AI agent for a ward, a principal for a creator and the guardians of z for those who decide for z.
Vocabulary: z holds the attestation t = attestation(z,t); y holds a record of z's attestation t = attestation_record(y,z,t).
Roles: the guardians of z = guardians(z), listed by z has y as a guardian, y is a guardian of z and y holds a record of z's attestation Proof.
