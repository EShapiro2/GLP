Anonymous Rendezvous
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity once.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ■ Befriend.  Alice and Bob, who trust each other and are not friends, may jointly become friends.
5. ■ Person unfriend.  Alice may end her friendship with Bob.
6. ■ Enter.  Alice may create a ward, Wendy, and establish Wendy's identity by choosing Wendy's key pair, and Wendy must consent.
7. ■ Make guardian.  Those who decide for Wendy may make Bob, a friend of a guardian of Wendy, a guardian and a friend of Wendy, with the consent of Bob.
8. ■ Resign.  Bob may end his guardianship of Wendy.
9. ■ End guardian.  Those who decide for Wendy, other than Bob, may end Bob's guardianship.
10. ■ Guardian unfriend.  Bob may end his friendship with Wendy, which ends his guardianship.
11. ■ Ward acquaint.  Wendy, a ward of Alice, and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
12. ■ Ward befriend.  Wendy may enter a relation with Bob with the consent of Bob and Alice, provided Wendy and Bob know each other and Alice and Bob are friends.
13. ■ Ward end.  Wendy, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Wendy.
14. ■ Unfriend.  Wendy, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Wendy.
15. ■ Ward unfriend.  Wendy, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Wendy.
16. ■ Wards acquaint.  Wendy, a ward of Alice, and Will, a ward of Bob, may jointly come to know each other.
17. ■ Wards befriend.  Wendy may enter a relation with Will with the consent of Will, Alice and Bob, provided Wendy and Will know each other, Alice knows Will and is a friend of Bob, Alice and Bob possibly one person.
18. ■ Wards of one guardian befriend.  Wendy may enter a relation with Will with the consent of Will, Alice and Bob, provided Wendy and Will know each other, Alice knows Will and is a friend of Bob, Alice and Bob possibly one person.
19. ■ Retire.  Those who decide for Wendy may retire Wendy; every guardianship and relation of Wendy then ends and Wendy can take no act.
20. ■ Open server.  Carol may make her service Dave a rendezvous server.
21. ■ Sign link.  Alice may register with Dave an invitation link of hers that names Dave, with a new serial.
22. ■ Invite by link.  Alice may register with Dave an invitation link of hers that names Dave, with a new serial.
23. ■ Use link.  Bob may present to Dave an invitation link of Alice's registered with Dave that has not expired; Dave then gives Alice Bob's address and Bob Alice's, and the link is spent.
24. ■ Present.  Alice and Bob, who are friends, may each present to Dave the other's signature over their friendship.
25. ■ Match.  Dave then gives each the address of the other.
26. ■ Present 2.  Alice and Bob, each holding a one-time key and knowing the public key of the other's, may each present to Dave the two public keys, signed with their own.
27. ■ Match 2.  Dave then gives each the address of the other.
