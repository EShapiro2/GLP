Backed Community
Backed Community extends Community, and weakens it.
Vocabulary: every treasurer of z stays backed, for x = all_stay_backed(x,z); x stays backed in z = stays_backed(z,x); z has a Dollar recorded as paid in by x = paid_in(z,x).
Roles: the treasurers of z = treasurers(z), listed by z has y in the role Treasurer and y holds the role Treasurer in z.
After g records a Dollar as paid into the account of z by x: z has a Dollar recorded as paid in by x.
Backed Community:0 - | *f* is a fraction, 0 ≤ f < 1. Orga's *account* is a bank account in Dollars, held in Orga's name by its treasurers. A treasurer, Alice, is *backed* when the Alice-coins Orga holds are at most the Dollars Alice paid into the account and the coins Alice holds of the other treasurers, leaving out the fraction f of them of whom she holds the most.
Backed Community:0 - | Alice is a person, Orga a community.
Backed Community:0 - | Environment: every treasurer of z stays backed, for x; x stays backed in z.
