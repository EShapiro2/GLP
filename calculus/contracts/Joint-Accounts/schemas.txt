Joint Accounts
Joint Accounts extends Coins.
Vocabulary: x has an account named n = account(x,n); x holds the joint account n with y = joint(x,n,y); x keeps m z-coins in n with y = joint_coin(x,z,n,y,*m).
x and y hold the joint account n: x holds the joint account n with y and y holds the joint account n with x.
After x and y open the joint account n under a new name of x's, which requires x does not have an account named n: x has an account named n and x and y hold the joint account n.
After x moves m z-coins into the joint account n of x and y: no longer x holds m z-coins, x keeps m z-coins in n with y and y knows z.
After y moves m z-coins into the joint account n of x and y: no longer y holds m z-coins, x keeps m z-coins in n with y and x knows z.
After x and y move m z-coins from the joint account n to x: no longer x keeps m z-coins in n with y and x holds m z-coins.
After x and y move m z-coins from the joint account n to y: no longer x keeps m z-coins in n with y and y holds m z-coins.
Joint Accounts:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Carol possibly Alice or Bob, Acct a joint account, Sum a number.
Joint Accounts:1 ■ Open account: account(Alice,Acct), joint(Alice,Acct,Bob), joint(Bob,Acct,Alice) <- knows(Alice,Bob), knows(Bob,Alice), ~account(Alice,Acct) guarded by Alice, Bob.
Joint Accounts:2 ■ Deposit: ~note(Alice,Carol,coin,*Sum), joint_coin(Alice,Carol,Acct,Bob,*Sum), knows(Bob,Carol) <- joint(Alice,Acct,Bob), joint(Bob,Acct,Alice), note(Alice,Carol,coin,*Sum) guarded by Alice possibly Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
Joint Accounts:2 ■ Partner deposit: ~note(Bob,Carol,coin,*Sum), joint_coin(Alice,Carol,Acct,Bob,*Sum), knows(Alice,Carol) <- joint(Alice,Acct,Bob), joint(Bob,Acct,Alice), note(Bob,Carol,coin,*Sum) guarded by Bob possibly Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
Joint Accounts:3 ■ Withdraw: ~joint_coin(Alice,Carol,Acct,Bob,*Sum), note(Alice,Carol,coin,*Sum) <- joint_coin(Alice,Carol,Acct,Bob,*Sum) guarded by Alice, Bob possibly Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
Joint Accounts:3 ■ Partner withdraw: ~joint_coin(Alice,Carol,Acct,Bob,*Sum), note(Bob,Carol,coin,*Sum) <- joint_coin(Alice,Carol,Acct,Bob,*Sum) guarded by Alice, Bob possibly Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
