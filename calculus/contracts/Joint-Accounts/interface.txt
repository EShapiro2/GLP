Joint Accounts
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity once.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ■ Issue.  Alice may issue Alice-notes.
5. ■ Swap.  Alice and Bob, who know each other, may jointly swap notes they hold.
6. ■ Pay.  Alice may pay notes she holds to Bob, whom she knows, with his consent.
7. ■ Pay issuer.  Alice may pay notes she holds to Bob, whom she knows, with his consent.
8. ○ Deposit.  Alice may deposit notes with Carol for Bob, on a condition the three agree, with the consent of Carol.
9. ○ Release.  Carol and Bob may jointly release them to Bob.
10. ○ Return.  Carol and Alice may jointly return them to Alice.
11. ■ Redeem.  Alice may redeem a Bob-coin she holds against any coin held by Bob, whom she knows.
12. ■ Open account.  Alice and Bob, who know each other, may jointly open a joint account, under a new name of Alice's.
13. ■ Account deposit.  Either of them may move coins they hold into their joint account.
14. ■ Partner deposit.  Either of them may move coins they hold into their joint account.
15. ■ Withdraw.  Alice and Bob may jointly move coins from their joint account to either of them.
16. ■ Partner withdraw.  Alice and Bob may jointly move coins from their joint account to either of them.
