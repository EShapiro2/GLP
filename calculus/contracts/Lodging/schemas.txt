Lodging
Lodging unites Community Network, Overlay and Recommendations, and extends and weakens the union (GRH, Grassroots Ride-Hailing, in preparation).
Vocabulary: y has l posted in z = posted(y,l,z); x has received l of y in z = received(x,l,y,z); x has nights of l booked with y = booked(x,l,y); y has nights of l booked by x = booked_by(y,l,x); x is at the place of l on the first night = at_place(x,l); x is checked in at l of y = checked_in(x,l,y); x leaves the place of l after the last night = leaves(x,l); x has checked out of l of y = checked_out(x,l,y); it is before the first night of l, for x = before_first_night(x,l); no check-in at l is recorded on the first night while x is at the place = no_show(x,l); the terms of l set the payment at n and the deposit at m, for x = terms(x,l,n,m); the terms of l release p to the customer and q to the provider on cancellation, for x = cancel_terms(x,l,p,q).
y is a host of z: y is a provider of z.
x is a traveller of z: x is a customer of z.
x and y have a booking of l: x has nights of l booked with y and y has nights of l booked by x.
After y posts l in z, which requires y does not have l posted in z: y has l posted in z.
After l of y goes to x in z, which requires x has not received l of y in z: x has received l of y in z.
After x passes l of y to w in z, which requires w has not received l of y in z: w has received l of y in z.
After y withdraws l from z: no longer y has l posted in z.
After x books nights of l of y in z locking n and m c-coins, which requires x and y do not have a booking of l: x and y have a booking of l, no longer x holds n c-coins, no longer y holds m c-coins and n and m c-coins are locked in z on l between the provider y and the customer x.
After x records x's check-in at l of y, which requires x is not checked in at l of y: x is checked in at l of y.
After x records x's checkout from l of y in z releasing to y the n and m c-coins locked: no longer x is checked in at l of y, x has checked out of l of y, no longer x and y have a booking of l, no longer n and m c-coins are locked in z on l between the provider y and the customer x, y holds n c-coins and y holds m c-coins.
After x cancels x's booking of l of y in z releasing p and q of the n and m c-coins locked: no longer x and y have a booking of l, no longer n and m c-coins are locked in z on l between the provider y and the customer x, x holds p c-coins and y holds q c-coins.
After x cancels x's booking of l of y in z taking back the n and m c-coins locked: no longer x and y have a booking of l, no longer n and m c-coins are locked in z on l between the provider y and the customer x, x holds n c-coins and x holds m c-coins.
After y cancels y's booking of l by x in z releasing p and q of the n and m c-coins locked: no longer x and y have a booking of l, no longer n and m c-coins are locked in z on l between the provider y and the customer x, x holds p c-coins and y holds q c-coins.
After x cancels x's booking of l of y in z releasing to y the n and m c-coins locked: no longer x and y have a booking of l, no longer n and m c-coins are locked in z on l between the provider y and the customer x, y holds n c-coins and y holds m c-coins.
After y cancels y's booking of l by x in z releasing to x the n and m c-coins locked: no longer x and y have a booking of l, no longer n and m c-coins are locked in z on l between the provider y and the customer x, x holds n c-coins and x holds m c-coins.
After y cancels y's booking of l by x in z taking back the n and m c-coins locked: no longer x and y have a booking of l, no longer n and m c-coins are locked in z on l between the provider y and the customer x, y holds n c-coins and y holds m c-coins.
