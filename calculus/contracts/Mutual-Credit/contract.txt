Mutual Credit
Mutual Credit instantiates Bank with mutual credit society bylaws and restricts and extends it.
A *mutual credit society* is a bank whose bylaws name one role, *member*, held by every participant; the members jointly decide its acts. A member is *in the red* by their coins in circulation less the other persons' coins they hold.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a mutual credit society.
1. ■ Leave: Organisation:3, only if the Orga-coins Bob holds and the Bob-coins Orga holds have been redeemed against each other.
2. ■ Redeem member's coin: Bob, a member, may redeem an Alice-coin he holds against an Orga-coin, provided Alice is a member.
3. ■ Credit limit. Denominated Currencies:2, Notes:3, Coins:1 and Denominated Currencies:4, only if every member is then in the red by at most 1000 coins.
