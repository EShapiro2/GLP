Rehearsals
Of Knowledge: An *identity* is a unique cryptographic key pair. A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of Friendship: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts.
Of Guardians: Bob *trusts* Orga, an organisation of Alice, when he trusts Alice. The *decision rule*, a parameter of Guardians, says who decides for an organisation and how. An organisation takes an act of a party by those who decide for the organisation. In clause 4 Alice and Bob are possibly one person. Default: any officer of z for those who decide for z.
Of Organisation: An *organisation* is a ward with *participants*, which include its *officers*, each with a *role*. The *bylaws*, a parameter of Organisation, name the roles of an organisation and say who decides each of its acts and how, their own amendment included. Parameters: x is entitled to propose m in z; x is entitled to vote on m in z; x is entitled to take m for z; the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z. Default: those who decide for z for those who decide the officers of z. Default: x is an officer of z for x is entitled to propose m in z. Default: x is an officer of z for x is entitled to vote on m in z. Default: x is an officer of z for x is entitled to take m for z. Default: z holds the votes of at least Votes persons on m, Double is Votes times 2 and z has fewer than Double persons as an officer for the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z.
Of Booking: An organisation has *rooms*, each with *slots*. A slot is *free* when it is not booked. A booking is *outstanding* until its slot has passed or it is cancelled. The bylaws name the *bound*, the number of outstanding bookings a participant may have. Environment: z has the slot s of the room r; x has fewer outstanding bookings in z than the bound.
Of Matching: *Posts* have a *test*, a parameter of Matching, whether two *match*. A person *holds* any post they made or forwarded to them. Environment: p and q match, for x.
Of Partners: A post is a *plan*, stating an activity, its place and its time, and whom its maker seeks for it. Two plans match when they are of the same activity, place and time and each maker is whom the other seeks.
Of Rehearsals: The activity is music, the place is a *studio*, an organisation that has rehearsal rooms, and the time is a slot.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Orga, Will and Gov organisations, Motion an act of Orga, Role a role, Votes and Double numbers, Res a room, Slot a slot, Post and Other posts.
Knowledge:1. ○ Establish: Alice may establish an identity. Alice will make herself known by her public key, sign her acts with her private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
Knowledge:2, Guardians:3. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
Knowledge:3, Guardians:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other, Alice knows Carol, Alice is a person, Bob is a person and Carol is a person.
Friendship:1, Guardians:3. ○ Befriend: Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other, Alice is a person and Bob is a person. Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
Friendship:2, Guardians:3. ■ Person unfriend: Alice may end her friendship with Bob, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
Friendship:3. ○ Loss of Trust. Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
Guardians:1. ■ Enter: Alice may create Orga, and Orga must consent, provided Alice is a person. Alice chooses Orga's key pair.
Guardians:2. ■ Guardian decisions.
Guardians:2, Organisation:4. ■ Appoint: Those who decide for Orga may make Bob an officer of Orga, with the consent of Bob, provided Orga is an organisation, Orga is not retired, Bob is a person, Orga has Alice as an officer, Bob counts Alice as a friend and Bob is a participant of Orga, and then Bob holds the role Role in Orga and Orga has Bob in the role Role. Resign: Bob may end his guardianship of Orga, and then Bob holds no role in Orga and Orga has Bob in no role. End officer: Those who decide for Orga, other than Bob, may end Bob's guardianship of Orga, and then Bob holds no role in Orga and Orga has Bob in no role. Officer unfriend: Bob may end his friendship with his organisation Orga, and then Bob holds no role in Orga and Orga has Bob in no role. Orga may not end a friendship with her officer.
Guardians:3. ■ Ward and person. Organisation acquaint: Orga and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Orga is an organisation, Orga has Alice as an officer and Bob is a person. Organisation befriend: Orga and Bob may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Orga is an organisation, Orga is not retired, Orga has Alice as an officer, Bob is a person, Orga and Bob know each other and Alice and Bob are friends. Organisation end: Orga may end its friendship with Bob, provided Orga does not have Bob as an officer. Unfriend: Bob may end his friendship with Orga, provided Bob is not an officer of Orga. Organisation unfriend: Alice may end the friendship of Orga and Bob, provided Orga has Alice as an officer and Bob is not an officer of Orga.
Guardians:4. ■ Wards. Organisations acquaint: Orga and Will may jointly come to know each other, provided Orga is an organisation, Orga has Alice as an officer, Will is an organisation and Will has Bob as an officer. Organisations befriend: Orga and Will may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice and Bob, provided Orga is an organisation, Orga is not retired, Orga has Alice as an officer, Will is an organisation, Will is not retired, Will has Bob as an officer, Orga and Will know each other, Alice knows Will and Alice and Bob are friends. Organisations of one officer befriend: Orga and Will may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Orga is an organisation, Orga is not retired, Orga has Alice as an officer, Will is an organisation, Will is not retired, Will has Alice as an officer and Orga and Will know each other.
Guardians:5, Organisation:4. ■ Retire: Those who decide for Orga may retire Orga, and then no one holds any role in Orga and Orga has no one in any role. Orga can then take no act.
Organisation:1. ■ Found: Gov may create Orga with Alice as its first officer in the role Role, with the consent of Alice, and Orga must consent, provided Alice is a person.
Organisation:2. ■ Acts of the organisation. Propose: Bob may propose Motion in Orga, provided Bob is an officer of Orga. Vote: Bob may vote on Motion in Orga, provided Bob is an officer of Orga, Orga is an organisation and Orga has Motion proposed. Act: Alice may take Motion for Orga, provided Alice is an officer of Orga, Orga holds the votes of at least Votes persons on Motion, Double is Votes times 2 and Orga has fewer than Double persons as an officer.
Organisation:3. ■ Participation. Join: Bob may become a participant of Orga, with the consent of those who decide for Orga. Leave: Bob may end his participation in Orga, provided Bob is not an officer of Orga. Officer leave: Bob may end his participation and his office in Orga. Expel: Those who decide for Orga may end Bob's participation in Orga, provided Bob is not an officer of Orga. Officer expel: Those who decide for Orga may end Bob's participation and Bob's office in Orga. Every role Bob holds in Orga ends with his participation.
Organisation:4. ■ Officers. The decision names his role.
Organisation:5. ■ Amend: Alice may amend the bylaws of Orga as Motion says, provided Alice is an officer of Orga, Orga is an organisation, Orga has Motion proposed, Orga holds the votes of at least Votes persons on Motion, Double is Votes times 2 and Orga has fewer than Double persons as an officer.
Organisation:6. ○ Accountability. Orga will be accountable at law for its undertakings where it is a juridical person, and every officer who decided an act of Orga will be accountable for it with Orga.
Booking:1. ■ Book: Alice may book Slot of Res in Orga, provided Alice is a participant of Orga, Orga has Alice as a participant, Alice has fewer outstanding bookings in Orga than the bound, Orga has the slot Slot of the room Res and Orga has Slot of Res booked by no one.
Booking:2. ■ Cancel: Alice may cancel her booking of Slot of Res in Orga.
