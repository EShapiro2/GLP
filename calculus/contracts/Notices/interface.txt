Notices
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity once.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ○ Befriend.  Alice and Bob, who trust each other and are not friends, may jointly become friends.
5. ■ Person unfriend.  Alice may end her friendship with Bob.
6. ■ Forward.  Alice, who holds a live broadcast, forwards it to her friend Bob, who does not hold it; Bob then holds it.
7. ■ Expire.  Alice ceases to hold a broadcast that is not live.
8. ■ Found.  Alice may create an organisation, Orga, and establish Orga's identity by choosing Orga's key pair, and Orga must consent.
9. ■ Appoint.  Those who decide for Orga may make Bob, a friend of an officer of Orga, an officer and a friend of Orga, with the consent of Bob, only if Bob is a participant; the decision names his role.
10. ■ Resign.  Bob may end his guardianship of Orga.
11. ■ End officer.  Those who decide for Orga, other than Bob, may end Bob's guardianship.
12. ■ Officer unfriend.  Bob may end his friendship with Orga, which ends his guardianship.
13. ■ Organisation acquaint.  Orga, an organisation of Alice, and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
14. ■ Organisation befriend.  Orga may enter a relation with Bob with the consent of Bob and Alice, provided Orga and Bob know each other and Alice and Bob are friends.
15. ■ Organisation end.  Orga, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Orga.
16. ■ Unfriend.  Orga, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Orga.
17. ■ Organisation unfriend.  Orga, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Orga.
18. ■ Organisations acquaint.  Orga, an organisation of Alice, and Will, an organisation of Bob, may jointly come to know each other.
19. ■ Organisations befriend.  Orga may enter a relation with Will with the consent of Will, Alice and Bob, provided Orga and Will know each other, Alice knows Will and is a friend of Bob, Alice and Bob possibly one person.
20. ■ Organisations of one officer befriend.  Orga may enter a relation with Will with the consent of Will, Alice and Bob, provided Orga and Will know each other, Alice knows Will and is a friend of Bob, Alice and Bob possibly one person.
21. ■ Retire.  Those who decide for Orga may retire Orga; every guardianship and relation of Orga then ends and Orga can take no act.
22. ■ Found by an organisation.  Alice, or an organisation with the consent of Alice, may create Orga (Guardians:1); Alice is then its first officer, and Orga must consent.
23. ■ Propose.  An officer, or a person the bylaws name, may propose an act of Orga.
24. ■ Vote.  Those who decide the act may vote on it.
25. ■ Act.  An officer the bylaws name for the act may take it for Orga after the votes decide it according to the bylaws.
26. ■ Join.  Bob may become a participant of Orga with the consent of those who decide for Orga.
27. ■ Leave.  Either Bob or those who decide for Orga may end Bob's participation, and with it every role he holds.
28. ■ Officer leave.  Either Bob or those who decide for Orga may end Bob's participation, and with it every role he holds.
29. ■ Expel.  Either Bob or those who decide for Orga may end Bob's participation, and with it every role he holds.
30. ■ Officer expel.  Either Bob or those who decide for Orga may end Bob's participation, and with it every role he holds.
31. ■ Amend.  Those who decide it may amend the bylaws.
32. ■ Notice.  Alice, an officer of Orga, may broadcast a notice.
33. ■ Notice forward.  Alice, a participant of Orga, may forward a live notice of Orga that she holds to Bob, a participant of Orga, who does not hold it; Bob then holds it.
