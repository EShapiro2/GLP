Notices
Of Knowledge: An *identity* is a unique cryptographic key pair. A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of Friendship: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts.
Of Broadcast: A *broadcast* is a message with a unique identifier, which its maker holds. Δ > 0 is a period of time, and a broadcast is *live* for Δ after it is made. Environment: m is live, for x.
Of Guardians: Bob *trusts* Orga, an organisation of Alice, when he trusts Alice. The *decision rule*, which the creator of an organisation decides, says who decides for an organisation and how. An organisation takes an act of a party by those who decide for the organisation. In clause 4 Alice and Bob are possibly one person.
Of Organisation: An *organisation* is a ward with *participants*, which include its *officers*, each with a *role*. The *bylaws*, which its founder decides, name the roles of an organisation and say who decides each of its acts and how, their own amendment included. Environment: the bylaws of z entitle x to propose motions; the bylaws of z entitle x to vote on m; the bylaws of z entitle x to take m; the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z; the bylaws of z entitle x to amend them as m says; the amendment m is decided according to the bylaws of z.
Of Notices: A *notice* is a broadcast that includes its text and the area it is for.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Cast a broadcast, Orga, Will and Gov organisations, Motion an act of Orga, Role a role.
Knowledge:1. ○ Establish: Alice may establish an identity. Alice will make herself known by her public key, sign her acts with her private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
Knowledge:2. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
Knowledge:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice is a person, Bob is a person, Carol is a person, Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
Friendship:1. ○ Befriend: Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice is a person, Bob is a person and Alice and Bob know each other. Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
Friendship:2, Guardians:3. ■ Person unfriend: Alice may end her friendship with Bob, provided Alice is a person and Bob is a person.
Friendship:3. ○ Loss of Trust. Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
Broadcast:1. ■ Forward: Alice forwards Cast to Bob, provided Alice and Bob are friends, Alice holds the broadcast Cast and Cast is live.
Broadcast:2. ■ Expire: Alice ceases to hold Cast, provided Cast is not live.
Guardians:1, Organisation:1. ■ Found: Alice may found Orga with Alice in the role Role, and Orga must consent, provided Alice is a person. Alice chooses Orga's key pair.
Guardians:2. ■ Guardian decisions.
Guardians:2, Organisation:4. ■ Appoint: Those who decide for Orga may make Bob an officer of Orga, with the consent of Bob, provided Orga is an organisation, Orga is not retired, Bob is a person, Orga has Alice as an officer, Bob counts Alice as a friend and Bob is a participant of Orga, and then Bob holds the role Role in Orga and Orga has Bob in the role Role. Resign: Bob may end his guardianship of Orga, and then Bob holds no role in Orga and Orga has Bob in no role. End officer: Those who decide for Orga, other than Bob, may end Bob's guardianship of Orga, and then Bob holds no role in Orga and Orga has Bob in no role. Officer unfriend: Bob may end his friendship with his organisation Orga, and then Bob holds no role in Orga and Orga has Bob in no role. Orga may not end a friendship with her officer.
Guardians:3. ■ Ward and person. Organisation acquaint: Orga and Bob may jointly come to know each other, provided Orga is an organisation, Orga has Alice as an officer and Bob is a person. Organisation befriend: Orga and Bob may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Orga is an organisation, Orga is not retired, Orga has Alice as an officer, Bob is a person, Orga and Bob know each other and Alice and Bob are friends. Organisation end: Orga may end its friendship with Bob, provided Orga does not have Bob as an officer. Unfriend: Bob may end his friendship with Orga, provided Bob is not an officer of Orga. Organisation unfriend: Alice may end the friendship of Orga and Bob, provided Orga has Alice as an officer and Bob is not an officer of Orga.
Guardians:4. ■ Wards. Organisations acquaint: Orga and Will may jointly come to know each other, provided Orga is an organisation, Orga has Alice as an officer, Will is an organisation and Will has Bob as an officer. Organisations befriend: Orga and Will may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice and Bob, provided Orga is an organisation, Orga is not retired, Orga has Alice as an officer, Will is an organisation, Will is not retired, Will has Bob as an officer, Orga and Will know each other, Alice knows Will and Alice and Bob are friends. Organisations of one officer befriend: Orga and Will may jointly become friends, with the consent of Alice, provided Orga is an organisation, Orga is not retired, Orga has Alice as an officer, Will is an organisation, Will is not retired, Will has Alice as an officer and Orga and Will know each other.
Guardians:5, Organisation:4. ■ Retire: Those who decide for Orga may retire Orga, and then no one holds any role in Orga and Orga has no one in any role. Orga can then take no act.
Organisation:1. ■ Found by an organisation: Alice may found Orga with Gov in the role Role, with the consent of Gov, and Orga must consent, provided Alice is a person.
Organisation:2. ■ Acts of the organisation. Propose: Bob may propose Motion in Orga, provided Bob is an officer of Orga and the bylaws of Orga entitle Bob to propose motions. Vote: Bob may vote on Motion in Orga, provided Bob is entitled to vote on Motion in Orga, Orga is an organisation and Orga has Motion proposed. Act: Alice may take Motion for Orga, provided Alice is entitled to take Motion for Orga and the votes decide Motion according to the bylaws of Orga.
Organisation:3. ■ Participation. Join: Bob may become a participant of Orga, with the consent of those who admit participants of Orga. Leave: Bob may end his participation in Orga, provided Bob is not an officer of Orga. Officer leave: Bob may end his participation and his office in Orga. Expel: Those who expel participants of Orga may end Bob's participation in Orga, provided Bob is not an officer of Orga. Officer expel: Those who expel participants of Orga may end Bob's participation and Bob's office in Orga. Every role Bob holds in Orga ends with his participation.
Organisation:4. ■ Officers. The decision names his role.
Organisation:5. ■ Amend: Alice may amend the bylaws of Orga as Motion says, provided Alice is entitled to amend the bylaws of Orga as Motion says and the amendment Motion is decided according to the bylaws of Orga.
Organisation:6. ○ Accountability. Orga will be accountable at law for its undertakings where it is a juridical person, and every officer who decided an act of Orga will be accountable for it with Orga.
Notices:1. ■ Notice: Alice may broadcast Cast as a notice of Orga, provided Alice is an officer of Orga and Orga has Alice as an officer.
Notices:2. ■ Notice forward: Alice may forward the notice Cast to Bob in Orga, provided Orga has Alice as a participant, Orga has Bob as a participant, Alice holds the broadcast Cast, Cast is live and Orga has the notice Cast.
