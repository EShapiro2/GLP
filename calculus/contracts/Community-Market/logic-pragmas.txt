Community Market
Community Market unites Community Network, Overlay and Recommendations, and extends and weakens the union (GRH, Grassroots Ride-Hailing, in preparation).
A *seller* is a provider and a *buyer* a customer. A *listing* names a good, its price in community coins and how it is delivered; its terms say what is released to whom on cancellation.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Orga a community network, Gov an organisation, Mark a fiat currency, Listing a listing, Sum, Total, Top, Pot, Back and Rest numbers, Skill a skill or a service; in clause 1 Carol is a buyer, and in clause 5 Skill is the listing.
1. ■ Listing. Post: Bob may post Listing in Orga, provided Bob is a seller of Orga. Bob posts the listing to the buyers he points to. Send listing: Listing of Bob goes to Alice in Orga, provided Bob has Listing posted in Orga, Bob points to Alice and Alice is a buyer of Orga. Pass: Alice may pass Listing of Bob to Carol in Orga, provided Alice is a buyer of Orga, Alice has received Listing of Bob in Orga, Alice points to Carol, Carol is a participant of Gov and Carol accepts a Mark-denominated Gov-coin in payment. Withdraw listing: Bob may withdraw Listing from Orga.
2. ■ Order: Alice may order the good of Listing from Bob in Orga locking Sum and Total Gov-coins, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice is a buyer of Orga, Bob has Listing posted in Orga, Alice and Bob know each other, the terms of Listing set the payment at Sum and the deposit at Total, Orga has the stake at the number S, Bob holds Held Gov-coins, Top is S plus Total and Held is at least Top. Alice's payment and the deposit the terms set for Bob, on top of his stake, are then locked.
3. ◆ Deliver: Alice may attest the delivery of the good of Listing by Bob in Orga releasing to Bob the Sum and Total Gov-coins locked. The payment and the deposit are then released to Bob.
4. ■ Cancel: Alice may cancel her order of Listing from Bob in Orga releasing Back and Rest of the Sum and Total Gov-coins locked, provided the terms of Listing release Back to the customer and Rest to the provider on cancellation, Pot is Sum plus Total and Pot is Back plus Rest. Cancel for the buyer: Alice may cancel her order of Listing from Bob in Orga taking back the Sum and Total Gov-coins locked, provided the terms of Listing release Pot to the customer and Rest to the provider on cancellation, Pot is Sum plus Total and Rest is less than 1. Cancel for the seller: Alice may cancel her order of Listing from Bob in Orga releasing to Bob the Sum and Total Gov-coins locked, provided the terms of Listing release Back to the customer and Pot to the provider on cancellation, Pot is Sum plus Total and Back is less than 1. Seller cancel: Bob may cancel his order of Listing by Alice in Orga releasing Back and Rest of the Sum and Total Gov-coins locked, provided the terms of Listing release Back to the customer and Rest to the provider on cancellation, Pot is Sum plus Total and Pot is Back plus Rest. Seller cancel for the buyer: Bob may cancel his order of Listing by Alice in Orga releasing to Alice the Sum and Total Gov-coins locked, provided the terms of Listing release Pot to the customer and Rest to the provider on cancellation, Pot is Sum plus Total and Rest is less than 1. Seller cancel for the seller: Bob may cancel his order of Listing by Alice in Orga taking back the Sum and Total Gov-coins locked, provided the terms of Listing release Back to the customer and Pot to the provider on cancellation, Pot is Sum plus Total and Back is less than 1. The payment and the deposit are then released to Alice and to Bob as the terms say.
5. ◆ Recommend: Recommendations:1, only if Alice has attested the delivery of the good of Skill by Bob.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: y has l posted in z = posted(y,l,z); x has received l of y in z = received(x,l,y,z); x has ordered the good of l from y = ordered(x,l,y); y has an order of the good of l from x = ordered_from(y,l,x); x has attested the delivery of the good of l by y = delivered(x,l,y); the terms of l set the payment at n and the deposit at m, for x = terms(x,l,n,m); the terms of l release p to the customer and q to the provider on cancellation, for x = cancel_terms(x,l,p,q).
y is a seller of z: y is a provider of z.
x is a buyer of z: x is a customer of z.
x and y have an order of l: x has ordered the good of l from y and y has an order of the good of l from x.
After y posts l in z, which requires y does not have l posted in z: y has l posted in z.
After l of y goes to x in z, which requires x has not received l of y in z: x has received l of y in z.
After x passes l of y to w in z, which requires w has not received l of y in z: w has received l of y in z.
After y withdraws l from z: no longer y has l posted in z.
After x orders the good of l from y in z locking n and m c-coins, which requires x and y do not have an order of l: x and y have an order of l, no longer x holds n c-coins, no longer y holds m c-coins and n and m c-coins are locked in z on l between the provider y and the customer x.
After x attests the delivery of the good of l by y in z releasing to y the n and m c-coins locked: x has attested the delivery of the good of l by y, no longer x and y have an order of l, no longer n and m c-coins are locked in z on l between the provider y and the customer x, y holds n c-coins and y holds m c-coins.
After x cancels x's order of l from y in z releasing p and q of the n and m c-coins locked: no longer x and y have an order of l, no longer n and m c-coins are locked in z on l between the provider y and the customer x, x holds p c-coins and y holds q c-coins.
After y cancels y's order of l by x in z releasing p and q of the n and m c-coins locked: no longer x and y have an order of l, no longer n and m c-coins are locked in z on l between the provider y and the customer x, x holds p c-coins and y holds q c-coins.
After x cancels x's order of l from y in z taking back the n and m c-coins locked: no longer x and y have an order of l, no longer n and m c-coins are locked in z on l between the provider y and the customer x, x holds n c-coins and x holds m c-coins.
After x cancels x's order of l from y in z releasing to y the n and m c-coins locked: no longer x and y have an order of l, no longer n and m c-coins are locked in z on l between the provider y and the customer x, y holds n c-coins and y holds m c-coins.
After y cancels y's order of l by x in z releasing to x the n and m c-coins locked: no longer x and y have an order of l, no longer n and m c-coins are locked in z on l between the provider y and the customer x, x holds n c-coins and x holds m c-coins.
After y cancels y's order of l by x in z taking back the n and m c-coins locked: no longer x and y have an order of l, no longer n and m c-coins are locked in z on l between the provider y and the customer x, y holds n c-coins and y holds m c-coins.
Environment: the terms of l set the payment at n and the deposit at m, for x; the terms of l release p to the customer and q to the provider on cancellation, for x.
