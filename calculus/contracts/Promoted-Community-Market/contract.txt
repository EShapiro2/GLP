Promoted Community Market
Promoted Community Market inherits from Promoted Matching and Community Market.
A post is an *offer* or a *request*. An *offer* is a listing of Community Market, and a *request* names a good and a price in community coins. An offer and a request match when the offer is of what the request seeks and the price of the request is at least the price of the offer. The budget of a post is community coins, set by its maker, and a forward costs one. By the *gossip protocol of equal parts*, a holder forwards a post to all their friends, each with an equal whole part of the rest of the budget. The holder's part is the cost and the remainder of the division.
Alice is a person, Orga a community network.
