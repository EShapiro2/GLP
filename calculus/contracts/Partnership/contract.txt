Partnership
Partnership weakens Corporation.
A *partnership* is a corporation whose bylaws name one role, *partner*, held by every officer; a person joins only with the consent of every partner.
Bob is a person, Orga a partnership.
1. ■ Join: Organisation:3 Join, with the consent of the partners of Orga.
2. ○ Partners' liability. Every partner will be accountable at law for the undertakings of Orga, with Orga.
