Community
Community instantiates Bank with community bylaws, and extends it.
x is a member of z: x is a participant of z.
x is a treasurer of z: x holds the role Treasurer in z.
After x swaps n x-coins for n z-coins: no longer x holds n x-coins, z holds n x-coins, no longer z holds n z-coins and x holds n z-coins.
Community:0 - | A *community* is a bank whose coins are Dollar-denominated. The coins it issues are *community coins*. Its bylaws name two roles, *member*, held by every participant, and *treasurer*.
Community:0 - | Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a community, Sum a number.
Community:1 ■ Draw: ~coin(Alice,Alice,*Sum), coin(Orga,Alice,*Sum), ~coin(Orga,Orga,*Sum), coin(Alice,Orga,*Sum) <- role(Alice,Orga,Treasurer), coin(Alice,Alice,*Sum), coin(Orga,Orga,*Sum) guarded by Alice.
Community:2 ○ | Fiat redemption.
Community:2 ○ | Any treasurer will redeem an Orga-coin Bob holds against one Dollar.
