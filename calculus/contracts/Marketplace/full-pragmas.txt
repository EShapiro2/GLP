Of Knowledge: An *identity* is a unique cryptographic key pair. A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of Friendship: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts.
Of Guardians: Bob *trusts* Orga, an organisation of Alice, when he trusts Alice. The *decision rule*, a parameter of Guardians, says who decides for an organisation and how. An organisation takes an act of a party by those who decide for the organisation. In clause 4 Alice and Bob are possibly one person. Default: any officer of z for those who decide for z.
Of Organisation: An *organisation* is a ward with *participants*, which include its *officers*, each with a *role*. The *bylaws*, a parameter of Organisation, name the roles of an organisation and say who decides each of its acts and how, their own amendment included. Parameters: x is entitled to propose m in z; x is entitled to vote on m in z; x is entitled to take m for z; the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z. Default: those who decide for z for those who decide the officers of z. Default: x is an officer of z for x is entitled to propose m in z. Default: x is an officer of z for x is entitled to vote on m in z. Default: x is an officer of z for x is entitled to take m for z. Default: z holds the votes of at least Votes persons on m, Double is Votes times 2 and z has fewer than Double persons as an officer for the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z.
Of Notes: A *note* includes its issuer's public key, is signed by their private key, and gives its holder a right against its issuer. An *Alice-note* is a note issued by Alice. In clause 2 Carol and Dave are possibly Alice, Bob or one person, in clause 3 Carol is possibly Alice or Bob, and in clause 4 Dave is possibly Alice, Bob or Carol.
Of Coins: A *coin* is a note that is a unit of its issuer's debt. An *Alice-coin* is a coin issued by Alice. Carol is possibly Alice or Bob. Kind of note: coin, y-coins for y-notes; its acts keep their names.
Of Bonds: A *bond* is a note that is a unit of its issuer's debt, due at its maturity date, which it includes. An *Alice-bond* is a bond issued by Alice. A bond is *mature* when its maturity date is at most the current date. Kind of note: bond, y-bonds due Day for y-notes.
Of Denominated Coins: *Dollar* names one denomination, and a *Dollar-denominated Alice-coin* is an Alice-coin issued as Alice's debt of one Dollar.
Of Denominated Currencies: A *Dollar-denominated Alice-bond* is an Alice-bond issued as Alice's debt of one Dollar, due at its maturity date. In clause 4 Carol is possibly Alice or Bob. Environment: the coins and bonds of z and w are of one denomination, for x.
Of Bank: A *bank* is an organisation whose business is issuing coins and bonds against the coins and bonds it holds.
Of Community: A *community currency* is a bank whose coins are Dollar-denominated and whose bylaws name one role, *treasurer*; the treasurers jointly decide its acts.
Of Backed Community: *f*, a fraction 0 ≤ f < 1, is a parameter of Backed Community. Orga's *account* is a bank account in Dollars, held in Orga's name by its treasurers. A treasurer, Alice, is *backed* when the Alice-coins Orga holds are at most the Dollars Alice paid into the account and the coins Alice holds of the other treasurers, leaving out the fraction f of them of whom she holds the most. Environment: every treasurer of z stays backed, for x; x stays backed in z.
Of Networked Organisation: A *networked organisation* is a backed community currency whose bylaws also name the role *arbiter*. A *provider* is a person whose *stake*, the Orga-coins they hold, is at least *S*, a number of Orga-coins, and a *customer* is a person who accepts Orga-coins. A treasurer is a provider. A *dispute* is over Orga-coins locked between a provider and a customer, on *terms* that set the arbiter's fee. In clause 2 Alice is the customer and Bob the provider. Parameters: x has z-coins locked as the customer of y; y has z-coins locked as the provider of x.
Of Marketplace: A *seller* is a provider and a *buyer* a customer. A *listing* names a good, its price in Orga-coins and how it is delivered; its terms say what is released to whom on cancellation. In clause 1 Carol is a buyer, and in clause 5 Skill is the listing.
