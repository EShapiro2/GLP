Bonds
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity once.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ■ Issue.  Alice may issue Alice-notes, for bonds, with maturity later than the current date.
5. ■ Issue bond.  Alice may issue Alice-notes, for bonds, with maturity later than the current date.
6. ■ Swap.  Alice and Bob, who know each other, may jointly swap notes they hold.
7. ■ Swap coin for bond.  Alice and Bob may jointly swap Sum Carol-coins for Total Dave-bonds due Day, provided Alice and Bob know each other.
8. ■ Swap bonds.  Alice and Bob, who know each other, may jointly swap notes they hold.
9. ■ Pay.  Alice may pay notes she holds to Bob, whom she knows, with his consent.
10. ■ Pay issuer.  Alice may pay notes she holds to Bob, whom she knows, with his consent.
11. ■ Pay bond.  Alice may pay notes she holds to Bob, whom she knows, with his consent.
12. ■ Deposit.  Alice may deposit notes with Carol for Bob, on a condition the three agree, with the consent of Carol.
13. ■ Deposit bond.  Alice may deposit notes with Carol for Bob, on a condition the three agree, with the consent of Carol.
14. ■ Release.  Carol and Bob may jointly release them to Bob.
15. ■ Release bond.  Carol and Bob may jointly release them to Bob.
16. ■ Return.  Carol and Alice may jointly return them to Alice.
17. ■ Return bond.  Carol and Alice may jointly return them to Alice.
18. ■ Redeem.  Alice may redeem a Bob-coin she holds against any coin held by Bob, whom she knows.
19. ■ Redeem coin against bond.  Alice may redeem a Bob-coin, or a mature Bob-bond, she holds against any coin or bond held by Bob, whom she knows.
20. ■ Redeem bond.  Alice may redeem a Bob-coin, or a mature Bob-bond, she holds against any coin or bond held by Bob, whom she knows.
21. ■ Redeem bond against bond.  Alice may redeem a Bob-coin, or a mature Bob-bond, she holds against any coin or bond held by Bob, whom she knows.
