Bonds
Bonds inherits from Coins.
A *bond* is a note that is a unit of its issuer's debt, due at its maturity date, which it includes. An *Alice-bond* is a bond issued by Alice. A bond is *mature* when its maturity date is at most the current date.
Alice and Bob are parties.
1. ■ Redeem: Alice may redeem a Bob-coin, or a mature Bob-bond, she holds against any coin or bond held by Bob, whom she knows.
