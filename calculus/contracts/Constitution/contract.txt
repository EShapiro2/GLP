Constitution
Constitution extends Organisation, and augments it.
A *constitutional democratic community* is an organisation whose bylaws name one role, *member*, held by every participant, and are *egalitarian*: the rule by which the members decide an act names no member and no other role.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a constitutional democratic community.
1. ■ Join: Organisation:3, only if the members decide it.
2. ■ Leave, Expel: Organisation:3, only if Bob or the members decide it.
3. ■ Amend: Organisation:5, only if the members decide it, and the amended bylaws are egalitarian.
