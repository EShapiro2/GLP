Constitution
Constitution restricts and strengthens Organisation.
x is a member of z: x is a participant of z.
Vocabulary: m keeps the bylaws of z egalitarian = egalitarian(z,m).
Constitution:0 - | A *constitutional democratic community* is an organisation whose bylaws name one role, *member*, held by every participant, and are *egalitarian*: the rule by which the members decide an act names no member and no other role.
Constitution:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Orga a constitutional democratic community, Motion an act of Orga, Votes and Double numbers.
Constitution:0 - | Environment: m keeps the bylaws of z egalitarian.
Constitution:1 ■ Join: Organisation:3 Join, on the votes of Orga <- vote_join_orga(Orga,>=Votes,Bob), product(Double,Votes,2), has_participant(Orga,<Double).
Constitution:1 ■ Vote of Orga for Join: vote_join_orga(Orga,Carol,Bob) <- voter_join_orga(Carol,Orga,Bob), knows(Orga,Bob), participant(Carol,Orga), ~vote_join_orga(Orga,Carol,Bob) guarded by Carol.
