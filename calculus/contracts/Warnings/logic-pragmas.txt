Warnings
Warnings extends Organisation, and weakens it.
A *warning* is a record in an organisation, made by one of its officers, that a participant broke its bylaws or an undertaking.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga an organisation.
1. ■ Warn: Alice may warn Bob in Orga, provided Alice is an officer of Orga and Orga has Bob as a participant.
2. ■ Expel: Organisation:3 Expel, only if Orga holds the warnings of at least 2 persons to Bob. Officer expel: Organisation:3 Officer expel, only if Orga holds the warnings of at least 2 persons to Bob.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: z holds x's warning to y = warning(z,x,y).
After x warns y in z, which requires z does not hold x's warning to y: z holds x's warning to y.
