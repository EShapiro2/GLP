Warnings
Warnings extends Organisation, and weakens it.
A *warning* is a record in an organisation, made by one of its officers, that a participant broke its bylaws or an undertaking; it names the incident. An officer holds at most one warning to a participant at a time, and the participant may answer it once.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga an organisation.
1. ■ Warn: Alice may warn Bob in Orga of an incident, provided Alice is an officer of Orga and Orga has Bob as a participant.
2. ■ Answer: Bob may answer a warning to him.
3. ■ Withdrawal. Withdraw: Alice may withdraw her warning. Annul: Those who decide for Orga may annul a warning.
4. ■ Expel: Organisation:3 Expel, only if two officers of Orga hold their warnings to Bob. Officer expel: Organisation:3 Officer expel, only if two officers of Orga hold their warnings to Bob.
