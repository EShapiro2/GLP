Warnings
Warnings extends and restricts Organisation.
A *warning* is a record in an organisation, made by one of its officers, that a participant broke its bylaws or an undertaking.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga an organisation.
1. ■ Warn: Alice may warn Bob in Orga, provided Alice is an officer of Orga and Orga has Bob as a participant.
2. ■ Expel: Organisation:3 Expel, only if two officers of Orga have warned Bob. Officer expel: Organisation:3 Officer expel, only if two officers of Orga have warned Bob.
