Rides
Rides extends Community Network and restricts and strengthens Second-Score Auction (GRH, Grassroots Ride-Hailing, in preparation).
A *driver* is a provider and a *rider* a customer.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a community network, Gov an organisation.
1. ■ Award: Second-Score Auction:2 Award, only if Alice is a customer of Orga, the fare and the deposit of Alice's call Trip are in Gov-coins, Alice holds the fare of Alice's call Trip in Gov-coins and Bob holds the deposit of Alice's call Trip on top of Bob's stake in Orga, and then Bob has coins locked in Orga as the provider of Alice and Alice has coins locked in Orga as the customer of Bob. Arrive: Auction:5, and then no longer Bob has coins locked in Orga as the provider of Alice and no longer Alice has coins locked in Orga as the customer of Bob. Cancel: Auction:6, and then no longer Bob has coins locked in Orga as the provider of Alice and no longer Alice has coins locked in Orga as the customer of Bob.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: the fare and the deposit of x's call c are in z-coins = in_coins(x,z,c); x holds the fare of x's call c = holds_fare(x,c); y holds the deposit of x's call c = holds_deposit(y,x,c).
x holds the fare of x's call c in z-coins: x holds the fare of x's call c and x holds a z-coin.
y holds the deposit of x's call c on top of y's stake in z: y holds the deposit of x's call c and y is a provider of z.
Environment: the fare and the deposit of x's call c are in z-coins; x holds the fare of x's call c; y holds the deposit of x's call c.
