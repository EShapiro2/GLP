Backchannel Feeds
Backchannel Feeds extends Resilient Feeds.
Vocabulary: z has received the item t of w's feed back from x = received_back(z,t,w,x).
After x forwards the item t of w's feed to z, which requires z does not hold the item t of w's feed: z holds the item t of w's feed and z has received the item t of w's feed back from x.
Backchannel Feeds:0 - | Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Item an item, Bob and Dave possibly one person.
Backchannel Feeds:1 ■ Back forward: item(Carol,Item,Dave), received_back(Carol,Item,Dave,Alice) <- pointer(Alice,Bob,Dave), pointer(Alice,Carol,Dave), item(Alice,Item,Dave), received(Alice,Item,Dave,Bob), ~item(Carol,Item,Dave) possibly Bob=Dave.
