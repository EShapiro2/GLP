Liquid Cooperative
Liquid Cooperative extends Cooperative DAO.
A member's *delegate* is another member whose vote counts for theirs.
Bob and Carol are persons.
1. ■ Delegating. Delegate: Bob may make Carol his delegate in Orga, provided Bob is a member of Orga, Carol is a member of Orga and Bob knows Carol. Revoke: Bob may revoke his delegation to Carol in Orga.
2. ■ Count: Orga counts Bob as voting on Motion, provided Bob is entitled to vote on Motion in Orga, Orga is an organisation, Orga has Motion proposed, Orga holds the delegation of Bob to Carol and Orga holds Carol's vote on Motion. Count expel: Orga counts Bob as voting on whether Orga ends Dave's participation in Orga, provided Bob is entitled to vote on whether Orga ends Dave's participation in Orga in Orga, Orga holds the delegation of Bob to Carol and Orga holds Carol's vote on whether Orga ends Dave's participation in Orga. Count officer expel: Orga counts Bob as voting on whether Orga ends Dave's participation and Dave's office in Orga, provided Bob is entitled to vote on whether Orga ends Dave's participation and Dave's office in Orga in Orga, Orga holds the delegation of Bob to Carol and Orga holds Carol's vote on whether Orga ends Dave's participation and Dave's office in Orga. Count amend: Orga counts Bob as voting on whether Alice amends the bylaws of Orga as Motion says, provided Bob is entitled to vote on whether Alice amends the bylaws of Orga as Motion says in Orga, Orga holds the delegation of Bob to Carol and Orga holds Carol's vote on whether Alice amends the bylaws of Orga as Motion says. A member who has not voted on a proposal counts as voting as their delegate did, down a chain of delegates, and can no longer vote on it.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: y has a delegate in z = delegating(y,z); z holds the delegation of y to x = delegation(z,y,x).
After y makes x y's delegate in z, which requires y does not have a delegate in z: y has a delegate in z and z holds the delegation of y to x.
After y revokes y's delegation to x in z: no longer y has a delegate in z and no longer z holds the delegation of y to x.
After z counts y as voting on m, which requires z does not hold y's vote on m: z holds y's vote on m.
After z counts y as voting on whether z ends w's participation in z, which requires z does not hold y's vote on whether z ends w's participation in z: z holds y's vote on whether z ends w's participation in z.
After z counts y as voting on whether z ends w's participation and w's office in z, which requires z does not hold y's vote on whether z ends w's participation and w's office in z: z holds y's vote on whether z ends w's participation and w's office in z.
After z counts y as voting on whether x amends the bylaws of z as m says, which requires z does not hold y's vote on whether x amends the bylaws of z as m says: z holds y's vote on whether x amends the bylaws of z as m says.
