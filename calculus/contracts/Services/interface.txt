Services
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity once.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ■ Befriend.  Alice and Bob, who trust each other and are not friends, may jointly become friends.
5. ■ Person unfriend.  Alice may end her friendship with Bob.
6. ■ Enter.  Alice may create a ward, Carol, and establish Carol's identity by choosing Carol's key pair, and Carol must consent.
7. ■ Make guardian.  Those who decide for Carol may make Bob, a friend of a guardian of Carol, a guardian and a friend of Carol, with the consent of Bob.
8. ■ Resign.  Bob may end his guardianship of Carol.
9. ■ End guardian.  Those who decide for Carol, other than Bob, may end Bob's guardianship.
10. ■ Guardian unfriend.  Bob may end his friendship with Carol, which ends his guardianship.
11. ■ Ward acquaint.  Carol, a ward of Alice, and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
12. ■ Ward befriend.  Carol may enter a relation with Bob with the consent of Bob and Alice, provided Carol and Bob know each other and Alice and Bob are friends.
13. ■ Ward end.  Carol, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Carol.
14. ■ Unfriend.  Carol, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Carol.
15. ■ Ward unfriend.  Carol, Bob or Alice may end the relation of Bob and Carol.
16. ■ Wards acquaint.  Carol, a ward of Alice, and Dave, a ward of Bob, may jointly come to know each other.
17. ■ Wards befriend.  Carol may enter a relation with Dave with the consent of Dave, Alice and Bob, provided Carol and Dave know each other, Alice knows Dave and is a friend of Bob, Alice and Bob possibly one person.
18. ■ Wards of one guardian befriend.  Carol may enter a relation with Dave with the consent of Dave, Alice and Bob, provided Carol and Dave know each other, Alice knows Dave and is a friend of Bob, Alice and Bob possibly one person.
19. ■ Retire.  Those who decide for Carol may retire Carol; every guardianship and relation of Carol then ends and Carol can take no act.
