Emergencies
Emergencies inherits from Broadcast and Organisation.
A *personal emergency* is a broadcast that includes the location of its maker, a description and the help its maker asks; it ceases to be live when its maker resolves it. A *village emergency* is a broadcast that includes instructions. A *resolution* of a personal emergency and an *answer* to it are broadcasts.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga an organisation.
1. ■ Personal emergency: Alice may broadcast a personal emergency, provided she has no live personal emergency of her own.
2. ■ Village emergency: Alice, an officer of Orga, may broadcast a village emergency.
3. ■ Emergency forward: Alice, a participant of Orga, may forward a live village emergency of Orga that she holds to Bob, a participant of Orga, who does not hold it; Bob then holds it.
4. ■ Resolve: Alice may broadcast the resolution of her personal emergency.
5. ■ Answer: Bob, who holds a live personal emergency, may broadcast an answer to it that says the help he will give.
6. ○ Help. Bob will give the help he answered.
