Emergencies
Emergencies unites Broadcast and Organisation, and extends the union.
Vocabulary: x has broadcast the personal emergency m = personal_emergency(x,m); x has broadcast the village emergency m of z = village_emergency(x,m,z); z has the village emergency m = village_emergency_of(z,m); z has the village emergency m with y = village_emergency_with(z,m,y); x has no live personal emergency = no_live_emergency(x); x has resolved m by k = resolved(x,m,k); y has answered m by k = emergency_answer(y,m,k).
After x broadcasts m as a personal emergency, which requires x has not made the broadcast m: x has made the broadcast m, x holds the broadcast m and x has broadcast the personal emergency m.
After x broadcasts m as a village emergency of z, which requires x has not made the broadcast m: x has made the broadcast m, x holds the broadcast m, x has broadcast the village emergency m of z and z has the village emergency m.
After x forwards the village emergency m to y in z, which requires y does not hold the broadcast m and x has not forwarded the broadcast m to y: y holds the broadcast m, y holds the broadcast m from x, x has forwarded the broadcast m to y and z has the village emergency m with y.
After x broadcasts k as the resolution of m, which requires x has not made the broadcast k and x has not resolved m by someone: x has made the broadcast k, x holds the broadcast k and x has resolved m by k.
After y broadcasts k as an answer to m, which requires y has not made the broadcast k: y has made the broadcast k, y holds the broadcast k and y has answered m by k.
Emergencies:0 - | A *personal emergency* is a broadcast that includes the location of its maker, a description and the help its maker asks; it ceases to be live when its maker resolves it. A *village emergency* is a broadcast that includes instructions. A *resolution* of a personal emergency and an *answer* to it are broadcasts.
Emergencies:0 - | Alice and Bob are persons, Orga an organisation, Cast and Reply broadcasts, Call a personal emergency.
Emergencies:0 - | Environment: x has no live personal emergency.
