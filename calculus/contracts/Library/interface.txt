Library
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity once.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ◆ Shelve.  Alice may put a thing of her own on her shelf, under a new name of hers.
5. ■ Lend.  Alice may lend Bob a thing from her shelf until a day later than the current date, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other; it then leaves the shelf, and Bob holds it.
6. ■ Return.  Bob may return a borrowed thing; it then goes back on Alice's shelf.
