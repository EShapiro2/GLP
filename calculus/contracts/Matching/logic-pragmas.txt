Matching
Matching extends Gossip.
Posts have a *test*, a parameter of Matching, whether two *match*.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Post and Other posts.
1. ■ Return: Bob forwards Other to Alice, provided Bob holds the post Post from Alice, Bob holds the post Other and Post and Other match.
2. ■ Meet: Alice and Carol may jointly come to know each other, provided Alice has made the post Post, Carol has made the post Other, Alice holds the post Other, Carol holds the post Post and Post and Other match.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: p and q match, for x = match(x,p,q).
After x forwards p to y, which requires y does not hold the post p from x: y holds the post p from x and y holds the post p.
Environment: p and q match, for x.
