Matching
Of Knowledge: An *identity* is a unique cryptographic key pair. A *party* to a contract is whoever has an identity, and a *person* is a party who established their own identity. Reflexive: x knows y.
Of Friendship: Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts.
Of Matching: *Posts* have a *test*, a parameter of Matching, whether two *match*. Posts have a *budget*, a parameter of Matching: what a post has when made, and the *cost* of a forward. A person *holds* any post they made or forwarded to them. Environment: p and q match, for x; n is a budget of a new post, for x; n covers the cost of a forward leaving m, for x.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Post and Other posts, Budget and Rest numbers.
Knowledge:1. ○ Establish: Alice may establish an identity. Alice will make herself known by her public key, sign her acts with her private key, and be accountable for every act signed with it.
Knowledge:2. ■ Acquaint: Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
Knowledge:3. ■ Introduce: Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
Friendship:1. ○ Befriend: Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other. Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
Friendship:2. ■ Unfriend: Alice may end her friendship with Bob.
Friendship:3. ○ Loss of Trust. Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
Matching:1. ■ Post: Alice may make Post with the budget Budget, provided Budget is a budget of a new post.
Matching:2. ■ Tell: Alice may forward Post to Bob with the budget Rest, provided Alice holds the post Post, Alice has the budget Budget for Post, Budget covers the cost of a forward leaving Rest and Alice and Bob are friends.
Matching:3. ■ Return: Bob forwards Other to Alice, provided Bob holds the post Post from Alice, Bob holds the post Other and Post and Other match.
Matching:4. ■ Withdraw: Alice may withdraw Post.
Matching:5. ■ Meet: Alice and Carol may jointly come to know each other, provided Alice has made the post Post, Carol has made the post Other, Alice holds the post Other, Carol holds the post Post and Post and Other match.
