Matching
Matching extends Gossip.
Posts have a *test*, a parameter of Matching, whether two *match*.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
1. ■ Return: Bob, who holds two matching posts, one from Alice, forwards to Alice the second post.
2. ■ Meet: Alice and Carol, both holding matching posts one made by Alice and one made by Carol, may jointly come to know each other.
