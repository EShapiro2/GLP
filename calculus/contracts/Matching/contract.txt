Matching
Matching extends Friendship.
*Posts* have a test whether two *match*. A person *holds* any post they made or forwarded to them.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
1. ■ Post: Alice may make a post.
2. ■ Tell: Alice who holds a post may forward it to her friend Bob.
3. ■ Return: Bob, who holds two matching posts, one from Alice, forwards to Alice the second post.
4. ■ Withdraw: Alice may withdraw a post she made.
5. ■ Meet: Alice and Carol, both holding matching posts one made by Alice and one made by Carol, may jointly come to know each other.
