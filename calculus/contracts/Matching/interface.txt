Matching
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity once.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ○ Befriend.  Alice and Bob, who trust each other and are not friends, may jointly become friends.
5. ■ Unfriend.  Alice may end her friendship with Bob.
6. ■ Post.  Alice may make Post with the budget Budget, provided Budget is a budget of a new post.
7. ■ Tell.  Alice may forward Post to Bob with the budget Part, provided Alice holds the post Post, Alice has the budget Budget for Post, Budget covers the cost of a forward leaving Rest, Alice and Bob are friends and the gossip protocol forwards Post to Bob with Part of Rest.
8. ■ Return.  Alice may make a post.
9. ■ Meet.  Alice who holds a post, the budget of which covers the cost, may forward it to her friend Bob with the rest of the budget.
