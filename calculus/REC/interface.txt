REC: Recommendations
1. ○ Establish.  Alice may establish an identity.
2. ■ Acquaint.  Alice and Bob may jointly come to know each other.
3. ■ Introduce.  Alice may introduce Carol to Bob, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol.
4. ◆ Recommend.  Alice may recommend Bob for Skill, provided Alice knows Bob.
5. ■ Show.  Bob may show Carol Alice's recommendation for Skill, provided Bob holds Alice's recommendation for Skill and Bob knows Carol.
6. ■ Withdraw.  Alice may withdraw her recommendation of Bob for Skill.
