GFF-FR: Fault-Resilient GFF
GFF-FR extends GFF.
D > 1, an integer, is a parameter of GFF-FR.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
1. ■ Redundant point: Alice adds a Carol-pointer to Bob, provided Alice has a Carol-pointer to someone, Alice has a Carol-pointer to fewer than D persons, Alice and Bob are friends and Bob has a Carol-pointer to someone.
