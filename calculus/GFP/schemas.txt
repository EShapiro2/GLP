GFP: Grassroots Forwarding with Provenance
GFP extends GSG.
Vocabulary: x holds an item of y = item(x,y).
After x creates an item by signing it: x holds an item of x.
After x forwards an item of z to y: y holds an item of z.
GFP:0 - | The *author* of an item is its first signer.
GFP:0 - | Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Carol possibly Alice or Bob.
GFP:1 ■ Sign: item(Alice,Alice) <- guarded by Alice.
GFP:2 ■ Forward: item(Bob,Carol) <- item(Alice,Carol), friend(Alice,Bob), friend(Bob,Alice) guarded by Alice possibly Alice=Carol, Bob=Carol.
GFP:2 ■ | Alice adds her signature to the signatures the item carries.
