GFP: Grassroots Forwarding with Provenance
GFP extends GSG.
The *author* of an item is its first signer.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Carol possibly Alice or Bob.
1. ■ Sign: Alice may create an item by signing it.
2. ■ Forward: Alice may forward an item of Carol to Bob, provided Alice holds an item of Carol and Alice and Bob are friends. Alice adds her signature to the signatures the item carries.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x holds an item of y = item(x,y).
After x creates an item by signing it: x holds an item of x.
After x forwards an item of z to y: y holds an item of z.
