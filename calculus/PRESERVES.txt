What each relation of the Calculus carries from its base to the contract written from it (EShapiro2/GLP#100).

A property here is a safety property, a set of runs, each a sequence of the participants' states: a contract has it
when every run of the contract is in the set (Alpern and Schneider, "Defining Liveness", Information Processing
Letters 21:181-185, 1985).  A property proved of a base once carries along every relation marked "carries", with no
new proof; along the others it is proved again.

restricts      carries.  Each act of the contract is an act of the base with conditions or guards added, so every
               run of the contract is a run of the base, and every safety property of the base holds of it.  In the
               theory of organisational forms it is a subform, a sharper code, every member of which is a member of
               the form (Polos, Hannan and Carroll, "Foundations of a Theory of Social Forms", ERIM Report
               ERS-2000-29-ORG, 2000, Definition 15 and Theorem 3; later Industrial and Corporate Change 11(1), 2002).  It is no behavioural subtype of Liskov and
               Wing, whose rules never let a subtype strengthen a precondition ("A Behavioral Notion of Subtyping",
               TOPLAS 16(6):1811-1841, 1994, Definitions 1 and 2).

strengthens    carries, on the states of the base.  The acts' added effects are of states the base does not have,
               and the conditions added are on those states, so the runs of the contract, its own states left out,
               are runs of the base.

extends        carries, on the states of the base, where no new act writes a state of the base: the contract is a
  conservative extension subtype of Liskov and Wing (Section 6.1, the new methods with empty explanations), and a
               conservative extension in CASL's sense (CoFI, CASL Summary v1.0.1, 2001, Section 6.1.4).
               tools/relations.py --conservative prints which extensions are so.

extends        does not carry until checked: a new act changes a state of the base, so a run of the contract need
  base-changing not be a run of the base.  Liskov and Wing's extension rule is the check: each new act's effect on
               the states of the base is that of a sequence of acts of the base (Definition 2), as Stays' Book moves
               coins as Coins' Pay issuer does.

relaxes        does not carry: an act loses a condition or a guard, so the base's runs are runs of the contract and
               not the other way; a safety property of the contract holds of the base.

instantiates   does not carry until checked: CASL's instantiation of a generic specification through a fitting
               morphism (Section 6.2), the parameter's words put for the parameter; a property that names the
               parameter is a different property after the map.

composes       does not carry until checked: CASL's union of specifications (Section 6.1); an act both contracts
               have from one contract they are written from is one act, with the conditions of both.

CASL's models are algebras and not runs; reading a model as a run of a contract is an analogy, stated as one.
