GAS: Grassroots App Store
GAS extends GSA.
Alice, Carol and Dave are persons.  A store is a list of programs its owner keeps under a name of their own.
1. ■ Store.  Carol may set up a store under a name of her own.
2. ■ Listing.  Carol may list in her store a program offered to her by a developer she trusts.
3. ■ Store Trust.  Alice may trust Carol's store, Carol being known to her, and may withdraw her trust.
4. ■ Installation from a Store.  Alice may install a program listed in a store she trusts, as long as the store's owner trusts its developer.
Pragmas
Stores: the stores Alice trusts.
Listings: the programs listed in Alice's store.
Store: Carol [Open store] <name>
Listing: Carol [List]
Store Trust: Alice [Trust store] [Distrust store]
Installation from a Store: Alice [Install]
