GPD: Grassroots Programs Dissemination
GPD extends GFP (GSC, From Grassroots Contract to Grassroots App, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06231).
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.  A program is an item that is a grassroots contract with its speech-act schemas and pragmas; its developer is its author.
1. ◆ Program Publication.  Carol may publish a program by signing it, certifying that its schemas say what its clauses say.
2. ■ Developer Trust.  Alice may trust Carol, whom she knows, as a developer, and may withdraw her trust.
3. ■ Program Offering.  Carol may offer a program she holds to her friend Alice by forwarding it to her.
4. ■ Program Installation.  Alice may install a program she published, as well as a program offered to her whose developer she trusts.
5. ■ Program Invitation.  Alice may invite her friend Bob to join her in a program she installed, with Bob's consent, Bob trusting its developer; Bob then has the program installed, with Alice's signature added, and each is a participant of the program with the other.
6. ■ Leaving a Program.  Alice may leave a program she installed; it is then no longer installed, and her participation in it with every participant ends.
Pragmas
Programs: the programs Alice installed.
Developers: the developers Alice trusts.
Offers: the programs offered to Alice.
