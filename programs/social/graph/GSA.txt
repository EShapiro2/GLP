GSA: Grassroots Super-App and App Store
GSA extends GSG (GSC, From Grassroots Contract to Grassroots App, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06231).
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.  A program is a grassroots contract with its speech-act schemas and pragmas, published under a name of its developer's, the person who publishes it, and compiled from them into the code its participants run.
1. ◆ Program Publication.  Carol may publish a program under a name of her own, certifying that its schemas say what its clauses say.
2. ■ Developer Trust.  Alice may trust Carol, whom she knows, as a developer, and may withdraw her trust.
3. ■ Program Offering.  Carol may offer a program she published to Alice, whom she knows.
4. ■ Program Installation.  Alice may install a program she published, as well as a program offered to her by a developer she trusts.
5. ■ Program Invitation.  Alice may invite her friend Bob to join her in a program she installed, with Bob's consent, Bob trusting its developer; Bob then has the program installed, and each is a participant of the program with the other.
6. ■ Leaving a Program.  Alice may leave a program she installed; it is then no longer installed, and her participation in it with every participant ends.
Pragmas
Programs: the programs Alice installed.
Developers: the developers Alice trusts.
Offers: the programs offered to Alice.
Program Publication: Carol [Publish] <program>
Developer Trust: Alice [Trust] [Distrust]
Program Offering: Carol [Offer]
Program Installation: Alice [Install own] [Install]
Program Invitation: Alice [Invite]
  Bob: Alice invites you to join her in a program.
Leaving a Program: Alice [Leave]
