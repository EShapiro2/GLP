GPE: Grassroots Physical Encounters
GPE extends GKK (GSC, From Grassroots Contract to Grassroots App, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06231).
Alice and Bob are persons.  A person is nearby Alice when her phone reaches theirs by Bluetooth or the local network, without the Internet; a place is where Alice is.
1. ■ Open Place.  Alice may declare the place she is in open, and may close it.
2. ■ Encounter.  Alice and Bob, nearby each other, who have both declared the place open, may know each other.
Interface pragmas
Nearby: the persons nearby whom Alice knows.
Open Place: Alice [Open place] [Close place]
Implementation pragmas
Open Place: while the place is open Alice's app sets the trust of Bluetooth and of the local network open, and closed otherwise (GNA).
Nearby: an environment predicate, nearby(Alice, Bob), the persons the networking layer reports nearby, which Alice's app keeps current.
Encounter: Alice stands on [Open place] until [Close place]; the two phones exchange keys (GNA, open trust).
