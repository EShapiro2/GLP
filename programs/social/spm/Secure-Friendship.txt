Secure Friendship
Secure Friendship extends Friendship.
The *identity record* of Alice is her *identity custodians*, persons she names, and her *threshold*, a share of them greater than half. The *records* of Alice are her friends and, for each friend, the friends of that friend. Alice suffers a *state loss* when her machine loses some or all of its state and she keeps her private key, and an *identity loss* when she loses her private key.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Alicia a new identity of Alice.
1. ■ Identity record. Alice may name Carol, whom she knows, her identity custodian, and Carol records that she is; then Alice may fix her identity record, once, with her threshold, more than half of her identity custodians. It then changes only by the replacement of a custodian's lost identity (Secure Friendship:5).
2. ■ Friendship updates. Tell: Alice tells each of her friends when she becomes a friend of Bob (Friendship:1) and when she ends a friendship (Friendship:2), and the friend records it. Send record: once Alice and Bob are friends and Alice has fixed her identity record, Alice sends Bob, once, her friends and her identity record, her custodians and her threshold, and Bob keeps them.
3. ■ Checkpoint. Alice may send each of her friends a checkpoint of her records and her identity record, and the friend keeps it in place of any earlier checkpoint from Alice.
4. ■ State recovery. Restore: After a state loss of Alice, which her machine records, each friend of Alice sends her, once for that loss, what he holds of her: her records, her identity record and the friendship updates she told him, and tells her if she is his identity custodian. Alice records them again, keeping for each friendship the latest she is sent.
5. ◆ Identity replacement. Replace: After an identity loss, Alice may establish a new identity, Alicia (Knowledge:1), and ask her identity custodians to vouch for it. Vouch: Carol, an identity custodian of Alice, may vouch, once, after she checks that Alicia is Alice, and sends her vouch to Alicia, with the friends of Alice she records if she is a friend of Alice. Present: Alicia shows the vouches she holds to each friend of Alice she knows. Rebind: once Bob, a friend of Alice, has been shown vouches from at least the threshold of the identity custodians he records for Alice, he takes Alicia in place of Alice, once: Alicia is then his friend in place of Alice, and his identity custodian if Alice was. Bob then sends Alicia what he holds of Alice, as in Restore (Secure Friendship:4), and tells his friends of the change.
Logic pragmas
Secure Friendship extends Friendship.
The *identity record* of Alice is her *identity custodians*, persons she names, and her *threshold*, a share of them greater than half. The *records* of Alice are her friends and, for each friend, the friends of that friend. Alice suffers a *state loss* when her machine loses some or all of its state and she keeps her private key, and an *identity loss* when she loses her private key.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Alicia a new identity of Alice, Share and Double numbers.
1. ■ Name custodian: Alice may name Carol her identity custodian, provided Alice knows Carol and Alice does not have a fixed identity record. Identity record: Alice may fix her identity record with the threshold Share, provided Alice has at least Share persons as an identity custodian, Double is Share times 2 and Alice has fewer than Double persons as an identity custodian. It then changes only by the replacement of a custodian's lost identity (Secure Friendship:5).
2. ■ Friendship updates. Tell: Alice tells Carol of the friendship of Alice and Bob, provided Alice and Carol are friends and Alice and Bob are friends. Tell end: Alice tells Carol of the end of the friendship of Alice and Bob, provided Alice and Carol are friends and Alice and Bob are not friends. Send record: Alice sends Bob the identity custodian Carol, provided Alice and Bob are friends, Alice has a fixed identity record and Alice has Carol as an identity custodian.
3. ■ Checkpoint: Alice sends Bob a checkpoint of Alice's friends and Alice's identity record, provided Alice and Bob are friends. Bob keeps it.
4. ■ State recovery. Restore: Bob restores the records of Alice, provided Alice has suffered a state loss, Bob counts Alice as a friend and Bob keeps a checkpoint of Alice.
5. ◆ Identity replacement. Replace: Alicia may ask Carol to vouch that Alicia is Alice, provided Alicia knows Carol and Carol is an identity custodian of Alice. Alicia asks only after an identity loss of Alice. Vouch: Carol may vouch that Alicia is Alice, provided Carol has been asked to vouch that Alicia is Alice and Carol is an identity custodian of Alice. Accept: Bob accepts Alicia in place of Alice on the vouch of Carol, provided Bob records Carol as an identity custodian of Alice, Carol has vouched that Alicia is Alice and at least the threshold of the identity custodians Bob records for Alice have vouched that Alicia is Alice. Accept custodian: Bob accepts Alicia as an identity custodian in place of Alice, provided Bob has accepted Alicia in place of Alice on the vouch of Carol. Bob tells Alicia the friends of Alice he records, and tells his friends of the change.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x has z as an identity custodian = custodian(x,z); z is an identity custodian of x = custodian_of(z,x); x has a fixed identity record = fixed(x); x has the threshold n = threshold(x,n); z records the friendship of x and y = records_friend(z,x,y); y records z as an identity custodian of x = records_custodian(y,x,z); y keeps a checkpoint of x = checkpoint(y,x); x has suffered a state loss = state_loss(x); x has recovered records from y = recovered(x,y); z has been asked to vouch that w is x = asked(z,w,x); z has vouched that w is x = vouched(z,w,x); at least the threshold of the identity custodians y records for x have vouched that w is x = threshold_vouched(y,x,w); y has accepted w in place of x on the vouch of c = accepted(y,w,x,c).
After x names z x's identity custodian, which requires x does not have z as an identity custodian: x has z as an identity custodian and z is an identity custodian of x.
After x fixes x's identity record with the threshold n, which requires x does not have a fixed identity record: x has a fixed identity record and x has the threshold n.
After x tells z of the friendship of x and y, which requires z does not record the friendship of x and y: z records the friendship of x and y.
After x tells z of the end of the friendship of x and y: no longer z records the friendship of x and y.
After x sends y the identity custodian z, which requires y does not record z as an identity custodian of x: y records z as an identity custodian of x.
After x sends y a checkpoint of x's friends and x's identity record: y keeps a checkpoint of x.
After y restores the records of x, which requires x does not count y as a friend: x counts y as a friend and x has recovered records from y.
After w asks z to vouch that w is x, which requires z has not been asked to vouch that w is x: z has been asked to vouch that w is x.
After z vouches that w is x, which requires z has not vouched that w is x: z has vouched that w is x.
After y accepts w in place of x on the vouch of c, which requires y has not accepted w in place of x on the vouch of anyone: no longer y counts x as a friend, y counts w as a friend, w counts y as a friend and y has accepted w in place of x on the vouch of c.
After y accepts w as an identity custodian in place of x: no longer y has x as an identity custodian, no longer x is an identity custodian of y, y has w as an identity custodian and w is an identity custodian of y.
Environment: x has suffered a state loss; at least the threshold of the identity custodians y records for x have vouched that w is x.
