Secure Friendship
Secure Friendship extends Friendship.
The *identity record* of Alice is her *identity custodians*, persons she names, and her *threshold*, a share of them greater than half. The *records* of Alice are her friends and, for each friend, the friends of that friend. Alice suffers a *state loss* when her machine loses some or all of its state and she keeps her private key, and an *identity loss* when she loses her private key.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Alicia a new identity of Alice.
1. ■ Identity record: Alice may fix her identity record, once. It then changes only by the replacement of a custodian's lost identity (Secure Friendship:5).
2. ■ Friendship updates. Tell: Alice tells each of her friends when she becomes a friend of Bob (Friendship:1) and when she ends a friendship (Friendship:2), and the friend records it. Send record: Alice sends Bob, when they become friends, her friends and her identity record, and Bob keeps them.
3. ■ Checkpoint: Alice sends each of her friends a checkpoint of her friends and her identity record, and the friend keeps it.
4. ■ State recovery. Restore: After a state loss of Alice, each friend of Alice sends her the friendship updates and the checkpoints they keep, and Alice records them again.
5. ◆ Identity replacement. Replace: After an identity loss, Alice may establish a new identity, Alicia (Knowledge:1), and ask her identity custodians to vouch for it. Vouch: Carol, an identity custodian of Alice, may vouch, once, that Alicia is Alice. Accept: Bob, a friend of Alice, accepts Alicia in place of Alice once at least the threshold of the identity custodians he records for Alice have vouched; Alicia is then his friend in place of Alice, and his identity custodian if Alice was. Bob tells Alicia the friends of Alice he records, and tells his friends of the change.
Logic pragmas
Secure Friendship extends Friendship.
The *identity record* of Alice is her *identity custodians*, persons she names, and her *threshold*, a share of them greater than half. The *records* of Alice are her friends and, for each friend, the friends of that friend. Alice suffers a *state loss* when her machine loses some or all of its state and she keeps her private key, and an *identity loss* when she loses her private key.
Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Alicia a new identity of Alice, Share and Double numbers.
1. ■ Name custodian: Alice may name Carol her identity custodian, provided Alice knows Carol and Alice does not have a fixed identity record. Identity record: Alice may fix her identity record with the threshold Share, provided Alice has at least Share persons as an identity custodian, Double is Share times 2 and Alice has fewer than Double persons as an identity custodian. It then changes only by the replacement of a custodian's lost identity (Secure Friendship:5).
2. ■ Friendship updates. Tell: Alice tells Carol of the friendship of Alice and Bob, provided Alice and Carol are friends and Alice and Bob are friends. Tell end: Alice tells Carol of the end of the friendship of Alice and Bob, provided Alice and Carol are friends and Alice and Bob are not friends. Send record: Alice sends Bob the identity custodian Carol, provided Alice and Bob are friends, Alice has a fixed identity record and Alice has Carol as an identity custodian. Send threshold: Alice sends Bob the threshold Share, provided Alice and Bob are friends and Alice has the threshold Share.
3. ■ Checkpoint: Alice sends Bob a checkpoint of Alice's friends and Alice's identity record, provided Alice and Bob are friends. Bob keeps it.
4. ■ State recovery. Restore: Bob restores the records of Alice, provided Alice has suffered a state loss, Bob counts Alice as a friend and Bob keeps a checkpoint of Alice. Restore friend: Bob restores the friendship of Alice with Carol, provided Alice has suffered a state loss, Bob counts Alice as a friend, Bob keeps a checkpoint of Alice and Bob records the friendship of Alice and Carol.
5. ◆ Identity replacement. Replace: Alicia may ask Carol to vouch that Alicia is Alice, provided Alicia knows Carol and Carol is an identity custodian of Alice. Alicia asks only after an identity loss of Alice. Vouch: Carol may vouch that Alicia is Alice, provided Carol has been asked to vouch that Alicia is Alice and Carol is an identity custodian of Alice. Present: Alicia may present to Bob the statement of Carol that Alicia is Alice, provided Alicia knows Bob, Alicia has received the vouch of Carol for Alice, Bob counts Alice as a friend and Bob records Carol as an identity custodian of Alice. Accept: Bob accepts Alicia in place of Alice, provided Bob holds Carol's statement that Alicia is Alice, Bob records the threshold Share of Alice and Bob holds the statements of at least Share persons that Alicia is Alice. Accept custodian: Bob accepts Alicia as an identity custodian in place of Alice, provided Bob has accepted Alicia in place of Alice. Replace custodian: Bob drops Alice from the identity custodians of Dave, provided Bob has accepted Alicia in place of Alice. Tell friends: Bob passes Alicia the friendship of Alice and Dave, provided Bob has accepted Alicia in place of Alice and Bob records the friendship of Alice and Dave. Bob tells Alicia the friends of Alice he records, and tells his friends of the change.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x has z as an identity custodian = custodian(x,z); z is an identity custodian of x = custodian_of(z,x); x has a fixed identity record = fixed(x); x has the threshold n = threshold(x,n); z records the friendship of x and y = records_friend(z,x,y); y records z as an identity custodian of x = records_custodian(y,x,z); y keeps a checkpoint of x = checkpoint(y,x); x has suffered a state loss = state_loss(x); x has recovered records from y = recovered(x,y); x has recovered the friendship with z from y = recovered_friend(x,z,y); z has been asked to vouch that w is x = asked(z,w,x); z has vouched that w is x = vouched(z,w,x); y records the threshold n of x = records_threshold(y,x,n); w has received the vouch of z for x = received_vouch(w,z,x); y holds c's statement that w is x = holds_statement(y,c,w,x); y has accepted w in place of x = accepted(y,w,x); w has learned from y of the friendship of x and d = learned_friend(w,y,x,d).
After x names z x's identity custodian, which requires x does not have z as an identity custodian: x has z as an identity custodian and z is an identity custodian of x.
After x fixes x's identity record with the threshold n, which requires x does not have a fixed identity record: x has a fixed identity record and x has the threshold n.
After x tells z of the friendship of x and y, which requires z does not record the friendship of x and y: z records the friendship of x and y.
After x tells z of the end of the friendship of x and y: no longer z records the friendship of x and y.
After x sends y the identity custodian z, which requires y does not record z as an identity custodian of x: y records z as an identity custodian of x.
After x sends y the threshold n, which requires y does not record the threshold n of x: y records the threshold n of x.
After x sends y a checkpoint of x's friends and x's identity record: y keeps a checkpoint of x.
After y restores the records of x, which requires x does not count y as a friend: x counts y as a friend and x has recovered records from y.
After y restores the friendship of x with z, which requires x does not count z as a friend: x counts z as a friend and x has recovered the friendship with z from y.
After w asks z to vouch that w is x, which requires z has not been asked to vouch that w is x: z has been asked to vouch that w is x.
After z vouches that w is x, which requires z has not vouched that w is x: z has vouched that w is x and w has received the vouch of z for x.
After w presents to y the statement of c that w is x, which requires y does not hold c's statement that w is x: y holds c's statement that w is x.
After y accepts w in place of x, which requires y has not accepted w in place of x: no longer y counts x as a friend, no longer x counts y as a friend, y counts w as a friend, w counts y as a friend and y has accepted w in place of x.
After y accepts w as an identity custodian in place of x: no longer y has x as an identity custodian, no longer x is an identity custodian of y, y has w as an identity custodian and w is an identity custodian of y.
After y drops x from the identity custodians of d: no longer y records x as an identity custodian of d.
After y passes w the friendship of x and d, which requires w has not learned from y of the friendship of x and d: w has learned from y of the friendship of x and d.
Environment: x has suffered a state loss.
