# sglp

sGLP's directory (Coordination Appendix B; renamed from svglp 2026-10-01): sGLP in GLP, as the stochastic pi-calculus was in FCP, written from sGLP's paper (the repository svGLP-Stochastic-Volitional-GLP at d2f64b6, Section 4) with nothing in the engine, the compiler or the checker.  The simulations written for sGLP's engine extension were removed with it on 2026-10-02 (git holds them, before that date).

A program rooted here: load `programs/sglp` and call its entry point.

- `self.glp` --- the directory: exposes `monitor.glp` and `person.glp` to the subtree; the horizon's units (`span/2`); the entry points `social_graph(Agents, Count, Unit, Seed)`, with the run declaration's mix, and `social_graph(Agents, Count, Unit, Seed, Hom, Wary)`, with the mix given by two integers 0..100: an agent is homophile for its first draw K =< Hom and wary for its second K =< Wary, K of 1..100, so `social_graph/4` is `social_graph/6` at 60 and 70; and `social_graph_draw(Agents, Seed, Hom, Wary, D)`, the population's draw of that run without running it, `D = draw(Homophile, Indifferent, Wary, Sociable)`.
- `monitor.glp` --- the monitor: the clock, the queue of pending requests, the release over the guard `when_idle`.
- `person.glp` --- the person process of each agent, and `construct/3`, the construct process of a compiled program in a simulation.
- `social_graph/` --- the Grassroots Social Graph in the translated form, by hand: `graph.glp` (the compiled program with the person channel, the four profiles translated, `one_of`, `length`, `nth`), `harness.glp` (the run, `run/6`, the mix its last two arguments), `run.sh` (a run through the REPL, its log and its friendship graph; the mix as its sixth and seventh arguments, defaults 60 and 70), `friendship.awk` (the friendship graph from a log, at the run's end and, with `months=<k>`, at the end of each of its first k months).
- The population's draw --- how many agents of each profile in each dimension, which the log does not show --- is printed by `run.sh`, as its line `draw homophile <h> indifferent <i> wary <w> sociable <s>`, and not written into the log (the choice the task of 2026-10-02 15:44 UTC left open): the log is the answers and the monitor's clock (sGLP's paper, Section 4, "The log"), and `run.sh` computes the draw with `social_graph_draw/5` in a REPL session of its own before the run, so that the run's log is byte for byte what it is without it.
- The run's cost, its log byte for byte the same (sGLP's code task of 2026-10-02 20:58 UTC, on Integration's measurement of 19:05 UTC).  S1: `harness.glp`'s `skip/3` draws an agent's block of 4096 (Appendix B) one `random/4` at a time, each reduced before the next is spawned, its clause waiting on the seed, where it spawned the 4096 at once, each suspended on the last; not the closed form, the seed times 16807^4096 mod 2^31 - 1, which is the engine's generator, GLP-Spec saying only that the next seed is determined by the seed.
- `coins/` --- coins among friends (sGLP's paper, Section 6; sGLP's code task of 2026-10-02 15:24 UTC): `circulation.awk`, the circulation (Definition "Circulation") replayed from a run's log alone, at the end of each month 1..12 (`months=<k>` for another number) with the holdings, the agents with a non-empty wallet and the cumulative pays, proposals, offers accepted and declined, then the run's totals and the clock; its reading of the log is in its header.  `tests/circulation/four.log` is a fixed log of four agents and `four.expected` its circulation computed by hand, test (viii).
- `tests/` and `test_sglp.sh` --- tests (i) to (iv) of the code task of 2026-10-02 00:06 UTC, (v) and (vi) of the task of 15:23 UTC, (vii) of the task of 15:44 UTC and (viii) of the task of 15:24 UTC, run by the suite's Section SGLP.
