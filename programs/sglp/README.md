## The vGLP sources and their compilation

sGLP's paper simulates the vGLP program the phone runs (Section 3, Definition "Simulation Program": "the clauses of ⌈M⌉ other than the construct processes' and the dispatcher's", with "the person process of a" on "the ask stream of the agent's initial goal" in their place; sGLP's task of 2026-10-09 20:15 UTC, item 2, and its answer of 21:21 UTC).  `social_graph/graph.vglp` and `coins/coins.vglp` are those programs, in vGLP's syntax (vGLP, Definition "Guarded Clause, Volitional Procedure, Interactive Type, Interactive Term, Ordinary Clause, Procedure, vGLP Program"): the types and the volitional procedures as Sections 5 and 6 print them, byte for byte, and the agent's clauses as Sections 5 and 6 describe them, Section 6's last item, "drop the declines of their own proposals", from sGLP 8b0f1dd (sGLP's answer of 2026-10-09 21:21 UTC, Q2).  `Content` and `Msg`, the messages', are the directories' own, the paper printing neither.  Each is read by GLP's parser in its vGLP mode, `Parser(Lexer(src).tokenize(), vglp: true).parseModule()`, refusing nothing; the program loader skips a `.vglp` beside a `.glp` of its name, so neither is loaded.  `graph.glp` and `coins.glp` are their canonical compilation (vGLP, Definition "Canonical Compilation"), by hand, clause for clause, and the person process reads the ask stream of `start` (`population.glp`, `person/6`; the harnesses).

The compilation, by the Definition, and the choices it leaves:

  1. Every procedure that reaches a question --- `ask` and `respond`, volitional, and in the social graph `agent` and `start`, in coins among friends `agent`, `paid`, `settle` and `start` --- has one more argument, last, its ask stream, `Stream(Ask)`, which its goal writes.  `held`, `add` and `take` reach none and are as the source has them.
  2. In a clause of such a procedure each call of a procedure that reaches a question is given a fresh writer as its ask stream, `D1`, `D2` in the order of the body, and the head carries `D?`: the one such call is given `D` itself; two are merged by `merge(D1?, D2?, D)`, the root's `merge/3`, after the body's own goals; with none, the head carries `[]`.  No clause of either program has more than two.
  3. Each volitional procedure `q` keeps its name for its asking clause, so that every call of `q` reaches it unchanged, and its clauses as guarded clauses, of arity n+2 with the interactive term and the ask stream, take the fresh name `q1`: the Definition's names, `ask` and `ask1`, `respond` and `respond1` (vGLP `19feee8`; sGLP's task 7 of 2026-10-10 08:14 UTC, where they were `ask_a` and `ask`, `respond_a` and `respond`).
  4. The asking clause is `q(S1, ..., Sn, [ask(T, t(X?))|D?]) :- q1(S1', ..., Sn', X, D).`, X and X? exchanged from the Definition's form since every interactive type here is in writer mode, `S'` the reader of `S` at an input position and the writer at an output position, the head carrying the other end.  `T` is the interactive type as a constant, `'Menu'`, `'Card'`, `'Offer'`, as the log prints it.
  5. `t`, "the name of T in lowercase, its type arguments appended, with `_w` for writer mode and `_r` for reader mode": `menu_w`, `card_w` and `offer_w`, as vGLP's compiler (`glp_runtime/lib/vglp/canonical.dart`) gives them.  GLP has no string operation to make it of a type's name, so the source's volitional declaration in the term form carries it, `volitional('Menu', menu_w, ...)` (below, "The term form"), and the transformation checks the functors distinct.
  6. The types the compilation adds, in each directory's `self.glp` beside the source's: `Ask ::= ask(Constant, Question).` and `Question ::= menu_w(Menu) ; card_w(Card).` (coins among friends: `menu_w(Menu) ; offer_w(Offer)`), each type in its mode, writer mode written `T`.

## The term form

sGLP's paper prints sGLP in its surface syntax, which GLP's reader does not read: `person p.`, `T =::= p.`, the run declaration and `G @ r` are not GLP terms, and GLP has no reader a program can call.  The transformation therefore reads an sGLP program in a term form, one GLP term per declaration and clause, in GLP-Spec's term syntax and nothing else (Definition "Logic Programs Syntax": a variable, a constant, a compound term `f(T1, ..., Tn)`, and the list notation), with no operator, as Jurix's contracts are written (`programs/jurix/contracts.glp`).  The paper's syntax is the surface form of this term form (Udi, 2026-10-03, to Integration Code: "term form (readable surface syntax can be used in papers even uf not identical to actual syntax)").  A source is a unit clause `source(V, S)`: `V` the vGLP program's interface, its types and its volitional procedure declarations, which the transformation reads for the person process; `S` the sGLP program, its items in order.  The two sources are `social_graph/graph_sglp.glp` and `coins/coins_sglp.glp`.
