# sglp

sGLP's directory (Coordination Appendix B; renamed from svglp 2026-10-01): sGLP in GLP, as the stochastic pi-calculus was in FCP, written from sGLP's paper (the repository svGLP-Stochastic-Volitional-GLP at d2f64b6, Section 4) with nothing in the engine, the compiler or the checker.  The simulations written for sGLP's engine extension were removed with it on 2026-10-02 (git holds them, before that date).

A program rooted here: load `programs/sglp` and call its entry point.

- `self.glp` --- the directory: exposes `monitor.glp` and `person.glp` to the subtree; the horizon's units (`span/2`); `one_of`, `length` and `nth`, shared by the examples' profiles; the entry points `social_graph(Agents, Count, Unit, Seed)`, with the run declaration's mix, and `social_graph(Agents, Count, Unit, Seed, Hom, Wary)`, with the mix given by two integers 0..100: an agent is homophile for its first draw K =< Hom and wary for its second K =< Wary, K of 1..100, so `social_graph/4` is `social_graph/6` at 60 and 70; and `social_graph_draw(Agents, Seed, Hom, Wary, D)`, the population's draw of that run without running it, `D = draw(Homophile, Indifferent, Wary, Sociable, As)`, `As` the list of `agent(A, Approach, Response)`, each agent's profiles, A of 1..Agents in order; and `coins(Agents, Friends, Count, Unit, Seed, Spend, Cred)`, coins among friends' run, agent a's friends the a-th list of Friends, the mix given by two integers 0..100: an agent is a spender for its first draw K =< Spend and cautious for its second K =< Cred, so the run declaration's is 50 and 50; and `coins_draw(Agents, Seed, Spend, Cred, D)`, that run's population's draw without running it, `D = draw(Spender, Saver, Generous, Cautious, As)`, `As` the list of `agent(A, Spending, Credit)`, A of 1..Agents in order, the draw not depending on the friends.
- `monitor.glp` --- the monitor: the clock, the queue of pending requests, the release over the guard `when_idle`.
- `person.glp` --- the person process of each agent, the social graph's `person/6` and coins among friends' `coins_person/6`, and `construct/3`, the construct process of a compiled program in a simulation.
- `social_graph/` --- the Grassroots Social Graph in the translated form, by hand: `graph.glp` (the compiled program with the person channel, the four profiles translated), `harness.glp` (the run, `run/6`, the mix its last two arguments), `run.sh` (a run through the REPL, its log, its draw beside the log and its friendship graph; the mix as its sixth and seventh arguments, defaults 60 and 70), `friendship.awk` (the friendship graph from a log, at the run's end and, with `months=<k>`, at the end of each of its first k months).
- The population's draw --- how many agents of each profile in each dimension, and which agent has which, which the log does not show --- is printed by `run.sh`, as its line `draw homophile <h> indifferent <i> wary <w> sociable <s>`, and not written into the log (the choice the task of 2026-10-02 15:44 UTC left open): the log is the answers and the monitor's clock (sGLP's paper, Section 4, "The log"), and `run.sh` computes the draw with `social_graph_draw/5` in a REPL session of its own before the run, so that the run's log is byte for byte what it is without it.  Each agent's profiles go to a `.draw` file beside the log, not to `run.sh`'s output (the choice the task of 2026-10-03 08:58 UTC left open): the log's path with its `.log` replaced by `.draw`, or with `.draw` added where it has none, holding the counts line, then one line `agent <a> <approach> <response>` per agent, a of 1..N in order, all from the one answer of `social_graph_draw/5`.
- The run's cost, its log byte for byte the same (sGLP's code task of 2026-10-02 20:58 UTC, on Integration's measurement of 19:05 UTC).  S1: `harness.glp`'s `skip/3` draws an agent's block of 4096 (Appendix B) one `random/4` at a time, each reduced before the next is spawned, its clause waiting on the seed, where it spawned the 4096 at once, each suspended on the last; not the closed form, the seed times 16807^4096 mod 2^31 - 1, which is the engine's generator, GLP-Spec saying only that the next seed is determined by the seed.  S2: `length/2`, then in `graph.glp` and since coins among friends in `self.glp`, is tail-recursive, its count in an accumulator (`length/3`), where a list of n elements left n additions suspended at once, each waiting on the count of the rest.  S3: an agent's `graph#start` is spawned by `harness.glp`'s `start_when/7` once `peers/6` reports both its lists built (`range/5`'s `done`, `both/3`), so that `start/6`'s guards `ground(Ss?)` and `ground(Os?)` walk each list once, where they re-walked it from its head at every new element.
- `coins/` --- coins among friends in the translated form, by hand (sGLP's paper, Section 6; sGLP's code task of 2026-10-02 15:24 UTC): `self.glp` (the directory's interface, the vGLP program's types --- Peer, Peers and YesNo the social graph's, Holding, Wallet, Request, Menu and Offer the paper's, Content and Msg its own --- the four person procedures for the person process, and `run/7`), `coins.glp` (the compiled program with the person channel, the agent's clauses of the task's item 1, the wallet's `held`, `add` and `take`, the four profiles translated, `request` and `issuer`), `harness.glp` (the run, `run/7`, the friends lists its second argument and the mix its last two; the population's draw, `draw/5`), `run.sh` (a run through the REPL on a friendship graph, its log, its draw beside the log and its circulation; the mix as its sixth and seventh arguments, defaults 50 and 50), `circulation.awk` (the circulation (Definition "Circulation") replayed from a run's log alone, at the end of each month 1..12 (`months=<k>` for another number) with the holdings, the agents with a non-empty wallet and the cumulative pays, proposals, offers accepted and declined, then the run's totals, the clock and the answers its replay does not allow; its reading of the log is in its header).  `tests/circulation/four.log` is a fixed log of four agents, a pay answered while a card waits and coins arriving while a card waits among its answers, and `four.expected` its circulation computed by hand, test (viii); `four.graph` is the graph of test (ix)'s four agents.
- Coins among friends' choices, where Section 6's prose and the task of 2026-10-02 15:24 UTC leave one open:
  1. The person process of coins among friends is `coins_person/6` in `person.glp`, beside the social graph's `person/6`, a procedure of its own: the two programs' Menus are both `menu/3`, and the alternatives of a type have distinct top-level functors (TGLP, "Type Declarations": type union is for types with disjoint top-level functors).  `person.glp` names coins among friends' Menu and Request `CoinsMenu` and `CoinsRequest`, the names being the social graph's and the monitor's; `construct/3` is parameterised, `procedure(T, X)`, so that both programs' asking clauses call it.
  2. `one_of`, `length` and `nth` are moved from `social_graph/graph.glp` to `self.glp`, shared by both programs' profiles, not copied, `length` in S2's form; the social graph's 100-agent year, seeds 20260927 and 1, is byte-identical with the move.
  3. The wallet's order: a holding of an issuer the wallet has none of goes at its end (`add/4`'s one walk), and the order is otherwise kept; it decides only which holding `request`'s `one_of` draws.
  4. A pay of a friend whose coins the wallet does not hold sends `pay(Id, 0)`, what the wallet holds up to the amount asked, and leaves the wallet; it does not arise, `request` paying an issuer its menu shows and only the agent's own pay taking a holding off (sGLP's answer of 2026-10-02 20:57 UTC, 1).
  5. While a card waits for its answer the agent takes neither a message nor its person's request (`settle/10` takes the answer alone), as Section 6 says since sGLP d6d5080 (sGLP's answer of 2026-10-02 20:57 UTC, 2); a request answered meanwhile is acted on after the card is answered.
  6. `agent/7` takes the person's request before a message, as `graph.glp` takes the choice first; the messages are taken in the task's order, propose, coins, pay, decline.  After a card answered yes the wallet is `add/4`'s to build, and the clauses of the coins, pay and decline messages, unlike the request's and the proposal's, do not wait on it (`ground(W?)`), so a message waiting then may be taken before the request (GLP-Spec, Reduce, of any goal of the resolvent).
  7. After a pay the agent asks again once the wallet the pay leaves is known (`paid/6`), the menu showing that wallet.
  8. The generous profile's goal is `accept`, the name Section 6 had at sGLP 8ddff2e; Section 6 now describes it without naming it.
  9. `Msg` is this directory's own (the task, item 1), its Content being pay, coins, propose and decline; Section 6 names Peer, Peers and YesNo as Section 5's, and not Msg, since sGLP dfb9a3a.
  10. The harness requires N >= 1 and N friends lists; the network's `tree/5` and `deliver/4` and the blocks' `skip/3` are the social graph's, copied with this directory's Msg, `skip/3` in S1's form.  An agent's `coins#start` is spawned at once, its friends list being ground in the goal.  The population's draw is printed as the social graph's is (above; sGLP's code task of 2026-10-03 09:45 UTC): `run.sh` computes it with `coins_draw/5` in a REPL session of its own before the run, prints its line `draw spender <s> saver <v> generous <g> cautious <c>`, and writes beside the log a `.draw` file, that line, then one line `agent <a> <spending> <credit>` per agent, a of 1..N in order, all from the one answer; `harness.glp`'s `tally/14` draws as `agents/10` does.
  11. `run.sh` refuses a graph on which an agent of 1..N is on no edge, as it refuses one with an edge outside 1..N: that agent's friends would be the empty list, on which `one_of` is never called (Section 5) and fails (Appendix B).
  12. `circulation.awk` moves a holding where the agent moves it, as far as the log shows (its header): a pay at its answer, or, answered while one of the agent's cards waits, when that card is answered, after the card's move and before the messages that waited (sGLP's code task of 2026-10-03 08:44 UTC, item 2); the proposer's holding of the other's coins when the proposer takes the coins message, at the yes, or, arriving while a card of the proposer's waits, when that card is answered, after its move and the request held, with the messages that waited, in the order they came, up to the next proposal (the task of 09:22 UTC); which card waits, and when, it reads from the log's order, each line being one Release.  Where a request and coins both wait at a card answered yes, the replay takes the request first, as choice 6 has it and its clauses do not fix.
