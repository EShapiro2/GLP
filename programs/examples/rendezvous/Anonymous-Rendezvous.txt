Anonymous Rendezvous
Anonymous Rendezvous extends Rendezvous.
A *one-time key* is a key pair its maker uses for one meeting.
Alice and Bob are persons, Dave a rendezvous server.
1. ■ Meet. Present: Alice and Bob, each holding a one-time key and knowing the public key of the other's, may each present to Dave the two public keys, signed with their own. Match: Dave then gives each the address of the other.

Anonymous Rendezvous
Anonymous Rendezvous extends Rendezvous.
A *one-time key* is a key pair its maker uses for one meeting.
Alice and Bob are persons, Dave a service, Key and Other one-time keys.
1. ■ Meet. Present: Alice may present to Dave the keys Key and Other signed with Key, provided Alice holds the one-time key Key, Alice knows the public key of Other, Alice knows Dave and Dave is a rendezvous server. Match: Dave matches Alice and Bob on Key and Other.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x holds the one-time key k = one_time_key(x,k); x knows the public key of k = knows_key(x,k); d has the keys k and l presented by x = presented_keys(d,k,l,x).
After x presents to d the keys k and l signed with k, which requires d does not have the keys k and l presented by x: d has the keys k and l presented by x.
After d matches x and y on k and l: no longer d has the keys k and l presented by x, no longer d has the keys l and k presented by y, x has the address of y and y has the address of x.
Environment: x holds the one-time key k; x knows the public key of k.
