GRV: Grassroots Rendezvous
GRV extends GSV.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Dave a ward.  A rendezvous server is a service.  An invitation link is a term Alice signs, naming Alice, a rendezvous server, a serial and an expiry.
1. ■ Server.  Carol may make her service Dave a rendezvous server.
2. ■ Registration.  Alice may register with Dave an invitation link she signed that names Dave, with a serial she has not registered with Dave before.
3. ■ Redemption.  Bob may present to Dave an invitation link registered with Dave that has not expired; Dave then gives Alice Bob's address and Bob Alice's, and the link is spent.
4. ■ Reconnection.  Alice and Bob, who are friends, may each present to Dave the other's signature over their friendship; Dave then gives each the address of the other.
Interface pragmas
Look: 🛰️, network
Server: Carol [Open server]
  Dave: Carol asks you to be a rendezvous server.
Registration: Alice [Invite by link]
Implementation pragmas
Redemption, Reconnection: the address of a person is the one at which the server's networking layer observes them, read by peer_address/2; a person given an address punches towards it by punch_udp/1, and the smaller key initiates.
Registration: an invitation link expires an hour after it is made.
Registration: Alice's app puts the link on the phone's clipboard, for her to paste into any channel she has.
Registration: an environment predicate, link(Alice, serial, expiry), the serial and the expiry of the next invitation link of Alice, which Alice's app keeps current.
