GRV: Grassroots Rendezvous
GRV extends GSV.
An *invitation link* of Alice's is a term she signs, naming her, a rendezvous server, a serial and an expiry.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Dave a service.
1. ■ Open server: Carol may make her service Dave a rendezvous server.
2. ■ Invite by link: Alice may register with Dave an invitation link of hers that names Dave, with a new serial.
3. ■ Use link: Bob may present to Dave an invitation link of Alice's registered with Dave that has not expired; Dave then gives Alice Bob's address and Bob Alice's, and the link is spent.
4. ■ Reconnection. Present: Alice and Bob, who are friends, may each present to Dave the other's signature over their friendship. Match: Dave then gives each the address of the other.
Logic pragmas
GRV extends GSV.
An *invitation link* of Alice's is a term she signs, naming her, a rendezvous server, a serial and an expiry.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Dave a service, Link an invitation link.
1. ■ Open server: Carol may make Dave a rendezvous server, provided Carol is a guardian of Dave.
2. ■ Invite by link: Alice may register Link with Dave, provided Alice has signed Link as an invitation link that names Dave, Alice knows Dave and Dave is a rendezvous server. Link has a new serial.
3. ■ Use link: Bob may present Link of Alice to Dave, provided Bob knows Dave and Dave does not hold Link expired. The link is then spent.
4. ■ Reconnection. Present: Alice may present to Dave the signature of Bob over the friendship of Alice and Bob, provided Alice and Bob are friends, Alice knows Dave and Dave is a rendezvous server. Match: Dave matches Alice and Bob.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: d is a rendezvous server = rendezvous_server(d); x has signed n as an invitation link that names d = link(x,n,d); d has n registered for x = registered(d,n,x); d holds n expired = expired(d,n); x has the address of y = address(x,y); d has the signature of y over the friendship of x and y presented = presented(d,x,y).
After x makes d a rendezvous server, which requires d is not a rendezvous server: d is a rendezvous server.
After x registers n with d, which requires d does not have n registered for x: d has n registered for x.
After y presents n of x to d: no longer d has n registered for x, x has the address of y and y has the address of x.
After x presents to d the signature of y over the friendship of x and y, which requires d does not have the signature of y over the friendship of x and y presented: d has the signature of y over the friendship of x and y presented.
After d matches x and y: no longer d has the signature of y over the friendship of x and y presented, no longer d has the signature of x over the friendship of y and x presented, x has the address of y and y has the address of x.

Implementation pragmas
Use link, Reconnection: the address of a person is the one at which the server's networking layer observes them, read by peer_address/2; a person given an address punches towards it by punch_udp/1, and the smaller key initiates.
Invite by link: an invitation link expires an hour after it is made.
Invite by link: the link is given as a web address, https://peoplesweb.org/i#<link>, which opens the Grassroots App where it is installed.
Invite by link: Alice's app puts the link on the phone's clipboard, for her to paste into any channel she has.
Invite by link: an environment predicate, link(Alice, Link, Dave), the next invitation link of Alice that names Dave, with its serial and its expiry, which Alice's app keeps current.
Use link: an environment predicate, expired(Dave, Link), the links whose expiry has passed, which Dave keeps current.
