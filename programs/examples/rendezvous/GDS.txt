GDS: Grassroots Directory Service
GDS extends GnW (GAI, Corralling AI Risks with Grassroots Contracts, arXiv).
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Dora a ward.  A directory is a ward operated by a machine with a page every person can read.  A listing is an email address of Alice's that the directory verified, with a name of her choosing if she gives one.
1. ■ Directory.  Carol may create a directory, Dora, as her ward.
2. ■ Listing.  Alice may list herself with Dora by an email address of hers that Dora verified, with a name if she chooses, listed or unlisted; she may unlist herself.
3. ■ Lookup.  Anyone may look a listed person up on Dora's page, and an unlisted person up by their email address and name.
4. ■ Contact.  Dora gives whoever looked Alice up an invitation link Alice's app makes for them.
Interface pragmas
Listing: Alice [List] <email> <name> [List unlisted] <email> <name> [Unlist]
Implementation pragmas
Listing: the directory verifies an email address by sending it a message with a link, which Alice opens.
Contact: on a lookup Dora asks Alice's Grassroots App for a link, which it makes and registers with her rendezvous server (GRV:2); where Alice's app cannot be reached, the lookup is answered so.
Lookup: the directory's page is at <address>/directory; a listed person is shown by name where they gave one and by email address otherwise, and a click on one is a lookup.
Contact: the link is given as text, to open in a Grassroots App, which redeems it (GRV:3).
