GDS: Grassroots Directory Service
GDS extends GSV.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Dora a ward.  A directory is a service with a page every person can read.  A listing is an email address of Alice's that the directory verified, with a name of her choosing if she gives one.
1. ■ Directory.  Carol may make her service Dora a directory.
2. ■ Listing.  Alice may list herself with Dora by an email address of hers that Dora verified, with a name if she chooses, listed or unlisted; her app then keeps Dora supplied with invitation links of hers; she may unlist herself.
3. ■ Lookup.  Anyone may look a listed person up on Dora's page, and an unlisted person up by their email address and name.
4. ■ Contact.  Dora gives whoever looked Alice up an invitation link of Alice's.
Interface pragmas
Look: 📇, network
Directory: Carol [Open directory]
  Dora: Carol asks you to be a directory.
Listing: Alice [List] <email> <name> [List unlisted] <email> <name>
  Dora: Alice asks to be listed.
  Alice [Unlist]
Implementation pragmas
Listing: the directory verifies an email address by sending it a message with a link, which Alice opens.
Listing, Contact: Alice's app makes invitation links every so often, registers each with her rendezvous server (GRV:2) and gives them to Dora; Dora keeps a buffer of unexpired ones, at least one, and asks Alice's app for more by how often Alice is looked up, two when one is taken, and so on; a link given to Dora expires a day after it is made, so Alice's app renews them daily while it runs, and a lookup finds none when it has been off longer.
Lookup: the directory's page is at <address>/directory; a listed person is shown by name where they gave one and by email address otherwise, and a click on one is a lookup.
Contact: the link is given as its web address, which opens the Grassroots App, which redeems it (GRV:3).
