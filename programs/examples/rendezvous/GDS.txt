GDS: Grassroots Directory Service
GDS extends GRV.
A *directory* is a service with a page every person can read.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Dora a service.
1. ■ Open directory: Carol may make her service Dora a directory.
2. ■ Listing. List: Alice may list herself with Dora by an email address of hers that Dora verified, with a name if she chooses, shown on Dora's page or not; her app then keeps Dora supplied with invitation links of hers. Delist: Alice may delist herself.
3. ■ Lookup: Bob may look Alice up, on Dora's page if she is shown there, and by her email address and name if not; Dora then gives Bob an invitation link of Alice's.
Logic pragmas
GDS extends GRV.
A *directory* is a service with a page every person can read.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Dora a service.
1. ■ Open directory: Carol may make Dora a directory, provided Carol is a guardian of Dora.
2. ■ Listing. List: Alice may list with Dora, provided Dora is a directory and Dora has verified an email address of Alice. Alice lists with a name if she chooses, shown on Dora's page or not, and her app then keeps Dora supplied with invitation links of hers. Delist: Alice may delist from Dora.
3. ■ Lookup: Bob may look Alice up with Dora, provided Bob knows Dora and Dora lists Alice. Bob looks Alice up on Dora's page if she is shown there, and by her email address and name if not.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: d is a directory = directory(d); d has verified an email address of x = verified(d,x); d lists x = lists(d,x); y holds an invitation link of x from d = holds_link(y,x,d).
After x makes d a directory, which requires d is not a directory: d is a directory.
After x lists with d, which requires d does not list x: d lists x.
After x delists from d: no longer d lists x.
After y looks x up with d: y holds an invitation link of x from d.

Implementation pragmas
Listing: Alice's app asks her for the email address, for a name if she chooses, and whether she is shown on Dora's page, and sends them with the listing.
Listing: the directory verifies an email address by sending it a message with a link, which Alice opens.
Listing: an environment predicate, verified(Dora, Alice), the persons an email address of whom Dora verified, which Dora keeps current.
Listing, Lookup: Alice's app makes invitation links every so often, registers each with her rendezvous server (GRV:2) and gives them to Dora; Dora keeps a buffer of unexpired ones, at least one, and asks Alice's app for more by how often Alice is looked up, two when one is taken, and so on; a link given to Dora expires a day after it is made, so Alice's app renews them daily while it runs, and a lookup finds none when it has been off longer.
Lookup: the directory's page is at <address>/directory; a person shown there is shown by name where they gave one and by email address otherwise, and a click on one is a lookup.
Lookup: the link is given as its web address, which opens the Grassroots App, which redeems it (GRV:3).
