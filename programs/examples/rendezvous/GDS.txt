GDS: Grassroots Directory Service
GDS extends GRV.
A *directory* is a service with a page every person can read.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Dora a service.
1. ■ Open directory: Carol may make her service Dora a directory.
2. ■ Listing. List me: Alice may list herself with Dora by an email address of hers that Dora verified, with a name if she chooses, shown on Dora's page or not; her app then keeps Dora supplied with invitation links of hers. Delist me: Alice may delist herself.
3. ■ Lookup: Bob may look Alice up, on Dora's page if she is shown there, and by her email address and name if not; Dora then gives Bob an invitation link of Alice's.
Logic pragmas
GDS extends GRV.
A *directory* is a service with a page every person can read.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Dora a service, Mail an email address, Name a name.
1. ■ Open directory: Carol may make Dora a directory, provided Carol is a guardian of Dora.
2. ■ Listing. List me: Alice may list with Dora by Mail under Name on its page, with the consent of Dora, provided Dora is a directory and Alice knows Dora. List me unshown: Alice may list with Dora by Mail under Name off its page, with the consent of Dora, provided Dora is a directory and Alice knows Dora. Dora consents having verified Mail as an email address of Alice. Alice lists with a name if she chooses, shown on Dora's page or not, and her app then keeps Dora supplied with invitation links of hers. Delist me: Alice may delist from Dora by Mail under Name on its page. Delist me unshown: Alice may delist from Dora by Mail under Name off its page.
3. ■ Lookup: Bob may look Alice up on Dora's page, provided Bob knows Dora and Dora lists Alice by Mail under Name on its page. Lookup by address: Bob may look Alice up with Dora by address, provided Bob knows Dora and Dora lists Alice by Mail under Name off its page. Bob looks Alice up on Dora's page if she is shown there, and by her email address and name if not.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: d is a directory = directory(d); d lists x by e under m on its page = lists_shown(d,x,e,m); d lists x by e under m off its page = lists_unshown(d,x,e,m); y holds an invitation link of x from d = holds_link(y,x,d).
After x makes d a directory, which requires d is not a directory: d is a directory.
After x lists with d by e under m on its page, which requires d does not list x by e under m on its page: d lists x by e under m on its page.
After x lists with d by e under m off its page, which requires d does not list x by e under m off its page: d lists x by e under m off its page.
After x delists from d by e under m on its page: no longer d lists x by e under m on its page.
After x delists from d by e under m off its page: no longer d lists x by e under m off its page.
After y looks x up on d's page: y holds an invitation link of x from d.
After y looks x up with d by address: y holds an invitation link of x from d.

Implementation pragmas
Listing: the directory verifies an email address by sending it a message with a link, which Alice opens.
Listing, Lookup: Alice's app makes invitation links every so often, registers each with her rendezvous server (GRV:2) and gives them to Dora; Dora keeps a buffer of unexpired ones, at least one, and asks Alice's app for more by how often Alice is looked up, two when one is taken, and so on; a link given to Dora expires a day after it is made, so Alice's app renews them daily while it runs, and a lookup finds none when it has been off longer.
Lookup: the directory's page is at <address>/directory; a person shown there is shown by name where they gave one and by email address otherwise, and a click on one is a lookup.
Lookup: the link is given as its web address, which opens the Grassroots App, which redeems it (GRV:3).
