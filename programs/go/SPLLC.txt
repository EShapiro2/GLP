SPLLC: Single-Person Limited-Liability Company
SPLLC instantiates the bylaws of GCorp and restricts it.
A single-person limited-liability company is a corporation whose bylaws name one role, the sole officer, who decides every act of the company, and admit no participant but the sole officer.
Alice is a person, Orga a single-person limited-liability company.
1. ■ Founding.  Alice may found Orga, holding its sole office.
2. ■ Acts of the company.  Orga may take an act by the decision of its sole officer.
