GAN: Grassroots Ad Network
GAN extends GNO and GDO (GRH, Grassroots Ride-Hailing, in preparation).
An advertiser is a provider and a member a customer.  A member's profile is in their state and nowhere else.  An ad names the profile it is for, the Orga-coins its advertiser pays for a view and for a click, the most one member may earn from it, and its budget, the most it pays in all.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga an ad network.
1. ■ Posting.  Bob, an advertiser, may post an ad to the members he points to, its budget locked on top of his stake, and a member may pass it to the members they point to.
2. ■ Showing.  Alice, a member, is shown an ad she received if it is for her profile.
3. ◆ Viewing and clicking.  Alice may attest that she viewed, or clicked, an ad shown to her, and the ad's budget then pays her its coins for the view or the click, up to the most one member may earn from it.
