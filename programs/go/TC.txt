TC: Trading Company
TC instantiates GCorp and restricts it.
A trading company is a corporation with three roles, buyer, keeper and seller; its officers jointly appoint and remove officers.  A good is a thing Orga buys and sells; Orga holds a good from its receipt by a keeper until its release.
Alice, Bob, Carol, Dave and Eve are persons, Orga a trading company.
1. ■ Founding.  Alice may found Orga and become its first buyer, keeper and seller.
2. ■ Officers.  The officers may appoint a participant a buyer, a keeper or a seller, and remove one.
3. ■ Purchase.  Alice, a buyer, and Dave, who know each other, may jointly decide that Orga pays Dave coins for a good Dave delivers to Orga.
4. ◆ Receipt.  Bob, a keeper, may record the receipt of a good Orga bought, and Orga then holds it.
5. ■ Sale.  Carol, a seller, and Eve, who know each other, may jointly decide that Eve pays Orga coins for a good Orga holds, which is then sold to Eve and Orga holds it no more.
6. ○ Keeping.  Bob, a keeper, keeps every good Orga holds, and releases a good sold to Eve to Eve.
