WC: Workers' Cooperative
WC instantiates GCoop with bylaws that delegate no act and extends it.
A *workers' cooperative* is a cooperative whose members work for it.
Bob is a person, Orga a workers' cooperative.
1. ■ Pay: Orga may pay Bob, a member, Dollar-denominated coins for his work.
2. ■ Distribute: Orga may distribute Dollar-denominated coins it holds to the members equally.
Logic pragmas
WC instantiates GCoop with bylaws that delegate no act, and extends it.
A *workers' cooperative* is a cooperative whose members work for it.
Bob and Carol are persons, Orga a workers' cooperative, Dollar a fiat currency, Sum a number, Carol possibly Bob or Orga.
1. ■ Pay: Orga may pay Bob Sum Carol-coins for Bob's work, provided Bob is a member of Orga and Carol has issued coins of the denomination Dollar.
2. ■ Distribute: Orga may distribute a Carol-coin to Bob, provided Bob is a member of Orga, Carol has issued coins of the denomination Dollar and Orga has distributed no more coins to Bob than to any other member.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: z has distributed a coin to y = distributed(z,y); z has distributed no more coins to y than to any other member = least_share(z,y).
After z pays y n w-coins for y's work: no longer z holds n w-coins and y holds n w-coins.
After z distributes a w-coin to y: no longer z holds a w-coin, y holds a w-coin and z has distributed a coin to y.
