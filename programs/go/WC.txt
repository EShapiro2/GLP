WC: Workers' Cooperative
WC instantiates the bylaws of GCoop and restricts it.
A workers' cooperative is a cooperative whose members work for Orga.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a workers' cooperative.
1. ■ Pay.  Orga may pay Bob, a member, Dollar-denominated coins for his work, as the members decide.
2. ■ Distribution.  The members may decide to distribute Dollar-denominated coins Orga holds, which Orga then pays to the members equally.
