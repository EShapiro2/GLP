SCB: Sovereign Central Bank
SCB instantiates the bylaws of GBank and restricts it.
A central bank is an organisation that is the sovereign of a denomination, Dollar: its Dollar-denominated coins and bonds are the sovereign coins and bonds of the Dollar.  It has two roles, governor and committee member; the governors jointly decide the acts of the bank, and the committee members, the monetary policy committee, set by a majority the policy rate for each term, the return over that term at which the bank lends its coins against bonds.  A participant who holds no role is a counterparty.
Gov is an organisation, Bob a person, Orga a central bank.
1. ■ Founding.  Gov may found Orga as the sovereign of the Dollar, naming its first governors and committee members.
2. ■ Counterparties.  Bob becomes a counterparty of Orga and ceases to be one by an act of Bob and the governors.
3. ■ Officers.  Gov may appoint a participant a governor or a committee member, and remove one.
4. ■ Policy rate.  The committee members may set the policy rate for a term.
5. ■ Lending.  Bob, a counterparty, and Orga may jointly decide to swap Orga-coins for Bob-bonds of a term, of face value the coins times one plus the policy rate for that term.
6. ■ Open market.  Bob, a counterparty, and Orga may jointly decide to swap coins and bonds they hold.
7. ○ Authority.  Gov founds Orga and appoints and removes its officers as the law of the Dollar provides.
