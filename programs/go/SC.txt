SC: Shareholding Corporation
SC instantiates the bylaws of GCorp and restricts it.
Share is a denomination, and a share of Orga is an Orga-coin denominated in Share.  A shareholding corporation is a corporation with two roles, shareholder, held by every participant who holds a share of Orga, and director; the shareholders decide by a majority of the shares they hold, and the directors jointly decide the other acts of Orga.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a shareholding corporation.
1. ■ Founding.  Alice may found Orga, which issues a number of shares to her, and become its first director.
2. ■ Shares.  Orga may issue shares by a decision of the shareholders; Bob becomes a participant of Orga when he accepts its shares, and a shareholder while he holds one.
3. ■ Directors.  The shareholders may elect a participant a director, and remove a director.
4. ■ Dividend.  The directors may decide a dividend, a number of Dollars a share, which Orga pays to each shareholder in Dollar-denominated coins for the shares they hold.
5. ■ Redemption.  DGC:6, for a coin of a denomination other than Share.
