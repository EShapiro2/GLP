CB: Community Bank
CB instantiates the bylaws of GBank and restricts it.
A community bank is a bank with two roles, member, held by every participant, and director, held by members the members elect; the directors jointly decide the acts of the bank, and the members jointly elect and remove directors.  A member's credit line is a number of coins the directors set.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a community bank.
1. ■ Founding.  Alice may found Orga and become its first member and director.
2. ■ Membership.  Bob becomes a member of Orga by an act of Bob and the directors; his membership ends by an act of Bob or of the directors.
3. ■ Directors.  The members may elect a member a director, and remove a director.
4. ■ Credit line.  The directors may set Bob's credit line.
5. ■ Credit.  Bob may swap Bob-coins he holds for Orga-coins, as long as the Bob-coins Orga holds do not exceed his credit line.
6. ○ Community currency.  A member shall accept Orga-coins in payment for their offerings at the prices they post.
7. ■ Leaving.  Upon ending membership, the Orga-coins Bob holds and the Bob-coins Orga holds are redeemed against each other, and whichever of the two is left holding coins of the other redeems them against any other coins held by the other, if there are any.
