Marketplace
Marketplace extends Networked Organisation and Overlay (GRH, Grassroots Ride-Hailing, in preparation) and restricts Recommendations.
A *seller* is a provider and a *buyer* a customer. A *listing* names a good, its price in Cur-coins and how it is delivered; its terms say what is released to whom on cancellation.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a networked organisation.
1. ■ Listing. Post: Bob, a seller, may post a listing to the buyers he points to. Pass: A buyer may pass it to the buyers they point to. Withdraw listing: Bob may withdraw it.
2. ■ Order: Alice, a buyer, may order the good from Bob with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other; Alice's payment and the deposit the terms set for Bob, on top of his stake, are then locked.
3. ◆ Deliver: Alice may attest that the good was delivered to her, and the payment and the deposit are then released to Bob.
4. ■ Cancel: Alice or Bob may cancel the order before delivery, and the payment and the deposit are then released to Alice and to Bob as the terms say.
5. ◆ Recommend: Recommendations:1, where Alice recommends Bob for the good after delivery.
Logic pragmas
Marketplace extends Networked Organisation and Overlay (GRH, Grassroots Ride-Hailing, in preparation) and restricts Recommendations.
A *seller* is a provider and a *buyer* a customer. A *listing* names a good, its price in Cur-coins and how it is delivered; its terms say what is released to whom on cancellation.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Orga a networked organisation, Cur an organisation, Mark a fiat currency, Listing a listing, Skill a skill or a service; in clause 1 Carol is a buyer, and in clause 5 Skill is the listing.
1. ■ Listing. Post: Bob may post Listing in Orga, provided Bob is a seller of Orga. Bob posts the listing to the buyers he points to. Send listing: Listing of Bob goes to Alice in Orga, provided Bob has Listing posted in Orga, Bob points to Alice and Alice is a buyer of Orga. Pass: Alice may pass Listing of Bob to Carol in Orga, provided Alice is a buyer of Orga, Alice has received Listing of Bob in Orga, Alice points to Carol and Carol accepts a Mark-denominated Cur-coin in payment. Withdraw listing: Bob may withdraw Listing from Orga.
2. ■ Order: Alice may order the good of Listing from Bob in Orga, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice is a buyer of Orga, Bob has Listing posted in Orga and Alice and Bob know each other. Alice's payment and the deposit the terms set for Bob, on top of his stake, are then locked.
3. ◆ Deliver: Alice may attest the delivery of the good of Listing by Bob in Orga. The payment and the deposit are then released to Bob.
4. ■ Cancel: Alice may cancel her order of Listing from Bob in Orga. Seller cancel: Bob may cancel his order of Listing by Alice in Orga. The payment and the deposit are then released to Alice and to Bob as the terms say.
5. ◆ Recommend: Recommendations:1, only if Alice has attested the delivery of the good of Skill by Bob.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: y has l posted in z = posted(y,l,z); x has received l of y in z = received(x,l,y,z); x has ordered the good of l from y = ordered(x,l,y); y has an order of the good of l from x = ordered_from(y,l,x); x has attested the delivery of the good of l by y = delivered(x,l,y).
y is a seller of z: y is a provider of z.
x is a buyer of z: x is a customer of z.
x and y have an order of l: x has ordered the good of l from y and y has an order of the good of l from x.
After y posts l in z, which requires y does not have l posted in z: y has l posted in z.
After l of y goes to x in z, which requires x has not received l of y in z: x has received l of y in z.
After x passes l of y to w in z, which requires w has not received l of y in z: w has received l of y in z.
After y withdraws l from z: no longer y has l posted in z.
After x orders the good of l from y in z, which requires x and y do not have an order of l: x and y have an order of l and coins are locked in z between the provider y and the customer x.
After x attests the delivery of the good of l by y in z: x has attested the delivery of the good of l by y, no longer x and y have an order of l and no longer coins are locked in z between the provider y and the customer x.
After x cancels x's order of l from y in z: no longer x and y have an order of l and no longer coins are locked in z between the provider y and the customer x.
After y cancels y's order of l by x in z: no longer x and y have an order of l and no longer coins are locked in z between the provider y and the customer x.
