Cooperative
Cooperative inherits from Constitution and Denominated Currencies (SGC, Sovereign Grassroots Currencies: A CBDC Architecture for Credit and Monetary Policy, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27727).
A *cooperative* is a constitutional democratic community whose bylaws name one more role, *board member*.
