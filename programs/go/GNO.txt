GNO: Grassroots Network Organisation
GNO extends GBCC and restricts it.
A network organisation is a backed community currency, Orga, with four roles: treasurer, as GBCC; provider, held by Bob while his stake, the Orga-coins he holds, is at least S, a number of Orga-coins the treasurers set; customer, held by whoever accepts Orga-coins; and arbiter.  A treasurer is a provider.  A dispute is over Orga-coins locked between a provider and a customer by an act of the organisation.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Orga a network organisation.
1. ■ Stake.  The treasurers may set S.
2. ■ Treasurers.  GBCC:1, where Bob is a provider.
3. ■ Arbiters.  The treasurers may appoint Carol an arbiter, and remove one.
4. ■ Arbitration.  Alice or Bob may raise a dispute to an arbiter, Carol, who then decides the release of the locked coins to Alice and to Bob, less the fee the terms set, which is released to her.
