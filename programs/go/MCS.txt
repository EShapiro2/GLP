MCS: Mutual Credit Society
MCS instantiates GBank, its bylaws those of a mutual credit society, and amends it.
A mutual credit society is a bank with one role, member, held by every participant, whose acts are decided by all its members jointly.  A member can be in the red by their coins in circulation less the other persons' coins they hold.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a mutual credit society.
1. ■ Founding.  Alice may found Orga and become its first member.
2. ■ Membership.  Bob becomes a member of Orga by an act of Bob and of the members of Orga; his membership ends by an act of Bob or of the other members.
3. ■ Redemption.  Bob, a member, may redeem an Alice-coin he holds, Alice a member, against an Orga-coin.
4. ■ Credit limit.  A member can be in the red by at most 1000 coins.
5. ■ Leaving.  Upon ending membership, the Orga-coins Bob holds and the Bob-coins Orga holds are redeemed against each other.
