GBank: Grassroots Bank
GBank extends GO and DGC (SGC, Sovereign Grassroots Currencies: A CBDC Architecture for Credit and Monetary Policy, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27727).
A bank is an organisation whose business is issuing coins and bonds against the coins and bonds it holds.
