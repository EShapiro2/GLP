Rides
Rides unites Community Network and Second-Score Auction, and extends and amends the union (GRH, Grassroots Ride-Hailing, in preparation).
A *driver* is a provider and a *rider* a customer.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a community network.
1. ■ Award: Second-Score Auction:2, only if the fare and the deposit are in community coins, Alice holds the fare, and Bob holds the deposit on top of his stake, and the fare and the deposit are then locked between the provider Bob and the customer Alice (Community Network:2).
Logic pragmas
Rides unites Community Network and Second-Score Auction, and extends and amends the union (GRH, Grassroots Ride-Hailing, in preparation).
A *driver* is a provider and a *rider* a customer.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a community network, Gov an organisation, Sum, Total, Top, Pot, Back and Rest numbers.
1. ■ Award: Second-Score Auction:2 Award, only if Alice is a customer of Orga, Bob is a provider of Orga, the fare and the deposit of Alice's call Trip are Sum and Total Gov-coins, Top is S plus Total and Held is at least Top, and then no longer Alice holds Sum Gov-coins, no longer Bob holds Total Gov-coins and Sum and Total Gov-coins are locked in Orga on Trip between the provider Bob and the customer Alice. Arrive: Auction:5, only if the terms of Trip release Back to Alice and Rest to Bob on arrival, Pot is Sum plus Total and Pot is Back plus Rest, and then no longer Sum and Total Gov-coins are locked in Orga on Trip between the provider Bob and the customer Alice, Alice holds Back Gov-coins and Bob holds Rest Gov-coins. Arrive in full: The fare and the deposit of Sum and Total Gov-coins locked in Orga on Trip are released to Bob in full on arrival with Alice, provided Alice and Bob are both at the destination, the refund min{P, λδ} is released to Alice, and the rest of the fare and the deposit to Bob, with δ = max{0, t_pickup − t₀ − τ}, the terms of Trip release Back to Alice and Pot to Bob on arrival, Pot is Sum plus Total and Back is less than 1. Cancel: Auction:6, only if the terms of Trip release Back to Alice and Rest to Bob on cancellation, Pot is Sum plus Total and Pot is Back plus Rest, and then no longer Sum and Total Gov-coins are locked in Orga on Trip between the provider Bob and the customer Alice, Alice holds Back Gov-coins and Bob holds Rest Gov-coins. Cancel for the rider: Alice may cancel her ride with Bob taking back the Sum and Total Gov-coins locked in Orga on Trip, provided no pickup of Alice's ride is recorded within the maximum pickup delay, the fare and the deposit are released to Alice if Alice was at the pickup point from t₀ to t₀ + T, and otherwise the fare to Alice and the deposit to Bob, the terms of Trip release Pot to Alice and Rest to Bob on cancellation, Pot is Sum plus Total and Rest is less than 1.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: the fare and the deposit of x's call c are n and m z-coins = fare_deposit(x,c,z,n,m); the terms of c release p to x and q to y on arrival = arrival_terms(x,y,c,p,q); the terms of c release p to x and q to y on cancellation = cancel_terms(x,y,c,p,q).
After the fare and the deposit of n and m c-coins locked in z on k are released to y in full on arrival with x: no longer x's fare is locked, no longer y's deposit is locked, no longer x and y have a ride, x has a pickup recorded with no one, y has a pickup recorded with no one, x has a release of the ride with y, y has a release of the ride with x, no longer n and m c-coins are locked in z on k between the provider y and the customer x, y holds n c-coins and y holds m c-coins.
After x cancels x's ride with y taking back the n and m c-coins locked in z on k: no longer x's fare is locked, no longer y's deposit is locked, no longer x and y have a ride, x has a pickup recorded with no one, y has a pickup recorded with no one, x has a release of the ride with y, y has a release of the ride with x, no longer n and m c-coins are locked in z on k between the provider y and the customer x, x holds n c-coins and x holds m c-coins.
Environment: the fare and the deposit of x's call c are n and m z-coins; the terms of c release p to x and q to y on arrival; the terms of c release p to x and q to y on cancellation.
