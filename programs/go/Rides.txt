Rides
Rides extends Networked Organisation and restricts and strengthens Second-Score Auction (GRH, Grassroots Ride-Hailing, in preparation).
A *driver* is a provider and a *rider* a customer.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a networked organisation.
1. ■ Award: Second-Score Auction:2, only if the fare and the deposit are in Orga-coins, Alice holds the fare, and Bob holds the deposit on top of his stake, and the fare and the deposit are then locked between the provider Bob and the customer Alice (Networked Organisation:2).
