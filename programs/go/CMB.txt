CMB: Commercial Bank
CMB instantiates the bylaws of GBank and restricts it.
A commercial bank is an organisation that may issue Dollar-denominated coins and bonds, with two roles, director and loan officer; the directors jointly decide the acts of the bank; a loan officer decides a loan within their lending limit, a number of Dollars the directors set, and the directors decide a loan above it.  A participant who holds no role is a customer.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Orga a commercial bank.
1. ■ Founding.  Alice may found Orga and become its first director.
2. ■ Customers.  Bob becomes a customer of Orga and ceases to be one by an act of Bob and the directors.
3. ■ Officers.  The directors may appoint a participant a director or a loan officer, and remove one.
4. ■ Deposit.  Bob, a customer, may swap Dollar-denominated coins he holds for Orga-coins.
5. ■ Loan.  Bob, a customer, and Carol, a loan officer, for a loan within Carol's lending limit, or the directors, for any loan, may jointly decide to swap Orga-coins for Bob-bonds.
6. ■ Lending limit.  The directors may set the lending limit of Carol, a loan officer.
