GCoop: Grassroots Cooperative
GCoop extends GO and DGC (SGC, Sovereign Grassroots Currencies: A CBDC Architecture for Credit and Monetary Policy, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27727).
A cooperative is an organisation with two roles, member, held by every participant, and board member; the members decide by a majority of the members, one member one vote, and the board members jointly decide the acts the members delegate to the board.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a cooperative.
1. ■ Founding.  Alice may found Orga and become its first member.
2. ■ Membership.  Bob becomes a member of Orga by an act of Bob and the members; his membership ends by an act of Bob or of the other members.
3. ■ Board.  The members may elect a member to the board, and remove one from it.
