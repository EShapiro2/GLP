Commercial Bank
Commercial Bank extends Bank, and weakens it.
A *commercial bank* is a bank that may issue Dollar-denominated coins and bonds, whose bylaws name two roles, *director*, held by its first officer, and *loan officer*; a loan officer decides a loan within their *lending limit*, a number of Dollars. A participant who holds no role is a *customer*.
Bob and Carol are persons, Orga a commercial bank.
1. ■ Leave, Expel: Organisation:3, only with the consent of both Bob and the directors.
2. ■ Bank deposit: Bob, a customer, may swap Dollar-denominated coins he holds for Orga-coins.
3. ■ Loan: Bob, a customer, and those who decide the loan for Orga may jointly swap Orga-coins for Bob-bonds.
4. ■ Set lending limit: The directors may set the lending limit of Carol, a loan officer.

Commercial Bank extends Bank, and weakens it.
A *commercial bank* is a bank that may issue Dollar-denominated coins and bonds, whose bylaws name two roles, *director*, held by its first officer, and *loan officer*; a loan officer decides a loan within their *lending limit*, a number of Dollars. A participant who holds no role is a *customer*.
Alice, Bob, Carol, Dave and Erin are persons, Dave possibly Bob, Orga a commercial bank, Dollar a fiat currency, Day a maturity date, Sum, Total, Limit, Votes and Double numbers.
1. ■ Leave: Organisation:3 Leave, only if Orga is an organisation, with the consent of the directors of Orga. Officer leave: Organisation:3 Officer leave, only if Orga is an organisation, with the consent of the directors of Orga. Expel: Organisation:3 Expel, only if Orga is an organisation, with the consent of Bob and the directors of Orga. Officer expel: Organisation:3 Officer expel, only if Orga is an organisation, with the consent of Bob and the directors of Orga.
2. ■ Bank deposit: Bob may swap Sum Dave-coins for Sum Orga-coins, provided Bob is a customer of Orga and Dave has issued coins of the denomination Dollar. Bank deposit own coins: Bob may swap Sum Bob-coins for Sum Orga-coins, provided Bob is a customer of Orga and Bob has issued coins of the denomination Dollar.
3. ■ Loan: Alice may swap Sum Orga-coins of Orga with Bob for Total Bob-bonds due Day, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice is a loan officer of Orga, Orga has the lending limit of Alice at the number Limit, Sum is at most Limit and Bob is a customer of Orga. Loan by the directors: Alice may swap Sum Orga-coins of Orga with Bob for Total Bob-bonds due Day, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice is a director of Orga, Orga holds the votes of at least Votes persons to lend Bob Sum Orga-coins due Day, Double is Votes times 2, Orga has fewer than Double persons in the role Director and Bob is a customer of Orga. Vote to lend: Erin may vote to lend Bob Sum Orga-coins of Orga due Day, provided Erin is a director of Orga and Orga has Bob as a participant.
4. ■ Set lending limit: Alice may set the lending limit of Carol in Orga at Limit, provided Alice is a director of Orga, Orga holds the votes of at least Votes persons to set the lending limit of Carol at Limit, Double is Votes times 2, Orga has fewer than Double persons in the role Director and Orga has Carol in the role Lender. Vote on lending limit: Erin may vote to set the lending limit of Carol in Orga at Limit, provided Erin is a director of Orga and Orga has Carol in the role Lender.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: z has the lending limit of x at the number n = lending_limit(z,x,n); z holds v's vote to set the lending limit of x at n = vote_limit(z,v,x,n); z holds v's vote to lend y n z-coins due e = vote_lend(z,v,y,n,e).
y is a customer of z: y is a participant of z and y holds no role in z.
x is a loan officer of z: x holds the role Lender in z and z has x in the role Lender.
Roles: the directors of z = directors(z), listed by z has y in the role Director and y holds the role Director in z; those who decide the loan for z = loan_deciders(z).
x is a director of z: x holds the role Director in z.
After y swaps n w-coins for n z-coins: no longer y holds n w-coins, z holds n w-coins, no longer z holds n z-coins and y holds n z-coins.
After v votes to lend y n z-coins of z due e, which requires z does not hold v's vote to lend y n z-coins due e: z holds v's vote to lend y n z-coins due e.
After x swaps n z-coins of z with y for m y-bonds due e: no longer z holds n z-coins, y holds n z-coins, no longer y holds m y-bonds due e, z holds m y-bonds due e and z holds no one's vote to lend y n z-coins due e.
the earlier lending limit of x in z has ended: z has the lending limit of x at no number.
After v votes to set the lending limit of x in z at n, which requires z does not hold v's vote to set the lending limit of x at n: z holds v's vote to set the lending limit of x at n.
After w sets the lending limit of x in z at n: the earlier lending limit of x in z has ended, z has the lending limit of x at the number n and z holds no one's vote to set the lending limit of x at n.
