ORH: Ride-Hailing Organisation
ORH extends GNO and amends GRA (GRH, Grassroots Ride-Hailing, in preparation).
A driver is a provider and a rider a customer.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a ride-hailing organisation.
1. ■ Driving.  GRA, where Bob bids and drives as a driver while his stake is at least S, the fare and the deposit in Orga-coins, and the deposit locked on top of his stake.
2. ■ Riding.  GRA, where Alice calls as a rider while she accepts Orga-coins and holds the fare.
Pragmas
Look: 🚕, network
Organisations: the organisations Alice participates in, and her roles in each.
Founding: Alice [Found] <organisation> <bylaws> <role>
Acts of the organisation: Bob [Propose] <act>
  Bob [Vote]
  Bob [Propose as named] <act>
  Bob [Vote as named] <credential>
Participation: Bob [Join]
  Orga: Bob asks to participate.
  Bob [Leave]
  Orga [Expel]
Officers: Orga [Appoint] <role>
  Bob: Orga asks you to take a role.
  Orga [Dismiss] <role>
  Bob [Resign role] <role>
Amendment: Orga [Amend] <bylaws>
Coins: the coins Alice holds.
Bonds: the bonds Alice holds.
Minting: Alice [Mint coin] <denomination> <amount> [Mint bond] <denomination> <maturity> <amount>
Swap: Alice [Swap] <amount given> <issuer wanted> <maturity wanted> <amount wanted>
  Bob: Alice asks to swap coins or bonds with you.
Acceptance: Alice [Accept] <limit>
Payment: Alice [Pay] <amount> [Payout] <amount> [Spend] <payee> <amount>
Redemption: Alice [Redeem] <issuer wanted> <maturity wanted>

Arbitration: Alice [Raise dispute] <arbiter>
  Bob [Raise dispute] <arbiter>
  Carol [Decide] <to customer> <to provider>

Bid: Bob [Bid] <fare> <pickup delay>
