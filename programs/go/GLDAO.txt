GLDAO: Grassroots Liquid DAO
GLDAO extends GDAO.
A member's delegate is another member whose vote counts for theirs.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Orga a DAO.
1. ■ Delegating.  Bob, a member, may make Carol, a member he knows, his delegate, one at a time, and may revoke it.
2. ■ Counting.  A member who has not voted on a proposal counts as voting as their delegate did, down a chain of delegates; a member whose chain ends with no vote, or in a cycle, is not counted.
