CC: Community Currency
CC instantiates the bylaws of GBank and restricts it.
A community currency is the Dollar-denominated coins of an organisation, Orga, with one role, treasurer; the treasurers jointly decide the acts of Orga.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a community currency.
1. ■ Founding.  Alice may found Orga for the Dollar and become its first treasurer.
2. ■ Treasurers.  Bob becomes a treasurer of Orga by an act of Bob and the treasurers; he ceases to be one by an act of Bob or of the other treasurers.
3. ■ Drawing.  Alice, a treasurer, may swap Alice-coins for Orga-coins.
4. ○ Fiat redemption.  Bob may redeem an Orga-coin he holds against one Dollar from any treasurer.
