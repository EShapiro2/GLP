CnC: Consumers' Cooperative
CnC extends GCoop and TC.
A consumers' cooperative is a cooperative that is a trading company whose customers are its members; its fee is a number of Dollars the members set, and the cost of a good is the coins Orga paid for it.
Alice, Bob and Eve are persons, Orga a consumers' cooperative.
1. ■ Membership.  GCoop:2, where Bob pays Orga the fee on becoming a member.
2. ■ Officers.  TC:2, where the members appoint and remove the buyers, keepers and sellers.
3. ■ Sale.  TC:5, where Eve is a member and pays the cost of the good.
