GDAO: Grassroots DAO
GDAO instantiates GCoop and restricts it.
A DAO is a cooperative with one role besides member, steward; the steward decides the admission of a member, and the members decide every other act of the DAO by a majority of the members.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a DAO.
1. ■ Founding.  Alice may found Orga and become its steward and first member.
2. ■ Joining.  Bob becomes a member of Orga by an act of Bob and the steward.
