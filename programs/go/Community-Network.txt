Community Network
Community Network extends Organisation and Denominated Currencies (SGC, Sovereign Grassroots Currencies: A CBDC Architecture for Credit and Monetary Policy, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27727), and restricts and strengthens Organisation.
A *community network* is created by a backed community, and uses its community coins. Its bylaws name the role *arbiter*. A *provider* is a member of the community whose *stake*, the community coins they hold, is at least *S*, a number of community coins, and a *customer* is a member of the community who accepts community coins. A *dispute* is over community coins locked between a provider and a customer, on *terms* that set the arbiter's fee.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Orga a community network.
1. ■ Set stake: Those who decide for Orga may set S.
2. ■ Arbitration. Raise: Either Alice or Bob, the parties to a dispute, may raise it to an arbiter, Carol. Arbitrate: Carol may then decide the release of the locked coins to Alice and to Bob, less the fee the terms set, which is released to her.
3. ■ Members. Found: Organisation:1, only if Alice is a member of the community. Join: Organisation:3, only if Bob is a member of the community.
4. ○ Loss of membership. Those who decide for Orga will end Bob's participation in Orga if he ceases to be a member of the community.

Community Network extends Organisation and Denominated Currencies (SGC, Sovereign Grassroots Currencies: A CBDC Architecture for Credit and Monetary Policy, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27727), and restricts and strengthens Organisation.
A *community network* is created by a backed community, and uses its community coins. Its bylaws name the role *arbiter*. A *provider* is a member of the community whose *stake*, the community coins they hold, is at least *S*, a number of community coins, and a *customer* is a member of the community who accepts community coins. A *dispute* is over community coins locked between a provider and a customer, on *terms* that set the arbiter's fee.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Orga a community network, Gov an organisation, S and Held numbers; in clause 2 Alice is the customer and Bob the provider.
1. ■ Set stake: Those who decide for Orga may set the stake of Orga at S, provided Orga is an organisation.
2. ■ Arbitration. Raise: Alice may raise to Carol her dispute with the provider Bob in Orga, provided coins are locked in Orga between the provider Bob and the customer Alice and Carol is an arbiter of Orga. Provider raise: Bob may raise to Carol his dispute with the customer Alice in Orga, provided coins are locked in Orga between the provider Bob and the customer Alice and Carol is an arbiter of Orga. Arbitrate: Carol may decide the release to Alice and to Bob of the coins locked between them in Orga. The coins are released less the fee the terms set, which is released to Carol.
3. ■ Members. Found: Organisation:1 Found, only if Alice is a participant of Gov, and then Orga has Gov as its community. Join: Organisation:3 Join, only if Orga has Gov as its community and Bob is a participant of Gov.
4. ○ Loss of membership. Those who decide for Orga will end Bob's participation in Orga if he ceases to be a member of the community.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: z has the stake at the number n = stake(z,n); z has c as its community = community(z,c); x has coins locked in z as the customer of y = customer_lock(x,y,z); y has coins locked in z as the provider of x = provider_lock(y,x,z); w holds the dispute of x and y in z = dispute(w,x,y,z).
coins are locked in z between the provider y and the customer x: y has coins locked in z as the provider of x and x has coins locked in z as the customer of y.
x is an arbiter of z: x holds the role Arbiter in z and z has x in the role Arbiter.
the earlier stake of z has ended: z has the stake at no number.
x is a customer of z: x knows z, z has Gov as its community, x is a participant of Gov and x accepts a Dollar-denominated Gov-coin in payment.
y is a provider of z: y knows z, z has Gov as its community, y is a participant of Gov, z has the stake at the number S, y holds Held Gov-coins and Held is at least S.
After g sets the stake of z at n: the earlier stake of z has ended and z has the stake at the number n.
After x raises to w x's dispute with the provider y in z, which requires w does not hold the dispute of x and y in z: w holds the dispute of x and y in z.
After y raises to w y's dispute with the customer x in z, which requires w does not hold the dispute of x and y in z: w holds the dispute of x and y in z.
After w decides the release to x and to y of the coins locked between them in z: no longer w holds the dispute of x and y in z and no longer coins are locked in z between the provider y and the customer x.
Parameters: x has coins locked in z as the customer of y; y has coins locked in z as the provider of x.
