GNL: Network Lodging
GNL extends GNO, GDO (GRH, Grassroots Ride-Hailing, in preparation) and REC.
A host is a provider and a traveller a customer.  A listing names a place, the nights offered and the price a night in Orga-coins; its terms say what is released to whom on cancellation.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a lodging organisation.
1. ■ Listing.  Bob, a host, may post a listing to the travellers he points to, and a traveller may pass it to the travellers they point to; Bob may withdraw it.
2. ■ Booking.  Alice, a traveller, and Bob, who know each other, may jointly book nights of his listing; Alice's payment for them and the deposit the terms set for Bob, on top of his stake, are then locked.
3. ■ Check-in and checkout.  Alice may record her check-in when she is at the place on the first night, and her checkout when she leaves it after the last; the payment and the deposit are then released to Bob.
4. ■ Cancelling.  Alice or Bob may cancel a booking before the first night, and the payment and the deposit are released to Alice and to Bob as the terms say; if no check-in is recorded on the first night while Alice is at the place, she may cancel and both are released to her.
5. ■ Dispute.  Alice, checked in, or Bob may raise a dispute over the payment and the deposit before checkout (GNO:4).
6. ◆ Review.  REC:1, where Alice recommends Bob for the stay after checkout.
