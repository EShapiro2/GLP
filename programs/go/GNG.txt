GNG: Network Gig Work
GNG extends GNO and GDO (GRH, Grassroots Ride-Hailing, in preparation).
A worker is a provider and a client a customer.  A job names the work, its price in Orga-coins and its deadline; its terms say what is released to whom on cancellation.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a gig-work organisation.
1. ■ Job.  Alice, a client, may post a job to the workers she points to, and a worker may pass it to the workers they point to; Alice may withdraw it.
2. ■ Engagement.  Alice and Bob, a worker, may jointly engage Bob for the job; Alice's payment and the deposit the terms set for Bob, on top of his stake, are then locked.
3. ◆ Delivery.  Bob may attest that he delivered the work to Alice.
4. ■ Acceptance.  Alice may accept delivered work, and the payment and the deposit are then released to Bob; she may reject it, raising a dispute (GNO:4).
5. ■ Cancelling.  Alice or Bob may cancel the engagement before delivery, and the payment and the deposit are released to Alice and to Bob as the terms say; if no delivery is attested by the deadline, Alice may cancel and both are released to her.
6. ◆ Review.  After acceptance, Alice may attest a review of the work, which Bob carries as a worker.
