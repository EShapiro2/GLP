Gigs
Gigs unites Community Network, Overlay and Recommendations, and extends and weakens the union (GRH, Grassroots Ride-Hailing, in preparation).
A *worker* is a provider and a *client* a customer. A *job* names the work, its price in community coins and its deadline; its terms say what is released to whom on cancellation.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a community network.
1. ■ Job. Post: Alice, a client, may post a job to the workers she points to. Pass: A worker may pass it to the workers they point to. Withdraw job: Alice may withdraw it.
2. ■ Engage: Alice may engage Bob, a worker, for the job with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other; Alice's payment and the deposit the terms set for Bob, on top of his stake, are then locked.
3. ◆ Deliver: Bob may attest that he delivered the work to Alice.
4. ■ Acceptance. Accept work: Alice may accept delivered work, and the payment and the deposit are then released to Bob. Reject: Alice may reject it, raising a dispute (Community Network:2).
5. ■ Cancelling. Cancel: Alice or Bob may cancel the engagement before delivery, and the payment and the deposit are then released to Alice and to Bob as the terms say. Lapse: If no delivery is attested by the deadline, Alice may cancel and both are then released to her.
6. ◆ Recommend: Recommendations:1, where Alice recommends Bob for the work after acceptance.

A *worker* is a provider and a *client* a customer. A *job* names the work, its price in community coins and its deadline; its terms say what is released to whom on cancellation.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Orga a community network, Gov an organisation, Kept, Sum, Total, Top, Pot, Back and Rest numbers, Job a job, Skill a skill or a service; in clause 1 Carol is a worker, in clause 4 an arbiter, and in clause 6 Skill is the job.
1. ■ Job. Post: Alice may post Job in Orga, provided Alice is a client of Orga. Alice posts the job to the workers she points to. Send job: Job of Alice goes to Bob in Orga, provided Alice has Job posted in Orga, Alice points to Bob and Bob is a worker of Orga. Pass: Bob may pass Job of Alice to Carol in Orga, provided Bob is a worker of Orga, Bob has received Job of Alice in Orga, Bob points to Carol, Carol is a participant of Gov, Orga has the stake at the number S, Carol holds Kept Gov-coins and Kept is at least S. Withdraw job: Alice may withdraw Job from Orga.
2. ■ Engage: Alice may engage Bob for Job in Orga locking Sum and Total Gov-coins, with the consent of Bob, provided Bob is a worker of Orga, Alice has Job posted in Orga, Alice and Bob know each other, the terms of Job set the payment at Sum and the deposit at Total, Top is S plus Total and Held is at least Top. Alice's payment and the deposit the terms set for Bob, on top of his stake, are then locked.
3. ◆ Deliver: Bob may attest the delivery of Job to Alice, provided Alice and Bob have an engagement for Job.
4. ■ Acceptance. Accept work: Alice may accept Job of Bob in Orga releasing to Bob the Sum and Total Gov-coins locked, provided Bob has attested the delivery of Job to Alice. The payment and the deposit are then released to Bob. Reject: Alice may reject Job of Bob raising to Carol the dispute in Orga, provided Bob has attested the delivery of Job to Alice, Sum and Total Gov-coins are locked in Orga on Job between the provider Bob and the customer Alice and Carol is an arbiter of Orga. Alice raises the dispute by Community Network:2.
5. ■ Cancelling. Cancel: Alice may cancel her engagement of Bob for Job in Orga releasing Back and Rest of the Sum and Total Gov-coins locked, provided Bob has not attested the delivery of Job to Alice, the terms of Job release Back to the customer and Rest to the provider on cancellation, Pot is Sum plus Total and Pot is Back plus Rest. Cancel for the client: Alice may cancel her engagement of Bob for Job in Orga taking back the Sum and Total Gov-coins locked, provided Bob has not attested the delivery of Job to Alice, the terms of Job release Pot to the customer and Rest to the provider on cancellation, Pot is Sum plus Total and Rest is less than 1. Cancel for the worker: Alice may cancel her engagement of Bob for Job in Orga releasing to Bob the Sum and Total Gov-coins locked, provided Bob has not attested the delivery of Job to Alice, the terms of Job release Back to the customer and Pot to the provider on cancellation, Pot is Sum plus Total and Back is less than 1. Worker cancel: Bob may cancel his engagement by Alice for Job in Orga releasing Back and Rest of the Sum and Total Gov-coins locked, provided Bob has not attested the delivery of Job to Alice, the terms of Job release Back to the customer and Rest to the provider on cancellation, Pot is Sum plus Total and Pot is Back plus Rest. Worker cancel for the client: Bob may cancel his engagement by Alice for Job in Orga releasing to Alice the Sum and Total Gov-coins locked, provided Bob has not attested the delivery of Job to Alice, the terms of Job release Pot to the customer and Rest to the provider on cancellation, Pot is Sum plus Total and Rest is less than 1. Worker cancel for the worker: Bob may cancel his engagement by Alice for Job in Orga taking back the Sum and Total Gov-coins locked, provided Bob has not attested the delivery of Job to Alice, the terms of Job release Back to the customer and Pot to the provider on cancellation, Pot is Sum plus Total and Back is less than 1. The payment and the deposit are then released to Alice and to Bob as the terms say. Lapse: Alice may cancel her engagement of Bob for Job in Orga taking back the Sum and Total Gov-coins locked, provided no delivery of Job is attested by the deadline. The payment and the deposit are then both released to Alice.
6. ◆ Recommend: Recommendations:1, only if Alice has accepted Skill of Bob.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x has j posted in z = posted(x,j,z); y has received j of x in z = received(y,j,x,z); x has engaged y for j = engaged(x,y,j); y is engaged by x for j = engaged_by(y,x,j); y has attested the delivery of j to x = delivered(y,j,x); x has accepted j of y = accepted(x,j,y); x has rejected j of y = rejected(x,j,y); no delivery of j is attested by the deadline, for x = lapsed(x,j); the terms of j set the payment at n and the deposit at m, for x = terms(x,j,n,m); the terms of j release p to the customer and q to the provider on cancellation, for x = cancel_terms(x,j,p,q).
y is a worker of z: y is a provider of z.
x is a client of z: x is a customer of z.
x and y have an engagement for j: x has engaged y for j and y is engaged by x for j.
After x posts j in z, which requires x does not have j posted in z: x has j posted in z.
After j of x goes to y in z, which requires y has not received j of x in z: y has received j of x in z.
After y passes j of x to w in z, which requires w has not received j of x in z: w has received j of x in z.
After x withdraws j from z: no longer x has j posted in z.
After x engages y for j in z locking n and m c-coins, which requires x and y do not have an engagement for j: x and y have an engagement for j, no longer x holds n c-coins, no longer y holds m c-coins and n and m c-coins are locked in z on j between the provider y and the customer x.
After y attests the delivery of j to x, which requires y has not attested the delivery of j to x: y has attested the delivery of j to x.
