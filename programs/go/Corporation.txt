Corporation
Corporation unites Organisation and Denominated Currencies, and extends the union (SGC, Sovereign Grassroots Currencies: A CBDC Architecture for Credit and Monetary Policy, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27727).
A *corporation* is an organisation that holds, pays and issues Dollar-denominated coins and bonds.
