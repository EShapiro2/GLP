Lodging
Lodging extends Community Network and Overlay (GRH, Grassroots Ride-Hailing, in preparation), and restricts Community Network and Recommendations.
A *host* is a provider and a *traveller* a customer. A *listing* names a place, the nights offered and the price a night in community coins; its terms say what is released to whom on cancellation.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a community network.
1. ■ Listing. Post: Bob, a host, may post a listing to the travellers he points to. Pass: A traveller may pass it to the travellers they point to. Withdraw listing: Bob may withdraw it.
2. ■ Book: Alice, a traveller, may book nights of Bob's listing with the consent of Bob, provided Alice and Bob know each other; Alice's payment for them and the deposit the terms set for Bob, on top of his stake, are then locked.
3. ■ Check-in and checkout. Check in: Alice may record her check-in when she is at the place on the first night. Check out: Alice may record her checkout when she leaves it after the last night; the payment and the deposit are then released to Bob.
4. ■ Cancelling. Cancel: Alice or Bob may cancel a booking before the first night, and the payment and the deposit are then released to Alice and to Bob as the terms say. No-show: If no check-in is recorded on the first night while Alice is at the place, she may cancel and both are then released to her.
5. ■ Raise: Community Network:2, only before checkout, and by Alice only if she is checked in.
6. ◆ Recommend: Recommendations:1, where Alice recommends Bob for the stay after checkout.

Lodging extends Community Network and Overlay (GRH, Grassroots Ride-Hailing, in preparation), and restricts Community Network and Recommendations.
A *host* is a provider and a *traveller* a customer. A *listing* names a place, the nights offered and the price a night in community coins; its terms say what is released to whom on cancellation.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Orga a community network, Gov an organisation, Mark a fiat currency, Listing a listing, Deal an agreement, Sum, Total, Top, Pot, Back and Rest numbers, Skill a skill or a service; in clause 1 Carol is a traveller, in clause 5 an arbiter and Deal is the listing, and in clause 6 Skill is the listing.
1. ■ Listing. Post: Bob may post Listing in Orga, provided Bob is a host of Orga. Bob posts the listing to the travellers he points to. Send listing: Listing of Bob goes to Alice in Orga, provided Bob has Listing posted in Orga, Bob points to Alice and Alice is a traveller of Orga. Pass: Alice may pass Listing of Bob to Carol in Orga, provided Alice is a traveller of Orga, Alice has received Listing of Bob in Orga, Alice points to Carol, Carol is a participant of Gov and Carol accepts a Mark-denominated Gov-coin in payment. Withdraw listing: Bob may withdraw Listing from Orga.
2. ■ Book: Alice may book nights of Listing of Bob in Orga locking Sum and Total Gov-coins, with the consent of Bob, provided Alice is a traveller of Orga, Bob has Listing posted in Orga, Alice and Bob know each other, the terms of Listing set the payment at Sum and the deposit at Total, Orga has the stake at the number S, Bob holds Held Gov-coins, Top is S plus Total and Held is at least Top. Alice's payment for them and the deposit the terms set for Bob, on top of his stake, are then locked.
3. ■ Check-in and checkout. Check in: Alice may record her check-in at Listing of Bob, provided Alice and Bob have a booking of Listing and Alice is at the place of Listing on the first night. Check out: Alice may record her checkout from Listing of Bob in Orga releasing to Bob the Sum and Total Gov-coins locked, provided Alice leaves the place of Listing after the last night. The payment and the deposit are then released to Bob.
4. ■ Cancelling. Cancel: Alice may cancel her booking of Listing of Bob in Orga releasing Back and Rest of the Sum and Total Gov-coins locked, provided it is before the first night of Listing, the terms of Listing release Back to the customer and Rest to the provider on cancellation, Pot is Sum plus Total and Pot is Back plus Rest. Cancel for the traveller: Alice may cancel her booking of Listing of Bob in Orga taking back the Sum and Total Gov-coins locked, provided it is before the first night of Listing, the terms of Listing release Pot to the customer and Rest to the provider on cancellation, Pot is Sum plus Total and Rest is less than 1. Cancel for the host: Alice may cancel her booking of Listing of Bob in Orga releasing to Bob the Sum and Total Gov-coins locked, provided it is before the first night of Listing, the terms of Listing release Back to the customer and Pot to the provider on cancellation, Pot is Sum plus Total and Back is less than 1. Host cancel: Bob may cancel his booking of Listing by Alice in Orga releasing Back and Rest of the Sum and Total Gov-coins locked, provided it is before the first night of Listing, the terms of Listing release Back to the customer and Rest to the provider on cancellation, Pot is Sum plus Total and Pot is Back plus Rest. Host cancel for the traveller: Bob may cancel his booking of Listing by Alice in Orga releasing to Alice the Sum and Total Gov-coins locked, provided it is before the first night of Listing, the terms of Listing release Pot to the customer and Rest to the provider on cancellation, Pot is Sum plus Total and Rest is less than 1. Host cancel for the host: Bob may cancel his booking of Listing by Alice in Orga taking back the Sum and Total Gov-coins locked, provided it is before the first night of Listing, the terms of Listing release Back to the customer and Pot to the provider on cancellation, Pot is Sum plus Total and Back is less than 1. The payment and the deposit are then released to Alice and to Bob as the terms say. No-show: Alice may cancel her booking of Listing of Bob in Orga taking back the Sum and Total Gov-coins locked, provided no check-in at Listing is recorded on the first night while Alice is at the place. The payment and the deposit are then both released to Alice.
5. ■ Raise: Community Network:2 Raise, only if Alice is checked in at Deal of Bob. Provider raise: Community Network:2 Provider raise, only if Bob has nights of Deal booked by Alice.
6. ◆ Recommend: Recommendations:1, only if Alice has checked out of Skill of Bob.
