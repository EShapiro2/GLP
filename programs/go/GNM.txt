GNM: Network Marketplace
GNM extends GNO and GDO (GRH, Grassroots Ride-Hailing, in preparation).
A seller is a provider and a buyer a customer.  A listing names a good, its price in Orga-coins and how it is delivered; its terms say what is released to whom on cancellation.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a marketplace.
1. ■ Listing.  Bob, a seller, may post a listing to the buyers he points to, and a buyer may pass it to the buyers they point to; Bob may withdraw it.
2. ■ Order.  Alice, a buyer, and Bob may jointly order the good; Alice's payment and the deposit the terms set for Bob, on top of his stake, are then locked.
3. ◆ Delivery.  Alice may attest that the good was delivered to her, and the payment and the deposit are then released to Bob.
4. ■ Cancelling.  Alice or Bob may cancel the order before delivery, and the payment and the deposit are released to Alice and to Bob as the terms say.
5. ■ Dispute.  Alice or Bob may raise a dispute over the payment and the deposit before they are released (GNO:4).
6. ◆ Review.  After delivery, Alice may attest a review of the good, which Bob's listing carries.
