Single-Person Company
Single-Person Company inherits from Corporation.
A *single-person limited-liability company* is a corporation whose bylaws name one role, the *sole officer*, and admit no participant but the sole officer.
Bob is a person, Orga a single-person limited-liability company.
1. ■ Join: Organisation:3, only if Bob is the first officer of Orga.
