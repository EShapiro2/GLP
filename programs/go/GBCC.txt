GBCC: Grassroots Backed Community Currency
GBCC extends CC and restricts it.
f is a fraction, 0 ≤ f < 1.  Orga's account is a bank account in Dollars, held in Orga's name by its treasurers.  A treasurer, Alice, is backed when the Alice-coins Orga holds are at most the Dollars Alice paid into the account and the coins Alice holds of the other treasurers, leaving out the fraction f of them of whom she holds the most.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a community currency.
1. ■ Treasurers.  CC:2, only if every treasurer stays backed.
2. ■ Drawing.  CC:3, only while Alice stays backed.
3. ◆ Account.  Alice, a treasurer, may pay Dollars into the account, and the treasurers jointly record them as paid in by Alice.
4. ○ Fiat redemption.  CC:4, from the account while it holds Dollars, and from a treasurer when it holds none.
