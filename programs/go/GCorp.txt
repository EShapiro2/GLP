GCorp: Grassroots Corporation
GCorp extends GO and DGC (SGC, Sovereign Grassroots Currencies: A CBDC Architecture for Credit and Monetary Policy, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27727).
A corporation is an organisation that holds, pays and issues Dollar-denominated coins and bonds.
Pragmas
Look: 🏢, money
Organisations: the organisations Alice participates in, and her roles in each.
Coins: the coins Alice holds.
Bonds: the bonds Alice holds.
