Gossip
Gossip extends Friendship.
*Posts* have a *budget*, a parameter of Gossip: what a post has when made, and the *cost* of a forward. Gossip has a *gossip protocol*, a parameter: to which of their friends a holder may forward a post, and with what part of the rest of the budget. A person *holds* any post they made or forwarded to them.
Alice and Bob are persons.
1. ■ Post: Alice may make a post.
2. ■ Tell: Alice who holds a post, the budget of which covers the cost, may forward it to her friend Bob with a part of the rest of the budget, both according to the gossip protocol.
