Friends Market
Friends Market composes Promoted Matching with Marketplace, with a test of the match of offers and requests and a budget of Orga-coins.
A post is an *offer* or a *request*. An *offer* is a listing of Marketplace, and a *request* names a good and a price in Orga-coins. An offer and a request match when the offer is of what the request seeks and the price of the request is at least the price of the offer.
Alice is a person, Orga a networked organisation.
1. ■ Post: Matching:1, a request only if Alice is a buyer of Orga and an offer only if she is a seller.
