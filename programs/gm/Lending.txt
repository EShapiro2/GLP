Lending
Lending instantiates Free Matching with a test of the match of loans, gifts and wishes and a fixed budget.
A post is a *loan* or a *gift*, stating a thing its maker lends or gives away, or a *wish*, stating a thing its maker seeks to borrow or receive. A loan or a gift matches a wish when it is of the thing the wish seeks.
