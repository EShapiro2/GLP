Matching
Matching extends Gossip.
Posts have a *test*, a parameter of Matching, whether two *match*.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
1. ■ Return: Bob, who holds two matching posts, one from Alice, forwards to Alice the second post.
2. ■ Meet: Alice and Carol, both holding matching posts one made by Alice and one made by Carol, may jointly come to know each other.
Logic pragmas
Matching extends Friendship.
*Posts* have a *test*, a parameter of Matching, whether two *match*. Posts have a *budget*, a parameter of Matching: what a post has when made, and the *cost* of a forward. A person *holds* any post they made or forwarded to them.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Post and Other posts, Budget and Rest numbers.
1. ■ Post: Alice may make Post with the budget Budget, provided Budget is a budget of a new post.
2. ■ Tell: Alice may forward Post to Bob with the budget Rest, provided Alice holds the post Post, Alice has the budget Budget for Post, Budget covers the cost of a forward leaving Rest and Alice and Bob are friends.
3. ■ Return: Bob forwards Other to Alice, provided Bob holds the post Post from Alice, Bob holds the post Other and Post and Other match.
4. ■ Withdraw: Alice may withdraw Post.
5. ■ Meet: Alice and Carol may jointly come to know each other, provided Alice has made the post Post, Carol has made the post Other, Alice holds the post Other, Carol holds the post Post and Post and Other match.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x holds the post p = post(x,p); y holds the post p from x = post_from(y,p,x); x has made the post p = made(x,p); p and q match, for x = match(x,p,q); x has the budget n for p = budget(x,p,n); n is a budget of a new post, for x = new_budget(x,n); n covers the cost of a forward leaving m, for x = covers(x,n,m).
After x makes p with the budget n, which requires x has not made the post p: x has made the post p, x holds the post p and x has the budget n for p.
After x forwards p to y with the budget n, which requires y does not hold the post p from x: y holds the post p from x, y holds the post p and y has the budget n for p.
After x forwards p to y, which requires y does not hold the post p from x: y holds the post p from x and y holds the post p.
After x withdraws p: no longer x has made the post p and no longer x holds the post p.
Environment: p and q match, for x; n is a budget of a new post, for x; n covers the cost of a forward leaving m, for x.
