GSN: Grassroots Social Network
Alice and Bob are persons.
1. ■ Message.  Alice may send a message to their friend Bob.
2. ■ Group.  Alice may create a group, of which they are the leader and first member.
3. ■ Joining.  Alice, the leader of a group, may add their friend Bob to it, by an act of both.
4. ■ Leaving.  Bob, a member of a group, may decide to leave it; when the leader leaves, the group ends.  A member of a group is removed from it if they cease to be a friend of its leader.
5. ■ Group message.  Bob, a member of a group, may send a message to the group, and the leader delivers it to every current member.
Pragmas
Groups: the groups Alice is a member of.
