GKK: Grassroots Keys and Knowledge
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
1. ○ Identity.  Alice may act once to establish their identity by choosing their unique key pair.  They make themselves known by the public key, sign their acts with the private key, and are accountable for every act signed with it.
2. ■ Acquaintance.  Alice and Bob may come to know each other by an act of both.
3. ■ Introduction.  If Alice and Bob know each other and Alice knows Carol, then an act of Alice and Bob can introduce Carol to Bob, following which Bob knows Carol.
4. ■ Signing.  Alice signs every item they create or forward, and whoever holds an item sees who signed it, in order.
Pragmas
Contacts: the persons Alice knows.
Identity: Alice [Establish]
Acquaintance: Alice [Acquaint] <person>
  Bob: Alice wishes that you know each other.
Introduction: Alice [Introduce] <person>
  Bob: Alice asks to introduce Carol to you.
