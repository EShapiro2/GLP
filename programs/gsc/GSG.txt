GSG: Grassroots Social Graph
GSG extends GKK.
Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts.
Alice and Bob are persons.
1. ○ Befriend: Alice and Bob, who trust each other and are not friends, may jointly become friends.
2. ■ Unfriend: Alice may end her friendship with Bob.
3. ○ Loss of Trust. Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
Logic pragmas
GSG extends GKK.
Alice *trusts* Bob when she knows Bob personally and believes him to keep the undertakings he signs under grassroots contracts.
Alice and Bob are persons.
1. ○ Befriend: Alice and Bob may jointly become friends, provided Alice and Bob know each other. Alice and Bob will become friends only if they trust each other.
2. ■ Unfriend: Alice may end her friendship with Bob.
3. ○ Loss of Trust. Alice will end her friendship with Bob if she ceases to trust him.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x counts y as a friend = friend(x,y).
x and y are friends: x counts y as a friend and y counts x as a friend.
After x and y become friends, which requires x and y are not friends: x and y are friends.
x and y are no longer friends: no longer x and y are friends.
After x ends x's friendship with y: x and y are no longer friends.
