GnW: Guardians 'n' Wards
GnW amends GSG.
Alice, Bob and Erin are persons, Carol and Dave wards.  A relation is a friendship or the membership of a group.  Bob trusts Carol, a ward of Alice, when he trusts Alice.
1. ■ Creation.  Alice may create a ward, Carol, and establish Carol's identity by choosing Carol's key pair.  Alice is then the creator, a guardian and a friend of Carol, a friendship Carol may not end.
2. ■ Joint guardianship.  Bob, a friend of a guardian of Carol, may become a guardian and a friend of Carol by an act of Bob and the guardians of Carol; Bob's guardianship ends by an act of Bob, by an act of the other guardians of Carol, or when Bob ends his friendship with Carol.  Carol may not end a friendship with her guardian.
3. ■ Ward and person.  Carol, a ward of Alice, and Bob may come to know each other by an act of both.  Carol enters a relation with Bob provided they know each other and Alice and Bob are friends, by an act of Carol, Alice and Bob.  Carol, and the agent in a relation with Carol, may end it, as may Alice.
4. ■ Ward and ward.  Carol, a ward of Alice, and Dave, a ward of Bob, may come to know each other by an act of both.  Carol enters a relation with Dave provided they know each other, Alice knows Dave and is a friend of Bob, by an act of Carol, Alice, Dave and Bob, Alice and Bob possibly one person.
5. ■ Retiring.  The guardians of Carol may retire Carol; every guardianship and relation of Carol then ends and Carol takes no act.
Pragmas
Wards: the wards Alice is a guardian of.
Creation: Alice [Enter] <name>
  Carol: Alice asks to be your guardian.
Joint guardianship: Alice [Make guardian]
  Bob: Alice asks you to be a guardian of her ward Carol.
  Erin: Alice asks that Bob be a guardian of your ward Carol.
  Alice [End guardian] <person>
  Erin: Alice asks to end Bob's guardianship of your ward Carol.
  Bob [Resign] [Guardian unfriend]
Ward and person: Bob [Ward acquaint] <person>
  Carol: Bob wishes that you know each other.
  Alice [Ward befriend]
  Carol: Alice asks that you and Bob befriend.
  Bob: Alice asks that you and her ward Carol befriend.
  Alice [Ward unfriend] <person>
  Carol [Ward end] <person>
Ward and ward: Carol [Wards acquaint] <person>
  Dave: Carol wishes that you know each other.
  Alice [Wards befriend]
  Carol: Alice asks that you and Dave befriend.
  Dave: Alice asks that you and Carol befriend.
  Bob: Alice asks that Carol and your ward Dave befriend.
Retiring: Alice [Retire]
  Erin: Alice asks to retire your ward Carol.
