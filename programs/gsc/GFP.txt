GFP: Grassroots Forwarding with Provenance
GFP extends GSG.
The author of an item is its first signer.
Alice and Bob are persons.
1. ■ Signing.  Alice may create an item by signing it.
2. ■ Forwarding.  Alice may forward an item she holds to her friend Bob, adding her signature to the signatures the item carries; Bob sees them.
Pragmas
Items: the items Alice holds, each with its signatures.
Signing: Alice [Sign] <text>
Forwarding: Alice [Forward]
