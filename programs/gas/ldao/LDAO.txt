LDAO: Liquid DAO
Alice and Bob are persons.  LDAO extends DAO.
1. ■ Delegating.  A member of a DAO, the steward included, may make another member, whom they know, their delegate in it, one at a time; the steward records the delegation.
2. ■ Revoking.  A member may revoke their delegation at any time; the steward erases it.
3. ■ Counting.  Once the delegate of a member who has not voted on a proposal has a vote on it counted, the steward counts the member as voting the same way, and the member cannot vote on it any more; so a vote passes down a chain of delegates, and a member whose chain ends with no vote, or runs into a cycle, is not counted.
Pragmas
Delegate: my delegate in each DAO.
Delegations: the delegations Alice records.
Delegating: Bob [Delegate] <delegate>
  Alice [Delegate own] <delegate>
Revoking: Bob [Revoke] <delegate>
  Alice [Revoke own] <delegate>
