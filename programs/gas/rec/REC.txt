REC: Recommendations
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.  REC extends GKK.
1. ◆ Recommending.  Alice may recommend Bob, whom they know, for something, a skill or a service, once for each thing; Bob is not asked and keeps the recommendation.
2. ■ Showing.  Bob may show a recommendation they hold to Carol, whom they know, once; Carol then sees who recommended Bob and for what, but does not thereby come to know the recommender.
3. ■ Withdrawing.  Alice may withdraw a recommendation; it is gone from Bob and from everyone Bob showed it to, and Alice may not make it again.
Pragmas
Given: the recommended people Alice vouches for.
Received: the endorsements Alice holds.
Seen: the vouched ones Alice saw.
Recommending: Alice [Recommend] <for what>
Showing: Bob [Show] <recommendation>
Withdrawing: Alice [Withdraw recommendation]
