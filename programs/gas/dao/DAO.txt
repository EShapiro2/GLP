DAO: Grassroots DAO
Alice and Bob are persons.  DAO extends GKK.
1. ■ Founding.  Alice may found a DAO under a name of their own not used before; Alice is its steward and first member.
2. ■ Joining.  Alice, the steward of a DAO, may admit Bob, whom they know and who is not yet a member, by an act of both.
3. ■ Proposing.  A member of a DAO may make a proposal to it, each proposal once; its steward records it and delivers it to every member but the steward, the proposer included.
4. ■ Voting.  A member who holds a proposal of their DAO may vote for or against it, once and before the steward decides it; the steward records the vote and delivers it to every member but the steward.
5. ■ Deciding.  The steward may decide a proposal, passed or rejected, once; the steward delivers the decision to every member but the steward.
6. ■ Majority.  The steward decides a proposal only once more than half of the DAO's members at that time have voted the same way on it, and passes it exactly when they voted for it.
Pragmas
DAOs: the DAOs Alice is a member of.
Proposals: the proposals Alice sees.
For: the yes votes Alice has seen.
Against: the no votes Alice has seen.
Passed: the passed proposals Alice knows.
Founding: Alice [Found] <name>
Joining: Alice [Admit] <person>
  Bob: Alice invites you to their DAO.
Proposing: Bob [Propose] <proposal>
  Alice [Propose own] <proposal>
Voting: Bob [Vote for] <proposal> [Vote against] <proposal>
  Alice [Vote own for] <proposal> [Vote own against] <proposal>
Deciding: Alice [Pass] <proposal> [Reject] <proposal>
