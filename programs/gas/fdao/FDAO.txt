FDAO: Funded DAO
Alice and Bob are persons.  FDAO extends DAO and GB.
1. ■ Funding.  A member of a DAO may give it coins they hold; its steward, who is not asked, keeps them apart as the DAO's, and they leave the DAO only as grants.
2. ■ Granting.  The steward may pay the proposer of a passed proposal, once per proposal, any amount of the DAO's coins of one issuer; the proposer is not asked.
3. ○ Amount.  The steward grants a passed proposal the amount it asks for, as far as the DAO's coins allow.
Pragmas
Treasury: the dao coins Alice keeps.
Grants: the granted ones Alice paid.
Funding: Bob [Fund] <issuer> <amount>
  Alice [Fund own] <issuer> <amount>
Granting: Alice [Grant] <proposal> <issuer> <amount>
  Alice [Grant own] <proposal> <issuer> <amount>
