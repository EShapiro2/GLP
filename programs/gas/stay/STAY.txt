STAY: Grassroots Stays
Alice and Bob are persons.  STAY extends GB.
1. ◆ Hosting.  Alice, who can host guests at a place, may list it under a name not among their places.
2. ■ Booking.  Bob, who knows Alice, and Alice may jointly decide that Bob stays at a place of Alice's on a night later than Alice's date, for a price Bob pays in Alice-coins; a place is booked at most once a night.
3. ■ Cancelling.  Before the night, by Alice's date, Alice may cancel a stay, paying Bob back its whole price in Alice-coins; Bob is not asked.
4. ■ Reviewing.  Once the night has come, by Bob's date, Bob may review their stay, once; Alice keeps the review and is not asked.
Pragmas
Places: the places Alice hosts.
Stays: the stays Alice has.
Guests: the guests Alice hosts.
Reviews: the reviews Alice received.
Hosting: Alice [List place] <place>
Booking: Bob [Book] <place> <night> <amount>
  Alice: Bob asks to stay at your place.
Cancelling: Alice [Cancel]
Reviewing: Bob [Review] <review>
