LIB: Grassroots Library
Alice and Bob are persons.  LIB extends GB.
1. ◆ Shelving.  Alice may put a thing of their own on their shelf, under a name they have not used before.
2. ■ Lending.  Alice and Bob, who know each other, may jointly decide that Alice lends Bob a thing from Alice's shelf until a day later than Alice's date; it leaves the shelf, and Bob holds it but cannot lend it on.
3. ■ Returning.  Bob may return a borrowed thing at any time; Alice is not asked, and it goes back on Alice's shelf.
4. ○ On time.  Bob returns a borrowed thing by the agreed day.
Pragmas
Shelf: the things Alice has.
Lent: the things lent out by Alice.
Borrowed: what Alice borrowed.
Shelving: Alice [Shelve] <thing>
Lending: Alice [Lend] <thing> <until>
  Bob: Alice offers to lend you a thing.
Returning: Bob [Give back]
