JOINT: Joint Accounts
Alice and Bob are persons.  JOINT extends GC.
1. ■ Opening.  Alice and Bob, who know each other, may jointly open a joint account, under a name of Alice's not already used for one.
2. ■ Depositing.  Either of them may move coins they hold into their joint account; the other is not asked.
3. ■ Withdrawing.  Coins leave a joint account only by an act of both, to either of them.
Pragmas
Joint: the joint money Alice shares.
Accounts: the accounts Alice shares.
Opening: Alice [Open account] <name>
  Bob: Alice asks to open a joint account with you.
Depositing: Alice [Deposit] <issuer> <amount>
Withdrawing: Alice [Withdraw] <issuer> <amount>
  Bob: Alice asks to withdraw from your joint account.
