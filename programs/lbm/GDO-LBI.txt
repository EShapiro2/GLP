GDO-LBI: Grassroots Directed Overlay, Location-Based, Bounded Out-Degree Instance
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.  GDO-LBI amends GDO.  A person has a location.  d > 0 is an integer and e > 0.
1. ■ Pointing to friend.  GDO:1, taken iff either Alice points to less than d persons and Bob is closest to Alice among all of Alice's unpointed friends, or Bob is closer to Alice by more than e than some person pointed to by Alice.
2. ■ Transitive pointing.  GDO:2, taken iff Carol is closer to Alice by more than e than some person pointed to by Alice.
3. ■ Ceasing to point.  GDO:3, taken iff Alice points to more than d persons, with Bob furthest from Alice among them.
Pragmas
Pointing: the persons Alice points to.
