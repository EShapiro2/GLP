GDO: Grassroots Directed Overlay
GDO extends GSG.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
1. ■ Point friend: Alice, who does not point to her friend Bob, may point to Bob.
2. ■ Point transitive: Alice, who knows Carol and points to Bob who points to Carol, may point to Carol.
3. ■ Unpoint: Alice, who points to Bob, may cease pointing to Bob.
Logic pragmas
GDO extends GSG.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
1. ■ Point friend: Alice may point to Bob, provided Alice and Bob are friends.
2. ■ Point transitive: Alice may point to Carol, provided Alice knows Carol, Alice points to Bob and Bob points to Carol.
3. ■ Unpoint: Alice may cease pointing to Bob.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x points to y = points_to(x,y).
After x points to y, which requires x does not point to y: x points to y.
After x ceases pointing to y: no longer x points to y.

Pointing: the persons Alice points to.
