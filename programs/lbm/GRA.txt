GRA: Grassroots Ride Auction
GRA extends GDO.
Alice is a rider, Bob a driver and Carol a person.  A person can be either a rider or a driver but not both.  A person has a location.
1. ■ Call.  Alice, who has no open call and no ride, may call for bids on a ride from the persons Alice points to and the persons they point to, stating the pickup point, the destination, the bidding period and the terms.
2. ■ Bid.  Bob, who received the call, may bid a fare and a pickup delay before the bidding period ends; Alice may not open a bid before then.
3. ■ Award.  When the bidding period ends, Alice awards the ride to the bidder the terms select among the bidders who have no ride, at the fare the terms set, and Alice's fare and the deposit the terms set for Bob are then locked; if the terms select no bidder, the call is closed.
4. ■ Pickup.  The pickup is recorded when Alice and Bob are both at the pickup point.
5. ■ Arriving.  When Alice and Bob are both at the destination, the fare and the deposit are released to Alice and to Bob as the terms say.
6. ■ Cancelling.  If no pickup is recorded within the maximum pickup delay, Alice may cancel, and the fare and the deposit are released to Alice and to Bob as the terms say.
7. ■ Withdrawing.  Alice may withdraw their open call before the bidding period ends.

Rides: every ride of Alice.
