Sanctions
Sanctions extends Organisation.
A *sanction* is a measure an organisation imposes on one of its participants; it lasts until it is lifted or annulled. What a sanction does, a parameter of Sanctions, is said by a contract written from it. The bylaws name the role *arbiter*.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Orga an organisation.
1. ■ Impose: Those who decide for Orga may impose a sanction on Bob, a participant of Orga.
2. ■ Lift: Those who decide for Orga may lift it.
3. ■ Appeal. Appeal: Bob may appeal his sanction to Carol, an arbiter of Orga. Uphold, Annul: Carol may then uphold the sanction or annul it.
Logic pragmas
Sanctions extends Organisation.
A *sanction* is a measure an organisation imposes on one of its participants; it lasts until it is lifted or annulled. What a sanction does, a parameter of Sanctions, is said by a contract written from it. The bylaws name the role *arbiter*.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Orga an organisation.
1. ■ Impose: Those who decide for Orga may impose a sanction on Bob in Orga, provided Bob is a participant of Orga.
2. ■ Lift: Those who decide for Orga may lift the sanction on Bob in Orga.
3. ■ Appeal. Appeal: Bob may appeal his sanction in Orga to Carol, provided Bob is under a sanction in Orga and Carol is an arbiter of Orga. Uphold: Carol may uphold the sanction on Bob in Orga. Annul: Carol may annul the sanction on Bob in Orga.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: z has imposed a sanction on y = sanction(z,y); y is under a sanction in z = under_sanction(y,z); w holds y's appeal in z = appeal(w,y,z).
x is an arbiter of z: x holds the role Arbiter in z and z has x in the role Arbiter.
After g imposes a sanction on y in z, which requires z has not imposed a sanction on y: z has imposed a sanction on y and y is under a sanction in z.
After g lifts the sanction on y in z: no longer z has imposed a sanction on y and no longer y is under a sanction in z.
After y appeals y's sanction in z to w, which requires w does not hold y's appeal in z: w holds y's appeal in z.
After w upholds the sanction on y in z: no longer w holds y's appeal in z.
After w annuls the sanction on y in z: no longer w holds y's appeal in z, no longer z has imposed a sanction on y and no longer y is under a sanction in z.
