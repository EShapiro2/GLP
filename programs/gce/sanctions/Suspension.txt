Suspension
Suspension instantiates Sanctions with a suspension for a sanction, and restricts Organisation.
A participant under a suspension keeps their place in the organisation but may not vote on its acts.
Bob is a person, Orga an organisation.
1. ■ Vote: Organisation:2 Vote, only if Bob is not under a suspension in Orga.
