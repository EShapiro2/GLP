Warnings
Warnings extends and restricts Organisation.
A *warning* is a record in an organisation that a participant broke its bylaws or an undertaking.
Bob is a person, Orga an organisation, Limit a number.
1. ■ Warn: Those who decide for Orga may warn Bob in Orga, provided Bob is a participant of Orga.
2. ■ Expel: Organisation:3 Expel, only if Orga holds Limit warnings to Bob. Officer expel: Organisation:3 Officer expel, only if Orga holds Limit warnings to Bob.
