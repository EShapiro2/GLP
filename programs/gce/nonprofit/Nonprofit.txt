Nonprofit
Nonprofit inherits from Corporation.
A *nonprofit* is a corporation that pays none of its coins, notes or bonds to its officers.
Alice and Bob are parties, Orga a nonprofit.
1. ■ Nondistribution. Pay, Pay issuer: Notes:3 and Coins:1; Pay accepted coins, Pay own accepted coins, Pay accepted coins to the issuer: Denominated Currencies:4; each only if Bob is not an officer of Alice.
