Chat
Chat strengthens Groups, and restricts it.
A *message* is what a person sends.
Alice and Bob are persons and Today a date.
1. ■ Message: Groups:1, only if the current date is Today, and then Alice has sent the message to Bob on Today and Bob has received it from Alice on Today.
