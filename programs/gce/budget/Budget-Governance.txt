Budget Governance
Budget Governance extends Constitution (GO, Grassroots Organisations: Building Digital Banks, Networks, Corporations and Cooperatives, in preparation).
A *budget* of a constitutional democratic community is a split of a *total* into three amounts it decides together, such as for parks, roads and schools; its current split is the *status quo*. The *distance* between two splits is the sum of the differences of their first, of their second and of their third amounts.
A budget is decided in *epochs*. In each, a member may state an *ideal value* for it, the budget they most prefer, and then propose budgets. A member's *gain* from a budget is the distance between their ideal value and the status quo less that between their ideal value and the budget; a member who stated none is taken to have the status quo as their ideal value.
The *required share* of the members, a parameter of Budget Governance, is at least one half and less than all; by default, more than half. Each budget has a *step*, a number more than zero.
The *score* of a budget is the largest gain that at least the required share of the members have from it.
The *leader* of an epoch is its proposal whose score has been shown to be the highest so far; when there is none, the leader's score is zero.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a constitutional democratic community, Fee a budget of Orga.
1. ■ Create: Alice may create a budget Fee of Orga, stating its status quo and its step, provided Alice is a member of Orga, its amounts each at least 0, on the votes of Orga for it.
2. ■ Open: Alice may open an epoch on Fee, stating its voting period and its proposal period, provided Alice is a member of Orga and no epoch on Fee is open.
3. ■ Voting. State ideal: Alice may state her ideal value for Fee once in an epoch, before its voting period ends, provided Alice is a member of Orga, its amounts each at least 0 and summing to the total of Fee. Abstain: When the voting period of an epoch on Fee ends, the status quo is taken as the ideal value of each member of Orga who stated none.
4. ■ Proposing. Propose value: After the voting period of an epoch on Fee ends, Alice may propose a split for Fee with a claimed score, provided Alice is a member of Orga, Alice has no proposal in the epoch, its amounts each at least 0 and summing to the total of Fee, and the claimed score is at least the leader's score plus the step of Fee.
5. ■ Lead: Before the proposal period of an epoch on Fee ends, a proposal of the epoch becomes its leader, provided at least the required share of the members gain at least its claimed score from it, and fewer than the required share gain more than it.
6. ■ Close. Close with a leader, Close without a leader: After the proposal period of an epoch on Fee ends, the epoch closes, and its leader, if it has one, becomes the status quo of Fee.

Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a constitutional democratic community, Fee a budget, Ep an epoch, Sx, Sy, Sz, Total, Step, Ix, Iy, Iz, Vx, Vy, Vz, Parta, Score, Lead, Need, Enough, Count, Rest and Sum numbers.
1. ■ Create: Alice may create the budget Fee of Orga at Sx by Sy by Sz of the total Total with the step Step, provided Alice is a member of Orga, Orga does not have the budget Fee, Sx is at least 0, Sy is at least 0, Sz is at least 0, Parta is Sx plus Sy and Total is Parta plus Sz, on the votes of Orga for it.
2. ■ Open: Alice may open the epoch Ep on Fee of Orga, provided Alice is a member of Orga and Orga has the budget Fee. Alice states its voting period and its proposal period.
3. ■ Voting. State ideal: Alice may state the ideal value Ix by Iy by Iz in Orga's epoch Ep, provided Alice is a member of Orga, Orga has the epoch Ep on Fee open, the voting period of Orga's epoch Ep has not ended, Orga has the total of Fee at the number Total, Ix is at least 0, Iy is at least 0, Iz is at least 0, Parta is Ix plus Iy and Total is Parta plus Iz. Abstain: Orga takes Sx by Sy by Sz as Bob's ideal value in Orga's epoch Ep, provided Orga has Bob as a participant, Orga has the epoch Ep on Fee open, the voting period of Orga's epoch Ep has ended, Orga has the budget Fee at Sx by Sy by Sz and Bob has not voted in Orga's epoch Ep.
4. ■ Proposing. Propose value: Alice may propose Vx by Vy by Vz with the score Score in Orga's epoch Ep, provided Alice is a member of Orga, Orga has the epoch Ep on Fee open, the voting period of Orga's epoch Ep has ended, Orga has the total of Fee at the number Total, Vx is at least 0, Vy is at least 0, Vz is at least 0, Parta is Vx plus Vy, Total is Parta plus Vz, Orga has the leading score of Ep at the number Lead, Orga has the step of Fee at the number Step, Need is Lead plus Step and Score is at least Need.
5. ■ Lead: Alice's proposal leads Orga's epoch Ep with Vx by Vy by Vz at Score, provided Alice has proposed Vx by Vy by Vz with the score Score in Orga's epoch Ep, the proposal period of Orga's epoch Ep has not ended, Orga has the leading score of Ep at the number Lead, Score is more than Lead, Orga has the epoch Ep on Fee open, Orga has the budget Fee at Sx by Sy by Sz, Orga counts at least Enough persons in Ep as gaining at least Score from Vx by Vy by Vz over Sx by Sy by Sz, Enough members suffice in Orga, Orga has at least Count persons as a participant, Orga has at most Count persons as a participant, Sum is Rest plus Enough, Sum is Count plus 1 and Orga counts at least Rest persons in Ep as gaining at most Score from Vx by Vy by Vz over Sx by Sy by Sz.
6. ■ Close. Close with a leader: Orga's epoch Ep on Fee closes with Vx by Vy by Vz, provided the proposal period of Orga's epoch Ep has ended and Orga has the leading value of Ep at Vx by Vy by Vz. Close without a leader: Orga's epoch Ep on Fee closes unchanged, provided the proposal period of Orga's epoch Ep has ended and Orga has the leading score of Ep at the number 0.
