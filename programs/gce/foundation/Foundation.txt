Foundation
Foundation weakens Nonprofit.
A *foundation* is a nonprofit whose bylaws name one role, *trustee*, held by every officer, and state a *purpose*; a person joins only with the consent of every trustee.
Bob is a person, Orga a foundation, Motion an act of Orga.
1. ■ Join: Organisation:3 Join, with the consent of the trustees of Orga.
2. ■ Amend: Organisation:5 Amend, only if Motion keeps the purpose of Orga.
3. ○ Purpose. The trustees will use the coins of Orga only for its purpose.
