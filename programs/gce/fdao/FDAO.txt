FDAO: Funded DAO
FDAO extends DAO and GC.
Alice and Bob are persons.
1. ■ Fund: A member of a DAO may give it coins they hold; its steward keeps them apart as the DAO's.
2. ■ Grant: The steward may grant the proposer of a passed proposal, once per proposal, any amount of the DAO's coins of one issuer.
3. ○ Amount. The steward will grant a passed proposal the amount it asks for, as far as the DAO's coins allow.
Logic pragmas
FDAO extends DAO and GC.
Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Delos a DAO and Prop a proposal, Sum a number, Carol possibly Alice or Bob, Dave possibly Alice, Bob or Carol.
1. ■ Fund: Carol may give Alice's Delos Sum Dave-coins, provided Carol is a member of Alice's Delos and Alice is the steward of Delos.
2. ■ Grant: Alice may grant Carol Sum Dave-coins of Delos for Prop, provided Alice is the steward of Delos, Alice holds Prop of Delos as passed and Carol has proposed Prop to Alice's Delos.
3. ○ Amount. The steward will grant a passed proposal the amount it asks for, as far as the DAO's coins allow.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x keeps n z-coins for d = dao_coin(x,z,d,*n); x has granted p of d = granted(x,p,d).
After y gives x's d n z-coins: no longer y holds n z-coins and x keeps n z-coins for d.
After x grants y n z-coins of d for p, which requires x has not granted p of d: no longer x keeps n z-coins for d, y holds n z-coins and x has granted p of d.
Interface pragmas
Treasury: the dao coins Alice keeps.
Grants: the granted ones Alice paid.
Fund: Carol [Fund] <sum>
Grant: Alice [Grant] <carol> <sum>
