Partnership
Partnership instantiates Corporation with partnership bylaws; x is a participant of z for x is entitled to propose m in z; x is a participant of z for x is entitled to take m for z; x is a participant of z for x is entitled to vote on m in z; z holds the votes of at least Votes persons on m, Double is Votes times 2 and z has fewer than Double persons as a participant for the votes decide m according to the bylaws of z, and restricts it.
A *partnership* is a corporation whose bylaws name one role, *partner*, held by every participant; the partners decide by a majority of the partners, but a person joins only with the consent of every partner.
Bob is a person, Orga a partnership.
1. ■ Join: Organisation:3 Join, with the consent of the partners of Orga.
2. ○ Partners' liability. Every partner will be accountable at law for the undertakings of Orga, with Orga.
