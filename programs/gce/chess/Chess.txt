Chess
Chess extends Friendship.
The *rules of chess* are the Laws of Chess of FIDE, the International Chess Federation. A *position* is the board with the side to move, the rights to castle and to capture en passant, and the positions since the last capture or pawn move, so that it shows a draw by repetition or by the fifty-move rule. The *time limit* of a game is the number of days a player has for each move.
Alice and Bob are persons.
1. ■ Start. Start: Alice and Bob may jointly start a game of chess with a time limit, provided they are friends; Alice then plays white and Bob black, from the first position. Start as black: Alice and Bob may jointly start a game of chess with a time limit, provided they are friends; Alice then plays black and Bob white, from the first position.
2. ■ Move: Alice may move in a game against Bob, provided it is her turn and the move is legal by the rules of chess; the game is then at the position after the move, from the date of the move.
3. ■ Win: Alice may claim a game against Bob, provided Bob is to move and is checkmated; Alice has then won the game.
4. ■ Time: Alice may claim a game against Bob on time, provided Bob is to move and the time limit has passed since the last move; Alice has then won the game.
5. ■ Resign: Alice may resign a game against Bob; Bob has then won the game.
6. ■ Draw. Draw: Alice and Bob may jointly agree a draw in a game. Claim draw: Alice may claim a draw in a game against Bob, provided the position is drawn by the rules of chess, as by stalemate, repetition or the fifty-move rule; the game is then drawn.
7. ○ Fair play. Alice will choose each of her moves herself, with no help from a chess engine.

Chess extends Friendship.
The *rules of chess* are the Laws of Chess of FIDE, the International Chess Federation. A *position* is the board with the side to move, the rights to castle and to capture en passant, and the positions since the last capture or pawn move, so that it shows a draw by repetition or by the fifty-move rule. The *time limit* of a game is the number of days a player has for each move.
Alice and Bob are persons, Game a game, Move a move, Board and Next positions, Side and Limit numbers, Day, Today and Due dates.
1. ■ Start. Start: Alice and Bob may jointly start Game at Board on Day with Limit days a move, provided Alice and Bob are friends, Board is the first position and the current date is Day. Start as black: Alice and Bob may jointly start Game as black at Board on Day with Limit days a move, provided Alice and Bob are friends, Board is the first position and the current date is Day.
2. ■ Move: Alice may move Game against Bob from Board of Day to Next of Today, provided Alice has the colour Side in Game, Side is the side to move of Board, 1 is the legality of Move on Board, Next is the position after Move on Board and the current date is Today.
3. ■ Win: Alice may claim Game against Bob at Board of Day, provided Bob has the colour Side in Game, Side is the side to move of Board and 1 is the mate of Board.
4. ■ Time: Alice may claim Game against Bob on time at Board of Day, provided Bob has the colour Side in Game, Side is the side to move of Board, Bob has the time limit Limit in Game, Due is Day plus Limit, the current date is Today and Today is more than Due.
5. ■ Resign: Alice may resign Game against Bob at Board of Day.
6. ■ Draw. Draw: Alice and Bob may jointly draw Game at Board of Day. Claim draw: Alice may claim a draw in Game against Bob at Board of Day, provided 1 is the draw of Board.
7. ○ Fair play. Alice will choose each of her moves herself, with no help from a chess engine.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x plays g against y at b since d = at(x,g,y,b,d); x has the colour s in g = colour(x,g,s); x has the time limit l in g = limit(x,g,l); x has started g = started(x,g); x has won g against y = won(x,g,y); x has drawn g with y = drawn(x,g,y); b is the first position = first(b); v is the legality of m on b = legal(v,m,b); c is the position after m on b = after(c,m,b); s is the side to move of b = side(s,b); v is the mate of b = mate(v,b); v is the draw of b = draw(v,b); the current date is e, for x = today(x,e).
Functions: b is the first position; v is the legality of m on b; c is the position after m on b; s is the side to move of b; v is the mate of b; v is the draw of b.
x and y play g at b since d: x plays g against y at b since d and y plays g against x at b since d.
After x and y start g at b on d with l days a move, which requires x has not started g: x has started g, x and y play g at b since d, x has the colour 1 in g, y has the colour 2 in g, x has the time limit l in g and y has the time limit l in g.
After x and y start g as black at b on d with l days a move, which requires x has not started g: x has started g, x and y play g at b since d, x has the colour 2 in g, y has the colour 1 in g, x has the time limit l in g and y has the time limit l in g.
After x moves g against y from b of d to c of e: no longer x and y play g at b since d and x and y play g at c since e.
After x claims g against y at b of d: no longer x and y play g at b since d and x has won g against y.
After x claims g against y on time at b of d: no longer x and y play g at b since d and x has won g against y.
After x resigns g against y at b of d: no longer x and y play g at b since d and y has won g against x.
After x and y draw g at b of d: no longer x and y play g at b since d, x has drawn g with y and y has drawn g with x.
After x claims a draw in g against y at b of d: no longer x and y play g at b since d, x has drawn g with y and y has drawn g with x.
