Committee Governance
Committee Governance extends Constitution (GO, Grassroots Organisations: Building Digital Banks, Networks, Corporations and Cooperatives, in preparation).
A *committee* of a constitutional democratic community is a choice of some of four candidates it makes, written as four numbers, one for each candidate, 1 where the candidate is chosen and 0 where not; the number chosen is its *size*, and its current choice is the *status quo*. The *distance* between two committees is the number of candidates one chooses and the other does not, the sum of the differences of their four numbers.
A committee is decided in *epochs*. In each, a member may state an *ideal value* for it, and then propose committees. A member's *gain* from a committee is the distance between their ideal value and the status quo less that between their ideal value and the committee; a member who stated none is taken to have the status quo as their ideal value.
The *required share* of the members is a parameter of Committee Governance; by default, more than half. Each committee has a *step*, a number more than zero.
The *score* of a committee is the largest gain that at least the required share of the members have from it.
The *leader* of an epoch is its proposal whose score has been shown to be the highest so far; when there is none, the leader's score is zero.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a constitutional democratic community, Board a committee of Orga.
1. ■ Create: Alice may create a committee Board of Orga, stating its status quo and its step, provided Alice is a member of Orga, its numbers each 0 or 1, on the votes of Orga for it.
2. ■ Open: Alice may open an epoch on Board, stating its voting period and its proposal period, provided Alice is a member of Orga and no epoch on Board is open.
3. ■ Voting. State ideal: Alice may state her ideal value for Board once in an epoch, before its voting period ends, provided Alice is a member of Orga, its numbers each 0 or 1 and summing to the size of Board. Abstain: When the voting period of an epoch on Board ends, the status quo is taken as the ideal value of each member of Orga who stated none.
4. ■ Proposing. Propose value: After the voting period of an epoch on Board ends, Alice may propose a committee for Board with a claimed score, provided Alice is a member of Orga, Alice has no proposal in the epoch, its numbers each 0 or 1 and summing to the size of Board, and the claimed score is at least the leader's score plus the step of Board.
5. ■ Lead: Before the proposal period of an epoch on Board ends, a proposal of the epoch becomes its leader, provided at least the required share of the members gain at least its claimed score from it, and fewer than the required share gain more than it.
6. ■ Close. Close with a leader, Close without a leader: After the proposal period of an epoch on Board ends, the epoch closes, and its leader, if it has one, becomes the status quo of Board.
