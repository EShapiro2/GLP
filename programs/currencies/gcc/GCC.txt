GCC: Grassroots Community Currency
GCC instantiates GBank with community currency bylaws and extends it.
A *community currency* is a bank whose coins are Dollar-denominated and whose bylaws name one role, *treasurer*; the treasurers jointly decide its acts.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a community currency.
1. ■ Draw: Alice, a treasurer, may swap Alice-coins for Orga-coins.
2. ○ Fiat redemption. Any treasurer will redeem an Orga-coin Bob holds against one Dollar.
Logic pragmas
GCC instantiates GBank with community currency bylaws, and extends it.
A *community currency* is a bank whose coins are Dollar-denominated and whose bylaws name one role, *treasurer*; the treasurers jointly decide its acts.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga a community currency, Sum a number.
1. ■ Draw: Alice may swap Sum Alice-coins for Sum Orga-coins, provided Alice is a treasurer of Orga.
2. ○ Fiat redemption. Any treasurer will redeem an Orga-coin Bob holds against one Dollar.
Logic pragmas.
x is a treasurer of z: x holds the role Treasurer in z.
After x swaps n x-coins for n z-coins: no longer x holds n x-coins, z holds n x-coins, no longer z holds n z-coins and x holds n z-coins.
