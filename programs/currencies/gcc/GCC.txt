GCC: Grassroots Community Currencies
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
1. ■ Community.  Alice may start a community, give it a unique name, and is then the first treasurer of the community.
2. ■ Minting.  While Alice is a treasurer of a community, they can mint community coins, which are declared as such, and as being denominated by a fiat currency.
3. ○ Redemption.  While Alice is a treasurer of a community, they undertake to redeem a community coin against a fiat coin of its denomination.
4. ■ Treasurers.  The treasurers of a community can appoint Alice as a new treasurer by their joint decision and the consent of Alice; they can remove a treasurer Bob by a joint decision of all treasurers except Bob.
5. ■ Payment.  Alice may decide to pay community coins they hold to a treasurer of the community, whom they know.
6. ■ Redemption in kind.  Alice may decide to redeem a community coin they hold against any coin held by a treasurer of the community, whom they know.
Pragmas

Communities: the communities Alice is a treasurer of.
