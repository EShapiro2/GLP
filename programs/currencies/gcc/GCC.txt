GCC: Grassroots Community Currencies
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.  GCC extends GDC: a community coin is a coin of the person who minted it.
1. ■ Community.  Alice may start a community, give it a unique name, declare its currency denomination, and is then the first treasurer of the community.
2. ■ Minting.  While Alice is a treasurer of a community, they can mint denominated community coins, which are declared as such.
3. ○ Fiat Redemption.  Alice may redeem a community coin they hold against a fiat coin of its denomination from the person who minted it, or from any treasurer of the community.
4. ■ Treasurers.  The treasurers of a community can appoint Bob as a new treasurer by their joint decision and the consent of Bob; they can remove a treasurer Carol by a joint decision of all treasurers except Carol.
5. ■ Coin Redemption.  Alice may decide to redeem a community coin they hold against any coin held by a treasurer of the community, whom they know.
Pragmas
Coins: the coins Alice holds.
Communities: the communities Alice is a treasurer of.
Community: Alice [Start] <name> <denomination>
Minting: Alice [Mint community coins] <amount>
Treasurers: Alice [Appoint] <person>
  Bob: Alice asks you to become a treasurer of their community.
  Alice: Alice asks to appoint Bob a treasurer of your community.
  Alice [Remove] <person>
  Alice: Alice asks to remove Carol as a treasurer of your community.
Coin Redemption: Alice [Redeem with treasurer] <community> <issuer given> <issuer wanted>
