BCC: Backed Community Currencies
BCC restricts GCC.
f is a fraction, 0 ≤ f < 1.  Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.  Alice's community coins out are those Alice minted and does not hold.  Alice is backed in a community if, leaving out the fraction f of the treasurers appointed to it, those of whom Alice holds the most coins in its denomination, Alice holds at least as many coins of the remaining treasurers but Alice as Alice has community coins of it out.  For a person removed as a treasurer of a community, the treasurers appointed to it are those appointed before they were removed.
1. ■ Treasurers.  GCC:2, only if every treasurer of the community stays backed.
2. ■ Minting.  GCC:3, only if Alice stays backed.
3. ■ Holding.  GC:2, GC:3, GC:4 and GCC:5, only if every treasurer, current or removed, who gives a coin by the act stays backed.

None: BCC adds no act, and GCC's lines stand.
