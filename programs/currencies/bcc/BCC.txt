BCC: Backed Community Currencies
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.  BCC extends GCC, with one parameter, the backing fraction f, declared on starting the community.
1. ■ Minting.  While Alice is a treasurer of a community, they may mint a community coin against personal coins, in the community's denomination, one from each of more than a fraction f of the other treasurers, which they hold and which are locked until the community coin is returned to them.
2. ■ Coin Redemption.  Alice may decide to redeem a community coin they hold from Bob, whom they know, against one of the coins backing it, if Bob is its issuer, or against any coin Bob holds, if Bob is a treasurer of the community.
