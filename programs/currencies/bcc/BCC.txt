BCC: Backed Community Currencies
BCC restricts GCC.
f is a fraction, 0 ≤ f < 1.  Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.  Alice's community coins out are those Alice minted and does not hold.  Alice is backed in a community if, leaving out the fraction f of its treasurers, those of whom Alice holds the most coins in its denomination, Alice holds at least as many coins of the remaining treasurers but Alice as Alice has community coins of it out.
1. ■ Treasurers.  GCC:2, only if every person with community coins of the community out stays backed.
2. ■ Minting.  GCC:3, only if Alice stays backed.
3. ■ Holding.  GC:2, GC:3 and GC:4, only if every person who gives a coin by the act stays backed.
