DGC: Denominated Grassroots Currencies
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.  Dollar names one denomination, and an Alice Dollar-denominated coin or bond, Alice-coin and Alice-bond for short, is one issued by Alice.
1. ■ Minting.  Alice may decide to issue their coins, of any denomination, and their bonds, of any denomination and of a maturity later than their own date.
2. ○ Date.  Alice keeps their date at the current date.
3. ■ Swap.  Alice and Bob may jointly decide to swap Dollar-denominated coins and bonds they hold.
4. ■ Acceptance.  Alice may decide to accept Carol-coins in payment up to a limit of their choosing, if they hold a Carol-coin or a Carol-bond; each Carol-coin paid to Alice counts against the limit, and Alice may decide again.
5. ■ Payment.  Alice may decide to pay Bob Dollar-denominated coins they hold that Bob accepts.
6. ■ Redemption.  Alice may decide to redeem a Bob-coin they hold against any Dollar-denominated coin or bond Bob holds, and to take a Bob-coin for a matured Bob-bond they hold.
7. ○ Pricing.  Alice prices their offerings in Alice-coins and accepts them in payment at those prices.
8. ○ Denomination.  An Alice-coin and an Alice-bond are each Alice's debt of one Dollar, the coin due now and the bond at its date; Alice redeems an Alice-coin on presentation at face value, by a Dollar-denominated coin or bond the bearer accepts or by one Dollar.
9. ■ Escrow.  Alice may deposit Dollar-denominated coins and bonds with Carol for Bob; Carol and Bob may jointly decide to release them to Bob, and Carol and Alice to return them to Alice, as the agreed condition is met or fails.
Pragmas
Coins: the coins Alice holds.
Bonds: the bonds Alice holds.
Minting: Alice [Mint coin] <denomination> <amount> [Mint bond] <denomination> <maturity> <amount>
Swap: Alice [Swap] <amount given> <issuer wanted> <maturity wanted> <amount wanted>
  Bob: Alice asks to swap coins or bonds with you.
Acceptance: Alice [Accept] <limit>
Payment: Alice [Pay] <amount>
Redemption: Alice [Redeem] <issuer wanted> <maturity wanted>
Escrow: Alice [Deposit] <amount>
  Carol: Alice asks to deposit coins or bonds with you.
  Carol [Release] <amount>
  Bob: Carol asks to release coins or bonds to you.
  Carol [Return] <amount>
  Alice: Carol asks to return coins or bonds to you.
