GDC: Grassroots Denominated Coins
Alice and Bob are persons.  GDC extends GC.  Dollar names one denomination, and a Dollar-denominated Alice-coin is an Alice-coin issued as Alice's debt of one Dollar.
1. ■ Minting.  Alice may decide to issue Alice-coins denominated by a fiat currency.
2. ○ Denomination.  A Dollar-denominated Alice-coin is Alice's debt of one Dollar, due now; Alice redeems it on presentation at face value, by a Dollar-denominated coin the bearer accepts or by one Dollar.
3. ○ Pricing.  Alice accepts Dollar-denominated Alice-coins in payment for their offerings at one Dollar each.
Pragmas
Minting: Alice [Dmint] <denomination> <amount>
Swap: Alice [Swap] <amount given> <issuer wanted> <denomination wanted> <amount wanted>
  Bob: Alice asks to swap coins with you.
Payment: Alice [Pay] <amount>
Redemption: Alice [Redeem] <denomination wanted> <issuer wanted>
