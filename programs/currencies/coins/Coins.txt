Coins
Coins extends Notes.
A *coin* is a note that is a unit of its issuer's debt. An *Alice-coin* is a coin issued by Alice.
Alice and Bob are parties.
1. ■ Pay issuer: Notes:3, without the consent of Bob, if the coins are Bob-coins.
2. ■ Redeem: Alice may redeem a Bob-coin she holds against any coin held by Bob, whom she knows.
3. ○ Pricing. Alice will price her offerings in Alice-coins and accept them in payment at those prices.
Logic pragmas
Coins extends Notes.
A *coin* is a note that is a unit of its issuer's debt. An *Alice-coin* is a coin issued by Alice.
Alice, Bob and Carol are parties, Sum a number, Carol possibly Alice or Bob.
1. ■ Pay issuer: Notes:3, without the consent of Bob, if Carol is Bob.
2. ■ Redeem: Alice may redeem a Bob-coin against a Carol-coin, provided Alice knows Bob.
3. ○ Pricing. Alice will price her offerings in Alice-coins and accept them in payment at those prices.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x holds a y-coin = note(x,y,coin); x holds n y-coins = note(x,y,coin,*n); x holds exactly n y-coins = note(x,y,coin,#n); z holds n w-coins in escrow from x for y on k = escrow_note(z,w,coin,x,y,k,*n).
After x pays the issuer y n y-notes of type t: no longer x holds n y-notes of type t and y holds n y-notes of type t.
After x redeems a y-coin against a z-coin: no longer x holds a y-coin, y holds a y-coin, no longer y holds a z-coin, x holds a z-coin and x knows z.
Interface pragmas
Coins: the coins Alice holds.
Swap: Alice [Swap] <amount given> <issuer wanted> <amount wanted>
  Bob: Alice asks to swap coins with you.
Pay: Alice [Pay] <amount>
  Bob: Alice asks to pay you coins.
Escrow: Alice [Deposit] <amount> <condition>
  Carol: Alice asks to deposit coins with you.
  Carol [Release] <amount>
  Bob: Carol asks to release coins to you.
  Carol [Return] <amount>
  Alice: Carol asks to return coins to you.
Pay issuer: Alice [Pay issuer] <amount>
Redeem: Alice [Redeem] <issuer wanted>
