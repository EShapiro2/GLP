GFF: Grassroots Feeds and Followers
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Bob and Carol possibly one person.  GFF extends GDO-L.  A feed is a label, named by its author.  Alice follows Carol's feed through Bob when Alice points to Bob with it.
1. ■ Feed.  Alice has a feed and may post to it an item, created or received.
2. ■ Delivery.  Alice, who follows Carol's feed through Bob, receives from Bob every item of Carol's feed that Bob holds.
Pragmas
Posts: Alice's feed.
Feeds: the feeds Alice follows; under a feed, the persons Alice follows it through and its items, each once.
Feed: Alice [Post] <item>
