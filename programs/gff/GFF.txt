GFF: Grassroots Feeds and Followers
Alice, Bob and Carol are agents.
1. ■ Feed.  Alice has a feed and may post to it an item, created or received.
2. ■ Following Volition.  Friends follow each other.  Alice, who knows Bob but is not their friend, may want to follow Bob, and subsequently cease to want to follow Bob.
3. ■ Following via Friend.  When Alice wants to follow Bob, Alice follows Bob through their friend Carol that follows Bob.
4. ■ Delivery.  Bob sends every item they post to every friend; Carol forwards to Alice who follows Bob through Carol every item on Bob's feed not signed by Alice.
5. ■ Updating.  Every so often, Carol updates their friend Alice whom Carol follows.
Pragmas
Posts: Alice's feed.
Feeds: the agents Alice wants to follow; under an agent, the friends Alice follows them through, the friends who follow them, and the items of their feed, each once, the signatures of each of its paths under it.
Feed: Alice [Post] <item>
Following Volition: Alice [Follow]
  Alice [Unfollow]
