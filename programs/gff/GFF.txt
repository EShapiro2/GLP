GFF: Grassroots Feeds and Followers
GFF extends GDO-L.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Bob and Carol possibly one person.  A feed is named by its author, and Alice follows Carol's feed through Bob when Alice has a Carol-pointer to Bob.
1. ■ Feed.  Alice has a feed and may post to it an item, created or received.
2. ■ Delivery.  Alice, who has a Carol-pointer to Bob, receives from Bob every item of Carol's feed that Bob holds.
Pragmas
Posts: the items Alice holds.
Feeds: the feeds Alice follows; under a feed, the persons Alice follows it through and its items, each once.
Feed: Alice [Post] <item>
