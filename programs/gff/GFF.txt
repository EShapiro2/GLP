GFF: Grassroots Feeds and Followers
Alice, Bob and Carol are agents.
1. ■ Feed.  Alice has a feed and may post to it an item, created or received.
2. ■ Following.  Friends follow each other.  Alice, who knows Bob, may decide to follow Bob, as well as end their following.  Alice, who decided to follow Bob and whose friend Carol follows Bob, follows Bob through Carol; Alice's following of Bob through Carol ends when Carol ceases to follow Bob or to be Alice's friend.
3. ■ Follower discovery.  Alice, who decided to follow Bob, asks their friend Carol whether Carol follows Bob, and Carol answers with a path to Bob, or that they do not.
4. ■ Delivery.  Bob sends every item they post to every friend; Carol, who follows Bob, sends every item on Bob's feed, after adding their signature, to every friend who follows Bob through Carol.
Pragmas

Feeds: the agents Alice follows.

% Following: when Alice decides to follow Bob, Alice's machine asks every friend whether they follow Bob (GFF:3) and follows Bob through the friend with the shortest path to Bob (GFF:2).
% Friendship: Alice, who decided to follow Bob and none of whose friends follows Bob, may befriend Bob (GSG:1), and friends follow each other (GFF:2).
% Shortening: once a day, Alice's machine asks every friend again (GFF:3) and, where a friend's path to Bob is shorter than Alice's, follows Bob through that friend instead (GFF:2).
% Reconnection: when Alice's following of Bob through Carol ends (GFF:2), Alice's machine asks every friend (GFF:3) and follows Bob through the friend with the shortest path to Bob (GFF:2).
