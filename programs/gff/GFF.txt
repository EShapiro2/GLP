GFF: Grassroots Feeds and Followers
Alice, Bob and Carol are agents; Bob and Carol need not be distinct.
1. ■ Feed.  Alice has a feed and may post to it an item, created or received.
2. ■ Following.  Bob follows their own feed.  Alice, whose friend Carol follows Bob, may follow Bob through Carol, as well as end their following.
3. ■ Follower discovery.  Alice may ask their friend Carol whether Carol follows Bob, and Carol answers with their path to Bob, as the signatures of the items they received show it, or that they do not.
4. ■ Delivery.  Carol, who follows Bob, sends every item on this feed, after adding their signature, to every friend who follows Bob through Carol.
Pragmas
Feeds: the feeds Alice follows, and the one Alice follows each through.
Feed: Alice [Post] <item>
Following: Alice [Follow] <person> <person>
  Alice [Unfollow]
Follower discovery: Alice [Ask] <person> <person>
  Carol: Alice asks whether you follow Bob.
