GFF-FR: Fault-Resilient Feeds and Followers
Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are agents.
1. ■ Redundant following.  Alice, who follows Bob through Carol, also follows Bob through their friend Dave that follows Bob.
2. ■ Redundant forwarding.  Alice, who follows Bob through Carol and through Dave and received from Carol an item of Bob's feed not signed by Dave, forwards it to Dave.
Pragmas
% Algorithms: Redundant following: when Alice wants to follow Bob, Alice's machine follows Bob through two friends known to follow Bob, when there are two (GFF-FR:1); Redundant forwarding is taken whenever such an item arrives (GFF-FR:2).
