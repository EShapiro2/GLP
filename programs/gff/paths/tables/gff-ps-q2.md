# GFF-I and GFF-PS path lengths, q = 2

Kleinberg's 100 x 100 grid, 2 long-range friendship(s) per person; seeds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, pooled; produced by programs/gff/paths/paths.py on the contracts of programs/gff at d37f58f7 (see the docstring and README.md).
Path and ratio are over the deciders who follow; ratio is path length over graph distance in the friendship graph; waiting is the fraction of deciders still waiting at the end; holders per decided follower is all who follow the feed (the author's friends included) over the deciders who follow; fan-in is the persons with a pointer to the author, per author.  Strategies: random, first (first in the friend list) and shortest (an oracle baseline).  p is the probability that an introduction GFF-PS needs is taken.
