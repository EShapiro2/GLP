GFF1: Fault-Resilient Feeds and Followers
Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are agents.
1. ■ Redundant following.  Alice, who follows Bob through Carol, may also follow Bob through their friend Dave that follows Bob.
2. ■ Redundant forwarding.  Alice, who follows Bob through Carol and through Dave and received an item of Bob's feed from Carol and not from Dave, forwards it to Dave.
Pragmas
Feeds: the agents Alice follows.
Redundant following: Alice [Follow] <person>
