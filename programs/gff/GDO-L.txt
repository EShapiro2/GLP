GDO-L: Grassroots Directed Overlay with Labels
GDO-L extends GDO.
Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave are persons, Carol and Dave possibly one person.  A pointer may be labeled by a person, and a pointer labeled by Dave is a Dave-pointer; Alice may have many pointers to Bob, labeled by different persons.  Friends Alice and Bob have a Bob-pointer from Alice to Bob and an Alice-pointer from Bob to Alice.
1. ■ Labeled pointing to friend.  Alice, who has no Dave-pointer to their friend Bob, may add one, if Bob has a Dave-pointer.
2. ■ Labeled transitive pointing.  Alice, who knows Carol and has a Dave-pointer to Bob or is Bob's friend, may add a Dave-pointer to Carol, if Bob has a Dave-pointer to Carol.
3. ■ Ceasing to point.  Alice, who has a Dave-pointer to Bob, may remove it.
Pragmas
Pointing: the persons Alice has pointers to, each with the labels of the pointers.
Labeled pointing to friend: Alice [Follow] <label>
Ceasing to point: Alice [Unfollow]
