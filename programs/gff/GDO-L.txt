GDO-L: Grassroots Directed Overlay with Labels
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.  GDO-L extends GDO.  A person has a label.  A labeled pointing is of one person to another with a label, and Alice may point to Bob with many labels, once with each.
1. ■ Labeled pointing to friend.  Alice, who does not point to their friend Bob with a label of Bob's or one Bob points with, may point to Bob with it.
2. ■ Labeled transitive pointing.  Alice, who knows Carol and points with a label to Bob who points with it to Carol, may point to Carol with it.
3. ■ Ceasing to point.  Alice, who points to Bob with a label, may cease pointing to Bob with it.
Pragmas
Pointing: the persons Alice points to, each with its label.
Labeled pointing to friend: Alice [Follow] <label>
Ceasing to point: Alice [Unfollow]
