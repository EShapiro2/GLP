LWGA: Association under the Israeli Model Bylaws
LWGA instantiates GCoop and restricts it.
An association is a cooperative with two roles, member, held by every participant, and committee member; the committee members decide the admission of a member when more than half of the votes cast are for; the members decide every other act of the association when more than half of the votes cast are for, and at least a quarter of the members voted, and the motion was moved at least 10 days before.
Alice and Bob are persons, Orga an association.
1. ■ Founding.  Alice may found Orga and become its first member.
2. ■ Admission.  Bob becomes a member of Orga by an act of Bob and the committee members.
3. ■ Voting against.  A member or a committee member may vote against a proposed act of Orga, once, in place of voting for it.
4. ■ Appeal.  Bob becomes a member of Orga also by an act of Bob and the members.
5. ■ Filling a seat.  The committee members may appoint a member to the committee.
