# The screens of GSNP's app

The screens of GAI's two figures of the app, "The GSNP app" and "Accountable AI agents", taken from GSNP's app, the full platform, which extends GAI (Code_inbox.md, GAI #8 Cowork, 2026-10-24 10:47 UTC: "The four screens of the GAI figure are taken from the GSNP app, the full platform").  The app is compiled from GSNP's contract and pragmas and those of the contracts it extends, and drawn by the construct family (`../README.md`, "The screens").  Each file is one phone's screen taken on the iOS Simulator, an iPhone 16e, 390 by 844 points at 3 pixels a point, its status bar at 9:41 with full signal, Wi-Fi and battery, in the light theme, set in Roboto; the simulator's screenshot is the display alone, so it is framed as the phone, in a black bezel with rounded corners, 1080 by 2220 pixels on white, as vGLP's figure of the app is (`vGLP/figures/gsg-app-friends.png`).

They are taken by `glp_multiagent/tool/sim_screens.sh`, which plays on the simulator the scenario of `glp_multiagent/test/apps_screens_test.dart`: it loads this app and runs its entry `miniapp/3`, once for Dan, Eve, Carol, Bob and Fay over four conversations, and once for Acme Ltd, Alice, Alf and Bob over four, Alf scripted by `../person.glp`, its script the AI agent's program; it takes each person's acts by tapping and typing on the tabs' controls.  From `glp_multiagent/`:

```bash
bash tool/sim_screens.sh
```

The script builds the bundled assets (`tool/sync_glp_assets.sh`), boots the simulator, sets its status bar, and runs the scenario there (`integration_test/apps_screens_test.dart`), one person's phone on the screen at a time; at each screen the host takes the simulator's screenshot, `xcrun simctl io <device> screenshot --mask=alpha`, and `tool/sim_shot.py` draws the bezel around it and writes it here, under the screen's name.  The same scenario runs headless in the suite, `flutter test test/apps_screens_test.dart`, the phones side by side in the test's view: it takes every act and checks every screen, and writes nothing.

The words on these screens are the contracts' pragmas, the platform's yes and no, and Peer and Speech, the names of the types of places no pragma names a field for (vGLP, Section 6.2, "The words a platform shows").  Where a button is drawn is vGLP's Section 6.2: an act that names an entry is drawn in that entry's row, the row filling it, and opens its form there; an act that names no entry is a form at the top of the tab of its contract; and a speech act is drawn with its signers, innermost first.  GAI's contract carries one question for each other guarding role of its line (`programs/gai/GAI.txt`, "Ward and person"): Alf, the AI agent, is asked "Alice asks that you and Bob befriend.", which its program answers, and Bob "Alice asks that you and her AI agent Alf befriend.".
