# GSNP's app

The app of GSNP, the grassroots social network with provenance, as a mini-app of the Grassroots Super-App (GSG paper, Section 2, "The Grassroots Super-App"), compiled down the abstraction cascade from GSNP's act schemas.  It is GSN's app, `../gsn`, on GSNP's platform: the same entry and network (`miniapp.glp`, the same file), and the same scripted person with GSNP's scripts, for the five persons of the play of GAI's GSNP figure and for the four agents of the play of GAI's Figure "The GAI app", Acme Ltd, the developer, Alice, Alf, her AI agent, and Bob: GSNP extends GAI, so it is the full platform of GAI's paper, and the screens of both figures are taken from it (`screens/README.md`).

## The contract

GSNP is GAI's contract (Coordination Appendix C; GSC's until 2026-10-20); its clauses and its pragmas are `programs/gai/GSNP.txt`, the clauses as GAI's paper has them, and its act schemas `programs/gai/GSNP.glp`.  GSNP extends GSN, and through it GSG and GKK, and GnW and GAI, whose clauses GKK:1 to GKK:3, GSG:1 and GSG:2, GSN:1 to GSN:5, GnW:1 to GnW:4 and GAI:1 to GAI:4 it cites (`../gkk/README.md`, `../gsg/README.md`, `../gsn/README.md`, `../gai/README.md`):

1. ■ *Authorship.*  Alice signs the content they create.
2. ◆ *Forwarding.*  Alice forwards content they received by sending it to Bob with the signatures it has and adding their own, and warrants that they know personally the agent they received it from.
3. ○ *Confidence.*  Alice shall disclose content they received only by forwarding it.

Its pragmas, after the line `Pragmas` of `GSNP.txt`, with those of GSN, GSG, GKK and GAI, which it extends (`../gsn/README.md`, `../gai/README.md`); Sign is GAI's line, "Developer: [Sign] <text>", one schema having one line:

    Items: the items Alice holds.
    Forwarding: [Forward] [Post forward] You warrant that you know personally the agent you received this from.

Twenty-five schemas over seventeen predicates: GKK's `establish`, `meet` and `introduce`; GSG's `befriend`, requiring that the two know each other, and `unfriend`; GnW's and GAI's seven for an AI agent and its principal, `enter`, `agent_meet`, `agents_meet`, `agent_befriend`, `agents_befriend`, `agent_unfriend` and `retire`, as `GAI.glp` has them (`../gai/README.md`); `sign`, `message` and `forward`; and GSN's `create`, `join`, `leave`, `close`, `drop`, `post_own`, `post`, `post_forward`, `deliver` and `deliver_own`, a group message carried as an item (`GSNP.txt`, its % lines).  Every guarding role forbids `retired`, and `create` and `join` forbid `agent`: an AI agent takes no group act.  `sign(p? ; x)` adds `item(x)` and `authored(x)` at `p`; `message(p?, q ; x)` requires `authored(x)` at `p` and adds `item(x)` at `q`, the item as its author signed it; `forward(p?, q ; x)` requires `item(x)` and forbids `authored(x)` at `p`, and adds `item(sgn(x, p))` at `q`, the item signed again by `p`.  A speech act is `speech(Utterance, Peer)`, an utterance or a speech act and the person who signs it, so `sgn(x, p)` is `speech(x, p)`.  The warranty of GSNP:2 and GSNP:3 describe no act: the platform asks nobody on a forward.

## The cascade

1. The act schemas, `programs/gai/GSNP.glp`; the checker's verdict `syntactically_grassroots_with_wards(meet, [agent], [guardianship(agent, identity, principal, principal_of)], [relay(agent_meet, ward(1, agent, 3), free(2)), relay(agents_meet, ward(1, agent, 3), ward(2, agent, 4))], [retired])` (`cascade/GSNP/verdict.txt`; GAI's paper, Appendix "Contracts with Wards").
2. The canonical CVA platform of the schemas, `platform.vglp`, as `programs/vglp/cva/compiler` prints it from GSNP.glp and the pragmas of GSNP.txt and of the contracts it extends; `bash programs/apps/compile.sh --check` re-prints it and compares.
3. GLP: the canonical compilation compiles `platform.vglp` when the directory loads.
4. The mini-app: `miniapp.glp` wraps the compiled agent and its dispatcher in the root's entry template `miniapp/4`.
