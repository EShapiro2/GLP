# GSNP's app

The app of GSNP, the grassroots social network with provenance, as a mini-app of the Grassroots Super-App (GSG paper, Section 2, "The Grassroots Super-App"), compiled down the abstraction cascade from GSNP's act schemas.  It is GSN's app, `../gsn`, on GSNP's platform: the same entry and network (`miniapp.glp`, the same file), and the same scripted person with GSNP's scripts, for the five persons of the play of GAI's GSNP figure and for the four agents of the play of GAI's Figure "The GAI app", Acme Ltd, the developer, Alice, Alf, her AI agent, and Bob: GSNP extends GAI, so it is the full platform of GAI's paper, and the screens of both figures are taken from it (`screens/README.md`).

## The contract

GSNP is GAI's contract (Coordination Appendix C; GSC's until 2026-10-20); its clause and its pragmas are `programs/gai/GSNP.txt`, the clause as GAI's paper has it (Section 4.1), and its act schemas `programs/gai/GSNP.glp`.  GSNP extends GSN, GSC's, and GAI, and through them GnW, GSG and GKK, whose clauses it cites (`../gkk/README.md`, `../gsg/README.md`, `../gsn/README.md`, `../gai/README.md`), so its platform is that of GAI's paper, Section 4:

1. ◆ *Provenance.*  Alice, who received an item from a friend, may send the item to their friend Bob after adding their signature to the signatures the item has; Bob can see the list of signatures in the received item.

Its pragmas, after the line `Pragmas` of `GSNP.txt`, with those of GSN, GAI, GSG and GKK, which it extends (`../gsn/README.md`, `../gai/README.md`), its Messaging and Group messaging lines in place of GSN's, the field of a message an item:

    Items: the items Alice holds.
    Messaging: Alice [Message] <item>
    Group messaging: Bob [Post] <item>
      Alice [Post own] <item>
    Provenance: Alice [Forward]
      Bob [Post forward]

Twenty-nine schemas over twenty predicates (`GSNP.txt`, its % lines): GKK's `establish`, `acquaint` and `introduce`; GSG's `befriend`, requiring that the two know each other, and `unfriend`, which with GSN:3 clears every membership between the two; GnW's and GAI's twelve for an AI agent and its guardians, `enter`, `make_guardian`, `end_guardian`, `resign`, `guardian_unfriend`, `agent_acquaint`, `agent_befriend`, `agent_unfriend`, `agents_acquaint`, `agents_befriend`, `retire` and `sign`, as `GAI.glp` has them (`../gai/README.md`); GSN's `message`, a message carrying an item its sender authored, and GSNP's `forward`; and GSN's `create`, `join`, `leave`, `remove`, `close`, `post_own`, `post`, `post_forward`, `deliver` and `deliver_own`, a group message carried as an item.  Every guarding role forbids `retired`, and `create` and `join` forbid `agent`: an AI agent takes no group act.  `sign(p? ; x)` adds `item(x)` and `authored(x)` at `p`; `message(p?, q ; x)` requires `authored(x)` at `p` and adds `item(x)` at `q`, the item as its author signed it; `forward(p?, q ; x)` requires `item(x)` and forbids `authored(x)` at `p`, and adds `item(sgn(x, p))` at `q`, the item signed again by `p`.  A speech act is `speech(Utterance, Peer)`, an utterance or a speech act and the person who signs it, so `sgn(x, p)` is `speech(x, p)`.  GSNP:1 is attested: the platform asks nobody on a forward.

## The cascade

1. The act schemas, `programs/gai/GSNP.glp`; the checker's verdict `syntactically_grassroots_with_wards(acquaint, [agent], [guardianship(agent, identity, principal, principal_of, guardian_of)], [relay(agent_acquaint, ward(1, agent, 3), free(2)), relay(agents_acquaint, ward(1, agent, 3), ward(2, agent, 4))], [retired])` (GAI's paper, Appendix "Contracts with Wards").
2. The canonical CVA platform of the schemas, `platform.vglp`, as `programs/vglp/cva/compiler` prints it from GSNP.glp and the pragmas of GSNP.txt and of the contracts it extends; `bash programs/apps/compile.sh --check` re-prints it and compares.
3. GLP: the canonical compilation compiles `platform.vglp` when the directory loads.
4. The mini-app: `miniapp.glp` wraps the compiled agent and its dispatcher in the root's entry template `miniapp/4`.
