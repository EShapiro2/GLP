# GSNP's app

The app of GSNP, the grassroots social network with provenance, as a mini-app of the Grassroots Super-App (GSA's paper, "The Grassroots Super-App"), compiled down the abstraction cascade from GSNP's speech-act schemas and pragmas.  It is GSN's app, `../gsn`, on GSNP's platform: the same entry and network (`miniapp.glp`, the same file), and GSN's app's scripted person with GSNP's scripts, for the five persons of the play of GAI's Figure "The GSNP app" and for the four agents of the play of GAI's Figure "Accountable AI agents", Acme Ltd, the developer, Alice, Alf, her AI agent, and Bob.  GSNP is the full platform of GAI's paper, and the screens of both figures are taken from it (`screens/README.md`).

## The contract

GSNP is GAI's contract.  Its relation to GAI and GSN and its pragmas are `programs/gai/GSNP.txt`, and nowhere else; it has no clause of its own.  Its speech-act schemas are `programs/gai/GSNP.glp`, an instance of GnW, GSC's, with GAI's schemas and its own.  The thirty-one schemas of the instance, as the checker's printer writes them, are `cascade/GSNP/schemas.txt`, and the checker's verdict `cascade/GSNP/verdict.txt`.

## The cascade

1. The speech-act schemas, `programs/gai/GSNP.glp`, and the checker's verdict on them, `programs/gsc`'s `check/2`.
2. The canonical CVA platform of the schemas, `platform.vglp`, as `programs/vglp/cva/compiler` prints it from GSNP.glp and the pragmas of GSNP.txt and of the contracts it is written from; `bash programs/apps/compile.sh --check` re-prints it and compares.
3. GLP: the canonical compilation compiles `platform.vglp` when the directory loads.
4. The mini-app: `miniapp.glp` wraps the compiled agent and its dispatcher in the root's entry template `miniapp/3`.

## The files

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `platform.vglp` | the canonical CVA platform of GSNP, as printed, with the words of its pragmas and its widget declarations |
| `miniapp.glp` | `miniapp/3`, the same file as `../gsg/miniapp.glp` and `../gsn/miniapp.glp` |
| `person.glp` | `miniapp_person/5`: the scripted person, by role, with the keys the person knows, by which its script names its peers: `dan`, `eve`, `carol`, `bob` or `fay`, and `acme_ltd`, `alice`, `alf`, the AI agent's program, or `agents_bob` |
| `plays.glp` | `play_provenance/5` and `play_agents/4`, the mini-app with no super-app, and `play_agents`'s hold on Alice's Retire until Bob's screen shows Alf's item |
| `self.glp` | the platform's types written again, the router's types, and the exports |
| `screens/` | the screens of GAI's two figures and their README |
| `../gsnp_boot.glp` | the super-app hosting the app on five smartphones, under `:boot` |

## Running it

From `glp_runtime/`:

```bash
printf '../programs/apps/gsnp\n:limit 30000000\nplay_provenance(D, E, C, B, F).\n:quit\n' | bin/glpc
```

`play_provenance` runs the play of GAI's Figure "The GSNP app": five executions, Dan's, Eve's, Carol's, Bob's and Fay's, over four conversations, Dan's with Eve, Eve's with Carol, Carol's with Bob and Bob's with Fay, the play handing each its ends of the root channels as the social graph does.  The acts and their order are in the comments of `person.glp`: each establishes their identity; each two come to know each other and befriend; Dan signs the item 'Flood on Main Street' and sends it to Eve in a message, which carries it as he signed it; and Eve, Carol and Bob each forward it to the next, signing it over, so that Fay holds it signed by Dan, Eve, Carol and Bob, in order.
