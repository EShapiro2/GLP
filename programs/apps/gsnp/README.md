# GSNP's app

The app of GSNP, the grassroots social network with provenance, as a mini-app of the Grassroots Super-App (GSG paper, Section 2, "The Grassroots Super-App"), compiled down the abstraction cascade from GSNP's act schemas.  It is GSN's app, `../gsn`, on GSNP's platform: the same entry and network (`miniapp.glp`, the same file), and the same scripted person with GSNP's scripts, for the five persons of the play of GAI's GSNP figure.

## The contract

GSNP is GAI's contract (Coordination Appendix C; GSC's until 2026-10-20); its clauses and its pragmas are `programs/gai/GSNP.txt`, the clauses as GAI's paper has them, and its act schemas `programs/gai/GSNP.glp`.  GSNP extends GSN, and through it GSG and GKK, whose clauses GKK:1 to GKK:3, GSG:1 and GSG:2 and GSN:1 to GSN:5 it cites (`../gkk/README.md`, `../gsg/README.md`, `../gsn/README.md`):

1. ■ *Authorship.*  Alice signs the content they create.
2. ◆ *Forwarding.*  Alice forwards content they received by sending it to Bob with the signatures it has and adding their own, and warrants that they know personally the agent they received it from.
3. ○ *Confidence.*  Alice shall disclose content they received only by forwarding it.

Its pragmas, after the line `Pragmas` of `GSNP.txt`, with those of GSN, GSG and GKK, which it extends:

    Items: the items Alice holds.
    Authorship: [Sign] <text>
    Forwarding: [Forward] You warrant that you know personally the agent you received this from.

Eighteen schemas over ten predicates: GKK's `establish`, `meet` and `introduce`; GSG's `befriend`, requiring that the two know each other, and `unfriend`; `sign`, `message` and `forward`; and GSN's `create`, `join`, `leave`, `close`, `drop`, `post_own`, `post`, `post_forward`, `deliver` and `deliver_own`, a group message carried as an item (`GSNP.txt`, its % lines).  `sign(p? ; x)` adds `item(x)` and `authored(x)` at `p`; `message(p?, q ; x)` requires `authored(x)` at `p` and adds `item(x)` at `q`, the item as its author signed it; `forward(p?, q ; x)` requires `item(x)` and forbids `authored(x)` at `p`, and adds `item(sgn(x, p))` at `q`, the item signed again by `p`.  A speech act is `speech(Utterance, Peer)`, an utterance or a speech act and the person who signs it, so `sgn(x, p)` is `speech(x, p)`.  The warranty of GSNP:2 and GSNP:3 describe no act: the platform asks nobody on a forward.

## The cascade

1. The act schemas, `programs/gai/GSNP.glp`; the checker's verdict `syntactically_grassroots` (`cascade/GSNP/verdict.txt`).
2. The canonical CVA platform of the schemas, `platform.vglp`, as `programs/vglp/cva/compiler` prints it from GSNP.glp and the pragmas of GSNP.txt and of the contracts it extends; `bash programs/apps/compile.sh --check` re-prints it and compares.
3. GLP: the canonical compilation compiles `platform.vglp` when the directory loads.
4. The mini-app: `miniapp.glp` wraps the compiled agent and its dispatcher in the root's entry template `miniapp/4`.

## Running it

From `glp_runtime/`:

```bash
printf '../programs/apps/gsnp\n:limit 6000000\nplay_provenance(D, E, C, B, F).\n:quit\n' | bin/glpc
```

`play_provenance` runs the play of GAI's GSNP figure (Code_inbox.md, GAI #3 Cowork, 2026-10-19 13:31 UTC, item 4): five executions, Dan's, Eve's, Carol's, Bob's and Fay's, over four conversations, Dan's with Eve, Eve's with Carol, Carol's with Bob and Bob's with Fay, the play handing each its ends of the root channels as the social graph does.  Each establishes their identity (GKK:1); in Dan and Eve, Eve and Carol, Carol and Bob, and Bob and Fay, the first meets the second by their key, and the second, asked by its hello, answers yes (GKK:2), and then the first offers the second friendship, and the second is asked and answers yes (GSG:1), befriend requiring that the two know each other; Dan signs the item `'Flood on Main Street'` (GSNP:1), which records him its author, and sends it to Eve in a message, which carries it as he signed it, and nobody is asked (GSN:1); Eve forwards it to Carol, and Carol to Bob, and nobody is asked (GSNP:2).  Bob then holds `speech(speech(speech(utterance('Flood on Main Street'), dan), eve), carol)`: the item signed by Dan, then by Eve, then by Carol.  Each person who passes the item on meets the next person, and then offers them friendship, once the item has reached them. ### On five smartphones

```bash
G=$(cd .. && pwd); printf ':artefact %s/programs/apps/gsnp %s/programs/social/graph/core\n:boot %s/programs/apps/gsnp_boot.glp %s/programs/social/graph/core\n:quit\n' $G $G $G $G | bin/glpc
```

As GSN's app's on two (`../gsn/README.md`, "On two smartphones"), on five isolates: Dan connects to Eve, and once connected Eve connects to Carol, Carol to Bob and Bob to Fay; each pair becomes friends in the super-app's social graph; once connected to the next, each invites them to `gsnp`; the four conversations open, and the steps of `play_provenance` run over them; each event reduces to quiescence and the boot settles.  Each person's lines are what the super-app told them, `sg(befriend(...))` and `sg(connected(...))`, and the views of `play_provenance` above.  Section APPS holds each person's super-app lines and their screen each exactly, in order: across the isolates the two are not ordered against each other.  Each screen is the play's, each person coming to know the next by that person's hello.

## The screens

What each person sees, as the construct family draws the app (`glp_multiagent/lib/ui_runtime/named_widgets.dart` and `tab_screen.dart`): every word on its screens is the contract's, from its pragmas and those of the contracts it extends, or one of the platform's four, yes, no, declined and unable (vGLP, Section 5.4, "The contract's words"; Section 6.2, "The words a platform shows").  GSN's app's tabs Contacts, Friends and Groups (`../gsn/README.md`, "The screens") and a fourth, Items, the list of GSNP's pragma "Items: the items Alice holds.": a row for each item the person holds, its text and under it its signers by name, its author and then each agent that forwarded it, in order (vGLP #20, Code_inbox.md, 2026-10-23 23:08 UTC, item 9: "an item's row its author and the agents that forwarded it"); and on it the field text with Sign, GSNP's "[Sign] <text>", proposing `sign(Me, x)`, `x` the text typed, signed by the person.  Message, GSN's "[Message] <text>", beside each person listed, proposes `message(Me, Q, x)`, which carries an item the person authored: the text they signed.

`glp_multiagent/test/apps_screens_test.dart` runs the app on five phones and takes each person's acts on the tabs' controls: each taps Establish; in Dan and Eve, Eve and Carol, Carol and Bob, and Bob and Fay, the first types the second's key into Meet's field and taps Meet, and the second, asked "Dan wishes that you know each other.", taps yes; the first taps Befriend beside the second, who taps yes; Dan types Flood on Main Street into Sign's field on his Items tab and taps Sign, and then taps Message beside Eve in his Friends tab, types the same text and taps Message, and Eve is not asked; and Eve's Items tab holds the item, with Dan under it.

Forwarding (GSNP:2) waits on vGLP: GSNP's [Forward] names, beside its proposer, a person and the item it forwards, and the paper places a button beside a person or a group the view lists, filling no place with an item (vGLP, Section 6.2, "The words a platform shows").  Until it does, the scenario stops at Eve's Items tab, and the screens in `screens/`, drawn by the app before its contract carried pragmas, are not re-taken (`screens/README.md`).

## The types written again

`self.glp` writes again the types of `platform.vglp` that the imported declarations of `platform#agent` and `platform#dispatch` carry, as `../gsn/self.glp` does, with GKK's classes `meet` and `introduce` and its atom `knows`, as `../gsn/self.glp` has them, and GSNP's classes `sign`, `forward`, `post_forward` and `deliver_own` and its atoms `item` and `authored` in place of GSN's `received` and `received_in`; the views, `view(S, K, A, Ts)`, and the notices, `refused(N, Q, D)`, by request name, as the platform prints them.  Once vGLP's compiler writes these into each module that imports a compiled procedure (vGLP, Section 6.3), these copies go.
