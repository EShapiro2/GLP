# GAI's app

The app of GAI, accountable AI agents, as a mini-app of the Grassroots Super-App (GSG paper, Section 2, "The Grassroots Super-App"), compiled down the abstraction cascade from GAI's act schemas and pragmas.  It is GSG's app, `../gsg`, on GAI's platform: the same entry and network (`miniapp.glp`, the same file), and the same scripted person with GAI's scripts, for the three agents of the example of GAI's paper (Section 3.2, and Table "The components that change in the example"): Alice and Bob, persons, and Alf, Alice's AI agent.  No screen of it goes in GAI's paper: the screens of GAI's Figure "The GAI app" are taken from GSNP's app, the full platform, which extends GAI (`../gsnp/screens/README.md`; Code_inbox.md, GAI #8 Cowork, 2026-10-24 10:47 UTC).

## The contract

GAI is GAI's contract (Coordination Appendix C); its clauses and its pragmas are `programs/gai/GAI.txt`, the clauses as GAI's paper has them, and its act schemas `programs/gai/GAI.glp`.  GAI extends GnW (`programs/gai/GnW.txt`), and through it GSG and GKK, whose clauses GKK:1 to GKK:3, GSG:1 and GSG:2 and GnW:1 to GnW:4 it cites (`../gkk/README.md`, `../gsg/README.md`):

1. ◆ *Developer.*  The developer of an AI agent, Alf, is the person who signed Alf's code.  Alice, who creates Alf, attests that Alf runs code signed by its developer.
2. ■ *Principal.*  Alice, the guardian of Alf, is Alf's principal.  Every act of Alf names its principal and its developer and carries its principal's attestation.
3. ○ *Accountability.*  The developer of Alf is accountable for Alf's defects, and Alf's principal for operating it recklessly.
4. ■ *Retiring.*  Alice retires their AI agent Alf by ending their friendship with it.

Its pragmas, after the line `Pragmas` of `GAI.txt`, with those of GSG and GKK, which it extends; GnW carries none of its own (`GnW.txt`):

    AI agents: those Alice is principal of.
    Guardianship: [Enter] <name> <code>
    Developer: [Sign] <text>
    Knowing and relations: [Agent meet] [Agents meet] Carol, an AI agent, asks to meet you.  [Agent befriend] [Agents befriend] Carol, an AI agent, asks to befriend you.
    Ending a relation: [Agent unfriend]
    Retiring: [Retire]

Thirteen schemas over twelve predicates (`GAI.glp`, its comments): GKK's `establish`, `meet` and `introduce` and GSG's `befriend` and `unfriend`, for persons, `meet`, `introduce` and `befriend` forbidding `agent` at both roles; `enter(p?, r? ; x)`, a person creating an AI agent and becoming its principal and its friend (GnW:1, GAI:2), which requires `item(x)` at the person, `x` the code signed by its developer, and adds `agent`, `principal(p)`, `friend(p)`, `knows(p)` and the attestation `attestation(sgn(x, p))` at the AI agent, and `principal_of(r)`, `friend(r)`, `knows(r)` and the record `agent_of(r, p, sgn(x, p))` at the person (GAI:1; GLP a336b30d); `agent_meet(r?, q?, p? ; y)` and `agents_meet(r?, s?, p?, q? ; y, z)`, an AI agent coming to know an agent by an act of both and of the principal of each AI agent among them (GnW:2), each AI agent presenting its attestation, which its principal's role requires recorded, `agent_of(r, p, y)`, so the principal's machine fills `y` from it, and the other party recording it (GAI:2); `agent_befriend(r?, q?, p?)` and `agents_befriend(r?, s?, p?, q?)`, an AI agent and an agent who know each other entering a friendship by an act of both and of the principal of each AI agent among them (GnW:2); `agent_unfriend(r, q, p?)`, the principal ending it (GnW:3); `retire(p?, r)` (GAI:4), which ends the friendship of the AI agent and its principal, adds `retired_of(r)` at the principal and `retired` at the AI agent; and `sign(p? ; x)`, by which the principal holds the code `enter` requires (GAI:1).  The messaging is GSNP's, which extends GAI (`../gsnp`).  An AI agent comes to know an agent only by `agent_meet` or `agents_meet`, since `meet` and `introduce` forbid `agent` at the role coming to know, so every agent an AI agent knows, other than its principal, holds its record (GAI's paper, Section 3.3, part 2).  GAI:3 describes no act.  The AI agent's guarded roles are taken by its program: "The principal's will is given once, when they create the AI agent; after that the AI agent acts on its own, and the principal takes part in each meeting of the AI agent and in each friendship it enters" (GAI's paper, Section 3.1).

## The cascade

1. The act schemas, `programs/gai/GAI.glp`; the checker's verdict `grassroots_with_wards(meet, [agent], [guardianship(agent, identity, principal, principal_of)], [relay(agent_meet, ward(1, agent, 3), free(2)), relay(agents_meet, ward(1, agent, 3), ward(2, agent, 4))], [retired])`: `meet` the introductory act, unobstructed but for `agent`, the guardianship `(agent, identity, principal, principal_of)`, the relay acts `agent_meet` and `agents_meet`, and `retired` the leaving mark (GAI's paper, Appendix "Contracts with Wards"; `cascade/GAI/verdict.txt`).
2. The canonical CVA platform of the schemas, `platform.vglp`, as `programs/vglp/cva/compiler` prints it from GAI.glp and the pragmas of GAI.txt and of the contracts it extends; `bash programs/apps/compile.sh --check` re-prints it and compares.
3. GLP: the canonical compilation compiles `platform.vglp` when the directory loads.
4. The mini-app: `miniapp.glp` wraps the compiled agent and its dispatcher in the root's entry template `miniapp/4`.

## Running it

From `glp_runtime/`:

```bash
printf '../programs/apps/gai\n:limit 6000000\nplay_agents(A, R, B).\n:quit\n' | bin/glpc
```

`play_agents` runs the example of GAI's paper on GAI's contract: three executions, Alice's, Alf's and Bob's, over three conversations, Alice's with Alf, Alf's with Bob and Bob's with Alice, the play handing each its ends of the root channels as the social graph does.  Alice and Bob establish their identities (GKK:1); Alice asks to meet Bob and he answers yes, and each knows the other (GKK:2); Alice signs the code of Alf, `speech(utterance('The code of Alf'), alice)`, and holds it as her item `k`, its developer on this contract, which has no message to carry a developer's code to her (GAI:1; on the full platform the developer is Acme Ltd, `../gsnp`); she enters her AI agent Alf with it, which answers yes, and she is its principal and its friend, the two knowing each other, Alf holding its attestation `sgn(k, alice)` and Alice its record (GnW:1, GAI:1, GAI:2); Alice, the principal, proposes Alf's meeting with Bob, her machine filling the attestation from her record, and Alf and Bob are asked and answer yes (GnW:2, `agent_meet`; vGLP 110caf4, Section 6.2), Bob recording `agent_of(alf, alice, y)`, `y` the attestation (GAI:2); once they know each other Alice proposes Alf's befriending Bob, and Alf and Bob answer yes (GnW:2, `agent_befriend`); once Alf and Bob are friends Bob offers Alice friendship and she answers yes (GSG:1); once they are friends Alice retires Alf (GAI:4), which ends her friendship with it, and Alf is asked nothing; and Bob ends his friendship with Alf, `unfriend(bob, alf)` (GSG:2).  The final states are those of the paper's table after Unfriend, less the item, which the AI agent signs and sends on the full platform.  Bob offers Alice friendship once Alf and he are friends, and Alice retires Alf once she and Bob are; Bob's view does not show that Alf is retired, so his `unfriend` and Alice's `retire`, Alf a participant of both, are concurrent, and the platform takes them in turn (vGLP, Section 3.2, "The protocol").  The play takes each act once its agent's screen shows what it waits on; the paper's table is one run of the protocol, not an order the persons see. ## The screens

What Alice and Bob see, as the construct family draws the app (`glp_multiagent/lib/ui_runtime/named_widgets.dart` and `tab_screen.dart`): every word on its screens is the contract's, from its pragmas and those of the contracts it extends, or one of the platform's four, yes, no, declined and unable, or the name of a type where no pragma names a field (vGLP, Section 5.4, "The contract's words"; Section 6.2, "The words a platform shows").  The tabs are the lists the pragmas name: Contacts and Friends, GKK's and GSG's (`../gkk/README.md`, `../gsg/README.md`), and AI agents, "those Alice is principal of", a row for each AI agent the person is principal of.  On AI agents, the tab of GAI's contract, are the acts of GAI's lines: Sign, with its field text, GAI's "[Sign] <text>", proposing `sign(Me, x)`, `x` the text typed, signed by the person; Enter, with its fields name and code, "[Enter] <name> <code>", proposing `enter(Me, r, x)`, GAI listing no item for the code to be picked from; and, beside each AI agent listed, the buttons of the acts whose variable its row fills at the principal's role, Agent meet, Agents meet, Agent befriend, Agents befriend, Agent unfriend and Retire, each drawn at each of its guarding roles (vGLP 110caf4, Section 6.2), the other party picked from the persons the view lists.  An approval question is drawn at the top of the tab of its act's contract, as its question with the buttons yes and no: GAI's "Alf, an AI agent, asks to meet you." and "Alf, an AI agent, asks to befriend you.", the names filled from the binding, put to each guarding role other than the proposer's, Alf's program and Bob (Section 5.4).

## The types written again

`self.glp` writes again the types of `platform.vglp` that the imported declarations of `platform#agent` and `platform#dispatch` carry, as `../gsnp/self.glp` does, with GKK's classes `meet` and `introduce` and its atom `knows`, as `../gsnp/self.glp` has them, and GAI's classes and its atoms `retired`, `agent`, `principal`, `principal_of`, `item`, `attestation`, `agent_of`, `retired_of` and `authored`.  Once vGLP's compiler writes these into each module that imports a compiled procedure (vGLP, Section 6.3), these copies go.
