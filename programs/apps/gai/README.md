# GAI's app

The app of GAI, accountable AI agents, as a mini-app of the Grassroots Super-App (GSG paper, Section 2, "The Grassroots Super-App"), compiled down the abstraction cascade from GAI's speech-act schemas and pragmas.  It is GSG's app, `../gsg`, on GAI's platform: the same entry and network (`miniapp.glp`, the same file), and the same scripted person with GAI's scripts, for the example of GAI's paper (Section 3.2, and Table "The components that change in the example"; GAI at e4d935c), Alice and Bob, persons, and Alf, Alice's AI agent.  No screen of it goes in GAI's paper: the screens of GAI's Figure "The GAI app" are taken from GSNP's app, the full platform, which extends GAI (`../gsnp/screens/README.md`; Code_inbox.md, GAI #8 Cowork, 2026-10-24 10:47 UTC).

## The contract

GAI is GAI's contract (Coordination Appendix C); its clauses and its pragmas are `programs/gai/GAI.txt`, the clauses as GAI's paper has them, and its speech-act schemas `programs/gai/GAI.glp`.  GAI extends GnW (`programs/gai/GnW.txt`), five clauses, and through it GSG and GKK, whose clauses GKK:1 to GKK:3, GSG:1 and GSG:2 and GnW:1 to GnW:5 it cites (`../gkk/README.md`, `../gsg/README.md`):

1. ○ AI developer and guardian accountability.  The developer of an AI agent, Alf, signs Alf's code, and is accountable for Alf's defects.  Every guardian of Alf is accountable for operating it recklessly.  The creator of Alf is its principal.
2. ■ AI acts.  Every act of Alf names its developer and its principal, and carries its principal's attestation, signed over Alf's public key, that Alf runs code signed by its developer.

Its pragmas, after the line `Pragmas` of `GAI.txt`, with those of GSG and GKK, which it extends; GnW carries none of its own (`GnW.txt`):

    AI agents: the AI agents Alice is a guardian of.
    Creation: Alice [Enter] <name>
      Carol: Alice asks to be your principal.
    Joint guardianship: Alice [Make guardian] <person>
      Bob: Alice asks you to be a guardian of their AI agent Carol.
      Alice [End guardian] <person>
      Bob [Resign] [Guardian unfriend]
    Ward and person: Alice [Agent acquaint] <person>
      Carol: Alice asks that you come to know Bob.
      Bob: Alice asks that you come to know their AI agent Carol.
      Alice [Agent befriend] <person>
      Carol: Alice asks that you and Bob befriend.
      Bob: Alice asks that you and their AI agent Carol befriend.
      Alice [Agent unfriend] <person>
    Ward and ward: Alice [Agents acquaint] <person> <person>
      Carol: Alice asks that you come to know Dave.
      Dave: Alice asks that you come to know Carol.
      Bob: Alice asks that Carol and your AI agent Dave come to know each other.
      Alice [Agents befriend] <person> <person>
      Carol: Alice asks that you and Dave befriend.
      Dave: Alice asks that you and Carol befriend.
      Bob: Alice asks that Carol and your AI agent Dave befriend.
    Retiring: Alice [Retire]
    AI developer and guardian accountability: Alice [Sign] <text>

Seventeen schemas over fifteen predicates, `friend` and `knows` reflexive (`GAI.glp`, its comments): GKK's `establish`, `acquaint` and `introduce` and GSG's `befriend` and `unfriend`, `acquaint`, `introduce` and `befriend` for persons, forbidding `agent`; `enter(p?, r? ; y)`, Alice creating the AI agent and becoming its principal, a guardian and its friend (GnW:1, GAI:1, GAI:2), which requires `authored(y)` at Alice, `y` her attestation, and adds `identity`, `agent`, `principal(p)`, `friend(p)`, `knows(p)` and `attestation(y)` at the AI agent and `principal_of(r)`, `guardian_of(r)`, `friend(r)`, `knows(r)` and the record `agent_of(r, y)` at Alice; `make_guardian`, `end_guardian`, `resign` and `guardian_unfriend` (GnW:2), a guardian other than the creator holding `guardian_of` of the AI agent and the record, and the AI agent `guarded_by` of that guardian; `agent_acquaint`, `agent_befriend` and `agent_unfriend` (GnW:3) and `agents_acquaint` and `agents_befriend` (GnW:4), proposed by a guardian, the two guardians of GnW:4 possibly one person; `retire(r, p?)` (GnW:5), the AI agent its first role, with the two listed roles `Carol[friend(*)]` and `Carol[guarded_by(*)]`, clearing `friend` and `guarded_by` at the AI agent and adding `retired` there and `retired_of(r)` at Alice; and `sign(p? ; x)` (GAI:1, GAI:2).  GAI:1's accountability describes no act.  The messaging is GSNP's, which extends GAI (`../gsnp`).

## The cascade

1. The speech-act schemas, `programs/gai/GAI.glp`, and the checker's verdict on them, `programs/gsc`'s `check/2`.
2. The canonical CVA platform of the schemas, `platform.vglp`, as `programs/vglp/cva/compiler` prints it from GAI.glp and the pragmas of GAI.txt and of the contracts it extends; `bash programs/apps/compile.sh --check` re-prints it and compares.
3. GLP: the canonical compilation compiles `platform.vglp` when the directory loads.
4. The mini-app: `miniapp.glp` wraps the compiled agent and its dispatcher in the root's entry template `miniapp/4`.

## Running it

From `glp_runtime/`:

```bash
printf '../programs/apps/gai\n:limit 6000000\nplay_agents(A, R, B).\n:quit\n' | bin/glpc
```

`play_agents` runs three executions, Alice's, Alf's and Bob's, over three conversations, one between each two of them, the play handing each its ends of the root channels as the social graph does, the persons scripted by `person.glp`.  Alice's attestation of Alf is `speech(utterance('Alf runs code signed by Acme Ltd'), alice)`, `y` below; on this contract, which has no message, nobody sends her the developer's code (GAI:1; on the full platform the developer is Acme Ltd, `../gsnp`).  A guardian whose own state an act does not change, proposing an act of their AI agent, waits until the act is no longer among their pending approvals, `done(K, N)` of `person.glp`, before the next.

The play is the example of GAI's paper (Section 3.2, and Table "The components that change in the example"), in its order: Alice and Bob establish their identities (GKK:1), come to know each other (GKK:2) and befriend (GSG:1), Alice proposing and Bob answering yes; Alice signs `y` (GAI:2; `sign`) and enters Alf with it, which answers yes (GnW:1; `enter`); Alice proposes Alf's acquaintance with Bob, her machine filling `y` from her record, Alf and Bob answering yes and Bob recording `agent_of(alf, y)` (GnW:3, GAI:2; `agent_acquaint`), and then Alf's befriending Bob, Alf and Bob answering yes (GnW:3; `agent_befriend`); and Alice retires Alf (GnW:5; `retire(alf, alice)`, the AI agent the schema's first role), which ends its friendships with her and with Bob, Bob in the listed role `Carol[friend(*)]`, nobody asked.  The final states are those of the paper's table after Retire, less the item, which Alf signs and sends to Bob on GSNP's app, the full platform: GAI has `sign` and no `message`, and Alf's signing would take its turn with Alice's retire, which nothing the persons see orders.  There is no unfriend: Retire ends Alf's friendships.

As GSNP's app's on five (`../gsnp/README.md`, "On five smartphones"), on three isolates, Alf on a smartphone of its own: Alice connects to Alf, and once connected Alf connects to Bob and Bob to Alice; each pair becomes friends in the super-app's social graph; once connected to the next, each invites them to `gai`; the three conversations open, and the steps of `play_agents` run over them, and the boot settles, `Boot settled: 3 agents (alice, alf, bob)`.  Section APPS holds each agent's super-app lines and their screen each exactly, in order; across the isolates Alf's and Bob's machines may write the persons they know a view later than in `play_agents`.

## The screens

What a person sees, as the construct family draws the app (`glp_multiagent/lib/ui_runtime/named_widgets.dart` and `tab_screen.dart`): every word on its screens is the contract's, from its pragmas and those of the contracts it extends, or one of the platform's four, yes, no, declined and unable, or the name of a type where no pragma names a field (vGLP, Section 5.4, "The contract's words"; Section 6.2, "The words a platform shows").  The tabs are the lists the pragmas name: Contacts and Friends, GKK's and GSG's, and AI agents, "the AI agents Alice is a guardian of", a row for each AI agent the person is a guardian of.  An act is drawn in the row of the tab whose predicate it requires or deletes at the proposer's role, naming the row's entry, with its fields beside it, and an act naming no entry on the tab of its contract (vGLP, Section 6.2).  An approval question is drawn at the top of the tab of its act's contract, as its question with the buttons yes and no.  `glp_multiagent/test/apps_screens_test.dart` runs the app for Alice, Alf and Bob, Alf scripted by `person.glp`, the play above taken by taps: each taps Establish; Alice acquaints Bob by key and befriends him, Bob answering yes; on her AI agents tab she signs `y` with Sign and enters Alf with Enter, its field name typed, `y` filled from the item she authored; in Alf's row she proposes Agent acquaint and then Agent befriend, Bob picked, and Bob answers each yes, GAI's questions "Alice asks that you come to know their AI agent Alf." and "Alice asks that you and their AI agent Alf befriend."; and in Alf's row of her Friends tab she taps Retire.  It writes no screen: GAI's figures are taken from GSNP's app (`../gsnp/screens/README.md`).

## The types written again

`self.glp` writes again the types of `platform.vglp` that the imported declarations of `platform#agent` and `platform#dispatch` carry, as `../gsnp/self.glp` does, with GKK's classes `acquaint` and `introduce` and its atom `knows`, as `../gsnp/self.glp` has them, and GAI's classes and its atoms, as `platform.vglp` prints them.  Once vGLP's compiler writes these into each module that imports a compiled procedure (vGLP, Section 6.3), these copies go.
