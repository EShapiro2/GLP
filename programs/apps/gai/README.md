# GAI's app

The app of GAI, accountable AI agents, as a mini-app of the Grassroots Super-App (GSA's paper, "The Grassroots Super-App"), compiled down the abstraction cascade from GAI's speech-act schemas and pragmas.  It is GSG's app, `../gsg`, on GAI's platform: the same entry and network (`miniapp.glp`, the same file), and GSN's app's scripted person with GAI's scripts, for the example of GAI's paper (Section 3.2, and Table "The atoms the acts of the example add and delete"), Alice and Bob, persons, and Alf, Alice's AI agent.  No screen of it goes in GAI's paper: the screens of GAI's Figure "Accountable AI agents" are taken from GSNP's app, the full platform (`../gsnp/screens/README.md`).

## The contract

GAI is GAI's contract.  Its clauses, its relation to GnW and GFP, and its pragmas are `programs/gai/GAI.txt`, and nowhere else; its speech-act schemas are `programs/gai/GAI.glp`, an instance of GnW, GSC's (`programs/gsc/GnW.txt`, `GnW.glp`), composed with GFP.  The nineteen schemas of the instance, as the checker's printer writes them, are `cascade/GAI/schemas.txt`, and the checker's verdict `cascade/GAI/verdict.txt`.

## The cascade

1. The speech-act schemas, `programs/gai/GAI.glp`, and the checker's verdict on them, `programs/gsc`'s `check/2`.
2. The canonical CVA platform of the schemas, `platform.vglp`, as `programs/vglp/cva/compiler` prints it from GAI.glp and the pragmas of GAI.txt and of the contracts it is written from; `bash programs/apps/compile.sh --check` re-prints it and compares.
3. GLP: the canonical compilation compiles `platform.vglp` when the directory loads.
4. The mini-app: `miniapp.glp` wraps the compiled agent and its dispatcher in the root's entry template `miniapp/3`.

## The files

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `platform.vglp` | the canonical CVA platform of GAI, as printed, with the words of its pragmas and its widget declarations |
| `miniapp.glp` | `miniapp/3`, the same file as `../gsg/miniapp.glp` |
| `person.glp` | `miniapp_person/5`: the scripted person, by role, with the keys the person knows, by which its script names its peers, the AI agent's script its program |
| `plays.glp` | `play_agents/3`, the mini-app with no super-app |
| `self.glp` | the platform's types written again, the router's types, and the exports |
| `../gai_boot.glp` | the super-app hosting the app on three smartphones, under `:boot` |

## Running it

From `glp_runtime/`:

```bash
printf '../programs/apps/gai\n:limit 6000000\nplay_agents(A, R, B).\n:quit\n' | bin/glpc
```

`play_agents` runs three executions, Alice's, Alf's and Bob's, over three conversations, one between each two of them, the play handing each its ends of the root channels as the social graph does, the persons scripted by `person.glp`.  The acts and their order are in the comments of `plays.glp` and `person.glp`: the acts of the paper's example, each person proposing an act once their own screen shows what it requires of the others, so that no attempt is refused as unable.  By GnW:3 Bob proposes that Alf and he know each other and Alice proposes their friendship, and neither sees on their own screen what the other's act did; so Alice creates Alf before she asks to come to know Bob, and Bob offers Alice friendship once he knows Alf.  The paper's example has the two persons befriend before Alf is created.

### On three smartphones

```bash
G=$(cd .. && pwd); printf ':artefact %s/programs/apps/gai %s/programs/social/graph/core\n:boot %s/programs/apps/gai_boot.glp %s/programs/social/graph/core\n:quit\n' $G $G $G $G | bin/glpc
```

The super-app hosts the app on three isolates, Alf on a smartphone of its own (`../gai_boot.glp`, Code's, and `programs/social/graph/core/gai_phone.glp`), and the scripts of `person.glp` run over the three conversations; Section APPS of the suite holds each agent's super-app lines and their screen.

## The screens

Every word on the app's screens is the contract's, from its pragmas and those of the contracts it is written from, or one of the platform's (vGLP, Section 5.4, "The contract's words"; Section 6.2).  `glp_multiagent/test/apps_screens_test.dart`, Code's, takes a play by taps on the tabs' controls.  It writes no screen of this app: GAI's figures are taken from GSNP's app (`../gsnp/screens/README.md`).

## The types written again

`self.glp` writes again the types of `platform.vglp` that the imported declarations of `platform#agent` and `platform#dispatch` carry, as `../gsn/self.glp` does: the classes, the proposals and the atoms as `platform.vglp` prints them.  Once vGLP's compiler writes these into each module that imports a compiled procedure (vGLP, Section 6.3), these copies go.
