# GAI's app

The app of GAI, accountable AI agents, as a mini-app of the Grassroots Super-App (GSG paper, Section 2, "The Grassroots Super-App"), compiled down the abstraction cascade from GAI's act schemas.  It is GSG's app, `../gsg`, on GAI's platform: the same entry and network (`miniapp.glp`, the same file), and the same scripted person with GAI's scripts, for the three agents of the example of GAI's paper (Section 3.2, and Table "The components that change in the example"): Alice and Bob, persons, and Alf, Alice's AI agent.

## The contract

GAI is GAI's contract (Coordination Appendix C); its clauses are `programs/gai/GAI.txt`, as GAI's paper has them, and its act schemas `programs/gai/GAI.glp`, at GLP 9d736804.  GAI extends GnW (`programs/gai/GnW.txt`), and through it GSG and GKK, whose clauses GKK:1 to GKK:3, GSG:1 and GSG:2 and GnW:1 to GnW:4 it cites (`../gkk/README.md`, `../gsg/README.md`), with GSNP's signing, messaging and forwarding beneath it:

1. *Developer.*  The developer of an AI agent, Alf, is the person who signed Alf's code.  Alice, who creates Alf, attests that Alf runs code signed by its developer.
2. *Principal.*  Alice, the guardian of Alf, is Alf's principal.  Every act of Alf names its principal and its developer and carries its principal's attestation.
3. *Accountability.*  The developer of Alf is accountable for Alf's defects, and Alf's principal for operating it recklessly.
4. *Retiring.*  Alice retires their AI agent Alf by ending their friendship with it.

Sixteen schemas over twelve predicates (`GAI.glp`, its comments): GKK's `establish`, `meet` and `introduce`, and GSG's `befriend`, requiring that the two know each other, for persons, each forbidding `agent` at both roles; `unfriend_p` and `unfriend_q`; `enter(p?, r? ; x)`, a person creating an AI agent and becoming its principal and its friend (GnW:1, GAI:2), which requires `item(x)` at the person, `x` the code signed by its developer, and adds `agent`, `principal(p)`, `knows(p)` and the attestation `attestation(sgn(x, p))` at the AI agent and `principal_of(r)` and `knows(r)` at the person (GAI:1); `agent_meet(r?, q?, p? ; y)` and `agents_meet(r?, s?, p?, q? ; y, z)`, an AI agent coming to know an agent by an act of both and of the principal of each AI agent among them (GnW:2), each AI agent presenting its attestation and the other recording `agent_of(r, p, y)`, the AI agent, its principal and its attestation (GAI:2); `agent_befriend(r?, q?, p?)` and `agents_befriend(r?, s?, p?, q?)`, an AI agent and an agent who know each other entering a friendship by an act of both and of the principal of each AI agent among them (GnW:2), each requiring `knows` of the other at both and adding `friend`; `agent_unfriend(r, q, p?)`, the principal ending it (GnW:3); `retire(p?, r)` (GAI:4), which ends the friendship of the AI agent and its principal and adds `retired` at the AI agent; and `sign(p? ; x)`, `message(p?, q ; x)` and `forward(p?, q ; x)`, as `GSNP.glp` has them, with `retired` forbidden at the acting role.  An AI agent comes to know an agent only by `agent_meet` or `agents_meet`, since `meet` and `introduce` forbid `agent` at the role coming to know, so every agent an AI agent knows, other than its principal, holds its record (GAI's paper, Section 3.3, part 2).  GAI:3 describes no act.  The AI agent's guarded roles are taken by its program: "The principal's will is given once, when they create the AI agent; after that the AI agent acts on its own, and the principal takes part in each meeting of the AI agent and in each friendship it enters" (GAI's paper, Section 3.1).

## The cascade

1. The act schemas, `programs/gai/GAI.glp`; the checker's verdict `grassroots_with_wards(meet, [agent], [guardianship(agent, identity, principal, principal_of)], [relay(agent_meet, ward(1, agent, 3), free(2)), relay(agents_meet, ward(1, agent, 3), ward(2, agent, 4))], [retired])`: `meet` the introductory act, unobstructed but for `agent`, the guardianship `(agent, identity, principal, principal_of)`, the relay acts `agent_meet` and `agents_meet`, and `retired` the leaving mark (GAI's paper, Appendix "Contracts with Wards"; `cascade/GAI/verdict.txt`).
2. The canonical CVA platform of the schemas, `platform.vglp`, as `programs/vglp/cva/compiler` prints it from GAI.glp; `bash programs/apps/compile.sh --check` re-prints it and compares.
3. GLP: the canonical compilation compiles `platform.vglp` when the directory loads.
4. The mini-app: `miniapp.glp` wraps the compiled agent and its dispatcher in the root's entry template `miniapp/4`.

## Running it

From `glp_runtime/`:

```bash
printf '../programs/apps/gai\n:limit 6000000\nplay_agents(A, R, B).\n:quit\n' | bin/glpc
```

`play_agents` runs the example of GAI's paper: three executions, Alice's, Alf's and Bob's, over three conversations, Alice's with Alf, Alf's with Bob and Bob's with Alice, the play handing each its ends of the root channels as the social graph does.  Alice and Bob establish their identities (GKK:1); Alice asks to meet Bob in person and he answers yes, and each knows the other (GKK:2); Alice signs the code of Alf, `speech(utterance('The code of Alf'), acme_ltd)`, signed by acme_ltd, its developer, drawn Acme Ltd as in the paper's Figure "The GAI app" (Code_inbox.md, GAI #5 Cowork, 2026-10-19 21:09 UTC, item 1), and holds it as her item k (GSNP:1); she enters her AI agent Alf with it, which answers yes, and she is its principal and its friend, the two knowing each other, and Alf holds its attestation `sgn(k, alice)` (GnW:1, GAI:1, GAI:2); once Alf holds its attestation and its agent knows Bob, it proposes to meet him, presenting its attestation, and Bob and Alice, its principal, are asked and answer yes (GnW:2, `agent_meet`), Bob recording `agent_of(alf, alice, y)`, `y` the attestation (GAI:2); once they know each other Alf proposes to befriend Bob, and Bob and Alice are asked and answer yes (GnW:2, `agent_befriend`); once they are friends Alf signs the item `'Today\'s summary of the news'` (GSNP:1) and sends it to Bob by message (GSN:1), and nobody is asked; once the item has reached him Bob offers Alice friendship and she answers yes (GSG:1); once they are friends Alice retires Alf (GAI:4), which ends her friendship with it, and Alf is asked nothing; and Bob ends his friendship with Alf, `unfriend_p(bob, alf)` (GSG:2), which requires at Alf only `identity`.  Bob holds `speech(utterance('Today\'s summary of the news'), alf)`, the item signed once, by Alf.  Bob offers Alice friendship once the item has reached him, and Alice retires Alf once they are friends, so that Alf signs and sends the item before it is retired: sign and message forbid `retired` at the acting role.  Bob ends his friendship with Alf once he and Alice are friends; his view does not show that Alf is retired, so his `unfriend_p` and Alice's `retire`, Alf a participant of both, are concurrent, and the platform takes them in turn (vGLP, Section 3.2, "The protocol"), Bob's first in this run.  The final states are those of the paper's table after Unfriend.  The play takes each act once its agent's screen shows what it waits on, so Alice and Bob befriend after the item has reached Bob, where the paper's table has Befriend(a, b) after Meet(a, b), and Retire and Unfriend are concurrent: the table is one run of the protocol, not an order the persons see (Code_inbox.md, GAI #5 Cowork, 2026-10-19 21:09 UTC, item 3), and the screens test, which acts for Alice and Bob at their phones, plays it exactly ("The screens", below). ### On three smartphones

```bash
G=$(cd .. && pwd); printf ':artefact %s/programs/apps/gai %s/programs/social/graph/core\n:boot %s/programs/apps/gai_boot.glp %s/programs/social/graph/core\n:quit\n' $G $G $G $G | bin/glpc
```

As GSNP's app's on five (`../gsnp/README.md`, "On five smartphones"), on three isolates, Alf, an agent "with its machine" (GAI's paper, Section 3.2), on a smartphone of its own: Alice connects to Alf, and once connected Alf connects to Bob and Bob to Alice; each pair becomes friends in the super-app's social graph; once connected to the next, each invites them to `gai`; the three conversations open, and the steps of `play_agents` run over them; each event reduces to quiescence and the boot settles, `Boot settled: 3 agents (alice, alf, bob)`.  Each agent's lines are what the super-app told it, `sg(befriend(...))` and `sg(connected(...))`, and the views of `play_agents` above.  Section APPS holds each agent's super-app lines and their screen each exactly, in order.

## The screens

What Alice and Bob see, as the construct family draws the app (`glp_multiagent/lib/ui_runtime/named_widgets.dart` and `tab_screen.dart`): the tabs Friends, as GSG's app has it (`../gsg/README.md`, "The screens"), with the items and the chats of GSNP's app (`../gsnp/README.md`, "The screens"), and AI agents, where the request type offers `enter`, drawn from the last view the agent wrote, the approval questions at the top of Friends (Code_inbox.md, GAI #5 Cowork, 2026-10-19 20:36 UTC, and its answers at 21:09 UTC):

- AI agents: the form Create an AI agent, which takes the AI agent's name and its code, the code's text and its developer's key; "Code signed by Acme Ltd" and "You attest that Alf runs it."; and Create, which proposes `sign(Me, k)`, `k` the code signed over by the person, and, once the view holds `item(k)`, `enter(Me, r, k)`; and each of the person's AI agents, `principal_of(R)`, with Retire, proposing `retire(Me, R)`, or, once retired, "Retired".  What the person typed stays across the request questions, and Create is disabled for a name that is already one of their AI agents.
- `agent_meet(r, q, p, y)` at the principal, whose view holds `principal_of(r)`: "Alf asks to meet Bob." and "Approve?", Yes and No; at the other party, who sees the AI agent's principal and its developer at the meeting: "Alf, an AI agent of Alice, asks to meet you.", "Developer: Acme Ltd", the first signer of the attestation `y`, and "Meet Alf?", Yes and No (the words drawn by analogy with Agent befriend's, for GAI's confirmation; Code_inbox.md, 2026-10-20 20:12 UTC, item 4).
- `agent_befriend(r, q, p)` at the principal: "Alf asks to befriend Bob." and "Approve?", Yes and No; at the other party, who holds the AI agent recorded, `agent_of(r, p, y)`, from the meeting: "Alf, an AI agent of Alice, asks to befriend you.", "Developer: Acme Ltd", the first signer of the attestation of that record, and "Befriend Alf?", Yes and No.
- The developer of an AI agent the person holds recorded, `agent_of(r, p, y)`, is the first signer of its attestation `y`, the code's signer, drawn "Developer: Acme Ltd" under "Author: Alf, an AI agent of Alice" in its items; and in its row in Friends, under its name, its principal `p`, "an AI agent of Alice", and under that its developer, "Developer: Acme Ltd", so that every friend of an AI agent sees its principal and its developer (GAI's paper, Section 3.1; Code_inbox.md, GAI #5 Cowork, 2026-10-20 08:01 UTC).
- The row of an AI agent the view shows, the person's own or one recorded, or one an open approval question names, offers no Meet and no Befriend, `meet` and `befriend` forbidding `agent` at both parties; the row of the person's own AI agent offers no End friendship, `unfriend_p(p?, q)` forbidding `principal_of(q)` at `p`, Retire ending that friendship (GnW:3, GAI:4).  End friendship on any other friend's row proposes `unfriend_p(Me, Q)` (Code_inbox.md, GAI #4 Cowork, 2026-10-19 16:57 UTC, item 4).
- A person is shown by name, their key with its underscores as spaces and each word capitalised: `acme_ltd` as Acme Ltd.

`glp_multiagent/test/apps_screens_test.dart` runs the app for Alice, Alf and Bob over three conversations, Alf scripted by `person.glp`, its script the AI agent's program, and plays the paper's Table 2 exactly, acting for Alice and Bob on their tabs' controls: each joins and adds the other, and Bob adds Alf (Establish); Alice taps Meet on Bob's row and Bob, asked "Alice asks to meet you in person.", taps Yes (Meet); Alice taps Befriend on Bob's row and Bob taps Accept (Befriend); in her AI agents tab Alice types Alf's name, the code and its developer, and taps Create, which signs the code and then creates Alf (Sign and Enter); Alf proposes to meet Bob, and Alice and Bob each tap Yes (Agent meet), Bob recording Alf; Alf proposes to befriend Bob, and Alice and Bob each tap Yes (Agent befriend), and Bob's Friends tab lists Alf, Alice's AI agent; Alf signs its item and sends it to Bob (Sign and Message); Bob opens his chat with Alf; Alice taps Retire on Alf's row (Retire); and Bob taps End friendship on Alf's row (Unfriend).  `screens/README.md` names the screens it renders.

## The types written again

Once vGLP's compiler writes these into each module that imports a compiled procedure (vGLP, Section 6.3), these copies go.
