# The screens of GFF's app

The screens of GFF's app, taken for GFF's paper (Code #16's task of 2026-10-28 13:0x UTC, from GFF #1 Cowork's), in the scenario of the app's play, `play_feeds` (`../plays.glp`, `../README.md`): Alice, Bob, Carol, Dave and Eve; the friendships Alice and Bob, Bob and Carol, Carol and Dave, Dave and Eve, friends following each other; Alice's two posts; Bob's, Carol's and Dave's introductions of Alice; Carol's, Dave's and Eve's Follow, each pressed once the update of a friend who follows Alice has reached them, their machines following through that friend; Alice's third post, reaching Eve along Alice, Bob, Carol and Dave; and Carol's Unfollow, Dave's following through Carol then ending (`path_broken`).  The app is compiled from GFF's contract and pragmas, `programs/gff/GFF.txt` and `GFF.glp` on the branch GFF-chooser at GLP `eb3e3386`, with those of GSG and GKK, which it extends, its unguarded parts taken by GFF's chooser, `../chooser.glp`.  Each file is one phone's screen taken on the iOS Simulator, an iPhone 16e, 390 by 844 points at 3 pixels a point, its status bar at 9:41 with full signal, Wi-Fi and battery, in the light theme, set in Roboto, framed as the phone in a black bezel with rounded corners, 1080 by 2220 pixels on white, as `../../gsnp/screens/README.md` describes.

They are taken by `glp_multiagent/tool/sim_screens.sh`, which plays on the simulator the scenario of `glp_multiagent/test/apps_screens_test.dart`, the test "GFF's app on five phones": it loads this app and runs its entry `miniapp/4` for the five over four conversations, and takes each person's acts by tapping, picking and typing on the tabs' controls.  From `glp_multiagent/`:

```bash
bash tool/sim_screens.sh "iPhone 16e" "GFF"
```

The same scenario runs headless, `flutter test test/apps_screens_test.dart --plain-name GFF`: it takes every act and checks every screen, and writes nothing.

The words on the screens are GFF.txt's pragmas, with GSG's and GKK's: the tabs Contacts, Friends, Posts and Feeds, from "Posts: Alice's feed." and "Feeds: the agents Alice wants to follow; under an agent, the friends Alice follows them through, the friends who follow them, and the items of their feed, each once, the signatures of each of its paths under it."; and the buttons of "Feed: Alice [Post] <item>" and "Following Volition: Alice [Follow]" and "  Alice [Unfollow]", with GSG's Befriend and GKK's Acquaint and Introduce.  A row of Feeds is an agent the person wants to follow; under it, in the order of the person's state, each item of the agent's feed the person holds, once, with under it, indented, the signers of each of its paths, the author first; and each friend who follows the agent, named once by each atom naming the agent: the friend the person follows the agent through, `follows(b, c)`, a friend whose update says they follow the agent, `friend_follows(c, b)`, and a friend who follows the agent through the person, `follower(d, b)`.  `programs/vglp/cva/compiler/CHOOSER.md`, "For vGLP, 6.2", states the drawing.

Taken on 2026-10-28, on branch GFF-shots3 off GFF-chooser at `eb3e3386`.
