# The screens of GB's app

The screens of GB's app for GB's paper (Code_inbox.md, Currencies #11 Cowork, 2026-10-20 19:57 and 20:27 UTC, task 2; Currencies #12 Cowork, 2026-10-23 22:31 UTC, point 4: "a mint of a bond, a loan asked of the borrower, the redemption of a matured bond, an escrow deposit"), in that order: Alice's, Bob's and Carol's phones as the construct family draws the app, from the last view each agent wrote (`../README.md`, "The screens").  Each file is one phone's screen taken on the iOS Simulator, an iPhone 16e, 390 by 844 points at 3 pixels a point, its status bar at 9:41 with full signal, Wi-Fi and battery, in the light theme, set in Roboto, framed as the phone in a black bezel with rounded corners, 1080 by 2220 pixels on white, as the screens of GC's app are (`../../gc/screens/`).

They are taken by `glp_multiagent/tool/sim_screens.sh`, which plays on the simulator the scenario of `glp_multiagent/test/apps_screens_test.dart`: it loads this app, GB as adopted from the Calculus, runs its entry `miniapp/3` for Alice, Bob and Carol, a conversation between Alice and each of the others, the test setting each agent's clock, day 1 first, and takes each person's acts by tapping and typing on the tabs' controls: each establishes their identity; Alice comes to know Bob and Carol by their keys; Alice issues five Alice-coins and three Alice-bonds maturing on day 10, and Bob four Bob-bonds maturing on day 5 (GB:1, Issue bond); Alice asks Bob to swap two Alice-coins for three Bob-bonds maturing on day 5, and he answers yes (GB:2, Swap coin for bond); the test sets the clocks to day 5; Alice redeems a mature Bob-bond against an Alice-coin Bob holds (GB:3, Redeem bond); and Alice deposits two Alice-coins with Carol, for Bob, on the condition she types, "day 10", and Carol answers yes (GB:4, Deposit).  From `glp_multiagent/`:

```bash
bash tool/sim_screens.sh
```

The same scenario runs headless in the suite, `flutter test test/apps_screens_test.dart`, the phones side by side in the test's view: it takes every act and checks every screen, and writes nothing.
