# GSN's app

The app of GSN, the grassroots social network, as a mini-app of the Grassroots Super-App (GSG paper, Section 2, "The Grassroots Super-App"), compiled down the abstraction cascade from GSN's act schemas.  It is GSG's app, `../gsg`, on GSN's platform: the same entry and network (`miniapp.glp`, the same file), and the same scripted person with GSN's scripts.

## The contract

GSN is GSC's contract (Coordination Appendix C); its clauses are `programs/gsc/GSN.txt`, and its act schemas `programs/gsc/GSN.glp`.  GSN extends GSG, which extends GKK, and cites their clauses, GKK:1 to GKK:3 and GSG:1 and GSG:2 (`../gkk/README.md`, `../gsg/README.md`).

Fourteen schemas: GKK's `establish`, `acquaint` and `introduce`, GSG's `befriend`, GSN's `unfriend`, which replaces GSG's and also ends every membership between the two, and `message`, `create`, `join`, `leave`, `remove`, `close`, `post_own`, `post` and `deliver` (`programs/gsc/GSN.glp`).  `post` and `deliver` require the member's friendship at the leader.  A group is named by its leader and a name, and content and a group's name are speech acts, `speech(Utterance, Peer)`, an utterance and the person who signs it.

## The cascade

1. The act schemas, `programs/gsc/GSN.glp`; the checker's verdict `syntactically_grassroots`.
2. The canonical CVA platform of the schemas, `platform.vglp`, as `programs/vglp/cva/compiler` prints it from GSN.glp; `bash programs/apps/compile.sh --check` re-prints it and compares.
3. GLP: the canonical compilation compiles `platform.vglp` when the directory loads.
4. The mini-app: `miniapp.glp` wraps the compiled agent and its dispatcher in the root's entry template `miniapp/3`.

## The files

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `platform.vglp` | the canonical CVA platform of GSN, as printed, with the words of its pragmas and its two widget declarations, `Approval =::= approval_sentence.` and `Request =::= social_menu(...)`, the request labels its pragmas give, its request question bare, of the request type `Request?` (vGLP, Section 5.4, "The compilation") |
| `miniapp.glp` | `miniapp/3`, the same file as `../gsg/miniapp.glp` |
| `person.glp` | `miniapp_person/5`: the scripted person, by role, with the keys the person knows, by which its script names its peers, `alice` or `bob` |
| `plays.glp` | `play_network/2`, the mini-app with no super-app |
| `self.glp` | the platform's types written again, the router's types, and the exports |
| `screens/` | the screens of GAI's figure of the app, and their README |
| `../gsn_boot.glp` | the super-app hosting the app on two smartphones, under `:boot` |

## Running it

From `glp_runtime/`:

```bash
printf '../programs/apps/gsn\n:limit 2000000\nplay_network(A, B).\n:quit\n' | bin/glpc
```

`play_network` runs two executions over one conversation, the play handing each its end of the root channel as the social graph does: Alice and Bob each establish their identity (GKK:1), ask to know each other, come to know each other (GKK:2) and become friends (GSG:1); Alice creates the group `friends` and adds Bob to it, and he answers yes; she sends Bob the message 'Hi Bob'; Bob sends the group the message 'Hi all', and Alice delivers it to him; Bob leaves the group.  Alice sends her message last: nothing on her screen shows a message committed, her state being unchanged by it, and her agent drops a request made while the message is open. ### On two smartphones

```bash
G=$(cd .. && pwd); printf ':artefact %s/programs/apps/gsn %s/programs/social/graph/core\n:boot %s/programs/apps/gsn_boot.glp %s/programs/social/graph/core\n:quit\n' $G $G $G $G | bin/glpc
```

As GSG's app's (`../gsg/README.md`, "On two smartphones"): the two super-apps befriend, Alice invites Bob to `gsn`, the conversation opens, and the steps of `play_network` run over it; each event reduces to quiescence and the boot settles.  Each person's lines, in order, held exactly by the suite's Section APPS, are what the super-app told them, `sg(connected(bob))` for Alice and `sg(befriend(alice))`, `sg(connected(alice))` for Bob, followed by the views of `play_network` above.  The Flutter super-app is not yet within reach for the reason `../gsg/README.md` names, Bob's acts for `gsn` not being in `superapp_ui/3`.

## The screens

What each person sees, as the construct family draws the app (`glp_multiagent/lib/ui_runtime/named_widgets.dart` and `tab_screen.dart`): Every word on its screens is the contract's, from its pragmas and those of the contracts it extends (vGLP, Section 5.4, "The contract's words"; Section 6.2, "The words a platform shows"); the request `known(Q)` is in no contract, so its button and its field are drawn by their type's names, known and Peer, and the two answers of every question, which no pragma names, as the constants yes and no.  GSG's app's tabs Contacts and Friends (`../gsg/README.md`, "The screens") and three of GSN's pragmas: Groups, "Groups: the groups Alice is a member of.", the atoms of `member`; Messages, "Messages: the messages Alice received.", the atoms of `received`; and Posts, "Posts: the posts Alice received in her groups.", the atoms of `received_in` (`programs/gsc/GSN.txt`).  In Friends, each friend with Unfriend and Message, GSN's "[Message] <text>", which opens the field text and proposes `message(Me, Q, x)`, `x` the text typed, signed by the person.  In Messages, each message received, an atom `received(x, a)`: its text, and under it its signer and its sender.  In Contacts, the field name with Create, GSN's "[Create] <name>", proposing `create(Me, n)`, Create naming no entry.  In Groups, each group the person is in, its name and its leader's name; in a group the person leads, Close, GSN's "[Close]", proposing `close(Me, n)`, Join, GSN's "[Join]", a picker of their friends not in it, proposing `join(Me, Q, n)`, and Post own, GSN's "[Post own] <text>", proposing `post_own(Me, x, n)`; in a group they do not lead, Leave, GSN's "[Leave]", proposing `leave(A, Me, n)`, and Post, GSN's "[Post] <text>", proposing `post(Me, A, x, n)`.  In Posts, each post delivered to the person in a group, an atom `received_in(x, a, n)`: its text, and under it its signer, the leader who delivered it and the group's name.  Remove, GSN's "[Remove]", bound by `listed(q, n)`, which no tab lists, is one button on the first tab with a picker of the members the person lists.  The approval question of a join is drawn at the top of the first tab, Contacts, badged, "Alice asks you to join her group.", the group's name under it, with yes and no.

`glp_multiagent/test/apps_screens_test.dart` runs the app for Alice and Bob over one conversation and takes each person's acts on the tabs' controls: each taps Establish and adds the other by key; Alice taps Acquaint and Bob yes; Alice taps Befriend and Bob yes; Alice taps Message beside Bob in Friends, types Hi Bob and taps Message, and Bob is not asked; Bob opens Messages, which lists it; Alice creates the group friends on Contacts and picks Bob under Join in Groups; Bob is asked on Contacts and taps yes; and Bob types Hi all in the group and taps Post, proposing `post(bob, alice, x, n)` in the order of post's head, and Alice's agent, which opens the delivery, delivers it to him, and his Posts lists it; Alice types Welcome in her group and taps Post own, and her agent delivers it to Bob; and Alice taps Close, and the group ends at both, Bob's machine voting unasked.  `screens/README.md` names the screens it renders.

## The types written again

`self.glp` writes again the types of `platform.vglp` that the imported declarations of `platform#agent` and `platform#dispatch` carry: `Peer`, `Speech`, `Utterance`, `Class`, `Proposal`, `Request`, `YesNo`, `Decision`, `Attempt`, `Cargo`, `Content`, `Net`, `Atom` and `Output`, and `Approval`; and the types the canonical compilation forms for them, `Question`, `Drawn` and `Ask(Q)`.  Once vGLP's compiler writes these into each module that imports a compiled procedure (vGLP, Section 6.3), these copies go.
