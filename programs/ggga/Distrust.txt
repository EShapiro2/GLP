Distrust
Distrust extends Friendship.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
1. ■ Distrust: Alice, who knows Carol, may declare distrust of Carol; if they are friends, this ends their friendship.
2. ■ Propagate: Alice, who does not know Carol, may declare distrust of Carol provided her friend Bob has declared it.
3. ■ Cease: Alice, who has declared distrust of Carol, may withdraw the declaration.
Logic pragmas
Distrust extends Friendship.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
1. ■ Distrust: Alice may declare distrust of Carol, provided Alice knows Carol and Alice and Carol are not friends. Distrust friend: Alice may declare distrust of her friend Carol, provided Alice knows Carol.
2. ■ Propagate: Alice may declare distrust of Carol, provided Alice does not know Carol, Alice and Bob are friends and Bob has declared distrust of Carol.
3. ■ Cease: Alice may withdraw her declaration of distrust of Carol.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: x has declared distrust of y = distrusts(x,y).
After x declares distrust of y, which requires x has not declared distrust of y: x has declared distrust of y.
After x declares distrust of x's friend y, which requires x has not declared distrust of y: x has declared distrust of y and x and y are no longer friends.
After x withdraws x's declaration of distrust of y: no longer x has declared distrust of y.
