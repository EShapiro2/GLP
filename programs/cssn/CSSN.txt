CSSN: Child-Safe Social Network
CSSN instantiates GSNW, a child for a ward and a parent for a guardian, and restricts and extends it.
Alice and Bob are adults, Carol and Dave children.
1. ◆ Parent.  Only a legal parent of a child, Alice, may create the ward Carol, operated by the child.
2. ■ A child's acquaintances.  Carol, a child of Alice, comes to know Bob by an act of Carol, Alice and Bob, provided Alice knows Bob; and comes to know Dave, a child of Bob, by an act of Carol, Alice, Dave and Bob, provided Alice knows Dave and Bob, Alice and Bob possibly one person.
3. ■ A child's group.  Carol may create a group with the consent of a guardian, Alice; only a child, Dave, may join it.
4. ○ Coming of age.  The creator of Carol may declare Carol of age, which ends every guardianship of Carol, Carol's friendships remaining, and Carol is then a person; the creator shall do so when Carol comes of age, and not before.
5. ○ A child with no guardian.  Alice, aware that Dave is a child though not a ward, shall neither befriend Dave nor let her child befriend Dave, and shall report it to Dave's parents if Alice knows who they are.

  Alice [Children acquaint]

  Dave: Alice wishes that you and Carol know each other.
  Bob: Alice wishes that Carol and your child Dave know each other.

  Alice: Bob asks that your child Carol join his group.
  Alice [Child unfriend] <person>

  Carol [Child leave] [Child remove]
