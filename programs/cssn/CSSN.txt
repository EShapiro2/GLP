CSSN: Child-Safe Social Network
Alice and Bob are adults, Carol and Dave children.
1. ◆ Parents.  Only an adult may create a child's identity, and is then the child's creator and a parent of the child.  Alice, the creator of Carol, may make Bob, whom Alice knows, a parent of Carol, by an act of the three, and may end that parenthood by their own act.  Alice declares themselves a parent of Carol only if they are Carol's legal parent or guardian.
2. ■ Meeting of a child.  To come to know Bob, Carol's acting parent Alice must know Bob; to come to know Dave, Carol's acting parent Alice and Dave's acting parent Bob must know each other.
3. ■ Friendship of a child.  To enter a friendship relation between Carol and Dave, Carol's acting parent Alice and Dave's acting parent Bob must be friends; to enter a friendship relation between Carol and Bob, Carol's acting parent Alice must be a friend of Bob.  While Alice is a parent of Carol, neither may end their friendship.
4. ○ Coming of age.  The creator of Carol may declare Carol of age, which ends every guardianship of Carol, Carol's friendships remaining; the creator shall do so when Carol comes of age, and not before.
5. ◆ A child with no guardian.  Alice, aware that Dave is a child though Dave's identity is not a ward, shall neither befriend Dave nor let their child befriend Dave, and shall report it to Dave's parents if Alice knows who they are.
6. ■ A child's group.  Only a child may join a group Carol leads.
