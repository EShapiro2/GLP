CSSN: Child-Safe Social Network
Alice and Bob are adults, Carol and Dave children.
1. ◆ Parent.  Only a legal parent of a child, Alice, may create the ward Carol, operated by the child.
2. ■ A child's acquaintances.  Carol, a child of Alice, comes to know Bob by an act of Carol, Alice and Bob, provided Alice knows Bob; and comes to know Dave, a child of Bob, by an act of Carol, Alice, Dave and Bob, provided Alice knows Dave and Bob.
3. ■ A child's group.  Carol may create a group with the consent of a guardian, Alice; only a child, Dave, may join it.
4. ○ Coming of age.  The creator of Carol may declare Carol of age, which ends every guardianship of Carol, Carol's friendships remaining, and Carol is then a person; the creator shall do so when Carol comes of age, and not before.
5. ○ A child with no guardian.  Alice, aware that Dave is a child though not a ward, shall neither befriend Dave nor let their child befriend Dave, and shall report it to Dave's parents if Alice knows who they are.
6. ■ A child's messages.  Carol may send a message to a friend, and to a group of which they are a member.
