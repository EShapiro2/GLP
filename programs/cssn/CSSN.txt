CSSN: Child-Safe Social Network
1. Parents.  Every ward is a child, and their guardians are their parents.  Alice, an adult, or Alice and Bob, two adults, jointly, may create a child, Carol, and are then Carol's parents.  Alice declares themselves a parent of Carol only if they are the legal parent of Carol.
2. Meeting of a child.  Carol, a child, and Bob, an adult, come to know each other only by an act of both and of a parent of Carol who knows Bob; Carol and Dave, two children, only by an act of both and of a parent of each, the two parents knowing each other.
3. Friendship of a child.  Carol and Dave, two children, become friends only if the two parents in the act are friends, and Carol and Alice, an adult, only if Alice is a friend of the parent in the act.
4. Coming of age.  Alice declares their child Carol of age, which ends the guardianship of every parent of Carol; Alice shall do so when Carol comes of age, and not before.
5. A child with no guardian.  Alice, who knows that Dave, an agent with no guardian, is a child, shall not befriend Dave, nor let their child befriend Dave, and shall report it to Dave's parents if they know them.
6. A child's group.  Only children may join a group that Carol, a child, leads.
