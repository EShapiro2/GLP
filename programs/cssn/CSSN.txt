CSSN: Child-Safe Social Network
1. ◆ Parents.  Only an adult may create a child's identity; two adults may do so by an act of both, and are then both parents of the child.  Alice declares themselves a parent of Carol only if they are Carol's legal parent.
2. ■ Meeting of a child.  To come to know Bob, Carol's acting parent must know Bob; to come to know Dave, the acting parents must know each other.
3. ■ Friendship of a child.  To enter a friendship relation between Carol and Dave, the acting parents must be friends; to enter a friendship relation between Carol and Alice, the acting parent must be a friend of Alice.
4. ○ Coming of age.  A parent of Carol ends their guardianship by declaring Carol of age, which ends the guardianship of every parent of Carol; a parent shall do so when Carol comes of age, and not before.
5. ◆ A child with no guardian.  Alice, aware that Dave is a child though Dave's identity is not a ward, shall neither befriend Dave nor let their child befriend Dave, and shall report it to Dave's parents if Alice knows who they are.
6. ■ A child's group.  Only a child may join a group Carol leads.

[Child-adult befriend] Your parent asks that you and Alice befriend.  Carol's parent asks that you and Carol befriend.

Joining: [Child join] Alice asks you to join their group.  Alice asks that your child Bob join their group.  [Children join] Alice asks you to join their group.  Your child Alice asks that Bob join their group.  Alice asks that your child Bob join their group.
Ending a relation: [Child unfriend] [Child leave] [Child close]
