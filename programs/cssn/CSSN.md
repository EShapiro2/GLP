# CSSN --- Child-Safe Social Network: the contract

CSSN's contract (Coordination Appendix C), the one copy, written by its owner (claude.md, "Sessions"); its act schemas are `CSSN.glp` beside it.  The paper, `/Users/udi/Grassroots/CSSN/main_CSSN.tex`, Section 2, quotes it.  CSSN extends GSNP and Wards, GSC's (Udi, 2026-10-18; Coordination, to_all 2026-10-18 19:23 UTC), and cites their clauses whole: GSG:1 to GSG:3 (`programs/gsc/GSG.md`), GSN:1 to GSN:5 (`GSN.md`), GSNP:1 to GSNP:3 (`GSNP.md`) and Wards:1 to Wards:4 (`Wards.md`), with the definitions of person, adult, child, agent, non-person agent and relation given there.  Its three clauses are Udi's approval of CSSN #5's proposal (Udi, 2026-10-18).

1. *Parents.*  Every ward is a child, and their guardians are their parents.  An adult, or two adults jointly, may create a child, and are then the child's parents.  A person declares themselves a parent of a child only if they are the legal parent of that child.
2. *Friendship of a child.*  Two children become friends only if the two parents in the act are friends, and a child and an adult only if the adult is a friend of the parent in the act.
3. *Coming of age.*  A parent declares the child of age, which ends the guardianship of every parent of the child; a parent shall do so when the child comes of age, and not before.

## The schemas

The schemas are in `CSSN.glp`, in GSC's notation above each term: GSNP's twelve (`programs/gsc/GSNP.glp`), with `child` forbidden at both roles of `befriend` and at the joiner's role of `join`, and eleven for children, which realise Wards: `enter` and `enter_2` (CSSN:1, Wards:1), `of_age` and `of_age_2` (CSSN:3, Wards:4), `child_befriend` and `child_adult_befriend` (CSSN:2, Wards:2), `child_join` and `child_join_2` (Wards:2), and `child_unfriend`, `child_leave` and `child_close` (Wards:3).  A parent's role in the children's schemas, and the sender's in `forward` and `post`, adds and deletes nothing, as a guarding role may (GSC, Definition "Act schema", at GSC bd0ebc4).  The marks are `child`, its guardianship `(child, identity, parent, parenting)`, the introductory act `befriend`, and the relay acts `child_adult_befriend` and `child_befriend` (GSC, Appendix F); the paper, Proposition "Certification" (Appendix B.3) with its proof in Appendix C.2, certifies the contract by them.
