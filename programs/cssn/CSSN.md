# CSSN --- Child-Safe Social Network: the contract

CSSN's contract (Coordination Appendix C), the one copy, written by its owner (claude.md, "Sessions"); its act schemas are `CSSN.glp` beside it.  The paper, `/Users/udi/Grassroots/CSSN/main_CSSN.tex`, Section 2, quotes it.  CSSN extends GSN, GSC's, and Wards, GAI's (Udi, 2026-10-20, replacing GSNP and Wards, both GSC's, of 2026-10-18; Coordination, to_all 2026-10-20 09:57 UTC), and cites their clauses whole: GSG:1 to GSG:3 (`programs/gsc/GSG.md`), GSN:1 to GSN:5 (`programs/gsc/GSN.md`) and Wards:1 to Wards:4 (`Wards.md`, GAI's), with the definitions of person, adult, child, agent, non-person agent and relation given there.  Its clauses 1 to 3 are Udi's approval of CSSN #5's proposal (Udi, 2026-10-18), and clauses 4 and 5 of CSSN #6's (Udi, 2026-10-19).

1. *Parents.*  Every ward is a child, and their guardians are their parents.  An adult, or two adults jointly, may create a child, and are then the child's parents.  A person declares themselves a parent of a child only if they are the legal parent of that child.
2. *Friendship of a child.*  Two children become friends only if the two parents in the act are friends, and a child and an adult only if the adult is a friend of the parent in the act.
3. *Coming of age.*  A parent declares the child of age, which ends the guardianship of every parent of the child; a parent shall do so when the child comes of age, and not before.
4. *A child with no guardian.*  An adult who knows that an agent with no guardian is a child shall not befriend them, nor let their child befriend them, and shall report it to the child's parents if they know them.
5. *A child's group.*  Only children may join a group a child leads.

## The schemas

The schemas are in `CSSN.glp`, in GSC's notation above each term: GSN's twelve (`programs/gsc/GSN.glp`, at GLP e364eed4, a group the community name `<a>.n`, `close` taking the listed role `a[listed(*, n)]`, every member the leader lists; no items and no signatures, a message being content received from a friend), with `child` forbidden at both roles of `befriend` and of `join`, the leader's role of `join` by CSSN:5, and `parent` and `parenting` of the other forbidden at the first role of `unfriend`, and thirteen for children, which realise Wards: `enter` and `enter_2` (CSSN:1, Wards:1), `of_age` and `of_age_2` (CSSN:3, Wards:4), `child_befriend` and `child_adult_befriend` (CSSN:2, Wards:2), `child_join` and `child_join_2` (Wards:2), `child_unfriend`, `child_leave` and `child_close` (Wards:3), and `parent_unfriend` and `parent_unfriend_2`, a parent's ending of their own friendship with their child, which ends their guardianship and, for a child with one parent, retires the child (Wards:3, Wards:4).  `child_unfriend` forbids `parenting` of the child at the friend's role, so only a parent unfriend ends the friendship of a parent and their child.  `retired`, which `parent_unfriend` adds, is forbidden at every guarding role, `establish`'s included, so a retired child takes no act.  A parent's role in the children's schemas, and the sender's in `message` and `post`, adds and deletes nothing, as a guarding role may (GSC, Definition "Act schema", at GSC bd0ebc4).  The marks are `child`, its guardianship `(child, identity, parent, parenting)`, the introductory act `befriend`, and the relay acts `child_adult_befriend` and `child_befriend` (the wards conditions, GAI's since 2026-10-20, formerly GSC's Appendix F); the paper, Proposition "Certification" (Appendix B.3) with its proof in Appendix C.2, certifies the contract by them, with `retired` its leaving mark, as syntactically grassroots with wards, by the paper's own Definition "Syntactically grassroots with wards" and Theorem "Grassroots realisation with wards and listed roles", which only CSSN uses (Udi, 2026-10-20, on GAI #5's proposal).  `child_close`, a parent's closing of a group their child leads, takes the listed role `r[listed(*, n)]`, every member the child leader lists, as `close` does (GSC, Appendix D.4, at GSC 8064cfb).  CSSN:4 describes no act and has no schema.
