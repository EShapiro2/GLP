1. *Authorship.*  An agent signs the content they create.
2. *Forwarding.*  An agent forwards content they received by sending it with the signatures it has and adding their own, and warrants that they know personally the agent they received it from.
3. *Confidence.*  An agent shall disclose content they received only by forwarding it.

## The schemas, and what they realise

The schemas are in `GSNP.glp`, in GSC's notation above each term: GSG's `establish`, `befriend` and `unfriend`; `sign`, `message` and `forward`; and GSN's group schemas, with a group message carried as an item.  `item(x)` in the state of `p` is a speech act `x` that `p` holds, signed by its author or by the agent who forwarded it to `p`, its author and every earlier forwarder signed inside it; `authored(x)` in the state of `p` says that `p` is the author of `x`.  A message (GSN:1) carries an item its sender authored as they signed it, and forwards an item they received; a group's leader delivers a member's message (GSN:5) by forwarding it, signed by the leader, and their own as they signed it (Udi, 2026-10-18; 2026-10-19, to GSC #4, on Code #11's finding that an author's message signed their item twice).  Every role but establish's requires `identity`, and the list below leaves it out.

- Clause 1: `sign(p? ; x)` --- at `p`, +`item(x)`, +`authored(x)`; and GSN:1 for content the sender created: `message(p?, q ; x)` --- at `p`, `authored(x)`; at `q`, `friend(p)`, +`item(x)`.
- Clause 2, and GSN:1 for content the sender received: `forward(p?, q ; x)` --- at `p`, `item(x)`, not `authored(x)`; at `q`, `friend(p)`, +`item(sgn(x, p))`.  The warranty describes no act.
- Clause 3 describes no act: every act of the contract that discloses an item its holder received is a forward.
- GSN:2 to GSN:4: `create`, `join`, `leave`, `close` and `drop`, as in `GSN.glp`; `close` takes the listed role `a[listed(*, n)]`, every member the leader lists, and ends every membership in one act (GSC, Appendix D.4).
- GSN:5, with clause 2: `post_own(a? ; x, n)` --- at `a`, `item(x)`, `member(<a>.n)`, not `posted(x, n)`, +`posted(x, n)`; `post(p?, a ; x, n)` --- a member's own item: at `p`, `authored(x)`, `member(<a>.n)`; at `a`, `member(<a>.n)`, `listed(p, n)`, `friend(p)`, +`posted(x, n)`; `post_forward(p?, a ; x, n)` --- an item the member received: at `p`, `item(x)`, not `authored(x)`, `member(<a>.n)`; at `a`, `member(<a>.n)`, `listed(p, n)`, `friend(p)`, +`posted(sgn(x, p), n)`; `deliver(a, q ; y, n)` --- at `a`, `member(<a>.n)`, `posted(y, n)`, `listed(q, n)`, `friend(q)`, not `authored(y)`, not `delivered(y, q, n)`, +`delivered(y, q, n)`; at `q`, `member(<a>.n)`, +`item(sgn(y, a))`; and `deliver_own(a, q ; y, n)` --- an item the leader authored: at `a`, `member(<a>.n)`, `posted(y, n)`, `authored(y)`, `listed(q, n)`, `friend(q)`, not `delivered(y, q, n)`, +`delivered(y, q, n)`; at `q`, `member(<a>.n)`, +`item(y)`.  Delivery is not guarded: the leader undertook it on creating the group, and signs what they deliver.
