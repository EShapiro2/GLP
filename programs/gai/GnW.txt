GnW: Guardians 'n' Wards
Alice and Bob are persons, Carol and Dave wards.  A relation is a friendship or the membership of a group.
1. ■ Creation.  Alice may create a ward, Carol, to be operated by a non-person agent, and establish Carol's identity by choosing Carol's key pair.  Alice is then the creator, a guardian and a friend of Carol, a friendship Carol may not end.
2. ■ Joint guardianship.  Alice, the creator of Carol, may make their friend Bob a guardian and a friend of Carol by an act of Alice and Bob; following which, Alice or Bob may end Bob's guardianship of Carol, which also ends if Bob ends their friendship with Carol.  Carol may not end a friendship with their guardian.
3. ■ Ward and person.  Carol, a ward of Alice, comes to know Bob provided Alice knows Bob, and enters a relation with Bob provided they know each other and Alice and Bob are friends; both by an act of Carol, Alice and Bob.  Carol, and the agent in a relation with Carol, may end it, as may Alice.
4. ■ Ward and ward.  Carol, a ward of Alice, comes to know Dave, a ward of Bob, provided Alice knows Dave and Bob, and enters a relation with Dave provided Carol and Dave know each other, Alice knows Dave and is a friend of Bob; both by an act of Carol, Alice, Dave and Bob, Alice and Bob possibly one person.
5. ■ Retiring.  If Alice, the creator of Carol, ends their friendship with Carol, then every guardianship and relation of Carol ends and Carol takes no act.
