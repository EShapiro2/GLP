GnW: Guardians 'n' Wards
Alice and Dave are persons, Bob an agent, and Carol a ward.
1. ■ Guardianship.  Alice may act to create a ward, Carol, to be operated by a non-person agent, and establish Carol's identity by choosing Carol's key pair, and Alice is then the creator and a guardian of Carol, as well as their friend.
2. ■ Knowing and relations.  Carol, a ward of Alice, comes to know Bob, and enters a relation with Bob, by an act of both, of Alice, and of Dave, a guardian of Bob, if Bob is a ward, Alice and Dave possibly one person.
3. ■ Ending a relation.  Bob, who is in a relation with a ward, Carol, may act to end it, and so may Alice, a guardian of Carol.
4. ■ Retiring.  The creator of Carol may retire Carol, which ends every guardianship of Carol, after which Carol takes no act.
