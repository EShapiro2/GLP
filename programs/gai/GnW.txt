GnW: Guardians 'n' Wards
1. ■ Guardianship.  Alice may act to create a ward, Carol, to be operated by a non-person agent, and establish Carol's identity by choosing Carol's key pair, and Alice is then the guardian of Carol as well as their friend.
2. ■ Knowing and relations.  Carol, a ward, comes to know Bob, and enters a relation with Bob, by an act of both and of a guardian of each ward among them.
3. ■ Ending a relation.  Bob, who is in a relation with a ward, Carol, may act to end it, and so may a guardian of Carol.  The friendship of Carol and their guardian Alice is ended only by Alice, and it then ends their guardianship.
4. ■ Ending guardianship.  Alice's guardianship of Carol ends by an act of a guardian of Carol.  Carol, left with no guardian, is retired, after which they take no act.
