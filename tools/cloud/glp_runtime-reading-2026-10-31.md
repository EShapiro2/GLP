## Needs a ruling (not mechanical)

1. compiler: the two PartialEvaluators diverge (analyzer.dart 783-1467 vs partial_evaluator.dart): no-unit-clause shortcut; refusal of a multi-clause procedure in a guard; `_`-prefixed renaming and PE_n vs PEn; `_isAnonymous` startsWith('_') vs == '_'; RemoteGoal/SpawnGoal dropped by the analyzer copy after guard unfolding.  The type check unfolds with one copy and compilation with the other.  Which is the paper's is GLP's (TGLP) to say.
2. analysis: `_substParam` sets a substituted parameter's mode `isInput: true` regardless, where `_substituteTypeExpr` and `_substituteToExpandedNames` flip it (param_expansion.dart 441-471 vs 954-961, 552-558).  TGLP's.
3. analysis: constant identity --- `sameTypeExpr` and `shadowedBy` compare constants by `==` (1 == 1.0), `sameConstant` and `constantLabel` hold 1 and 1.0 apart.  TGLP's.
4. vglp: the old compilation (clause_compilation.dart, types.dart, mediator.dart's old half, program_compilation.dart 34-173) is reached by no .vglp in programs/ (67 files, none with `*(` or `display`); its comments say it stays "until its owner ports it", which the tree shows done.  vGLP's to retire.
5. wire: message_vocabulary.dart is imported by nothing in lib, bin or glp_multiagent; its header says the wiring "is C4's, gated on C3".  IGLP's to wire in or delete.
6. compiler: parser.dart's `parse()` ("legacy") skips `-module(` where `parseModule` refuses it, and both are live (glp_engine.dart 888, 900, 1159; canonical.dart 1508).  GLP's.
7. compiler: `_parseGoalOrGuard`, `_parseAtom`, `_parseGoal` admit different subsets of `:=`, `=..`, `=`, `@`; grammar or gap, GLP-Spec's.
8. runtime: `rt.gq.enqueue` vs `enqueueReactivatedGoal` (which also drops the goal from `suspended`); merging moves the moment a goal leaves `suspended`, which saveState could observe.  IGLP's.
9. multiagent: MessageType.agentMessage is never constructed but its index is in the saved-state format; GlobalSendRegistry.saveState writes one cell twice.  Removing either changes the saved-state bytes.  IGLP's.
10. compiler: error.dart's phase-to-category map returns null for 'loader', 'partial_evaluator', 'typecheck', 'validation', so those diagnostics print without a category prefix.  Fixing changes printed text.
