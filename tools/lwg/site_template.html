From a governing document to a GrassApp

LWG reads the rules an organization already has and turns them into a grassroots contract, keeping a record of what became of every provision. Prototype: four documents carried through four modules, and a way to bring your own.

Your document

  1. Paste the governing document.

  2. Take the prompt to Claude (or another model) and send it as a message. It contains your document and the instructions.

  3. Paste the reply here.

Nothing leaves this page: the document and the reply stay in your browser.
