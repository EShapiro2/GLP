# Questions: Association under the Israeli Model Bylaws

1. (provision 1(a)) The app has no registrar. Does the association also register with the Registrar of Amutot?  **Answer:** Keep registration out for now. (Nimrod)
2. (provision 1(c)) An appeal against a refusal goes to the general meeting. Build it as a second vote by the members?  **Answer:** Yes, or an appeal committee. (Nimrod) Built as a second vote by the members.
3. (provision 2(c)) Dues need coins. In which coins are dues paid, and does the committee's decision need the members' vote each time?  **Answer:** In the coins of the association. (Nimrod) Not built yet.
4. (provision 3(a)) Leaving takes effect at once in the app; the bylaws ask 30 days' notice. Who records a member's death?  **Answer:** Leaving takes effect at once. Nobody records a death for now; maybe a registrar committee later. (Nimrod)
5. (provision 3(b)) The meeting may expel only on the committee's proposal and for four grounds. Who attests the ground, and must a committee member move the motion?  **Answer:** Any member may move the motion; no ground is attested. (Nimrod)
6. (provision 3(c)) How is the member heard: a written response shown to all before the vote, or a live hearing?  **Answer:** A signed act of the member, kept on record. (Nimrod) Not built yet.
7. (provision 6) Ten days' notice: should a motion stay open for at least ten days before it can be decided?  **Answer:** Yes. (Nimrod) Built: ten days.
8. (provision 7) The audit committee is not built. Reports to the meeting are an undertaking of the committee?  **Answer:** Not needed; every act is logged. (Nimrod)
9. (provision 8(a)) Preset used: quorum in ballots. The bylaws count those present at the opening; here it is those who voted. Agreed?  **Answer:** Agreed. (Nimrod)
10. (provision 8(b)) If a motion fails for lack of quorum, may it be moved again with no quorum, as the adjourned meeting allows?  **Answer:** No second call for now; the motion may be moved again under the same rule. (Left to Claude by Nimrod)
11. (provision 10) The chair may break a tie. With no chair, a tie fails. Agreed?  **Answer:** Agreed: a tie fails. (Nimrod)
12. (provision 12) At least two committee members: enforce, or leave to the members?  **Answer:** Do not enforce. (Nimrod)
13. (provision 13(b)) Who records a court order that ends a committee member's office?  **Answer:** Anyone can record it. (Nimrod) Not built yet.
14. (provision 14(a)) Build co-option by the committee, or leave elections to the members only? (The bylaws limit it to a vacancy and until the next meeting; neither limit is built.)  **Answer:** Yes. (Nimrod) Built.
15. (provision 18) Which acts need two signers?  *not answered; default used*
16. (provision 21) State the transfer of surplus assets as an undertaking of the members?  **Answer:** Not yet. (Nimrod)
