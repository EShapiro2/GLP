Make a mini-app

Write a grassroots contract, and GAS2 takes it down the same cascade it takes every contract of GLP's main down: GSC reads the act schemas and checks that the contract is grassroots, the pragmas say what the person sees, vGLP compiles the platform, and the REPL certifies the mini-app. What comes out is a link: whoever opens it on GAS2 adds the mini-app, which their own device certifies under their own key, and two people who added it can use it together.

A contract is two files, as in GLP's tree: its text, NAME.txt (the title line, the clauses, and after Pragmas the tabs and buttons of the mini-app), and its act schemas, NAME.glp (the term contract_named/2 prints). Start from a contract here, give it a name of its own, and change what you like.
