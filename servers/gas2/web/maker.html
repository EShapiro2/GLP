Make a mini-app

Write a contract, and our server turns it into a mini-app: the same programs that make every mini-app here. You get a link; whoever opens it in GAS2 adds your mini-app, and two people who added it can use it together.

1. Start from a contract

as
Pick one that is close to what you want, and give yours a short name of capitals (like MYC) and a title.

2. Change it

The rules and the app's words (NAME.txt): the title line, the clauses, and after Pragmas the tabs and buttons of the app.
The rules in exact form, the act schemas (NAME.glp).

3. Make it

Takes about a minute. Our server checks that the contract is grassroots, compiles it, and certifies the mini-app. Then this page gives you its link, or tells you the step where it stopped and why.
