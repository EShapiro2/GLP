GAS2 on your device

Your own Grassroots Super-App. Its mini-apps are the contracts on this site that the cascade takes all the way down, and your device certifies each one under your own key. You meet people by their keys, and the two of you then use a mini-app together.

The apps are built from the GAS2 branch by GitHub's machines.

There is no iPhone app yet. GAS2 on the web is the same page and the same super-app, run for you on talmonlab's server over the relay; the server keeps your key, so it could act as you, and your browser keeps the only way back to you there. Add it to the home screen from Safari's Share menu.

A first test, with two devices

  1. Open GAS2 on both devices and tap Trust, so that each trusts its own compiler.
  2. On both, install the same mini-app, for example GKK, Grassroots Keys and Knowledge.
  3. One of you copies their key and sends it to the other, who pastes it under People and taps Add. The first is asked whether to be friends and taps Yes.
  4. Both open the mini-app. One taps Invite next to the other, and the other taps Accept theirs. After a moment the mini-app shows its state on both.
  5. Each taps Establish to establish an identity. Then one taps Acquaint and picks the other, who is asked and taps Yes. Now each lists the other under Contacts.

Messages between the two devices go through talmonlab's relay, encrypted end to end between your two keys. The relay sees who writes to whom and when, and nothing else. Your key pair stays on your device.
