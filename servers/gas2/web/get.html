GAS2 on your device

Your own Grassroots Super-App. Its mini-apps are the contracts on this site that the cascade takes all the way down, and your device certifies each one under your own key. You meet people by their keys, and the two of you then use a mini-app together.

The apps are built from the GAS2 branch by GitHub's machines.

AndroidGAS2.apk: allow installing from your browser, then open it WindowsUnzip, then run gas_phone.exe in the GAS2 folder MacUnzip and open GAS2; if macOS says it cannot open it, see below LinuxUnpack, run ./run.sh, and open the address it prints Any browser, or an iPhoneGAS2 on the web: your super-app runs on talmonlab's server, which holds your key iPhone, sideloadedGAS2.ipa, installed with Sideloadly or AltStore and a free Apple ID; renewed every 7 days. Not tested yet

On a Mac, GAS2 is not signed by Apple, so the first time macOS refuses to open it. Open System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down to "GAS2 was blocked", and click Open Anyway. Or, in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine followed by a space and GAS2 dragged into the window, then Enter.

GAS2 on the web is the same app, run for you on talmonlab's server: the server keeps your key, so it could act as you. On an iPhone, add it to the home screen from Safari's Share menu.

On a device your key stays on the device. Messages go directly over Bluetooth or the local network where they can, and otherwise through talmonlab's relay, encrypted end to end: the relay sees who writes to whom and when, and nothing else.
