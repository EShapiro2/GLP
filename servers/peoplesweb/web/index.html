The Grassroots App Store

Social contracts that run.

Apps owned and run by the people who use them, on their own phones: no company, server or blockchain in the middle. Each app starts as a contract, a few rules in plain English saying who may do what, with whose agreement. Any group can start an app on its own, and separate groups can later join into one. Programs turn the contract into the app, and a theorem guarantees that every app built this way can grow like that, from the bottom up.
