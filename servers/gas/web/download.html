Get the app

GAS on your own device: each app runs on it, between you and the people you use it with. People near you show up by themselves; people far away you add with a link.

🤖 Android

Test version Download for Android
An .apk file, about 86 MB
  1. Tap Download, then open the downloaded file.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps (Install unknown apps), then go back and tap Install.
  3. Open GAS, pick an app, and allow Bluetooth and nearby devices when asked.

Not from the Play Store yet, which is why Android asks.

💻 Mac

Coming soon Download for Mac
  1. Tap Download; the Mac unzips it into GAS in Downloads. Drag it into Applications.
  2. Open it. The Mac says it cannot check GAS: click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway.
  3. Allow Bluetooth and the local network when asked.

A GAS.app to drag into Applications.

Not from Apple's App Store and not yet signed, which is why the Mac asks.

📱 iPhone

In the browser Open GAS on the web
  1. Open it in Safari, then tap Share → Add to Home Screen. GAS then opens like an app.

Until GAS is on Apple's App Store: your agent runs on talmonlab, which keeps your key, and meets people over the Internet. Works on any phone or computer.

🪟 Windows

Coming soon Download for Windows
  1. Download, then right-click the zip and choose Extract All.
  2. Open the GAS folder and double-click GAS. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Allow GAS on private networks when Windows asks, so people on the same Wi-Fi find you.

For Windows 10 and 11.

Not yet signed, which is why Windows asks. No Bluetooth on Windows: people on the same Wi-Fi, or anywhere over the Internet.

Meeting people far away

In an app, tap the people at the top. Share your invite link (or show your QR code); the other person pastes it (or scans it) and taps Add. Messages between you then go through talmonlab's relay, encrypted end to end: the relay sees who writes to whom, never what.
