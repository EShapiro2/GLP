Make an app

Write a contract, and GAS turns it into an app: it checks the contract is grassroots, compiles it, and gives you an app to try here and to get on phones.

Start from and change it: rename it on its first line, add clauses, their words under Pragmas, and their speech acts under Schemas.

How the text is laid out

As a contract's owner writes it: lines starting with % about it; its first line ID: Title, the ID a few capital letters of your own; its clauses in English; then Pragmas, the words the app shows (lists first, as Contacts: the persons Alice knows., then a line per clause, as Acquaintance: Alice [Acquaint] <person>); then Schemas, its speech acts.

A speech act is a head and a line per role: greet(Alice?, Bob) : then at Alice: identity, knows(Bob) and at Bob: identity, +greeted(Alice). A ? marks a role that must agree; + adds, - removes, not forbids. Variables come after a ; in the head; a line % kinds: c person, x act, d number says what one is when GAS cannot tell.

An app you make is unlisted: only people with its link can get it. How it works.
