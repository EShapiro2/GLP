# Claudese — do not use

Claude's stock words and phrases, banned in this project: to Udi, to another Claude, and in every document and paper. Use My English; follow the style guide. Udi adds more over time.

**My English is Udi's own** --- the English of `writing-style-guide.md`, which is how every text here is written. Claudese is what it is not.

🔴 **The deep structure under the entries above** (Udi, 2026-09-22), found by analysing a discussion of the thirty-nine post-nominal "only"/"alone" occurrences and of one paragraph of SGC.  Four constructions, each with its My English; a word-level ban above is usually an instance of one of them.

1. A qualifier that repeats what the sentence already says --- "guarded by the payer alone", "by redemptions alone", "only it can always issue" --- My English: delete it: "guarded by the payer", "by redemptions", "only it can issue".  The exclusion is in the statement; the qualifier adds emphasis and no content.  It stays where deleting it reverses the claim, and there the word is "solely".  Where it cannot be deleted at all the subject is wrong: "What the coins alone do not do" --- My English: "What the money component does not do".

2. An abstract noun as the subject of a negated verb, where an agent acted --- "The concept does not require this" --- My English: name who did what: "That is a choice those designs made, not something a CBDC must be."

3. Our own contribution written as a fault of others, the proposal left to be inferred --- "It is a limitation of these designs and not of the concept" --- My English: "we" as the subject and the proposal in the sentence: "Here we propose to augment the CBDC coins with CBDC bonds, so that credit and the instruments of monetary policy sit inside the CBDC."

4. The same claim restated in three registers in place of one claim with its warrant --- "credit creation and monetary-policy operations remain outside the CBDC.  These designs leave them outside; the concept does not require it.  It is a limitation of these designs and not of the concept." --- My English: say it once, then why, then what follows.

🔴 **The rule these four come from: think of Udi as the reader** (Udi, 2026-09-22): "Why don't you use this clear language in the paper, only with me?  think of me as the reader".  Where a passage in a paper and the same point said to Udi in chat differ, his is the My English and the paper's is the Claudese, so write the paper in the language you write to him.  Sent to Coordination for the style guide as well.

What Udi approved in the same discussion, and which is therefore My English: "solely" where the negation is partial; the focus adverb before the pronoun, "only it can issue", in place of "it alone"; "neither ... nor" for a coordinated negation, in place of a negation repeated on each object ("create no credit, open no lending channel"); the subject named in place of a qualifier; and a paragraph of three sentences --- the claim, its warrant, the proposal.
"X-dependent" as a predicate ("whether the central bank may issue it is jurisdiction-dependent", "implementation-dependent", "deployment-dependent") --- My English: say who decides, with the verb: "whether the central bank may issue it is a question for each jurisdiction", "each deployment decides it".  It is the bare possessive as predicate above in a hyphenated adjective, and construction 2 of the four: an abstraction in the predicate where someone decides (Currencies #4, 2026-09-22)
a body sentence built of terms of art ("not denied legal effect for being electronic, which is what the statutes provide", "negotiable within the bills and notes acts", "the authority to issue it is statutory: the digital pound is to be introduced only once Parliament has passed the primary legislation") --- My English: the plain word in the body and the exact formulation in the citation: "legally binding also in electronic form~\cite{...}", "whether it is negotiable", "whether the central bank may issue it~\cite{boe2026progress}".  A sentence a reader cannot parse without the statute is unreadable however correct it is, and correcting a loose claim by importing the source's formulation is what produces one (Udi, 2026-09-22: "Nobody can read this.  I keep mine.")
a repair that adds a referring noun phrase ("and the three have yet to be measured", after a sentence listing three things) --- My English: the adverb that points back, "and have yet to be similarly measured".  The reader carries on with an adverb and has to count back with a noun phrase; and the repair holds the sentence count of what it repairs (Udi, 2026-09-22)
"X is no Y" with a predicate noun ("a sovereign grassroots coin is no fiat coin", "a swap across denominations is no act of the contract", "a participant of every payment and no guard of it", "are no part of them") --- My English: "is not a Y": "a sovereign grassroots coin is not a fiat coin", "is not an act of the contract", "and not a guard of it", "are not part of them".  The quantifier "no X" as subject or object is not this and stands ("no demand for conversion increases them", "the parties need no mutual credit"), nor is the existential "there is no X" (Udi, 2026-09-22)

🔴 **The present tense for what is not present** (Udi, 2026-11-07, to GSC #13, verbatim: "Claudese tends to say everything in present tense.  My English uses tenses AND modalities: present is only for things that are indeed in the present, including eternal.  EVERYTHING ELSE is either past or future, may or must, possibly or necessarily, etc., but not present.").  A sentence in the present tense claims a fact that holds now or always; a consequence, an obligation, a permission or an impossibility is not such a fact, and its verb says which it is: will or would, may or must, can or cannot.  Two of his edits, on one sentence of GSC's Section 2: "once Alice knows that Bob breached an undertaking she no longer trusts him" --- My English: "she can no longer trust him", an impossibility, since the sentence says what knowledge of a breach rules out, not what Alice does; "and by GSG:3 she ends the friendship" --- My English: "and by GSG:3 she must end the friendship", an obligation, since the clause binds her and the sentence does not report an act.  The style guide's §39 gives the tenses, past, eternal and future; this entry adds the modal verbs, and the test is the same: ask whether the sentence states what is, what was, what will be, what must be or what may be, and write that verb.

A rule written as a description, with its mechanics, its exclusions and its insistence --- My English: the rule as an instruction, in plain verbs, said once.  Six of Udi's corrections, each GFF #4's then his: "plain words, his, never a paper's or a tool's", with a second sentence saying the rule again --- "plain words, his", once; "two to four short lines, one fact a line" --- "terse"; "sentences only, no write of any kind" --- "No writing"; "Udi approves recommendations; he does not decide ... whatever its cost" --- "Ask Udi to approve a recommendation, not make a decision.  Always propose the right thing, ignoring cost"; "last, alone, marked Q: or Request:, and nothing after it" --- "comes last"; "the first line is ..., then the handover, then stop" --- "say ..., write a handover, and stop" (Udi, 2026-11-07, to GFF #4, approving; reported by GFF, written in by Coordination #13).
